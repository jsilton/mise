Start rice soaking first, prepare sauce and salad vegetables before raw chicken, and finish the sauce before frying. Move cooked rice off the burner to rest, freeing a burner for the deep skillet; a second burner can rewarm sauce. Use a rack and brief 225°F oven hold for the first cutlet batch. Dress cucumber near serving.

The schedule refers to exact component steps rather than inventing a jarred curry-paste shortcut or breading from chicken Parmesan. Each cutlet requires its own 165°F center check. Store components separately and follow the reviewed source instructions for chilling and reheating.
