## Exact actionable correction

Replace Cooking Strategy step 2 with:

> **Start the cucumber while the rice cooks:** Crack and cut the half-batch cucumber, add half the salad's specified salt and drain for fifteen minutes, preferably chilled. Mix half its dressing while it drains, and continue with the fish preparation. When the fifteen minutes are up, shake off the liquid, blot the cucumber gently, and refrigerate it undressed until serving. Keep the dressing separate. This short extra preparation is included in the meal allowance.

Reason: the current step does not explicitly say that rice and cucumber preparation overlap. More importantly, leaving salted cucumber in its colander until step 6 can make the fifteen-minute drain a roughly forty-minute salting period while marination, roasting and greens finish. Shaking/blotting after the specified drain and chilling undressed preserves the reviewed side's intended texture and seasoning more reliably. There is ample safe opportunity to do this during refrigerated fish marination, before the broiler needs attention.

No change is needed to the final taste-before-dressing instruction: it preserves the salad's salt correction and avoids adding its entire soy dressing automatically.
