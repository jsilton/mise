## Components and proportions

One curry batch provides 450 g raw chicken and 300 g pepper/bamboo for four; half the rice batch gives 180 g dry rice across four modest portions; 600 g cucumber supplies four crisp vegetable sides. The coconut-rice portion is intentionally small alongside coconut sauce, and its optional sugar is omitted. Plain jasmine rice remains a lighter explicit alternative, not an incorrectly named short-grain link.

## Schedule and composition

Allow 50–55 minutes and two burners. Start rice first, use its full cook/rest rather than halving time, drain cucumber while preparing curry, handle vegetables before raw poultry, and dress cucumber last. The rice rest overlaps curry finishing. There is no phantom broccoli or green/yellow paste substitution. Salt is considered across paste, fish sauce, rice, and soy-vinegar dressing; the curry is tasted before all fish sauce is added. The side's Chinese preparation is named honestly rather than presented as Thai.
