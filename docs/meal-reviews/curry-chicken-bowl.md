Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical meal test recorded.

## Components and portions

Read the entire reviewed Slow-Cooker Coconut Chicken Curry and Cucumber Raita recipes. The main already includes 450 g raw chicken, 600 g vegetables, four cups cooked brown rice, 200 g mango, cilantro, and lime for four portions. Do not add a second rice or broccoli component. Its brown rice is explicitly a prepared ingredient with package-based yield and timing; this is not a new generic dry-rice formula. A quarter raita batch starts with 120 g yogurt and 75 g cucumber and yields roughly 5/8–3/4 cup; serve approximately 1/2 cup total (two tablespoons each).

## Work order and balance

The main supplies sweet mango and coconut richness; lime provides adjustable acidity and a small raita portion offers cold, tangy contrast. The recipe is Indian-inspired and does not use Thai paste. Its four-hour HIGH schedule cannot be represented by a 30-minute stovetop meal. Brown rice preparation overlaps the cooker period; raita's 25-minute preparation is near serving. About 4 hr 30 min elapsed and 40 min active allow loading, rice work, small raita preparation, and final garnishes. LOW has a separate 5–6-hour cooking allowance. Keep cold sauce separate from cooked components and do not claim it improves over several days.
