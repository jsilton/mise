# Shawarma Plate: individual meal review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no whole-meal kitchen test.

Read revised shawarma-style roast chicken and its tahini component, basmati rice, and fattoush. Each has an individual editorial record. Four plates use one 680 g chicken batch with its included 1/2 cup prepared sauce, half the basmati recipe (200 g dry rice), and one four-side fattoush batch with 120 g pita. The bread in the salad justifies the smaller rice portion.

Removed redundant standalone tahini and the additional whole-cauliflower side from the default composition. Those are not missing ingredients: the chicken already includes sauce and the plate already supplies protein, starch, and fresh vegetables. The whole cauliflower remains available as a separate expanded-menu option with its own longer cooking time.

Corrected marinade to the main's 4–24 hours refrigerated, and cooking to oven roast plus brief skillet finishing. Pita toasts at 375°F before the oven rises to 425°F for chicken. Rice and chopped vegetables overlap roasting; sauce is measured once; pita and dressing join the salad only at service. About five hours elapsed includes four-hour marination, or roughly one hour after marination.

Kitchen-test questions: one-oven temperature transition, one-cook chopping/roasting/rice coordination, half-batch rice evaporation, pita texture at service, and four-plate balance. No physical meal test recorded.
