## One-cook schedule

Allow 75–80 minutes elapsed and about 65 minutes active. Start the rice soak first, do vegetable/sauce prep during soaking, then handle chicken and clean. Rice cooks on one burner while chicken sears in two batches on the other. Move rice off heat for resting, then use that burner and a second skillet for the broccoli's two batches. Cooked chicken waits briefly, and the glaze is made only when broccoli is almost finished. Rice can stay covered through this short finish; longer delays require proper hot holding or prompt chilling.
