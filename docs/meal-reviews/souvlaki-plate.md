# Greek Souvlaki Plate: individual meal review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no whole-meal kitchen test.

Read revised souvlaki, rice-and-orzo pilaf, canonical Greek village salad, and tzatziki. All have individual recipe review records. Four portions use 680 g raw chicken, the 200 g rice/60 g orzo batch, one four-side salad batch, and about 1/2 cup tzatziki. A quarter sauce batch supplies this with some variation from cucumber draining; a full sauce batch is unnecessary for four plates.

Removed the overnight recommendation for small lemon-marinated chicken pieces. The main specifies 30 minutes–4 hours refrigerated, even skewers, and 165°F verification. The pilaf needs about 40 minutes including its covered rest. Tzatziki needs cucumber drainage and 15 minutes chilling. The salad is assembled close to service.

A 75–90-minute schedule allows vegetable work and sauce during marination, rice cooking before the grill finish, five-minute chicken rest, and last-minute salad dressing. Additional pita is unnecessary with rice-and-orzo already supplying starch. The generic Greek salad reference now resolves to the consolidated village salad.

Kitchen-test questions: actual one-cook prep and grill workload, pilaf hold during chicken finishing, sauce yield at quarter scale, salt across olives/feta/tzatziki, and portion balance. No real service test is claimed.
