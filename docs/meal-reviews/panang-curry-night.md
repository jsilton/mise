## Two-burner work order

The original 20-minute rice and 20-minute active meal promises omitted the actual 30-minute soak and ten-minute rest. New schedule allows 70–75 minutes elapsed and about 45 minutes active. Start rice soaking first, prepare curry during the soak, then rice cooks while the curry develops. Rice moves off heat to rest, freeing a burner for edamame. Curry chicken is finished to 165°F and can pause briefly off heat while edamame is drained, rather than simmering through the entire side preparation.

The schedule follows the actual main's coconut/paste, sauce reduction, then chicken/pepper order. It does not add chicken before establishing the sauce or automatically ladle a soupy panang over rice.
