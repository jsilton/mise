## One-cook sequence and actual time

Default elapsed time is approximately 85 minutes, allowing the gohan's 65-minute rinse/soak/heat/simmer/rest process followed by seasoning, brief cooling and final assembly. Bean preparation, fresh vegetables and separate clean dressings fit during soaking/cooking. Estimated hands-on work is 40 minutes: roughly five minutes rice rinsing/setup, five to eight minutes further rice handling, ten minutes vegetables/dressings, several minutes bean handling, five minutes fish work and several minutes plating and cleaning the raw-fish work area. The independent challenge favored this allowance over an optimistic thirty-five minutes. This is a plausible planning allowance, not a measured stopwatch total. Prep/cook metadata describe stages whose work overlaps; they are not a substitute for the full elapsed field and narrative.

A second small saucepan or the package's microwave method lets beans cook while rice soaks or simmers. They must be cooked early, drained and chilled promptly in a shallow container, rather than left to cool during a last-minute fish marinade. The same-day schedule begins with thawed fish; overnight refrigerator thawing and shopping/source checks are outside the eighty-five minutes. Raw seafood remains refrigerated while the vegetable and rice work occurs. Ready-to-eat work precedes fish cutting, then the raw work area is cleaned/sanitized.

The meal explicitly overlaps fish cutting and the approximately five-to-ten-minute refrigerated dressing with rice cooling and final setup, and it is no longer an exact anti-curing claim. Barely warm rice is a brief serving texture, not a holding or safety target. The meal measures the four-cup allocation, sets extra rice aside before raw fish is introduced, and promptly refrigerates excess. The advance-rice route explicitly allows approximately 35–45 minutes when reheating to the component's 165°F leftover endpoint and briefly cooling again before fish; a generic thirty-minute assertion should not hide that work. Ingredient temperature and reheating equipment can extend it.

The proposal points to the complete reviewed Shrimp and Grits Night as a fully cooked seafood menu with four portions and a five-person scaling instruction. Its shellfish/dairy/pork identity remains visible on that menu; it is not claimed appropriate for every allergy or dietary preference.

Raw tuna remains the shoyu meal's identity for diners who deliberately choose it. Source guidance is inherited from the reviewed main rather than converted into a supplier guarantee. Yellowfin's specific parasite exemption does not mean all tuna is risk-free. Domestic freezing, acid, texture and a marinade clock cannot establish suitability. Refrigerator/vacuum-pack thawing directions remain consistent with the main.

Same-day raw preparation and earlier supplier limits govern. Do not save assembled bowls or reuse raw-contact sauce at the table. Untouched rice/beans are separate cooked leftovers, refrigerated promptly in shallow containers and used under general three-to-four-day guidance; their reheating endpoint does not repair prior mishandling. Preparation and service time are counted together. No made-ahead raw-fish meal-prep claim or precise sensory shelf life is supplied.
