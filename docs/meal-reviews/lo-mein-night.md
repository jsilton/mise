# Chicken Lo Mein and Smashed Cucumber: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical meal test recorded.

Read full original meal, revised main, cucumber and aromatic-oil recipe. The main contains 450 g fresh raw egg noodles, 450 g chicken and over 700 g vegetables/sprouts for five. One full 600 g cucumber recipe is explicitly divided into five smaller portions instead of pretending its normal four-side yield matches five. Removed the additional edamame course from this plan; its extra protein and separate hot-pan work were unnecessary for the intended noodle dinner. All required components have individual reviews; aromatic oil is optional and now also individually reviewed.

The old meal treated cucumber draining as a marinade, relied on off-heat protein completion, missed the main's real pan load and declared microwaving inferior. Revised schedule separates the cucumber salt/drain and final dressing, cooks chicken fully, follows two complete noodle rounds, and accounts for the extra fresh prep. Neutral oil is the base method; making scallion oil cannot disappear into the meal headline.

Allow 75 minutes elapsed and about 65 active for one cook: cucumber and chicken prep, full vegetable cutting/drying and sauce division, noodle boiling, two chicken batches and two finishing rounds, then cucumber dressing. First noodle round waits only for the second, loosely covered. The 65-minute standalone main estimate is intentionally longer than the original forty-five-minute claim. A restaurant-style burner is not an excuse for an unworkable schedule. The five portions include both noodle batches.
