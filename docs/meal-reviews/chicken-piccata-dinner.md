# Chicken Piccata Dinner: individual meal review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no whole-meal kitchen test.

Read the complete revised piccata, fresh egg pasta, and arugula recipes. Each now has its own individual editorial record. One batch of each yields four main plates: 680 g raw chicken, a 290 g-flour pasta batch, and four light arugula sides. The salad supplies acidity and fresh texture beside the rich yolk pasta and butter sauce.

The piccata is unfloured; removed the contradictory flour dredge, extra wine, and extra cheese from the old meal method. The small pan sauce coats the chicken, rather than being represented as enough sauce for the entire pasta batch.

The old under-30-minute claim ignored dough production and its hour rest. Mixing/kneading takes roughly 15 minutes, resting 60, rolling/cutting another 30, and final chicken/pasta cooking about 20 minutes with overlap. The meal now allows about 2–2 1/4 hours from scratch, or 35–45 minutes with shaped fresh pasta ready. Prep chicken and dry salad during the dough rest; roll before frying; boil pasta near the sauce finish; dress salad at plating.

Kitchen-test questions: verify one-cook rolling/frying coordination, pasta sticking with the small pan sauce, four-plate portions, and final serving temperature. Editorial timing is an estimate, not an observed service test.
