## Formula, process, timing and scale

Start with one fully baked and cooled pastry shell; making, chilling and baking the dough is separate preparation. Have the chocolate chopped before the filling reaches its endpoint.

Use a saucepan with room for three cups of milk plus the remaining filling ingredients and for stirring without splashing. The crust may not hold all the pudding; keep small dishes ready.

For more filling, use additional prepared shells or pudding dishes. Smaller or larger filling batches still need constant stirring and the same thick, bubbling endpoint; the time does not scale directly.
