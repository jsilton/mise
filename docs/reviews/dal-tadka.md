Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

The original interchangeable lentil types had different cook times. Specified 200 g split hulled yellow mung with 720 ml initial water, adjusted by consistency, for four servings with rice or bread. Separated softening lentils from aromatic cooking, cooked onion before delicate garlic and ground spices, and placed acid/seasoning adjustments with clear texture cues. Added correct optional dairy/gluten notes for ghee and wheat-containing hing. Forty-five minutes applies to the stated mung, not all dal. Removed unverifiable nutrition.

## Relationships and composition

Dal is a pulse-based main that benefits from a starch and fresh vegetable component. It is not a duplicate of whole-lentil dal makhani or chana masala. Review neighboring yellow dal recipes for actual lentil species and finishing method before combining records. The meal should not automatically require multiple breads and rice.
