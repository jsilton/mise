# Yellow Squash Casserole: individual recipe review

Date: 2026-11-26. Editorial review only; no kitchen test recorded.

Source: Craig Plummer's handwritten notebook page, photographed and sent by the user. Family recipe, so the method and amounts stay as written (`stepText: preserved`) and improvements sit in the Suggested Tweak.

Transcribed from the card: 6 medium squash boiled until tender and drained well; 1/2 stick butter, 1 tbsp onion, 2 eggs, saltine crackers crumbled, salt and pepper beaten in with a masher; Parmesan sprinkled on top; 350°F for about 30 minutes.

Additions needed to cook, marked here and not on the page: a 2-quart baking dish (the card says only that it bakes), draining until it stops dripping, cooling the squash 5 minutes before the eggs so they do not scramble, and 1/4 cup Parmesan as the amount. The card gives about 6 saltines. The "2" in "2 egg" is overwritten on the card and is read as 2.

Suggested Tweak: press water out of the drained squash, soften the onion in the butter first, and add a buttered saltine topping.
