## Decisions

Dried navy beans are brined overnight, simmered until just tender, then baked at 325°F with bacon, onion, ketchup, molasses, brown sugar, mustard and smoked paprika, covered for 1 hour 30 minutes and uncovered for 45 to 60 minutes. Cider vinegar goes in at the end. Canned beans and a meatless version are given as swaps.
