# Hoisin-Glazed Meatballs: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- The beef method never incorporated its listed panko. Both versions now use a measured milk-and-crumb binder, mixed before the meat.
- The original 160°F endpoint was wrong for turkey. Separate beef and poultry endpoints now appear in the method and teaching checkpoints.
- The old 1 oz claim ignored binder weight. 680 g meat plus roughly 150 g binder/aromatics yields about 24 small 35 g meatballs; five main servings require rice and vegetables.
- Reduced added soy and removed extra salt and glaze soy because the two bottled sauces already contribute salt. Reduced the second sweet sauce from 1/3 to 1/4 cup. These are editorial balance decisions pending tasting, not tested optima.
- Removed the redundant sear after baking, repaired the skillet sequence, and replaced an unsafe universal frozen-meatball shortcut with package-specific guidance.
- Removed unsupported sauce-history and authenticity claims. Increased hands-on preparation allowance to 20 minutes.

## Relationships and composition

Plain rice supplies a neutral starch; broccoli or spinach balances a rich, sweet glaze. Choose one vegetable and rice, rather than serving every pairing. This remains distinct from Italian tomato-sauce meatballs by seasoning, sauce, and meal context.

## Kitchen-test questions

Confirm 24-piece yield, turkey tenderness, sauce quantity and sweetness across sauce brands, and oven browning without the redundant sear. Verify the 45-minute workflow with one cook.
