# Peruvian-Style Beef Empanadas: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-10-04. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Scope and attribution

New recipe adapted from a family recipe discussion. No existing empanada recipe was present in the collection at review time. Keep the original conversation and identifying details out of the public repository. This is a mild Peruvian-style home adaptation, not a claim of a definitive traditional formula. Preserve the exclusion of hard-boiled eggs, olives and raisins; an optional exterior egg wash is a separate choice. Tomato paste remains a small measured part of the filling.

## Four-lens review and decisions

- **Practicality:** One 12-count package of approximately 5-inch bakeable discs is the batch boundary. Use 1/2 lb (225 g) beef, 1/2 cup chopped onion, 2 tbsp finely diced yellow pepper and 1 garlic clove. These are recipe-development quantities, not a mechanical one-third conversion of the larger discussion batch. Weigh or freeze the remaining raw beef if buying a larger package. Do not publish an unverified claim of an exact cooked filling volume.
- **Yield and geometry:** Twelve portions at 2 tbsp require 1 1/2 cups of filling. Start with 1 1/2 tbsp per wrapper and distribute additional filling only up to about 2 tbsp when the seam closes comfortably. Wrapper thickness and cooked yield vary. Surplus is stored separately, not forced into pastry. Do not equate spoon volume to a purported exact gram weight. The USDA buying guide's 75% planning yield for cooked, drained beef with no more than 15% fat supports approximately 170 g cooked beef from 1/2 lb before vegetables, but does not validate this recipe's cup yield.
- **Cultural context:** Name the dish Peruvian-style and acknowledge a family adaptation. The Peru Delights source supports varied fillings, the use of cooled filling and a powdered-sugar finish; it does not establish this beef formula or baking temperature. Retain lime as the acidic finish. Use the repository's South American cuisine tag and Peru origin without inventing history or authenticity claims.
- **Technique:** Brown the beef before cooking the vegetables so it can make direct pan contact. Transfer it out to soften finely cut onion and pepper, then bloom garlic and spices briefly and cook the tomato paste. Use only 1–2 tbsp broth to release browned bits, reduce until no free liquid remains, and spoon off pooled fat. The filling stays moist rather than dry or soupy. Prompt shallow refrigeration replaces a deep counter-cooling bowl. Package instructions control thawing and baking; the 400°F conventional/20–25-minute starting point is limited to suitable bakeable pastry. Check the underside and filling temperature, not top color alone.
- **Flavor balance:** Mild cumin, sweet paprika, oregano and a pinch of turmeric retain the warm seasoning profile. One teaspoon paste adds depth without making a tomato sauce. Start with 1/4 tsp fine salt and taste after fully cooking; disc sodium varies. Lime cuts pastry richness; powdered sugar stays optional. Pair with a crisp green salad, not an additional starch.

## Timing, safety and representation

- Allow 1 hour 50 minutes from thawed discs, including chopping, roughly 20 minutes filling cook, 20–30 minutes chilling, assembly, baking and a 10-minute eating rest. Approximately 40–45 minutes is active. Thawing, extra chilling and additional oven batches are explicit additions. This is not labeled as a sub-45-minute weeknight recipe.
- Cook ground beef to 160°F / 71°C before cooling and bring the filled empanadas to 165°F / 74°C when baking. Cooling and storage use USDA shallow-container, refrigeration, time and reheating guidance. Count the 3–4-day refrigerator window from the filling's first cook, not from a later pastry bake.
- Use structured formula version 1 with explicit ingredient destinations. Compile the readable ingredients, yield and directions; do not maintain duplicate independent methods. Quantities scale, while 5-inch wrapper diameter, border width, oven temperature and per-wrapper filling capacity remain fixed.
- Do not add unverified nutrition, a family rating or a kitchen-tested label. The egg wash does not justify an egg-free tag; store-bought discs may also contain milk or egg, so no blanket dietary claim is made.
