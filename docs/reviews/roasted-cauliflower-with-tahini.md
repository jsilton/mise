# Whole Roasted Cauliflower with Tahini: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Preserved the whole-head presentation rather than silently converting to florets. The original 35–40-minute uncovered roast could brown a 900 g head before its center softened.
- Added a covered hot-water phase, separate knife checks before/after roasting, draining/drying, and late application of spiced oil. The expected total is now about 90 minutes, with size-dependent extension, not a guaranteed 50-minute side.
- Retained 3 tbsp oil but divided it across phases; named/divided salt and measured optional chile. Replaced the separate small tahini formula with 1/2 cup of the individually reviewed sauce and an explicit usesBase relationship.
- Sauce making overlaps roasting. Added a short rest, four side portions, storage/reheat guidance, and removed unsupported custard-like and “all flavor is color” absolutes. Removed unverified nutrition.

## Relationships and kitchen-test questions

This is a substantial separate side, not a necessary extra on the already complete shawarma/fattoush/rice plate. It is distinct from the sauce itself and from roasted cauliflower florets by its whole-head heat transfer and presentation. Test covered-phase time, core incision, head size, browning versus spice bitterness, final sauce quantity, and the 90-minute workflow before any kitchen-tested claim.
