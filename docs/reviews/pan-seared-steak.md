# pan-seared-steak: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Pan-Seared Steak with Butter Baste was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (the everyday beef dinners (steak, burgers, pot roast, meatballs and meat lasagna)). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Two 1 1/2-inch ribeye or strip steaks serve five when sliced. They are salted 45 minutes ahead, seared in cast iron with a flip every minute, then basted with butter, garlic and thyme to 125°F and rested 8 minutes. A medium option (135°F) and top sirloin are given as swaps. After review the baste time went to 4 to 6 minutes and the total to 30 minutes plus salting.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check that 6 minutes of searing plus 4 to 6 minutes of basting lands a 1 1/2-inch steak at 125°F, and that two 1 lb steaks fit a 12-inch skillet.
