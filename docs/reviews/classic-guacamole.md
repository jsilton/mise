## Process, timing, scaling and cold handling

About15 minutes preparation for the original four-avocado batch. Serve soon for best color, ideally within an hour; a longer wait belongs in the refrigerator. More avocados take longer to prepare and do not require a longer rest.

Keep at 40°F / 4°C or below and refrigerate within 2 hours total out of refrigeration, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C. Store covered in direct surface contact and use by the next day for best color/texture. Serve small portions and replenish from the refrigerator. A brown surface is not a safety test; lime and wrap do not preserve a batch left out too long.
