# Sun-Dried Tomato Chicken Pasta: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Retained approximately 225 g penne and 450 g chicken for four portions, with 240 ml half-and-half. Defined chicken slice thickness and low-moisture mozzarella, replacing the nonexistent sharp-mozzarella requirement. Reduced drained sun-dried tomatoes from 113 to 85 g and increased spinach from an unweighed cup to 120 g for a more balanced sauce.
- Listed the previously method-only two tablespoons tomato oil and chicken/pasta salt separately. Reconciled the half-cup versus one-cup pasta-water conflict as a one-cup reserve added gradually, not all at once.
- Cooked chicken before garlic/tomatoes so the high-heat stage does not scorch aromatics. Added 165°F checks before removal rather than relying on a later one-minute toss.
- Removed the promise that boiling mozzarella into cream yields glossy silk. Cheese now melts in small additions off heat, with normal stretch acknowledged and pasta-water adjustment. Accounted for all red pepper flakes, paprika, spinach and basil.
- Removed malformed chicken-rest prose, unsupported history, extra bread/heavy Caesar pairings, and unverified nutrition. The Sargento source establishes the cheese type and culinary use; Cheese Science Toolkit explains the connected protein structure behind melt and stretch. Neither proves our sauce stability.
- Compared against the covered Boursin/orzo bake and retained both: their pasta hydration, cheese, equipment and workflow produce meaningfully different dishes.
