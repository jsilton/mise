# Classic Coleslaw: individual consolidation review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: consolidated after considered review; no physical kitchen test.

Read the complete classic-coleslaw and coleslaw files. Both are mayonnaise-dressed green cabbage and carrot with cider vinegar, sugar, Dijon, celery seed and salt-draining before a refrigerated rest. The duplicate uses one cup mayonnaise, one tablespoon sugar and one teaspoon Dijon; the retained version used three-quarter cup mayonnaise, two tablespoons sugar and one tablespoon Dijon. These are balance variations within the same creamy slaw, not distinct styles.

The duplicate had a fifteen-minute total that excluded ten minutes draining and fifteen-to-thirty minutes resting, unlisted draining salt and no explicit final pepper addition. Its note prohibited earlier dressing while the other record allowed overnight holding. Neither universal deadline establishes texture for every cabbage. The canonical coleslaw now defines trimmed vegetable weights, eight side portions, a staged dressing and a thirty-minute drain plus thirty-minute chill, with realistic moisture/storage caveats.
