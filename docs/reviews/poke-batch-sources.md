The FDA model code's parasite-freezing schedules are -20°C or colder for 7 days; -35°C or colder until solid then 15 hours at that temperature; or -35°C until solid then 24 hours at -20°C or colder. These are recorded here for review accuracy, not offered as a household-freezer recipe. A freezer dial or “previously frozen” label cannot establish the required product history. The public drafts ask for supplier controls instead.

No source access failure prevented the primary findings. No secondary blog, commercial “sushi-grade” marketing, Reddit explanation, raw fish photo or freshness odor check was used to certify raw suitability. External source nutrition, exact yields and kitchen-test status were not transferred to these drafts.
