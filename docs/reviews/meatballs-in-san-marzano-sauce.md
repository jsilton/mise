# meatballs-in-san-marzano-sauce: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Beef and Pork Meatballs in San Marzano Sauce was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (the everyday beef dinners (steak, burgers, pot roast, meatballs and meat lasagna)). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

A bread and milk panade keeps 1 lb beef and 1/2 lb pork tender. The meatballs brown under the broiler and finish in a two-can San Marzano sauce built in the recipe, so the page stands alone and does not link the one-can base sauce. After review the sauce salt went to 1 1/2 tsp and the bread soaks before it is mashed. Spaghetti is given as another way to serve it.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check the yield (about 20 golf-ball meatballs), broiler browning at 6 inches and the sauce salt after 30 minutes of simmering.
