## Timing, capacity and scaling

Allow 35–45 minutes: 10 minutes prep, water heating, 15–20 min simmer after boiling, draining/brief steam drying and hand mashing. Warm milk during the simmer. Potato quarter size and pot capacity may extend cooking.

Scrub skin-on potatoes and cut similar quarters; eight medium is a count, not a supported new weight.

Warm the milk while potatoes cook. All measured milk/butter/Parmesan belongs in the mash; warm the milk before adding it.

Larger batches need a suitably larger pot or separate batches; keep pieces similar and judge tenderness, not multiplied time.
