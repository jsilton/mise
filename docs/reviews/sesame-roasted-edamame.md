# Sesame-Roasted Shelled Edamame: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Considered comparison and corrections

Read this original and the individually reviewed boiled-pod Edamame with Sea Salt. Kept them distinct: one serves beans from boiled pods, while this side roasts shelled beans and seasons the edible surface. Removed the shelled-or-in-pod interchangeability and defined 450 g edible beans for four sides, a much larger edible yield than 450 g pods. No duplicate consolidation is warranted.

Added product-specific cooking/ready-to-eat preparation before drying. Seapoint's FAQ explicitly describes its own blanched shelled beans as ready to eat; this does not establish that every manufacturer's frozen product is ready to eat. The recipe therefore follows the selected package rather than claiming a universal raw/frozen state.

Replaced 15 minutes at 400°F plus guaranteed crispness with 30–40 minutes at 375°F, checking a cooled bean and describing a drier browned exterior with a tender-to-chewy center. Total is about 55 minutes including preparation and draining, with extra thawing time if needed. Separated 1 tbsp neutral roasting oil from 2 tsp toasted sesame finishing oil instead of roasting in an ambiguous 1.5 tbsp sesame oil. Retained 2 tsp soy, 1 tbsp seeds and 1/4 tsp garlic powder, now using toasted seeds; reduced and staged extra salt to 1/4 tsp Diamond Crystal or 1/8 tsp fine. All optional flakes are measured.

Removed invented universal Japanese snack-culture/children/lasting-crunch claims, corrected the origin to Japanese-inspired, and marked leftovers poor for texture. Prompt chilling is explicit; the beans are not a shelf-stable commercial snack. Gluten-free variation checks tamari and the rest of the ingredients. Deleted unverified nutrition.
