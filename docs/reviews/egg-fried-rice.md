# egg-fried-rice: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

Set three cups (450 g) cooked rice, four eggs, and vegetables for four modest main portions. Reduced soy sauce from 3 to 1.5 tbsp and sesame oil from 1 tbsp to 1 tsp; neutral oil remains the cooking fat. Explicitly account for draining vegetables, separating scallion whites/greens, scrambling eggs separately, breaking cold clumps, and avoiding a smoking-oil requirement. Explain surface dryness without claiming day-old rice is mandatory. Added prompt cooling/storage context and reheating to 165°F. Twenty-five minutes requires already-cooked chilled rice, not an uncounted fresh batch.

## Relationships and composition

Fried rice already supplies starch and eggs. Pair with vegetables or a modest protein, not another full rice dish. Keep vegetable, shrimp, and other protein variants related only after comparing their actual quantities and cooking sequences. The parent method should not silently absorb incompatible raw-protein steps.

## Kitchen-test questions

Test pan size and half-batch behavior on a home burner, soy brand variation, cold grain separation, and whether four portions fit the intended meal.
