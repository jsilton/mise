# char-siu: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

Specified 1.36 kg shoulder cut into roughly 2-inch-thick strips for eight portions; removed the implication that tenderloin follows the same schedule. Reserved clean glaze before raw pork contact. Reduced oven heat from 425°F to 375°F to moderate scorching in a sugar-rich coating. Added a 145°F minimum and a 10-minute rest; color of the glaze is not a safety endpoint. The 9-hour-30-minute total explicitly includes at least eight hours marinating. Removed unverifiable nutrition.

## Relationships and composition

Char siu is a reusable roast-pork component, suited to plain rice and fresh/crisp vegetables. Char siu buns and fried rice consume measured portions of this base rather than duplicating a roast-pork recipe. Full usesBase/meal quantity reconciliation remains pending.
