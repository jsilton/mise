## Timing and storage

About 15 minutes preparation plus roughly 35–60 minutes total roasting, including the final 5-minute glaze, is a 50–75-minute prep-and-roast subtotal. Start heating during prep, but load only once the oven reaches 425°F; any heating still needed after prep extends elapsed time. A mix with more dense beets can take longer than the first check window. Turning and checking are active work during the roast. More sheets or sequential batches extend elapsed time.

Refrigerate promptly in shallow covered containers within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below; use within 3–4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F. The glaze and sweet potatoes soften in covered storage. Reheat spread out for even heating; pieces that were already very soft will not recover a firm center.
