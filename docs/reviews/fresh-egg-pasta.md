# Fresh Egg Pasta: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Retained the original 290 g flour, two eggs, three yolks, and tablespoon oil, preserving the yolk-enriched identity. Replaced dry-dough correction by extra oil with up to 30 ml water added incrementally after a squeeze test. Exact final hydration requires weighing eggs and a physical test.
- Named salt type and separated dough salt, cooking salt, dusting flour, and cooking water. Flour weight is authoritative rather than an inconsistent cup conversion.
- Added a realistic 45-minute active allowance for mixing, kneading, rolling, and cutting; retained a one-hour covered rest, for a total about 1 hr 50 min rather than 90 minutes with only 20 minutes preparation.
- Replaced universal machine settings 6/7 with approximately 1 mm for ribbons, clarified keeping unused dough covered, and added handling corrections.
- Replaced floating/1–3-minute-only doneness with a cooked-center check over a 2–5-minute starting range. Added raw-flour/egg handling, cold holding, freezing, and no shelf-stable air-drying claim.
- King Arthur source supports handling/rest/rolling concepts; its three-whole-egg formula is different and is not presented as a validation of this dough. Removed unverified nutrition.

## Relationships and kitchen-test questions

Four portions work with piccata or Marsala; those small pan sauces coat the chicken, not a large separate pasta sauce. The Piccata Dinner's two-hour plan remains appropriate. Test egg mass, water requirement across flours, 1 mm ribbon texture, kneading and rolling time, four-portion yield, and refrigerated/frozen handling.
