# Spinach Madeleine: individual recipe review

Date: 2026-11-26. Editorial review only; no kitchen test recorded.

Source: recipe text the user sent from Texas the Beautiful Cookbook. Not a family recipe, so it is edited for technique. The dish began in River Road Recipes from the Junior League of Baton Rouge.

Kept from the source: all ingredient amounts, the roux made with the reserved spinach liquid and evaporated milk, the seasonings and cubed pepper Jack, a 1 1/2-quart dish at 350°F, serves 6 to 8 (listed as 6).

Edits: the vegetarian tag is left off because Worcestershire has anchovy. The spinach cooks without added water. The onion softens in the butter before the flour goes in, so it cooks without browning the roux. The optional buttered crumbs are given as 1/2 cup panko with 1 tbsp melted butter. Bake time is 15 to 20 minutes for an assembled hot dish and 30 to 35 from cold. Jalapeño processed cheese is listed as the swap, closer to the original.

Added to the Thanksgiving Feast meal as a side at the user's request.
