# meat-lasagna: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Meat Lasagna with Beef and Pork Ragù was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (the everyday beef dinners (steak, burgers, pot roast, meatballs and meat lasagna)). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

The ragù is part of the recipe, as the owner asked, and the existing pressure cooker bolognese is not linked. It uses 1 1/2 lb meat and two cans of tomatoes for four noodle layers with ricotta on three. After review the milk has its own step after the meat browns, the dish sits on a rimmed baking sheet, the description no longer says slow-simmered and the total is 3 hr 5 min. No-boil noodles are a swap.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check that the ragù volume covers four layers with enough for the top, whether 15 oz ricotta is enough for three layers and whether a deep 9-by-13 dish holds it.
