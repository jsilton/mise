# Chicken Baked in Cream Soup: individual recipe review

Date: 2026-11-26. Editorial review only; no kitchen test recorded.

Source: Craig Plummer's handwritten notebook page. Family recipe, method and amounts kept as written, improvements in the Suggested Tweak.

From the card: chicken pieces seasoned lightly with salt and pepper, a can of cream soup on top, covered with foil with a small vent hole, baked 1 hour at 350°F, then 10 minutes at 375°F.

Additions needed to cook: 3 lb bone-in, skin-on pieces as the amount (the card gives none), a 9x13-inch dish and cooking spray, removing the foil for the 375°F stage, the 165°F doneness check, and stirring the sauce to spoon over at the end. The greased dish is on the card. The card does not name the soup, so cream of chicken or cream of mushroom is listed.
