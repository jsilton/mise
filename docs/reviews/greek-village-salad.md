# Greek Village Salad (Horiatiki): individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Individual comparison and corrections

Read both greek-village-salad and greek-salad in full. Both are four-portion tomato/cucumber/pepper/onion/olive/feta salads with the same assembly method and no lettuce. Differences in feta shape, olive volume, oregano, vinegar, and optional capers do not constitute a separate recipe or meaningful technique variation. Consolidate greek-salad into this canonical record and preserve its URL.

- Standardized vegetables by weight (600 g tomato, 300 g cucumber, 120 g pepper, 50 g onion). Defined four generous side portions.
- Chose 60 g olives instead of the duplicate's full cup, 150 g feta rather than 170–200 g, 3 tbsp oil rather than 4, and 1 tbsp vinegar with a small optional adjustment. These are deliberate balance decisions pending tasting.
- Salt comes after tasting with the brined ingredients. Capers and pepper remain measured options. Pitted olives are the default; pit disclosure is explicit for alternatives.
- Removed cultural gatekeeping and false claims that cold tomatoes have no flavor or that salad must never be refrigerated. Added safe chilled holding and next-day texture guidance.
- Vegetarian status now depends on feta rennet. Removed both unverified nutrition estimates.
