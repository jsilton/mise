# Classic Creamy Coleslaw: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Full comparison and canonical decision

Read both complete coleslaw and classic-coleslaw records. Both use green cabbage/carrot, mayonnaise, cider vinegar, sugar, Dijon, celery seed and a salt/drain stage before chilling. Different mayo/mustard/sugar amounts and contradictory holding windows do not establish two distinct dishes. Retain coleslaw as the canonical Classic Creamy Coleslaw; consolidate classic-coleslaw with a redirect and update all internal references. Both original records receive separate considered-review accounting.

Defined 800 g trimmed cabbage and 150 g carrot rather than a two-pound whole head and variable carrots. Set eight side portions instead of vague four-to-six large servings. Use 6 g draining salt, 150 g mayonnaise, 30 ml staged cider vinegar, 15 ml Dijon, one tablespoon staged sugar, optional half-teaspoon celery seed and quarter-teaspoon pepper. This is a light-drain method with restrained salt, not the high-salt/sugar rapid purge-and-rinse formula in Kenji's recipe. Added a taste/rinse/dry response for unexpectedly salty shreds, and avoided unlisted mandatory extra salt.

Reconciled timing to fifteen minutes prep plus thirty minutes draining and thirty minutes chilling: 1 hr 15 min. Removed claims of guaranteed overnight crunch, universally sharper cider vinegar than white vinegar, non-negotiable celery seed and a strict thirty-minute dressing deadline. Cold storage and small service portions replace implied unlimited buffet holding. Original canonical nutrition was absent; removed duplicate's unverified estimate with its obsolete file. Both linked barbecue meals receive only the slaw dependency/timing correction and remain pending full review.
