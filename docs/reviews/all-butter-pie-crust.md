## Why it was added

All-Butter Pie Crust was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (pantry sauces, plain egg skills and cookout sides). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Single 9-inch crust by hand: 160 g flour to 113 g butter, 3 to 4 tbsp ice water, butter flattened into visible flakes, chilled 1 hour as a disk and 30 minutes in the plate. The disk softens 5 to 10 minutes before rolling. Blind baking, 1 1/2 batches for a deep-dish plate and a double crust are given as other ways to make it. The slug is fixed because the pumpkin pie links to it.

## Kitchen-test questions

Confirm the water range with the flour the family buys, and the blind-bake times in a glass and a metal plate.
