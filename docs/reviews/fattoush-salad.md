# Fattoush Salad: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Standardized pita at 120 g and the high-variation vegetables by weight: 400 g tomatoes, 200 g cucumber, 100 g radish, 150 g lettuce, plus herbs/scallions. Defined four generous side portions.
- Reduced pita oil from 3 to 2 tbsp and dressing oil from 4 to 3 tbsp. Lemon starts at 2 tbsp with a third optional because sumac and molasses also bring acidity; retained fruity molasses and separate sumac uses.
- Added drying, gradual dressing, thin-layer pita baking, cooling, and a candid description of eventual bread softening rather than a permanent crunch promise.
- Kept the 30-minute workflow by explicitly overlapping vegetable preparation with pita baking. Removed universal authenticity and “non-negotiable” claims and unverified nutrition; no entire external recipe was adapted.

## Relationships and kitchen-test questions

Fattoush already contains bread. The shawarma plate therefore uses a smaller rice portion and does not need extra pita as well. Test pita thickness/oil coverage, sumac/molasses/lemon variability, dressing volume, herb balance, four-side-portion yield, and 30-minute workflow.
