# Roasted Chicken, Broccoli and Cheddar Bake: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

Read the full original, which was labeled sheet-pan despite a saucepan and separate final baking dish. Reframed as a roast-then-sauce Cheddar bake, with two independent roasting trays and a 3-quart dish. The original chicken had no specified chunk size, oil was reused without accounting, salt/pepper were absent and broiling two minutes was offered as a substitute for casserole heating. All are corrected.

Retained 680 g chicken/450 g broccoli/360 ml broth/115 g cheese for four, with 28 g butter and 16 g flour for the roux. Added defined cuts, two tablespoons roast oil plus one tablespoon crumb oil, measured salt/pepper and 30 g panko. Two trays let broccoli leave at barely tender while chicken reaches 165°F. Sauce is thickened before cheese melts off heat; final baking verifies the center instead of only top color. Total seventy minutes includes preparation, independent roast, assembly, final bake and short settle.

Removed lighter-than-canned-soup and crisp-broccoli-in-sauce guarantees. Cheese source supports the general role of starch and gentle heat, using a different sauce formula; it does not certify this broth/roux ratio. No nutrition is estimated. Arugula is a fresh optional accompaniment instead of presenting extra rice and mashed potatoes as requirements.
