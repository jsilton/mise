# Chicken and Dumplings: individual recipe review

Date: 2026-11-26. Editorial review only; no kitchen test recorded.

Source: Craig Plummer's handwritten notebook page, titled Luxury Chicken & Dumplings for the brand of dried flat dumplings it uses. Family recipe, method and amounts kept as written, improvements in the Suggested Tweak. The page lists the dumplings generically, with frozen dumplings or egg noodles as swaps.

From the card: a small cut-up chicken boiled in lightly salted water with a quartered onion 15 to 20 minutes, boned, about 2 quarts of skimmed broth with a can of cream of chicken soup, salt and pepper, brought to a boil, dumplings added and cooked about 30 minutes.

Additions needed to cook: about 3 lb for the chicken, the 165°F check, straining the broth, cooling the chicken before boning, and topping up with water to reach 2 quarts. The 8 oz package size is on the card.
