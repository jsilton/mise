## Yield and planning decisions

The former one-package wrapper instruction is replaced with wrappers as needed for all the filling, expressly noting that package counts vary and extra wrappers should be available. There is insufficient evidence for a precise final wonton count. The original six portions are retained as portion allocation: 24 oz raw meat/seafood across six portions is 4 oz before egg, vegetables and wrappers. No numbered wonton yield is invented.

60–90 minutes is an editorial planning estimate for preparation, wrapping and serial cooking, not a measured timer. The exact number of batches and wrapper thickness remain variables. Homemade wrappers can take longer to cook; the checked center endpoint governs. Broth or dipping sauce is an optional serving allowance, not an uncounted compulsory recipe component. The small seasoning sample uses a nonstick skillet, so no unlisted frying oil is introduced.

Remove no-cook and add boil in cooking methods. Remove unverified nutrition. The old 55-minute header and unspecified package were unsupported; this version gives an explicitly estimated planning range and sufficient wrappers without inventing an exact count. No kitchen-tested claim is granted.
