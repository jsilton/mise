# Caramelized Black Pepper Chicken: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

The original ingredient pattern closely matches Charles Phan's dish, confirmed against his primary Food52 demonstration. The high sugar/fish-sauce ratio is intentional in that context, so it was not arbitrarily labeled incorrect or reduced just because it looked intense. Restored the missing 60 ml water and 1 tbsp cooking oil, named the source, and changed pepper from 1 tbsp to 2 tsp as an editorial midpoint with a milder option. Chiles are optional.

The original method seared 680 g chicken in one layer without pan capacity guidance, then only tossed it in glaze without a measured poultry endpoint. Specified two batches, gentle finishing to 165°F, and separate temporary versus finished-food plates. Glaze is monitored by flow, not dark color; it must stay loose enough while chicken finishes. Forty minutes replaces a 30-minute promise that did not allow batches. Removed nutrition including 13,170 mg sodium per serving, which was not credible as published.

## Relationships and composition

Plain steamed rice and a fresh/pickled vegetable fit the intense glaze. Replaced coconut rice and another rich eggplant sauce pairing with plainer choices. This remains distinct from soy-based black pepper chicken and a true caramel-sauce braise; neighboring recipes need ingredient comparison before any merge.

## Kitchen-test questions

Test sauce quantity at 680 g chicken, salt variation between fish-sauce brands, 2 tsp pepper with/without chiles, evaporation in the stated skillet, and the 40-minute workflow. Source demonstration supports the dish lineage, not kitchen testing of this edited version.
