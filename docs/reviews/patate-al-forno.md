## Timing and storage

About ten minutes to wash, cut and coat plus 35–40 minutes roasting is a 45–50-minute prep-and-roast subtotal, with extra time if centers are not tender. Preheating may overlap preparation, but load only once the oven reaches 450°F; any heating still needed after prep extends elapsed time. No required rest. Larger batches need more sheet area or sequential roasts rather than a multiplied cooking time.

Refrigerate promptly in shallow covered containers within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below; use within 3–4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F. Serve freshly roasted for the browned edges. Covered storage softens them; reheat spread out on a suitable pan and check the reheating temperature.
