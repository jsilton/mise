## Why it was added

## Decisions

Thai satay-style cooked sauce. Red curry paste is fried in a quarter of the coconut milk, then finely ground roasted peanuts, palm sugar, water and the rest of the coconut milk simmer 8 to 10 minutes until the peanuts soften. Peanut butter is the supermarket swap. Tamarind and fish sauce go in off the heat. One tablespoon of curry paste keeps it mild for kids. Swaps give brown sugar, lime juice and soy sauce.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check thickness warm and cold, and heat level with a common supermarket curry paste.
