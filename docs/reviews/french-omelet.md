# french-omelet: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

French Omelet was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (pantry sauces, plain egg skills and cookout sides). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Three eggs for one omelet in an 8-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat, stirred to small curds, set briefly on low and rolled off the heat. No browning. Chives are the only garnish and a Gruyère version is given as another way to make it.

## Kitchen-test questions

Time the curd stage on the home burner and confirm medium heat does not brown the butter.
