# Hyderabadi-style Chicken Biryani: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Preserved the defining raw-chicken/partly cooked rice kacchi structure. Used Chef Ranveer Brar's primary recipe only to verify that layering context, not as proof of our quantities, timings, or a kitchen test. His parboiled sella rice and quantities are different; no wholesale adaptation or claimed equivalence.
- Retained 1.13 kg boneless thighs and 500 g rice for six generous portions. Specified maximum chicken thickness, aged non-parboiled basmati, a 20–30-minute soak, and sensory par-cooking checks in place of “exactly 5–6 minutes.”
- Named salt type in both stages: 1 tbsp Diamond Crystal (or 1 1/2 tsp fine) for the chicken mixture, 2 tbsp Diamond Crystal (or 1 tbsp fine) in 2.4 liters drained rice water. Original unspecified kosher measures were ambiguous. This is not a sodium calculation; most boiling water is discarded.
- Reduced ghee from 56 to 28 g and explicit added onion oil from 3 to 2 tbsp. Increased frying oil supply from 120 to 240 ml to support two batches of 600 g onions; only a measured drained portion is added to the pot, with absorption still requiring testing. Golden onion endpoint replaces bitter mahogany target.
- Reduced optional floral water from 2 tbsp to 1 tsp and distinguished food-grade water from concentrate. Combined divided herbs so every half is accounted for. Removed unlisted extra oil for blooming rice spices; whole spices now infuse the boiling water.
- Added pot dimensions, active gentle-steam cue, chicken AND rice 165°F checks before rest, incremental finishing, and measured conditional water. Removed lowest-burner universal timing, no-lid-check prohibition, and reheating by minutes alone.
- Reconciled 6 1/2-hour total with minimum 4-hour refrigerated marinade, prep, onion/rice/dum cooking, and 15-minute rest. Removed longer-is-always-better marinating, unsupported spice penetration/history claims, “biryani improves” guarantee, and unverified nutrition.

## Relationships and composition

This is already a composed rice-and-protein main. Removed extra saffron rice and naan from pairings; raita and fresh kachumber supply coolness and acidity. Retained as a distinct kacchi biryani method, not converted into a generic cooked-chicken rice casserole. Side recipes remain individually pending and are not certified by these links.

## Kitchen-test questions

High priority before any kitchen-tested label: verify rice hydration and final moisture with the named rice class; chicken center and rice temperature distribution; 10–11-inch pot fit; burner control; onion frying time and fat uptake; salt and floral-water intensity; total 6 1/2-hour workflow. The current review is editorial only. Test equipment variations explicitly rather than claiming one universal dum duration.
