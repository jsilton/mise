## Decisions

Kansas City style, tagged American only. Onion is softened in oil, spices are bloomed, then ketchup, cider vinegar, molasses, brown sugar, Worcestershire and yellow mustard simmer 20 to 25 minutes to about 2 1/2 cups. Smoked paprika gives the smoke with no liquid smoke. Not tagged vegetarian or gluten-free because Worcestershire sauce varies.

## Kitchen-test questions

Measure the finished yield and check sweetness against the pulled pork and the slider glaze.
