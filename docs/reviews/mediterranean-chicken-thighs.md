# Lemon-Oregano Chicken with Roasted Peppers: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

Read the complete original, Mediterranean Chicken Night, related chicken bowls, shawarma and feta-chicken recipe. Retained the lemon-oregano thigh/pepper/onion roast as distinct from grilled grain bowls, spice-led shawarma and balsamic breast/feta cooking. Replaced undefined half-cup bottled dressing and automatic extra salt with 900 g thighs, 3 tbsp oil, 2 tbsp lemon, two garlic cloves, 2 tsp oregano, density-defined salt and pepper. Bottled vinaigrette is an explicit replacement for the entire marinade rather than an addition. Removed claims that dressing is universally perfectly balanced or that thirty minutes distributes herbs throughout meat.

Marinating is refrigerated for thirty minutes to four hours; it does not reset raw storage time. The original counter marinade was corrected. Defined 400 g trimmed peppers, 200 g onion, separate oil/salt/pepper and optional parsley. Two half-sheet pans keep raw chicken juices off the vegetables and allow independent endpoints. Lift chicken from excess marinade and discard it; no raw-contact serving drizzle. All thighs reach measured 165°F / 74°C, with checks starting at twenty-five minutes; vegetable centers must also be tender. Eighty minutes includes marinating, overlapping prep/preheat, roast and five-minute rest. Removed automatic bone-in time and two-spice shawarma equivalence; the reviewed shawarma has its own link.

Moved the complete serving spread into the composed meal, with an explicit link and one set of quantities. Chicken recipe now provides meat and roasted vegetables; its bread/sauce relationships are additions, not duplicates. The meal retains measured fresh tomatoes, cucumber, lettuce, feta, pita and tzatziki. Legacy nutrition removed.
