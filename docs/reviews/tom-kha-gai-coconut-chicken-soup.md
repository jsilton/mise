# Tom Kha Gai (Galangal Coconut Chicken Soup): individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and formula decisions

Read all original ingredients, steps and appended notes. Retained 450 g breast, standardized 400 ml coconut milk and 480 ml unsalted stock, and weighed mushrooms/tomatoes at 225/100 g. Four modest bowls have roughly a quarter of the meat and soup each; this is not four large meal-sized soup bowls plus unlimited garnish. Rice is explicitly separate.

Specified 30 g galangal rather than four unspecified slices, two trimmed lemongrass stalks and four large lime leaves, with a ten-minute infusion and straining before chicken/vegetables. Galangal remains defining; the previous casual ginger, lemon-zest and lime-zest equivalences were removed. Kept a frozen-galangal option and accurately renamed the complete tofu/vegetable-stock/tamari variation. Removed the extra late whole green chiles and misleading implication that guests can avoid already-infused heat by pushing them aside.

Reduced fish-sauce supply from 3 to 2 tbsp and sugar from 1 tbsp to 1 tsp, using unsalted stock and staged off-heat seasoning. Retained a measured 3 tbsp lime supply with tasting, rather than an aggressive pre-seasoning and later correction. Mushrooms begin before breast, and thin 1/4-inch chicken plus tomatoes finish with a 165°F endpoint. Removed the cumbersome optional strain after all solids and unlisted coconut-cream drizzle, extra basil and lime-wedge garnish. The 40-minute schedule replaces inconsistent 20/25-minute claims. Gluten-free status is now conditional on products; unverified nutrition was deleted.

## Sources, relationships and tests

Pailin's primary recipe and notes support galangal identity, removing infusion aromatics, off-heat lime and late cooking of breast. Her thigh formula, amounts, short breast guidance and up-to-one-week storage claim are not our recipe or safety basis; this version uses a thermometer and FoodSafety.gov's prompt chilling/3–4-day framework. Kept separate from paste-based green/red/panang curries. No extra coconut rice, basmati or garlic-bread requirement is introduced.
