# authentic-roman-alfredo: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

The original 114 g butter for 225 g pasta was unusually rich alongside 85 g cheese. Set an editorial starting ratio of 55 g butter, 85 g cheese, and 225 g dried pasta for two substantial portions. Weigh cheese rather than treating fluffy grated cup measures as fixed. Reserved water is a supply, not an instruction to pour in all 240 ml. Added a modest named-salt amount for the water and an off-heat, small-batch cheese incorporation method. Heating the pot counts toward the 25-minute total. Removed legacy nutrition that did not reconcile with the formula.

## Relationships and composition

Keep distinct from cream-based Alfredo and from cacio e pepe: this dish uses butter and Parmigiano, rather than making all white pasta sauces aliases. The rich pasta benefits from a peppery green salad; garlic bread is optional extra starch, not a required component. A remaining corpus-wide comparison must inspect neighboring white-sauce pasta recipes.

## Kitchen-test questions

Test the revised ratio with two dried pasta brands, how much cooking water is actually needed, and how long different pans need to cool before cheese. Fresh pasta substitution changes yield and starch concentration and needs a separate weighed trial.
