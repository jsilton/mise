# Thai Green Curry with Chicken: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Formula, method and identity review

Read the complete original and compared it with the reviewed red curry and pending panang. Kept 680 g thigh meat, standardized coconut milk at 400 ml and vegetables at 250 g eggplant, 150 g pepper and 75 g bamboo. Added 240 ml unsalted stock because the original insisted on a coating rather than a fluid green-curry sauce despite its large solid load. Retained 3–4 tbsp paste with strength guidance, staged the 1 tbsp fish-sauce supply and modest 1 tsp sugar, and measured basil at 20 g.

The original listed oil but never used it, demanded unshaken cream and visible cracking from every can, and claimed this was essential. Now gives a measured cream/paste stage with conditional use of that oil and a clear fragrant-not-scorched endpoint. Chicken is no longer supposedly seared in wet paste; it cooks in the curry with a 165°F check and a separate eggplant-center check. Total is 45 minutes including heating and the staged simmer. Removed the obligatory lime juice and extra bird chiles; paste supplies heat and makrut leaves/basil supply aroma. A small ordinary-zest substitution is clearly different, not an equivalent whole-lime replacement. Leaves are removed before serving.

Corrected the unconditional gluten-free label to a verified-package option. Removed extra coconut-rice pairing in favor of a plain base and cool cucumber option; neither is included in main ingredient quantities. Deleted unverified nutrition, which was plainly inconsistent with the chicken and coconut quantities.
