## Safety, time and four-lens judgment

Taste seasoning before adding raw egg. Bake at the retained 375°F with 25 minutes covered and 15–20 uncovered, extending until representative centers in every dish reach 165°F. [FDA Food Code 2026, §3-401.11(A)(3)](https://www.fda.gov/media/194741/download?attachment=), explicitly covers stuffed pasta; this is used as cooking guidance, not represented as home-cooking law. Rest at least ten minutes. About 90 minutes assumes prepared sauce and warm assembly; cold pans and extra oven rounds add time. Refrigeration and reheating follow [USDA guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety), and bakeware instructions control cold-to-hot transitions.
