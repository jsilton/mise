## Timing and storage

About 10 minutes preparation plus 18–20 minutes first roast and 8–10 minutes maple finish is a 36–40-minute prep-and-roast subtotal. Start heating during preparation, but load only once the oven reaches 425°F; any heating still needed after prep extends elapsed time. Firm centers may need longer before glazing. Turning/brushing is active work during the roast; no rest is required. Extra sheets/batches extend elapsed time rather than multiplying the two oven windows.

Refrigerate promptly in shallow covered containers within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below; use within 3–4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F. The maple coating becomes softer and stickier in covered storage. Reheat with space around the wedges and watch the syrup while checking the safe reheating endpoint.
