# soft-dinner-rolls: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Soft Dinner Rolls was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (the Thanksgiving table (pumpkin pie and rolls)). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

A milk, egg and butter dough (420 g flour, about 61 percent hydration, 1 packet instant yeast) is kneaded in a stand mixer, shaped into 15 rolls and baked touching in a 9-by-13 pan at 350°F to 190°F. After review the finish is two plain steps, butter then flaky salt. Active dry yeast and an overnight rise are swaps.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check the rise times in a cool kitchen, the dough feel at 3 1/2 cups of scooped flour against 420 g, and the bake time.
