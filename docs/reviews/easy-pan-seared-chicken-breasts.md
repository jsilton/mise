# easy-pan-seared-chicken-breasts: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

Specified 680 g chicken as 4–6 cutlets of 1/2-inch thickness, four servings, a 12-inch pan, and two batches. Dredging flour is a supply with excess discarded. Added oil division, removal of scorched residue between batches, and a measured 165°F center endpoint. Replaced the implausible 15-minute promise with 35 minutes including preparation, both batches, and a short rest. Resting is not a substitute for reaching the endpoint. Removed unverifiable nutrition.

## Relationships and composition

Corrected pairings to rice pilaf, arugula salad, or roasted broccoli, each with a clear starch/freshness/vegetable role. Distinct from breaded Milanese and tomato-cheese Parmesan: this is a lightly floured, directly seared cutlet. Its technique can be reused in composed dishes without duplicating the complete base recipe.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check pan capacity, cutlet yield from differently sized breasts, second-batch scorching, and the 35-minute allowance.
