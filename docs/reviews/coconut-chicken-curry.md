Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Removed incorrect Thai origin/cuisine and the claim that mango alongside this curry establishes a Thai balancing practice. This curry-powder/garam-masala slow-cooker preparation is explicitly Indian-inspired and distinct from the paste-based Thai red curry.
- Kept 450 g thighs and 240 ml coconut milk; standardized 600 g vegetables. Reduced tomato paste from 4 to 2 tbsp and garam masala from 1 tbsp to 1 tsp, moving it to the finish. These are editorial flavor decisions pending tasting.
- Replaced interchangeable breast/thigh timing with a thigh default and an earlier-check breast option. Added fully thawed handling, pot size, 165°F and carrot endpoints.
- Original one cup cooked rice was only a quarter cup per main serving. Specified four cups (approximately 780 g) prepared brown rice for four bowls, with its preparation separate. Mango and cilantro now have amounts; lime supplies optional acidity rather than calling every ripe mango acidic.
- Corrected total to 4 hr 20 min on HIGH, with separate LOW allowance, and removed duplicated additional rice/vegetable pairings and unverified nutrition.
