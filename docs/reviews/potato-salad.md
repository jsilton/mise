# potato-salad: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Potato Salad was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (pantry sauces, plain egg skills and cookout sides). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Yukon Golds are cut first, boiled from cold in salted water, then tossed hot with cider vinegar before a mayonnaise and yellow mustard dressing. Celery, scallions, dill pickles and four hard-boiled eggs go in. The eggs come from the new boiled egg recipe through usesBase. Total time counts cooling and a 1 hour chill.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check salt after chilling and whether 3/4 cup mayonnaise is enough for 3 lb potatoes.
