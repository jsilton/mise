## Timing and storage

About five minutes preparation plus 8–12 minutes roasting is a 13–17-minute prep-and-roast subtotal. Start heating during preparation, but load only once the oven reaches 425°F; any heating still needed after prep extends elapsed time. Extra sheets or successive batches extend elapsed time. Keep the stalk geometry and thickness check; no separate rest is required.

Refrigerate promptly in shallow covered containers within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below; use within 3–4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F. For the best stem texture, serve after the lemon finish. Refrigerated stalks soften; reheat only until the safe reheating endpoint rather than expecting freshly browned tips.
