# pumpkin-pie: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Pumpkin Pie was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (the Thanksgiving table (pumpkin pie and rolls)). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Canned pumpkin is cooked on the stove with brown sugar and spices before cream, milk, two eggs and a yolk go in. The filling bakes at 350°F in a blind-baked shell. After review the pie uses a 9-inch deep-dish plate with 1 1/2 batches of the site's all-butter pie crust (usesBase), has pairings, uses the chill-to-set tag and covers the crust edge with a foil ring after 20 minutes. Roasted sugar pumpkin and evaporated milk are swaps.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check the filling level in a deep-dish shell, the bake time to a 175°F center and whether the blind-baked edge overbrowns.
