# classic-beef-stew: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

Set 900 g chuck for six servings, reduced broth from 960 to 720 ml as a starting braising supply, and use the pot's liquid level and tenderness cues rather than a fixed time alone. Carrots enter later to avoid collapsing during the full meat braise. Four hours includes preparation and batch browning. Removed the false implication that wine reduction removes all alcohol. Added clear fork-tender texture and storage guidance, and removed unverifiable nutrition.

## Relationships and composition

Stew is already a composed main with vegetables and potatoes, so bread is optional and a fresh salad adds contrast. Keep it distinct from red-wine-forward bourguignon only if the sauce, vegetables, and technique remain meaningful differences. The full corpus comparison is pending.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check pot diameter, evaporation, braising liquid depth, chuck variability, vegetable addition times, and final six-serving yield.
