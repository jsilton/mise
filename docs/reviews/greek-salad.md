# Greek Salad: individual consolidation review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: consolidated after considered review; no kitchen test.

Compared the complete ingredient lists, four-portion yields, and methods of greek-salad and greek-village-salad. Both make the same tomato/cucumber/pepper/onion/olive/feta salad, with oil, vinegar and oregano, and the same assembly process. Neither includes lettuce. The generic record uses one cup olives rather than one-third, 6 oz versus 7 oz feta, and more vinegar; these are seasoning differences, not a distinct preparation worth a separate recipe.

Retained greek-village-salad as canonical because its name identifies the style clearly and it already links to the souvlaki family. Reconciled amounts, optional pepper/capers, measured vegetable weights, salt-after-brine tasting, and storage in that individually reviewed recipe. Removed both unverified nutrition claims and rigid authenticity/refrigeration statements.

The old Greek Salad URL redirects to the canonical recipe and preserves query/fragment bookmarks. All recipe and meal references move to the canonical slug. No original source is dropped without this record and the alias manifest. Flavor, yield, and storage texture remain kitchen-test questions.
