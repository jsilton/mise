# Chicken and Wild Rice Soup: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

The original fixed 40-minute plus 20-minute grain schedule applied to an unspecified wild-rice blend. Manufacturer evidence confirms substantial processing-dependent variation. Replaced it with separately cooked, measured pure wild rice (160 g), with an explicit blend substitution. This is a deliberate two-pot method to decouple rice texture, vegetables, and chicken, not a claim that the old one-pot schedule works for every product.

Retained 900 g chicken for eight substantial bowls and reduced default broth from 12 to 8 cups, with up to 2 cups for adjustment; grain quantity is reduced from 1.5 cups blend to 1 cup pure wild rice. Added the omitted oil, salt, and pepper. Garlic now cooks briefly after vegetables instead of ten minutes. Default meat is thighs, with breast as a monitored leaner option. Added 165°F and gentle-simmer cues. Replaced an incorrect promise of naturally creamy soup with an explicitly brothy outcome. Removed implausible 90-calorie legacy serving estimate.

## Relationships and composition

The soup includes protein, grain, and vegetables. A small salad or optional bread is sufficient; three sweet/starchy autumn sides were excessive and poorly differentiated. Distinct from creamy wild-rice casseroles and soups with flour/cream; do not merge them just for sharing grain and chicken.

## Kitchen-test questions

Verify the revised soup volume, eight-bowl yield, broth additions, and 85-minute workflow with dark-finished wild rice. Compare quick-cook wild rice and one labeled blend. Check storage separation and reheating texture. The manufacturer source supports grain behavior, not this untested soup formula.
