# Thai-Inspired Chicken Meatball Soup: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and formula review

Read the original and compared the one-pot yogurt/panko/chard meatballs and ricotta meatball bowl. This coconut-broth soup is distinct by seasoning, structure and serving form; neither comparison recipe receives a review label from this reading.

Kept 900 g ground chicken for six, but specified 24 roughly 40 g finished-mixture balls instead of undefined two-inch balls. Defined 30 g ginger and 20 g cilantro in place of a variable four-inch root and bunch; separated ginger/garlic and cilantro stems/leaves. Retained 45 ml fish sauce, with 30 ml in meat and the final 15 ml staged in broth. Added all missing oil (45 ml, allocated across three batches and aromatics) and a measured optional teaspoon sugar. Increased broth from 480 to 960 ml alongside 400 ml coconut milk to supply actual soup around six large meat portions. Kept 140 g spinach and increased lime supply to a staged 30 ml.

Replaced the grated-aromatic moisture-barrier claim with restrained explanation of mixing/binding and actual doneness control. Defined three batches, immediate continuation after partial browning, a gentle 10–12-minute simmer with 165°F center checks, and one-hour elapsed time instead of 30 minutes. Removed unexplained pot wiping followed by dry sautéing, vague five-minute sear for the whole batch, unrelated extra vegetable pairings and unverified nutrition. Rice remains a separate optional preparation.
