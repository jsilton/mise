# Holy Basil Chicken (Pad Kra Pao): individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

Read the entire original. Corrected holy-basil identity: the old title/note invoked pad kra pao but listed Thai basil and explained its anise aroma as though it defined this dish. Now holy basil is default and alternatives describe the actual difference. Kept separate from the eggplant/Thai-basil main; ground chicken and a fish/soy glaze are its own composition.

Retained 680 g ground chicken and four eggs for four servings. Reduced fish sauce from 4 to 2 tbsp and sugar from 2 tbsp to 2 tsp while retaining 1 tbsp soy and 60 ml stock. The former claim of equal fish sauce/sugar was false, and the combined seasoning was unnecessarily strong. This is an editorial flavor decision with brand variation and tasting still required; it does not copy another recipe's formula.

Measured all oil at 4 tbsp: 2 tsp per chicken batch, 2 tsp aromatics, 2 tbsp shallow egg frying. Added the previously absent egg cooking method, firm-white/yolk endpoint, raw-poultry temperature check, second-pan plan, and 40-minute schedule. Removed smoking oil and color-only chicken checks. Lime now has an amount and is optional, rather than universally squeezed over the herb aroma. Rice is explicitly separate; unrelated cilantro-lime/basmati pairings are replaced by the site's accurately named plain short-grain rice option. Gluten-free substitution now checks both salty sauces. Deleted unverified nutrition.
