# teriyaki-sauce: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Teriyaki Sauce was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (pantry sauces, plain egg skills and cookout sides). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

The four-ingredient Japanese formula: equal parts soy sauce, mirin and sake with half as much sugar, reduced by about a third to a glaze with no cornstarch. Swaps cover dry sherry or water for sake, supermarket aji-mirin with half the sugar, and tamari for gluten-free.

## Kitchen-test questions

Measure the reduced yield and confirm the cooled sauce brushes on as a glaze for the sheet-pan drumsticks.
