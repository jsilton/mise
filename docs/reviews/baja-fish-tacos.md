# baja-fish-tacos: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Baja Fish Tacos was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (white fish and fish tacos). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Cod strips are fried in a thin lager, flour and cornstarch batter at 375°F and served in corn tortillas with shredded cabbage, lime crema and cilantro. Plain cabbage is used in place of the site's creamy coleslaw because that is the Baja style. After review the oil went to 6 cups in a 5 1/2-quart Dutch oven, batches are fixed at 5 pieces, the total went to 55 minutes and the vibe changed from quick to comfort to match the time.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check the oil depth, the batter thickness with 1 cup lager and the number of fry batches for 1 1/2 lb fish.
