# Moroccan-Inspired Carrot Salad with Orange and Mint: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

Read full original, tagine and composed dinner. Corrected the vegan label conflict by removing honey and offering a small optional teaspoon of sugar. Weighed 680 g peeled carrot and 20 g optional raisins. Reduced the heavy perfume/spice/sweet stack: blossom water begins at 1/4 tsp, cumin 1/2 tsp, paprika 1/4 tsp, cinnamon 1/8 tsp; juice is 60 ml orange plus 30 ml lemon, oil 30 ml, with measured staged salt. Optional sugar/raisins are specifically omitted by default in the apricot-braise meal.

Removed unsupported sharmoula naming, universal food-processor condemnation and the fifteen-minute headline that omitted a 15–30-minute rest. Thirty-five-minute total includes twenty-minute chill; herbs join at serving. A processor coarse shred is a valid practical choice. No promise that salt and acid pull out sweetness as a fixed mechanism remains; observable water release and softening are explained without overclaiming chemistry.

Christine Benlafquih’s primary Taste of Maroc recipe supports the carrot/orange/floral flavor family. It does not establish this added cumin-paprika-mint formula as the definitive salad, so origin/title are explicitly inspired and it is distinguished from cooked chermoula carrots. Federal produce chilling guidance supports storage. No nutrition is fabricated.
