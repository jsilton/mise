# Japanese Eggplant with Chicken and Thai Basil: individual consolidation review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: consolidated after considered review; no physical kitchen test.

Read both complete originals. This record used 450 g eggplant with only 227 g sliced breast, a starch marinade, scallions, wine and a stock glaze; the other used 450 g eggplant with 340 g minced chicken, garlic/chile/ginger and similar fish/soy/sugar seasoning. Both separately cooked chicken and eggplant before combining with Thai basil. Meat cut and small seasoning differences are variations within this dish, not sufficient reason for duplicate discovery records.

This version's nominal four-minute covered eggplant sequence did not establish a tender center. The method used about five tablespoons oil without listing a quantity, put basil in early, never used scallion greens, and gave chicken only one minute per side without a temperature endpoint. Its four-serving yield implied very little meat per diner. The other record had unlisted salting/steaming water and incorrectly described this eggplant dish as pad kra pao.

Retained stir-fried-chicken-and-eggplant-with-thai-basil as the canonical recipe, now with equal 450 g chicken/eggplant, explicit oil and water, two eggplant batches, complete chicken cooking, a light measured glaze, and late greens/basil. Preserved a considered ground-chicken variation instead of retaining a duplicate. Omitted Shaoxing wine from this combined version and labeled its style Thai-inspired. The separate holy-basil chicken recipe remains distinct and pending its own review.

The old URL redirects with query and fragment preservation. Original baseline accounting remains unchanged. The canonical record documents sauce, texture, portion and timing questions requiring physical cooking; consolidation is not a claim that those tests occurred.
