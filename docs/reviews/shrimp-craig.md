# Shrimp Craig: individual recipe review

Date: 2026-11-26. Editorial review only; no kitchen test recorded.

Source: Craig Plummer's handwritten notebook page, for 2. Family recipe, method and amounts kept as written, improvements in the Suggested Tweak.

From the card: 24 jumbo shrimp (15 count), butterflied, soaked in seasoned salt and lemon juice 5 minutes and no more than 15, a stick of clarified butter, a garlic clove, parsley, hot paprika, cooked 7 to 8 minutes, served over rice. "Need good French white wine" is written above the title and is read as a drink alongside, mentioned in the chef's note.

Additions needed to cook: 2 tbsp parsley as the amount, how to clarify the butter, and the doneness cue, 1 lemon (the card says only lemon), peeling and deveining, mincing the garlic, and lifting the shrimp out of the lemon juice before cooking. Ghee and sweet paprika with cayenne are listed as supermarket swaps.
