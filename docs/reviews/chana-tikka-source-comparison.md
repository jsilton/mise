## What the external references establish

- Swasthi Shreekanth's [chana recipe](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/chana-masala/) is a primary authored comparison, directly readable. It supports tender cooked beans entering the curry and a reduced aromatic base. It is not the provenance of our source-free creation.
- Swasthi's [tikka recipe](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/chicken-tikka-masala/) and Hari Ghotra's [tikka recipe](https://www.harighotra.co.uk/chicken-tikka-masala-recipe) are primary authored comparisons, not recovered source credits. Their other ingredients and equipment differ. The author-hosted Hari print result was also read at `https://www.harighotra.co.uk/chicken-tikka-masala-recipe?element=05d23629-622c-416a-a19f-5a7419178177&format=raw&item_id=2527&method=download&task=callelement`.
- [FoodSafety.gov temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) was directly accessible. USDA's [chicken handling](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/poultry/chicken-farm-table) and [leftovers page](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety) returned direct-open errors, but primary indexed text was available (full relevant leftovers text and specific chicken-marination guidance). Limits are disclosed rather than calling failed fetches direct reads.

The records distinguish source-supported practice from newly chosen operational weights, salt definitions, reserve liquids, pan sizes, timing estimates and yields. No physical cooking, tasting or measured batch yield is claimed. Preserve legitimate source attribution in neighboring recipes without attaching those authors to these two unattributed originals.
