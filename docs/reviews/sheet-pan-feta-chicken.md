# Balsamic Chicken with Tomatoes and Feta: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

Read the full original and compared the lemon-oregano thigh roast and whipped feta. Balsamic/mustard/honey breasts with tomato juices and hot feta remain a distinct preparation; no title-only consolidation. Removed the extra whipped-feta pairing because this recipe already contains cheese. A simple green salad or measured grain is a clearer accompaniment.

Defined four 170 g boneless breast portions, evened to about 3/4 inch, 600 g tomatoes split 400/200, 180 g onion and 150 g feta in four slabs. The former 225 g feta plus separate suggested cheese dip was disproportionate. Two tablespoons balsamic and one tablespoon honey preserve the sweet-acid idea with less sticky syrup; mustard, oregano, garlic powder, meat salt, pepper, oil and basil are all listed and allocated. A clean reserved finish avoids using a raw-contact bowl as a late drizzle. Vegetables start with chicken, then later tomatoes and feta supply a less-collapsed contrast.

Replaced the instruction to remove chicken at 160°F and assume carryover with measured 165°F / 74°C for each center. Removed the generic rest wording and retained a purposeful five-minute rest after the endpoint. Finished chicken can leave before vegetable reduction; do not dry meat to chase a sauce. Later tomatoes are not crispy and wet feta is not promised a crust. Fifty-five minutes includes prep, heating, 25–30-minute roast and rest. Legacy nutrition removed.

FoodSafety.gov minimum-temperature and handling pages, plus USDA leftovers guidance, were accessed for the endpoint, raw-contact and storage rules. Flavor proportions and the revised staging are editorial development from the original, not a claim of an externally tested copy.

Kitchen questions: breast thickness/weight, first checks at twenty minutes, tomato water variability, honey scorching, feta brand salinity, sauce intensity, four-person yield and fifty-five-minute timing. No physical test recorded.
