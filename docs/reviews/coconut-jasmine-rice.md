# Coconut Jasmine Rice: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Compared the complete Coconut Jasmine Rice and rich-coconut-rice records. Both are jasmine rice cooked with canned coconut milk, water, salt, optional sugar/pandan, and a covered rest. The different richness levels do not constitute separate dishes. The generic record's Caribbean attribution is unsupported by its pandan/jasmine preparation; it also never uses its optional leaf.
- Retained coconut-jasmine-rice as canonical. Weighed its two-cup rice amount as 360 g, retained 400 ml coconut milk, and reduced water from 360 to 240 ml as an editorial starting formula. Removed the false universal 50/50 rule and supplied tenderness/dryness adjustments. Six portions are a side yield, not six complete meals.
- Reduced optional sugar from 1 tbsp to 1 tsp and named the salt type. Full-fat milk remains the default; a measured lighter variation is supported instead of claiming low-fat milk cannot work.
- Corrected total from 25 to 40 minutes, included full rest and pandan removal, and withdrew the unsupported identical rice-cooker timing claim. Replaced the sweeping Thai-staple assertion with Southeast Asian-inspired context. Primary indexed Hot Thai Kitchen text supports gentle cooking, rest, richness variation, and the narrower Thai context; direct opening was unavailable, so no unseen content is claimed.
- Distinct from rice-and-peas (beans, thyme, chile and allspice) and spiced coconut chicken rice (a complete protein/rice dish). Removed unverified nutrition.
