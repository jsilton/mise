# Slow-Cooker Chicken Ramen with Miso: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Individual corrections and portion review

The original omitted its six cups water, scallion whites and finishing sesame seeds from ingredients, had unmeasured eggs/nori, and described six hours of broth cooking as a sauce-coating endpoint. Now lists every component and distinct water supply, specifies five bowls and gives complete optional-egg cooking. Eggs are cooked firm in this version rather than labeling them soft-boiled without a method.

Retained approximately 1.35 kg skin-on bone-in legs; increased starting broth water from the method-only 1.42 liters to 2 liters and fresh noodles from 340 to 600 g for five main bowls. Weighed shiitake at 15 g and kombu at 5 g. The broth target is 1.8–2 liters after straining and skimming. Bok choy remains 450 g but stems and leaves now have separate saucepan cooking stages instead of a fixed ten-minute slow-cooker HIGH promise.

Miso is measured at 100 g and staged after cooking rather than half a cup at the start plus an unlisted extra spoon at the end. Retained 30 ml each mirin and tamari, moving mirin into the broth and staging tamari after tasting. Added thawing, chicken temperature and tenderness checks, straining/skimming, complete mushroom/bone handling, gentle kombu infusion, package noodle timing, five measured optional toppings, and 6 hr 50 min planned LOW workflow. Removed the probiotic claim, misleading restaurant-gelatin guarantee, redundant miso-soup/dashi/potato pairings and unverified nutrition.

## Distinction and source limits
