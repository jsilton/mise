## Timing and storage

About 10 minutes preparation plus roughly 20 minutes before glazing and 5 minutes after is an approximately 35-minute prep-and-roast subtotal when centers are nearly tender on the first check. Load only once the oven reaches 425°F; any heating still needed after prep extends elapsed time. Larger halves or extra sheets may take longer. Whisking the glaze overlaps roasting. The separate preheated-pan variation also requires its bare sheet to be hot before loading. No rest is required; serve the glazed sprouts promptly.

Refrigerate promptly in shallow covered containers within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below; use within 3–4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F. The glazed leaves soften in covered storage. Reheat gently enough to avoid burning the honey coating while reaching the safe reheating endpoint.
