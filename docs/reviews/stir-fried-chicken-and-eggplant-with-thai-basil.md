## Findings and ingredient accounting

Fully compared this record with japanese-eggplant-with-chicken-and-thai-basil. Both have Asian eggplant, chicken, Thai basil, garlic, soy/fish-sauce seasoning, separate browning and a combined glaze. The old 227 g sliced versus 340 g minced chicken, optional heat, ginger, scallion and wine differences did not justify two discovery entries. Kept this canonical name; retained a considered ground-chicken variation and redirected the other original.

Set 450 g chicken and 450 g eggplant for four main portions alongside separately prepared rice. The original 340 g chicken made a smaller protein serving. Retained the modest fish/soy/sugar balance, added a measured 60 ml stock glaze and the other version's thin starch marinade, and specified 45 ml total cooking oil. Omitted wine as unnecessary to this particular combined formula. All marinade, sauce and steaming water are listed distinctly. Scallion greens now appear in the method, and basil goes in off heat.

Removed the unmeasured salt-and-sweat stage and its unsupported crispness/oil-absorption guarantee. Measured oil plus covered water addresses the actual tender-center requirement. The old 15-minute cook/60-minute total mismatch is replaced with a 50-minute schedule allowing two eggplant batches and one chicken batch; crowded chicken needs extra time. Added 165°F endpoints and protected garlic from a smoking pan. Removed the generated nonsensical rest note and unverified nutrition.
