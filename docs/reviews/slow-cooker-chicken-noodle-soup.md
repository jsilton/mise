# Slow-Cooker Chicken Noodle Soup: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Individual findings

Read the complete source and compared Lemon Chicken Orzo Soup. Corrected ten supposedly massive bowls to six dinner portions: 680 g raw chicken, 1.9 liters stock, 550 g named vegetables and 225 g weighed dry fusilli. The former two cups pasta was ambiguous and the meat/stock did not support ten large mains. Specified boneless skinless thigh as the default for slow cooking, fully thawed handling, cooker capacity, measured salt/pepper and a staged 30 ml lemon supply. Removed grill metadata and unverified nutrition.

Pasta now cooks separately near service instead of relying on 30–40 minutes on LOW to be al dente. Chicken and carrots have separate endpoints, and an earlier-check breast variation replaces blanket interchangeability over six to eight hours. The 6 hr 30 min planning time includes prep and finish, with a clearly different HIGH window. Storage separates noodles and broth, cools shallow portions and reheats outside the slow cooker.

## Distinction, sources and tests

Kept distinct from the stovetop orzo soup: this is an unthickened, slow-cooked thigh broth with separately cooked spiral pasta; the orzo version uses diced breast, sautéed vegetables and a small flour/starch contribution. Similar lemon/parsley finishes alone do not justify a merge. Chicken Noodle Soup Night must match six portions, the actual slow-cooker setting and separate pasta pot.

USDA supports thawing, cooker handling and reheating principles, not a universal exact cooking time. Test stock concentration, six-bowl yield, carrot dimensions, cooker settings, thigh versus breast, pasta quantity, and final lemon/salt balance. No physical kitchen test recorded.
