# Garlic Roasted Potatoes: editorial review

2026-09-06. Written review only; no physical kitchen test.

**Evidence and identity:** Compared current file with earliest recipe commit 045e5ba276b560e4f7d3d485b4860f610d295bac and directly read [Ina Garten's published recipe](https://barefootcontessa.com/recipes/garlic-roasted-potatoes). The six ingredient amounts and original method match that recipe; attribution is now explicit. Retain the later Yukon Gold option and late garlic addition as adaptations, not claims about the original technique. Original 45–60 minute roasting window is restored; current official page allows at least an hour. Timing is cue-led and may extend.

**Ingredient ledger:** Preserve 3 lb potatoes, 1/4 cup oil, 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, 1 tsp pepper, 2 tbsp garlic and 2 tbsp parsley. Three-quarters of oil coats potatoes with salt/pepper; remaining quarter carries late garlic; all parsley finishes. Removed the parenthetical six cloves because clove size is variable and the measured minced amount governs. No new gram equivalents, fine-salt conversion or cooked-volume yield. Eight side portions retained.

**Method and scaling:** Similar-size pieces, dry surfaces and single-layer pan space make browning instructions actionable. One half-sheet only if uncrowded; otherwise two, rotated/swapped. Half batches keep piece size and time cues; larger batches increase pan area or use rounds. Garlic enters when potatoes are nearly tender, resolving the competing five/ten-minute prose; cues govern its approximately five-minute finish. All oil splits are proportional to the scaled list. The 75-minute planning total includes preparation/preheat plus up to an hour roasting, with longer ovens/pieces explicitly allowed. Removed unsupported nutrition and steam method tag.

**Relationships:** Distinct from mashed potatoes and smashed/parboiled variants: these roast raw, halved/quartered with garlic and parsley. Pork/turkey pairings remain sensible starch sides but do not certify those meals. Baked Cod Dinner needs coordinated 400°F oven handling and half a potato batch for four; its old 425°F/short schedule is not compatible with these components.

**Handling:** Reuse already reviewed FoodSafety.gov cooling, cold-storage and leftover-temperature evidence from the component review packets. Prompt shallow refrigeration, cold storage and 165°F reheating are shared handling rules, not proof of this dish's flavor. The oven reheating method is an editorial choice, with garlic browning monitored.

**Kitchen questions:** Actual pan loading for 3 lb, salt crystal variation, oven heat and 45–60-minute tenderness, late-garlic distribution, crispness after holding, eight-side portion adequacy and half/double pan configurations.
