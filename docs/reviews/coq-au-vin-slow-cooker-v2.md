# Slow-Cooker Chicken in Red Wine: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Compared directly with the reviewed bone-in stovetop coq au vin. Retained this as an explicit variation because boneless thighs, abundant vegetables, pearl onions, and unattended slow cooking serve a different practical need. Renamed honestly and linked with isVariationOf.
- Preserved 900 g chicken, about 450 g mushrooms, six carrots standardized at 450 g, and 283 g pearl onions for six portions. Specified boneless skinless and fully thawed ingredients.
- Replaced an unspecified soup-mix packet and unmeasured garlic powder/salt with unsalted stock, tomato paste, fresh garlic, measured salt/pepper, and Worcestershire. Increased wine to 240 ml but reduced it before adding 120 ml stock; the cooker also captures substantial vegetable moisture.
- Added missing cooking oil and eliminated unlisted spray. Carrots are smaller and below the meat. Added immediate transfer/cooking after the partial sear and a 165°F endpoint plus separate vegetable tenderness check.
- Replaced one teaspoon cornstarch added at the beginning with a separately controlled finish: up to two tablespoons slurry, added gradually to simmering sauce. This avoids assuming an arbitrary slow-cooker temperature will thicken sauce reliably at the end.
- Replaced 195-minute promise with up to 4 hr 50 min on HIGH, and separate LOW allowance. Added appropriate chilling/reheating guidance and removed exaggerated excellence/nutrition claims.
