## Timing and storage

About five minutes preparation plus 12–15 minutes roasting is a 17–20-minute prep-and-roast subtotal. Start heating during setup, but load only once the oven reaches 425°F; any heating still needed after prep extends elapsed time. Washing/splitting large bunches or using extra sheets can take longer. No rest is required; add the reserved juice close to service.

Refrigerate promptly in shallow covered containers within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below; use within 3–4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F. The florets and roasted lemon soften in covered storage. Reheat with the garlic and lemon; expect softer florets and lemon rounds.
