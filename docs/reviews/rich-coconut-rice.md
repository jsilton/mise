Read the complete rich-coconut-rice and coconut-jasmine-rice recipes. Both cook jasmine rice in canned coconut milk and water with salt, optional sugar and pandan, then rest covered. The former uses 1.5 cups rice to a 14-ounce can and one cup water; the latter uses two cups rice to a 13.5-ounce can and 1.5 cups water. These are richness/hydration differences within the same preparation, not distinct dishes requiring duplicate records.

Its Caribbean attribution is not established by the jasmine/pandan formula.
