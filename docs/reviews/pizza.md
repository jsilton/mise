These amounts are proposed to make the recipe executable, not certified household measurements. Each pie gets half of every topping. Neither proves this combined formula. The family topping identity and generous cheese are deliberate.

## Four-lens decisions

Practicality: default to a cool, lightly oiled metal pan, with an independent stone-and-peel option, rather than transferring a loaded pizza onto an ambiguously preheated pan. [Jim Lahey's Bon Appétit method](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/no-knead-pizza-dough) supports distinct equipment routes. The ingredient list specifies prepared dough; cold or frozen dough preparation is additional. Plan 60–70 minutes for two serial pan bakes, or about 100 minutes with the hour-long stone preheat. These are planning allowances, not measured service times.

Cultural context: remove unsupported claims about what Neapolitans would recognize. No authenticity promise is needed for this family topping combination.

Science: moisture control comes from low-moisture mozzarella and dried greens/pineapple; a browned rim does not prove the wet center is baked. A 500°F, 12–15-minute bake is a starting bracket informed by [Kenji's home-oven pizza](https://www.seriouseats.com/new-york-style-pizza), with bottom/center cues controlling the actual finish. Late spinach is included in that bracket. [Reynolds rates its parchment to 425°F](https://www.reynoldsbrands.com/tips-and-how-tos/tips-baking-parchment-paper); this draft avoids paper entirely rather than claiming all papers share that limit.

Flavor: preserve sweet pineapple, peppery meat, spinach and whole-milk mozzarella. Ready-to-eat pepperoni makes the topping state explicit. Remove the unconditional vegetarian tag; omission is a separate option, with other ingredients checked.
