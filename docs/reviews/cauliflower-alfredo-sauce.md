## Timing, capacity and scaling

About 10 minutes preparation plus 12–15 minutes simmering and blending; allow 25–35 minutes including heating the pot. Garlic can cook during the simmer. Cooling required by the actual machine and reheating add time; pasta is separate.

Check the actual model’s manual before blending hot food. Use only a vessel and lid expressly approved for hot ingredients, following that model’s fill, venting and speed instructions. Never blend hot food in a sealed personal cup. If the machine is not approved, cool the mixture as its manual requires before blending, then reheat gently; this adds time.

Use the complete dairy-free combination together; swapping only the milk leaves butter and Parmesan in the sauce.

Larger quantities need appropriate pot and blender capacity; cook to softness and use model-approved blending batches rather than increasing fill depth.

## Storage and relationships
