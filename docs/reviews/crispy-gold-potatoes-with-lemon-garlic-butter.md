# Crispy Gold Potatoes with Lemon-Garlic Butter: editorial review

2026-09-14. New variation requested for 3 lb petite gold potatoes alongside baked cod and green beans. Written review only; no physical kitchen test.

**Identity and evidence:** Builds on the existing Garlic Roasted Potatoes recipe and its attribution to [Ina Garten](https://barefootcontessa.com/recipes/garlic-roasted-potatoes), read directly for this review. The higher oven temperature, smaller specified pieces, separate garlic-butter finish, lemon and dill option are new editorial choices, not claims about Garten's method. Keep the base recipe intact and declare the variation relationship.

**Ingredient ledger and flavor:** All 3 lb potatoes roast in 1/4 cup oil with 1 tsp fine sea salt and 1/2 tsp pepper. Salt uses an explicit fine texture rather than a volume conversion from unspecified kosher salt. All 3 tbsp butter carry 1 tbsp garlic, 2 tsp zest and either 2 tbsp parsley or 1 tbsp dill. Optional juice begins at 1 tsp because the accompanying cod may already be lemony. Eight side servings are approximately 6 oz raw potatoes each; no nutrition or kitchen-tested claim is added.

**Method and practicality:** Two 13-by-18-inch metal pans provide room for the batch. Similar-size halves or quarters, dry surfaces, direct cut-face contact and rack rotation make the browning directions actionable. The 40–50-minute roast is an estimate for these pieces at 425°F, with knife tenderness and actual color controlling completion. Garlic cooks separately for 1–2 minutes over gentle heat and never spends the roast on the sheet pan. Zest and herbs enter off heat. Prep plus roasting gives 65 minutes; finishing work overlaps the roast.

**Meal coordination:** The existing cod recipe uses 400°F. An explicit sequential oven option finishes the potato roast first, holds the pans uncovered during the fish bake, then refreshes the potatoes briefly at 400°F and adds the butter. Green beans cook on the stovetop. This adds approximately 15–25 minutes; no assertion that three pans fit in one oven or that the entire dinner takes only 65 minutes. Existing composed meals are unchanged.

**Handling:** Directly checked [FoodSafety.gov cooling and refrigeration guidance](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety) and [leftover reheating temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures). Applied shallow refrigeration within two hours (one above 90°F), storage at or below 40°F, and reheating to 165°F. Oven reheating is an editorial texture choice; watch the garlic.

**Kitchen questions:** Confirm two-pan timing, cut-face release, seasoning, herb preference, garlic-butter distribution and crispness after the sequential cod bake. The written variation is ready to cook, not empirically validated.
