## Decisions

Plum tomatoes are salted and drained for 15 minutes so the salsa is not watery. White onion gets a cold water rinse. Serrano leads as the usual chile in Mexico, with jalapeño as the milder swap for kids. A final pinch of salt replaces what drains away.

## Kitchen-test questions

Measure the drained yield and confirm 2 tbsp lime juice is right for supermarket tomatoes.
