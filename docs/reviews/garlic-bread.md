## Individual recipe and meal review

Read the complete original. Defined bread at 400 g rather than a large baguette-or-loaf of unlimited size, and set six side portions/twelve pieces. Reduced butter from 113 to 85 g for this loaf, retained four garlic cloves and two tablespoons parsley, weighed Parmesan at 25 g and specified salt type with a lower-salt option. The ratio is editorial and needs tasting against actual bread density; it is not a universal loaf formula.

Selected one split-loaf method so surface area and baking time are understandable, replacing two different cutting methods with the same short cook. Added preheating, already-soft-butter assumption, an even spread, 12–15-minute center/edge check, and a watched optional broil with no parchment. Removed unsupported precise invention dates, the categorical claim that softer bread cannot work, and unverified nutrition. Origin now describes this butter/cheese version as Italian-American home cooking without claiming an established historical date.

Kept separate from fresh bread baking and plain warmed pita. Removed the unnecessary plain fresh-pasta pairing; tomato soup, the noodle-soup meal and a fresh salad remain contextual options. Chicken Noodle Soup Night uses half a batch shared six ways, because the soup already contains pasta. The full garlic-bread batch remains six larger side portions.

King Arthur's indexed primary recipe description supports warming the loaf before its final crisping; its foil-wrapped method is different from this split-loaf bake.
