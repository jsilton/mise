# Everyday Arugula Salad: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Retained 142 g leaves, 2 tbsp oil, and 1 tbsp lemon: roughly 35 g leaves and 11 ml dressing supply per side portion. Added a gradual dressing step rather than assuming all leaves need the full supply.
- Fixed unmeasured cheese and seasonings: 20 g cheese, 1/8 tsp fine salt, and 1/8 tsp pepper. Parmesan volume is explicitly loose shavings; weight is authoritative.
- Added washing/drying and a clear immediate-service sequence. Removed false “heat balance,” bitterness-from-dressing, and universal Italian-foraging claims.
- Vegetarian claim is now an option requiring appropriate rennet, rather than assuming traditional Parmesan is vegetarian. Removed unverified nutrition.

## Relationships and composition

One whole four-portion batch is the component for four Milanese cutlets. Its acidity and peppery leaves complement rich pasta and Marsala; no extra starch here. This is a simple dressed leaf salad, distinct from more elaborate composed arugula salads.
