## Formula, process, timing and scale

Use the stated 9-inch glass pie plate; the publisher specifies about 4 cups capacity. Have a food thermometer ready.

Keep egg yolks refrigerated until preparing the filling; room-temperature yolks are not needed for this method. Use a fine probe and check the filling rather than the hot plate or crust.

For multiple pies, divide original-size batches among separate matching plates. A half batch in the same plate is thinner and will cook sooner; begin checking earlier. Do not multiply baking time or pour several batches into one plate.

At least 8 hr 45 min plus crust and pie cooling: about 20 minutes preparation, 10 minutes crust baking, filling baking beginning at 15 minutes and at least 8 hours refrigeration. Extra time to reach 160°F is additional. Whip the cream just before serving.
