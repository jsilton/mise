# Korean-inspired Chicken Glass-Noodle Salad: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Original method referenced a never-made galbi marinade, omitted cooking oil, double-used sesame oil, and never incorporated optional cucumbers. Two measured sauces now account for every ingredient; finishing dressing never contacts raw poultry.
- Reduced dry noodles from 340 g to 280 g (70 g per main portion), soy from six to four tablespoons, and sesame seeds from 1/4 cup to one tablespoon. These editorial balance choices retain substantial chicken and pepper; tasting still needed.
- Ground chicken is the default; sliced thighs have a separate thickness, batching, and temperature instruction. Added 165°F verification and 35-minute allowance.
- Removed the false definition of galbi, nonexistent pear juice, enzyme claim, and phantom hours-long marinade. Title honestly identifies a Korean-inspired adaptation while preserving its URL.
- Replaced universal noodle boiling time with package guidance plus a texture endpoint. Removed unverified nutrition.

## Relationships and composition

The noodle salad already supplies starch, protein, and raw vegetables. Oi muchim is an optional component, now linked through usesBase, not a second full side serving. Only 1/2 cup of the four-portion cucumber batch is needed; its preparation is excluded from the 35-minute main recipe. Bean sprouts are an optional additional vegetable. Distinct from japchae by cold dressing, raw pepper, and preparation; no consolidation warranted without reviewing that neighbor.
