# jammy-and-hard-boiled-eggs: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Jammy and Hard-Boiled Eggs was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (pantry sauces, plain egg skills and cookout sides). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Boiling-water start for cold large eggs, with the heat turned down only after the eggs are in, then an ice bath. Times are 6 minutes runny, 7 jammy, 9 soft-set and 11 hard-boiled, which matches the 7-minute jammy eggs already used in the two ramen recipes. Role is base because avocado toast, the ramen bowls, the rice bowl station and the new potato salad all call for boiled eggs.

## Kitchen-test questions

Confirm the four times on the home stove with six eggs in a medium saucepan, and how much the water cools when the eggs go in.
