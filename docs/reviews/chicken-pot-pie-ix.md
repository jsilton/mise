# Chicken Pot Pie: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Added the onion, salt, and pepper that appeared only in the old method. Weighed vegetables and moved carrots into the initial sauté so raw dice are not sealed into a short bake.
- Clarified 450 g chicken as cooked, boneless yield. This formula intentionally uses already-cooked chicken; its preparation is separate, and the user is explicitly told not to substitute raw pieces.
- Rebalanced filling to 56 g butter and 45 g flour for 580 ml broth/milk, 450 g chicken, and 350 g vegetables. This is a thick gravy for pastry containment, with low-sodium broth and staged final salt. Eight slices are a pastry-rich meal portion rather than eight large chicken portions.
- Replaced the misleading Apple Pie/vodka shortcut with complete savory pastry ingredients and handling: 300 g flour, 225 g butter, measured salt, and 100–120 ml cold water according to dough cohesion. Cold-fat/hydration principles are supported by the King Arthur guide, not proof that this particular pie has been tested.
- Specified a deep metal pan and capacity, cool filling, bottom heat, vents, egg wash, two oven temperatures, a center temperature check, and a 20-minute rest. A standard shallow plate or ingredient-only scaling cannot be assumed equivalent.
- Corrected the 90-minute promise to about three hours including scratch pastry and overlapping filling/chilling. Removed the false alcohol/air-pocket explanation, inaccurate cream-based velouté label, and unverified nutrition.
- Replaced a second rich Caesar-style pairing with the individually reviewed arugula salad. Apple Pie itself remains pending review and is not certified by this change.
