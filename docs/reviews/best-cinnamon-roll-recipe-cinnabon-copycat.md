## Practicality and teaching

The new note describes soft pull-apart crumb, buttery cinnamon spirals and tangy icing. Tangzhong thickening pre-gelatinizes starch; https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2021/02/05/tangzhong-method-soft-pillowy-cinnamon-rolls supports moisture retention. The cooled paste must be just warm before it meets yeast; soft but cool filling butter is spreadable without being runny. Preserve the author's butter-rich style, not an inferred health reformulation.

The old 150-minute total cannot cover a 120-minute final rise plus 30-minute rest, kneading, shaping, baking and cooling. The 3–4-hour window and four-hour header are approximate planning allowances. Filling overlaps the dough rest; frosting overlaps the bake. A slow rise may take longer. The observable doubled-volume cue takes precedence. The 45-minute active estimate is not a stopwatch measurement.

Frosted-leftover cold handling follows FDA refrigerator guidance: https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/refrigerator-thermometers-cold-facts-about-food-safety . No old room-temperature multi-day cream-cheese-frosting claim is restored.
