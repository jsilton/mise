# pot-roast: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Sunday Pot Roast was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (the everyday beef dinners (steak, burgers, pot roast, meatballs and meat lasagna)). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

A 3 1/2 lb tied chuck roast is browned, then braised at 300°F with onions, tomato paste, red wine and beef broth for 2 hours before carrots and Yukon Golds go in for the last hour. The gravy is skimmed, reduced and finished with red wine vinegar. After review the vegetables get their own teaspoon of salt, the pot is named as 6 to 7 quarts and the storage note freezes it without the potatoes. A slow cooker method is a swap.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check tenderness at 3 to 3 1/4 hours, the gravy thickness from 2 tbsp flour and the salt in the vegetables.
