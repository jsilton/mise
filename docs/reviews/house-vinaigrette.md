# house-vinaigrette: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

House Vinaigrette was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (pantry sauces, plain egg skills and cookout sides). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Red wine vinegar, shallot, Dijon and olive oil at a little under 3 to 1 oil to vinegar, with 3/4 tsp kosher salt per cup so it seasons greens rather than tasting right off a spoon. The shallot soaks in the vinegar for 10 minutes. Honey is 1 tsp and maple syrup is the vegan swap.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check the salt level on plain romaine and on arugula.
