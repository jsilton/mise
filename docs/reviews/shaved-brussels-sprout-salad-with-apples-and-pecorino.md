Wash and dry sprouts and apple. Remove damaged outer leaves and tough bases, then shave thinly using a guarded mandoline or a sharp knife. Stop before fingers approach the blade.

Keep the total dressing amount intact: set aside a small share for the apple, then use the remainder for sprouts. Add the apple and its reserved dressing back together.

## Process, timing, scaling and cold handling

About15 minutes preparation and15 minutes dressed resting give30 minutes elapsed. Extra refrigerator chilling is optional and adds time; no final chilling step is required after a room-temperature rest. Thin shaving and apple cutting are the active work.

Keep at 40°F / 4°C or below and refrigerate within 2 hours total out of refrigeration, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C. Best served on the preparation day; refrigerate leftovers covered and use by the next day for texture. The apple and raw sprouts soften after dressing. Do not use vinegar or the appearance of the apple as a safety test.
