## Cooking and safety decision

Replace the unexplained 4–5-minute instruction and mandatory-lid/aroma claim with roughly ten minutes covered heating/cooking, bean-head tenderness and a measured endpoint. Time is an estimate, not a kill step. Open the lid as necessary to prevent the small volume boiling dry. The 20-minute total includes handling and draining; chilling is additional.

FDA advises thorough cooking and avoidance of raw/lightly cooked sprouts by vulnerable groups:
https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/selecting-and-serving-produce-safely

UC ANR Publication 8151, page 1, explicitly supports heating sprouts to steaming hot or above 165°F / 74°C. It states this treatment is most applicable to mung bean sprouts; applying its general cooked-sprout endpoint to soybean sprouts is conservative editorial guidance, not validation of this pot/timing combination:
https://ucanr.edu/sites/default/files/2024-02/393187.pdf

FoodSafety.gov supports prompt shallow-container refrigeration and time limits. A two-day quality target is editorial, not a species-specific shelf-life claim:
https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety
