# crispy-parmesan-roasted-broccoli: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

Specified 680 g broccoli for four side portions on a 13 × 18-inch tray, with 3 tbsp oil and 30 g Parmesan. Split the oil so minced garlic and cheese go in near the end instead of scorching during the full roast. Added dry-floret preparation, pan spacing, a knife-tender stem endpoint, and lemon added to taste. The 35-minute estimate includes preheating and preparation. Doubled batches require additional tray space. Removed unverifiable nutrition.

## Relationships and composition

This is a cheese-finished roasted side, distinct from steamed broccoli, broccoli slaw, and broccoli cheddar casseroles. Its crisp edges and lemon balance richer mains; avoid pairing it with several other oven dishes unless the meal plan accounts for tray capacity and temperature.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check floret size, tray load, garlic browning, cheese adhesion, and 35-minute workflow on convection and conventional ovens. The nut finish is an explained alternative, not a tested equivalent cheese crust.
