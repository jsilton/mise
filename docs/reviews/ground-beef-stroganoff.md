# Ground Beef Stroganoff: individual recipe review

Date: 2026-11-26. Editorial review only; no kitchen test recorded.

Source: Craig Plummer's handwritten notebook page. Family recipe, method and amounts kept as written, improvements in the Suggested Tweak.

From the card: 1 1/2 lb ground beef, salt and pepper, 1/2 onion, 1 garlic clove, 2 tbsp flour, 3 1/2 oz mushrooms, 1 tbsp dry mustard, 1 can golden mushroom soup (or 6 oz tomato paste), 1 tbsp lemon juice, 8 oz sour cream added at the end without boiling, served over noodles. The card says only "after simmer" for the sour cream. "Wine?" is written on the card and is offered only as a tweak.

Additions needed to cook: 1 cup water to loosen the condensed soup, a 30-minute covered simmer time, 12 oz wide egg noodles as the amount, the browning time, taking the pan off the heat and not boiling once the sour cream is in, slicing the mushrooms, and the noodle time. The card gives the mushrooms as about 3 1/2 oz. An arrow beside the lemon juice may mean it goes in earlier, and it is left with the simmer.
