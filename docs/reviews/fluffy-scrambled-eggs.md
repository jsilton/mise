# fluffy-scrambled-eggs: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

Retitled the dish Soft Scrambled Eggs to reflect the small moist curds taught by the method. Six large eggs and 28 g butter provide two substantial servings. Eggs are whisked first; cooking is controlled by gentle heat and stirring. Corrected the claim that adding cream stops cooking: it cools and enriches, but residual heat still matters. Added a 160°F endpoint for the egg dish and removed raw pools as an acceptable finished cue. Ten minutes assumes ingredients are ready. Removed unverifiable nutrition.

## Relationships and composition

Keep distinct from large-curd diner eggs, omelets, and steamed egg custards. Pair with toast and a fresh or cooked vegetable according to the meal; toast is not implied by the ingredient yield. Serving multiple guests requires attention to holding and continued setting, not just multiplied eggs.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check the temperature measurement in a shallow pan, curd moisture at the stated endpoint, stirring effort, and how quickly the dish must be plated.
