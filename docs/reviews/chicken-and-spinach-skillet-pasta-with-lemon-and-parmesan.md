# Chicken & Spinach Pasta with Lemon: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

The original 454 g pasta for four servings was generous relative to 450 g chicken; set 340 g dried pasta for four substantial balanced plates. Replaced an unreliable ten-cup/large-bag spinach measure with 280 g, and specified 30 g Parmesan. Removed unquantified extra garnish and separated chicken salt from pasta-water salt. The lemon is measured by zest and incremental juice, not automatically the full variable-size fruit.

Removed a nonexistent roasting method and unsupported historical claims about Italian cooking. Added poultry temperature, spinach-in-batches instructions, and off-heat cheese incorporation. Deleted the broken generated instruction to rest 'lb Chicken' for ten minutes after the entire pasta was finished. Forty minutes allows preparation, water heating, and realistic chicken cooking. Legacy nutrition could not possibly represent 1 lb dried pasta and was removed.

## Relationships and composition

This is a complete pasta main with protein and greens. A small salad is optional; garlic bread and a rich Caesar are not required complements. Keep distinct from Alfredo by the lemon/wine/spinach sauce and from a standalone chicken cutlet by the integrated small-piece method.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check pan volume for 280 g spinach, starch concentration by pasta brand, sauce cling with 30 g cheese, and the timing overlap for one cook. Test gluten-free pasta separately before claiming identical performance.
