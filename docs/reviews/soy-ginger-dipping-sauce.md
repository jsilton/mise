## Process, timing, scaling and cold handling

About 5 minutes hands-on mixing and 10 minutes resting give 15 minutes elapsed. Make it during dumpling preparation with clean utensils. Larger batches take longer to grate and mince; rest time does not multiply.

Keep at 40°F / 4°C or below and refrigerate within 2 hours total out of refrigeration, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C. Preserve the existing 3–4-day limit for unused sauce. Keep dipped servings separate and discard those leftovers; fresh aromatics, oil and vinegar do not establish shelf stability.
