# Coq au Vin with Chicken Thighs: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Verified the original uses bone-in skin-on thighs, not boneless chicken. Retained 900 g for four main portions with sides; standardized mushrooms at 225 g and carrots at approximately 250 g.
- Added missing salt, pepper, parsley quantity/use, optional supplemental oil, fat management, pan size, and a 165°F minimum plus separate tenderness cue.
- Mushrooms brown separately and return near the end, fixing a crowded all-vegetable sauté that claimed browning without allowing moisture evaporation.
- Retained wine/stock at 480/240 ml but provided reduction and liquid-height cues. Wine reduction allowance increased; the original 2 1/2-hour overall allowance is credible with batches and braising.
- Added gentle-simmer control, softened-skin expectation, excess-fat skimming, sauce reduction before off-heat butter, and specific rescue steps.
- Removed “Authentic French Standard,” magical butter claims, unverified nutrition, and any implication that cooking removes all alcohol.

## Relationships and composition

Choose one starch and a green vegetable rather than three separate potato/bread pairings. This is distinct from Marsala by bone-in braising, red wine, bacon, vegetables, and long connective-tissue cooking. The original record is retained, with a clearer title.

## Kitchen-test questions

Test one-layer fit and liquid height in the stated Dutch oven, large versus small thigh timing, tenderness at/after 165°F, mushroom texture, final sauce volume, and salt from bacon. Verify the 2 1/2-hour schedule.
