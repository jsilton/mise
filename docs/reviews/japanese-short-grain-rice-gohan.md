Date: 2026-09-06. Status: consolidated after considered review; no physical kitchen test.

Compared all ingredients and directions against Steamed White Rice (Short Grain). One uses 2.5 cups water per two cups rice; the other 2.25 cups. A small hydration difference and a ten-minute versus optional longer soak are equipment/texture adjustments, not distinct dishes.
