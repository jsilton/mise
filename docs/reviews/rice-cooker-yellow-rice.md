## Decisions

- Rename the title to Spanish Rice at the existing `rice-cooker-yellow-rice` URL so bookmarks remain valid. Describe it as a home adaptation of Spanish-style saffron yellow rice, without claiming an authentic regional recipe or equating it with Mexican tomato rice.
- Replace powdered onion and garlic with sautéed onion, red bell pepper and fresh garlic. Keep butter and saffron; supply a turmeric alternative with its flavor difference. Add no sugar. Include optional measured MSG for savory depth and an initial measured fine-salt amount for low-sodium stock, with a fully salted-stock exception and a final tasting instruction.
- Specify 1 US cup dry regular long-grain white rice and 1¼ US cups stock (296 ml, approximately 300 ml). Rinse and drain; measure stock separately; draw the saffron steep from that total. Do not combine the US cup with the pot's standard WHITE fill line. Explain the alternative 180 ml supplied-cup batch as 225 ml stock and approximately three-quarters of the remaining ingredients.
- Scope the method to a computerized Zojirushi, with MIXED as the general setting and model-specific seasoned-rice instructions taking precedence. Do not imply the ratio covers basic single-switch cookers, stovetop cooking or all rice varieties. No separate soak or additional covered rest: the computerized cycle includes these stages.
- Cook off free vegetable moisture before loading, dissolve seasoning in the liquid, put sautéed vegetables on top, and fluff at the completion signal. Retain an incremental next-batch hydration adjustment because rice, vegetables and model vary.
- Keep measured quantities in the structured formula so ingredient and step scaling remain synchronized. Keep the review label editorial-review and do not publish an unsupported successful-test claim.
