## Independent primary technique and safety evidence

- [Namiko Hirasawa Chen, Miso Salmon](https://www.justonecookbook.com/miso-salmon/) supports comparable fillet thickness, checking bones, miso scorching, foil rather than parchment and adjusting broiler distance. Her formula removes marinade and uses a lower fish endpoint. Her time/endpoint is **not** copied as proof for either proposed retained-glaze formula at 145°F.
- [Namiko Hirasawa Chen, stovetop Japanese rice](https://www.justonecookbook.com/how-to-cook-rice/) distinguishes kitchen and rice-cooker cups and gives 300 g rice/400 ml water with soak, covered cook and rest. Sticky uses 400 g/535 ml as an editorial proportional starting quantity and a larger pot; it deliberately extends the original no-soak schedule, without claiming that the archived method cannot work.
- [Mahatma Jasmine White Rice](https://mahatmarice.com/products/jasmine-white-rice/) gives 45 g per quarter cup, 1.5 volumes water per volume rice and about fifteen minutes covered cooking. These support the two chosen jasmine amounts. A ten-minute rest and rinsing/draining procedure are practical editorial choices. Package/appliance variation is disclosed. The page's marketing or inaccurate equivalence of jasmine with glutinous dessert rice is not adopted.
- [FDA seafood guidance](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/selecting-and-serving-fresh-and-frozen-seafood-safely) supplies the 145°F fish endpoint and prompt handling limits. [FDA outdoor food handling](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/handling-food-safely-while-eating-outdoors) explicitly covers reserving clean marinade, refrigeration and bringing reused raw-contact marinade to a boil (including the lower Four Steps section). Honey's exact one-minute boil is an editorial operating instruction, not a source-validated universal minimum duration.
- [FoodSafety.gov temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) supplies leftover reheating to 165°F. [FoodSafety.gov four steps](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety) supports separation and prompt shallow cooling, but does **not** directly support a marinade-reuse-boiling claim. That citation mismatch was corrected.
- [USDA FSIS leftover storage answer](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/How-long-can-you-keep-leftovers-in-the-refrigerator) explicitly states three-to-four days in its indexed primary result. Direct opening timed out; the [full FSIS leftovers page](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety) was similarly confirmed by primary search results but returned 403 on opening. Disclose this access limit. No claim that the FoodSafety.gov finfish raw-storage row itself establishes a cooked-salmon duration is made.
