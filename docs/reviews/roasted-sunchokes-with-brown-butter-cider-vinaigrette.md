## Timing and storage

Allow about 70 minutes as a prep-and-roast subtotal: roughly 10 minutes to prepare and start the roast, then about an hour in the oven. Load only once the oven reaches 375°F; any heating still needed after prep extends elapsed time. Sauce and finishing work overlap the roast, giving about 20 minutes active work. Larger reductions and extra sheets can take longer; half-volume vinegar and tender centers are the endpoints. No separate rest is required.

Refrigerate promptly in shallow covered containers within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below; use within 3–4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F. Serve while warm for the fresh spinach/chive contrast. On reheating, the leaves soften further and the vinaigrette may separate; toss again to coat the softened pieces.
