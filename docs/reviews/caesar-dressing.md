# caesar-dressing: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Caesar Dressing was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (pantry sauces, plain egg skills and cookout sides). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Hand-whisked emulsion of anchovy, garlic, egg yolk, lemon, Dijon and Worcestershire with a 2 to 1 blend of neutral and olive oil so it does not turn bitter, finished with Parmigiano Reggiano. The yolk is a pasteurized one, named in the ingredient line, because the dressing is not cooked and the family has three kids. 2 tbsp mayonnaise is the swap. Storage is 2 days.

## Kitchen-test questions

Confirm 1/4 tsp kosher salt is enough with four anchovies, and the yield.
