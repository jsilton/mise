# matzo-ball-soup: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Matzo Ball Soup was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (the Jewish holiday table). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

The matzo balls use 4 eggs, 1/4 cup schmaltz, 1/4 cup seltzer and 1 cup matzo meal, chilled 30 minutes and simmered covered for 35 minutes in salted water so the soup stays clear. The soup is the site's homemade chicken stock (usesBase) with carrots, celery and dill. Oil, store-bought broth and added chicken are swaps. The review asked for no changes.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check that the batter makes 12 walnut-size balls that cook through in 35 minutes, and the salt for 10 cups of unsalted stock.
