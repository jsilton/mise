# Whipped Feta with Honey and Pistachios: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

Read the full original and its suggested pairings. No duplicate whipped-feta source was found. This remains a cream-cheese/feta spread with honey and crunch, distinct from hot baked feta in the chicken recipe. Removed automatic pairings with feta-heavy village salad and unrelated charred guacamole. Plain pita and zucchini are useful serving partners. Removed its suggestion from the already ricotta/yogurt-rich pending Herby Chicken Meatball Bowl; that relationship correction does not count as reviewing the bowl.

Defined 225 g drained feta and 115 g cream cheese, two tablespoons blending oil plus one teaspoon finishing oil, staged two-to-three teaspoons lemon, 2 g garlic, one tablespoon honey, measured paprika/cayenne/dill and 20 g optional toasted pistachios. Removed unmeasured oil drizzle, automatic flaky salt and arbitrary nationality-based creaminess claims. The original quarter-cup blending oil plus unmeasured finishing oil was reduced; this adjusted formula still needs tasting. Water is a bounded by-teaspoon correction. Accept slight fine grain and stop processing if the mixture warms/loosens rather than claiming all graininess proves insufficient machine time.

Forty minutes includes the original thirty-minute cream-cheese softening that had been omitted from total time. Estimated 1 1/2–1 3/4 cups means eight appetizer portions, not eight whole meals; optional bread/vegetable serving quantities are explicitly additional. Suitable cheese rennet governs the vegetarian option; nuts can be omitted. Yogurt substitution is tangier/softer, not an identical formula. Refrigerated storage and topping timing replace the unqualified claim that advance holding always improves garlic and texture. Legacy nutrition removed.

Direct reference: https://www.themediterraneandish.com/creamy-whipped-feta-dip/ supports feta moisture, processing, thickening when chilled and optional toppings, but uses yogurt and a different formula. The original cream-cheese/honey identity is retained. FoodSafety.gov handling guidance supports the cold-storage/service limits.

Kitchen questions: cream-cheese brands, feta moisture/salt, processor minimum volume, grain and oil separation, garlic strength, honey quantity, final measured yield and chilled spreadability. No physical test recorded.
