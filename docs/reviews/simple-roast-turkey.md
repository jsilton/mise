# Dry-Brined Roast Turkey with Cider: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

Read the entire original, the separately reviewed Good Eats-inspired wet-brined whole turkey and bone-in turkey breast, and Thanksgiving Practice. This 10–12 lb dry-brined bird with cider/wine remains meaningfully distinct from a 14–16 lb aromatic liquid-brined bird. It is retained under its original URL with a descriptive title, linked alternatives and a fixed-bird scaling explanation. No extra canonical recipe or consolidation is counted.

The original had undefined butter, wine and herb quantities, a bottle without volume, salt specified by an unspecified coarse-kosher teaspoon, unlisted onion, inconsistent lemon use, one-to-three-day brining plus twelve-hour drying, and a total unrelated to a clear schedule. Defined 25 g weighed salt for a fully thawed unenhanced 10–12 lb bird, about 0.46–0.56% of gross bird weight, not edible meat. This is an editorial salt amount, not an exact conversion of USDA's tablespoon rule or a claimed optimum. Pepper is two teaspoons, butter 45 g, dry hard cider 330 ml, wine 120 ml, onion 200 g, garlic twelve cloves and herbs six thyme/two small rosemary/six sage. One lemon supplies zest for the rub and wedges for the pan. Water starts at 240 ml with up to 480 ml as needed, explicitly allocated.

Twenty-four hours uncovered refrigerated salting combines seasoning and surface drying. The raw-storage window does not restart. Removed the one-hour room-temperature instruction, raw-poultry washing and bag/air-dry sequencing contradiction. A fully refrigerator-thawed bird is required; about three thawing days are additional, and a cold-water-thawed bird is not directed into another overnight hold. Weighed salt avoids crystal-brand volume ambiguity; enhanced/kosher/pre-brined birds need another plan.

Changed the 450°F blast/350°F finish and guessed 1.5–2 hours to a steady 325°F with chart-based 2 3/4–3 1/4-hour planning, early checking and explicit possible extension at the upper size boundary. FoodSafety.gov lists 8–12 lb unstuffed birds at 2 3/4–3 hours and 12–14 lb at 3–3 3/4; our planning range is not a guarantee for every 12 lb bird. The final endpoint requires both breasts, inner thighs and wing areas, clear of bone, all at least 165°F / 74°C. Thirty-minute rest follows completed cooking. Twenty-nine hours conservatively includes twenty-four hours salting, forty-five preparation, about three-and-a-quarter roasting and half-hour resting.

The cavity is empty, with aromatics and liquid below the rack. This avoids packed-cavity timing ambiguity and makes their use explicit. Cider sugars require pan-liquid checks; neither the cider nor repeated basting is said to infuse all meat or guarantee juiciness. Removed 'seasoning to the bone,' mandatory magical rest language and unsupported nutrition. Ten portions with sides replace an unexplained twelve; the yield still needs a real carving/portion test. Gravy is a separate preparation, not a complete sauce created by merely mentioning drippings. The stock substitution preserves 450 ml pan-liquid volume without implying roasting eliminates all beverage alcohol.

Updated Thanksgiving Practice only where it directly contradicted this bird's day-before preparation, cooking schedule and empty-cavity method. Its stuffing/potato/cranberry recipes, gravy and full service plan remain pending. This correction does not count as a meal review.

Sources accessed: https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/meat-poultry-charts (direct full chart); https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/poultry/poultry-basting-brining-and-marinating (indexed dry-brining, raw storage and salt/protein text; direct request 403); https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/poultry/happy-thanksgiving (indexed serving and shallow-container storage guidance). The herb/cider formula originates in this repository; no external author's exact recipe or physical testing is asserted.

Kitchen questions: salt distribution under skin, 25 g salinity across the bird-size range, actual fridge drying, crispness at steady 325°F, roast duration and breast/leg endpoint spread, degree of cider concentration and lemon bitterness, drippings suitability, and ten-person edible yield. This substantial method revision must be cooked and documented before stronger reliability claims.
