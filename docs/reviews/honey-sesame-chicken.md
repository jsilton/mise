# Honey Sesame Chicken: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and formula review

The complete original used 2 tbsp unlisted frying oil, allocated 1 tbsp of its 3 tbsp soy to marinade then ambiguously added soy again, and instructed wiping away the starch coating. Corrected oil and soy accounting explicitly: 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil across two batches/aromatics, 1 tbsp marinade soy and 2 tbsp sauce soy. Kept 680 g thighs, 1 tbsp coating starch, 1 tbsp vinegar, garlic, ginger, seeds and three scallions. Reduced honey from 4 to 3 tbsp and toasted sesame oil from 1 tbsp to 2 tsp; added 60 ml water to create a controllable glaze rather than an immediately concentrated syrup. These flavor adjustments require tasting, not a claim of established superiority.

Specified boneless skinless thigh and 1-inch pieces, two batches and 165°F before glazing. The old few-minute single-layer instruction did not establish safe cooking for 680 g unspecified chunks. New 40-minute total includes two 6–8-minute batches. Aromatics see lower heat and sesame oil finishes off heat. Added coating and sauce science, reduction/thickness cues and recoverable versus burned-glaze troubleshooting. Removed unsupported healthier/takeout-superiority claims, promises of lasting crunch, and incorrect China origin. Deleted unverified nutrition.

## Relationships and tests

Fixed the link labeled Jasmine Rice that actually led to rice pilaf. The reviewed plain short-grain white rice is now accurately named; the main includes neither rice nor broccoli quantities. Honey Sesame Chicken Night still requires its broccoli component review and actual rice-soak/rest schedule. This pan-seared honey glaze differs from the more aromatic ground-chicken basil stir-fry and from the site's other thickened soy/chicken preparations.

ACS supports browning principles and Exploratorium supports starch thickening, not this exact formula. Test honey/salt/acid balance, sauce thickness after chicken juices, starch adhesion, oil sufficiency, batch size, four portions and 40-minute timing. No physical kitchen test recorded.
