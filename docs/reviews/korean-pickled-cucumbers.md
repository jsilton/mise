# Oi Muchim (Korean Cucumber Salad): individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Findings and corrections

- Replaced variable cucumber counts with 600 g, specified salt brand/fine alternative, and reduced soy from 1 tbsp to 2 tsp because salting already seasons the cucumbers.
- Corrected grill metadata to no-cook/toss. The dish is a fresh seasoned cucumber salad, not fermented or shelf-stable pickles.
- Changed paper-thin 1/8-inch slices plus forceful squeezing to 1/4-inch slices, 20-minute draining, and gentle blotting. Rinse only if a tasted slice is too salty. No guarantee that water release stops permanently.
- Reconciled 30-minute total: 10 minutes prep plus 20 minutes salting, dressing made during the wait; removed additional unexplained 10-minute marinade.
- Removed unverified nutrition; clarified vegan ingredients and gluten-free tamari option. Reduced generic leftover optimism to fresh-service preference.

Maangchi’s primary oi-muchim page was checked for dish identity and fresh-service context. Her formula differs and does not include this salting stage; it is not claimed as the source of these revised quantities or as a kitchen test.

## Relationships and composition

Keeps its original URL because multiple recipes link to it. It is an optional measured component of Korean-inspired chicken glass-noodle salad and a side for bibimbap/kalbi. Distinct from smashed cucumber salad: sliced rather than smashed, gochugaru-led seasoning rather than black-vinegar/garlic dressing. Compared and retained, not a redundant duplicate.
