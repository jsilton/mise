## Full comparison and corrections

Kept this record separate from Slow-Cooker Chicken Noodle Soup after reading both. This is a stovetop diced-chicken soup with small in-broth orzo, sautéed vegetables and flour; the other is a long-cooked unthickened broth with shredded thigh and separately boiled fusilli. Shared lemon/parsley alone does not make them duplicates.

Kept 680 g chicken, defined breast and 3/4-inch pieces, weighed orzo at 180 g, and standardized onion/carrot/celery at 100/150/100 g. Replaced vague salt/pepper with measured staged salt and pepper, substituted a specific half-teaspoon thyme for the tiny ambiguous Italian blend, added 1 tsp zest, and increased lemon juice supply from 30 to 45 ml with staged tasting. Exact lemon preference and salt depend on ingredients and require cooking.

Corrected golden-brown-and-soft carrot promises in 5–7 minutes to a 7–9-minute softening stage with defined small cuts. Retained the existing 165°F check but made it separate from noodle tenderness and added the practical earlier-finished-chicken response. Consistency is adjusted before acid, and make-ahead noodles can be cooked/stored separately. The 45-minute schedule accounts for heating and preparation. Removed an extra couscous pairing and unverified nutrition; Greek salad remains a contrasting optional side.
