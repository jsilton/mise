# Honey-Lime Chicken Thighs for Tacos: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

Read the full original, linked pickled onions and cilantro-lime rice, and Honey Chipotle Lime Chicken Bowls. The latter uses chipotle/mustard breast meat and a grain-bowl format, so is not a duplicate of these honey/soy/fish-sauce thighs. It remains pending. This recipe is explicitly a Mexican-inspired home taco with a mixed seasoning approach, not equivalent to slow-cooked pork carnitas or a claim that fish sauce disappears for every eater.

Kept 1360 g thighs as eight two-taco portions. Defined sixteen small tortillas, 300 g avocado, one cup drained pickled onions, measured cilantro and optional sesame. Rice is a replacement starch for another bowl, not another required taco ingredient. The original unmeasured serving list and default pairings with another taco main were corrected. The individually reviewed onion dependency is declared through usesBase. Full onion preparation is included in the eighty-minute plan; with onions ready allow about sixty-five minutes.

Glaze retains three tablespoons each honey/reduced-sodium soy and one fish sauce, but splits the four tablespoons lime between glaze and fresh finish. Garlic, oil, cumin, paprika and pepper are listed; automatic extra salt is omitted. Half the glaze is kept clean before raw contact, and clean brushing is specified. Two rimmed broiler-safe trays account for three pounds of meat. Removed parchment under the broiler, verified against Reynolds' direct product instructions. Every thigh reaches measured 165°F / 74°C before the optional brief color finish; no pull-at-160°F carryover promise. Two trays broil separately, with a hot-oven alternative and a five-minute rest before slicing. Burned honey is not praised as an essential black crust. Legacy nutrition removed.
