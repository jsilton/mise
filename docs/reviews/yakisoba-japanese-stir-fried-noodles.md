# Chicken Yakisoba: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

Read the full original and compared both Chinese noodle entries. Kept yakisoba distinct by wheat noodle product, Worcestershire-led sauce, vegetables and Japanese garnishes. Removed the incorrect interchangeable buckwheat-soba/fresh-ramen/dried-yakisoba line and unsupported gluten-free badge. Set 600 g pre-steamed noodles, 300 g chicken and 600 g weighed vegetables plus scallions for four; original vague protein and two-cup vegetable choices prevented reliable yield and timing.

Sauce now uses two tablespoons each Worcestershire/oyster/ketchup plus one each soy/mirin. This is Mise's adjustment to the original oyster-heavy blend, not a copy of Nami's sauce formula. Use only homemade or bottled sauce, never both with package sachets. Measured optional toppings replace unlimited mayonnaise/bonito/ginger. Vegetarian substitution changes animal sauces and bonito as well as protein. No promise of gluten-free wheat yakisoba remains.

Two whole finishing rounds make the 45-minute schedule credible for a 12-inch pan. Oil is six teaspoons total, one for chicken and two for vegetables in each round. Chicken reaches 165°F before final mixing. Removed smoke-the-oil instruction, high-heat-is-nonnegotiable claim, under-ten-minutes ultimatum and smell-as-proof-of-sauce-balance. Nami's primary page supports pre-steamed wheat noodle identity and dish structure; this chicken quantity, sauce and two-batch timing need separate testing. Federal handling applies independently.

Removed extra plain rice pairing because noodles already supply starch; cucumber remains a contrasting side. Test noodle packet moisture/loosening, 600 g/four plate yield, sauce brands, cabbage cut and two-round timing/holding. No physical kitchen test recorded.
