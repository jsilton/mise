## Primary methods consulted

- [Swasthi Shreekanth — Saffron Rice](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/saffron-rice/): directly read the complete recipe and relevant ingredient/method discussion. It uses rinsed unsoaked basmati, distinguishes aged and newer grain water needs, includes a covered cook and ten-minute rest, and cautions that extra spices can obscure saffron. The draft retains its own whole spices, onion, milk and original liquid; it is not attributed to Swasthi or represented as her exact formula.
- [Dassana Amit — Saffron Rice](https://www.vegrecipesofindia.com/saffron-rice-or-kesar-rice/): directly read in full. Her Indian-style whole-spice/ghee rice uses a 20–30-minute soak and a different water allowance. This establishes a relevant culinary form and contrasts with the unsoaked method; it does not authorize combining her soaked ratio with another recipe's timing. Her suggested turmeric option gives different flavor and is not adopted as an equivalent saffron replacement. Unrelated warming-health and pressure-cooker claims are not used.
- [Sohla El-Waylly — How to Use Saffron](https://www.seriouseats.com/how-to-use-saffron): directly read the full article. Warm-water or milk infusion and direct addition to wet cooking are alternatives. A fifteen-minute steep is useful here, not proof that milk is essential or that every cold extraction fails. The public recipe uses the warm milk already present and includes all of it in the liquid total.
- [Burlap & Barrel — Smoked Black Cardamom](https://www.burlapandbarrel.com/products/smoked-black-cardamom): directly read the ingredient/sourcing/flavor sections. This is a primary supplier description of a stronger smoky/resinous spice distinct from green cardamom. The product on the current page is ground; it is **not** a quantity guide for whole pods. Reducing the optional three black pods to one is a disclosed editorial flavor decision. No brand is recommended in ingredients.
- [Dassana Amit — Cucumber Raita](https://www.vegrecipesofindia.com/cucumber-raita-recipe/): directly read. Supports cumin/yogurt/cucumber/herbs, grated versus chopped options and short quality storage. Our existing reviewed page chooses gently drained grated cucumber and measured unstrained yogurt, rather than copying her current chopped formula or health claims.
- [USDA FSIS — Leftovers and Food Safety](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety): current direct fetch returned 403; indexed primary content was read in the preceding same-session recipe review and supports shallow chilling, two-hour/one-hot-hour limits, 40°F storage, 3–4 days and 165°F reheating. Do not describe the 403 as proof that guidance is absent.

These primary sources support techniques and handling, not the exact composed-meal fractions, 110-minute clock, grain water uptake or personal flavor approval. Those are explicit editorial judgments in the two individual records. No physical test is claimed.
