# Cream of Potato and Bacon Soup: individual recipe review

Date: 2026-11-26. Editorial review only; no kitchen test recorded.

Source: Craig Plummer's handwritten notebook page. Family recipe, method and amounts kept as written, improvements in the Suggested Tweak.

From the card: 5 to 6 bacon strips fried, onion and celery cooked in the fat (the card crosses out "potatoes" and writes "onions & celery" here), 1 to 2 carrots, 2 large white potatoes, 3 cups water, 2 chicken bouillon cubes, white pepper and Mrs. Dash, simmered at least 30 minutes, 1 medium onion or 1/2 large, 1 cup cream added after at least half an hour. The title follows the card. Mrs. Dash is listed generically as salt-free herb seasoning.

Additions needed to cook: crumbling the bacon on top (the card does not say where it goes), potato dice size, cooking times, heavy cream as the kind of cream, covering the pot, adding the carrots with the potatoes, and not boiling once the cream is in.
