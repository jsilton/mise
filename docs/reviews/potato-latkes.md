# potato-latkes: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Classic Potato Latkes was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (the Jewish holiday table). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Russets and onion are grated together, wrung dry, and bound with the potato starch left in the bowl, eggs and matzo meal. They fry in 1/4 inch of oil at 350°F and hold on a rack in a 250°F oven. After review the oil went to 3 cups so there is enough to top up, the oven is heated in step 4, batches are fixed at 5 and the total went to 1 hour. Flour and a food processor are swaps.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check the yield (about 18), the salt level and how much of the 3 cups of oil four batches use.
