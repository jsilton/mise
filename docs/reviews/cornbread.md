# Buttermilk Cornbread: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Individual formula and pan review

Read the complete cornbread and both chili meal plans, plus the existing barbecue references. The original omitted cooling from total time and treated 8-inch and 9-inch pans as interchangeable while giving one bake window. It combined one tablespoon baking powder and half a teaspoon soda with 240 ml buttermilk and two cups unspecified dry weights; its 113 g butter and two sweeteners also made the categorical low-sugar description unhelpful.

Define 140 g fine cornmeal and 120 g AP flour, two teaspoons powder and one-quarter teaspoon soda, explicit salt type, 85 g butter, 25 g brown sugar, 21 g honey, two eggs and 240 ml cultured buttermilk. Add the missing pan grease separately. This is a specific lightly sweet, flour-containing American version, not a universal Southern standard. The reduced butter/sweetener and extra egg aim for a tender but sliceable crumb; actual baking must confirm the balance. Do not claim exact acid neutralization across all buttermilk brands.

Selected one 8-inch metal pan; revised mixing order keeps hot butter away from directly cooking egg, calls for even dry leaveners and brief final folding, and bakes promptly. Twenty-to-twenty-five-minute baking is governed by center set/tester cues, followed by ten minutes cooling; total is fifty minutes. Buttermilk variation now specifies regular yogurt/milk rather than assuming Greek yogurt thickness is universal. Nine normal portions or twelve smaller chili accompaniments have explicit cutting instructions; surplus is planned, not silently served as a second starch portion. Removed unverified nutrition.
