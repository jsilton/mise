# Quick Refrigerator-Pickled Red Onions: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial review only; no physical kitchen test recorded.

Read the complete original and all ingredients used by the honey-lime taco preparation. No second standalone onion-pickle recipe was found among the linked preparations. Replaced the unspecified large onion with 200 g peeled onion, the variable kosher-salt volume with 6 g fine/pickling salt, and unspecified vinegar strength with labeled 5% acidity. Retained the original equal 120 ml vinegar/water and 12 g sugar, defining a sharp condiment rather than an entire side. Salt and sweetness are editorial flavor choices; the UC/OSU references use the same equal-vinegar/water framework with differing seasoning amounts. The full source is a refrigerator preparation, not a shelf-stable canning formula.

The original ten-minute total omitted the stated thirty-to-sixty-minute pickle rest. Forty-five minutes includes slicing, brine heating and a first refrigerated taste, while fuller flavor takes a day. Heatproof pint capacity, headspace, submersion, thin slices, undiluted top-up guidance and prompt refrigeration are explicit. Yield is approximately 1 1/2 cups drained onion, not the jar volume. A full batch supplies the taco recipe's one cup with extra, which keeps its own preparation date. Pink color is not a guarantee that thick onion has lost its raw bite.

Directly read UC Master Food Preservers' Quick Refrigerator Pickles (July 2025), including its onion method and two-week refrigerated limit: https://ucanr.edu/sites/default/files/2025-07/MFP%20Quick%20Refrigerator%20Pickles.pdf . OSU's directly read refrigerator method supports simmered brine and prompt refrigeration: https://extension.oregonstate.edu/imported-publication/refrigerator-pickled-vegetables . Retained a two-week limit, not OSU's longer general vegetable claim. Removed legacy nutrition.
