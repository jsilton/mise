# smash-burgers: new recipe editorial record

Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Smash Burgers was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (the everyday beef dinners (steak, burgers, pot roast, meatballs and meat lasagna)). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

Twelve 2-oz balls of 80% lean beef make six double cheeseburgers with American cheese, a mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard and pickle sauce and toasted potato buns. After review the patties cook two at a time, one burger per batch, so they do not crowd a 12-inch skillet, and the total went to 30 minutes.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check the 1 1/2 minute first side on a home burner, the salt level on thin patties and whether six batches hold the 20 minute cook time.
