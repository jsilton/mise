# Sushi-roll sources and exact applicability

Checked 6 September 2026 unless marked reused. No source establishes that our final recipe was physically tested.

- [Namiko Hirasawa Chen: Sushi Rolls / Hosomaki](https://www.justonecookbook.com/sushi-rolls/) — read complete relevant method and ingredients. Supports half/three-quarter/full nori options, long-edge orientation, measured rice, gentle rolling and knife cutting. Her half-sheet single-fill roll uses about 80 g rice and much less tuna than our originals. Our 19 x 15 cm wrapper, larger fillings and exact counts are editorial development, not copied test results. Do not adopt the page's label-only raw sourcing or towel-insulated refrigeration advice over government guidance.
- [Namiko Hirasawa Chen: Spicy Tuna Roll](https://www.justonecookbook.com/spicy-tuna-roll/) — read complete recipe and relevant discussion. Supports the broad American-style sushi context and shows that its half-sheet roll has rice outside. Its fish amount is approximately 57 g per roll, but its 110 g outside rice and sauce differ. It does not validate transferring that capacity to a nori-outside roll, nor our original's exclusive Los Angeles/1980s invention statement.
- [Namiko Hirasawa Chen: California Roll](https://www.justonecookbook.com/california-roll/) — read the actual half-sheet rice-outside method and eight-piece cuts to compare wrapper placement. It is not a substitute formula or evidence that every Philly/crunch roll must be inside-out. Its avocado knife-strike method is not adopted.
- [Kewpie USA product page](https://www.kewpieshop.com/products/kewpie-mayonnaise) — accessed; identifies egg-yolk-based mayonnaise and specific ingredient list. Product-specific, not a guarantee covering all “Japanese mayonnaise” or substitutes. No source nutrition is transferred.
- [Otafuku Premium Tenkasu](https://www.otafukusauce.com/e/products/retail/013.html) — accessed fully. Prepared crisp product is explicitly offered for sushi, in a 50 g bag, with wheat listed. Page nutrition gives one tablespoon as 10 g. That is about 53 g for one-third cup if applied to this specific product; no universal volume/weight equivalence is asserted. Actual current package controls. The separate US foodservice page `https://otafukufoods.com/products/tenkasu` returned an internal access error and was not used to claim another formulation.
- [FDA: Selecting and Serving Seafood Safely](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/selecting-and-serving-fresh-and-frozen-seafood-safely) — full page verified in the immediately preceding poke batch and reused here. Applies to raw tuna sourcing limits, residual hazards after freezing, susceptible diners, refrigerator thawing, clean equipment and cold handling. Same-day planning in our recipes is conservative editorial practice, not a scientifically measured shelf life for these rolls.
- [FDA Food Code 2022](https://www.fda.gov/media/164194/download) — relevant primary text verified in the preceding batch and reused: 3-402.11–12 lists specific parasite exemptions including yellowfin and supplier records; 3-501.13 addresses removing reduced-oxygen packaging before refrigerator thawing. These are model-code controls, not a blanket declaration of local law. No domestic freezing process is prescribed and no exemption is described as protection against all hazards.
- The final proposed shari already cites primary rice technique and official [FoodSafety.gov handling](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety), [reheating endpoints](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) and [general cooked-leftover timing](https://www.foodsafety.gov/blog/food-safety-and-eating-out). Those verified boundaries are reused for unused clean rice, not assigned to assembled raw-fish rolls.

Source history was read directly from git and preserved under history/. No corresponding original HTML or named author source was found for these three rolls. Removing unsupported origin details does not establish a different inventor or origin story.
