# Roasted Black Bean Tacos with Avocado Crema: individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

Read the entire original and soft-bean taco recipe. Retained a distinct roast/avocado-crema preparation, renamed to clarify that the beans have crisp edges while the tortillas are soft. The original promise of caramelized charred edges rivaling carnitas was unsupported. Reduced six main servings to four for the same 510 g drained beans; twelve five-inch tortillas now give three small tacos per person. Removed extra guacamole and rice from default pairings because avocado sauce and tortillas already fill those roles.

Added proper drying, a stated half-sheet pan, a coarse one-third crush rather than a half-bean smear, and taste/texture checkpoints before blackening. Lowered soy from a tablespoon to two teaspoons and removed redundant pre-roast salt; salty brine/cotija are now staged or optional. Kept zest on the beans but moved lime juice to the crema/table to avoid wetting freshly dried edges. Crema is a measured 150 g avocado/120 g dairy formula with controlled lime, brine, water and optional salt. Cabbage, cheese, jalapeños, cilantro, hot sauce and wedges all have quantities. Total includes actual drying/preheat and topping work overlapped with roasting.

Kenji's primary bean-burger recipe supports moisture reduction and split skins during roasting only. It uses different heat and a burger workflow; this taco method and 425°F window are editorial decisions requiring kitchen validation. No carnitas equivalence or universally crisp result is inferred. Mission supports packaged tortilla warming; federal sources support storage. Legacy nutrition removed.
