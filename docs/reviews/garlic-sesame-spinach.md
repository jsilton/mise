# Sigeumchi Namul (Sesame Spinach): individual editorial review

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: editorial review; no physical kitchen test recorded.

## Complete recipe and relationship review

Read the entire original and its General Tso's Night usage. The original was already a Korean blanched/dressed banchan despite the generic garlic-sesame URL and meal's incorrect sauté instruction. Retained that identity and stable URL, added a descriptive title, replaced basmati with plain short-grain rice as the contextual base pairing, and distinguished it from hot sautéed greens.

Kept 450 g baby spinach as four explicitly small side portions. Increased sesame oil from 1 to 2 tsp and seeds from 1 to 2 tsp for a measurable light coating; kept 1 tsp garlic but staged it. Specified salt type: up to 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal or 1/4 tsp fine, staged after water removal. Added blanching water and cold/ice water to the list and removed unlisted salt from the water. Two 225 g batches in a large pot avoid assuming the full raw volume fits in 2 liters at once.

Replaced exact thirty-second cooking and bone-dry wringing with wilt/stem checks, prompt cooling, gentle handful pressing and strand separation. Expanded total time from seven to twenty minutes to include heating, batches and dressing. Removed unverified nutrition and obsolete singular season/healthiness fields. The salt-based formula is plant-based and contains no intentional gluten ingredient; this is not a guarantee against packaged-product cross-contact.
