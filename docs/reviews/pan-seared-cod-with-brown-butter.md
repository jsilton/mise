Date: 2026-11-10. Editorial review only. No physical kitchen test recorded.

## Why it was added

Pan-Seared Cod with Brown Butter and Lemon was written new on 2026-11-10 to fill a gap named in the Nov 10 collection review (white fish and fish tacos). It is not an import and has no outside source. It is authored as formula version 1.

## Decisions

A 1 1/2 lb fillet of cod or halibut is cut into five pieces so it fits a 12-inch nonstick skillet and feeds the family. It is salted 10 minutes, dusted with flour, seared 4 to 5 minutes, then flipped and basted while the butter browns. Capers, lemon juice and parsley finish the sauce off the heat. The review asked for no changes.

## Kitchen-test questions

Check that the butter browns without burning in the 2 to 3 minutes the second side takes, and that five pieces fit without steaming.
