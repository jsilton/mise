# Shared internal-consistency packet v1

This first worker trial measures extraction and internal contradiction detection, not external culinary knowledge. All claims below derive from the repository's explicit behavior/contract, not a cooking source. Primary-source culinary packets remain required before evaluating scientific corrections.

IC1 — Quantity controls scale ingredient list values, not method prose. Fixed arithmetic in method text may conflict with scaled ingredients. Verify an actual conflict before flagging; dimensions and temperatures should not scale. Source: docs/RECIPE_STANDARD.md, serving-control contract.
IC2 — An ingredient with no method destination, or a method addition with no listed allowance, is an accounting question. It does not prove the ingredient should be removed or a new dose invented. Source: docs/RECIPE_REVIEW_PIPELINE.md, worker contract.
IC3 — Total elapsed time must account for mandatory sequential preparation, cooking and rests. Overlap must be actually executable. Unknown preheating or equipment time stays unknown; do not turn it into an invented correction. Source: docs/RECIPE_STANDARD.md, timing requirements.
IC4 — A title similarity or matching main ingredient only nominates a duplicate comparison. No merge without full identity/source decision. Source: docs/RECIPE_STANDARD.md, consolidation requirements.
IC5 — Manufacturer names, source credits and culinary ingredient names are different concepts. Keep legitimate author attribution and useful composition constraints. No salt-density conversions without evidence. Source: docs/RECIPE_STANDARD.md, public presentation.
IC6 — Unverified mechanism, historical or safety claims are unresolved unless supported by an applicable source. A lack of citation alone does not prove a claim false. Do not generate alternative science from memory. Source: docs/RECIPE_REVIEW_PIPELINE.md, knowledge boundaries.

Expected use: identify complete ingredient/process ledgers, direct contradictions and unresolved questions. Escalate culinary judgments. The first trial cannot measure whether source packets improve scientific understanding; it provides an explicitly limited internal-consistency baseline.
