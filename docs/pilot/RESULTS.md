# Pilot decision: restrict lightweight workers to bounded maintenance

Root read all 24 frozen recipes, their ingredient ledgers, findings and unresolved items, and the 64 independent reference findings. All six method families have source-backed worker outputs. The earlier four-recipe internal-only pasta baseline remains separate and is not pooled into the source-backed results.

## Measured findings

- Routine proposals: 6 accepted unchanged out of 7 (85.7%). The rejected proposal invented a `waffle-iron` taxonomy. The 90% target was not reached.
- Reference findings: 29 detected, 29 missed, 6 retained as conditional concerns rather than confirmed defects. Detection is generous: identifying part of a compound issue or explicitly escalating the relevant question counts; it is not a correct finished edit. These are reference-relative findings, not an exhaustive count of culinary defects.
- Both reference critical endpoint concerns (scallop doneness and carbonara eggs) were detected by the source-backed workers. This does not offset the many material misses, including equipment compatibility, split quantities, missing alternatives and unqualified scientific claims.
- Actual billable tokens and dollars are unavailable. Reliable comparable turnaround and active expert-time measurements were not obtained. Original batter and grain timestamps were acknowledged as invented/estimated; they are excluded. Later equal start/end times are not accepted as zero-duration work. There is no supported cost or time saving claim.
- No generated recipe prose was published automatically. The proposed taxonomy addition was rejected. Findings also contained unsupported assertions: the jerk finishing marinade was treated as raw-pork-contact despite being reserved before contact; some unverified nutrition was labeled a material error without supporting calculation. These are evidence-quality failures, not accepted culinary changes.

The workers often mapped an ingredient to some step and marked it clear without checking whether every alternative survived, whether its full amount was used, or whether fixed step amounts scaled. Adding a longer checklist did not cure this. The quick-batter comparison even called a missing greasing-butter issue “detected” by citing an unrelated blueberry-allocation question; root rejected that comparison. Self-review by the same inexpensive worker is not independent verification.

## Operating change

Stop broad lightweight whole-recipe reviews under this protocol. Use deterministic checks for exact duplicate links, known taxonomy, source-span matching and quantity arithmetic. Lightweight agents may perform narrowly specified extraction or presentation tasks with mechanically verifiable outputs; they do not decide a recipe passed.

Continue stronger whole-recipe editing in method-family batches, reusing the packets and all sealed findings. Resolve each listed exception in context, plus review attribution, relationships, yield and teaching. Do not repeat the pilot just to manufacture a passing percentage. If another model is evaluated, use a small fresh calibration set, clock observations recorded by the coordinator, and actual outputs before widening scope.

The first targeted release contains 13 corrections across 9 recipes, recorded in FIRST_CORRECTIONS.json: six accepted metadata/link changes, preserved pilaf alternatives and scalable fat split, a compatible-pan condition and bay-leaf removal for brisket, controlled juice return for pulled pork, honey distributed across both root-vegetable trays, and a supported scallop endpoint. These changes do not mark those recipes comprehensively reviewed. Remaining formula, timing, storage and relationship work stays queued. Frozen pilot inputs remain untouched.

## Remaining evaluation limitations

The pilot's quality failure is established. Its economic comparison is incomplete and cannot be recovered from invented timestamps or message spacing. The checker therefore continues to report evaluationComplete false; that is not a reason to keep commissioning the failed approach. Full recipe review and the website goal continue using the revised division of work.
