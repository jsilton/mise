# Soups and fruit salads: planned kitchen tests

No physical results are recorded. Use the recipe commit published with this plan. Record ingredient products and prepared weights, cookware dimensions, actual active/elapsed time, temperatures where specified, finished quantities and sensory observations. Photograph the real prepared dish separately from site artwork. Read the actual blender manual before testing; do not test prohibited hot-liquid configurations.

## First priorities

1. **Tomato Soup:** Cook the full stated butter/flour/tomato formula in the proposed8-quart pot. Record headroom, time to heat after adding tomatoes, scraping frequency, flour smoothness, final volume and eight-portion allocation. Taste sweetness/acid with the specified sugar/honey and dairy before proposing a change. Record shallow-container cooling separately from serving time.
2. **Peach, Tomato and Beet Salad:** Record raw beet dimensions, covered-baking time at425°F, water level and knife tenderness; time handling/peeling and final cooling. Check the two-hour estimate, eight small portions and whether slices stay distinct after dressing. A purchased cooked-beet test does not validate the raw-beet schedule.
3. **Grilled Cheese and Tomato Soup:** Record actual bread/cheese dimensions, mayonnaise used, sandwich pan rounds, crust/melt endpoint and soup hold. Test the55-minute one-cook sequence and two generous portions. A smooth soup alone does not establish successful sandwich coordination.

## Remaining checks

- **Roasted Tomato Basil Soup:** Record fresh tomato water loss, protected garlic doneness, tray fit, optional-cream and no-cream texture, actual volume and six generous portions; check90-minute estimate.
- **Zucchini Soup:** Record squash maturity, additional garnish quantity actually used, vegetable tenderness, blender performance, final consistency and four light portions; check50-minute estimate.
- **Spicy Peach and Avocado Salad:** Record fruit firmness and chile heat, the15-minute rest, avocado folding, juice released and eight-side allocation.
- **Thai Green Mango Salad:** Record mango variety, prepared weight and sourness; measure lime/fish sauce actually used, herb condition and cutting time. Check four side portions rather than assuming identical mango yields.
- **Sesame Tomato Salad:** Record tomato varieties, soy/sesame products, dressing used versus served separately, juice after five minutes and six-side allocation. Preserve the full dressing formula for the baseline trial.

For failures, record the stage and observed cause before revising the formula. Retest the revised version and seek a second cook/kitchen before claiming strong reproducibility. Written review and arithmetic validation do not supply these sensory results.
