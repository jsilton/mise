# Pasta release: planned kitchen checks

These are executable tests, not completed results. Use the recipe versions published with this record and record the commit, ingredient products, cookware dimensions, actual quantities used, photographs and elapsed/active times. Cook the baseline batch before testing scaled batches. Never infer tasting results from artwork or passing site checks.

## Priority 1: rich cheese and butter finishes

Cook Lemon Ricotta Pasta at its full listed quantities. Record ricotta type/texture, lemon juice obtained and used, cooking-water reserve and actual addition. Start the clock before heating water. Photograph the bowl immediately after tossing and a plated portion five minutes later. Record whether the pasta was fully tender before the bowl finish, whether oil pools, and whether the four portions are suitable. Preserve the half-cup oil baseline during this first test; any proposed adjustment is a separate comparison, not an undocumented correction.

Cook Shrimp Scampi Dinner for four using its full main and salad and half bread batch. Record shrimp size, number of searing rounds, pasta package duration, pan capacity, wine reduction and water used. Follow the one-hour sequence, baking bread before the attended sauce finish. Record bread temperature/texture at service and whether tossing the salad delays pasta. Check shrimp at its stated endpoint before removal. Photograph the sauce immediately and after five minutes; log separation, tenderness and combined salt/acid balance. Evaluate the actual sixty-minute schedule rather than reporting the recipe estimate as a result.

## Priority 2: yields and coordination

Cook Shrimp Linguine with Fresh Tomato, White Wine & Basil. Weigh purchased shell-on shrimp, peeled/deveined meat and discarded shells separately; use the full listed pound of edible shrimp. Record tomato pulp volume, stock volume after straining and after adjustment, reduction time, pan fill and actual pasta-water addition. Include peeling, grating and the optional topping in the clock. Assess the five portions and whether the seventy-five-minute allowance works for one cook. Do not generalize a shell-on conversion from one purchase.

Cook Crispy Pancetta, Sweet Pea & Lemon Ricotta Pasta. Record pancetta product, dice size, rendered fat produced and retained, pea package instructions and cooking time. Record how long the skillet takes to stop sizzling, lemon juice and water added, and sauce texture after five minutes. Assess three generous portions. A different pancetta product requires checking its rendering behavior again.

## Priority 3: representative remaining variants

- Lemon Broccoli Tortellini: record filled-pasta package directions, broccoli size/tray fit, garlic color, all vegetable and pasta finish times, and water used. Test the forty-five-minute coordination and four portions.
- Lemony Broccoli Pasta: record broccoli size, when it enters the pot, time for boiling to resume, independent pasta/vegetable doneness and pea cooking. Assess six portions and the thirty-five-minute clock.
- Shrimp Vodka Pasta: record broccoli crown size, pan fit, vodka reduction time and flavor, sauce consistency and four portions. No short cooking test establishes an alcohol-free result.
- Soy Sauce Butter Pasta with Shrimp and Shiitakes: record purchased mushroom weight versus usable caps, mushroom moisture release, water added, soy product and salt balance. Assess four modest portions and the forty-five-minute clock.

For each test, record deviations and their reason. If an outcome fails, identify the stage that failed before changing the formula. Repeat the revised version before claiming the issue is resolved. Physical cooking and tasting require a human participant; none have been recorded for this batch.
