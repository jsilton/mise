# Leavening: individual lesson review

Date: 2026-09-06. Editorial lesson; no physical demonstration or cornbread test recorded.

Adds the ninth public lesson, linked explicitly to the individually reviewed Buttermilk Cornbread. Explains chemical gas formation alongside the need for a setting crumb, distinguishes soda from double-acting powder, and connects acid choice, measured dose, dry dispersion, folding, pan depth, preheating and prompt baking to observable outcomes. The side-by-side comparison uses plain text rather than a simulated rise whose numbers would imply experimental evidence.

The scope is chemically leavened quick breads. It does not apply the brief-fold instruction to yeast dough, treat powder as a cup-for-cup soda substitute, or imply extra leavener fixes every dense result. Troubleshooting distinguishes formula, heat, mixing and pan causes. A small open-cup activity illustrates acid/base gas formation; it is explicitly not a predictor of loaf height. Quantities are an editorial demonstration proposal, not a completed measurement.
