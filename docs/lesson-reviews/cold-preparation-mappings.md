# Individually considered lesson mappings

Proposals only; apply after accepting `cold-preparation`. These arrays reflect the full repository recipes read on 2026-09-06. No methods, recipe formulas, meals or calendar labels should change with this lesson.

| Recipe                 | Proposed complete learning.techniques array | Specific reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `philly-tuna-roll`     | `[cold-preparation, starch]`                | Cold block cheese strips are a defining method and troubleshooting point. Small chilled fish/cheese portions and dried cucumber directly practice three controls. Starch still explains the prepared rice.                                                                                                    |
| `new-york-crunch-roll` | `[cold-preparation, starch]`                | Ready-to-eat flakes are added immediately before rolling and each roll is cut for service. Keeping garnishes dry and reserve tuna cold has a direct texture and sequence payoff. No guarantee of lasting crunch is added.                                                                                     |
| `spicy-tuna-roll`      | `[seasoning, starch, cold-preparation]`     | Chopped dressed tuna is divided and returned to refrigeration, then brought out one portion at a time. The clean dipping-water rule is relevant. Retain seasoning and starch because mayonnaise balance and weighed rice remain central to this recipe.                                                       |
| `master-sushi-rice`    | `[starch, seasoning, cold-preparation]`     | Its last two steps distinguish short steam release for immediate assembly from prompt refrigeration of unused rice. Keep starch and seasoning first; this lesson addresses the transition after cooking, not the hydration formula.                                                                           |
| `poke-bowl-bar`        | `[cold-preparation, seasoning]`             | Replace `temperature`, whose meat-doneness emphasis is poorly matched here. This recipe measures cold seafood over ice, uses separate serving utensils, and keeps rice, vegetables and dry finishes separate until each bowl is eaten. Seasoning still explains cumulative soy/cucumber/garnish salt.         |
| `tzatziki-sauce`       | `[cold-preparation, seasoning]`             | Grating, short salting and squeezing control sauce dilution; chilling alone is not the draining step. Its existing explicit focus is water management. Preserve dill/lemon and its own batch yield.                                                                                                           |
| `mint-tzatziki`        | `[seasoning, cold-preparation]`             | Fine dice is blotted rather than squeezed, preserving the household's crunchy cucumber pieces. The lesson explains this distinction without forcing the dill version's procedure onto it. Retain seasoning first because balancing mint, yogurt, garlic and a little honey remains the recipe's stated focus. |

The proposed lesson's four practice cards are Philly Tuna Roll (firm cutting), Dill Tzatziki (water management), Sushi Rice (Shari) (post-cooking transition) and Poke Bowl Bar (measured cold service). This gives one distinct exercise per major application instead of listing all three tuna rolls as if they teach three different cold-chain rules. Mint and New York still receive the lesson through their explicit arrays; omission from the four-card subset is not rejection of their fit.

Do not add `no-cook` or `assemble` to the lesson's `methods`: that would imply a considered mapping for many pending recipes. Do not newly certify linked meals or calendars through this change. Other poke recipes might fit, but require a separate deliberate mapping decision rather than propagation from a shared word or ingredient.
