## Chef's Note

Shave the sprouts into fine ribbons so a short rest in the dressing is enough to soften their bite. Tart apple balances the honey, and Pecorino adds a generous sharp, salty finish. Adapted from [Love and Olive Oil](https://www.loveandoliveoil.com/2013/04/shaved-brussels-sprout-salad.html).

## Directions

1. **Mix dressing:** Whisk vinegar, honey and olive oil in a large bowl until combined. Add black pepper and only a little salt at first; the cheese will add more salt.

2. **Shave sprouts:** Trim tough stem ends and remove damaged leaves. Wash and dry the sprouts, then slice into paper-thin ribbons with a sharp knife or food processor slicing disc. Discard any remaining large, hard core pieces.

3. **Cut apple:** Core the washed apple and cut into fine matchsticks. Toss immediately with a small spoonful taken from the measured dressing to limit browning.

4. **Dress and rest:** Add sprouts and the dressed apple to the remaining dressing. Toss thoroughly so all the ribbons are coated, then let stand for about 15 minutes, tossing once during the rest.

5. **Finish:** Fold in the Pecorino, taste a complete bite, then add black pepper or a little more salt only if needed. Serve immediately, or refrigerate until serving.
