## Chef's Note

Farro is a wheat grain with a pleasantly chewy bite. Let it cool before adding the vegetables, then fold in the feta and fresh herbs near serving so the salad keeps its distinct textures.

## Directions

1. **Cook farro:** Rinse the farro. Bring broth and the listed salt to a boil in a saucepan, add farro, then reduce to a gentle simmer and cover. Follow the package cooking time, checking for tenderness; many pearled products take about 25–30 minutes. Add hot water if the pan is drying before the grain is tender. Drain excess liquid once the grains are cooked through but still chewy.

2. **Cool:** Spread drained farro in a thin layer on a clean tray. Let excess steam escape, then refrigerate the shallow tray as needed until the grain is no longer warm. Do not wait to assemble while the grain is still hot.

3. **Prepare vegetables:** While the grain cooks and cools, wash and dry the produce. Dice cucumber, tomatoes and seeded pepper into roughly 1/2-inch pieces; finely dice onion. Combine these with the olives in a large bowl.

4. **Mix dressing:** Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic and black pepper until combined. Whisk again before using if the liquids separate.

5. **Assemble:** Add cooled farro and all the dressing to the vegetables and toss gently. Fold in feta, parsley, mint and dill shortly before serving. Taste a complete spoonful before adding extra salt. Serve promptly or refrigerate until serving.
