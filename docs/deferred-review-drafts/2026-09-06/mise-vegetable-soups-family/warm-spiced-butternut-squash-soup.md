## Chef's Note

The squash peels and seeds make a fragrant stock with thyme and whole spices. Strain that stock thoroughly, then simmer the flesh until it crushes easily. Olive oil supplies richness without cream; vinegar, pomegranate and cilantro finish the warm spice.

## Directions

1. **Prepare squash:** Scrub the squash before peeling. Remove seeds and cube the flesh into even bite-size pieces. Reserve sound peels, seed-cavity trimmings and seeds; discard stem and damaged pieces.
2. **Make the broth:** Heat **one quarter of the oil** in a Dutch oven over medium. Add reserved trimmings and cook about **8 minutes**, stirring, until lightly browned. Add all the measured water, allspice, peppercorns, thyme, cloves, cinnamon and star anise. Bring to a simmer, cover and cook **45 minutes**. Strain into a heatproof bowl and discard all solids. Carefully wipe the pot clean.
3. **Soften onion and squash:** Heat the **remaining oil** in the pot over medium. Add onion, cover and cook about **5 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until softened. Add cubed squash, cover and cook **10 minutes**, stirring occasionally; it should start softening without needing to brown.
4. **Simmer:** Return all the strained broth to the pot and season lightly with salt. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover and cook about **40 minutes**, until squash crushes easily with a spoon. Extend if cubes are still firm.
5. **Blend:** Remove from heat and let active bubbling stop. Blend with an immersion blender, keeping its guard submerged and stopping before lifting it. Alternatively, cool to your countertop blender’s permitted temperature and blend in as many small batches as its hot-liquid fill limit requires, arranging the lid and steam vent exactly as its instructions specify. Start low; never blend hot soup in a sealed personal cup.
6. **Finish and serve:** Stir in all the measured vinegar and red pepper flakes. Taste for salt and warm gently if needed. Divide into the intended portions and distribute pomegranate arils and cilantro on top.
