## Chef's Note

Carrot and celery root supply most of the body. Gingersnaps add spice, sweetness and a little starch; apple, sour cream and vinegar keep those flavors balanced. Wait until both roots crush readily before blending, then warm the dairy gently.

## Directions

1. **Soften aromatics:** Melt butter in a large heavy pot over medium. Add onion, leek and fennel with salt and pepper. Cook about **9 minutes**, stirring, until soft and just starting to color.
2. **Cook the base:** Add carrots, celery root, apple, crumbled gingersnaps, curry powder, garlic, ginger and thyme. Cook about **10 minutes**, stirring frequently, until the roots begin softening. Lower heat if cookie crumbs or spices catch.
3. **Simmer:** Add all the stock, scrape the bottom and bring to a simmer. Cook about **25 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until carrot and celery root crush easily. Extend as needed; remove and discard thyme stems. Prepare the seeds and herbs while it cooks.
4. **Blend:** Remove from heat and let active bubbling stop. Blend with an immersion blender, keeping its guard submerged and stopping before lifting it. Alternatively, cool to your countertop blender’s permitted temperature and blend in as many small batches as its hot-liquid fill limit requires, arranging the lid and steam vent exactly as its instructions specify. Start low; never blend hot soup in a sealed personal cup.
5. **Finish:** Whisk a little warm soup into the sour cream to loosen it, then stir that mixture and all the measured vinegar into the pot off heat. Warm gently, without a rolling boil, and adjust salt and pepper.
6. **Serve:** Divide into the intended portions and finish with toasted pumpkin seeds, mint and cilantro. Keep garnishes separate from any soup being stored.
