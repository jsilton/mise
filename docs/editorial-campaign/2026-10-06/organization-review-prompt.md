# Prompt: compare culinary organizations with Mise

Review **[organization or list of organizations]**, separately or together, against the current recipe collection in https://github.com/jsilton/mise and its live site, https://jordansilton.com/mise/.

Read the current culinary standards, household preferences and review progress first. Inventory fresh main; deduplicate renamed recipes and distinct variants by their actual ingredients and methods. Do not assume that an older collection count or complete-review campaign covers later additions.

Identify which organizations publish useful primary recipe evidence, what their remit and regional perspective are, and what their editorial or testing claims actually establish. Prestige and popularity do not certify a particular formula. Read each proposed recipe's complete ingredients, method, variations and notes at its original publisher. Prefer original-language material where translation could change ingredient state or technique. Explain disagreements between sources.

Recommend a small ranked set of missing dishes that would be excellent additions for this household. Compare each with Mise's closest existing recipe: distinctive culinary value, defining ingredients, regional context, equipment, active versus elapsed work, and practical reasons to cook it. Avoid duplicate dishes dressed up with a new title. Skip tiramisu and whole-duck projects; skin-on duck breasts are welcome.

For each recommendation, supply a direct primary-source link, the actual published quantities and yield, every ingredient's preparation and method destination, pan capacity, heat and doneness, resting/chilling/storage requirements, and what remains unsupported or needs kitchen testing. Check applicable safety guidance against primary FDA/USDA sources. Do not manufacture nutrition, yield precision or tested claims. Distinguish the source's formula from any suggested household adaptation.

Preserve family authorship, cultural context, deliberate richness, useful notes and distinct variants. Do not remove butter, sugar, salt or complexity merely to simplify or make a recipe seem healthier. Consider substitutions only when their effects are explicit. Flag source gaps instead of reconstructing a formula silently.

Return a concise comparison table, a prioritized shortlist with evidence and uncertainties, and a first disjoint batch of roughly five recipes suitable for independent culinary review. This is a research review: present concrete proposals before changing the collection. Website publication and native Paprika import are separate actions; private identities, native archives and photos must remain private.
