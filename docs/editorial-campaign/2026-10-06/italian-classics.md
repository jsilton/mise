# Seven Italian classics — October 6, 2026

Seven authorized additions are accepted locally after complete source review, independent challenge and integration-owner inspection. Tiramisù is excluded. No existing recipe is replaced and every existing identity and private binding is preserved. These additions are separate from the original 409-source campaign and do not resolve its outstanding holds.

Pesto alla Genovese follows the complete current World Pesto Championship version. Pasta alla Norma, Ribollita, Risotto alla Milanese and Brasato al Barolo follow their complete Academy versions. Caponata follows the complete Visit Sicily regional version. Passatelli explicitly follows Artusi’s first formula; its disagreement with the Academy reuse-book formula is documented. Defining cheeses, oils, butter, marrow, sugar and distinct source variants are retained.

[Independent challenge](italian-classics-independent.json). Each recipe has an individual exact-version record in docs/reviews. Combined validation, browser verification and exact production evidence will be recorded before publication is reported. No physical kitchen testing or native-app synchronization.
