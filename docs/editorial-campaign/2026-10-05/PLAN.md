## Staffing and throughput

Four available agent slots: three capable whole-recipe editors and the integration owner. At Jordan’s request, the third slot moved from waiting acceptance work to active review; independent peer review rotates at batch handoff, and the integration owner challenges consequential findings before inspecting complete diffs. Review groups contain five to ten recipes with relevant process/evidence overlap. Reviewer assignments are disjoint; source snapshots/hashes prevent accidental replacement of concurrent work. Shared research is reused within groups and across later groups, with recipe-specific applicability and exclusions retained. No new lightweight-model experiment or repeat of the failed pilot is scheduled. Deterministic scripts handle membership, identities, references, quantities and serialization; they do not decide culinary approval.

The first baking and poultry groups each contain six recipes; the third group contains the five already repaired desserts, reusing that source evidence for their still-pending complete review. Baking focuses on unresolved leavening, ingredient allocation, browned-butter quantity, pan/timing and source questions. Poultry focuses on baked/air-fried chicken, ingredient destinations, batch capacity, doneness and storage. These are nominations, not automatic defects.

Each reviewer reads complete current recipes and saved originals, relevant publisher evidence and existing accepted records. They account for every ingredient and preparation/allocation/destination; examine ratios, yield, pan capacity, cooking state, heat/doneness, active/elapsed schedules, rests/chilling/storage, scaling and linked meals. Output is an isolated full-source proposal, individual review record, exact before/after findings with evidence/applicability/confidence, unresolved questions and kitchen-test needs. No-change and held are valid dispositions.

A rotating independent peer editor challenges every consequential change and checks prerequisites for complete editorial status. The integration owner inspects full diffs, preserves identity/defining ingredients/richness/authorship/variants/source links and prior fixes, and alone writes main. Structured authored formula and compiled ingredients/yield/directions change together. Quantities or scientific claims cannot be invented to clear an audit warning. Review fields must be useful recipe-specific content, not generic filler. Public presentation remains cooking-focused, with review disclosure kept in internal records/the shared standards page.
