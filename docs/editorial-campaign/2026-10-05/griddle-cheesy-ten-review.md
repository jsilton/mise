Every recipe has a complete author review, independent whole-recipe challenge, any required bounded final challenge, and root inspection of the complete cooking diff. The frozen group evidence predates this release and is reused rather than repeated. Each ingredient is followed through preparation, allocation and its method destination, including source variations. Ratios, planning yields, vessel capacity, heat, doneness, active versus elapsed time, rests, storage, scaling and linked meals were considered. Paired public records retain exact before/after fields and bodies, evidence, confidence, source disagreements and remaining questions.

The griddle recipes retain complete butter quantities for both single-round and multiple-round cooking, original syrup and bread options, and conditional bread and batter cues. Broader elapsed ranges include the complete batch rather than just one round. The cheese dishes keep their original richness, family authorship and distinct variants. Raw Mexican chorizo is distinguished from air-dried sausage; oven and broiler guidance depends on actual vessel ratings and center checks. Earlier egg, casserole and leftover safety repairs remain in place. Recovered squash, mixed-crumb and reheating options have complete ingredient destinations; source assertions do not certify measured cooked squash yield or actual pan capacity.

Primary FDA/USDA handling guidance is applied to the relevant food category. It does not establish recipe-specific clocks or eliminate all risk. Exact native originals, when established by existing private stable bindings, remain private. The Git-only Classic French Toast is explicitly distinguished from independently recovered originals. Source changes and household adaptations are explained, not silently substituted. Unverified legacy nutrition and unsupported country assertions are not replaced with new precision or invented origins. All culinary cuisine tags, family/source attribution, source links and meaningful notes remain.
