# Mise recipe image pilot — Batch 01

20 original PNGs, each **1536 × 1024**. Review assets only; no site files, mappings, configuration or deployment changed.

## Review

- [Full-frame contact sheet](review/full-contact-sheet.png)
- [Middle-half phone-card crops](review/middle-half-contact-sheet.png)
- [Exact supplied recipe prompts](prompts/batch-01-source.md)
- [Generation and revision prompts](prompts/generation-and-revision-log.json)
- [QA and SHA-256 checksums](qa.json)

Style reference for every generation: [first-test cookbook margarita](https://github.com/jsilton/mise/blob/images/mise-style-tests-2026-10-31/docs/image-tests/2026-10-31/images/margarita-01-cookbook.png). New prompts override the reference's props, framing and background variation.

## QA notes

All 20 originals and their **768 × 1024 center-half crops (x384–1152)** were visually inspected. Complete vessels remain inside those crop bounds. No steam, extra garnish or additional props observed. Optional props were omitted throughout to keep the family consistent and uncluttered. Rejected oversized compositions were regenerated; the biscuits were refined to show one pulled apart. Local cast shadows are retained as requested.

Backgrounds have very small corner-to-corner variation, but generated colors are **not mathematically exact hex matches**. No artificial recoloring, resizing or cropping was applied to the delivered originals. Contact sheets are review previews only. Image appearance is not a cooked-recipe verification.
