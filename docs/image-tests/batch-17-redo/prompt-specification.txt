Shared style, for every image:
Bright, true-to-life cookbook photograph. Camera at about 40 degrees, 50mm lens, the whole dish in sharp focus. Soft, even daylight from the upper left, with one short soft shadow to the lower right. Background: a smooth, matte, seamless surface and wall in the backdrop color given for the image, one flat color edge to edge, evenly lit, with no vignette, no gradient, no darker corners and no horizon line. No props: only the dish on the backdrop. Keep any accent color to slate blue (#486B7B) or apricot (#F59443), used on one small thing only. Framing: the dish sits dead center, left to right and top to bottom, and is about half the frame width, so a tall, narrow crop of the middle half still shows all of it. A tall glass uses height instead, about three quarters of the frame height. Landscape 3:2, 1536 x 1024. Style reference: match the attached reference image for light, white balance, camera height, scale and how flat the backdrop is. Change only the dish and the backdrop color, and leave out its props. Fresh, lively and appetizing, natural color, bright and airy. Avoid: steam, smoke or vapor of any kind, text, logos, hands, people, dark or moody light, wooden tables, marble, patterned surfaces, props, cutlery, napkins, garnishes not listed for the dish, cartoon or illustration style, plastic-looking food, exaggerated saturation.
