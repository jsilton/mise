# Brand-neutral recipe and meal review

Proposal only; no repository edits. Generated against the current working tree after the root agent's 49 prose replacements. Mint Tzatziki is excluded because the root already retained its existing fine-salt measure. This pass removes ingredient/product recommendations, preserves source attribution, and does not certify previously unreviewed recipes.

## Deliverables and application

- `brand-cleanup.patch`: exact reversible unified patch, 143 files (135 recipes, 8 meals).
- `originals/src/content/` and `proposed/src/content/`: complete before/after snapshots for review; do not blindly copy them over a changing repository.
- `snapshot-manifest.json`: SHA-256 values for every changed file.
- `changes.json`: exact old/new replacements and categories; salt entries retain full changed lines.
- `salt-exact-lines.json`: all 96 exact before/after salt lines, with retained alternatives verified.
- `ingredient-numeric-audit.json`: ingredient lines whose numeric tokens change, including deletion of redundant package counts and addition of fixed alcohol strength.
- `build-proposal.py`: repeatable proposal generator. Reads the current repository and writes only this temporary folder; aborts on an absent exact replacement. It is not a repository editing script.
- `originals-lint.txt`, `proposed-lint.txt`: explicit recipe lint results. Both report zero errors and the same three existing warnings across 135 recipes.

Root should review the diff and apply the patch only after a fresh `git apply --check`; reverse with `git apply -R` if needed. Application was checked successfully against the current tree. Existing source/sourceUrl/learning.sources values are byte-equivalent after frontmatter parsing. Stable filenames, aliases, links and named family/author attribution are retained; this avoids silently breaking URLs that contain former product names.

Every ingredient/meal occurrence already offered the retained fine-salt amount; there is no Diamond-Crystal-only dose and no inferred density or mass conversion. - Coconut Rice and Peas retains 3/4 tsp fine salt total and uses 1/2 tsp in the initial infusion, leaving 1/4 tsp for finishing. The old method itself supplied that 1/2 tsp alternative to one teaspoon of Diamond Crystal.
- Falafel retains 6 g weighed salt, its approximate 1 tsp fine-salt equivalent and up to 3 g reserve; weight remains authoritative.
- Greek Village Salad and Tahini Sauce already listed fine salt first; their reversed branded alternatives are removed.

## Numerical ambiguities and separate culinary triage

These are pre-existing or product-dependent limits, not reasons to retain a brand recommendation. This pass does not award new culinary review status or invent missing measurements.

1. **Purple rice:** 40-minute total and 18–20-minute stove simmer are not established for a mixed white/whole-grain purple product. The patch requires checking actual grain tenderness and package directions; extended hydration can over-soften the white rice. A future individual review should evaluate separate precooking or soaking, net rice yield and the Korean attribution. No invented package-equivalent cook time is supplied.
2. **Pressure-cooker eggplant:** only 1/4 cup water is written in the method, not the ingredient list. Broad generic appliance use cannot guarantee pressure formation without scorching. The new generic note requires the actual appliance minimum liquid/fill/release instructions, and step 3 explicitly prohibits using less thin liquid than the chosen manual requires. This may mean more than the original quarter-cup and more evaporation in the final sauté; root should prioritize full review before presenting this method as verified. Adding a random universal cup of water would materially change texture and is not done here. Other pressure soups retain their old pressure/release times; their total-time arithmetic is also not reviewed by this cleanup.
3. **Banana pudding:** one box of wafers has no net weight. Current packages vary; exact original package size is unknown. Do not infer a historic weight from today’s branded box. The existing egg-free tag also conflicts with three egg yolks and needs culinary review.
4. **Cookie cheesecake:** 32 cookies listed, 12 crushed for crust, then 1.5 cups quartered cookies folded in; the unused balance is not defined by weight or count. Chef’s Note says half crust/half filling, which conflicts with twelve of thirty-two. Generic regular-size cookies preserve intent but do not resolve this existing allocation.
5. **Cookie truffles:** 16 oz cookies listed, while method reserves nine and processes “remaining 36,” implying forty-five cookies. Net package weight and count are not guaranteed equivalent. Retain mass for shopping; independent recipe review should resolve garnish allocation by weight.
6. **Candy cookies:** 1.5 cups total, Chef’s Note says fold in three-quarters (1.125 cups), method says one cup; garnish count for 72 cookies is uncertain. Cookie cake’s separate 1.25-cup total is correctly split 0.5 + 0.75 cups.
7. **Strawberry-rhubarb shortcake:** two tbsp elderflower liqueur listed with roasting sugar, plus an unlisted splash in whipped topping. This pass does not invent the splash size.
8. **Golden Veil:** the current “1” serving contains approximately 8 oz spirits/liqueur before six oz wine, citrus and dilution. That existing yield is suspect; generic labels do not make it a verified single serving.
9. **Steven’s Margaritas:** ingredients are 2:1:1 oz, prose says 1:1:1. Red Zone labels 0.6 cup as 5 oz (4.8 oz mathematically); the old rounding remains. Both need individual review, including yield/timing.
10. **Generic blends:** cheese moisture, commercial baking-mix sugar/fat/leavening, curry paste salt/heat, liqueur sugar and seasoning sodium vary. Exact fixed mass/volume and narrowed product category are retained; no nutrition-driven reductions or asserted sensory equivalence are introduced.
