## Chef's Note

Evenly cut vegetables roast more predictably than a pan of mixed sizes. Massage the kale until flexible while they cook, then toss with the vegetables while warm. The full maple-Dijon dressing coats the greens and roasted vegetables, with Parmesan and cranberries adding salty and sweet contrast. Adapted from [Kalejunkie](https://kalejunkie.com/warm-roasted-veggie-salad-with-maple-dijon-vinaigrette/).

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Heat oven to 375°F / 190°C. Scrub and cut the sweet potato into 1/2-inch cubes. Trim sprouts, halve small ones and quarter large ones. Cut the onion into wedges.

2. **Roast:** Toss sweet potato, sprouts and onion with the roasting oil, salt and black pepper. Spread in an uncrowded layer on a rimmed sheet pan. Roast about 25–35 minutes, turning halfway, until a knife enters the thickest centers easily and edges have browned lightly. Check from 20 minutes if pieces are small.

3. **Mix dressing:** While vegetables roast, whisk dressing oil, cider vinegar, maple syrup, Dijon and garlic until combined and lightly thickened. Taste and add salt and black pepper as needed. Whisk again before using if separated.

4. **Massage kale:** Put the washed, dried and chopped kale leaves in a large bowl. Sprinkle with a small pinch of salt and squeeze and rub for about 2 minutes, until darker and flexible.

5. **Combine and serve:** Let roasted vegetables stop steaming fiercely, then add them to the kale while still warm. Add cranberries, Parmesan and all the dressing. Toss with serving tongs until evenly coated, taste for seasoning and serve promptly.
