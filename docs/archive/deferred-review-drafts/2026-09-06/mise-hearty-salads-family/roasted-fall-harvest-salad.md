## Chef's Note

Rub the kale until it feels flexible, then use the tahini dressing to coat the leaves and grains. The roasted chickpeas add browned, chewy contrast; pepitas bring the crispness. Apple cider supplies mellow sweetness, while lemon supplies the sharp acidity. Adapted from [Munching with Mariyah](https://munchingwithmariyah.com/roasted-fall-harvest-salad/).

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Heat the oven to 450°F / 230°C. Drain, rinse and dry chickpeas well. Peel and seed the small squash and cut into 1/2-inch cubes. Rinse quinoa and wash, dry and chop the kale, discarding tough stems.

2. **Roast:** Toss squash and chickpeas with the roasting oil, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, cayenne and salt to taste. Spread in one uncrowded layer on a rimmed sheet pan. Roast about 25–30 minutes, turning halfway, until squash is tender and chickpeas are browned in spots. Let the pan cool briefly before assembly.

3. **Cook quinoa:** Meanwhile, bring quinoa and its listed water to a boil in a small saucepan. Cover and simmer gently about 12–15 minutes, or according to the package, until tender and water is absorbed. If still firm, add a small splash of water and continue briefly. Remove from heat and rest covered for 10 minutes, then fluff and let steam escape.

4. **Massage kale:** Put kale in a large bowl with the massaging oil and a small pinch of salt. Squeeze and rub the leaves for about 2 minutes, or until darker and more flexible.

5. **Make dressing:** Whisk tahini, lemon juice, cider and maple syrup. If too thick to drizzle, whisk in a little more cider. Taste and season with salt as needed.

6. **Assemble:** Toss kale and warm quinoa with half the dressing. Add roasted squash and chickpeas, then scatter with toasted pepitas and optional raisins or dates. Drizzle with the remaining dressing and serve.
