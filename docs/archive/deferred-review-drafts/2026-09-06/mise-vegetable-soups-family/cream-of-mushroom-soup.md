## Chef's Note

Let mushroom juices evaporate before looking for golden edges. Blend most of the mushrooms for body and keep a portion whole for contrast. Cream rounds out the broth; a little dry sherry gives a nutty finish.

## Directions

1. **Brown mushrooms:** Melt the butter in a wide pot over medium to medium-high. Add both mushrooms and cook uncovered, stirring periodically, about **12–18 minutes**, until released water mostly evaporates and golden patches form. Reduce heat if butter or mushrooms begin to scorch. Season lightly with salt and pepper and reserve about **1 cup cooked mushrooms per 16 oz raw cremini** used. If working in rounds, divide butter between them and return the mushrooms for the next step, keeping the garnish separate.
2. **Soften aromatics:** Lower heat to medium-low. Add shallot to the mushrooms left in the pot and cook **2–3 minutes**, stirring, until softened; add garlic for **30–45 seconds**. Stir in thyme.
3. **Simmer:** Add stock and scrape up browned bits. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook about **10 minutes**, until mushrooms are tender.
4. **Blend:** Remove from heat and let active bubbling stop. Blend with an immersion blender, keeping its guard submerged and stopping before lifting it. Alternatively, cool to your countertop blender’s permitted temperature and blend in as many small batches as its hot-liquid fill limit requires, arranging the lid and steam vent exactly as its instructions specify. Start low; never blend hot soup in a sealed personal cup.
5. **Finish:** Stir in cream, sherry and reserved mushrooms. Warm gently for **1–2 minutes**, until hot throughout, then taste for salt and pepper. Divide into the intended soup portions.
