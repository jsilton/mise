## Chef's Note

Fennel leads this soup, supported by a little carrot and potato. Slow covered cooking keeps the vegetables mild and soft before broth is added. Brown-butter chestnuts and maple crème fraîche provide richness and contrast in each bowl.

## Directions

1. **Start the vegetables:** Melt **two thirds of the butter** in a heavy pot over medium. Add fennel, onion, carrots, potato, thyme and bay leaf; season with salt and pepper.
2. **Sweat gently:** Reduce to medium-low, cover and cook **45–60 minutes**, stirring and checking the bottom periodically, until very soft without browning. Reduce heat further if needed; add a small splash of water only if the bottom dries before vegetables soften.
3. **Simmer:** Add broth and bring to a gentle simmer. Cook **8–10 minutes**, or until potato is falling apart. Remove thyme stems and bay leaf.
4. **Blend:** Remove from heat and let active bubbling stop. Blend with an immersion blender, keeping its guard submerged and stopping before lifting it. Alternatively, cool to your countertop blender’s permitted temperature and blend in as many small batches as its hot-liquid fill limit requires, arranging the lid and steam vent exactly as its instructions specify. Start low; never blend hot soup in a sealed personal cup. Stop once smooth; strain if you want a finer texture, then return to the pot and warm gently. Taste for salt and pepper.
5. **Make the toppings:** In a small pan over medium, heat the **remaining butter** with the chestnuts, stirring often, about **4 minutes** until golden-brown butter flecks appear and smell nutty. Transfer promptly to a heatproof bowl. In another small bowl stir crème fraîche with all the maple syrup.
6. **Serve:** Divide soup into the intended portions. Distribute maple cream, chestnuts and their brown butter across the bowls.
