# Corpus usefulness audit

Audit date: 2026-09-10. Scope is the current source corpus under `src/content`, the immutable recipe/meal baselines, aliases, review registers, explicit technique maps, and review records. The scan used front matter and relationship fields first; it did not load the full recipe corpus into context, rerun the shared test/QA suites, browse, or claim cooking tests. Counts are source-state counts, not live-site usage telemetry.

## Coverage and denominators

There are 604 canonical recipe Markdown files: 602 food recipes plus two preserved craft records, `no-cook-playdough` and `play-dough`, which are excluded from food discovery by the editorial audit while their source pages remain retained. The immutable recipe baseline has 612 originals: 602 originals remain canonical, 10 have explicit recipe aliases, and 2 canonical food files are added recipes (`mint-tzatziki` and `turkey-pan-gravy`). The register records 223 of 612 originals as editorial-reviewed, 10 as consolidated after review, 379 pending, and 0 kitchen-tested. Both added recipes are editorial-reviewed. Evidence: [recipe review register](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/docs/recipe-review-register.json:2), [recipe aliases](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/src/data/recipe-aliases.json:2), and [craft exclusion rule](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/scripts/recipe-editorial.mjs:113).

The two redirects are explicit in [meal aliases](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/src/data/meal-aliases.json:2). There are 254 recipe review Markdown records and 50 meal review records; record-file count is not used as a substitute for register status.

## Findings and coverage observations

1. **Review coverage defines the current uncertainty — confirmed coverage state.** Only 223/612 original recipes (36.4%) are individually editorial-reviewed; 379/612 (62.0%) remain pending, and 0/612 are kitchen-tested. Meal coverage is 47/88 reviewed (53.4%), 39/88 pending, and 0/88 kitchen-tested. This is a known uncertainty boundary, not a claim that pending recipes are defective. Next action: continue bounded individual recipe and composed-meal review, prioritizing recipes used by pending meals; keep kitchen-test status separate. Evidence: [recipe-review-register.json:2](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/docs/recipe-review-register.json:2), [meal-review-register.json:2](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/docs/meal-review-register.json:2), [RECIPE_STANDARD.md:18](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/docs/RECIPE_STANDARD.md:18).

2. **Dietary metadata coverage varies — confirmed coverage observation.** 270/604 canonical recipes (44.7%) have a missing or empty dietary array, including explicit empty arrays on `thai-mango-salad` and `yakisoba-japanese-stir-fried-noodles`. A missing claim is not evidence that a recipe belongs to a dietary category or that a filter currently omits an eligible recipe. Role, difficulty, cuisine, and total-time fields are present for all 604; cooking methods are missing for one. Next action: review dietary claims during recipe work; retain `*-option` tags where alternatives are conditional. Evidence: [config.ts:50](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/src/content/config.ts:50), [corpus-metrics.json](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/docs/audits/2026-09-10/corpus-metrics.json).

3. **Meal composition is well connected but time planning is incomplete — medium severity, confirmed.** All 86 canonical meals have at least one component; the scan found 251 recipe references spanning 149 unique recipes and 0 orphan component references. However, 38/86 meals (44.2%) lack `totalTime`, and 39/88 baseline meals remain pending review. This supports browsing and reuse while limiting reliable planning comparisons. Next action: add or review elapsed and active workflow times as each meal is editorially reviewed. Evidence: [config.ts:168](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/src/content/config.ts:168), [corpus-metrics.json](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/docs/audits/2026-09-10/corpus-metrics.json), [meal-review-register.json:2](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/docs/meal-review-register.json:2).

4. **The recipe set is main-heavy, with few explicit base dependencies — confirmed distribution.** Roles are 352 main, 109 side, 59 dessert, 43 base, 22 drink, and 19 condiment. Pair suggestions are widespread (589/604 recipes), but only 17 recipes declare `usesBase`, with `master-sushi-rice` the most reused base at five references. These are user-value observations rather than defects: pair browsing is broad, while explicit component-building paths are less common. Next action: during review, add base relationships only where the recipe genuinely depends on a reusable component; do not bulk-fill links. Evidence: [corpus-metrics.json](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/docs/audits/2026-09-10/corpus-metrics.json), [config.ts:97](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/src/content/config.ts:97).

## Strengths

The corpus has complete role, difficulty, cuisine, and total-time presence; its role mix supports full meals as well as bases, sides, desserts, drinks, and condiments. Pair suggestions cover 589/604 canonical recipes, all 86 current meals resolve their component references, and explicit aliases preserve 10 recipe and 2 meal identities. The collection also records meaningful variation signals such as `usesBase`, `isVariationOf`, and conditional dietary options rather than forcing every relationship into a flat list. The household preference file gives concrete constraints for preserving the herb-forward cornbread dressing and mint identity of the standalone tzatziki, which should guide future review without being turned into invented global filters ([preferences](/Users/jsilton/Sites/mise/docs/HOUSEHOLD_PREFERENCES.md:3)).

## Limitations and next useful pass

This is a source inventory and bounded semantic screen. It does not establish recipe deliciousness, safety, cultural correctness, live usage, search ranking, or user preference beyond the recorded household guidance. Similarity scores are lexical signals and can miss conceptual duplicates or over-score shared dish names. Dietary screening intentionally treats alternatives and product composition as context questions. The next useful pass is to join pending-review recipes to the 39 pending meals and prioritize components with high reuse, while preserving the current aliases and family variations.

Integration note: the exploratory title-similarity calculation removes protein terms such as chicken, beef and pork, so identical normalized title scores can pair distinct dishes. These six comparisons do not establish comprehensive near-duplicate detection. Review status, missing dietary tags, role distribution and internal example-list gaps are coverage observations, not automatically defects.
