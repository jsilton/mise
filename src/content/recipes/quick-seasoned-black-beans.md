## Chef's Note

Canned black beans simmered in their own liquid with garlic, cumin and Mexican oregano. Mashing a few beans against the side of the pot thickens the liquid into a silky sauce while most stay whole. A squeeze of lime at the end wakes everything up.

## Directions

1. **Bloom the spices:** Heat the oil in a 2-quart saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant but still pale, 30 to 45 seconds. Stir in the cumin and cook for 15 seconds.
2. **Add the beans:** Pour in the beans with their liquid and crumble in the oregano. Turn the heat to medium and bring to a gentle simmer.
3. **Thicken:** Simmer uncovered, stirring often and mashing a few beans against the side of the pot, until the liquid lightly coats the beans, 8 to 12 minutes. Add a splash of water if they get too thick.
4. **Finish:** Take the pan off the heat and stir in the lime juice and pepper. Taste and add salt if it needs it.
