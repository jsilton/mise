## Chef's Note

These beans keep most of their shape, with a few mashed into the cooking liquid to give it body. Garlic and cumin warm briefly in olive oil before the cans go in. Add lime at the end and taste against the beans' existing salt before seasoning further.

## Directions

1. **Warm the aromatics:** Open the cans without draining. Heat oil in a heavy saucepan over medium-low. Add garlic and stir about 30–45 seconds until fragrant but pale. Stir in cumin for about 15 seconds, then promptly add the beans and all their liquid.
2. **Simmer:** Crush the oregano between your fingers into the pot. Stir and raise heat to medium until gently bubbling, then lower to maintain an uncovered simmer.
3. **Thicken lightly:** Simmer about 8–12 minutes, stirring along the bottom. Mash a few beans against the side of the pot, roughly a tenth of the batch, and stir them back in. Keep most beans whole. Continue until the liquid lightly coats them but they still spoon easily. If too dry, add water a small splash at a time; watery cans may need longer reduction.
4. **Finish:** Turn off heat, stir in lime juice and black pepper, and taste before adding any of the measured salt. Add more salt only if needed. Divide into four generous side portions; final volume depends on the cans and reduction. For a short wait cover off heat; for longer service keep hot at 140°F / 60°C or above, stirring and loosening as needed.
