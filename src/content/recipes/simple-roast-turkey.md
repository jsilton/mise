## Chef's Note

A day-ahead dry brine of salt, pepper and lemon zest seasons this turkey through and dries the skin so it roasts up crisp and brown. Hard cider, white wine, garlic and herbs in the pan perfume the bird and keep the drippings from scorching, which gives you a great start on gravy.

## Directions

1. **Dry-brine the turkey:** A day ahead, remove the neck and giblets and pat the turkey dry. Mix the salt, pepper and lemon zest. Rub it all over the bird, working some under the loosened skin of the breast and thighs. Set the turkey breast-side up on a rack over a rimmed pan and refrigerate, uncovered, for 24 hours. Refrigerate the zested lemon for the pan.
2. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 325°F with a rack in the lower third.
3. **Set up the pan:** Scatter the garlic, onion, lemon wedges, thyme, rosemary and sage in a roasting pan. Pour in the cider, wine and water. Set the turkey on the rack breast-side up, tuck the wing tips under and brush the skin with the melted butter. Leave the cavity empty.
4. **Roast:** Roast until the thickest part of the breast and the innermost thigh both read 165°F, 2 3/4 to 3 1/4 hours. Check the pan every hour and add hot water when it is nearly dry. Tent any spot that browns too fast with foil.
5. **Rest and carve:** Move the turkey to a carving board and let it rest for 30 minutes. Meanwhile, strain the pan juices and skim the fat to make [Turkey Pan Gravy](/mise/recipes/turkey-pan-gravy). Carve and serve.
