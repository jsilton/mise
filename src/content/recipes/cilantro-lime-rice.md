## Chef's Note

This is the rice behind burrito bowls and taco night. Jasmine rice cooks up fragrant and tender. The cilantro and lime go in after the rice rests, so they stay green and bright.

## Directions

1. **Simmer the rice:** Combine the rice, water and salt in a heavy 2-quart saucepan. Bring to a boil, then cover, turn the heat to low and simmer without lifting the lid until the water is absorbed, about 15 minutes.
2. **Rest covered:** Take the pan off the heat and leave it covered for 10 minutes so the grains finish tender. Chop the cilantro and zest and juice the lime while it rests.
3. **Fold in the cilantro and lime:** Fluff the rice with a fork. Gently fold in the oil, cilantro, lime zest and the smaller amount of lime juice. Taste and add more lime juice if it needs brightening.
