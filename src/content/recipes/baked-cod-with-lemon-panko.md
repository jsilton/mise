## Chef's Note

Lemon-garlic butter seasons the underside while buttery panko protects the top and adds crunch. Use one lemon for both zest and juice, and spoon the pan juices around the cooked fillets to keep the crumb topping crisp.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Heat the oven to **400°F / 205°C**. Pat the thawed fish dry, check for pin bones and arrange the portions in a shallow baking dish to check the fit. Zest the lemon, then juice it. Melt the butter.
2. **Make the base:** Mix **half the butter, all the lemon juice and half the garlic**, with salt and pepper to taste. Pour into the dish and set the fish on top in one layer. Season the tops lightly.
3. **Add the crumbs:** Mix panko with **the remaining butter and garlic**, lemon zest, paprika and parsley. Distribute over the fillets and press gently into an even layer without packing it into a thick mound.
4. **Bake and check:** Bake on the center rack, beginning to check at **10–12 minutes**; many portions take **12–20 minutes**, depending on thickness. Insert a thermometer from the side into the thickest center of each portion: aim for **145°F / 63°C**. The flesh should separate into opaque flakes. Remove thinner pieces as they finish. If the topping darkens too soon, shield it loosely with foil; do not keep fully cooked fish baking solely to darken pale crumbs.
5. **Serve:** Lift each fillet carefully onto a plate and spoon the lemon butter **around or beneath** it. Serve promptly with a vegetable and, if wanted, a separately prepared potato or rice side.
