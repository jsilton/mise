## Chef's Note

Lemon-garlic butter pools under the fish to keep it moist, and butter-tossed panko on top bakes into a golden crust. It is a fast, bright weeknight dinner that suits any mild white fish. Patting the cod completely dry keeps the crumbs crisp.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Zest the lemon, then juice it.
2. **Make the lemon butter:** In a 9x13-inch baking dish, stir together half the butter, all the lemon juice, half the garlic and a pinch each of salt and pepper. Lay the cod on top in a single layer and season the tops with salt and pepper.
3. **Top with crumbs:** In a small bowl, mix the panko with the rest of the butter and garlic, the lemon zest, paprika and parsley. Press it onto the fillets in an even layer.
4. **Bake:** Bake until the crumbs are golden and the fish is opaque and flakes easily in the thickest part, 12 to 15 minutes. Thick fillets can take up to 20 minutes.
5. **Serve:** Lift the fillets onto plates and spoon the lemon butter from the dish around them.
