## Chef's Note

A weeknight braise built on the Chinese pantry: hoisin, soy, garlic and ginger. The thighs cook low and slow until they pull apart, then their juices boil down in a saucepan into a glossy glaze. A splash of balsamic at the end sharpens the sweet hoisin.

## Directions

1. **Load the cooker:** In a 3- to 4-quart slow cooker, stir together the hoisin, soy sauce, tomato paste, water, garlic, ginger, pepper flakes and two-thirds of the balsamic. Add the chicken and turn to coat.
2. **Slow-cook:** Cover and cook on low for 4 to 5 hours or on high for 2 to 3 hours, until the thighs pull apart easily with a fork. Lift the chicken onto a platter.
3. **Reduce the sauce:** Pour the cooking liquid into a wide saucepan and skim off the fat. Boil over medium-high heat, stirring often, until glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon, 8 to 12 minutes. Add a splash of water if it turns sticky.
4. **Coat the chicken:** Pull the chicken into large chunks. Stir the rest of the balsamic into the sauce, then add the chicken and its juices. Fold over low heat until hot and coated, 2 to 3 minutes.
5. **Serve:** Taste for salt and scatter the scallions over the top.
