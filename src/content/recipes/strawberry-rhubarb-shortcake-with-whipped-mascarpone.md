## Chef's Note

Tall buttermilk shortcakes with a hint of pink peppercorn, piled with roasted strawberries and rhubarb and a soft mascarpone cream. Roasting the fruit with lemon, vanilla and elderflower turns its juices into a thick syrup that soaks into the biscuit without making it soggy. A hot oven gives the shortcakes a quick, high rise.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment, one of them rimmed for the fruit.
2. **Make the dough:** Whisk the flour, granulated sugar, baking powder, salt and pink peppercorns in a large bowl. Cut in the cold butter until the pieces are the size of peas. Stir in the buttermilk just until a shaggy dough forms.
3. **Shape and chill:** Turn the dough onto a floured counter and knead 3 times. Pat into a 6-by-9-inch rectangle about 1 inch thick and cut six 3-inch squares. Set them on the flat sheet and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
4. **Macerate the fruit:** While the dough chills, toss the strawberries and rhubarb with the lemon wheels and juice, sugar, elderflower liqueur, vanilla seeds and pod, and thyme sprigs. Let stand 10 to 15 minutes.
5. **Roast the fruit:** Spread the fruit on the rimmed sheet and roast until the rhubarb is tender and the juices are syrupy, about 20 minutes. Let it cool, then pick out the vanilla pod, thyme stems and lemon wheels.
6. **Bake the shortcakes:** Raise the oven to 425°F and let it heat fully. Brush the tops of the cold shortcakes with cream and sprinkle with the coarse sugar. Bake until risen, golden on top and browned underneath, 15 to 18 minutes.
7. **Whip the mascarpone cream:** While the shortcakes bake, beat the mascarpone with the sugar and vanilla until smooth. Slowly pour in the cream and whip just until it holds soft peaks. Stir in the elderflower liqueur and refrigerate until serving.
8. **Assemble:** Split the warm shortcakes. Spoon the fruit and its syrup over the bottoms, add a dollop of mascarpone cream and a few thyme leaves, and set the tops on.
