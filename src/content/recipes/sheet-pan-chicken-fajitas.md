## Chef's Note

These Tex-Mex chicken fajitas use the oven for the peppers, onion and chicken together. Two pans leave room for the filling to roast, though the vegetables will still release some juice. Add half the lime before cooking and the rest afterward; broiling is optional, and the skinless chicken does not need a crisp crust.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven and prepare toppings:** Heat the oven to 375°F / 190°C with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Lightly coat the two rimmed pans, or their foil liners, with the separate teaspoon of oil, dividing it equally. Chop cilantro and set out the tortillas; keep the avocado whole until near service.
2. **Cut and season:** Slice peppers into 1/4-inch-wide strips and onion into 1/4-inch slices, separating the layers. Pat chicken dry and cut across the grain into strips about 1/2 inch thick and 2–3 inches long; split very thick breast sections horizontally first. Mix all eight seasonings in the large bowl with the filling's tablespoon of oil and half the lime juice. Add chicken, peppers and onion and toss thoroughly. Roast as soon as the oven is ready, or cover and refrigerate briefly while it finishes heating.
3. **Load both pans:** Divide the filling evenly between the pans, keeping chicken strips separate and flat. Spread the vegetables around them in a loose layer rather than a heap. Each pan holds about half the chicken, peppers and onion. Wash the raw-food bowl, utensils and preparation surface before they touch cooked food or toppings.
4. **Roast and check:** Roast for about 20–25 minutes, turning the filling and exchanging the upper and lower pans after about 10 minutes. Begin checking chicken at 15 minutes. Insert a thin-tip thermometer sideways into the thickest part of several substantial strips on each pan; every checked strip must reach 165°F / 74°C. Continue cooking any unfinished pan and recheck. The peppers should be tender but still hold their shape. Transfer done chicken to a clean covered bowl if the vegetables need longer.
5. **Optional browning:** Once the chicken is safely cooked, remove it to the clean bowl if not already there. Leave the vegetables spread on their pans, spooning off a large pool of juice if necessary. Broil one pan at a time about 6–8 inches from the element for roughly 1–3 minutes, watching continuously for browned edges. Keep the other pan and chicken out of the broiler. Skip this step if the vegetables already have enough color. Recombine the hot filling and add the remaining lime juice.
6. **Warm tortillas:** While the finished filling is covered for this brief step, microwave the tortillas in stacks of four between lightly damp paper towels for about 30–45 seconds per stack, adding short bursts until hot and flexible. Follow the package if it gives a different method. Wrap the warm stack in a clean towel. For a skillet, warm one or two tortillas at a time in a dry pan over medium-high, about 10–30 seconds per side; allow several extra minutes for all twelve.
7. **Serve:** Slice the optional avocado. Divide the filling among twelve tortillas and add the cilantro and avocado, serving two fajitas per person. Offer any remaining filling alongside; do not overfill a tortilla until it tears. Offer salsa or hot sauce if desired. Rice, beans and guacamole have their own preparation times in the complete dinner plan.
