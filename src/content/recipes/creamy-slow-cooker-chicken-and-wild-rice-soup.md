## Chef's Note

Chicken and wild rice soup is a Minnesota classic, with nutty, chewy wild rice in a creamy broth. The slow cooker does the long simmer. At the end, brown the mushrooms in a skillet and build the cream sauce in the same pan, so the mushrooms taste roasted and the dairy stays silky.

## Directions

1. **Load the cooker:** Season the chicken with salt and pepper and put it in a 6-quart slow cooker. Add the stock, wild rice, onion, carrots, celery, garlic, thyme, rosemary and bay leaves.
2. **Slow-cook:** Cover and cook on low until the rice is tender with some grains split open, 6 to 8 hours. The chicken should reach 165°F.
3. **Shred the chicken:** Lift the chicken onto a board and shred it with two forks. Fish out and discard the bay leaves.
4. **Brown the mushrooms:** Melt the butter for the mushrooms in a large, deep skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring now and then, until their liquid has cooked off and they are well browned, 10 to 12 minutes.
5. **Make the sauce:** Lower the heat to medium and add the butter for the sauce. When it melts, stir in the flour and cook until lightly golden, about 1 minute. Slowly whisk in the milk and half-and-half and cook, whisking, until smooth and thickened, 4 to 5 minutes.
6. **Finish the soup:** Stir the chicken and the mushroom sauce into the slow cooker. Cover and cook until hot throughout, about 10 minutes. Loosen with more half-and-half if it is thick, season with salt and pepper and serve topped with parsley.
