## Chef's Note

Set out prepared grains, cooked proteins, vegetables and sauces so each person can build a bowl. Choose one grain and one or two cooked proteins rather than treating every alternative as one shopping total. Keep the full chosen supplies available and use bowls large enough for the toppings; store any unused prepared components cold. Children can choose from the same prepared components, with an adult managing hot food and any chopping.

## Directions

1. **Start the Base:** Divide the full listed supply of one cooked grain across the listed portions; the original four-bowl batch begins with about half a cup per bowl. Serve properly refrigerated grain cold, or reheat stored cooked grain to 165°F if serving hot. Do not leave a large batch on the counter waiting for room temperature.

2. **Add the Protein:** Place your chosen protein in a section of the bowl - soft-boiled eggs on one side, roasted chickpeas in a pile, grilled chicken strips arranged, or tofu cubes clustered. Divide the full chosen protein supplies across the portions, or keep unused prepared protein refrigerated. If serving soft-boiled eggs with undercooked yolks, use pasteurized-in-shell eggs; people at higher risk should choose thoroughly cooked eggs. No raw protein belongs in this assembly step.

3. **Layer the Vegetables:** Arrange vegetables in sections around the grain and protein - create little piles and lines so each component is visible. Start with softer items (greens, avocado), then add fresh vegetables (cucumber, tomato, pepper). Wash fresh produce under running water before preparing it. Use the separate lime-juice allowance on the avocado if wanted. Keep any unused prepared vegetables refrigerated.

4. **Add Crunch:** Scatter toasted seeds, crispy fried onions, wonton strips, or nuts over the top. Use the full listed seed supply and the separate unmeasured optional crunchy toppings as wanted. Keep unused toppings separate so they do not become wet.

5. **Dress:** Drizzle sauce over the entire bowl, or serve it on the side for people to control their own ratio. Use your separately prepared sauce as wanted; no sauce batch volume or equal yield across choices is assumed. Keep any unused perishable sauce refrigerated.

6. **Finish:** Top with fresh herbs (cilantro, basil, mint depending on your chosen direction) and sliced scallions. Use the full listed scallion supply, with the unmeasured herbs as wanted. Taste before serving and use only the separate optional salt or acid allowance if needed.

### Assembly Ideas (Mix and Match)

**Asian-Inspired:** Jasmine rice + shredded rotisserie chicken + cucumber + shredded red cabbage + scallions + sesame seeds + miso-ginger dressing

**Mediterranean:** Farro + soft-boiled eggs + arugula + roasted red pepper + cherry tomatoes + Kalamata olives + crumbled feta + lemon-herb vinaigrette

**Mexican-Forward:** Cilantro-lime rice + black beans + grilled chicken + sliced avocado + shredded red cabbage + radishes + cilantro + crispy fried onions + cilantro-lime crema

**Vegan:** Quinoa + roasted chickpeas + spinach + shredded carrot + cucumber + avocado + toasted pumpkin seeds + tahini sauce

### Batch Preparation

- Cook grains and proteins separately using their own complete recipes and package instructions; the 25-minute plan here covers assembly, not those cooking clocks.
- Cool cooked components promptly in shallow containers and refrigerate at 40°F or below. Use refrigerated cooked leftovers within 3–4 days, counting from the earliest preparation rather than restarting the clock when assembled; freeze portions for later.
- Keep roasted chickpeas refrigerated rather than assuming three days at room temperature. Their crispness after storage is a quality question; add them near serving.
- Wash and prepare vegetables ahead only with clean equipment and cold storage. Keep sauces cold and stir or shake before use.
- Assemble near service. Count preparation and serving together and discard perishable food left out over 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F.

The assembly ideas above use separately prepared optional olives, feta, black beans, radishes or pumpkin seeds where named; those amounts are unmeasured and do not replace a listed protein or sauce recipe.
