## Chef's Note

Keep the skins on ripe peaches for the source’s rosy color. Reserve all the drained peach syrup for the chilled custard and keep the fruit refrigerated until adding it. Gentle heat and constant stirring help the egg base stay smooth, but a double boiler does not prevent curdling by itself.

## Directions

1. **Prepare fruit:** Wash, pit and chop the skin-on peaches; measure the listed amount as chopped fruit, not a count of whole peaches. Mix with 2/5 of the total sugar and all the juice from the washed, listed lemon portion. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours.
2. **Heat milk:** Place all the milk in the top of a double boiler over gently simmering water and bring the milk just to a simmer.
3. **Temper eggs:** Whisk all the eggs and yolks with the remaining 3/5 sugar and the listed pinch of salt. Slowly whisk about 1/3 of the hot milk into this mixture, then return all of it to the remaining hot milk. The tempering milk is borrowed from the full listed milk, not extra milk.
4. **Cook custard:** Stir continuously over gentle heat until the custard coats the back of the spoon and reaches 160°F /71°C, checking the stirred mixture with a thermometer away from the bowl bottom. Start checking around 15 minutes rather than cooking for an exact duration. Do not boil the custard. No raw egg tasting.
5. **Cool and chill:** Transfer to a clean bowl and whisk in all the cream. Cool promptly, using shallow containers or a clean bowl in an ice-water bath while stirring; keep bath water out. Cover and refrigerate until thoroughly cold, allowing at least 4 hours as the current planning reference. Do not leave it on the counter for that chilling period.
6. **Drain and divide:** Drain the peaches into the cold custard, stirring in all the collected syrup. Return the fruit to the refrigerator. If the full base exceeds the actual ice-cream maker’s maximum fill, divide all the well-mixed base and fruit proportionally among churn batches, keeping unused portions refrigerated.
7. **Churn:** Churn the cold base according to the actual machine’s instructions. Add each batch’s allocated peaches near the finish if the machine permits, or fold them into the finished soft ice cream so the full fruit amount is used without overloading the machine.
8. **Firm:** Transfer to covered freezer-safe containers with expansion room and freeze about 2 hours or longer until the desired firmness. Serve promptly after scooping.
