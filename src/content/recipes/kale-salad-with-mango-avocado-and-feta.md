## Chef's Note

Kale provides a sturdy base for ripe mango, creamy avocado and salty feta. Rub the chopped leaves briefly with part of the measured oil, then add orange dressing gradually. Fold in avocado last and scatter seeds at serving for contrast. Adapted from [What A Girl Eats’ Kale Salad with Mango, Avocado and Feta](https://whatagirleats.com/kale-salad-mango-avocado-feta/).

## Directions

1. **Prepare kale:** Remove tough ribs, wash leaves, dry thoroughly and chop finely. Put in a wide bowl and add the oil labeled for softening kale with a small pinch of salt. Rub gently for about 1–2 minutes until darker and more flexible, stopping before the leaves become wet and limp.

2. **Prepare fruit and dressing:** Peel, pit and cube mango. Finely dice onion and crumble feta. Zest and juice the orange; whisk its juice and zest with the oil labeled for dressing, garlic, Dijon and honey. Taste and add salt and pepper as needed.

3. **Combine gradually:** Toss kale with about half the dressing, then gently fold in mango, onion and feta. Taste a leaf with fruit and cheese. Add more dressing only if needed for a light coating and reserve the rest.

4. **Finish:** Dice avocado just before serving and fold it in gently. Scatter with toasted seeds, divide among side plates and serve promptly.
