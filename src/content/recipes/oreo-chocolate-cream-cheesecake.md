## Chef's Note

This is Kraft's chocolate cheesecake, a Chocolate Creme Oreo crust under a dense chocolate filling with more cookies folded through and scattered on top. Beat the cream cheese completely smooth before the eggs go in, then mix slowly. Less air means a creamy cake with no cracks. Chill it overnight for clean slices.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the pan and cookies:** Heat the oven to 325°F with a rack in the middle. Oil the bottom and sides of a 9-inch springform pan. Finely crush half the cookies, filling and all, in a food processor. Cut the rest of the cookies into quarters.
2. **Bake the crust:** Stir the fine crumbs with the melted butter until evenly moistened. Press them firmly over the bottom of the pan. Set the pan on a rimmed baking sheet and bake for 10 minutes, then cool on a rack.
3. **Melt the chocolate:** Microwave the chocolate at half power in 30-second bursts, stirring after each, until just melted. Let it cool until lukewarm.
4. **Beat the cheese:** Beat the cream cheese, sugar and vanilla with an electric mixer on medium-low speed until completely smooth, 2 to 3 minutes, scraping the bowl often. Beat in the melted chocolate until the color is even.
5. **Add the eggs:** On low speed, add the eggs one at a time, mixing only until each one disappears. Scrape the bowl.
6. **Fill the pan:** Fold about two-thirds of the quartered cookies into the batter with a spatula. Spread it over the crust and scatter the rest of the cookies on top.
7. **Bake the cheesecake:** Bake on the baking sheet until the edges are set and the center few inches tremble together when you nudge the pan, 50 to 70 minutes. Start checking at 50 minutes.
8. **Cool:** After 10 minutes on a rack, run a thin knife around the edge of the cake. Let it cool on the rack for 1 hour.
9. **Chill:** Refrigerate the cake in the pan for at least 6 hours, or overnight, until cold and firm in the center.
10. **Slice:** Release the ring and leave the cake on the pan base. Cut with a sharp knife, wiping it clean between cuts.
