## Chef's Note

Sukju namul is the seasoned bean sprout side that shows up at almost every Korean meal. The sprouts cook just until tender, then get a simple dressing of sesame oil, garlic and scallion. Squeeze them well after cooling so the dressing coats them instead of turning watery.

## Directions

1. **Blanch the sprouts:** Bring the water and the salt for the water to a boil in a large pot. Add the sprouts, return to a boil and cook until translucent and tender with a slight crunch, 2 to 3 minutes.
2. **Cool and squeeze:** Drain the sprouts and rinse them under cold water until cool. Gently squeeze small handfuls to press out the water without crushing them.
3. **Season and serve:** In a bowl, stir the scallion, garlic, sesame oil and half the seasoning salt. Add the sprouts and sesame seeds and toss well. Taste, add the rest of the salt if it needs it and serve.
