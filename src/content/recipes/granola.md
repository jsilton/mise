## Chef's Note

This is the Gwock family's granola, made of oats, nuts, seeds and wheat germ toasted with maple syrup and cinnamon. Spread it in a single layer so it toasts evenly, and add the dried fruit only after the granola cools so the fruit stays soft and chewy.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Put the oats, the nuts and seeds and the wheat germ in a large bowl.
3. **Coat the oats:** In another bowl, mix the maple syrup, oil, cinnamon and vanilla. Pour it over the oats and stir until everything is coated.
4. **Bake:** Spread the granola in a single layer on a rimmed baking sheet. Bake, stirring a couple of times, until golden, about 20 minutes.
5. **Add the fruit:** Let the granola cool completely on the sheet, then stir in the dried fruit.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Cut the oil to 1/4 cup, or 1/3 cup for richer clusters. The granola comes out crisper and less greasy and is less likely to scorch.
- Add 1/2 tsp sea salt to the maple mixture to balance the sweetness.
- Start with 1/4 cup wheat germ, which suits this amount of oats.
