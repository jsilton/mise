## Chef's Note

Whisk the oil and maple mixture just before pouring, then toss until the oats, nuts and seeds are evenly coated. Spread a thin layer so the center can brown along with the edges. The granola firms as it cools; add the dried fruit only after cooling so it stays chewy.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Toss the oats, chopped nuts and seeds in a large bowl.
2. **Coat:** Whisk the oil, maple syrup, vanilla, cinnamon and salt together, then immediately pour over the dry mixture. Toss until everything is evenly coated.
3. **Spread:** Spread in a thin, even layer on a rimmed baking sheet. For a larger batch, use additional sheets or bake separate loads rather than making a deep layer.
4. **Bake:** Bake for 20–25 minutes, stirring about every 8 minutes and moving the edges toward the center. Watch the color toward the end; remove when golden throughout, before the edges scorch. Oven heating must be complete before loading.
5. **Cool and finish:** Let the granola cool completely on the sheet; it will firm as it cools. Stir in all the dried fruit.
6. **Store:** Transfer the fully cooled granola to a dry airtight container. Keep in a cool, dry place and follow the storage instructions for the nuts, seeds and dried fruit used.

## Cooking Notes

Dried blueberries may replace the entire listed dried-fruit amount. Canola and avocado oil are exclusive choices for the full listed oil amount. Choose sunflower or sesame seeds for the full seed amount; use the full mixed-nut amount as well.

One batch means the full ingredient set; measure the cooled volume if needed. Cooling takes additional time before packing. Keep the layer thin when scaling; extra sheets or successive loads extend elapsed time.
