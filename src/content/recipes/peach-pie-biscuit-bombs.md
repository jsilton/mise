## Chef's Note

These are peach pie tucked inside a flaky refrigerated biscuit, with a little melted Brie for richness. Brown butter makes them. It goes on before baking for color and again after for a nutty, glossy finish.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 375°F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
2. **Brown the butter:** Melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Swirl until the solids turn golden and smell nutty, 3 to 4 minutes, then pour it into a small bowl.
3. **Fill the biscuits:** Split each biscuit in half horizontally. Divide the peaches and Brie among the bottom halves, then sprinkle with the brown sugar and cinnamon and drizzle with the honey.
4. **Seal and brush:** Set the top halves on, pinch the edges firmly closed and roll each one gently into a ball. Arrange them 2 inches apart on the sheet and brush with half the brown butter.
5. **Bake:** Bake until deep golden and the dough around the filling is cooked through, 12 to 15 minutes. Cut one open to check.
6. **Finish and serve:** Brush with the rest of the brown butter and serve warm, while the Brie is soft.
