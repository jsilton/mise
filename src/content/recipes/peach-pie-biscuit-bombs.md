## Chef's Note

Split each biscuit horizontally, distribute all the peach and Brie filling among the pieces, then pinch the seams closed. Brown butter goes on before and after baking; reserve a clean portion for the finishing brush. A golden top is a useful color cue, but cut one open to check that the dough around the filling has baked through.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 375°F and line a baking sheet with parchment. The original eight-bomb batch uses a package containing eight large flaky biscuits. Keep the dough refrigerated until ready to shape and check its package handling and baking instructions.
2. **Brown the butter:** Melt all the listed butter over medium heat, stirring until its solids turn golden brown and smell nutty. Remove from the heat before the solids burn. Set aside a clean portion for the after-baking finish before using the rest on raw dough; use a clean brush for that finish.
3. **Fill:** Split each biscuit in half horizontally. Divide the full peaches, rind-free Brie, brown sugar, cinnamon and honey evenly among the bottom halves. The peach-piece and cheese-spoon counts vary with cutting size; use the complete listed amounts rather than stopping at a fixed number of pieces or spoonfuls.
4. **Seal and brush:** Replace the top halves, pinch all the edges closed and gently round each filled biscuit. Arrange about 2 inches apart and brush lightly with the pre-bake brown butter. Use extra sheets or separate loads to preserve that spacing when scaling.
5. **Bake:** Bake in the fully heated oven, beginning to check at 12 minutes; 12–15 minutes is a reference for the original biscuit type. Look for golden tops, then cut one open and check the dough around the filling for a baked crumb rather than a wet dough ring. If dough remains wet, continue baking, loosely shielding tops if needed; actual package and filled-dough geometry can require more time. Do not taste raw dough.
6. **Finish:** Brush with all the clean reserved brown butter and serve warm while the Brie is soft. Use a clean serving plate and utensil after checking doneness.

## Cooking Notes

Fresh peaches are the main filling. The same full listed cup amount may instead use drained canned peaches or mostly thawed frozen peaches; thaw in the refrigerator and drain excess liquid before measuring.

A separate Brie-and-honey option uses 3–4 oz Brie for the original eight-bomb batch, with the original honey drizzle optional up to 1 tbsp. Scale that full cheese range and optional honey allowance with the batch; do not add it to the main four-ounce cheese and full honey amounts. The complete biscuit, peach, brown-sugar, cinnamon and butter amounts stay the same. Extra moisture or a different biscuit product can change the filling seal and bake time.
