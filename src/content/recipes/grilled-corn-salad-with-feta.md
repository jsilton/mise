## Chef's Note

Grill the corn until lightly charred, then let it cool enough to handle before cutting off the kernels. Feta, onion and fresh herbs season the sweet corn without a heavy dressing.

## Directions

1. **Continue:** Set the grill to medium-high. Husk the corn. Grill 10 to 12 minutes, turning often, until charred in spots. Transfer to a cutting board and set aside to cool.

2. **Prepare:** Cut the kernels off the cobb.

3. **Continue:** Put the corn, red onion and cheese in a medium salad bowl. Drizzle with the olive oil and toss.

4. **Continue:** Add the fresh herbs and toss again. Taste and add salt and pepper. Serve.

5. **Continue:** Corn can be grilled 1 day ahead before assembling the salad. Can be eaten warm or cold.
