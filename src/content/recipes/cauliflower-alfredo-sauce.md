## Chef's Note

A lighter Alfredo from Chocolate Covered Katie: cauliflower simmered in milk blends into a silky sauce with real body. Garlic butter and Parmesan make it taste like comfort food instead of a vegetable swap. Simmer the cauliflower until it crushes with a fork, or the sauce will be gritty.

## Directions

1. **Simmer the cauliflower:** Put the cauliflower and milk in a small saucepan and bring to a boil over medium heat. Cover, lower the heat and simmer gently until the florets crush easily with a fork, 12 to 15 minutes. Stir now and then so the milk doesn't scorch.
2. **Cook the garlic:** While the cauliflower simmers, melt the butter in a small skillet over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant but not browned, about 1 minute.
3. **Blend the sauce:** Pour the cauliflower and milk into a blender. Add the garlic butter, Parmesan, salt and nutmeg. Blend until completely smooth, about 1 minute, with the lid's center cap off and a towel over the top to let steam out.
4. **Serve:** Toss with hot pasta right away, thinning the sauce with a splash of pasta water or milk if it's thick.
