## Chef's Note

This baked salmon-carp terrine modernizes traditional Passover gefilte fish. Pulse fish in food processor just until coarsely ground, not paste - this ensures meaty texture. Baking in a water bath (bain-marie) helps cream and eggs set into silky, souffle-like structure. Serve chilled with fresh horseradish salad for brightness.

## Directions

1.  **Sweat:** Heat 1 tbsp of the olive oil in a skillet. Sauté the onion for 8-10 minutes until soft and translucent (do not brown).
2.  **The Grind:** Pulse the carp and salmon in a food processor until coarsely ground. Transfer to a large bowl with the onions.
3.  **Bind:** Stir in the cream, matzo meal, dill, the measured salt and pepper, eggs, shredded carrot, lemon zest, and 1 tsp of the lemon juice. Whisk in 3/4 cup cold water until smooth.
4.  **Prep:** Preheat oven to 325°F. Line the bottom and sides of a 9x5 loaf pan with nonstick baking parchment rated for at least 325°F, leaving enough overhang to fold over the surface. Spread the fish mixture into the pan, fold the parchment over it, and cover the pan with foil. Keep the paper inside the covered pan; do not use wax paper or ordinary plastic wrap.
5.  **Bain-Marie:** Place the loaf pan inside a 9x13 baking dish. Fill the larger dish with boiling water until it reaches halfway up the loaf pan.
6.  **Bake:** Bake for 1 hour 15 minutes until the center is firm (160°F).
7.  **Set:** Remove from the water bath and cool briefly on a rack, then refrigerate promptly, within 2 hours of cooking (1 hour if the room is above 90°F / 32°C). **Chill for at least 4 hours**, until cold throughout, before inverting onto a platter and peeling away the parchment. Allow at least 5 hours 45 minutes for preparation, baking and chilling, plus cooling time; making it the day before gives more flexibility.
8.  **Finish:** Toss arugula, radishes, and horseradish with the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil and remaining lemon juice (2 tbsp plus 2 tsp), seasoning to taste. Serve the chilled slices topped with the fresh salad for the mandatory **Acid Balance**.
