## Chef's Note

Steam cooks the broccoli gently while a separate low-heat pan mellows the minced garlic. Check a thick stem rather than relying on green color or an exact minute count. Let excess water drain before dressing so the oil and seasoning cling. This moist, tender side differs from seared or oven-roasted broccoli.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the steamer:** Add water to a saucepan so it stays below the basket. Bring to a steady boil. Arrange prepared broccoli loosely in the basket; use a larger pot or two batches if tightly packed.
2. **Steam and check:** Cover and steam about 4–6 minutes. Check a thick stem with a small knife: it should yield with slight resistance. Extend briefly if still hard; do not wait for florets to fall apart.
3. **Warm garlic oil:** While broccoli steams, put oil and garlic in a small saucepan over low to medium-low heat. Warm about 1–2 minutes, watching continuously, until gently bubbling and fragrant but still pale. Remove from heat promptly.
4. **Drain and dress:** Lift the basket carefully and let water drain without shaking the delicate florets vigorously. Transfer broccoli to a warm bowl, add garlic oil, pepper and half the salt, then fold gently. Taste and add the remaining salt only if needed.
5. **Serve:** Divide into four small side portions and serve promptly. Keep the broccoli uncovered during a brief wait so trapped steam does not continue softening it.
