## Chef's Note

Plain steamed broccoli made worth eating. While the broccoli steams, minced garlic warms gently in olive oil until it turns soft and sweet, then everything gets tossed together with salt and pepper.

## Directions

1. **Steam the broccoli:** Bring an inch of water to a boil in a saucepan fitted with a steamer basket, keeping the water below the basket. Add the broccoli, cover and steam until a knife slides into a thick stem with a little resistance, 4 to 6 minutes.
2. **Warm the garlic oil:** While the broccoli steams, warm the olive oil and garlic in a small saucepan over medium-low heat until the garlic bubbles gently and smells fragrant but is still pale, 1 to 2 minutes. Take it off the heat.
3. **Dress and serve:** Lift out the basket and let the broccoli drain well. Toss it in a warm bowl with the garlic oil, salt and pepper and serve right away.
