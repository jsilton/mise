## Chef's Note

Pesto and ricotta sit beneath the tomatoes, while the cheese and full measured olive oil finish the folded pastry. Pat the tomatoes dry before layering, but expect some juice as they bake. Keep the butter cold and check the pastry base as well as the golden folded edge.

## Directions

1. **Make and chill dough:** Pulse all-purpose flour, cold butter, ice-cold water, granulated sugar, and sea salt as follows: combine flour, sugar and salt, then add all cold butter and pulse until crumbly with visible pieces. Add the listed ice water and pulse briefly until the dough holds when pressed. Form a disk, wrap tightly and chill for 30 minutes. Do not work the pastry until the butter disappears into a warm paste.
2. **Heat oven and dry tomatoes:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Slice medium heirloom tomatoes thinly, lay them on clean absorbent towels and pat the tops dry. Remove surface moisture without pressing the slices into pulp; the tomatoes will still release some juice during baking. Use a rimmed baking sheet and parchment rated for the oven temperature.
3. **Roll and fill:** Use all-purpose flour, whole-milk ricotta, and basil pesto for assembly. Before rolling or filling, decide the pan load and divide the full measured dough, pesto, ricotta and dried tomatoes proportionally among the galettes being made. For the original batch, roll one 12-inch circle with a 2-inch border; a circle up to 14 inches may have a border up to 3 inches. Leave enough pastry to fold and check that its allocated filling can fit before spreading pesto or ricotta. Transfer each circle to the lined sheet, spread its allocated pesto within the border, distribute its allocated ricotta and lay its allocated tomatoes on top. Keep every measured portion in the planned galettes; do not wait until all the dough is filled to decide on additional galettes.
4. **Fold and finish crust:** Fold the dough border over the tomato edges, pleating without stretching. Use sharp cheddar or Gruyère and extra-virgin olive oil to finish the border: brush all the measured oil onto the folded pastry, then sprinkle all the shredded chosen cheese over it. The optional serving drizzle is a separate allowance.
5. **Bake:** Bake at 425°F, beginning checks around 25–30 minutes for the original galette, until the pastry edges are golden and baked through and the tomatoes are hot and juicy. Lift an edge carefully to inspect the base; continue if pastry underneath is still pale and doughy, shielding an already-browned edge if needed. Keep additional galettes uncrowded and allow extra oven rounds as needed.
6. **Serve:** Serve warm with fresh chives and basil, flaky sea salt and chile flakes, extra-virgin olive oil (if using), and whole-milk ricotta (if using). Distribute the herbs and finishing seasoning; add the separate extra oil drizzle or ricotta dollops only if wanted. Keep the full measured oil and ricotta already used in the galette distinct from these optional extras.
