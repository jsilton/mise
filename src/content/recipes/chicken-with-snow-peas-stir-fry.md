## Chef's Note

Chicken with snow peas is a Cantonese home-style stir-fry. A quick coat of cornstarch, soy and oil, called velveting, keeps the sliced breast silky over high heat. Cook the chicken and peas separately, then bring them together in a light, glossy sauce so the peas stay bright and crisp.

## Directions

1. **Coat the chicken:** Toss the chicken with the cornstarch, soy sauce and oil for the coating. Refrigerate for 10 minutes while you get everything else ready.
2. **Mix the sauce:** Whisk the sauce ingredients together in a small bowl.
3. **Sear the chicken:** Heat most of the peanut oil in a wok or wide skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Spread the chicken in one layer and leave it for 1 minute. Stir-fry until the outside is no longer pink, 1 to 2 minutes more, then move it to a plate.
4. **Cook the snow peas:** Add the rest of the oil and stir the garlic for 15 seconds. Add the snow peas and stir-fry until bright green, 1 to 2 minutes. If they are still hard, add the water, cover for 30 to 60 seconds, then uncover and let the water cook off.
5. **Glaze the stir-fry:** Return the chicken and its juices to the pan. Stir the sauce to pick up the settled cornstarch and pour it in. Toss until the sauce bubbles and turns glossy and the chicken reaches 165°F, 1 to 2 minutes.
6. **Finish and serve:** Turn off the heat, stir in the sesame oil and serve right away.
