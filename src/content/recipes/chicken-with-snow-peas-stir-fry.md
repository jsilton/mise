## Chef's Note

A thin cornstarch coating gives the chicken a smooth surface; it cannot protect lean meat from unlimited high heat. Cook the chicken and snow peas separately so each reaches the right texture, then combine them briefly in sauce. This is a light main for four with rice, or one dish in a shared meal.

## Directions

1. **Coat chicken:** Mix the coating teaspoon of cornstarch, teaspoon of soy, and teaspoon of oil. Toss with chicken and refrigerate for 10 minutes while preparing the remaining ingredients.
2. **Mix sauce and trim peas:** Whisk all sauce ingredients together in a bowl. Trim snow peas and dry them well. Keep the finishing sesame oil separate.
3. **Cook chicken:** Heat 2 tsp of the stir-frying oil in a wide skillet or wok over medium-high heat until shimmering. Spread chicken in one layer, cooking in batches if needed. Leave for about 1 minute, then turn and stir for another 1–2 minutes. Transfer to a plate; it will finish in the sauce.
4. **Cook peas:** Add the remaining teaspoon of stir-frying oil. Stir garlic for 15 seconds, then add snow peas and stir-fry 1–2 minutes. If the pods remain hard, add the measured water and cover for 30–60 seconds. Uncover and let excess water evaporate; taste a pea for a crisp-tender bite.
5. **Glaze and verify:** Return chicken and any plate juices. Re-stir the sauce so settled starch is suspended, then pour it in. Bring to a simmer, tossing, until glossy, about 1–2 minutes. Check several thick chicken slices from the side with a thermometer, avoiding the pan; they must reach 165°F / 74°C. Cook longer if necessary, removing peas temporarily if they are already tender.
6. **Finish:** Turn off heat, stir in sesame oil, and serve promptly with plain rice.
