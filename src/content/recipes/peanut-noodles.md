## Chef's Note

These Chinese-American-style noodles can be served warm or chilled. Whisk the peanut sauce before dressing the noodles. Add the listed water gradually until the sauce coats rather than clumps; the amount depends partly on the peanut butter and noodle shape. Dress promptly for a warm meal, or refrigerate in shallow containers for a cold one.

## Directions

1. **Make sauce:** Whisk all the peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, main sesame oil, honey or brown sugar, garlic, ginger and red pepper flakes. Gradually whisk in water from the listed water range until smooth and thick enough to coat noodles; reserve any not yet needed for the toss. Taste if desired and use the separately listed additional-seasoning allowance only if needed.
2. **Cook noodles:** Bring a pot of water to a boil and salt from the allowance. Cook all the noodles according to their package until cooked but still firm. Drain well without rinsing. If preparing the noodles separately in advance, toss with the separately listed additional sesame oil; this is extra to the full sauce oil, not borrowed from it. Refrigerate promptly in a shallow container.
3. **Dress:** Toss all the noodles with all the sauce in a bowl with enough room to turn them. Use any reserved measured water first if the sauce clumps, then additional water from the genuine loosening allowance as needed. Do not leave cooked noodles at room temperature awaiting a later meal.
4. **Finish:** Wash and prepare the scallions, cilantro and lime. Add all the scallions and cilantro, sesame seeds and optional chopped roasted peanuts. Serve the lime wedges alongside. Optional washed carrots/cucumber, edamame cooked according to its package, or blanched broccoli can be added as toppings; keep them separate from the measured sauce formula.
5. **Cold service:** For a cold meal, refrigerate the dressed noodles promptly in shallow containers until chilled. Before serving, loosen as needed with a little additional water. Keep cold until serving rather than cooling on the counter for an unspecified period.
