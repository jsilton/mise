## Chef's Note

A fall grain salad of spiced roasted squash and crisp chickpeas over quinoa and tender massaged kale. A tahini dressing with apple cider and lemon ties it together. It holds up well, so it makes a good Sunday batch for weekday lunches.

## Directions

1. **Roast the squash and chickpeas:** Heat the oven to 450°F. On a sheet pan, toss the chickpeas and squash with the cumin, coriander, cinnamon, cayenne, most of the oil and half the salt. Spread in one layer and roast, stirring once, until the squash is tender and the chickpeas are crisp at the edges, 25 to 30 minutes.
2. **Cook the quinoa:** Bring the quinoa, water and a pinch of the salt to a boil in a small saucepan. Cover, lower the heat and simmer until the water is absorbed and the grains are tender, about 12 minutes. Let stand covered for 10 minutes, then fluff.
3. **Massage the kale:** Put the kale in a large bowl with the rest of the oil and a pinch of the salt. Squeeze and rub it with your hands until soft and dark green, about 2 minutes.
4. **Make the dressing:** Whisk the tahini, lemon juice, cider, maple syrup and the rest of the salt until creamy. Add a splash more cider if it is too thick to drizzle.
5. **Toss the salad:** Add the quinoa and half the dressing to the kale and toss well.
6. **Top and serve:** Top with the squash, chickpeas, pepitas and raisins. Pass the rest of the dressing at the table.
