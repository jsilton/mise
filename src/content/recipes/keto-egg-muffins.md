## Chef's Note

These are grab-and-go eggs for a low-carb week: cured chorizo and scallions in the bottom of each cup, eggs whisked with pesto, and sharp cheddar inside and on top. The chorizo is already cured, so it just warms and seasons the eggs. Pull them as soon as the centers set so they stay tender, not rubbery.

## Directions

1. **Fill the tin:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 12-cup muffin tin well, with no paper liners. Divide the chorizo and scallions among the cups.
2. **Whisk the eggs:** In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, pesto, salt and pepper until frothy and even. Stir in three-quarters of the cheddar.
3. **Fill the cups:** Pour the egg mixture into the cups until each is about three-quarters full, stirring between pours so the cheese stays mixed in. Sprinkle the rest of the cheddar on top.
4. **Bake:** Bake until the centers are set and the tops are lightly golden, 15 to 20 minutes.
5. **Cool and unmold:** Let the muffins cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then run a knife around each one and lift it out.
