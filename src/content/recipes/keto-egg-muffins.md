## Chef's Note

Use ready-to-eat air-dried Spanish chorizo or salami, not raw sausage. Finely diced meat and scallions distribute their flavor through the eggs, while pesto is whisked into the base. The cured meat is already prepared; it does not need to render from raw to create a crisp bottom.

## Directions

1. **Prep:** Preheat the oven to 350 °F. Grease standard muffin cups generously with the listed butter. The original batch uses about 12 cups; prepare more or fewer standard cups for different quantities, keeping the same fill depth. Use chorizo or salami labeled ready to eat, not a raw fresh sausage.

2. **The Base:** Divide the diced ready-to-eat chorizo or salami and scallions evenly among the prepared cups.

3. **The Eggs:** Whisk eggs with all the pesto and the salt and black pepper until blended without separate white streaks. No thick foam is needed.

4. **Fold:** Stir three-quarters of the shredded cheddar into the egg mixture; save one-quarter for topping.

5. **Fill:** Divide the egg mixture among the cups with about one-quarter headroom, stirring gently between pours to redistribute cheese. Sprinkle the reserved quarter of the cheese over the tops. Add extra cups if needed rather than overfilling.

6. **Bake:** Bake for about 15–20 minutes as a first range, until the centers are set with no liquid egg and reach 165 °F / 74 °C. Cup size and fill depth change the clock; check without touching the metal tin and continue baking if needed.

7. **Serve:** Let stand in the tin 5 minutes before loosening and removing. Refrigerate leftovers promptly, use within 3–4 days and reheat centers to 165 °F / 74 °C.
