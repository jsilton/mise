## Chef's Note

A bright Caribbean-style slaw made to sit next to jerk pork. Mango and lime cut through smoky, spicy meat, and a pinch of allspice ties the slaw to the jerk seasoning. Slice the cabbage as thin as you can so it softens a little in the dressing but keeps its crunch.

## Directions

1. **Whisk the dressing:** Whisk the lime juice, oil, honey, mustard, salt, pepper and allspice in a small bowl until well combined.
2. **Toss the slaw:** Put both cabbages, the mango, bell pepper, onion, cilantro and scallions in a large bowl. Pour the dressing over and toss gently from the bottom so the mango doesn't break.
3. **Rest and serve:** Cover and refrigerate until the cabbage bends but still crunches, 15 to 30 minutes. Toss again, taste for lime and salt, and serve with a slotted spoon.
