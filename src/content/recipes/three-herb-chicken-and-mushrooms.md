## Chef's Note

This Better Homes & Gardens dish is a simple take on the French bistro classic of chicken with mushrooms. Starting the thighs skin-side down in a cold pan renders the fat slowly for the crispest skin. Rosemary and thyme cook into the sauce, parsley goes in fresh at the end, and Dijon gives it a sharp, creamy finish.

## Directions

1. **Season the chicken:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Pat the chicken dry and season it all over with the chicken salt and the pepper.
2. **Brown the skin:** Place four thighs skin-side down in a cold 12-inch skillet and turn the heat to medium. Cook until the skin is deep golden and releases easily, 8 to 12 minutes. Flip for 1 minute, then move them skin-side up to a rimmed baking sheet. Repeat with the rest of the thighs, adding the oil only if the pan is dry.
3. **Roast:** Roast until the thighs reach at least 165°F away from the bone, 25 to 35 minutes. They are most tender at 175°F. Let them rest uncovered.
4. **Brown the mushrooms:** While the chicken roasts, pour off all but a thin layer of fat from the skillet. Add the mushrooms and the mushroom salt and cook over medium-high heat, stirring now and then, until their liquid cooks off and they brown, 8 to 10 minutes.
5. **Cook the herbs and flour:** Lower the heat to medium. Stir in the rosemary and thyme for 30 seconds, then sprinkle in the flour and stir for 1 minute.
6. **Reduce the wine:** Pour in the wine and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer until it has reduced by half, 2 to 3 minutes.
7. **Finish the sauce:** Stir in the broth and simmer gently until lightly thickened, 5 to 7 minutes. Turn the heat to low and stir in the Dijon and any juices from the resting chicken. Taste for salt, then turn off the heat and stir in the parsley.
8. **Serve:** Spoon the mushrooms and sauce around the chicken, leaving the skin uncovered so it stays crisp.
