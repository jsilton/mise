## Chef's Note

Mongolian beef is a Chinese-American restaurant dish with no real ties to Mongolia. Thin strips of flank steak get a light cornstarch coat and a hard sear in small batches, so the edges brown instead of steaming. The ginger and scallion glaze goes on at the very end, just long enough to coat the beef.

## Directions

1. **Coat the beef:** Pat the steak strips dry. Toss them with the cornstarch for the beef until every piece is coated, then shake off the loose excess.
2. **Mix the sauce:** Stir the soy sauce, water and brown sugar together until the sugar dissolves. In a small cup, stir the sauce cornstarch into the cold water.
3. **Sear the beef:** Heat a wok or large heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Add a third of the frying oil and a third of the beef in a single layer. Sear until browned, 1 to 2 minutes per side, then move to a plate. Repeat with the rest of the oil and beef.
4. **Cook the aromatics:** Lower the heat to medium. Pour off any excess oil and scrape out any burnt starch. Add the oil for the aromatics, the ginger and the chilies and stir for 20 seconds. Add the garlic and scallion whites and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
5. **Thicken the sauce:** Pour in the soy mixture and simmer for 1 minute. Stir the cornstarch slurry, add it and simmer until the sauce is glossy and lightly coats a spoon, about 30 seconds.
6. **Toss and serve:** Return the beef and its juices to the pan with the scallion greens. Toss over medium heat until the beef is coated and the greens just wilt, 30 to 60 seconds. Serve right away.
