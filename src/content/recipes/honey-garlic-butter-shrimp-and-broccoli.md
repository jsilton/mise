## Chef's Note

This is the richer honey-garlic version: butter, plenty of minced garlic and a generous sweet glaze. Cook the broccoli until its stems yield before coating it, and keep the cooked shrimp out while the sauce thickens. Adapted from [Wendie Williams’ Honey Garlic Butter Shrimp and Broccoli](https://butteryourbiscuit.com/honey-garlic-butter-shrimp-broccoli/).

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Pat shrimp dry and cut broccoli into small florets with thin stems. Whisk honey, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, pepper flakes and cornstarch in a bowl until no dry starch remains.

2. **Cook shrimp:** Melt half the butter over medium to medium-high without letting it darken. Add shrimp in a single layer; if necessary use batches sharing that butter allocation. Cook about 1–2 minutes on each side, continuing as needed until firm, pearly and opaque through the center; a fine-tip thermometer inserted into a thick shrimp should read 145°F / 63°C. Transfer to a clean plate.

3. **Cook broccoli:** Add the olive oil and broccoli. Sauté over medium heat about 5–7 minutes, turning, until a stem yields with slight resistance. If stems remain firm, add a small splash of water, cover briefly and check again; uncover to evaporate free water. Season lightly with pepper and only a little salt if needed, remembering the soy sauce. Transfer to a plate.

4. **Thicken glaze:** Lower heat to medium-low. Add remaining butter, stir the sauce bowl again and pour it in. Bring to a gentle simmer, stirring, about 1–2 minutes until glossy and lightly thickened. Do not boil hard or reduce to a sticky candy. Loosen with a little water if necessary.

5. **Combine:** Return cooked shrimp, broccoli and plate juices. Turn gently for about 30 seconds just to warm and coat. Taste before adding more salt or pepper. Serve promptly over separately prepared rice.
