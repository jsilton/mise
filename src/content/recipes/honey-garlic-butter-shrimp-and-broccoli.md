## Chef's Note

A Chinese-American weeknight stir-fry with shrimp and broccoli in a sweet, savory honey-garlic butter glaze. Sear the shrimp and broccoli separately and take them out of the pan, then thicken the glaze on its own. Everything goes back in only to coat, so the shrimp stay tender.

## Directions

1. **Mix the sauce:** Whisk the honey, soy sauce, red pepper flakes and cornstarch in a small bowl until no dry starch remains.
2. **Sear the shrimp:** Pat the shrimp dry and season lightly with salt and pepper. Melt half the butter in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp in a single layer and cook until pink and just opaque, 1 to 2 minutes per side. Move them to a plate.
3. **Cook the broccoli:** Add the olive oil and broccoli to the same pan over medium heat. Cook, turning, until bright green and the stems are tender-crisp, 5 to 7 minutes. If the stems are still hard, add a splash of water and cover for 1 minute. Move the broccoli to the plate.
4. **Bloom the garlic:** Lower the heat to medium-low and melt the rest of the butter. Add the garlic and ginger and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
5. **Thicken the glaze:** Stir the sauce again and pour it in. Simmer, stirring, until glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon, 1 to 2 minutes. Loosen with a splash of water if it gets sticky.
6. **Toss and serve:** Return the shrimp, broccoli and their juices to the pan. Toss for 30 seconds to coat and serve right away over rice.
