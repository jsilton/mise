## Chef's Note

This is Jordan Silton's Thai chicken curry: chicken, peppers and onion simmered in coconut milk with red curry paste, brown sugar and Cajun seasoning. Butter makes the sauce round and rich, and fresh Thai basil goes in at the very end.

## Directions

1. **Start the rice:** Cook the jasmine rice in a rice cooker.
2. **Mix the sauce:** In a bowl, whisk the coconut milk, brown sugar, Cajun seasoning and curry paste until the paste dissolves.
3. **Simmer the sauce:** Melt the butter in a wok over medium heat. Add the sauce and keep it at an active simmer for 3 minutes.
4. **Cook the chicken:** Add the chicken, peppers and onion. Return to an active simmer and cook until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are tender, about 15 minutes.
5. **Serve:** Stir in the basil and serve over the rice.

## Suggested Tweak

- Use boneless, skinless chicken thighs in place of the breasts. They stay juicy through the simmer.
- Fry the curry paste in 1 tbsp oil for 2 minutes before the coconut milk goes in, so it turns fragrant.
- Leave out the Cajun seasoning and stir in 1 tbsp fish sauce at the end.
- Simmer 4 to 5 torn makrut lime leaves with the coconut milk for the classic Thai red curry aroma. If you can't find them, stir in 1 tsp grated lime zest at the end.
- Add a drained can of sliced bamboo shoots with the chicken.
