## Chef's Note

Garlic and ginger are cooked gently before the sauce thickens. The starch gives a light coating, while soy and oyster or mushroom sauce supply most of the salt. Vinegar and sesame oil go in near the end for a clearer aroma.

## Directions

1. **Measure:** Mix broth, both soy sauces, oyster or mushroom sauce, wine, sugar and white pepper. In a separate cup, stir the cornstarch into the 2 tsp cold slurry water.
2. **Cook aromatics:** Warm neutral oil in a small saucepan over medium-low. Add garlic and ginger and stir for 30–45 seconds, until fragrant but still pale. Lower heat if the edges start coloring.
3. **Simmer and thicken:** Add the broth mixture and bring to a gentle simmer. Cook about 1 minute. Stir the slurry again and pour it in while stirring. Simmer 30–60 seconds, until lightly thickened with no chalky starch taste. Do not reduce to a sticky syrup.
4. **Finish and measure:** Take off heat; stir in vinegar and sesame oil. Carefully pour into a heatproof measuring jug. Add a little hot water, if needed, to reach 240 ml. If the sauce exceeds 240 ml, return it to the saucepan and simmer briefly to the stated yield, then measure again.
5. **Use or chill:** Use 120 ml as the starting portion for the linked 600 g garlic-eggplant recipe. For other cooked vegetables or protein, begin with a little sauce and add only enough to coat; their moisture and seasoning differ. Half this recipe makes the 120 ml needed for one eggplant batch. Chill unused sauce promptly in a shallow container.
