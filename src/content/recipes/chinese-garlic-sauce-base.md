## Chef's Note

This is the brown garlic sauce behind Chinese-American takeout favorites like broccoli in garlic sauce. Cooking the garlic and ginger gently in oil first gives it a rounder flavor than a raw mixed sauce. A spoonful of cornstarch makes it glossy enough to cling.

## Directions

1. **Mix the sauce:** Stir the broth, both soy sauces, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, sugar and white pepper together in a bowl. In a cup, stir the cornstarch into the cold water.
2. **Cook the aromatics:** Warm the oil in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and ginger and stir until fragrant but still pale, 30 to 45 seconds.
3. **Thicken the sauce:** Add the broth mixture and simmer gently for 1 minute. Stir the cornstarch mixture again and pour it in while stirring. Simmer until glossy and lightly thickened, 30 to 60 seconds. If it gets thicker than heavy cream, whisk in a splash of hot water.
4. **Finish the sauce:** Off the heat, stir in the rice vinegar and sesame oil. Use it warm, or cool it and chill.
