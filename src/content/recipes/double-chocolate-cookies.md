## Chef's Note

Dutch-process cocoa and a generous disc filling make these large cookies deeply chocolatey. Plan for the full 24–36-hour dough chill, then bake a few well-spaced portions at a time. Stop when the edges are set and the centers are still soft.

## Directions

1. **Dry mix:** Whisk the flour, Dutch-process cocoa, kosher salt, baking powder and baking soda until evenly mixed and free of lumps.

2. **Cream:** With a paddle attachment, beat the softened butter and both sugars until very light, about 5 minutes, scraping the bowl. Beat in the egg and vanilla until combined.

3. **Combine and chill:** On low speed, mix in the dry ingredients just until no dry pockets remain, then briefly mix in the chocolate discs. Press plastic wrap against the dough and refrigerate for 24–36 hours.

4. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a large baking sheet with parchment. Keep the remaining dough covered and refrigerated between loads.

5. **Portion:** Make balls slightly larger than golf balls, about 3 1/2 oz each. The original batch makes roughly 9–10 cookies. Put no more than 5 of these large portions on the original-size sheet, with ample room to spread; use fewer if the actual sheet cannot provide that space. Scaling changes the amount of dough and number of loads, not the portion size or pan capacity.

6. **Bake:** Once the oven reaches 350°F, bake for about 18 minutes, until the edges are set and the centers remain slightly soft. Bake the rest in further loads as needed.

7. **Cool:** Let the cookies firm on the sheet for about 10 minutes, then carefully transfer them to a rack. Let the sheet cool before loading more dough. Serve warm once firm enough to lift, or cool completely before packing.

## Cooking Notes

For the same listed chocolate volume, choose semisweet or bittersweet discs. For the original batch, the discs are 2 cups / 305 g. Alternatively, replace the discs with chocolate chips: the original batch uses 2 cups / 340 g. When scaling, scale that chosen alternative’s volume and weight together; buy and use one option. Chips and discs will distribute differently.

For rack cooling straight from the oven, move the whole parchment sheet with the baked cookies onto a rack instead of leaving them on the pan for 10 minutes. Let them firm there before lifting individual cookies.
