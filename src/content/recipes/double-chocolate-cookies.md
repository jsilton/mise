## Chef's Note

These huge, dark cookies are Samantha Seneviratne's, made with Dutch-process cocoa and chocolate discs that melt into wide pools. The secret is a full day in the fridge. The long rest deepens the flavor into something close to toffee and gives the cookies soft, fudgy centers.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Whisk the flour, cocoa, salt, baking powder and baking soda in a medium bowl until no lumps remain.
2. **Cream the butter:** In a stand mixer with the paddle, beat the butter and both sugars on medium-high until very light and fluffy, about 5 minutes, scraping the bowl. Beat in the egg and vanilla.
3. **Mix the dough:** On low speed, add the dry ingredients and mix just until no dry streaks remain. Mix in the chocolate discs.
4. **Chill the dough:** Press plastic wrap onto the surface of the dough and refrigerate for 24 to 36 hours.
5. **Shape the cookies:** Heat the oven to 350°F and line a large baking sheet with parchment. Roll the cold dough into balls a little larger than golf balls. Set 5 on the sheet, well apart, and keep the rest of the dough in the fridge.
6. **Bake:** Bake until the edges are set and the centers still look slightly soft, about 18 minutes.
7. **Cool and repeat:** Let the cookies firm up on the sheet for 10 minutes, then move them to a rack. Let the sheet cool, then bake the rest of the dough.
