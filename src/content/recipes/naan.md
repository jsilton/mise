## Chef's Note

Meera Sodha learned this naan from her aunt Harsha. There's no tandoor, just a hot, dry skillet. Yogurt and milk make the dough soft and a little tangy, and the bread puffs into brown-spotted bubbles in under two minutes. Roll and cook in a steady rhythm, and keep the finished breads wrapped in a towel so they stay supple.

## Directions

1. **Proof the yeast:** Stir the sugar and yeast into the warm milk. Let stand until foamy, 5 to 10 minutes.
2. **Mix the dough:** In a large bowl, whisk the flour, baking powder and salt. Add the yeast milk, yogurt and oil and mix until no dry flour remains.
3. **Knead:** Turn the dough onto a floured board and knead until smooth and elastic, 5 to 8 minutes. It should stay soft and a little tacky. If it feels stiff, knead in a splash more milk.
4. **Let it rise:** Rub the dough with the oil for the bowl and put it in a bowl. Cover and leave in a warm spot until doubled, 60 to 90 minutes.
5. **Divide the dough:** Punch the dough down and cut it into 12 pieces. Roll each into a ball, cover and rest for 10 minutes.
6. **Make the garlic butter:** If using, melt the butter with the garlic in a small pan over low heat until fragrant but not browned, about 1 minute.
7. **Heat the pan:** Heat a dry 12-inch cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat for 5 minutes.
8. **Roll the naan:** On a lightly floured surface, roll a ball into an oval about 5 by 8 inches and 1/8 inch thick. Keep the other balls covered.
9. **Cook the naan:** Brush off the loose flour and lay the oval in the hot pan. Cook until bubbles rise and the underside has brown patches, 30 to 45 seconds. Flip and cook 30 to 45 seconds more, then flip again and press with a spatula for 10 to 15 seconds. Turn the heat down a little if the spots blacken before the center cooks.
10. **Brush and wrap:** Brush the hot naan with the garlic butter, if using, and wrap it in a clean towel. Roll and cook the rest the same way.
