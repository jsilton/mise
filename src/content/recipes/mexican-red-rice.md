## Chef's Note

Arroz rojo is the red rice served beside nearly every Mexican plate, from enchiladas to tacos. Toast the rinsed rice in oil until golden and nutty before the tomato goes in. That toasting keeps the grains fluffy and separate.

## Directions

1. **Rinse the rice:** Rinse the rice in several changes of cool water until the water runs clear. Drain it well in a fine-mesh strainer and shake out as much water as you can.
2. **Toast the rice:** Warm the oil in a heavy saucepan over medium heat. Add the rice and stir constantly until the grains are opaque, lightly golden and smell nutty, 3 to 5 minutes.
3. **Add the aromatics:** Stir in the onion and garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute, without browning the garlic.
4. **Add the liquid:** Stir in the tomatoes, broth and salt and bring to a boil, scraping up any grains stuck to the bottom.
5. **Simmer the rice:** Lay the cilantro sprig on top. Cover tightly, turn the heat to low and simmer without lifting the lid until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is tender, 15 to 18 minutes.
6. **Rest and fluff:** Take the pan off the heat and leave it covered for 10 minutes. Pull out the cilantro and fluff the rice with a fork.
