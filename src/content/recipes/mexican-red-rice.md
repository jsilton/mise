## Chef's Note

Toasting brings a lightly nutty flavor to this tomato rice. It does not seal the grains against water: the rice still needs enough liquid and gentle cooking to soften. Measure the tomato, drain the rinsed rice well, and leave time for the covered rest before serving.

## Directions

1. **Rinse and drain:** Rinse the dry rice in several changes of cool water until less cloudy. Drain thoroughly in a fine-mesh strainer, shaking out excess water while you dice the onion and mince the garlic.
2. **Toast:** Warm the oil in a heavy saucepan over medium heat. Carefully add the drained rice; residual water can spatter. Stir constantly for about 3–5 minutes, until the grains look opaque and lightly golden and smell nutty. Lower the heat if individual grains darken quickly.
3. **Add aromatics:** Stir in the onion and garlic. Cook for about 1 minute, stirring, until fragrant without browning the garlic.
4. **Add liquid:** Carefully stir in the measured tomato puree, broth and salt. Bring to a gentle boil, stirring once to distribute the rice and release any grains stuck to the base.
5. **Simmer and check:** Place the cilantro sprig on top. Cover tightly and reduce to a low simmer. Cook about 15–18 minutes without repeatedly opening the lid. Taste a grain: it should have no hard center, and free liquid should be absorbed. If firm and dry, add a little hot water and continue covered; if firm with liquid remaining, continue gently without adding more.
6. **Rest:** Remove from heat once tender and leave covered for 10 minutes.
7. **Finish:** Remove the cilantro sprig. Fluff gently with a fork and divide into four side portions.
