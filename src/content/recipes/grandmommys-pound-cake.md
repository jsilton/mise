## Chef's Note

Lemon, almond and vanilla give this rich pound cake its familiar flavor. Cream the softened butter and sugar until light, then mix the flour gently. The slow large-cake bake and the warmer loaf bake are separate routes; let the center of each cake determine when it is ready.

## Directions

1. **Choose the pans and oven route:** For one large cake, heat the oven to 275°F and use a roomy tube or Bundt pan. For the three-loaf route, heat the oven to 300°F and use three loaf pans for the original batch. Prepare every pan with baking pan spray and additional flour, reaching corners and the center tube. Have an extra small cake pan ready if needed; the batter must not fill any pan more than two-thirds full.
2. **Cream the butter and sugar:** Beat unsalted butter and granulated sugar at medium speed until noticeably paler and fluffy, scraping the sides and bottom of the bowl several times. Start checking after about 5 minutes; the texture matters more than a fixed volume increase. The butter should hold air rather than look oily or melted.
3. **Add the eggs gradually:** Add large eggs one at a time at medium-low speed, allowing each to incorporate before adding the next. Scrape the bowl between additions.
4. **Add the flour gently:** At low speed, mix in all-purpose flour in several small additions. Stop as soon as the flour disappears, then check the bottom of the bowl for unmixed streaks.
5. **Add all three extracts:** Fold in lemon extract, almond extract, and vanilla extract by hand until evenly distributed.
6. **Fill with room to rise:** Scrape the batter into the prepared pan or divide it evenly among the loaf pans. Smooth the tops. Keep the batter level at or below two-thirds of each pan’s depth; if it exceeds that level, put the excess in the prepared extra pan rather than packing the main pan. A shallower loaf will bake more quickly than a deeper one.
7. **Bake by the center:** Bake a large cake at 275°F, using 90 minutes as the starting plan and beginning checks around 75 minutes. Bake the loaf route at 300°F, using 60 minutes as the starting plan and beginning checks around 45 minutes if the loaves are shallow. Keep the chosen temperature fixed as ingredient quantities change. Continue until the cake is set, the top springs back under a light touch, and a long tester or thin knife inserted into the thickest crumb in several places comes out without wet batter. Avoid the center tube, pan and surface cracks when checking. Tent loosely with foil if the top darkens before the middle finishes. Check individual loaves separately and remove each as it finishes. Start checking small overflow cakes earlier; their shallower depth can shorten the bake considerably.
8. **Cool before slicing or wrapping:** Rest in the pan on a rack for about 20 minutes. Loosen accessible edges, turn out carefully and cool completely on the rack before slicing or wrapping. If the cake is still fragile, let it settle a few minutes longer before turning it out.
