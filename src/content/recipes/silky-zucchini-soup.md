## Chef's Note

Grant Achatz's zucchini soup gets its silky body from the squash itself, with no cream. Cook the onion and zucchini slowly in butter and olive oil without letting them brown, so the soup stays bright green and sweet. Raw zucchini matchsticks on top add a fresh crunch.

## Directions

1. **Soften the onion:** Melt the butter with the olive oil in a 3-quart saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the onion, garlic and a pinch of salt and white pepper. Cook, stirring, until soft and translucent but not browned, 7 to 8 minutes.
2. **Sweat the zucchini:** Add the sliced zucchini and cook, stirring often, until soft, about 10 minutes. Lower the heat if it starts to brown.
3. **Simmer:** Add the stock and water and bring to a gentle simmer. Cook until the zucchini crushes easily against the side of the pot, about 10 minutes.
4. **Blend:** Take the pot off the heat and blend with an immersion blender until completely smooth.
5. **Season and serve:** Stir in the lemon juice and season with salt and white pepper. Ladle into bowls and top with the zucchini matchsticks and a drizzle of olive oil.
