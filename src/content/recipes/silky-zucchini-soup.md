## Chef's Note

Butter and olive oil soften the onion and zucchini before blending; no cream is needed. Cook until the squash crushes easily, then blend to the texture your appliance can achieve. Add the measured lemon at the end and keep raw matchsticks separate for a crisp garnish.

## Directions

1. **Soften onion:** Melt butter with olive oil in a saucepan over medium-low. Add onion and garlic with a little salt and white pepper. Cook about **7–8 minutes**, stirring, until soft without browning.
2. **Cook zucchini:** Add the measured sliced zucchini and cook about **10 minutes**, stirring frequently, until softened. Lower heat if the bottom starts browning.
3. **Simmer:** Add stock and all the measured water. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook about **10 minutes**, until zucchini crushes readily with a spoon. While it cooks, wash and finely cut the additional raw garnish zucchini.
4. **Blend:** Remove from heat and let active bubbling stop. Blend with an immersion blender, keeping its guard submerged and stopping before lifting it. Alternatively, cool to your countertop blender’s permitted temperature and blend in as many small batches as its hot-liquid fill limit requires, with the lid and steam vent arranged exactly as its instructions specify. Start low; do not use a sealed personal cup for hot soup.
5. **Finish and serve:** Stir in the measured lemon juice, then taste for salt and white pepper. Warm gently if needed and divide into the intended soup portions. Add raw zucchini matchsticks just before serving.
