## Chef's Note

Adapted from Michele Rosen’s coconut-milk Zuppa Toscana, an American restaurant-style sausage, potato and kale soup. Keep all the rendered sausage fat and the full-fat coconut milk for its rich broth. Add the garlic after the onion softens and the kale near the end; potato and leaf tenderness matter more than an exact minute.

## Directions

1. **Render sausage:** In a Dutch oven or soup pot with enough room for the broth, potatoes and remaining supplies plus stirring headroom, brown raw hot or sweet bulk Italian sausage over medium heat, breaking up lumps. Start checking around 8 minutes and continue until cooked: ground pork/beef sausage reaches 160°F / 71°C; poultry sausage reaches 165°F / 74°C. Transfer the cooked sausage to a clean plate, leaving all the rendered fat in the pot. Divide every supply proportionally among enough pots or complete batches if the load will not fit.
2. **Foundation:** Use garlic cloves, yellow onion, and red pepper flakes for this stage. In all the retained sausage fat, cook the onion about 4–5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and cook 30–45 seconds until fragrant, then stir in all the measured flakes.
3. **Simmer potatoes:** Add Russet potatoes, prepared chicken bone broth, sea salt, and black pepper and bring to a boil. Reduce to a simmer and begin checking the potato cubes after 10–12 minutes; continue until a fork passes through their centers easily. Keep all the measured broth; tender potatoes, rather than a fixed clock, govern the finish.
4. **Coconut finish:** Stir in full-fat coconut milk and return all the cooked sausage. Heat gently for about 5 minutes, stirring until evenly hot; retain all the coconut milk and sausage fat.
5. **Wilt kale:** Add fresh kale. Begin checking after about 1 minute and continue until the leaves are wilted and tender enough to eat; tougher leaves need longer.
6. **Serve:** Taste the cooked soup and use additional sea salt (if using), additional black pepper (if using), and olive oil (if using) as desired. Ladle into bowls; the soup thickens further as it stands.

## Peeled-potato variation

For the peeled-potato version, replace the main potatoes with two large Russets, about 1½ lb in the original batch, peeled and cut into ½-inch cubes; scale both the count and approximate weight with the batch. Replace the listed measured pepper-flake amount with an unmeasured to-taste allowance, sprinkled on the sausage while browning. Keep the full original proportions of sausage, onion, garlic, broth, kale and coconut milk, and omit the optional finishing-oil drizzle. Fully cook the sausage as in step 1 and retain its fat; cook the onion until translucent, then garlic for about 45 seconds. Add potatoes, salt and pepper, then broth; start checking potato tenderness around 10 minutes after it returns to a simmer. Add the full coconut milk and sausage for about 5 minutes, then wilt the kale and season after cooking.

Mushrooms or cauliflower instead of potatoes were also named options, with instructions to proceed to the coconut finish after the broth boils. Their replacement quantities and complete cooking endpoints were not supplied; settle those before using that branch as a formula.
