## Chef's Note

These brownies have dense, fudgy centers, chewy edges, and a noticeable olive-oil flavor. Keep the 8-inch square pan: a wider pan makes a thinner layer that bakes faster. Fold only until no dry flour remains, and cool completely before cutting so the soft center has time to firm up. Budget about 2 hours 55 minutes, including at least 2 hours of cooling; a warm pan may need longer.

## Directions

1.  **Prep:** Preheat oven to 325°F. Spray an 8x8 baking pan and line with parchment paper so it hangs over the edges. Spray the parchment too.
2.  **Dry Mix:** In a medium bowl, whisk together the sugar, flour, cocoa, powdered sugar, chocolate chips, and salt.
3.  **Wet Mix:** In a large bowl, whisk eggs, olive oil, water, and vanilla until well combined and slightly frothy.
4.  **Combine:** Sprinkle the dry ingredients over the wet. Fold gently with a spatula until **just combined**. The batter will be very thick.
5.  **Bake:** Spread evenly in the prepared pan. Bake for 40-45 minutes until a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs.
6.  **The Finish:** Allow to cool completely in the pan for at least 2 hours before slicing. This is mandatory for the structure to set.

## Variations

Use canola oil in place of the same listed olive-oil amount for a more neutral flavor. For a separate less-sweet version of this fixed batch, use 1 cup granulated sugar instead of 1½ cups; keep every other quantity, the 8-inch square pan and the method unchanged. Keep the moist-crumb cue and complete cooling; texture and baking time may differ.
