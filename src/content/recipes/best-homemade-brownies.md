## Chef's Note

These fudgy brownies come from Michelle Lopez's Weeknight Baking by way of Love and Lemons. Extra-virgin olive oil gives them a fruity depth that suits dark chocolate, and powdered sugar in the dry mix bakes into a thin, crackly top. Let them cool completely so the center sets.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the pan:** Heat the oven to 325°F. Spray an 8-inch square pan, line it with parchment that hangs over two sides and spray the parchment.
2. **Mix the dry ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk the granulated sugar, flour, cocoa, powdered sugar, chocolate chips and salt.
3. **Mix the wet ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, olive oil, water and vanilla until slightly frothy.
4. **Fold the batter:** Sprinkle the dry mixture over the wet and fold with a spatula until no dry streaks remain. The batter will be very thick.
5. **Bake:** Spread the batter evenly in the pan. Bake until a toothpick in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs, 40 to 45 minutes.
6. **Cool and cut:** Cool completely in the pan, at least 2 hours, then lift out by the parchment and cut into squares.
