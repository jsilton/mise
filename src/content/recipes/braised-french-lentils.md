## Chef's Note

French green lentils suit a side dish where the grains should remain distinct. Simmer gently and taste several grains: their centers should be tender rather than hard, even if the skins hold their shape. Stir in mustard and vinegar once the lentils are tender, then fold in parsley before serving.

## Directions

1. **Soften vegetables:** Heat Olive oil, Medium onion, Medium carrots, and Celery stalks in a Dutch oven or heavy saucepan over medium heat, adding the vegetables once the oil is warm. Cook, stirring, about 6–7 minutes, until onion is translucent and the diced vegetables soften; allow longer as needed.
2. **Add garlic:** Stir in Garlic cloves and cook about 1 minute until fragrant, without darkening it.
3. **Bring to a simmer:** Add Dry French green lentils, Fresh thyme sprigs, Bay leaf, and Low-sodium vegetable or chicken broth, stir, and bring to a boil. Reduce promptly to a gentle simmer and partially cover.
4. **Check centers and liquid:** Simmer, stirring occasionally, beginning checks after 25–30 minutes. Taste several lentils: the centers should be tender and creamy with no hard core while the grains hold their shape. Have Water ready if evaporation exposes drying lentils before their centers soften; do not keep cooking a dry pan just to meet the timer. Most broth should be absorbed, leaving moist lentils rather than scorched grains.
5. **Finish and serve:** Remove all thyme stems and bay leaves. Stir in Dijon mustard, Red wine vinegar, Fresh parsley, Kosher salt, and Black pepper, folding the parsley through just before serving. Use the listed kosher salt; different crystal types need different spoon volumes.
