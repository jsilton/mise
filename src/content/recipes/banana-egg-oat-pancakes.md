## Chef's Note

Banana, eggs and oats make a flourless pancake with a soft, almost crepe-like center. The banana sweetens the batter and helps it brown. Keep the heat at medium-low and let the first side set before you flip, so the egg cooks through without scorching.

## Directions

1. **Make the batter:** Mash the banana in a medium bowl with a fork until almost smooth. Whisk in the eggs, oats, nut butter, cinnamon and salt until well combined.
2. **Heat the pan:** Heat a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat and add a little butter or coconut oil to coat it.
3. **Cook the first side:** Spoon the batter into 3-inch circles, leaving room to flip. Scatter a few chocolate chips on top, if using. Cook without moving them until the edges are set and the bottoms are golden, 2 to 3 minutes.
4. **Flip and finish:** Slide a thin spatula fully under each pancake and flip it gently. Cook until the center is set, about 1 minute more. Repeat with the rest of the batter, adding more fat to the pan as needed.
5. **Serve:** Serve hot with fresh fruit and maple syrup.
