## Chef's Note

Mash the ripe banana well, then whisk in the eggs and rolled oats. These small pancakes are delicate: let the first side set before moving them and use medium-low heat so the banana browns while the egg cooks through.

## Directions

1. **Mash:** In a medium bowl, mash the banana with a fork until mostly smooth.
2. **Mix:** Whisk in the eggs, rolled oats, nut butter, cinnamon, and salt until evenly combined. Small oat flakes may remain; this batter is mixed by hand rather than blended.
3. **Heat:** Heat a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat. Add enough of the separate butter or coconut oil allowance to lightly coat the surface.
4. **Cook the first side:** Spoon small, roughly 3-inch circles into the pan, leaving room to flip. Add a few chocolate chips to the wet surface if using. Leave the pancakes undisturbed until the edges are set and the underside is golden, about 2–3 minutes. If the bottom darkens while the top is still loose, lower the heat; do not wait for bubbles as the only signal.
5. **Flip and finish:** Slide a thin spatula fully underneath each pancake and flip gently. Cook for about 30–60 seconds more, then continue as needed until the center is set with no liquid egg batter. A thin thermometer inserted from the side should read 160°F. Repeat with the remaining batter, refreshing the light coating of butter or coconut oil as needed.
6. **Serve:** Serve hot with fresh fruit and maple syrup if desired.
