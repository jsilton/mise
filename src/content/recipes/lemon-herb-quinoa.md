## Chef's Note

Quinoa is only bland when it's rushed. Rinse off its bitter coating, toast the grains in the dry pan until nutty, and dress them with lemon, olive oil and three fresh herbs after cooking so everything stays bright. It works under grilled fish, chicken or roasted vegetables.

## Directions

1. **Rinse the quinoa:** Rinse the quinoa in a fine-mesh strainer under cold running water for 30 seconds, rubbing the grains with your fingers. Shake it well to drain.
2. **Toast the quinoa:** Put the wet quinoa in a dry 3-quart saucepan over medium heat. Stir until the water cooks off and the grains smell nutty and start to pop, 3 to 5 minutes.
3. **Simmer:** Add the water or broth and salt and bring to a boil over high heat. Cover, lower the heat to the lowest simmer and cook without lifting the lid until the liquid is absorbed and the grains are tender, 15 minutes.
4. **Rest:** Take the pan off the heat and leave it covered for 5 minutes.
5. **Dress and serve:** Fluff with a fork. Fold in the oil, lemon juice, zest, parsley, mint, dill and pepper. Taste for salt and serve warm, at room temperature or cold.
