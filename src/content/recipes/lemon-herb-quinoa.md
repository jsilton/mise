## Chef's Note

Lemon, parsley, mint and dill give this quinoa a fresh finish alongside grilled fish, chicken or roasted vegetables. Keep the lid on during most of the simmer, then check both the grain and the pan: absorbed liquid alone does not mean the center is tender. The five-minute rest comes before the olive oil, lemon and herbs.

## Directions

1. **Rinse and drain:** Rinse the dry quinoa in a fine-mesh strainer under cool running water for about 30 seconds, then shake and drain thoroughly. Follow any specific preparation directions on the package.
2. **Toast, if wanted:** Put the drained quinoa in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir until excess surface water has evaporated and the grains smell lightly nutty, about 2 minutes or a little longer if still wet. Skip this step for a milder flavor.
3. **Simmer and check:** Add the measured water or broth and salt. Bring to a boil, cover tightly and lower the heat to a gentle simmer. Cook about 15 minutes without repeatedly lifting the lid. Taste a grain and check for pooled liquid; the grain should be tender without a hard center. If firm and dry, add a little hot water and continue covered. If firm with liquid remaining, continue simmering without adding more.
4. **Rest:** When the grain is tender and free liquid has absorbed, remove from heat and leave covered for 5 minutes.
5. **Dress:** Fluff gently with a fork. Add olive oil and measured lemon juice, then fold in zest, parsley, mint, dill and black pepper. Taste before adding any more salt. Divide into four generous side portions; serve warm, or cool promptly for a chilled serving.
