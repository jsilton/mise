## Chef's Note

This is Craig Plummer's potato soup, started the old-fashioned way by cooking the onion and celery in the fat left from frying the bacon. The bouillon and bacon fat season the whole pot, and the cream goes in only after the potatoes are completely soft.

## Directions

1. **Fry the bacon:** Fry the bacon strips in a large pot over medium heat until crisp, 8 to 10 minutes. Lift them onto paper towels and leave the fat in the pot.
2. **Cook the onion:** Add the medium onion and celery ribs to the bacon fat. Cook, stirring, until soft and translucent, about 6 minutes.
3. **Simmer the potatoes:** Add the large white potatoes, carrots, water, chicken bouillon cubes, white pepper, and salt-free herb seasoning blend and bring to a boil. Turn the heat to low, cover and simmer until the potatoes are very tender, at least 30 minutes.
4. **Add the cream:** Stir in the heavy cream and heat until steaming, about 5 minutes, without letting it boil.
5. **Top with bacon:** Ladle the soup into bowls and crumble the bacon over the top.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Mash about a third of the potatoes against the side of the pot before adding the cream. The soup turns thick and velvety.
- Use 3 cups chicken stock in place of the water and bouillon for a cleaner flavor.
- Top each bowl with shredded sharp cheddar and sliced green onion along with the bacon.
