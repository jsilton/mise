## Chef's Note

These kabobs follow Lebanese shish taouk, chicken soaked overnight in garlicky yogurt with lemon, paprika and a pinch of cinnamon. The yogurt keeps the thighs juicy and browns into a spiced crust on the grill. Thread the onion in thin layers so it softens by the time the chicken is done.

## Directions

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Whisk the yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice and zest, garlic, paprika, cumin, cinnamon, red pepper flakes, salt and black pepper in a large bowl. Add the chicken and onion and turn to coat. Cover and refrigerate for 8 to 24 hours.
2. **Heat the grill:** Heat a grill to medium-high, leaving one side cooler. Clean the grates and wipe them with the neutral oil.
3. **Thread the skewers:** Thread the chicken loosely onto twelve 10- to 12-inch metal skewers, about three pieces each, with thin onion layers between the pieces and at both ends. Let the excess marinade drip off so only a thin coat remains.
4. **Grill the kabobs:** Grill over the hot side with the lid closed, turning every 3 minutes once the coating releases, until browned in patches and 165°F in the largest pieces, 12 to 15 minutes. Slide any skewers that darken too fast to the cooler side. On a small grill, cook in two rounds and keep the first round loosely covered.
5. **Rest and serve:** Rest the kabobs for 5 minutes on a platter. Scatter the parsley over the top and serve with the lemon wedges.
