## Chef's Note

Allspice, thyme, hot chile and citrus season this Jamaican-inspired shoulder. The covered braise gives a shreddable texture, while a small clean reserve of marinade brightens the finish. Reserve that portion before the meat is added, and keep it cold and clearly separate from the marinade that cooks with the pork.

## Directions

1. **Blend and reserve:** Blend all marinade ingredients until mostly smooth. Before handling the pork, measure 1/4 cup into a clean covered container, label it for finishing and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or colder.
2. **Marinate:** Score the pork about 1/4 inch deep. Coat it with the remaining marinade, cover and refrigerate for 8–24 hours. Keep the clean finishing portion in its separate container.
3. **Heat the oven:** Heat to 325°F / 165°C. Keep the pork refrigerated until ready to sear; it does not need to reach room temperature.
4. **Sear:** Scrape excess marinade from the pork into a bowl reserved for the braise. Heat the Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear the pork, turning to brown its broad surfaces, about 3–4 minutes per side. Lower the heat if the sugary coating starts to blacken.
5. **Braise:** Add the scraped raw-contact marinade, stock and orange juice around the pork. Cover and cook in the oven about 3–3 1/2 hours, until a fork easily separates the meat. Check that liquid remains; add a little hot water if needed. The thickest part must reach at least 145°F / 63°C, but shreddable tenderness requires further cooking. Continue covered if still firm.
6. **Rest and reduce:** Move the pork to a board and rest for 20 minutes. During the rest, skim fat from the braising liquid and simmer it uncovered until glossy but still pourable, about 10 minutes. The rest also exceeds the 3-minute minimum after reaching the pork safety temperature.
7. **Shred and finish:** Shred the meat, discarding large pockets of fat. Toss with the reduced sauce and the clean 1/4 cup marinade reserved before raw contact. Do not use any unbraised scraped marinade here.
8. **Serve or reheat:** Serve in eight portions, or cool promptly for later meals. To reheat, spread pork and sauce in a covered baking dish at 325°F / 165°C until the center reaches 165°F / 74°C. Start checking after 20–25 minutes; a cold, deep dish takes longer.
