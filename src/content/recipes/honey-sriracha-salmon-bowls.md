## Chef's Note

A sweet-hot salmon bowl with jasmine rice, cool cucumber, avocado, edamame and a drizzle of sriracha mayonnaise. Sear the salmon cubes in two batches so they brown instead of steam. The leftover marinade then boils down in the empty pan into a glossy glaze for tossing.

## Directions

1. **Cook the rice:** Combine the rice and its water in a 2-quart saucepan and bring to a boil. Cover, turn the heat to low and simmer until the water is absorbed, about 15 minutes. Let it stand covered off the heat for 10 minutes, then fluff.
2. **Marinate the salmon:** Whisk the soy sauce, honey, sriracha, garlic and the water for the sauce in a bowl. Add the salmon and turn to coat. Refrigerate for 20 minutes.
3. **Cook the edamame:** While the salmon marinates, boil the edamame in a small pot of water until tender, 3 to 5 minutes. Drain.
4. **Sear the first batch:** Lift the salmon out of the marinade and let it drip, saving the marinade. Heat half the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Add half the salmon in a single layer and cook until browned underneath, 2 to 3 minutes. Turn and cook until just opaque in the center, 2 to 3 minutes more. Move it to a plate.
5. **Sear the second batch:** Wipe out any blackened bits, add the rest of the oil and cook the remaining salmon the same way.
6. **Reduce the glaze:** Pour the saved marinade into the skillet and boil for 1 minute. Lower the heat and simmer until glossy and lightly coating a spoon, 1 to 2 minutes. Turn off the heat and fold in the salmon and its juices. Loosen with a splash of water if the glaze turns sticky.
7. **Build the bowls:** Slice the avocado. Divide the rice among four bowls and top with the salmon, cucumber, avocado and edamame. Drizzle with sriracha mayonnaise and sprinkle with sesame seeds, if using.
