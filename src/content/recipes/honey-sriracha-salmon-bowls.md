## Chef's Note

Adapted from Claire Cary's Eat With Clarity recipe, this contemporary salmon bowl combines a sweet-hot glaze with cucumber, avocado, edamame and sriracha mayonnaise. Cooking the fish in two batches creates browned faces without crowding the pan. Reduce the sauce after removing the fish so it becomes glossy without repeatedly boiling the tender cubes.

## Directions

1. **Start the jasmine rice:** Rinse the rice and drain well. Put it in a heavy 2-quart saucepan with the measured 240 ml water. Bring to a boil, cover and reduce to low; simmer about 15 minutes until absorbed. Move off the heat and rest covered for 10 minutes, then fluff. Follow the rice package if its method differs. This small batch gives roughly 2 cups cooked rice for four bowls.
2. **Marinate the fish:** While the rice heats, whisk reduced-sodium soy sauce, honey, sriracha, garlic and the separate 3 tbsp sauce water in a bowl. Check the salmon for pin bones, cut into even 1-inch cubes and gently turn in the marinade. Cover and refrigerate for 20 minutes. Wash the raw-fish board, utensils and your hands before preparing ready-to-eat toppings.
3. **Prepare toppings and edamame:** During the marinade, slice cucumber and portion the mayonnaise. Bring the 750 ml water to a boil in a small saucepan, add the frozen shelled edamame and cook according to its package, usually 3–5 minutes, until steaming hot and tender. Drain. Slice avocado shortly before assembly. If using the cucumber salad variation, start its salting before the fish marinade, allow 5–10 extra minutes overall, and omit the plain sliced cucumber.
4. **Drain and organize two batches:** Lift fish from the marinade with a slotted spoon, letting excess drip back into its bowl; scrape off large garlic clumps into that liquid. Reserve all the drained marinade for the cooked sauce, keeping it separate from toppings and cooked fish. Divide the salmon into two equal batches. Have a clean plate ready for cooked fish.
5. **Brown the salmon:** Heat a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat, then add 2 tsp oil. Add half the salmon in a single layer with space between pieces. Cook about 2–3 minutes on the first face until browned, then turn and cook another 3–5 minutes, turning onto another face as needed, until the thickest cubes reach 145°F / 63°C. Insert a fine-tip thermometer sideways into their centers. Remove pieces as they finish. Reduce heat if the honey starts darkening rapidly. Transfer to the clean plate, remove any blackened deposits from the pan, and repeat with the remaining 2 tsp oil and fish. Allow the pan to recover heat between batches.
6. **Boil and reduce the sauce:** Remove all cooked fish. If the pan has bitter black residue, clean it before proceeding. Carefully pour in the reserved raw-contact marinade and bring to a full bubbling boil throughout, stirring. Maintain that boil for 1 minute, then lower the heat and simmer about 1–3 minutes until glossy and lightly spoon-coating; stop before it turns sticky or stringy. Turn off the heat and fold in all the cooked fish and its juices just long enough to coat.
7. **Assemble:** Divide all the rice, salmon and sauce, cucumber, avocado and edamame among four bowls. Each gets approximately 1/2 cup rice, a quarter of the fish, a quarter of the vegetables and 2 tbsp sriracha mayonnaise. Add the optional sesame seeds. Serve promptly; the browned faces soften once coated, so these are glazed salmon bites rather than a lasting crisp crust.
