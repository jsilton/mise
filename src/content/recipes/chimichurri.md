## Chef's Note

Chimichurri is Argentina's sauce for the parrilla, spooned over grilled steak and sausages. Chop the parsley and oregano by hand so the sauce stays loose and bright green instead of turning to paste. Dissolving the salt and garlic in the vinegar first gives it a sharp, punchy bite.

## Directions

1. **Chop the herbs:** Finely chop the parsley and oregano by hand until they are small, separate pieces, not a paste.
2. **Season the vinegar:** Stir the garlic and salt into the vinegar in a bowl. Let it sit for a minute so the salt dissolves.
3. **Mix the sauce:** Stir in the herbs, red pepper flakes and black pepper, then the olive oil.
4. **Rest and serve:** Let the sauce sit for 30 minutes so the garlic mellows. Stir and taste for salt before spooning it over the food.
