## Chef's Note

This is a practical use for cooked chicken, with the raw-meat cooking already done. It is distinct from [Weeknight Ground-Turkey Tacos](/mise/recipes/tacos), which begin with raw ground meat. Keep the toppings measured and let each diner choose a few: cabbage supplies crunch, avocado and crema supply richness, and salsa or lime can supply sharpness. Every taco need not contain every topping.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the cold components first:** Shred cabbage, slice radishes, dice onion and chop cilantro. Portion crema, salsa, optional cheese and hot sauce. Slice the optional jalapeño and cut the serving lime. Keep perishables chilled until the bar is ready; leave the avocado whole for now.
2. **Start the filling:** Heat oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium. Stir cumin for 15–20 seconds, then immediately add the measured 60 ml broth and cooked chicken. Toss, cover and heat gently, stirring every couple of minutes. Add extra broth a tablespoon at a time only if the pan is drying.
3. **Verify and season:** After roughly 6–10 minutes, check several portions with a thermometer; continue until the reheated chicken reaches 165°F / 74°C throughout. Remove the lid briefly if liquid pools. Taste before adding any measured lime or salt, since cooked chicken and broth may already be seasoned. Cover off heat for the brief tortilla warming period.
4. **Warm tortillas and finish avocado:** Warm tortillas in a dry skillet over medium-high, about 10–30 seconds per side until hot and flexible, wrapping each batch in a clean towel. Alternatively, microwave stacks of four to six between damp paper towels for 30–45 seconds, adding short bursts if needed. Slice avocado and gently toss with its separate tablespoon of lime juice. This helps limit browning but does not preserve it indefinitely.
5. **Set the bar and serve promptly:** Set out hot filling, warm tortillas and the chilled toppings. Offer three small tacos per person, about 33 g cooked chicken per tortilla before toppings. A thin layer of cabbage and a small spoonful of sauce are enough to start. Taste a bite before adding lime or hot sauce.
6. **Manage any longer serving period:** Replenish small dishes rather than leaving all the food out at once. For an extended buffet, keep cooked meat at 140°F / 60°C or above and cold toppings at 40°F / 4°C or below. Refrigerate unheld perishables within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C.
