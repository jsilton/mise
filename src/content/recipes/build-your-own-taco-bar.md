## Chef's Note

A weeknight taco bar built on cooked chicken, like a rotisserie bird or leftovers, so dinner is mostly chopping. Blooming the spices in oil before the chicken goes in gives the filling real flavor, and crunchy cabbage, radish and lime keep every taco bright. Set it all out and let everyone build their own.

## Directions

1. **Bloom the spices:** Heat the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium heat. Add the cumin, ancho chile powder, garlic powder, oregano and chipotle and stir until fragrant, 15 to 20 seconds.
2. **Heat the chicken:** Add the chicken and broth right away and toss to coat in the spices. Cover and cook, stirring every couple of minutes, until the chicken is steaming hot all the way through, 6 to 10 minutes. Add a splash more broth if the pan goes dry.
3. **Season the filling:** Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the lime juice and salt, taste and add more salt if it needs it. Cover to keep warm.
4. **Warm the tortillas:** Heat a dry skillet over medium-high heat. Warm the tortillas one or two at a time until hot, soft and lightly charred in spots, 10 to 30 seconds per side. Stack them in a clean towel as you go.
5. **Slice the avocados:** Slice the avocados and toss them gently with their lime juice and a pinch of salt.
6. **Set out the bar:** Put the chicken, tortillas, avocado, cabbage, radishes, onion, cilantro, jalapeño, crema, salsa and cheese out in bowls. Serve with lime wedges and hot sauce.
