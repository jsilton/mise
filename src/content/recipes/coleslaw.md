## Chef's Note

Creamy slaw works best when the cabbage remains distinct beneath the dressing. A measured salt-and-drain stage removes some water before mayonnaise is added. It does not guarantee a dry bowl the next day, so judge the actual texture. Cider vinegar, mustard and a little sugar give a balanced finish; celery seed is an optional aromatic, not a requirement for authenticity.

## Directions

1. **Salt and drain:** Put prepared cabbage and carrot in a large colander over a bowl. Toss evenly with the measured 6 g salt and leave for 30 minutes, tossing once halfway through.
2. **Dry the vegetables:** Press handfuls gently to release loose liquid without grinding the shreds into a pulp. Spread briefly on a clean towel and pat away surface moisture. Taste a shred; if unusually salty, rinse briefly in cold water, drain again and dry thoroughly before proceeding.
3. **Dress:** Whisk mayonnaise, mustard, half the vinegar, half the sugar, optional celery seed and pepper in a large bowl. Fold in the vegetables. Taste, then add the remaining vinegar and sugar gradually only if needed. Do not add extra salt automatically.
4. **Chill:** Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes. Stir gently and check the seasoning again before serving. If liquid has collected, lift out the slaw with a slotted spoon rather than repeatedly diluting the dressing through the pool.
5. **Serve:** Divide into eight side portions. For four diners, make half a batch. Keep chilled until serving; its creamy, cool crunch pairs with a burger, wings or barbecue.
