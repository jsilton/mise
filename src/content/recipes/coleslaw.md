## Chef's Note

This is the creamy, tangy deli slaw that belongs next to barbecue. Salting and draining the cabbage first pulls out its water, so the dressing stays creamy instead of turning soupy. Cider vinegar adds sharp tang, and celery seed gives the classic old-school flavor.

## Directions

1. **Salt the cabbage:** Toss the cabbage and carrots with the salt in a large colander set over a bowl. Let them drain for 30 minutes, tossing once halfway through.
2. **Dry the vegetables:** Squeeze the vegetables by the handful to press out the liquid, then pat them dry on a clean towel. Taste a shred, and if it is too salty, rinse briefly and dry again.
3. **Make the dressing:** In a large bowl, whisk the mayonnaise, vinegar, mustard, sugar, celery seed and pepper until smooth.
4. **Toss and chill:** Fold the vegetables into the dressing until every shred is coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes. Stir again and taste for vinegar and sugar before serving.
