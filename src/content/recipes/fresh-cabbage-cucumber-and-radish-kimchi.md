## Chef's Note

Ginger, gochugaru and rice vinegar give this fresh kimchi its punch, while cabbage, cucumber, daikon and small radishes provide different textures. Salt them separately, then drain and gently squeeze before adding the dressing. The original batch makes about 12 cups, depending on vegetable size and draining, and needs at least four hours in the refrigerator before serving.

## Directions

1. **Salt the vegetables:** Put the cabbage, cucumbers, daikon and radishes in four separate large bowls. Divide the listed kosher salt into four equal portions and toss one portion thoroughly with each vegetable. Let stand for 1 hour, tossing once halfway through. Keep total room-temperature handling, including preparation, under 2 hours; if the kitchen is above 90°F, do this salting rest in the refrigerator.
2. **Blend the dressing:** While the vegetables rest, combine the ginger, garlic, gochugaru, unseasoned rice vinegar, toasted sesame oil, fish sauce, scallions and sugar in a blender. Purée until almost smooth. Keep the dressing refrigerated until the vegetables are ready.
3. **Drain and squeeze:** Drain each bowl thoroughly, discarding all the released liquid. Gently squeeze each handful to remove excess moisture without crushing the vegetables. Do not rinse after salting.
4. **Dress the vegetables:** In a very large bowl, toss all the drained vegetables with all the ginger-gochugaru dressing until evenly coated. If necessary, divide both vegetables and dressing between two bowls and combine the batches afterward.
5. **Chill:** Transfer to clean, shallow covered containers and refrigerate promptly at 40°F or colder for at least 4 hours before serving. An overnight chill gives the flavors more time to mingle.
6. **Serve cold:** Toss before serving, spoon out small side portions with a clean utensil and promptly return the remaining kimchi to the refrigerator. Use within 3–4 days of preparation.
