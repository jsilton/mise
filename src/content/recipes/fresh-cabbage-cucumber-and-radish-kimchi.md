## Chef's Note

This is geotjeori, the fresh Korean kimchi that is eaten within days instead of left to ferment. Cabbage, cucumber, daikon and red radishes each bring their own crunch. Salting them first pulls out their water, so the garlicky ginger and gochugaru dressing clings to every piece instead of washing away.

## Directions

1. **Salt the vegetables:** Put the cabbage, cucumbers, daikon and radishes in four separate large bowls. Divide the salt evenly among the bowls and toss well. Let stand for 1 hour, tossing once halfway through.
2. **Blend the dressing:** While the vegetables stand, purée the ginger, garlic, gochugaru, rice vinegar, sesame oil, fish sauce, scallions and sugar in a blender until almost smooth.
3. **Drain the vegetables:** Drain each bowl and discard the liquid. Squeeze the vegetables by the handful to press out the extra water without crushing them. Do not rinse.
4. **Dress the kimchi:** In your largest bowl, toss all the vegetables with the dressing until every piece is evenly coated red.
5. **Chill the kimchi:** Pack the kimchi into jars or containers, cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or overnight for deeper flavor. Toss before serving.
