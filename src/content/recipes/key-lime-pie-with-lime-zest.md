## Chef's Note

A classic Key lime pie: condensed milk, egg yolks and lime juice baked in a graham cracker crust and topped with whipped cream. A generous spoonful of lime zest in the filling makes it brighter and more floral than juice alone. Chill it completely so it slices clean.

## Directions

1. **Make the crust:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Pulse the graham crackers in a food processor to fine crumbs. Add the sugar and salt, then pulse in the melted butter until the mixture looks like wet sand.
2. **Bake the crust:** Press the crumbs evenly over the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie plate. Bake until lightly golden and fragrant, 10 to 12 minutes. Lower the oven to 325°F.
3. **Whisk the filling:** While the crust bakes, whisk the condensed milk, lime juice, lime zest, egg yolks and salt until smooth.
4. **Bake the pie:** Pour the filling into the warm crust. Bake until the edges are set and the center still jiggles slightly, 15 to 17 minutes.
5. **Chill:** Cool the pie on a rack for 30 minutes, then refrigerate until cold and fully set, at least 3 hours or overnight.
6. **Top and serve:** Whip the cream to soft peaks and spread it over the cold pie just before serving.
