## Chef's Note

Lime zest gives this version a more aromatic filling. Bake the crumb crust before filling it, then check the custard with a thermometer; lime juice thickening alone does not cook the eggs.

## Directions

1. **Mix filling:** If using fresh limes, wash and dry them before zesting or squeezing, then measure the juice and zest. Whisk all condensed milk, lime juice, lime zest, egg yolks and filling salt together. Set aside while preparing the crust; refrigerate if preparation is delayed.

2. **Make crust:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Process the chosen graham-cracker supply to fine crumbs. Add all the crust sugar and crust salt, then pulse in all melted, cooled butter until the mixture resembles wet sand. The listed cracker count and crumb volume are alternatives, not two separate supplies.

3. **Bake crust:** Press all crumb mixture evenly into the bottom and sides of a 9-inch pie dish for the original batch. Bake about 10–12 minutes until lightly golden and fragrant. Cool briefly. Use separate original-size pies for multiples or appropriate smaller dishes for a reduced batch, with earlier checks; do not force extra filling into the same shell.

4. **Bake filling:** Give the entire filling a final whisk and pour it into the crust. Reduce the oven to 325°F and bake about 15–17 minutes for the original pie, until set around the edges with slight movement in the center. Check for 160°F /71°C in the filling and continue gently if needed. A colder filling or different dish can take longer.

5. **Chill:** Cool briefly on a rack, then refrigerate within 2 hours after baking, or 1 hour above 90°F /32°C. Chill at least 3 hours or overnight until fully cold and set through the center.

6. **Finish:** Just before serving, whip all the heavy cream with clean utensils to soft peaks and spread it over the chilled pie. Keep refrigerated until serving.
