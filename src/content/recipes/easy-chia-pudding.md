## Chef's Note

Stir the seeds into the full listed milk, wait five minutes, then stir again before refrigerating. The second stir breaks up early clumps. Three to four tablespoons seeds per cup of milk gives a range of thickness, and milk composition changes the result. For the separate blended chocolate recipe, see [Chocolate Chia Pudding](/mise/recipes/chocolate-chia-pudding); its ingredients and yield are different.

## Directions

1. **Combine:** In a bowl or jar with room to stir, whisk all the listed chia seeds, milk, chosen maple syrup or honey, and vanilla. The original batch uses 3–4 tbsp seeds per 1 cup milk; use the listed scaled quantities.

2. **First set:** Let stand for 5 minutes before the second stir.

3. **Stir again:** Stir thoroughly or close the jar securely and shake, then inspect the bottom and sides for clumps and break them up.

4. **Chill:** Cover and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight, until the pudding is thick and spoonable.

5. **Adjust:** If too thick, stir in a little additional milk. If still thin after sufficient chilling, add about 1 tbsp additional seeds at the original one-cup batch; adjust proportionally for another batch, stir and refrigerate for another 30 minutes or longer until thick. These adjustments are additions beyond the base formula, not a promise that every product sets at one exact time.

6. **Serve:** Wash and drain fresh berries, then top the pudding with the listed berries, almond butter and prepared granola as wanted. These toppings are unmeasured allowances; keep granola separate until serving for crunch.
