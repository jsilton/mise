## Chef's Note

Chia pudding is the easiest make-ahead breakfast there is: chia seeds soak up milk overnight and set into a thick, creamy pudding. The trick is a second stir five minutes in, which breaks up the clumps that form as the seeds start to gel. Use less chia for a looser pudding and more for a thick one.

## Directions

1. **Mix:** In a jar or bowl, whisk the chia seeds, milk, maple syrup and vanilla.
2. **Stir again:** Let it stand 5 minutes, then stir or shake hard to break up any clumps of seeds.
3. **Chill:** Cover and refrigerate until thick and spoonable, at least 2 hours or overnight. If it is still thin, stir in a spoonful more chia and chill 30 minutes longer.
4. **Serve:** Loosen with a splash of milk if it is too thick. Top with berries, almond butter and granola.
