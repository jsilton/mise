## Chef's Note

Kids see a bright green drink and taste banana, mango and vanilla. Frozen mango makes it cold and thick, avocado makes it silky, and the spinach blends away completely when it goes in near the bottom of the blender.

## Directions

1. **Load the blender:** Put the milk, oats, spinach, banana, avocado, almond butter and vanilla in a blender in that order, then add the frozen mango on top.
2. **Blend:** Blend on low, then turn it up to high. Blend until smooth and evenly green with no flecks of spinach, about 1 minute.
3. **Adjust and serve:** If it's too thick, blend in a splash more milk. Taste, blend in maple syrup if you want it sweeter and serve right away.
