## Chef's Note

Frozen mango and avocado give this smoothie body. Include the nut butter and vanilla in the blender, and blend until the greens are finely broken down. Add extra milk only as needed for the texture you want.

## Directions

1. Wash the fresh produce before cutting, unless the spinach package is already washed and ready to eat. Peel the banana, remove the avocado skin and pit, and follow any preparation instructions on the frozen mango package.

2. Load the blender according to its manufacturer's instructions, using all the listed milk, oats, spinach, banana, avocado, mango, nut butter and vanilla. In a conventional jug blender, begin with the milk and softer ingredients before adding the frozen fruit. Keep within the actual working fill limit; blend proportional batches if needed.

3. Start at low speed and increase as the appliance permits. Begin checking around 60 seconds, stopping to scrape or redistribute only with the motor off. Continue until smooth, with no large leafy pieces.

4. Use the separate additional milk allowance a little at a time if too thick. If wanted, add the optional listed maple syrup gradually within its full listed range and blend again; do not add a fresh full dose at every adjustment.

5. Divide into the listed smoothie portions and serve promptly.

## Cooking Notes

For a gluten-free version, use certified gluten-free oats or the same listed amount of millet flakes in place of rolled oats.
