## Chef's Note

A short 425°F roast gives asparagus browned tips while the stems keep a little bite. Stalk thickness matters more than a fixed clock: thin asparagus may be ready before thick stalks on the same sheet. Finish the hot vegetables with lemon juice and, if wanted, the Parmesan or zest topping.

## Directions

1. **Heat and dry:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Wash the asparagus under running water, trim the woody ends and pat the stalks dry. Set out a rimmed baking sheet with room for one layer; use additional sheets or batches if the measured bunches do not fit.
2. **Coat the stalks:** Toss bunch of fresh asparagus, extra-virgin olive oil, kosher salt, and black pepper on the baking sheet or in a bowl, then spread in a single layer without piling. Keep very thin and thick stalks in separate areas so the thin ones can be removed first if needed.
3. **Check the stems and tips:** Roast for 8–12 minutes, checking at the early end for thin stalks. The stems should be tender with a little resistance when pierced, and the tips may be lightly browned. Remove thinner stalks as they are ready; continue thicker ones if still firm. Browning is useful, but black tips and limp stems are not the goal.
4. **Add lemon and serve:** Use lemon and grated Parmesan or finely grated lemon zest (if using): squeeze the lemon juice over the hot asparagus. If using the optional finish, choose the measured Parmesan or zest and sprinkle it over the stalks. Measure zest separately if that is the chosen topping; the lemon portion for juice is not a verified equivalent of the measured zest amount. Serve promptly.
