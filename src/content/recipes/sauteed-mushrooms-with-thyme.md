## Chef's Note

These are the buttery bistro mushrooms you want beside a roast chicken or a pork tenderloin. Mushrooms are mostly water, so crowd the pan and they steam gray. Brown them in batches with room to spare, then finish with butter, garlic and thyme and a splash of sherry to lift the browned bits.

## Directions

1. **Brown the first batch:** Heat a third of the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add a third of the mushrooms in a single layer and cook undisturbed until deep golden underneath, 3 to 4 minutes. Flip and cook 2 to 3 minutes more, then move them to a plate.
2. **Brown two more batches:** Repeat twice with the rest of the oil and mushrooms, half at a time.
3. **Add the aromatics:** Return all the mushrooms to the pan and lower the heat to medium. Add the butter, garlic and thyme and toss until the butter foams and the garlic turns golden, 1 to 2 minutes.
4. **Deglaze:** Pour in the sherry and scrape up the browned bits. Cook until the liquid has cooked away, about 30 seconds, then season with the salt and pepper.
5. **Serve:** Take the pan off the heat, scatter with parsley and serve right away.
