## Chef's Note

Pimento cheese, the Southern spread of sharp cheddar and sweet red peppers, becomes a baked macaroni here. A milk sauce thickened with a roux holds the cheese, and a panko and Parmesan topping bakes into a crisp lid. Shred the cheddar from the block so it melts smooth.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter an 11-by-7-inch or 9-by-9-inch baking dish.
2. **Boil the macaroni:** Cook the macaroni in a large pot of well-salted boiling water until al dente, then drain. While it cooks, start the sauce.
3. **Make the roux:** Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook, whisking, until it smells toasty but has not browned, about 1 minute.
4. **Thicken the sauce:** Slowly whisk in the milk, then the salt, cayenne and garlic powder. Cook, whisking constantly, until the sauce is smooth and thick enough to coat a spoon, 3 to 5 minutes.
5. **Melt the cheese:** Turn the heat to low. Stir in the cheddar and pimentos until the cheese melts and the sauce is glossy. Take the pan off the heat and fold in the macaroni.
6. **Top and bake:** Spread the macaroni in the dish. Mix the panko and Parmesan and scatter them evenly over the top. Bake until the edges bubble and the topping is golden, 15 to 20 minutes. Serve hot.
