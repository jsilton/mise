## Chef's Note

Drain the salted zucchini while making the avocado-and-pistachio pesto. The pesto has its own olive oil; separate measured oil cooks the garlic, shrimp and noodles. Heat the zucchini just until hot but still firm, then toss gently with the pesto off heat so the strands and creamy sauce stay distinct.

## Directions

1. **Drain zucchini:** Prepare medium zucchini and salt: spiralize zucchini, stopping at a soft seedy core where needed. Toss with the measured draining salt in a colander and let stand for 15 minutes while making pesto. Gently squeeze out released water without crushing the strands. Larger amounts need enough colander space; the strands can still release water after draining.
2. **Make pesto:** Pulse ripe avocado, fresh basil, unsalted shelled pistachios, fresh lemon juice, salt, ground pepper, and extra-virgin olive oil in a food processor or blender suited to this cold mixture, until finely chopped and creamy. All this quarter-cup pesto oil at the original batch belongs in the sauce; the pan oils are separate. Keep raw shrimp and its utensils away from the ready-to-eat pesto.
3. **Cook shrimp:** Use extra-virgin olive oil, garlic cloves, shrimp, and celery-salt-and-paprika seafood seasoning. Heat the shrimp-pan oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Stir garlic about 30 seconds until fragrant, add shrimp and all the seafood seasoning, and cook, turning as needed. Start checking around 3–4 minutes. Cook until the thickest shrimp are firm, pearly and opaque throughout; FDA guidance is 145°F for seafood when checking with a suitable thermometer. Pink color or a fixed minute count alone does not establish doneness. Transfer to a clean large bowl, using clean utensils. Reduce heat if garlic browns too fast; divide this stage’s oil across batches if needed.
4. **Heat noodles:** Add extra-virgin olive oil to the pan, then all the drained zucchini. Toss gently over medium-high heat, beginning to check around 3 minutes, until hot but still firm. Use additional loads if the pan cannot comfortably toss all the strands; split this stage’s oil across those loads rather than repeat the full allowance.
5. **Combine and serve:** Transfer hot zucchini to the clean shrimp bowl. Off heat, add all the pesto and toss gently until coated, then serve promptly. The measured garlic all cooked with the shrimp, not in a second pesto dose. All quantities, including pesto, are allocated across the whole batch.
