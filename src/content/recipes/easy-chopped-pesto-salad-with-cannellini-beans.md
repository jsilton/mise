## Chef's Note

A chopped salad hearty enough for lunch: creamy cannellini beans, little mozzarella balls, cherry tomatoes and crisp cucumber, all coated in basil pesto sharpened with red wine vinegar. Dry the beans and cheese well so the dressing clings instead of pooling in the bowl.

## Directions

1. **Combine the salad:** Put the tomatoes, cucumber, mozzarella, shallot and beans in a large bowl.
2. **Dress it:** Add the pesto, vinegar, salt and pepper. Fold gently with a broad spoon, lifting from the bottom, until everything is coated and the beans are still whole.
3. **Rest and serve:** Let the salad stand 10 minutes so the shallot mellows and the flavors come together. Taste for salt and serve.
