## Chef's Note

Cannellini beans and mozzarella make this chopped cucumber-and-tomato salad substantial. Drain the beans and pat off excess water before tossing; pesto and red-wine vinegar form the dressing.

## Directions

1. **Step 1:** Gently toss everything together in a large bowl and let sit for at least 10 minutes before serving.

2. **Step 2:** This salad will keep well in the refrigerator, too!
