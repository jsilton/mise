## Chef's Note

Drain the cannellini beans and mozzarella well and dry the chopped vegetables so water does not dilute the pesto dressing. Fold gently to keep the beans intact, then allow ten minutes before serving. Pesto and cheese already contribute salt, so taste the fully mixed salad before any extra seasoning.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Wash and dry tomatoes and cucumber. Halve tomatoes, peel and dice cucumber, drain and halve mozzarella, and thinly slice shallot. Drain and rinse cannellini beans and let excess water drain away. Combine these ingredients in a large bowl.

2. **Dress:** Add the measured pesto, red wine vinegar, kosher salt and pepper.

3. **Fold and rest:** Fold gently until the dressing reaches all the ingredients. Let stand 10 minutes before serving, or refrigerate for that interval if preparing ahead.

4. **Serve or store:** Taste only after the dressing is fully distributed; add extra seasoning only if needed. Serve using clean utensils and refrigerate the unused salad promptly.
