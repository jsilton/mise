## Chef's Note

Blending feta with a little cream cheese turns the crumbly, salty cheese into a smooth spread. Honey, smoked paprika and pistachios on top give each scoop something sweet, smoky and crunchy against the tang of feta and lemon. Block feta in brine whips creamier than pre-crumbled.

## Directions

1. **Whip the cheese:** Crumble the feta into a small food processor. Add the cream cheese, the olive oil (saving a little for the top), lemon juice, zest and garlic. Process in short bursts, scraping down the bowl, until smooth and creamy, 1 to 2 minutes. If the mixture won't move, add a splash of cool water.
2. **Taste and spread:** Taste and add more lemon if it needs it. The feta is salty enough, so skip the salt. Spread in a shallow dish and make swirls with the back of a spoon.
3. **Top and serve:** Drizzle with the honey and the rest of the oil. Dust with smoked paprika and cayenne, then scatter with dill and pistachios.
