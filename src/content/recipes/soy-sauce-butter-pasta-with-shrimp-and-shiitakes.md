## Chef's Note

Shiitakes supply the earthy flavor while soy sauce and Parmesan season the butter coating. Let the mushrooms lose their excess moisture before finishing the sauce, then add pasta water only if the strands need loosening. Adapted from [Sarah Leung’s Soy Sauce Butter Pasta](https://thewoksoflife.com/soy-sauce-butter-pasta-shrimp-shiitakes/).

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Bring pasta water to a boil. Slice mushrooms, mince shallots and garlic, chop parsley and grate Parmesan. Pat shrimp dry. Salt the pasta water moderately; soy and cheese will season the finished dish.

2. **Cook shrimp:** Heat olive oil over medium-high. Cook shrimp in a single layer, about 1–2 minutes per side or as needed until firm, pearly and opaque through the center; a fine-tip thermometer inserted into a thick shrimp should read 145°F / 63°C. Transfer to a clean plate.

3. **Cook mushrooms and aromatics:** Reduce heat to medium. Add one-third of the butter and the shallots; cook about 3 minutes until beginning to soften. Add mushrooms and cook about 5–7 minutes, turning, until softened with some brown edges. Add garlic for the last 30–45 seconds. If the pan dries and shallots begin catching, loosen with a small splash of water rather than burning them.

4. **Reduce and cook pasta:** Add wine and simmer about 2–3 minutes until reduced by roughly half. Meanwhile cook pasta about 1 minute short of its package al dente time; for very quick angel hair, start it only when the mushrooms are ready. Reserve the listed pasta water before draining.

5. **Coat:** Add pasta and soy sauce and toss over low heat until the pasta is tender with a little bite, using a small splash of reserved water as needed. Turn off heat. Add the remaining two-thirds of the butter and toss until melted into the sauce, then fold in Parmesan, parsley, shrimp and their juices. Add further water only if the coating is tight.

6. **Serve:** Taste before adding salt; it may need none beyond the soy and cheese. Add black pepper and divide the pasta and shrimp evenly. Serve promptly.
