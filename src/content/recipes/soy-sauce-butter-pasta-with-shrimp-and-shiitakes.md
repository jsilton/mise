## Chef's Note

Butter and soy sauce is a classic pairing in wafu pasta, the Japanese style of Western pasta. Here it carries shrimp, shiitakes and white wine. Brown the shiitakes well before the wine goes in, because their roasted edges flavor the whole sauce.

## Directions

1. **Sear the shrimp:** Put a large pot of lightly salted water on to boil. Heat the oil in a deep 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Cook the shrimp in a single layer until pink and just opaque, 1 to 2 minutes per side, then move them to a plate.
2. **Brown the mushrooms:** Lower the heat to medium and add a third of the butter and the shallots. Cook until the shallots start to soften, about 2 minutes. Add the mushrooms and cook, turning now and then, until soft and browned at the edges, 5 to 7 minutes. Stir in the garlic for the last 30 seconds.
3. **Reduce the wine:** Pour in the wine and simmer until reduced by half, 2 to 3 minutes. Take the pan off the heat.
4. **Cook the pasta:** Boil the pasta until 1 minute short of al dente. Scoop out a mug of the pasta water, then drain.
5. **Toss and finish:** Add the pasta and soy sauce to the skillet over low heat. Toss, adding splashes of pasta water, until the pasta is tender and coated, about 1 minute. Off the heat, toss in the rest of the butter until glossy, then fold in the Parmesan, parsley, shrimp and their juices.
6. **Serve:** Season with black pepper, taste for salt and serve right away.
