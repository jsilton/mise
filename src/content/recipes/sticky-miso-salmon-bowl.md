## Chef's Note

Andy Baraghani's salmon bowls from NYT Cooking. Grapefruit zest and juice give the sweet miso and honey glaze a bright, slightly bitter edge, and the broiler turns it sticky and browned in spots. Buttered scallion rice underneath and cool cucumber, avocado and nori on top make it a full dinner.

## Directions

1. **Soak the rice:** Rinse the rice in several changes of cool water until only a little cloudy, then drain well. Put it in a heavy 3-quart saucepan with the cooking water and soak for 30 minutes.
2. **Mix the glaze:** While the rice soaks, whisk the miso, honey, ginger, grapefruit zest, grapefruit juice and the glaze oil until smooth.
3. **Cook the rice:** Bring the rice to a boil over medium heat with the lid slightly ajar. Cover tightly, turn the heat to low and simmer until the water is absorbed, 12 to 15 minutes. Take it off the heat and leave it covered for 10 minutes.
4. **Glaze the salmon:** As the rice simmers, heat the broiler with a rack about 8 inches from the element. Line a rimmed sheet pan with foil and rub it with the oil for the pan. Pat the salmon dry, set it on the foil with space between the fillets and spread the glaze evenly over the tops and sides.
5. **Broil the salmon:** Broil, turning the pan if it browns unevenly, until the glaze bubbles with dark brown patches and the thickest part of the fish flakes, 10 to 15 minutes. If the top darkens before the center is done, move the pan to a lower rack.
6. **Finish the rice:** Fold the butter and scallions into the rested rice.
7. **Build the bowls:** Divide the rice among four bowls and top each with a salmon fillet, cucumber, avocado and nori. Pass the kimchi and chile crisp at the table.
