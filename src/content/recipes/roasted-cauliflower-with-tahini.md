## Chef's Note

A whole roasted cauliflower is a showpiece side, rubbed with cumin and smoked paprika and finished with lemony tahini. A covered start steams the center tender, then a hot uncovered roast turns the outside deep golden. Bring it to the table whole and cut it into wedges there.

## Directions

1. **Trim the cauliflower:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Trim the base so the head sits flat and cut a shallow cross into the stem. Rub it with a little of the oil and half the salt.
2. **Steam it covered:** Set the cauliflower stem side down in a baking dish, pour the hot water around it and cover tightly with foil. Bake until a knife meets only some resistance in the center, 30 to 40 minutes.
3. **Spice the outside:** Lift the cauliflower onto a rimmed sheet pan and let the steam escape for a few minutes. Stir the rest of the oil with the cumin, paprika, turmeric, pepper and the rest of the salt, and brush it all over.
4. **Roast:** Roast uncovered, turning the pan once, until deep golden and a knife slides easily into the core, 30 to 40 minutes.
5. **Finish and serve:** Rest 5 minutes, then move it to a platter. Drizzle with the tahini sauce and scatter with parsley, pomegranate seeds and Aleppo pepper.
