## Chef's Note

White chicken chili trades tomatoes and red chiles for white beans, green chiles and chewy hominy. Mashing half the beans thickens the broth without any flour or canned soup. Sour cream and lime go in at the very end, so the chili tastes creamy and bright.

## Directions

1. **Mash the beans:** Put half the beans in a 6-quart slow cooker with a splash of the broth. Mash them into a loose paste.
2. **Load the cooker:** Add the rest of the beans, the hominy, onion, garlic, green chiles and their juices, cumin, oregano, coriander, pepper, salt and the rest of the broth. Stir, then nestle the chicken thighs into the liquid.
3. **Slow cook:** Cover and cook on LOW until the chicken shreds easily and the onion is tender, 4 to 5 hours. On HIGH, it takes 2 to 3 hours.
4. **Shred the chicken:** Lift the chicken onto a board and shred it with two forks. Stir it back into the pot. For a thicker chili, mash a few more beans against the side of the cooker.
5. **Finish with sour cream:** Turn off the cooker and let the bubbling stop. Whisk the sour cream with a ladle of hot broth until smooth, then stir it into the chili. Stir in the lime juice, taste and add more salt if it needs it.
6. **Serve:** Ladle into bowls and top with the scallions and Monterey Jack.
