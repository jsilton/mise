## Chef's Note

Hominy is corn processed with an alkaline treatment called nixtamalization; canned hominy is already cooked and brings a chew unlike the soft beans. Mashing some white beans gives this broth body. Sour cream and lime enter after the long cook so their amount and texture can be controlled. This six-serving home version uses measured spices and plain ingredients rather than an undefined seasoning packet.

## Directions

1. **Mash part of the beans:** Drain and rinse beans and hominy separately. In a bowl, mash half the beans with about 1/2 cup of the measured broth into a loose paste. Leave the remaining beans whole.
2. **Load the cooker:** Add the bean paste, whole beans, hominy, finely diced onion, garlic, green chiles and juices, cumin, oregano, coriander, pepper and remaining measured broth to a 6-quart slow cooker. Nestle in thawed thighs. Cover and start immediately.
3. **Cook and check:** Cook on LOW about 4–5 hours or HIGH about 2–3 hours. Check several thick pieces for 165°F / 74°C and easy shredding, and check onion tenderness. Remove cooked chicken to a clean board. If onion is still firm, finish the base in a saucepan before proceeding.
4. **Shred and set texture:** Shred chicken coarsely and return it to the hot base. Mash a few more beans against the pot wall if more body is wanted. Taste and add salt gradually only if needed. Turn off heat and wait for bubbling to stop.
5. **Finish gently:** Whisk sour cream with a ladleful of hot broth in a bowl until smooth, then stir it back into the chili. Add half the lime juice, taste, then add more if needed. Serve promptly rather than leaving the finished dairy mixture at a boil.
6. **Serve:** Divide among six bowls and finish with scallions and optional Monterey Jack, up to 10 g per portion. Bread or slaw is a separate side; no additional cream cheese, avocado or tortilla-chip topping is part of this formula.
