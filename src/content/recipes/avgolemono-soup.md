## Chef's Note

Avgolemono is the Greek egg and lemon chicken soup. Eggs whisked with lemon juice thicken the broth to a silky, creamy body with no dairy at all. Warming the eggs slowly with hot broth before they go into the pot keeps them smooth instead of scrambled.

## Directions

1. **Cook the rice:** Bring the broth to a boil in a large pot. Add the rice, lower the heat and simmer until tender, about 15 minutes (8 to 10 minutes for orzo).
2. **Whisk the eggs:** While the rice cooks, whisk the eggs in a medium bowl until foamy, about 2 minutes. Slowly whisk in the lemon juice.
3. **Add the chicken:** Stir the chicken into the pot and simmer until heated through, about 3 minutes. Turn the heat to low so the soup barely simmers.
4. **Temper the eggs:** Whisking the eggs constantly, ladle in about a third of the hot broth, one ladle at a time.
5. **Thicken the soup:** Pour the egg mixture back into the pot. Stir constantly over low heat until the soup turns creamy and opaque, about 2 minutes. Don't let it boil.
6. **Season and serve:** Taste, season with salt and white pepper and serve right away.
