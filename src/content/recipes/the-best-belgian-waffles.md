## Chef's Note

These Belgian waffles from Food.com get their crisp shell and airy, tender center from egg whites whipped to stiff peaks and folded in at the very end. Fold gently and cook the batter right away so the air stays in.

## Directions

1. **Heat the iron:** Heat the waffle iron.
2. **Whip the whites:** In a clean, dry bowl, beat the egg whites until they hold stiff, glossy peaks. Stop before they look dry or grainy.
3. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Sift the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt into a large bowl.
4. **Mix the wet ingredients:** In another bowl, whisk the egg yolks, milk, oil and vanilla until smooth.
5. **Make the batter:** Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and whisk just until combined. Small lumps are fine.
6. **Fold in the whites:** With a rubber spatula, gently fold the whites into the batter until no streaks remain.
7. **Cook the waffles:** Grease the hot iron and pour in enough batter to cover the grids, leaving room for it to rise. Cook until deep golden and the steam has almost stopped, 5 to 7 minutes. Keep finished waffles in a single layer on a rack while you cook the rest.
8. **Serve:** Serve hot with maple syrup.
