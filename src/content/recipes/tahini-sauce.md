## Chef's Note

Tahini sauce is the everyday dressing of the Middle East, poured over falafel, shawarma and roasted vegetables. Lemon makes the paste seize up thick and grainy at first. Keep whisking in ice water a spoonful at a time and it turns pale, silky and pourable.

## Directions

1. **Mix the base:** In a bowl, whisk the tahini, lemon juice, garlic, salt and cumin. It will turn thick and grainy. That is normal.
2. **Whisk in the water:** Add the ice water a tablespoon at a time, whisking after each addition, until the sauce is pale and smooth. Stop when it is thick enough for a dip, or keep going until it pours like heavy cream for a drizzle.
3. **Season:** Taste and add more lemon or salt if it needs it.
