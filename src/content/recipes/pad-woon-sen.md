## Chef's Note

Pad woon sen is a Thai home-style stir-fry of glass noodles, egg and vegetables, lighter than pad Thai. Char the cabbage and pepper over high heat for a smoky edge. A little water in the sauce helps the slippery noodles soak it up evenly instead of clumping.

## Directions

1. **Soak the noodles:** Cover the noodles with hot, not boiling, water. Soak until they bend easily with no hard center, about 10 minutes. Drain, rinse with cold water and drain well.
2. **Mix the sauce:** Whisk the oyster sauce, fish sauce, soy sauce, sugar and water in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves.
3. **Scramble the eggs:** Heat a splash of oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the eggs and a pinch of salt and stir until just set, about 1 minute. Move them to a plate and chop roughly.
4. **Char the vegetables:** Add a little more oil and turn the heat to high. Add the cabbage, bell pepper and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring now and then, until charred at the edges but still crisp, 3 to 4 minutes. Add them to the plate with the eggs.
5. **Soften the aromatics:** Turn the heat to medium-high and add a little more oil. Cook the onion and carrot until soft, about 2 minutes. Add the garlic and stir until fragrant, about 45 seconds.
6. **Toss the noodles:** Add the noodles and pour the sauce around the edge of the pan. Toss until the noodles are glossy and have soaked up most of the sauce, about 3 minutes.
7. **Finish:** Return the eggs and vegetables to the pan and toss until hot. Take the pan off the heat and fold in the chile and scallions.
