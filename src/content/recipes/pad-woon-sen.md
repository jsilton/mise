## Chef's Note

Mung bean glass noodles need to soften before they enter the pan. Cook the eggs separately and give the vegetables room to soften; the sauce should coat the noodles, rather than disappear into a dry pan. Add the garlic after the onion and carrot begin to soften so it does not scorch.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Wash the fresh vegetables and herbs. Slice the cabbage and pepper thinly; peel and grate the carrot, slice the onion and garlic, and keep the chile and scallions for the finish. Have a clean plate ready for the cooked eggs and vegetables.
2. **Soften noodles:** Cover the mung bean glass noodles with hot, not boiling, water, starting checks around 10 minutes for the original noodle product. They should bend easily without a hard center. Follow different package preparation instructions if required by your product, then drain well. The combined-vegetable variation below retains the cold rinse.
3. **Mix sauce:** Whisk together all the oyster sauce, fish sauce, soy sauce and sugar.
4. **Cook eggs:** Heat a splash of the listed oil over medium-high heat in a large wok or skillet until shimmering. Add all the whisked eggs and a pinch of the salt allowance. Stir until fully set with no liquid egg remaining, beginning checks around 1 minute. Transfer to the clean plate and roughly chop.
5. **Cook cabbage:** Add enough of the oil allowance to coat the pan. Add all the cabbage and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring, until softened and charred in spots, beginning checks around 3–4 minutes. Transfer to the cooked-egg plate. Work in additional batches if the cabbage cannot move freely in the pan.
6. **Cook pepper:** Add another splash of oil, all the pepper and a pinch of salt. Stir until softened, beginning checks around 3–4 minutes, then transfer to the same cooked-food plate.
7. **Soften aromatics:** Add fresh oil from the allowance. Cook all the onion and carrot, beginning checks around 2 minutes, then add all the garlic and cook about 45 seconds until fragrant without burning. Continue as needed for softened onion and carrot.
8. **Coat noodles:** Add the drained noodles, all the cooked eggs and vegetables, then all the sauce around the perimeter of the pan. Toss over medium-high heat until evenly coated and hot, beginning checks around 3–5 minutes. Reduce heat if the pan dries or the noodles stick; do not wait for every trace of sauce to vanish.
9. **Finish:** Take off heat and fold in all the sliced chile and scallions. Serve promptly hot, or refrigerate promptly for a chilled meal.

## Combined-Vegetable Variation

Use the same full ingredient quantities. After soaking and draining, rinse the noodles in cold water and drain well. Cook the eggs as above. Instead of separate cabbage and pepper stages, cook both together in a lightly oiled pan until softened with a little char, starting checks around 3–4 minutes; divide into batches if crowded. Transfer to the cooked-egg plate. Keep the onion/carrot followed by garlic stage, then add noodles and all the sauce. Return all the cooked eggs and vegetables, toss until hot and evenly coated, and finish off heat with the full chile and scallions.
