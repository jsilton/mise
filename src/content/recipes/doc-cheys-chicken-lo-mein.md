## Chef's Note

Doc Chey’s lo mein uses a soy, wine and oyster-sauce mixture with chicken and several vegetables. Have the cooked noodles and sauce ready before heating the wok, and return the cooked chicken only near the end.

## Directions

1. **Step 1:** Cut chicken into thin strips and season with salt and pepper. Whisk soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, oyster sauce and sugar together until sugar dissolves. Prepare the cooked noodles and vegetables before heating the pan.

2. **Step 2:** Heat oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken in uncrowded batches, about 3 minutes or as needed, until the thickest pieces reach 165°F / 74°C. Transfer to a clean plate.

3. **Step 3:** Add garlic and ginger to the pan and stir about 30 seconds. Add onions, carrots, bok choy and edamame. Stir-fry about 4 minutes, until vegetables are tender-crisp; use fully cooked edamame prepared according to its package.

4. **Step 4:** Add noodles and the prepared Cantonese sauce. Return chicken and toss about 3 minutes, until hot and coated and the sauce has reduced. Divide between bowls and garnish with scallions.
