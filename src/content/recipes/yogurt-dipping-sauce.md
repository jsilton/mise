## Chef's Note

This is the lemon and herb yogurt sauce Guy Fieri spoons over falafel in pita. Lemon zest and cumin turn plain yogurt bright and savory in five minutes. A short rest in the fridge lets the herbs flavor the whole bowl.

## Directions

1. **Mix the sauce:** In a small bowl, stir the yogurt, lemon zest, lemon juice, cilantro, parsley, cumin and salt until smooth. Taste and add more salt if it needs it.
2. **Chill:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 15 minutes so the flavors come together, or serve right away.
