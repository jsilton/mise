## Chef's Note

Lemon zest and juice brighten the yogurt while cumin, cilantro and parsley add aroma. Mix and chill while preparing the food it will accompany.

## Directions

1. **Mix:** Mix the yogurt, lemon zest, lemon juice, cilantro, parsley and cumin together in a small bowl.

2. **Continue:** Add salt to taste.

3. **Continue:** Chill until ready to use.
