## Chef's Note

Mix lemon zest, juice and chopped herbs through plain yogurt for a cool, cumin-scented dip. Stir thoroughly before tasting so the zest and cumin are evenly distributed. Keep it refrigerated while preparing the food it will accompany.

## Directions

1. **Mix:** Stir yogurt, lemon zest, lemon juice, cilantro, parsley and cumin together in a small bowl until evenly combined.

2. **Season:** Add the listed salt to taste and stir thoroughly.

3. **Chill or serve:** Cover and refrigerate until needed, or serve immediately in small dipping dishes. Keep unused dip refrigerated and separate from portions people have dipped into.
