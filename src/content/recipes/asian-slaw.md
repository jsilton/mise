## Chef's Note

Green and red cabbage give this slaw a firm crunch, and a ginger, soy and toasted sesame dressing balances sweet, salty and sour. With no mayonnaise, it stays light and bright. It belongs beside grilled meats, tacos and rice bowls.

## Directions

1. **Make the dressing:** Shake the vinegar, soy sauce, both oils, honey, ginger, garlic and sriracha (if using) in a small jar until combined.
2. **Toss the slaw:** Shortly before serving, in a large bowl, toss both cabbages, the carrots and the scallions with the dressing until evenly coated.
3. **Finish:** Just before serving, fold in the cilantro, peanuts and sesame seeds.
