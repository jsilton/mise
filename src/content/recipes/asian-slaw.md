## Chef's Note

Green and red cabbage bring a firm crunch to ginger, soy and toasted sesame. Toss just before serving and add the peanuts at the end. As the dressed vegetables sit they release liquid and soften, so choose the timing for the texture you want.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the vegetables:** Thinly shred and measure both cabbages. Cut carrots into thin matchsticks and slice scallions. Put them in a large bowl; prepare the cilantro and peanuts separately.
2. **Mix the dressing:** Whisk or shake the vinegar, soy sauce or tamari, both oils, honey or maple syrup, ginger, garlic and optional chile sauce until combined. Stir again just before pouring.
3. **Dress:** Pour over the cabbage, carrots and scallions. Lift and turn from the bottom until evenly coated. Taste a complete forkful before adjusting any seasoning.
4. **Finish:** Fold in cilantro, peanuts and toasted sesame seeds just before serving. Divide into six side portions. If holding briefly, cover and refrigerate the dressed vegetables, adding the toppings at service; lift portions out of any pooled liquid.
