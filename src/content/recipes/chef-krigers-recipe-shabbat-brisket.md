## Chef's Note

Chef Kriger’s Camp Micah brisket carries the familiar Shabbat combination of ketchup, Dijon, grape jelly, carrots and onion. This measured home adaptation keeps the generous sweet-savory sauce and uses a covered braise, an overnight refrigerated rest and thin cold slicing to make tender portions that reheat well.

## Directions

1. **Season the brisket:** Heat the oven to 325°F / 163°C. Use a heavy covered roasting pan or Dutch oven that holds the meat flat with the vegetables and sauce and at least 1 inch of headroom; an approximately 8-quart vessel is a starting point, but check the fit. Have first-cut beef brisket, kosher salt, and black pepper ready. Season the meat on both sides with the salt and pepper. Place it fat-side up. If needed, cut it into two pieces to fit without stacking.
2. **Add the vegetables:** Arrange baby carrots and large yellow onion around and over the meat.
3. **Mix the full sauce:** Whisk together ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard, grape jelly, brown sugar, garlic powder, onion powder, and water until the jelly is dispersed. Pour over the meat and vegetables, coating the top. The meat need not be submerged. Cover tightly with a lid or foil.
4. **Braise until genuinely tender:** Braise covered, starting tenderness checks around 3½ hours and allowing roughly 3½–5 hours, sometimes longer for a thick or firm piece. Check that the sauce is gently bubbling rather than drying or scorching; use hot water only if needed to keep the edges moist. A fork should enter the thickest part easily and turn with little resistance. The minimum whole-beef safety endpoint is 145°F / 63°C with a 3-minute rest; brisket needs further cooking for this tender braise. Continue cooking until the thickest meat reaches that tenderness.
5. **Cool safely and refrigerate overnight:** Move the cooked meat, every bit of sauce and the vegetables into shallow containers. Divide the meat into smaller pieces as needed and keep it in a single layer; retain its grain direction for slicing. Refrigerate promptly at 40°F / 4°C or below, within 2 hours of finishing cooking, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C. Do not cool the entire deep pan on the counter. Refrigerate overnight, about 8–12 hours, until cold throughout.
6. **Slice and reheat with all the sauce:** Heat the oven to 325°F / 163°C. Slice the cold brisket thinly across the grain and arrange in a shallow roasting dish. Bring all the sauce to a boil in a saucepan, stirring so the sweet sauce does not scorch; pour it and the carrots and onions over the slices. Cover and heat, checking around 45 minutes and allowing about 45–60 minutes or longer, until the meat reaches 165°F / 74°C throughout. Serve with the full sauce.

## Cooking Notes

Keep all of the sauce for serving. For a milder condiment balance, use half the displayed Worcestershire, Dijon and grape-jelly amounts; the other quantities and method stay the same. This reflects the low end of the original sauce ranges. Use separate complete pans when scaling beyond the vessel’s comfortable capacity.
