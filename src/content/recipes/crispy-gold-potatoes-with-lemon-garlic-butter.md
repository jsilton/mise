## Chef's Note

These are Ina Garten's roasted potatoes with a smoky twist. Smoked sea salt and sweet paprika season them before a hot roast that turns the cut faces deep brown and crisp. The garlic goes in at the end, warmed in butter, so it stays sweet instead of scorching on the pan.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 400°F convection with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Make sure the potatoes are completely dry.
2. **Season the potatoes:** Toss the potatoes with the olive oil, smoked salt, pepper and paprika. Divide them between two rimmed half-sheet pans, cut faces down, with space around each piece.
3. **Roast:** Roast for 20 minutes, then rotate the pans and swap racks. Turn the potatoes once their undersides are golden and release easily from the pan. Keep roasting until browned all over and a knife slides easily into the largest piece, 35 to 45 minutes in all.
4. **Make the garlic butter:** In the last few minutes of roasting, melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and stir until fragrant and pale gold, 1 to 2 minutes.
5. **Toss and serve:** Pour the garlic butter over the hot potatoes and toss them right on the pans. Taste for salt and serve right away.
