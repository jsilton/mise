## Chef's Note

Roast eggplant separately from the pepper and squash, then add each tray to the tomato sauce when its vegetables are tender. The source finish uses basil, red-pepper flakes, oregano and a measured teaspoon of olive oil. The lemon-finish variation stays separate so every oil and seasoning has one destination.

## Directions

1. **Prepare eggplant tray:** Heat the oven to 425°F with racks in the middle and upper third. Use large rimmed baking sheets, bare or lined with parchment rated for 425°F. Toss eggplant, extra-virgin olive oil, and fine sea salt on one tray, using all its oil and salt, then spread in one layer. Divide among more trays if necessary rather than crowding.
2. **Prepare other vegetables:** Toss large bell pepper, medium-to-large zucchini, large yellow squash, extra-virgin olive oil, and fine sea salt on a separate tray with all its oil and salt. Spread in one layer. Put the eggplant on the middle rack and the other vegetables on the upper rack; roast 15 minutes before the first stir.
3. **Start sauce while trays roast:** In a large Dutch oven or soup pot over medium heat, cook medium yellow onion, extra-virgin olive oil, and fine sea salt using all the base oil and salt, stirring until the onion is tender with some golden edges, about 8–10 minutes.
4. **Add garlic:** Stir cloves of garlic into the onion and cook about 30 seconds until fragrant, lowering heat if it begins to darken.
5. **Simmer tomatoes:** Add ripe tomato, using the full prepared amount, and stir up any browned bits. Reduce heat to maintain a gentle simmer while the trays finish; the sauce should thicken without catching.
6. **Finish trays separately and combine:** After the first 15 minutes, stir each tray and swap rack positions. Begin checking eggplant around 10 minutes later; when tender and golden at the edges, fold it into the simmering tomatoes. Keep the pepper and squash roasting until tender and browned at the edges, often another 5–10 minutes after the eggplant. Fold all the remaining vegetables and their oil into the sauce and simmer together 5 minutes. Check each vegetable rather than treating one roasting clock as universal.
7. **Finish off heat:** Remove from heat and stir extra-virgin olive oil, fresh basil, red pepper flakes, dried oregano, black pepper, fine sea salt, and fresh lemon juice (if using) through the full pot: use all the measured finishing oil, basil, red-pepper flakes and crumbled oregano for the source route, then black pepper and extra salt only to taste. The optional lemon is for the distinct variation below, not an automatic addition to the source finish.
8. **Serve:** Divide the whole pot into the listed portions. At the original batch, the six more-modest portions may instead serve four as generous mains. Use extra-virgin olive oil (if using) and fresh basil (if using) if desired. Serve hot or briefly cooled for room-temperature service; refrigerate promptly if waiting longer.
