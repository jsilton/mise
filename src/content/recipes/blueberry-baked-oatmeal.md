## Chef's Note

Berries form a juicy layer beneath the oats, with a smaller amount on top. The toasted seeds are mixed through the oats, rather than added only as a topping. Pour the milk mixture through the dry layer and press down any dry oat patches gently so the center cooks evenly. Half the measured butter goes into the mixture and the rest finishes the cooked oatmeal.

## Directions

1. **Prepare and divide berries:** Preheat the oven to 375°F and grease a 9-inch square baking dish for the original batch with the separate pan-fat allowance. Wash and drain fresh blueberries; use frozen berries without thawing. Use all the measured berries: reserve a small portion for the top and spread the rest over the bottom.

2. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Combine all the oats, toasted sunflower or pumpkin seeds, cinnamon, measured kosher salt and baking powder. Spread evenly over the bottom berries.

3. **Mix the wet ingredients:** Whisk the milk, eggs, maple syrup, vanilla and half the measured melted butter until combined. Keep the other half of the butter for finishing.

4. **Hydrate and top:** Pour the wet mixture evenly over the oats. Gently wiggle the dish to let the milk settle through, then press down any dry oats on the surface. Scatter all the reserved berries over the top. If using the optional raw sugar, scatter all its listed amount over the surface before baking.

5. **Bake:** Load only once the oven has reached 375°F. Bake about 40–45 minutes with fresh berries, or start checking around 45–50 minutes with frozen berries. Continue until the top is golden and the center is set without liquid egg mixture; an egg-dish center should reach 160°F. Berry temperature, dish material and deeper filling can extend cooking.

6. **Finish and rest:** Drizzle with all the remaining measured butter. Let stand for a few minutes so it holds together when portioned. Serve warm with optional yogurt or whipped cream, extra maple syrup or honey, and additional fresh fruit as desired. These serving allowances are separate from the measured batter. Refrigerate promptly for cold service.
