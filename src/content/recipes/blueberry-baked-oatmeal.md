## Chef's Note

This is Kathryne Taylor's baked oatmeal from Cookie and Kate, made nut-free with toasted seeds in place of pecans. Most of the berries go under the oats, where they bake into a jammy layer, and the rest burst on top. Press the oats down into the milk so every bit soaks and the center bakes creamy.

## Directions

1. **Line the dish with berries:** Heat the oven to 375°F and butter a 9-inch square baking dish. Spread about two thirds of the blueberries over the bottom.
2. **Mix the dry ingredients:** In a bowl, stir together the oats, seeds, cinnamon, baking powder and salt. Spread the mixture over the berries.
3. **Mix the wet ingredients:** Whisk the milk, eggs, maple syrup, vanilla and half the melted butter until smooth.
4. **Pour and top:** Pour the milk mixture over the oats. Wiggle the dish so the liquid sinks through, and press down any dry oats on top. Scatter the rest of the berries over the top and sprinkle with the sugar.
5. **Bake:** Bake until the top is golden and the center is set with no liquid when you press it, 40 to 45 minutes, or 45 to 50 minutes with frozen berries.
6. **Finish and serve:** Drizzle the rest of the butter over the top and let it rest for 5 minutes. Serve warm with yogurt and more maple syrup.
