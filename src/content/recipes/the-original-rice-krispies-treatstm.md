## Chef's Note

Melt the butter and fresh marshmallows gently over low heat, then remove the pan from heat before adding the salt, vanilla and cereal. Stir until the marshmallows have melted fully; leaving a prescribed fraction unmelted is not needed. Use the full butter and cereal amounts, and press gently instead of packing the mixture firmly.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** For the original batch, lightly grease a 9×13×2-inch pan and spatula with butter or cooking spray. Have wax paper available for pressing. Scaling needs proportional pan area or additional pans to keep a similar layer thickness; do not cram extra full batches into the original pan.
2. **Melt gently:** Use fresh marshmallows and unsalted butter in a large saucepan: melt the butter over low heat, then add the marshmallows and stir until completely melted and smooth. Remove from heat; there is no fixed stovetop melting time.
3. **Flavor off heat:** Stir sea salt and pure vanilla extract into the melted mixture off the heat.
4. **Coat the cereal:** Fold in crisp rice cereal until evenly coated, scraping the bottom and sides. Use the full cereal amount.
5. **Press:** Transfer the full mixture to the prepared pan, or divide across the selected proportional pans. Use the greased spatula or wax paper to press gently into an even layer; avoid compacting it firmly.
6. **Cool:** Let cool at room temperature until set and cool enough to cut. Thirty minutes is the current planning allowance, not a guaranteed finish; thickness and room conditions can change it.
7. **Cut and serve:** Cut into the listed number of planning portions, or smaller squares as desired. The original two-inch-square suggestion is a cutting option, not a claim that a 9×13-inch pan produces twelve exact two-inch squares. Serve the same day for best texture.

## Cooking Notes

### Marshmallow alternatives

Choose ONE marshmallow route. The full listed 10 oz fresh marshmallows is the main version. For the original 6-cup cereal batch, the publisher’s alternatives are 5½ cups miniature marshmallows OR one 7 oz jar of marshmallow crème in place of all the marshmallows. Scale the chosen total with the cereal, keeping jar size fixed while scaling the jar count or crème weight. The miniature volume describes the source product, not a universal weight conversion for every marshmallow. Keep all the current butter, cereal, salt and vanilla amounts in either option. Diet, reduced-calorie and tub margarine are not recommended by the source; the main uses the full unsalted butter.

### Microwave route

Use a microwave-safe bowl with enough room for the butter and marshmallows to expand and for the full cereal to be folded in afterward. For the original batch, microwave the full butter and chosen marshmallow amount on HIGH for the source reference of 3 minutes, stirring after 2 minutes, then stir until smooth. Times vary with the microwave and volume; follow its instructions and the smooth-melt endpoint rather than multiplying the clock when scaling. Stir in the full salt and vanilla off heat, fold in the entire cereal, then press, cool and cut as above. The bowl capacity and microwave wattage are not specified in the source; divide a larger batch or use suitable larger equipment instead of assuming it fits.
