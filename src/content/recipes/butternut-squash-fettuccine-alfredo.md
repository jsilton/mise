## Chef's Note

Roast the squash until a fork passes through its center, then purée it with all the cream, broth and Parmesan. The garlic should be fragrant rather than blackened. Use reserved pasta water only as the finished sauce needs it; no fixed amount must go into the bowl.

## Directions

1. **Roast:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Wash the squash before peeling and cutting; measure the listed cubed amount. Toss all squash and minced garlic with all the olive oil and the listed salt and pepper. Spread in one layer on a rimmed tray, using additional trays if needed. Begin checking at 25 minutes and continue until the centers yield easily to a fork and edges have golden patches, often around 30 minutes. Stir if needed and stop before the garlic blackens; transfer all squash, garlic and their oil for the sauce.

2. **Cool and purée:** Let the squash cool for the source’s initial 10-minute rest, then continue cooling if required by the actual blender or food-processor manual. That rest alone is not permission to load hot food. Never use a sealed personal cup for hot food; use permitted loads and temperatures. Blend all the squash, garlic and retained oil with all the heavy cream, broth, measured Parmesan and nutmeg until smooth. Use approved smaller batches and recombine them without adding liquid merely to reach the blades.

3. **Cook pasta:** While the squash roasts or cools, boil the full dry fettuccine in the listed cooking-water and salt allowance to al dente according to its package. Reserve 1/2 cup cooking water for the original batch, proportionally adjusted with the batch size, then drain. If equipment-required cooling takes longer, delay cooking the pasta so it is ready near the sauce.

4. **Warm and combine:** Return pasta to a roomy pot and add all the puréed sauce. Toss over low heat until the sauce coats the noodles and the food is hot throughout, especially if it was cooled for blending. Scrape the full sauce from the processor; do not discard cream or cheese to thin it.

5. **Adjust:** Add the reserved pasta-water supply a tablespoon at a time only until the sauce coats the noodles without a dry paste or a watery pool. Reserve unused water rather than pouring it all in automatically.

6. **Serve:** Distribute all the chopped parsley and serve promptly with the separate optional extra Parmesan.
