## Chef's Note

The Kitchn's weeknight twist on Alfredo blends roasted butternut squash with cream and Parmesan into a silky, golden sauce. Roasting the squash until its edges caramelize gives the sauce real depth, and a little nutmeg makes it taste rich instead of sweet.

## Directions

1. **Roast the squash:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the squash and garlic with the olive oil and a good pinch of salt and pepper and spread them in one layer on a rimmed sheet pan. Roast, stirring once, until the squash is fork-tender with golden edges, 25 to 30 minutes.
2. **Blend the sauce:** Let the squash cool for 10 minutes. Scrape it into a blender with the garlic and oil, then add the cream, broth, Parmesan and nutmeg. Blend until completely smooth, with the lid's center cap off and a towel over the top to let steam out.
3. **Cook the pasta:** While the squash roasts, bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the fettuccine until al dente, 10 to 12 minutes. Scoop out a mug of the pasta water, then drain.
4. **Toss with the sauce:** Return the pasta to the pot over low heat. Add the sauce and toss until it coats every noodle and is hot, 1 to 2 minutes. Loosen it with pasta water a splash at a time until glossy, then season with salt and pepper.
5. **Serve:** Scatter with parsley and serve with more Parmesan.
