## Chef's Note

This slow-cooker adaptation pairs a little red wine with beefy onion seasoning, mushrooms and sweet carrots. Scraping the chicken’s browned bits into the wine adds flavor without another pan; keep the sauce lightly coating so it carries through the rice.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the vegetables:** Lightly spray a 5–6 quart slow-cooker crock. Put the 1/2-inch carrot pieces on the bottom, then add pearl onions, mushrooms and garlic. The pearl onions can go in frozen; keep the chicken refrigerated until the pan is ready.
2. **Brown the chicken:** Pat the thighs dry and season with the 1/2 tsp black pepper. Heat a lightly greased skillet over medium-high heat. Brown in uncrowded batches, about 2–3 minutes per side, until golden patches form. Transfer directly to the vegetables. Do not refrigerate or pause the recipe with partly cooked chicken.
3. **Deglaze:** Take the skillet briefly off the heat and pour in the measured 1/2 cup wine. Return to heat and scrape up brown bits for about 30–60 seconds; do not reduce the wine by half. Pour into the cooker, leaving any black, burned residue behind.
4. **Season:** Mix the 1 tsp cornstarch with 1 tsp cold water until smooth. Stir it into the cooker with the 1 tsp Worcestershire, 1 tsp thyme, one envelope Lipton Beefy Onion Soup Mix and 1 tsp garlic powder. Distribute seasoning through the liquid as best you can. No stock is needed by default; the vegetables and chicken release liquid. Cover and start cooking immediately.
5. **Slow-cook:** Cook on HIGH, beginning checks at 2 1/2 hours. The original range is 2 1/2–3 hours, but use the food as the endpoint: several thighs must reach at least 165°F / 74°C in the thickest part, separate easily with a fork, and carrots must yield to a knife. Continue if necessary. If chicken is ready first, lift it out and finish firm carrots in a saucepan with some sauce.
6. **Check the sauce:** If you like its consistency, leave it alone. If watery, lift out the chicken and vegetables, transfer cooking liquid to a saucepan and bring to a simmer. Re-stir the optional 1 tsp cornstarch plus 1 tsp cold-water slurry; add gradually while stirring and simmer about 1 minute, stopping at a light coating consistency. Do not reduce an already salty sauce to thicken it.
7. **Serve:** Taste a bite of chicken, sauce and cooked rice together before adjusting pepper, garlic powder or salt. Return chicken and vegetables to the hot sauce if separated and serve over rice. Wine remains an ingredient after cooking; this is not an alcohol-free dish.
