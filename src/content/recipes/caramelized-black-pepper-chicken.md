## Chef's Note

Charles Phan of The Slanted Door made this Vietnamese caramel chicken famous, with black pepper as the main flavor instead of a background note. Brown sugar stands in for a dark homemade caramel, and fish sauce and rice vinegar balance the sweetness. It is punchy by design, so serve it with plenty of plain rice.

## Directions

1. **Mix the sauce:** Stir together the brown sugar, fish sauce, water, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic, black pepper and chiles in a bowl.
2. **Brown the chicken:** Pat the chicken dry. Heat half the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Add half the chicken in a single layer and cook until golden in patches, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Move it to a plate and repeat with the rest of the oil and chicken. It finishes cooking in the sauce.
3. **Soften the shallot:** Lower the heat to medium. Add the shallot and cook, stirring, until soft, about 3 minutes. Add a splash of water if the browned bits start to darken too fast.
4. **Reduce the sauce:** Pour in the sauce and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer, stirring, until the sugar dissolves and the sauce coats a spoon, 2 to 4 minutes.
5. **Glaze the chicken:** Return the chicken and its juices to the pan. Simmer, turning the pieces, until the glaze is thick and glossy and the chicken reads 165°F in the center, 3 to 6 minutes. Add a splash of water if the glaze turns sticky before the chicken is done.
6. **Serve:** Scatter with cilantro and serve with rice.
