## Chef's Note

Sunomono means "vinegared thing," and it is the small, bright side that balances a rich Japanese meal. Slice the cucumbers paper-thin and salt them briefly to draw out water, so the sweet-sour dressing stays sharp. Serve it alongside curry, katsu or a rice bowl.

## Directions

1. **Slice the cucumbers:** Slice the cucumbers into paper-thin rounds with a sharp knife or a mandoline.
2. **Salt and drain:** Toss the slices with the salt for the cucumbers in a colander set over a bowl. Let them sit for 10 minutes. Gently squeeze small handfuls to press out the water, then put them in a clean bowl.
3. **Mix the dressing:** While the cucumbers drain, stir the rice vinegar, sugar and salt for the dressing until no grains remain. Stir in the ginger, if using.
4. **Dress and chill:** Pour the dressing over the cucumbers and toss. Refrigerate for 5 to 30 minutes.
5. **Serve:** Divide the cucumbers and a little of their dressing among small bowls. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and shichimi, if using.
