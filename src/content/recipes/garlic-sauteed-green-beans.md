## Chef's Note

A hot skillet blisters the beans in spots, and a quick covered steam makes them tender all the way through. Sliced garlic goes in at the end over lower heat so it turns pale gold, not bitter. The squeeze of lemon is what makes them taste bright.

## Directions

1. **Brown the beans:** Heat most of the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Add the beans in a shallow layer and leave them alone for 2 minutes. Toss and cook until browned in spots, 3 to 5 minutes more.
2. **Steam until tender:** Turn the heat to medium, add a splash of water and cover. Cook until the beans are tender with a little bite, 2 to 4 minutes, then uncover and let any water cook off.
3. **Add the garlic:** Turn the heat to medium-low. Push the beans to one side and add the rest of the oil, the garlic and the pepper flakes to the gap. Stir until the garlic is fragrant and pale gold, 30 to 60 seconds, then toss it through the beans.
4. **Season and serve:** Take the pan off the heat. Season with salt and pepper, add the lemon juice and toss. Serve right away.
