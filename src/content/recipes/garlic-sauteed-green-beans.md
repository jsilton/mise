## Chef's Note

Brown the beans first, then check a bite before adding garlic. If the center is still firm, a small splash of water and a brief covered steam can finish it without burning the surface. Lower the heat for the sliced garlic and add the measured lemon off heat.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Trim, wash and thoroughly dry the beans. Slice garlic and measure lemon juice. Set aside **one-quarter of the oil for garlic**; the remainder is for cooking the beans.
2. **Brown:** Heat **the bean oil** in a broad skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Add beans in a shallow layer with room to toss, splitting beans and this oil between rounds if crowded. Leave undisturbed about **2 minutes**, then toss and cook another **3–5 minutes** until browned in patches. Reduce heat if they scorch.
3. **Check the center:** Taste a cooled bean. If still firm, lower heat to medium, add a **small splash of water carefully** and cover for **2–4 minutes**, then check again. Add another small splash only if the pan dries before the beans become tender. Uncover and let excess water evaporate. Skip steaming if already tender. If browning in rounds, finish their tenderness separately before combining.
4. **Add garlic gently:** Return all beans to the pan if using rounds. Reduce heat to medium-low and move the skillet off the burner briefly if still very hot. Push beans aside, add **the reserved oil**, garlic and optional pepper flakes. Stir for about **30–60 seconds** until fragrant and lightly golden, then toss through the beans.
5. **Season and serve:** Remove from heat. Add salt, black pepper and **the measured lemon juice**. Toss and taste, then divide into side portions and serve promptly.
