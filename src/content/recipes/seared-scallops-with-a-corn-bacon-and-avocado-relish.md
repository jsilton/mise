## Chef's Note

Gibson Borelli's summer plate sets seared sea scallops on a warm relish of fresh corn, bacon and avocado. Buy dry-packed scallops and pat them very dry so they take a deep golden crust in a couple of minutes. Cooking the corn in the bacon fat ties the smoky pork to the sweet seafood.

## Directions

1. **Cook the bacon:** Cook the bacon in a large skillet over medium heat, in two batches, until crisp, 8 to 10 minutes per batch. Drain it on paper towels and chop it. Pour off all but a thin layer of fat.
2. **Cut the corn:** While the bacon cooks, stand each ear on an upside-down small bowl set inside a large bowl and slice off the kernels. Add the bell pepper, onion and half the parsley.
3. **Sauté the corn:** Cook the corn mixture in the bacon fat over medium heat, stirring, until the onion softens and the corn is lightly browned, about 5 minutes. Take the pan off the heat and let it cool for 2 minutes.
4. **Finish the relish:** Gently fold in the avocado and bacon. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper and spoon onto four plates.
5. **Sear the scallops:** Wipe out the skillet, add a thin film of oil and heat over high heat until it just begins to smoke. Pat the scallops dry and season with salt. Sear in two batches, without moving them, until a deep golden crust forms, about 2 minutes. Turn and cook until just opaque in the center, about 1 minute more.
6. **Serve:** Set the scallops on the relish. Squeeze the lemon over the top and scatter with the rest of the parsley.
