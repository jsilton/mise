## Chef's Note

Dry sea scallops and a dry surface help the pan brown them, but a golden crust does not establish that the centers are cooked. The corn relish uses the full cooked bacon and avocado; sauté the corn and onion in neutral oil, then fold in the bacon and avocado off the heat. Lemon and parsley finish both components.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the Relish:** Use an upside-down small bowl inside a large bowl as a stand to cut corn kernels off the cobs. Add diced onion, bell pepper and half the measured parsley. Have the fully cooked chopped bacon and diced avocado ready. Pat the scallops dry with paper towels and keep them refrigerated until searing.

2. **Sauté:** Heat the measured corn oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the corn mixture for about 5 minutes, stirring, until the onion begins to soften; caramelization is optional, not a stopping requirement. Use more pan space or separate proportional batches if crowded. Take off the heat and let cool for about 2 minutes before folding in the avocado.

3. **Finish the Relish:** Fold all the diced avocado and fully cooked bacon into the warm corn mixture. Season with the relish salt and pepper to taste. Keep the relish and serving utensils away from raw scallops.

4. **Sear the Scallops:** Wipe the skillet and coat with the separate searing oil over medium-high heat. Season scallops with their salt and pepper allowance. Add in a single uncrowded layer; use additional pans or batches as needed. Sear until the underside develops a golden crust, then turn. Check the center of a thick scallop with an instant-read thermometer inserted from the side; cook to 145°F. The flesh should be firm, pearly, and opaque throughout. Lower the heat if the crust darkens before the center is done. Remove each scallop as it finishes; size and pan heat determine the time. Let them stand on a clean plate while you divide the relish.

5. **Assemble:** Divide all the warm corn-avocado-bacon relish among the portions, then add the measured scallops. The original batch plans four scallops per portion; portion sizes depend on actual scallop size.

6. **Finish:** Squeeze the measured lemon over the scallops and garnish with the remaining half of the parsley. Serve promptly.

## Relish Variation

For the original relish preparation, use medium onion in place of the listed small-onion quantity, and use enough neutral oil to coat the corn pan in place of the measured corn oil. Keep the full corn, bacon, avocado, pepper, parsley, lemon and scallop quantities. Use the same separate searing oil, doneness checks and batch guidance.
