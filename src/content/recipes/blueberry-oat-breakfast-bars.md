## Chef's Note

These are British-style flapjacks from Ciara Attwell of My Fussy Eater, made with honey, nut butter and fresh blueberries. Press the oats in hard and chill the slab for a full hour. As the coconut oil and nut butter firm up, they bind the oats into bars that travel well.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the pan:** Heat the oven to 340°F. Line an 8-inch square baking dish with parchment, leaving some overhang to lift the slab out.
2. **Melt the binder:** In a large saucepan over medium heat, melt the coconut oil, honey, nut butter and salt, stirring until smooth, about 2 minutes. Take the pan off the heat.
3. **Coat the oats:** Stir in the oats until every flake is coated. Gently fold in the blueberries.
4. **Press the slab:** Scrape the mixture into the dish. Press it down very firmly with the back of a spoon, right into the corners, into an even, compact layer.
5. **Bake:** Bake until the edges just turn golden, 15 to 18 minutes.
6. **Cool and chill:** Cool in the pan for 20 minutes, then refrigerate until firm, at least 1 hour or overnight.
7. **Drizzle and cut:** Whisk the yogurt and orange juice together and drizzle over the cold slab. Lift it out with the parchment and cut into 8 bars. Serve cold.
