## Chef's Note

Press the coated oats firmly into the corners as well as the center of the pan. Baking to lightly golden edges is followed by cooling and a long cold rest; cut the bars only once the slab is firm. The choice of coconut oil or butter and nut butter affects the texture, so chilling helps the bars hold together without guaranteeing that every nut butter sets alike.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 340°F (170°C). Line an 8-inch square baking dish with parchment for the original batch, leaving enough paper to lift the cooled slab. Wash and drain the fresh blueberries.

2. **Melt:** Gently melt all the measured coconut oil or butter, honey or maple syrup, and almond or peanut butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Stir until smooth and combined, then take off the heat.

3. **Coat and fold:** Stir in all the oats until evenly coated. Gently fold in all the drained blueberries without deliberately crushing them.

4. **Press:** Transfer the whole mixture to the prepared dish. Press firmly with the back of a spoon across the base and into the corners, making an even, compact layer.

5. **Bake:** Load only once the oven has reached 340°F / 170°C. Bake about 15–18 minutes, until the edges are just beginning to turn golden. The hot slab will still be delicate; browning is followed by cooling and chilling.

6. **Cool and chill:** Let the slab cool in the pan until manageable, then refrigerate promptly for at least 1 hour and until firm enough to cut; overnight chilling is an option. Keep the slab in its pan while it sets.

7. **Drizzle:** Whisk all the measured yogurt or coconut cream with all the orange juice until smooth. Drizzle over the cold, firm slab.

8. **Cut and serve:** Lift with the parchment and cut into the listed number of bars; the original batch plans eight. Serve cold, or let individual bars stand briefly for serving. Return the rest to the refrigerator.
