## Chef's Note

Chill this cutout dough until firm, then let it become just pliable enough to roll without cracking. Keep the thickness even and bake similarly sized shapes together. Cool the cookies completely before adding icing.

## Directions

1. **Dry mix:** Sift the measured flour, sea salt and baking powder together into a bowl. Keep any flour for dusting the rolling surface separate from the measured dough flour.

2. **Cream:** With a paddle attachment, beat the softened butter and sugar until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes. Scrape the bowl, beat in the eggs one at a time, then add the vanilla.

3. **Mix and chill:** Gradually mix in the dry ingredients only until evenly incorporated. Divide the original batch into 2 disks; use additional manageable disks for a larger batch. Wrap tightly and refrigerate for at least 2 hours and up to 2 days.

4. **Prepare to roll:** When ready to bake, start preheating the oven to 375°F and line sheets with parchment. If dough chilled longer than 2 hours is too hard to roll, let it soften slightly at room temperature for 15–30 minutes, just until pliable. Work with one disk while keeping the other covered and refrigerated.

5. **Roll and cut:** Lightly flour the surface and rolling pin using the separate dusting allowance. Roll to 1/4-inch thickness and cut with 2 1/2-inch cutters, or similarly sized shapes. Reroll the scraps gently. Place 1 inch apart on the prepared sheets; bake different sizes in separate loads.

6. **Bake:** Load only after the oven reaches 375°F. Bake one sheet at a time for 10–12 minutes, beginning to check at 8 minutes, until the bottom edges just begin to turn golden brown. Portion size, shape and actual oven determine when they are ready.

7. **Cool and decorate:** Let the cookies firm on the sheet for about 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack and cool completely. Serve plain or decorate the cool cookies with separately prepared [Royal Icing](/mise/recipes/royal-icing). Use the icing recipe’s pasteurized-egg-white route. Allow the sheet to cool before loading more dough.

## Cooking Notes

For other cutter sizes, keep the same 1/4-inch dough thickness and bake similarly sized pieces together. Their finished count and baking time will differ from the original 2 1/2-inch cutter route.

Plain cookies need no icing. Royal icing, melted chocolate, buttercream or powdered-sugar finishes are optional, separately prepared decorations; no amounts are included in this dough. Their ingredients and storage requirements must be planned separately.
