## Chef's Note

Gambas al ajillo is the tapas bar classic of Madrid, shrimp sizzled in garlic, chile and olive oil. It cooks in under five minutes, so have everything by the stove before the pan goes on. Use your best olive oil, because it becomes the sauce, and bring plenty of bread to soak it up.

## Directions

1. **Season the shrimp:** Pat the shrimp dry and toss them with the salt and smoked paprika.
2. **Toast the garlic:** Warm the olive oil in a cast-iron skillet or cazuela over medium heat until it shimmers. Add the garlic and guindillas and cook, swirling the pan, until the garlic is pale golden and fragrant, 30 to 45 seconds. Lower the heat if it colors too fast.
3. **Sear the shrimp:** Raise the heat to medium-high and add the shrimp in a single layer. Cook without moving for 1 minute, then flip and cook for 30 seconds more.
4. **Add the sherry:** Pour in the sherry and let it bubble until the shrimp are pink, opaque and curled into a loose C, about 30 seconds.
5. **Finish and serve:** Take the pan off the heat and stir in the lemon juice and parsley. Bring the skillet to the table still sizzling, with crusty bread for the oil.
