## Chef's Note

These are The Woks of Life family's all-purpose pork and greens dumplings, the kind you fold by the hundred and freeze for busy nights. Squeeze the greens dry and stir the filling hard in one direction until it turns sticky. That keeps the dumplings juicy and the filling in one tender, springy piece.

## Directions

1. **Blanch the greens:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Blanch the greens until just wilted, about 1 minute, then drain and rinse under cold water.
2. **Chop the greens:** Squeeze out as much water as you can, then chop the greens very finely. A few pulses in a food processor work too.
3. **Mix the filling:** Put the greens, pork, Shaoxing wine, neutral oil, sesame oil, salt, soy sauce, white pepper and water in a large bowl. Stir hard in one direction until the filling turns sticky and paste-like, 6 to 8 minutes.
4. **Test the seasoning:** Fold one dumpling and boil it until cooked through, about 5 minutes. Taste it and add a little more salt or soy sauce to the filling if it needs it.
5. **Fold the dumplings:** Dampen the edge of a wrapper with water and put a small spoonful of filling in the center. Fold it in half, pinch the top closed, then make two pleats on each side and pinch to seal. Set the dumplings on parchment-lined trays, not touching, and keep the wrappers you aren't using covered.
6. **Boil:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add the dumplings in batches, return to a boil and cook until they float and the filling is cooked through with no pink, 6 to 8 minutes.
7. **Serve:** Lift the dumplings out with a slotted spoon and serve hot with black vinegar, chili oil or dipping sauce.
