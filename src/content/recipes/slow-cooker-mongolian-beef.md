## Chef's Note

Mongolian beef is a Chinese-American takeout favorite: thin slices of flank steak in a glossy soy and brown sugar sauce. Tossing the beef in cornstarch before it goes into the cooker thickens the sauce as it cooks. Flank is lean, so it cooks on high and comes out as soon as it is tender.

## Directions

1. **Coat the beef:** Toss the steak with the cornstarch in a bowl until every slice is coated. Tip the beef and any loose cornstarch into the slow cooker.
2. **Add the sauce:** Whisk the soy sauce, brown sugar, water, sesame oil, garlic and ginger together. Pour over the beef and stir to coat.
3. **Slow-cook:** Cover and cook on high until the beef is tender and the sauce is thick and glossy, 1 1/2 to 2 hours. Start checking at 1 1/2 hours. Flank turns dry and stringy if it cooks much longer, so skip the low setting.
4. **Finish:** Stir in the green onions and serve right away.
