## Chef's Note

Chicken braised with apricots, almonds and warm spices draws on the sweet and savory cooking of Morocco. This one simmers in a Dutch oven with chickpeas and preserved lemon, whose salty, floral bite keeps the apricots in check. Bone-in thighs give the sauce its body.

## Directions

1. **Season the chicken:** Pat the thighs dry and season them all over with the salt for the chicken and the pepper.
2. **Sear the chicken:** Heat half the oil in a wide Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear half the thighs skin-side down until deep golden, 6 to 8 minutes, then turn and cook 2 minutes more. Move them to a plate and repeat with the rest of the oil and chicken.
3. **Cook the onions:** Pour off all but a thin layer of fat. Cook the onions over medium heat, stirring, until soft and light golden, 10 to 12 minutes.
4. **Bloom the spices:** Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds. Add the cumin, ginger, paprika, turmeric, coriander and cayenne and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in the tomato paste and cook until it darkens, about 1 minute.
5. **Start the braise:** Pour in the stock and scrape up the browned bits. Add the apricots, cinnamon stick and saffron with its water. Nestle the chicken back in skin-side up with its juices, so the liquid comes partway up the meat. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover, lower the heat and cook for 30 minutes.
6. **Toast the almonds:** While the chicken braises, toast the almonds in a dry skillet over medium-low heat, stirring, until golden, 3 to 5 minutes. Tip them onto a plate.
7. **Add the chickpeas:** Stir the chickpeas and preserved lemon into the sauce around the chicken. Cover and simmer until the chicken pulls easily from the bone, 15 to 20 minutes. Remove the cinnamon stick.
8. **Season the sauce:** If the sauce is thin, lift out the chicken and simmer the sauce uncovered until spoonable. Season with the salt for the sauce a little at a time, since the preserved lemon is salty. Stir in the honey if the sauce tastes sharp, then the lemon juice and half the herbs. Return the chicken to the pot.
9. **Serve:** Scatter the almonds and the rest of the herbs over the top.
