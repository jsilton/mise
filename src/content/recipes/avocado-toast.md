## Chef's Note

Avocado toast is only as good as its bread and its avocado. Thick sourdough toasted until truly crunchy holds up under the topping, and its tang plays against the rich avocado. Mash the avocado roughly with plenty of citrus and salt, since without them it tastes flat.

## Directions

1. **Toast the bread:** Toast the bread until deep golden and crisp all the way through, so a slice stays rigid when you hold it flat.
2. **Mash the avocado:** Scoop the avocado into a bowl. Add the citrus juice and a generous pinch of flaky salt, then mash roughly with a fork, leaving some chunks.
3. **Assemble:** Spread the avocado over the toast all the way to the edges. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with flaky salt and red pepper flakes.
4. **Top and serve:** Add the eggs, pickled onions or everything seasoning, if using. Eat right away.
