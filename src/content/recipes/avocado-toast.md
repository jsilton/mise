## Chef's Note

Ripe avocado should yield gently under pressure. Mash it roughly with the full listed citrus juice, then spread it on sturdy, deeply golden toast. Add the oil drizzle and chosen toppings at serving so the bread keeps its crunch.

## Directions

1. **Toast:** Toast all the listed bread slices until deeply golden with crisp edges and a sturdy surface, using the toaster’s suitable setting. Extra slices may need additional rounds.

2. **Mash:** Wash and dry the avocado before cutting. Halve it, remove the pit and scoop all flesh into a bowl. Add all the listed lemon or lime juice and some of the unmeasured flaky salt; mash with a fork, leaving some pieces.

3. **Assemble:** Divide all the avocado mixture among the listed bread slices.

4. **Season:** Drizzle the listed unmeasured olive oil over the toasts. Add flaky salt and red-pepper flakes to taste.

5. **Choose toppings:** Add any desired listed toppings, preparing them separately: washed cherry tomatoes, radishes or microgreens, prepared pickled onions, everything-bagel seasoning, or an already-cooked egg. For ordinary shell eggs, cook whites and yolks until firm; if choosing the soft-yolk variation, use shell eggs treated by pasteurization or another approved Salmonella-control method. Cut cooked egg into portions for the toasts. Egg cooking time is separate from the toast clock.

6. **Serve:** Serve promptly. The bread softens under the avocado and toppings; the avocado darkens with air exposure.
