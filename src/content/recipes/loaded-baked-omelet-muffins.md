## Chef's Note

Use cooked bacon and thawed spinach squeezed free of excess water. Divide the finely diced vegetables among the cups, then add the eggs and cheese. The filling supplies savory flavor; a crisp bottom is not guaranteed once it is surrounded by moist egg.

## Directions

1. **Prep:** Preheat the oven to 350 °F. Generously spray standard muffin cups, including the sides, and set the tin on a baking sheet. Start with cooked, chopped bacon. Prepare the frozen spinach according to its package if cooking is required, cool enough to handle, squeeze dry and measure the listed spinach amount after draining. These preparations add time if they are not already done.

2. **The Base:** Divide the cooked bacon, squeezed spinach, finely diced tomato, onion and pepper among the cups. The original batch uses about 12 standard cups; different quantities need more or fewer cups at the same size and fill depth.

3. **The Eggs:** Whisk the eggs, measured kosher salt and black pepper until the whites and yolks are blended. Do not whip to a thick foam.

4. **Assemble:** Divide the eggs evenly among the filled cups, leaving about one-quarter headroom. Sprinkle all the cheddar over the tops. Use additional cups if needed rather than pouring over the rim.

5. **Bake:** Bake for about 20–25 minutes, until the centers are set and opaque with no liquid egg, and reach 165 °F / 74 °C. Check a center without touching the metal tin; surface color does not replace the temperature check.

6. **Cool and store:** Let stand 5 minutes before loosening and removing. Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers and use within 3–4 days, or freeze individually for up to 3 months for quality. Reheat the center to 165 °F / 74 °C; a fixed 45-second microwave time cannot cover chilled and frozen portions.
