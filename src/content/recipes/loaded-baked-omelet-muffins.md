## Chef's Note

A make-ahead breakfast of eggs baked in a muffin tin over bacon, spinach, tomato and softened pepper and onion. Squeeze the spinach bone-dry and cook the onion and pepper first so the muffins bake up tender, not watery.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Spray a 12-cup muffin tin well, sides included.
2. **Soften the onion and pepper:** Warm the oil or bacon fat in a small skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion and bell pepper, stirring, until soft, 3 to 4 minutes.
3. **Fill the cups:** Divide the bacon, spinach, tomato and the onion and pepper among the cups.
4. **Add the eggs:** Whisk the eggs, salt and pepper until smooth and evenly yellow. Pour into the cups until each is about three-quarters full and sprinkle the cheddar on top.
5. **Bake:** Bake until puffed and set in the center with no liquid egg, 20 to 25 minutes. Cool 5 minutes, then run a knife around each muffin and lift it out.
