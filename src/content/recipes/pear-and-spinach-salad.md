## Chef's Note

A strong honey-Dijon dressing needs only a light hand on baby spinach. Soak the onion while preparing everything else, dry it well and slice the pear just before tossing. Taste a bite with fruit before adding more dressing. Adapted from [Chowhound’s Pear and Spinach Salad](https://www.chowhound.com/recipes/pear-and-spinach-salad-27663).

## Directions

1. **Soak onion:** Put thinly sliced onion in ice water for 10 minutes. Meanwhile wash and dry spinach if needed and prepare the dressing. Drain onion and pat thoroughly dry.

2. **Whisk dressing:** Combine Dijon, vinegar, the measured dressing water, olive oil and honey. Whisk until smooth. Add salt and pepper to taste; the mustard already contains salt.

3. **Assemble:** Core and thinly slice pear. Put spinach, pear and dry onion in a wide bowl. Start with about one-third of the mixed dressing and lift gently to coat. Taste a leaf with pear, then add more dressing only as needed; reserve any excess.

4. **Serve:** Adjust black pepper if desired and divide among side plates. Serve immediately while spinach retains its texture.
