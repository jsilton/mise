## Chef's Note

This is a simple fall salad of sweet pear, tender spinach and a honey Dijon vinaigrette. A quick soak in ice water takes the harsh edge off the red onion so it stays crisp but mild. Dress the leaves lightly and serve right away.

## Directions

1. **Soak the onion:** Put the onion in a bowl of ice water for 10 minutes. Drain and pat it completely dry.
2. **Make the dressing:** While the onion soaks, whisk the Dijon, vinegar and honey in a small bowl. Slowly whisk in the olive oil until the dressing thickens slightly. Season with salt and pepper.
3. **Toss the salad:** Put the spinach, pear and onion in a wide bowl. Drizzle with about half the dressing and toss gently until the leaves are lightly glossy. Taste and add more dressing if it needs it.
4. **Serve:** Finish with a few grinds of black pepper and serve right away.
