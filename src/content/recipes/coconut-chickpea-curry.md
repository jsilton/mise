## Chef's Note

Jessica Hylton's coconut chickpea curry gets its warmth from a generous spoonful of garam masala and its brightness from lime. Brown the onion well in coconut oil and bloom the spices in that fat before the tomatoes go in. That is what gives a quick pantry curry real depth.

## Directions

1. **Brown the onion:** Heat the coconut oil in a wide pot over medium heat. Cook the onion, stirring often, until soft and golden at the edges, 8 to 10 minutes.
2. **Bloom the spices:** Stir in the garlic, garam masala, curry powder, cumin and red pepper flakes, if using. Cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds, without letting them darken.
3. **Cook down the tomatoes:** Add the tomatoes and their juice, the salt and the pepper, scraping the bottom of the pot. Cook uncovered, stirring and pressing the tomatoes, until thick and jammy, 5 to 7 minutes.
4. **Simmer the curry:** If using coconut flour, whisk it into a little of the coconut milk. Stir the chickpeas and all the coconut milk into the pot. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered over medium-low, stirring now and then, until the sauce is creamy and coats a spoon, 10 to 12 minutes. Add a splash of hot water if it gets too thick.
5. **Finish and serve:** Turn off the heat. Stir in the lime juice a little at a time, tasting as you go, then season with salt. Scatter with cilantro and serve with rice.
