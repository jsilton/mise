## Chef's Note

Bone-in chicken legs slow-cook with dried shiitake, garlic and scallion into a rich broth for this home-style ramen. Kombu steeped in at the end and miso whisked in just before serving give it deep savor. Keep the miso off the boil so it stays fragrant.

## Directions

1. **Start the broth:** Put the chicken legs, water, scallion whites, garlic, shiitake and mirin in a 6-quart slow cooker. Refrigerate the scallion greens for serving.
2. **Slow-cook:** Cover and cook on Low until the meat pulls easily from the bone, 5 to 6 hours.
3. **Strain the broth:** Lift out the chicken and mushrooms. Strain the broth into a 4-quart saucepan and skim off as much fat as you like.
4. **Steep the kombu:** Heat the broth until steaming but not boiling. Add the kombu, take the pan off the heat and let it steep for 10 minutes, then remove it. Meanwhile, shred the chicken, discarding the skin and bones, and slice the mushroom caps, discarding the stems.
5. **Cook the eggs:** If using eggs, bring a large pot of water to a boil. Lower in the eggs and simmer for 7 minutes for jammy yolks. Cool them in ice water, then peel and halve.
6. **Cook the bok choy:** Bring the broth back to a gentle simmer. Add the bok choy stems and cook for 2 to 3 minutes. Add the leaves, chicken and mushrooms and cook until the leaves wilt and everything is hot, 1 to 2 minutes. Turn off the heat.
7. **Stir in the miso:** Whisk most of the miso with a ladle of hot broth until smooth, then stir it into the pot. Taste and add the rest of the miso and the tamari until the broth tastes rich and well seasoned.
8. **Boil the noodles:** Bring the egg pot back to a boil, or a fresh pot of water, and cook the noodles as the package says. Drain.
9. **Assemble:** Divide the noodles among five bowls. Add the chicken, mushrooms, bok choy and broth. Top with the scallion greens, an egg, two pieces of nori and a pinch of sesame seeds.
