## Chef's Note

This is a home chicken-miso noodle soup using a slow cooker for the chicken broth. Kombu contributes glutamate and dried shiitake contributes guanylate; combining these savory compounds helps build depth before salt is adjusted. The result is not a promise of restaurant tonkotsu texture. Add miso near serving to preserve its aroma, after the broth volume and vegetable texture are settled.

## Directions

1. **Start the broth:** Put thawed chicken legs, 2 liters broth water, scallion whites, garlic, shiitake and mirin in a 6-quart slow cooker. Cover and start immediately. Keep scallion greens refrigerated for serving.
2. **Cook chicken:** Cook on LOW about 5–6 hours or HIGH about 3–4 hours until each leg reaches 165°F / 74°C away from bone and the meat pulls away readily. Extend if needed. Transfer chicken to a clean tray and mushrooms to a small bowl; let chicken cool only enough to handle.
3. **Strain and infuse:** Strain broth into a 4-quart saucepan and discard spent scallion and garlic. Skim excess surface fat. Aim for about 1.8–2 liters broth, adding a little hot water if needed. While the broth is steaming hot but not boiling, add kombu and leave off heat for about 10 minutes, then remove it. During the infusion, discard chicken skin/bones and shred meat; discard tough mushroom stems and slice the caps.
4. **Prepare optional eggs and noodles:** Bring the noodle/egg water to a boil in a separate pot. If using eggs, lower them in carefully and simmer gently about 11–12 minutes until whites and yolks are firm. Transfer to ice water for 5 minutes, then peel and halve. Return the pot water to a boil and cook ramen according to its package, draining just before assembly. If omitting eggs, simply cook the noodles near serving.
5. **Cook greens and warm meat:** Bring the strained broth back to a gentle simmer. Add bok choy stems and cook about 2–3 minutes; add leaves, chicken and mushrooms and cook until stems are tender, leaves wilted and chicken is heated through to 165°F / 74°C. Turn off heat.
6. **Finish with miso:** Whisk about three-quarters of the miso with a ladleful of hot broth until smooth, then stir it into the pot. Taste and add remaining miso and tamari gradually only as needed. Do not boil the seasoned soup for a long hold.
7. **Assemble:** Divide noodles among five large bowls. Add chicken, mushrooms, bok choy and about 350–400 ml broth per bowl. Finish with sliced scallion greens and the optional egg halves, two nori pieces and a little sesame per bowl. Serve promptly.
