## Chef's Note

A Dutch baby is a German-American oven pancake, closer to a giant popover than a flapjack. Thin blended batter hits a pan of sizzling butter in a hot oven, and the burst of steam puffs it into tall, golden walls with a custardy middle. Lemon and powdered sugar are the classic finish.

## Directions

1. **Melt the butter:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Put the butter in a 9x13-inch metal baking pan and set it in the oven to melt while the oven heats, 5 to 10 minutes. Take it out if the butter starts to brown.
2. **Blend the batter:** Blend the eggs, milk, flour, vanilla and salt on high until smooth and frothy, 30 to 45 seconds.
3. **Fill the pan:** When the oven is hot and the butter sizzles, pour the batter into the center of the pan. Do not stir.
4. **Bake:** Bake until puffed, deep golden and set in the center, about 20 minutes. Keep the oven door closed for the first 15 minutes.
5. **Serve:** The pancake sinks as it cools, so serve it right away. Dust it with powdered sugar and squeeze lemon over, or pour on warm maple syrup.
