## Chef's Note

These are Nana's biscuits, mixed by hand in the bowl with Crisco and buttermilk, rolled into small balls and flattened with the palm. No rolling pin or cutter needed. Self-rising flour does the work, so handle the dough just enough to bring it together.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Grease a cookie sheet.
2. **Mix the dough:** Put the flour in a bowl. Add the Crisco, the baking soda and a little buttermilk and mix with your hand. Keep adding buttermilk a little at a time until the dough comes together. Add a little more flour if it is too sticky.
3. **Shape:** Roll the dough into small biscuits, set them on the cookie sheet and flatten each one with your hand.
4. **Bake:** Bake for 10 minutes. If the tops aren't brown, broil for 1 to 2 minutes, watching so they don't burn.

## Suggested Tweak

- Use 6 tbsp cold butter in place of the Crisco for richer, more tender biscuits. Rub it into the flour until a few pea-size pieces remain.
- Bake at 450°F for 9 to 12 minutes and brush the tops with a little buttermilk first, so they brown without the broiler. Keep the broiler as a backup.
