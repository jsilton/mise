## Chef's Note

Lemon juice and zest go into the batter, with a separate lemon supply for the buttercream. Use a measure-for-measure blend containing xanthan gum and mix just until incorporated. Cool the cupcakes completely before spreading the full buttercream.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line enough standard cupcake cups with paper liners; the original batch uses 12 cups. Wash lemons before zesting and juicing, and measure the batter and frosting juice separately.
2. **Dry:** Whisk all the flour blend, baking powder and salt together.
3. **Wet:** Beat all the oil and granulated sugar together about 1 minute. Add all the eggs one at a time, beating after each, then stir in all the vanilla, batter lemon juice and zest.
4. **Combine:** Add all the dry mixture and the full batter milk supply, mixing only until incorporated without dry flour pockets. Do not taste raw batter.
5. **Bake:** Divide all batter among the liners, about two-thirds full, using more or fewer same-sized cups when scaling. Bake about 18–20 minutes as the original-batch first check, until the center is set and a toothpick comes out clean; continue if wet batter remains.
6. **Cool:** Cool completely on a rack before frosting. Extra pans or batches add elapsed time; do not frost warm cakes.
7. **Buttercream:** Beat all the softened butter until creamy. Beat in all the separate frosting lemon juice, then add the full confectioners’ sugar in several portions, starting on low speed. Gradually add the listed frosting milk as needed for a spreadable consistency; it is separate from the full batter milk. Scrape and beat until smooth, without promising a stiff peak.
8. **Finish:** Divide all frosting among the cooled cupcakes. Store covered in the refrigerator as described below.
