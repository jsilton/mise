## Chef's Note

Bright lemon cupcakes from Gluten-Free Palate that nobody will guess are gluten-free. A 1-to-1 flour blend with xanthan gum holds the crumb together, and oil keeps it moist. Lemon juice and zest flavor the cake, and a tangy lemon buttercream finishes it.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Heat the oven to 350°F and line 12 standard muffin cups with paper liners. Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
2. **Beat the wet ingredients:** In a large bowl, beat the oil and sugar with a mixer for 1 minute. Add the eggs one at a time, beating after each. Stir in the vanilla, lemon juice and zest.
3. **Make the batter:** Add the flour mixture and milk and mix on low just until no dry flour remains.
4. **Bake:** Fill the liners two-thirds full. Bake until the centers spring back and a toothpick comes out clean, 18 to 20 minutes.
5. **Cool:** Cool the cupcakes completely on a rack, about 1 hour.
6. **Make the buttercream:** Beat the butter until creamy. Beat in the lemon juice, then the confectioners' sugar a cup at a time on low speed. Add the milk a spoonful at a time until the frosting is smooth and spreadable.
7. **Frost:** Spread or pipe the buttercream over the cooled cupcakes.
