## Chef's Note

Lean turkey dries out fast, so these burgers start with a panade of milk-soaked panko that keeps them juicy. Worcestershire and a spoonful of barbecue sauce give the mild meat savory depth. Mix with a light hand and never press the patties in the pan.

## Directions

1. **Soak the crumbs:** Stir the panko and milk together in a large bowl and let soak for 5 minutes.
2. **Mix the meat:** Add the Worcestershire, barbecue sauce, garlic powder, salt, pepper and turkey. Mix gently with your hands just until combined.
3. **Shape the patties:** Divide into four portions and shape into patties a little wider than the buns. Press a shallow dent into the center of each so they cook flat.
4. **Cook the burgers:** Heat the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium heat. Cook the patties without pressing until well browned and 165°F in the center, 5 to 6 minutes per side. If using cheese, lay it on for the last minute and cover the pan to melt it.
5. **Toast the buns:** Move the burgers to a plate. Toast the buns cut side down in the skillet until golden, about 1 minute.
6. **Build the burgers:** Set each patty on a bun and top with spinach, avocado and red onion.
