## Chef's Note

Puffy Muffin is the credited source name for this pulled-chicken salad. Gently cook the tenders, pull them into bite-sized pieces, then coat with the full mayonnaise dressing. Grapes and celery stay distinct. The onion-and-lemon preparation below keeps those additions; the simpler chicken, celery, grapes and mayonnaise option remains separate. No muffin batter or baking is part of either salad.

## Directions

1. **Poach:** Put enough water to cover the raw tenders in a pot with room for the actual load; use the separate poaching salt allowance if wanted. Bring to a gentle simmer, add all the chicken and maintain that gentle heat. Begin checking around 8–10 minutes, continuing until several thick tenders reach 165°F in their centers. Thickness and the water returning to temperature determine the clock.

2. **Pull and cool:** Drain to a clean plate or shallow tray. As soon as cool enough to handle safely, use clean forks to pull the chicken into bite-sized pieces. Keep the cooked chicken and utensils away from raw chicken equipment. Spread shallowly and refrigerate promptly to cool before combining; a five-minute counter wait does not establish a chilled center.

3. **Mix the dressing:** Whisk all the listed mayonnaise, lemon juice, measured sea salt and black pepper together in a clean bowl. Keep the separate additional seasoning and mayonnaise allowances for any adjustment after mixing.

4. **Combine:** Wash the celery, grapes and onion under running water before preparing them. Add all the finely diced celery, quartered grapes and minced onion to the cooled chicken. Fold in all the dressing with a fork or spoon until evenly coated.

5. **Chill:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes after mixing and until cold throughout. Taste cold and use only the separate additional salt, pepper or mayonnaise allowances if wanted.

6. **Serve:** Serve cold as a salad or with separately prepared bread. The original batch is planned for four portions; use the listed portion count when scaling.

## Simpler Pulled-Chicken Variation

Keep the same two-cup raw-tender, half-cup celery, two-thirds-cup grape and half-cup mayonnaise amounts for the original batch, scaling them with the list. Omit the onion and lemon juice. Replace the measured dressing salt and pepper with salt and ground black pepper to taste. Follow the same 165°F poaching, clean pulling, prompt cooling and cold-service steps; stir the mayonnaise into the chicken, celery and grapes with a fork or spoon, then use the separate additional mayonnaise allowance only if needed.
