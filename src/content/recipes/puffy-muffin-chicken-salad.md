## Chef's Note

This is chicken salad in the style of Puffy Muffin, the Southern bakery and lunch spot known for it. Red grapes and celery bring sweetness and crunch, and a little red onion and lemon keep the mayonnaise bright. Gently poached tenders stay moist, so the salad tastes fresh, not dry.

## Directions

1. **Poach the chicken:** Bring a pot of well-salted water to a gentle simmer. Add the chicken tenders and cook at a bare simmer until the thickest piece reaches 165°F, 8 to 10 minutes. Drain.
2. **Shred the chicken:** While the chicken is still warm, pull it into bite-sized shreds with two forks. Spread it on a plate and let it cool to room temperature, about 15 minutes.
3. **Make the dressing:** In a small bowl, whisk the mayonnaise, lemon juice, sea salt and pepper until smooth.
4. **Combine:** In a large bowl, toss the chicken with the celery, grapes and red onion. Pour the dressing over and fold gently until every piece is coated.
5. **Chill and serve:** Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes so the flavors come together. Taste, add more salt, pepper or mayonnaise if it needs it, and serve cold.
