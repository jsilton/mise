## Chef's Note

Adapted from the Gwock Family recipe. Parboil the beans until they begin yielding, then drain before mixing so the condensed soup stays concentrated. Most of the cheddar and half the fried onions go inside; the reserved amounts finish on top near the end of baking.

## Directions

1. **Prepare dish:** Heat oven to 350°F. For the original batch, use unsalted butter to butter a 9×13-inch oven-safe baking dish. Use enough dishes for similar filling depth with room below the rim when scaling.
2. **Parboil beans:** Set out low-sodium chicken broth or water and fresh green beans. Bring the blanching liquid to a boil in a saucepan, then add the beans. Begin checking around 5–6 minutes after adding beans: they should yield with slight resistance rather than remain hard or fall apart. Drain immediately without rinsing. Keep blanching liquid separate from casserole filling; discard it, or cool promptly and refrigerate separately if saving for another use.
3. **Combine filling:** Set out condensed cream of mushroom soup, half-and-half, garlic powder, kosher salt, black pepper, cooked ham, yellow onion, 4/5 of the sharp cheddar cheese, and 1/2 of the canned French fried onions. Whisk all the soup, half-and-half, garlic powder, salt and pepper together. Fold in all the drained beans, cooked ham, diced onion and the allocated cheese/fried onions. Four-fifths of the full cheddar and half the fried onions go inside; reserve the rest for the finishing stage. Do not dilute the condensed soup with the blanching broth.
4. **Heat the full casserole:** Transfer all filling to the buttered dishes. Bake at 350°F, starting checks around 30 minutes for the original pan, until bubbling toward the center. This begins the final check rather than certifying the center by color alone.
5. **Add reserved topping:** Scatter 1/5 of the sharp cheddar cheese and 1/2 of the canned French fried onions over the full filling, dividing the topping among dishes. Continue baking, starting checks around 10 minutes later, until the center reaches 165°F /74°C and the onions are golden. Check every dish; tent lightly if toppings brown before its center is ready.
6. **Rest and serve:** Rest 5 minutes before dividing the full casserole into the listed portions. The short rest thickens the hot sauce; keep leftovers chilled promptly.
