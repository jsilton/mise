## Chef's Note

This is the Gwock family's green bean casserole, made hearty with diced ham and plenty of cheddar. Blanching the beans in chicken broth seasons them before they go into the creamy sauce, and the last of the fried onions goes on near the end so it stays crisp.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 10x12-inch baking dish with butter.
2. **Heat the broth:** Bring low-sodium chicken broth or water to a boil in a medium saucepan over high heat.
3. **Blanch the beans:** Add green beans and cook until tender-crisp, 5 to 6 minutes. Drain and save the broth for another use.
4. **Make the sauce:** In a large bowl, whisk together condensed cream of mushroom soup, half-and-half, garlic powder, kosher salt, and black pepper.
5. **Mix the filling:** Stir in the drained beans, ham, onion, 4/5 of the cheddar cheese, and 3/4 of the canned French fried onions.
6. **Top and bake:** Pour the mixture into the baking dish and sprinkle with 1/5 of the cheddar cheese. Bake until the edges bubble, about 30 minutes.
7. **Add the onions:** Scatter 1/4 of the canned French fried onions over the top. Bake until bubbling and crusty, about 10 minutes more.
8. **Rest and serve:** Let the casserole sit for 5 minutes, then serve.

## Suggested Tweak

Grate a block of sharp cheddar yourself. It melts more smoothly than bagged shredded cheese and has more flavor. The family card lists 1 cup of fried onions but also says to add 1/2 cup at the end. For a crunchier top, use 1 1/4 cups and save 1/2 cup for the last 10 minutes.
