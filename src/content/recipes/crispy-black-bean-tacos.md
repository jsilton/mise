## Chef's Note

Roasting cooked beans drives off moisture and can give split skins crisp edges. This is a different texture from the soft filling in [Black Bean Tacos with Lime Crema](/mise/recipes/black-bean-tacos-with-lime-crema). Keep acid in the crema or at the table: pouring lime juice over the whole hot tray immediately would wet the surfaces you just dried.

## Directions

1. **Heat and dry:** Heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C with a rack in the center. Rinse and drain beans, then spread on a clean towel and blot thoroughly. Measure the crema ingredients and toppings.
2. **Season without making a paste:** On an 18-by-13-inch rimmed sheet, lightly crush about one-third of the beans with a fork, leaving coarse pieces and many whole beans. Toss with olive oil, soy sauce, cumin, smoked paprika and garlic powder. Spread into one layer; avoid thin mashed smears at the pan edges. No extra salt is needed before the salty sauce and optional cheese are considered.
3. **Roast and check texture:** Roast 20–25 minutes, gently turning after about 12 minutes. Begin checking around 18 minutes: cool a small sample before tasting for dry, crisp edges with a tender center. Remove before small pieces blacken or the centers become hard. Toss with lime zest after roasting; keep lime juice off the tray.
4. **Make the crema while beans roast:** Blend avocado, sour cream, 1 tbsp lime juice, half the brine, the crema cilantro, garlic and 2 tbsp water until smooth. Add the remaining brine, extra teaspoon of lime and optional salt only after tasting. Add the extra tablespoon of water only if too thick. Keep cool until serving.
5. **Finish the accompaniments:** Shred cabbage, portion optional cotija and jalapeños, chop the serving cilantro and cut lime wedges. Near the end of roasting, warm tortillas in a dry skillet over medium-high for about 10–30 seconds per side, or microwave stacks of four to six between damp paper towels for 30–45 seconds, extending briefly if needed. Wrap briefly in a clean towel.
6. **Build at the table:** Divide beans among twelve small tortillas, three per person. Add cabbage, a modest spoonful of crema and the chosen toppings. Serve immediately with optional hot sauce and lime wedges. Keep unassembled beans spread out briefly rather than trapping their steam in a covered bowl.
