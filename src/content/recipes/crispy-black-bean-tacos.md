## Chef's Note

These tacos treat black beans like meat. You smash some, season them with soy sauce and smoked paprika, and roast them until the edges crisp while the centers stay creamy. A tangy avocado crema spiked with pickled jalapeño brine brings richness and acid in one spoonful.

## Directions

1. **Dry the beans:** Heat the oven to 425°F with a rack in the center. Spread the rinsed beans on a clean towel and blot them very dry.
2. **Season the beans:** Tip the beans onto an 18-by-13-inch rimmed baking sheet and lightly crush about a third of them with a fork. Toss with the olive oil, soy sauce, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder and salt, then spread them in one layer.
3. **Roast:** Roast, stirring once halfway through, until the skins split and the edges are dark and crisp but the centers are still tender, 20 to 25 minutes. Toss with the lime zest.
4. **Blend the crema:** While the beans roast, blend the avocado, sour cream, lime juice, jalapeño brine, cilantro, garlic, water and salt until smooth and pourable. Taste and add more lime or salt.
5. **Char the tortillas:** Heat a dry cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat, or use a gas flame. Char each tortilla 20 to 30 seconds per side until pliable with dark spots. Stack them in a clean towel to stay warm.
6. **Build the tacos:** Fill each tortilla with beans, cabbage and a drizzle of crema. Top with cotija, pickled jalapeños and cilantro. Serve right away with lime wedges and hot sauce.
