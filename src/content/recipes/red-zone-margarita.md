## Chef's Note

A game-day margarita in deep crimson. Reposado tequila stands up to bitter Campari, and a spiced cranberry syrup ties them together like a light red wine. Salt goes into the batch instead of on the rim, which tames the bitterness and makes every sip bright.

## Directions

1. **Make the syrup:** The day before, combine the cranberry juice, sugar, cinnamon, thyme and cloves in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Simmer 10 minutes, then take it off the heat and steep 30 minutes.
2. **Strain and chill:** Strain the syrup through a fine-mesh sieve into a jar and refrigerate until cold.
3. **Freeze the fruit ice:** Put 3 cranberries and 3 blueberries in each cavity of a large ice cube mold. Fill with water and freeze overnight until solid.
4. **Mix the batch:** In a large pitcher, combine the tequila, lime juice, cranberry syrup, Cointreau, Campari and salt. Stir until the salt dissolves completely. Refrigerate until game time, with no ice in the pitcher.
5. **Taste and adjust:** Taste the batch. It should taste strong, since shaking will dilute it. Add a splash more syrup if it is too bitter, or more lime if it is too sweet.
6. **Shake each drink:** Pour 4 oz of the batch into a shaker, fill with ice and shake hard until the tin is frosted, 10 to 12 seconds.
7. **Serve:** Set a fruit ice cube in a rocks glass and strain the drink over it. Squeeze an orange peel over the glass to spray its oils, rub it around the rim and drop it in.
