## Chef's Note

Cold, separated rice is easier to toss without crushing it. Cook the eggs into ribbons before combining them with the sausage and rice. Lap cheong products differ: the cured appearance does not establish that a sausage is ready to eat; its label determines the preparation needed.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the rice:** Break up cooked jasmine rice gently. Keep it cold until ready to add it to the pan. The listed amount is cooked rice, not dry rice.
2. **Make the egg ribbons:** Use large eggs, 1/2 of the toasted sesame oil, and 1/3 of the peanut oil for this stage. Whisk the eggs with their half of the sesame oil. Use the egg-stage peanut oil across the omelet batches. In a hot wok, cook thin omelets with roughly one-third of the original three-egg mixture at a time, adjusting the number of omelets for the listed quantity. Cook until fully set without liquid egg; an egg-dish center is 160°F / 71°C. Move to a clean board, roll and slice into ribbons. Set aside.
3. **Cook the sausage:** Use Chinese sausages (lap cheong), green onions, and 2/3 of the peanut oil for this stage. Add this stage’s peanut oil to the wok. Add the prepared sausage and all the green onions. Stir-fry over medium-high heat until browned and the labeled cooking requirements are met; 2–3 minutes is a browning check, not proof raw sausage is cooked. Reduce the heat if sugars scorch.
4. **Add the aromatics:** Add garlic cloves and red chili and stir-fry for about a minute until fragrant, lowering the heat if the garlic catches. Omit the chili or serve it separately for milder portions.
5. **Fry the rice:** Add the separated rice and turn it over high heat in an uncrowded pan, pressing out remaining clumps gently. Work in batches rather than packing the wok; 2–3 minutes is a first check.
6. **Season and heat through:** Add frozen peas, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, and 1/2 of the toasted sesame oil, including the remaining half of the sesame oil. Toss until peas and rice are hot throughout, checking the cold-rice mixture at 165°F / 74°C in several places. Do not stop at a fixed two-minute clock if cold clumps remain.
7. **Fold and serve:** Fold in all the fully cooked egg ribbons and serve promptly among the listed portions. More omelets, sausage preparation or rice batches lengthen the schedule.
