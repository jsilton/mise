## Chef's Note

Roast the small sweet-potato cubes until tender before the eggs go in. Cook the sausage and wilt the spinach first, retaining their full mixture for the casserole. Spread the filling evenly, then bake until the center is set and reaches 165°F; a browned edge does not measure the middle.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. Wash the fresh vegetables before preparing them. Lightly grease an oven-safe 9×13-inch casserole dish for the original batch with the separate oil spray. Check its actual depth: when the full filling will not leave headroom, divide every ingredient among additional dishes before roasting or assembling, keeping similar depth.
2. **Roast potatoes:** Toss all the peeled 1/4-inch sweet-potato cubes with the full listed avocado oil in the prepared dish or dishes. Bake for about 15–20 minutes, until a cube in the center is tender when pierced; remove from the oven.
3. **Mix eggs:** While the potatoes roast, whisk all the eggs, almond milk, measured green onion, hot sauce, salt and pepper together. Fold in all the cheddar.
4. **Cook sausage:** Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add all the raw ground breakfast sausage or ground pork, breaking it up as it cooks. Cook ground pork to 160°F /71°C, or poultry sausage to 165°F /74°C; use raw ground sausage and follow its package instructions. Use additional skillet rounds if necessary rather than crowding it.
5. **Cook vegetables:** Add all the yellow onion, red and green bell peppers and garlic to the cooked sausage. Sauté for about 3–5 minutes, until the vegetables begin to soften. Reduce the heat if the garlic darkens before the vegetables soften.
6. **Wilt spinach:** Add all the spinach and stir for about 30 seconds to 1 minute, until wilted. Remove the skillet from the heat. Retain the full cooked sausage, vegetables and pan juices for assembly.
7. **Assemble:** Divide the full cooked skillet mixture among the roasted-potato dishes and toss together. Pour the egg mixture evenly over them, dividing all the eggs and filling among the dishes in the same proportions.
8. **Bake and serve:** Bake at 400°F for about 35–40 minutes, beginning checks at 30 minutes, until the egg center is set and reaches 165°F /74°C in each dish. A deeper load can take longer. Let stand until safe to handle and firm enough to slice, then cut into the listed planning count of slices. Add the separate optional extra green onion if desired.
