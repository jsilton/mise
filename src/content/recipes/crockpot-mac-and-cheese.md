## Chef's Note

This is the hands-off mac and cheese for game day and potlucks. The uncooked pasta goes straight into the slow cooker and drinks up the milk as it cooks. Sharp cheddar brings the flavor, and deli American cheese and evaporated milk keep the sauce smooth.

## Directions

1. **Fill the cooker:** Spray a 6-quart slow cooker with cooking spray. Add the macaroni, whole milk, evaporated milk, both cheeses, salt, pepper, dry mustard, garlic powder and cayenne. Stir until the pasta is submerged, then dot the top with the butter.
2. **Cook on low:** Cover and cook on LOW for 1 hour. Stir well, scraping the bottom and sides.
3. **Finish cooking:** Keep cooking on LOW, stirring every 30 minutes, until the pasta is tender and the sauce is thick and creamy, 30 minutes to 2 hours more depending on your cooker.
4. **Rest and serve:** Turn off the cooker and uncover. Let the mac and cheese stand 5 to 10 minutes to thicken, then stir and serve.
