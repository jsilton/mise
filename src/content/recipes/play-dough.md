## Chef's Note

This is the classic cooked play dough, soft and smooth and much nicer than the store-bought kind. Cream of tartar is what keeps it pliable for months, so don't leave it out. It's a craft, not a snack, so keep it out of little mouths.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Whisk the flour, salt and cream of tartar in a medium nonstick saucepan.
2. **Add the liquids:** Whisk in the water, oil and food coloring until no lumps remain.
3. **Cook the dough:** Set the pan over medium heat and stir constantly with a wooden spoon. The mixture looks runny, then suddenly clumps. Keep stirring until it pulls away from the pan in a single ball, 3 to 5 minutes.
4. **Knead:** Turn the dough out onto a clean counter and let it cool until comfortable to touch, about 5 minutes. Knead until smooth, about 1 minute.
