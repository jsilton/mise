## Chef's Note

Regina’s pumpkin breakfast cookies combine rolled and quick oats with cranberries and pepitas. The mixed oat texture gives a substantial cookie, and the eggs help hold the mixture together. Flatten the scoops before baking: this dough keeps the shape you give it.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet with parchment. Use additional sheets or oven loads when needed; scaled dough should not be crowded onto the original sheet.
2. **Combine oil and honey:** Whisk coconut oil and honey in a small bowl. If necessary, warm them gently together in a suitable microwave-safe bowl or on the stove only until fluid, then let them cool until warm rather than hot before they touch the eggs.
3. **Mix the eggs and pumpkin:** Whisk plain pumpkin puree and large eggs in a separate bowl until combined, then stir in the warm oil-honey mixture.
4. **Mix oats and inclusions:** In a large bowl, stir old-fashioned rolled oats, quick-cooking oats, ground flaxseed, unsweetened dried cranberries, pumpkin seeds (pepitas), pumpkin pie spice, and sea salt together until evenly distributed, including all the salt.
5. **Combine thoroughly:** Add the pumpkin mixture to the oat mixture and stir until all the oats and inclusions are coated and the mixture holds together.
6. **Scoop and flatten:** Scoop about ¼ cup per cookie onto the lined sheet. Flatten each into a roughly ¾-inch-thick disk; these cookies do not spread themselves. The original batch plans twelve cookies; use all the dough and keep similar portion size when scaling.
7. **Bake:** Wait until the oven reaches 350°F. Bake for 15–20 minutes per load, until the edges are lightly browned and the center feels firm. Check again if the center is still loose; sheet loads and cookie thickness can change the time.
8. **Cool:** Let the cookies cool completely on the baking sheet before lifting them or closing them in an airtight container.

## Cooking Notes

### Egg-free and sweetener choices

Maple syrup or agave may replace the full listed honey amount. To make the publisher’s egg-free route, omit both eggs from the original batch and soak the already listed ¼ cup ground flaxseed in ½ cup water until gelled. Keep this flax out of the dry bowl, then add the gel to the pumpkin with the other wet ingredients; do not add a second dose of flaxseed. Scale both the existing flax and added water with the batch. For a vegan route, also replace the honey with maple syrup or agave. Keep all oats, oil, pumpkin, cranberries and pepitas. Gelled-flax cookies can bind and bake differently; check their centers.

Use certified gluten-free oats for a gluten-free preparation and check the other ingredients. A mix of chopped nuts or sunflower seeds and chocolate chips may replace the pepitas at their total listed volume; this changes texture, sweetness and allergen suitability. The main uses the full pepitas.

### Keeping the cookies

Cool fully before closing the container. The publisher’s plain-cookie plan is 3–4 days airtight at room temperature or about a week refrigerated. For longer keeping, freeze cooled cookies in sealed bags and aim to use within four months for quality. Thaw a portion before serving; do not pack warm cookies into a closed container.
