## Chef's Note

Gin leads, with cognac for depth and a smaller measure of mezcal for smoke. Lemon and honeyed herbal liqueur shape the base; cold Prosecco finishes it. Use a roomy wine glass and keep the sparkling wine out of the shaker.

## Directions

1. **Chill the glasses:** Chill a stemmed wine glass with at least 10 US fluid ounces capacity for each full-size drink, about 10 minutes. Have the sparkling wine cold. The listed one-portion drink is 5½ fluid ounces before ice dilution; allow generous headroom.
2. **Shake each base:** Measure the soft juniper-forward dry gin, unaged mezcal, cognac, fresh lemon juice, French honeyed herbal liqueur (40% ABV), light agave nectar, dash orange bitters, and ice cubes for the selected servings. For whole servings, divide the liquid base and bitters into equal drink portions; the listed one-portion base is 2½ fluid ounces plus 1 dash bitters. Shake one portion at a time with moving ice, leaving room in the closed shaker, about 15 seconds until very cold. For a fractional serving, prepare one smaller drink with its scaled base rather than a fractional number of glasses. Do not put Prosecco in the shaker.
3. **Strain and top:** Double-strain each shaken portion into its chilled glass. Divide the brut Prosecco among the corresponding drink portions, pouring slowly: 3 fluid ounces per listed full-size drink, or the corresponding fraction for a smaller drink. Give one gentle lift with a bar spoon if needed to combine, preserving the bubbles.
4. **Add the aromas:** Divide the lemon peel twist and fresh thyme sprig among the drinks. Express each lemon peel over its drink and add it. Lightly tap each thyme sprig between your palms and place it at the rim. Serve immediately.

## Variations and serving notes

**Several drinks:** Scale all measured ingredients, including bitters and one garnish of each kind per drink. The listed one-drink base is 2½ US fluid ounces plus 1 dash bitters; divide a larger unshaken base into equal drink portions, shake each with fresh ice, then add 3 fluid ounces Prosecco to each at the listed drink size. Keep the glass capacity and per-drink topping fixed; the volume increase is for additional glasses.
