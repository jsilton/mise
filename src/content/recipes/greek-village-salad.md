## Chef's Note

This horiatiki-style salad centers on tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, olives, and feta. Keep the pieces substantial enough to retain their individual texture. Tomato juice becomes part of the dressing, so a modest amount of oil and vinegar is enough. Taste the vegetables with the brined cheese and olives before adding salt.

## Directions

1. **Prepare vegetables:** Wash and dry the produce, cut as listed, and place tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, and onion in a wide serving bowl. If the onion tastes particularly sharp, rinse its slices briefly, then drain and dry them first.
2. **Dress lightly:** Add olive oil, the initial tablespoon of vinegar, and oregano. Toss gently. Fold in olives and optional capers.
3. **Add feta and taste:** Lay feta slices on top. Taste a complete bite with a little cheese and olive. Add some of the measured salt only if needed, optional pepper, and the extra teaspoon of vinegar only if the vegetables need more brightness.
4. **Serve:** Divide among four plates, distributing vegetables, olives, and feta. Serve promptly. Keep any prepared-ahead components refrigerated and combine them shortly before eating.
