## Chef's Note

Thick, soft bakery-style cookies loaded with semisweet chocolate. Plenty of brown sugar keeps them chewy, and baking soda and baking powder together give them lift. Pull them while the centers still look soft. They finish setting on the hot pan and stay tender for days. Pick this one for a big, soft batch the same day.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Heat the oven to 375°F and line baking sheets with parchment. Whisk the flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
2. **Cream the butter:** In a stand mixer, beat the butter and both sugars on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes. Beat in the eggs and vanilla until smooth.
3. **Make the dough:** On low speed, add the dry ingredients and mix just until no flour shows. Stir in the chocolate chips.
4. **Shape:** Roll the dough into 2-tablespoon balls and set them 2 inches apart on the sheets.
5. **Bake:** Bake one sheet at a time until the edges are just turning golden and the centers look set but soft, 8 to 10 minutes.
6. **Cool:** Let the cookies rest on the hot sheet for 2 minutes, then move them to a rack to cool.
