## Chef's Note

Use the edge color to judge these soft cookies: remove them when the edges are just starting to brown and the centers have set while staying soft. This dough uses equal cup measures of brown and granulated sugar. A short pan rest helps the cookies become firm enough to move.

## Directions

1. **Prepare and dry mix:** Start preheating the oven to 375°F. Line baking sheets with parchment and set out a cooling rack. Whisk the flour, baking soda, baking powder and sea salt together.

2. **Cream:** Beat the softened butter and both sugars until combined and light, about 2 minutes, scraping the bowl. Beat in the eggs and vanilla until evenly mixed.

3. **Combine:** Add the dry mixture just until no flour pockets remain, then distribute all the chocolate chips through the dough.

4. **Shape:** Roll about 2 tablespoons of dough into each ball and place 2 inches apart on the prepared sheets. Keep waiting dough covered and refrigerated; do not load dough onto a hot sheet.

5. **Bake:** Once the oven reaches 375°F, bake for 8–10 minutes per tray. Remove when the edges are just beginning to brown and the centers have set but remain soft. If the center is still liquid or raw-looking, continue baking and check again.

6. **Cool:** Rest on the sheet for about 2 minutes, then transfer carefully to a rack once the cookies hold together. Allow more pan time if they are too fragile to lift. Cool completely before packing. Repeat with the remaining dough on cooled sheets.

## Cooking Notes

For the same listed butter amount, salted butter is an alternative to unsalted butter; it makes a saltier cookie alongside the listed sea salt. The original batch uses 1 cup of butter and 1 tsp sea salt. Choose one butter type and do not taste the raw dough to decide on seasoning.

For larger cookies, use 2–3 tablespoons of dough per ball, keeping the 2-inch gaps and adding space if portions would touch. Larger portions change the finished count and may need longer baking; follow the edge and center cues.

For make-ahead dough, cover and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below for up to 3 days, then portion and bake in the preheated oven; cold dough may need longer. For freeze-ahead, freeze dough portions separately, then pack them airtight and thaw them in the refrigerator before baking. Check their actual doneness rather than using a fixed frozen-dough bake time.
