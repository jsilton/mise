## Chef's Note

Some candies are folded through the dough and the remaining portion is pressed onto the tops. Keep that reserved portion separate before scooping; distributing it by the actual number of cookies avoids running short while decorating.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F and have ungreased baking sheets and a cooling rack ready.
2. **Cream:** Beat shortening or unsalted butter, light brown sugar, and granulated sugar until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes, scraping the bowl.
3. **Add eggs:** Beat in large eggs and pure vanilla extract until evenly combined.
4. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Whisk all-purpose flour, baking soda, and sea salt in a separate bowl, then gradually stir into the wet mixture just until no dry flour remains.
5. **Fold in candies:** Fold 2/3 of the candy-coated milk chocolate candies into the dough, reserving the rest cleanly for the tops. This is 1 cup folded and ½ cup reserved in the original 72-cookie planning batch; both allocations follow the listed batch proportionally.
6. **Shape:** Drop teaspoonfuls onto the ungreased sheets, leaving space for spreading. Keep the portions consistent; the listed yield is a planning count for these small cookies. A larger batch needs more sheets and oven loads, not larger dough balls.
7. **Decorate:** Distribute 1/3 of the candy-coated milk chocolate candies across the dough portions, pressing them in slightly. Use all of this reserved amount; the number of candies on each cookie depends on candy size and the actual cookie count.
8. **Bake:** Wait for the oven to reach 350°F, then bake for 9–11 minutes per load, until the edges are lightly golden and the centers are set but soft. Keep remaining dough refrigerated at 40°F / 4°C or below between loads.
9. **Cool:** Leave the cookies on their sheets for 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack to cool completely before packing.

## Cooking Options

### Original candy allocation

For Robbi’s shortening route, fold half of the listed total candies into the dough and reserve half for decoration: ¾ cup for each destination in the original 72-cookie planning batch. Keep every other listed ingredient amount and the 350°F, 9–11-minute bake. This replaces the main two-thirds/one-third allocation; do not add another candy portion. Use a few of the reserved candies on each cookie and distribute the whole remainder across the actual batch.

The listed same-volume unsalted-butter option is a separate fat choice. Use softened butter in place of all the shortening, not both; its spread and texture can differ.
