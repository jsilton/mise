## Chef's Note

Steep the tea in boiling water before adding the milk and vanilla. This batch has more oats than milk-and-tea liquid by volume and can be quite thick after soaking, so stir and use the optional milk splash afterward. The full oats, tea liquid and honey remain in the mixture; choose containers by the actual amount rather than a fixed jar count.

## Directions

1. **Steep:** Use boiling water and Earl Grey tea bag for the infusion: put the tea bag in a heatproof mixing bowl, pour in the boiling water and steep for 5 minutes. Remove and discard the tea bag. Follow the vessel’s hot-liquid instructions; do not pour boiling tea into cold glass jars.
2. **Sweeten tea:** Stir pure honey into the hot tea until dissolved.
3. **Add milk:** Whisk in almond or whole milk, pure vanilla extract, and sea salt.
4. **Add oats:** Stir in old-fashioned rolled oats until the tea mixture coats all the oats.
5. **Refrigerate:** Divide the entire mixture into clean containers with enough room for the mixture and later stirring; use more containers for a larger batch. The original batch makes two planned portions. Cover and refrigerate promptly at 40°F / 4°C or below for at least 6 hours, preferably 12. If the mixture is still warm, use shallow containers and cool it in the refrigerator rather than waiting for complete counter cooling.
6. **Check texture:** Stir, then use additional milk (if using) if needed to loosen the oats.
7. **Finish:** Top with stroopwafel or crisp cookie and additional honey (if using), distributing the full cookie amount across the batch. Add the extra honey only if desired.

## Cooking Notes

Quick oats give a softer texture; old-fashioned rolled oats stay chewier. Choose the texture you prefer and adjust liquid after the overnight soak. The full milk amount may be replaced with another milk of choice.

An alternate tea strength uses a 3–5-minute steep, then the same complete mixture and at-least-6-hour refrigerated soak. The cookie can be omitted for the plain version; if used, keep the full listed amount and divide it among the portions. Extra milk and honey remain unmeasured, conditional serving adjustments.
