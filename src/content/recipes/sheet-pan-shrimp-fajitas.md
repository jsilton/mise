## Chef's Note

Peppers and onions take longer than shrimp. Roast the vegetables first, then add the shrimp and cook only until opaque. This staging keeps the vegetables tender without leaving the shrimp in the oven for their whole cook.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Heat oven to 425°F. Line a large rimmed sheet pan with parchment rated for that temperature, or use a suitable bare rimmed pan. Mix all the measured spices in a small bowl. Thaw frozen shrimp in the refrigerator and pat dry; remove tails if preferred for tacos.

2. **Roast the Vegetables:** Toss sliced peppers and onion with half the measured oil and about half the seasoning. Spread in one layer, using additional pans if crowded, and roast about 12–15 minutes, until beginning to soften.

3. **Add the Shrimp:** Toss peeled, deveined shrimp with the remaining oil and seasoning and all the measured lime juice. Add to the vegetables, spreading shrimp in one layer; use a second pan if crowded. Divide the full supplies proportionally across the pans.

4. **Finish:** Roast about 6–10 minutes more, checking early, until the thickest shrimp reach 145°F with pearly, opaque flesh throughout. Remove as soon as done. Warm the measured tortillas separately and serve with all the shrimp and vegetables and optional lime wedges.
