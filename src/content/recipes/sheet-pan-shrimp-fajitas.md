## Chef's Note

These fajitas from Healthy Fitness Meals cook on one sheet pan in under 30 minutes. The peppers and onion roast first, since they take longer, and the shrimp go in only for the last few minutes. A smoky paprika and cumin rub and a squeeze of lime do the rest.

## Directions

1. **Mix the seasoning:** Heat the oven to 425°F and line a large rimmed sheet pan with parchment. Stir the seasoning ingredients together in a small bowl.
2. **Roast the vegetables:** On the pan, toss the peppers and onion with half the oil and half the seasoning. Spread them in one layer and roast until they start to soften, 12 to 15 minutes.
3. **Season the shrimp:** While the vegetables roast, pat the shrimp dry. In a bowl, toss them with the rest of the oil, the rest of the seasoning and the lime juice.
4. **Roast the shrimp:** Spread the shrimp over the vegetables in one layer. Roast until the shrimp are pink and opaque all the way through, 6 to 10 minutes.
5. **Warm the tortillas and serve:** Wrap the tortillas in damp paper towels and microwave until soft, about 45 seconds. Fill them with the shrimp and vegetables and serve with lime wedges.
