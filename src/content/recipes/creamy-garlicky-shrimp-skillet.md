## Chef's Note

Shrimp in a garlicky Parmesan cream tastes far fancier than the 40 minutes it takes. Jarred roasted peppers and smoked paprika turn the sauce a deep coral and give it a sweet, smoky edge. The shrimp cook right in the cream, so pull them the moment they turn opaque.

## Directions

1. **Boil the pasta:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the fettuccine 1 minute short of al dente, starting the sauce while it cooks. Scoop out the pasta water, then drain.
2. **Warm the peppers:** Melt the butter in a deep 12-inch skillet over medium heat. Add the peppers and cook until warmed through, about 2 minutes. Add the garlic and paprika and stir until fragrant and the butter turns red, 30 to 45 seconds. Don't let the garlic brown.
3. **Simmer the cream:** Pour in the cream and bring it to a simmer. Lower the heat to medium-low and cook, stirring, until it lightly coats a spoon, 3 to 4 minutes.
4. **Cook the shrimp:** Season the shrimp with salt and pepper and add them to the sauce in one layer. Simmer, turning once, until pink and just opaque in the center, 2 to 3 minutes.
5. **Toss with cheese:** Add the hot pasta and turn off the heat. Toss in the Parmesan a handful at a time until melted and glossy, adding splashes of pasta water if the sauce gets tight.
6. **Season and serve:** Taste for salt, since the cheese adds plenty. Top with parsley and lots of black pepper and serve right away.
