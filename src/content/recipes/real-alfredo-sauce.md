## Chef's Note

This cream-based Alfredo is a rich sauce for pasta or vegetables. For the initial preparation, finely grate the Parmesan before heating the pan and melt it gently, without boiling the cheese mixture. The fifteen-minute estimate covers the sauce only; cook any pasta separately and reserve some cooking water before draining. Warm plain water works when serving the sauce without pasta.

## Directions

1.  **Melt:** In a large skillet or saucepan over medium heat, melt the butter.
2.  **Simmer:** Pour in the heavy cream and add the garlic. Let it simmer gently for 2 minutes until slightly thickened.
3.  **Emulsify:** Take the pan off the heat and gradually whisk in the finely grated Parmesan. If it needs more warmth to melt, return briefly to low heat while stirring; do not let the cheese mixture boil.
4.  **Bind:** Whisk in the listed warm pasta water or plain water a little at a time, stopping when the sauce flows and lightly coats a spoon. You may not need all of it; pasta water varies in salt and starch content.
5.  **Season:** Stir in salt, pepper, and a tiny pinch of nutmeg if using.
6.  **Toss:** Use immediately over separately cooked pasta or vegetables, adding sauce gradually to coat. It thickens as it stands. Refrigerate unused sauce in a shallow covered container within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C), at 40°F / 4°C or below, and use within 3–4 days. To reheat leftovers, stir frequently over controlled heat and bring to a rolling boil, adding a little water if needed. Reheating may separate the sauce; it is best freshly made.
