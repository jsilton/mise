## Chef's Note

Stir the full Scotch, apple brandy, honeyed herbal liqueur and anise-forward bitters with ice. Serve either strained up or over a fresh large cube; the latter continues diluting as it stands.

## Directions

1. Combine all the listed Scotch, apple brandy, honeyed herbal liqueur and bitters in a mixing glass with stirring ice. Leave working room for stirring; mix proportional batches if needed.

2. Stir and begin checking around 20–30 seconds, continuing until chilled and diluted to taste. The ice size and temperature affect the actual time.

3. Strain into a chilled coupe for an up drink, or into a rocks glass over a separate fresh large ice cube. Choose a glass with room for the full drink and any serving ice.

4. If using the optional orange or lemon peel, wash the fruit before cutting the peel, express it over the drink and add it. Serve immediately.
