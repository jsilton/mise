## Chef's Note

A smoky, spirit-forward stirred drink. Laphroaig's peat smoke meets the baked-apple warmth of bonded Laird's, and Bénédictine's honey and herbs tie the two together. Stir it long enough to soften the smoke and serve it very cold.

## Directions

1. **Stir:** Combine the Scotch, apple brandy, Bénédictine and bitters in a mixing glass. Fill with ice and stir until very cold, 20 to 30 seconds.
2. **Strain:** Strain into a chilled coupe, or into a rocks glass over a large ice cube.
3. **Garnish:** Twist the orange or lemon peel over the drink to release its oils, then drop it in.
