## Chef's Note

A one-skillet weeknight dinner from The Kitchn: spicy chicken sausage, charred broccolini and orzo cooked in the same pan. Pull the sausage and broccolini out while the orzo simmers so the broccolini keeps its bite, then fold everything back together with Parmesan.

## Directions

1. **Brown the sausage:** Heat the olive oil in a 10-inch or larger skillet with a lid over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and cook until browned on both sides, 3 to 4 minutes. Move it to a plate.
2. **Char the broccolini:** Add the broccolini to the same pan with a pinch of salt and pepper. Cook, stirring now and then, until the tips char and the stems are tender-crisp, about 5 minutes. Add it to the sausage.
3. **Toast the orzo:** Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic and orzo and stir until the garlic is fragrant and the orzo smells nutty, 30 to 45 seconds.
4. **Simmer the orzo:** Pour in the broth and scrape up the browned bits. Bring to a boil, then cover, turn the heat to low and simmer until the orzo is tender and has soaked up most of the broth, 10 to 12 minutes.
5. **Finish and serve:** Stir the sausage and broccolini back in and cook until hot, 1 to 2 minutes. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the Parmesan, adding a splash of hot water if the orzo is tight. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper. Serve with more Parmesan and red pepper flakes.
