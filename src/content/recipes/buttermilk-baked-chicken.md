## Chef's Note

Pat and Gina Neely's oven-fried chicken: a long buttermilk soak, then a crunchy cornflake and Parmesan crust that bakes instead of fries. The buttermilk seasons the meat and gives the crumbs something to grip, and baking on a rack lets hot air reach underneath so the bottom stays crisp.

## Directions

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Whisk the buttermilk, hot sauce, lemon juice and salt in a large bowl or zip-top bag. Add the onion, garlic, thyme sprigs and chicken and turn to coat. Cover and refrigerate for 3 to 12 hours.
2. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 400°F with a rack in the center. Line an 18-by-13-inch rimmed sheet pan with foil, set an oven-safe wire rack on it and brush the rack with a little of the oil.
3. **Make the crumbs:** Toss the crushed cornflakes with the rest of the oil until lightly moistened. Mix in the Parmesan, chopped thyme and pepper and spread the crumbs in a shallow dish.
4. **Coat the chicken:** Lift the chicken from the marinade, pick off the onion, garlic and thyme and let the extra buttermilk drip away. Press each piece into the crumbs on all sides. Set the pieces skin side up on the rack, about an inch apart.
5. **Bake:** Bake without turning until the crust is deep golden and the thickest part of each piece reads 165°F, 35 to 55 minutes. Start checking the breasts and wings at 30 minutes and move pieces to a plate as they finish.
6. **Rest and serve:** Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving.
