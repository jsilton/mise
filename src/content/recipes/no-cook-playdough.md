## Chef's Note

This soft, stretchy playdough is a craft, not a food, and it keeps for months. Boiling water cooks the flour as you stir, so there's no pot to scrub. Cream of tartar keeps the dough smooth and springy.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dry ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the flour, salt and cream of tartar.
2. **Mix the hot liquid:** In a heatproof jug, stir the boiling water and oil together. For one color, add the coloring now.
3. **Make the dough:** Pour the hot liquid slowly into the flour mixture, stirring with a long spoon, until a shaggy dough forms.
4. **Knead:** When the dough is cool enough to handle all the way through, turn it out and knead until smooth and no longer sticky, 2 to 3 minutes. If it stays sticky, knead in a little more flour.
5. **Color it:** For several colors, divide the plain dough and knead a different color into each piece.
