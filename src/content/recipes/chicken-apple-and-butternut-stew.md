## Chef's Note

This stew depends on contrast: savory chicken, sweet squash and cider, aromatic sage, and tart apple. The ingredients do not soften at the same speed. Smaller parsnip pieces get a head start; squash simmers with the chicken; apple goes in only near the end.

## Directions

1. **Brown chicken in batches:** Pat chicken dry and season with half the salt and the pepper. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high. Brown half the chicken for about 3 minutes per side, then transfer to a temporary plate. Repeat with remaining oil and chicken. It need not be cooked through yet.
2. **Soften the base:** Reduce heat to medium. Add onion, parsnip, and remaining salt. Cook about 7–9 minutes, stirring, until onion is softened and lightly golden. Add a small splash of stock if the browned residue threatens to scorch. Add sage and ginger and stir for 30 seconds.
3. **Disperse the flour:** Sprinkle flour over the vegetables and stir for 1 minute. Gradually stir in cider, scraping up golden residue and smoothing out flour pockets. Add stock and bring to a simmer.
4. **Simmer chicken and squash:** Return chicken and its accumulated juices. Add squash. Keep at a gentle simmer, partially covered, for about 18–25 minutes, stirring occasionally. Continue until squash and parsnip are tender and several larger chicken pieces reach 165°F / 74°C. Add stock if the sauce becomes too thick before the vegetables soften.
5. **Add apple late:** Fold in the apple and simmer 5–8 minutes, until hot and just tender but still holding its shape. Stir gently so the squash stays intact.
6. **Balance and serve:** Taste a spoonful containing sauce and vegetables. Add optional cider vinegar 1 tsp at a time if the stew needs more tartness, and adjust salt or pepper. Serve in six bowls with a green vegetable or salad.
