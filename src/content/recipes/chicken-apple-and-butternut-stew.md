## Chef's Note

This is a fall stew of browned chicken thighs, butternut squash and parsnip simmered in apple cider with sage and ginger. Tart Granny Smith apple goes in for the last few minutes, so it stays bright and holds its shape against the sweet squash.

## Directions

1. **Brown the chicken:** Pat the chicken dry and season it with most of the salt and the pepper. Heat half the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown half the chicken, about 3 minutes per side, and move it to a plate. Repeat with the rest of the oil and chicken. It does not need to be cooked through.
2. **Soften the vegetables:** Lower the heat to medium. Add the onion, parsnip and the rest of the salt. Cook, stirring and scraping up the browned bits, until the onion is soft and lightly golden, 7 to 9 minutes. Stir in the sage and ginger for 30 seconds.
3. **Build the sauce:** Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and stir for 1 minute. Slowly stir in the cider, smoothing out any lumps, then add the stock and bring to a simmer.
4. **Simmer the stew:** Return the chicken and its juices and add the squash. Simmer gently, partly covered and stirring now and then, until the squash is tender but holds its edges and the chicken reaches 165°F, 18 to 25 minutes. Add a splash of stock if the sauce gets too thick.
5. **Add the apple:** Gently fold in the apple. Simmer until hot and just tender with a little bite, 5 to 8 minutes.
6. **Finish and serve:** Taste and add more salt and pepper. If it tastes too sweet, stir in the cider vinegar a teaspoon at a time.
