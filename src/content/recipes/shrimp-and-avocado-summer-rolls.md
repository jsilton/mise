## Chef's Note

Vietnamese summer rolls, goi cuon, wrap shrimp, rice noodles and fresh herbs in soft rice paper. This Food & Wine version adds creamy avocado and serves the rolls with nuoc cham, the salty, sour and sweet sauce of fish sauce, lime and chile. Drain the noodles and shrimp well so the rolls stay tight and dry.

## Directions

1. **Make the sauce:** Stir the sugar into the warm water until it dissolves. Add the fish sauce, lime juice, chile and garlic. Taste and add a little more lime or sugar until it tastes balanced.
2. **Cook the noodles:** Cook the vermicelli as the package directs until tender. Drain, rinse under cold water and drain well. Snip long strands with scissors.
3. **Cook the shrimp:** Bring a saucepan of salted water to a boil. Add the shrimp, lower to a simmer and cook until pink and opaque, 2 to 3 minutes. Move them to a bowl of ice water until cold, then pat dry and halve lengthwise.
4. **Soften a wrapper:** Dip one rice paper wrapper in a wide dish of warm water until just flexible, about 5 seconds. Lay it flat on a cutting board. It keeps softening as it sits.
5. **Fill and roll:** Lay an even share of the noodles, avocado and herbs in a strip across the lower third, leaving the sides clear. Set an even share of the shrimp halves in a row, pink side down, just above the strip. Fold the bottom over, fold in the sides and roll up snugly. Repeat with the rest of the wrappers.
6. **Serve:** Serve the rolls right away with the dipping sauce and chile-garlic sauce.
