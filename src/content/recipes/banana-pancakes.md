## Chef's Note

Ripe banana adds flavor and moisture to this buttermilk batter. Fold the wet and dry mixtures together until all flour is moistened, then stop while small lumps remain. A five-minute rest gives the flour time to hydrate before the pancakes meet the hot griddle.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dry ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon.
2. **Mix the wet ingredients:** In another bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, mashed bananas, eggs, cooled melted butter, and vanilla until evenly combined.
3. **Fold and rest:** Add the wet mixture to the dry and fold gently with a spatula until no dry flour pockets remain, scraping the bowl bottom and sides. Small banana lumps and hydrated batter lumps are fine. Stop mixing and let the batter rest for 5 minutes.
4. **Heat the griddle:** During the rest, heat a griddle or large nonstick skillet over medium heat, about 325°F for an electric griddle. Lightly grease with the separate butter or oil allowance.
5. **Cook:** Pour 1/3 cup batter per pancake, leaving room to turn them. Cook until bubbles form and begin to pop, the edges look set and the underside is golden, about 2–3 minutes. Flip gently and cook about 1–2 minutes more, until golden underneath and cooked through with no wet batter in the center. Lower the heat if the surface colors too quickly. Repeat with the remaining batter, refreshing the light coating of griddle fat as needed.
6. **Serve or freeze:** Serve with sliced bananas, maple syrup and optional serving butter. For freezing, separate cooked pancakes on a rack to cool promptly, layer between parchment and seal in a freezer bag. Freeze for up to 2 months for quality. Reheat in a toaster or oven until hot throughout.
