## Chef's Note

Chicken Marsala grew up in Italian-American restaurants, built on the fortified wine of Marsala in Sicily. Thighs stay juicy, and a light coat of flour helps them brown and gives the sauce body. Dry Marsala reduced over browned mushrooms and prosciutto makes a deep, nutty sauce, and cold butter swirled in at the end makes it glossy.

## Directions

1. **Pound and season:** Lay the thighs between sheets of plastic wrap and pound the thick parts to an even 1/2 inch. Season both sides with half the salt and the pepper.
2. **Dredge the chicken:** Spread the flour in a shallow dish. Coat each piece lightly and shake off the excess.
3. **Brown the chicken:** Heat half the oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Brown half the chicken until golden, 3 to 4 minutes on the first side and 2 to 3 minutes on the second. It finishes cooking in the sauce. Move it to a plate and repeat with the rest of the oil and chicken.
4. **Brown the mushrooms:** Lower the heat to medium. Add the prosciutto and stir for 1 minute. Add the mushrooms and cook, stirring now and then, until their liquid cooks off and they brown, 6 to 8 minutes.
5. **Reduce the Marsala:** Take the pan off the burner and pour in the Marsala. Return it to medium heat and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer until the wine reduces by half, 4 to 6 minutes.
6. **Simmer the chicken:** Add the stock and simmer for 2 minutes. Return the chicken and its juices to the pan. Simmer gently, turning once, until the thickest pieces reach 165°F, 3 to 5 minutes. Move the chicken to a warm platter.
7. **Finish with butter:** Turn off the heat and let the bubbling settle. Swirl in the cold butter a few pieces at a time until the sauce is glossy. Stir in the parsley, taste and add the rest of the salt if it needs it. Spoon the sauce and mushrooms over the chicken.
