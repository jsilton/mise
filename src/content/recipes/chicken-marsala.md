## Chef's Note

This version pairs chicken thighs with fresh mushrooms and dry Marsala. A light flour coating helps the chicken brown and gives the sauce some body. Prosciutto is a seasoning here, so the stock is unsalted and final salt comes only after reduction. Cooking with wine does not make a dish reliably alcohol-free; use the stock-and-lemon alternative when needed.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the cutlets:** Open thighs flat, trim loose fat, and gently pound only the thick sections to about 1/2 inch between sheets of plastic wrap. Season with half the measured salt and the pepper. Spread flour in a shallow dish, dredge lightly, and shake off excess. Discard leftover flour after raw chicken contact.
2. **Brown in batches:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Cook half the chicken about 3–4 minutes on the first side and 2–3 minutes on the second, until golden; it can finish cooking later in the sauce. Transfer to a plate. Repeat with the remaining oil and chicken. Reduce heat if the flour browns too quickly; discard any black, burned bits.
3. **Brown mushrooms:** Lower heat to medium. Add prosciutto and stir for 1 minute. Add mushrooms and cook 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until their liquid has evaporated and they begin to brown. If the pan becomes dry and sticks before the mushrooms release moisture, add a tablespoon of stock and scrape gently.
4. **Make the sauce:** Take the pan briefly off the burner to pour in the Marsala. Return to medium heat and scrape up the golden pan residue. Simmer until the wine reduces by about half, roughly 4–6 minutes. Add stock and simmer 2–3 minutes; the sauce should still be pourable.
5. **Finish cooking the chicken:** Return the chicken and its plate juices to the pan. Simmer gently for about 3–5 minutes, turning once, until the thickest parts reach 165°F / 74°C. Continue if needed, adding stock if the sauce gets too thick. Transfer the cooked chicken to a clean serving platter.
6. **Finish with butter:** Turn off heat and let vigorous bubbling subside. Swirl in the cold butter a few pieces at a time. Add parsley, taste, and use the reserved salt only if needed. Spoon sauce and mushrooms over the chicken and serve. Choose mashed potatoes or pasta, with a green vegetable alongside.
