## Chef's Note

Three ingredients and no baking: crushed chocolate sandwich cookies bound with cream cheese, rolled into balls and dipped in chocolate. Chill the balls until very firm before dipping so the hot chocolate sets into a clean shell instead of melting them.

## Directions

1. **Crush the cookies:** Set aside about a fifth of the cookies for garnish. Pulse the rest in a food processor to fine, sandy crumbs. Pulse the reserved cookies separately to crumbs and keep them aside.
2. **Make the filling:** In a large bowl, beat the cream cheese with the main batch of crumbs until it forms a smooth, dark dough with no pale streaks.
3. **Shape and chill:** Roll the dough into 1-inch balls and set them on a parchment-lined tray. Refrigerate until very firm, 30 to 60 minutes.
4. **Melt the chocolate:** Melt the chocolate in a dry bowl set over barely simmering water, stirring until smooth. Take it off the heat and let it cool for a few minutes.
5. **Dip and garnish:** Dip each cold ball in the chocolate with a fork, tap off the extra and set it back on the tray. While the coating is still wet, sprinkle with the reserved crumbs and a little flaky salt.
6. **Set:** Refrigerate until the shells are firm, about 1 hour. Keep the truffles chilled until serving.
