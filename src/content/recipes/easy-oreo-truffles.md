## Chef's Note

Use the full measured cookie mass, including the cream filling, and reserve part of those same cookies for garnish. Firm cold balls are easier to coat than soft ones. Let the melted chocolate cool enough not to soften them on contact, then keep the finished cream-cheese truffles refrigerated until serving.

## Directions

1. **Crush and reserve:** Weigh the full listed cookie amount, including its cream filling. Finely crush one-fifth and reserve these crumbs for garnish; crush the remaining four-fifths for the filling. The source’s original 16-ounce package was roughly 45 cookies, with 9 reserved and 36 inside; do not force those literal counts on a different package or scale. A sealed bag and rolling pin is the source alternative to a processor.
2. **Mix:** Beat all the softened cream cheese with all the four-fifths filling crumbs until evenly combined. The reserved garnish crumbs are not an additional cookie supply.
3. **Shape:** Roll into roughly 1-inch balls, aiming for about the listed count as a planning yield. Use more or fewer same-sized balls when scaling and enough lined trays to hold them.
4. **Chill:** Refrigerate the balls about 30–60 minutes until firm enough to dip. Keep cream-cheese preparation and service within the cold-handling limits below.
5. **Melt:** Melt all the baking chocolate in a dry heatproof bowl over barely simmering water, or in short microwave bursts with stirring between them. Start with the existing 30-second bursts and shorten as it melts. Keep water out; allow the chocolate to cool a little if it softens the cold balls on contact.
6. **Dip:** Coat each cold ball using a fork or dipping tool, tap off excess and place on a lined tray. Work in batches, keeping waiting balls refrigerated. Use the full chocolate supply for coating, recognizing that a dipping bowl may retain a little excess.
7. **Garnish:** While the coating is wet, distribute all the reserved cookie crumbs among the balls. Add the optional flaky salt if desired.
8. **Set:** Refrigerate at least 1 hour until the coating is firm. Store covered in the refrigerator until just before serving. Dipping chocolate that has touched cream-cheese crumbs belongs in refrigerated handling; the source’s room-temperature leftover-chocolate note applies only to a clean separate supply.
