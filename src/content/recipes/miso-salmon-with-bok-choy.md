## Chef's Note

This is Jordan's miso salmon, and it comes together in the time the rice takes to cook. Honey, lemon and miso make a sweet, tangy marinade that caramelizes under the broiler. The bok choy cooks in the same garlic and scallions, then gets a glossy finish of oyster sauce.

## Directions

1. **Start the rice:** Cook the rice in a rice cooker.
2. **Mix the marinade:** Whisk the soy sauce, mirin, rice vinegar, honey, lemon juice, miso, salt, pepper and half the scallions and garlic in a bowl.
3. **Marinate the salmon:** Coat the salmon completely in the marinade. Let it sit for at least 10 minutes, and ideally 30, while the oven heats to 400°F.
4. **Roast and broil the salmon:** Set the salmon on a broiling rack over a foil-lined pan. Roast in the oven or toaster oven without turning until nearly cooked through, 8 to 10 minutes, depending on thickness. Then switch to broil and cook until the miso on top is caramelized, 1 to 2 minutes.
5. **Cook the bok choy:** While the salmon cooks, heat the sesame oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Cook the rest of the scallions and garlic for 1 minute. Add the bok choy, season with salt and pepper and sauté until almost tender, 6 to 8 minutes.
6. **Add the oyster sauce:** Turn the heat to low, add the oyster sauce and toss until the bok choy is evenly coated, 1 minute.
7. **Serve:** Serve the salmon over a bed of rice and bok choy.

## Suggested Tweak

- Set aside 2 tbsp of the marinade before the salmon goes in. Brush it over the fillets just before broiling for a thicker, stickier glaze.
- Let the extra marinade drip off the fillets before roasting so the tops brown instead of steaming.
- Halve the bok choy lengthwise instead of chopping it. Sear it cut side down in two batches, then add a splash of water and cover for 1 to 2 minutes to cook the thick stems.
- Cook the bok choy in a neutral oil and add a little sesame oil at the end with the oyster sauce, so its toasted flavor stays fresh.
- Use jasmine rice. Thick fillets may need 12 to 16 minutes in the oven before the broil.
