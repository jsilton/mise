## Chef's Note

Shari is the seasoned rice at the heart of sushi. The word sushi refers to this vinegared rice, not the fish. Fold the seasoning in while the rice is hot and fan it as you go, so every grain turns glossy and stays distinct.

## Directions

1. **Rinse the rice:** Put the rice in a bowl, cover it with cold water and swish gently with your hand. Drain and repeat 3 or 4 times, until the water runs mostly clear, then drain well.
2. **Soak with kombu:** Put the rice and the cold water in a heavy 3-quart saucepan. Wipe the kombu with a damp cloth and add it. Soak for 30 minutes.
3. **Cook the rice:** Bring to a boil over medium heat with the lid ajar. Pull out the kombu as soon as the water starts to bubble. Cover, turn the heat to low and simmer until the water is absorbed, 12 to 15 minutes.
4. **Make the seasoning:** While the rice simmers, stir the rice vinegar, sugar and salt in a small bowl until they dissolve. Warm it for a few seconds in the microwave if the sugar is slow.
5. **Rest the rice:** Take the pan off the heat and leave it covered for 10 minutes. The grains should be tender to the center with no water left in the pot.
6. **Season the rice:** Turn the hot rice into a wide wooden, glass or ceramic bowl. Drizzle the seasoning over a rice paddle onto the rice. Cut through the rice with slicing strokes and fold it over gently, without mashing or stirring.
7. **Fan and cool:** Keep folding while you fan the rice with your other hand until the steam dies down and the grains are glossy and just warm, 5 to 10 minutes. Cover with a damp towel until you're ready to use it.
