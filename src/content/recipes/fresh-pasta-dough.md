## Chef's Note

This is Bon Appétit's everyday pasta dough, just flour, eggs, a little olive oil and salt. The stand mixer does the kneading. Give the dough its full rest so the gluten relaxes and the sheets roll out smooth instead of springing back.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dough:** Put the flour and salt in the bowl of a stand mixer and make a well in the center. Add the eggs and oil. Stir with a fork, pulling the flour into the eggs, until a shaggy dough forms.
2. **Knead the dough:** Fit the mixer with the dough hook and knead on low speed until the dough is smooth, elastic and slightly tacky, 8 to 10 minutes.
3. **Rest the dough:** Wrap it tightly and let it rest for at least 30 minutes at room temperature, or up to 1 day in the fridge.
4. **Roll the sheets:** Cut the dough into four pieces and keep three wrapped. Pass one through the widest setting of the pasta roller, fold it in thirds and roll it through again. Then pass it once through each narrower setting, dusting lightly, until it is as thin as your shape needs, about 1 mm for fettuccine. If a sheet springs back, cover it and let it rest for 5 minutes.
5. **Cut the pasta:** Cut the sheets into ribbons, or fill them for ravioli, and dust with flour or semolina.
6. **Boil the pasta:** Boil in a large pot of well-salted water until tender with a slight bite, 2 to 3 minutes for ribbons.
