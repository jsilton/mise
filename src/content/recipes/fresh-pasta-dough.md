## Chef's Note

This three-egg dough includes the full olive oil and kosher salt. Knead until smooth and elastic, then keep it covered during the rest and rolling. A piece that springs back benefits from another short covered rest; do not force it through a narrow roller gap.

## Directions

1. **Mix:** Place all the measured dough flour and salt in the stand-mixer bowl. Make a well and add all the beaten eggs and olive oil. Mix by hand until a shaggy dough forms; keep hands clear before starting the mixer.
2. **Knead:** Fit the dough hook and use the actual mixer’s recommended speed and dough capacity. Knead about 10 minutes, checking for a smooth, elastic dough. If kneading by hand, work until the same texture develops rather than treating the clock as a guarantee.
3. **Rest:** Wrap tightly and rest at least 30 minutes at ordinary room temperature. For advance preparation, wrap and refrigerate for up to 1 day; refrigerate sooner in a hot kitchen or if rolling is delayed. Do not taste raw dough.
4. **Roll:** Divide into manageable pieces and keep the others wrapped. Start at the roller’s widest setting, fold and pass through to form an even sheet, then narrow gradually, dusting only as needed. Machine numbers differ; stop at the thickness required for the chosen shape. See [Fresh Egg Pasta](/mise/recipes/fresh-egg-pasta) for rolling technique, using this recipe’s full formula rather than its different yolk-enriched formula.
5. **Shape and cook:** Cut or fill for the chosen pasta dish. Follow that dish’s cooking method and check for a cooked center without raw floury dough; filled pasta also needs its filling’s endpoint. This recipe’s zero cook time describes making the dough, not serving it raw.
