## Chef's Note

A summer cookout plate from Good Housekeeping. Corn charred on the grill is tossed with a coarse basil, mint and Parmesan sauce, almost a chunky pesto, and spooned over crisp-skinned chicken thighs. Plenty of lemon keeps it bright against the rich dark meat.

## Directions

1. **Season the chicken:** Heat a grill to medium-high. Pat the chicken dry and season it generously all over with salt and pepper.
2. **Grill the chicken:** Grill skin side down first, turning once or twice, until the skin is crisp and the thickest part reaches 165°F, 20 to 25 minutes. Move pieces to a cooler spot if the fat flares up.
3. **Grill the corn:** While the chicken cooks, grill the corn, turning now and then, until tender and charred in spots, about 10 minutes.
4. **Make the herb sauce:** Pulse the basil, mint, Parmesan and garlic in a food processor until finely chopped. Add the oil and lemon juice and pulse just to combine. Season with salt and more lemon juice to taste.
5. **Mix the salsa:** Cut the kernels off the cobs into a bowl and toss them with the herb sauce.
6. **Serve:** Set the chicken on a platter and spoon the corn salsa over the top.
