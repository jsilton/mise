## Chef's Note

This flourless cake is dense and fudgy beneath the full chocolate-and-cream glaze. Espresso at the listed dose can lend a hint of mocha. Let the cake cool completely before glazing, and let the ganache set before slicing. Its Passover context comes from the source; choose ingredients suitable for the dietary requirements of the people you are serving.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 375°F. For the original batch, grease an 8-inch round pan, line its bottom with parchment and grease the parchment with the separate pan-butter allowance.
2. **Melt:** Melt all the cake chocolate and the full measured butter in a microwave-safe bowl in short bursts, stirring between them, until smooth. Start with the existing 30-second bursts and shorten as it melts; do not scorch the chocolate. A very-low-heat burner is the source alternative.
3. **Mix:** Stir in all the sugar, salt, espresso powder and vanilla. When the mixture is warm rather than hot enough to cook eggs on contact, add all the eggs one at a time, beating briefly until smooth. Mix in all the sifted cocoa only until combined; do not taste raw batter.
4. **Bake:** Spoon all the batter into the prepared pan. Bake about 25 minutes as a first check. Look for the thin crust, moist crumbs on a tester and at least 200°F in the center, as the source specifies. A dark pan may reach these cues earlier. At other scales, divide the whole batter among appropriate pans before baking; no fixed-pan or linear-time claim is made.
5. **Cool:** Cool in the pan for 5 minutes, loosen the edges and invert onto a plate. A little edge crumbling can happen. Cool completely before glazing.
6. **Glaze:** Heat all the cream until steaming, then pour it over all the separate glaze chocolate. Let stand about 5 minutes and stir gently until smooth. If needed, warm briefly and stir rather than adding unlisted cream. Keep water out of the chocolate bowl.
7. **Finish:** Pour all the glaze over the cool cake and spread to the edges, letting it drip down the sides. Allow it to set before slicing. Use a knife dipped in hot water and wiped dry between slices. Serve plain or with the optional whipped cream or ice cream.
