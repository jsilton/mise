## Chef's Note

King Arthur Baking created this cake for Passover, and it has become our favorite gluten-free dessert all year. It is dense and fudgy under a shiny chocolate glaze. A little espresso powder deepens the chocolate without making it taste like coffee.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the pan:** Heat the oven to 375°F. Grease an 8-inch round cake pan, line the bottom with parchment and grease the parchment.
2. **Melt the chocolate:** Melt the chocolate chips for the batter with the butter in a microwave-safe bowl in 30-second bursts, stirring after each, until smooth.
3. **Mix the batter:** Stir in the sugar, salt, espresso powder and vanilla. Beat in the eggs one at a time until the batter is smooth and glossy. Add the cocoa and stir just until combined.
4. **Bake the cake:** Spread the batter in the pan. Bake until the top has formed a thin crust and the center reads 200°F on an instant-read thermometer, about 25 minutes.
5. **Cool and unmold:** Cool in the pan for 5 minutes. Run a knife around the edge, invert the cake onto a serving plate and peel off the parchment. Let it cool completely, about 1 hour.
6. **Make the glaze:** Heat the cream until steaming. Pour it over the glaze chocolate chips, let stand for 5 minutes, then stir gently until smooth and glossy.
7. **Glaze the cake:** Pour the glaze over the cooled cake and spread it to the edges so it drips down the sides. Let it set for about 30 minutes. Slice with a knife dipped in hot water and wiped dry, and serve with whipped cream or ice cream.
