## Chef's Note

The Gwock family recipe uses eggs and chunks of medium cheese rather than a flour-thickened sauce. Add milk until the macaroni is almost covered, as the original specifies; the exact milk volume and cheese type were not recorded. Beat the eggs before combining and use the center temperature to decide when the casserole is ready.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 475°F. For the original batch, use a 9 × 9-inch baking dish rated for that temperature, with enough depth and headroom for the milk-filled pasta. Cut all the cheese into chunks and thinly slice all the margarine. Beat all the eggs with the listed salt in a separate bowl.
2. **Boil:** Boil all the macaroni in salted water, beginning the original recipe's check at 10 minutes and checking the actual package and pasta texture. Drain well and let the pasta cool from steaming-hot to warm before adding beaten eggs.
3. **Combine:** Mix the full cheese and margarine supplies through the drained pasta, then fold in all the beaten eggs and salt evenly. Transfer to the baking dish. Add milk until the macaroni is almost covered; stir gently so the egg mixture is distributed throughout. Do not overfill the vessel.
4. **Bake:** Bake at 475°F, checking before the end of the original 20–25-minute window. Continue until the center reaches 165°F / 74°C and the custard is set, without a pool of liquid egg. Check each dish if the batch is divided.
5. **Stand:** Stand 5 minutes before serving.
