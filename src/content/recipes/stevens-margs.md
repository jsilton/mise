## Chef's Note

This is Steven's margarita: equal parts La Gritona reposado, Cointreau and fresh lime, with a squirt of agave. A pinch of smoked sea salt goes right into the shaker, so every sip is seasoned and the salt brings out the oak in the reposado.

## Directions

1. **Chill the glass:** Fill a rocks glass with ice and set it aside while you mix.
2. **Shake:** Put the tequila, Cointreau, lime juice, smoked salt and agave in a shaker filled with ice. Shake hard until the shaker is frosty, 15 to 20 seconds.
3. **Strain and serve:** Double-strain the drink over the ice in the glass and serve right away.

## Suggested Tweak

For a stronger, more tequila-forward drink, use 2 oz tequila to 1 oz each of Cointreau and lime juice. Squeeze a lime wedge over the top and drop it in to garnish.
