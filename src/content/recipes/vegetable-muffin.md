## Chef's Note

Puree the carrots, greens and wet ingredients, then fold in the cocoa-and-flour mixture and chocolate chips. Keep the cups at or below ¾ full, and check the center rather than relying on the dark color.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F before mixing. Line a standard 12-cup muffin tin. Use baking spray (if using) only if needed for the liners. Wash carrots and greens unless the greens are labeled prewashed and ready to eat, and drain them. Prepare the listed grated carrots and mashed banana if using. If using coconut oil, melt and cool it slightly before combining it with eggs. Follow the actual blender capacity and loading instructions; use proportional loads for a larger batch.
2. **Puree:** Blend spinach or kale, ripe banana or unsweetened applesauce, carrots, coconut or avocado oil, maple syrup, large eggs, vanilla extract, and apple cider vinegar until the carrots and greens are pureed. This wet mixture is blended before adding flour.
3. **Dry mix:** In a large bowl, whisk white whole-wheat flour, cocoa powder, ground cinnamon, baking soda, baking powder, and salt together. Both leaveners and the full salt amount go into this mixture.
4. **Combine:** Fold the wet puree into the dry mixture just until no dry flour remains, then fold in dark chocolate chips.
5. **Fill and bake:** Fill cups no more than ¾ full and sprinkle with additional chocolate chips (if using). Bake excess batter separately rather than overfilling. Bake 20–22 minutes, until a center tester comes out without wet batter; melted chocolate is different from raw batter.
6. **Cool:** Rest in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack. Cool completely before sealing for storage. Freeze cooled muffins airtight for up to 1 month for quality.

## Cooking Notes

For the loosely packed greens option, use the same listed cup amount without pressing the leaves down; this changes the amount of greens from a packed cup. Honey can replace the listed maple syrup volume, and a cup-for-cup gluten-free flour blend can replace the listed flour volume. These are separate choices, with potentially different moisture and crumb; do not serve honey-containing muffins to children under one year.

For the separate version without chocolate chips, omit the main chips and add granulated sugar to the wet mixture: a couple tablespoons (2 tbsp) for the original 12-muffin batch, scaled proportionally. Optional extra topping chips are also omitted in that version. For a dairy-free version with chips, check the chip ingredients as well as the other chosen ingredients.

For an alternative high-speed-blender mixing route, first puree all the wet ingredients and vegetables as directed. If the appliance instructions permit this flour batter and the load fits, add all the dry-mixture ingredients to the puree and process just until mixed instead of folding the wet and dry mixtures in a bowl. Fold the chips in by hand, then follow the same filling, baking and cooling steps.
