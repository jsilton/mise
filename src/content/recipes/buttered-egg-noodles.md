## Chef's Note

Buttered egg noodles are the soft, simple side that belongs under a braise, a stew or anything with gravy. Toss the hot noodles with butter and a splash of their starchy cooking water, and the two come together into a glossy coating instead of a greasy puddle.

## Directions

1. **Boil the noodles:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a rolling boil. Add the noodles and cook until tender with no hard center, 7 to 8 minutes.
2. **Save some water:** Before draining, scoop out the starchy pasta water. Drain the noodles.
3. **Toss with butter:** Return the noodles to the pot over low heat. Add the butter, salt, pepper and pasta water. Toss until the butter melts into a glossy coating, 30 to 60 seconds.
4. **Serve:** Scatter with parsley and serve hot.
