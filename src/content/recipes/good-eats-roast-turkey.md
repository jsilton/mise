## Chef's Note

Alton Brown's Good Eats turkey taught a generation to brine. A sweet, spiced brine seasons the meat all the way through, and a half hour at 500°F before a steady roast at 350°F browns the skin without drying the breast. An apple, onion and cinnamon steamed inside the bird perfume it from within.

## Directions

1. **Make the brine:** Put a quarter of the stock in a large pot with the salt, brown sugar, peppercorns, allspice and candied ginger. Bring to a boil over high heat, stirring until the salt and sugar dissolve. Take it off the heat, stir in the rest of the stock and refrigerate until cold, about 2 hours.
2. **Brine the turkey:** Pour the cold brine and the ice water into a 5-gallon food-safe bucket or a brining bag set in a deep pan. Submerge the turkey breast side down, cover and refrigerate for 12 hours.
3. **Dry the skin:** Lift out the turkey, let the cavity drain and discard the brine. Pat the bird dry, set it on a rack over a rimmed sheet pan and refrigerate it uncovered until the skin feels dry and papery, about 4 hours.
4. **Steam the aromatics:** Heat the oven to 500°F with a rack in the lowest position. Put the apple, onion, cinnamon stick and water in a microwave-safe bowl and microwave on high for 5 minutes.
5. **Stuff and oil the bird:** Set the turkey breast side up on the rack in a roasting pan. Drain the aromatics and put them in the cavity with the rosemary and sage. Tuck the wing tips under the bird and rub the skin all over with the oil.
6. **Roast at 500°F:** Roast on the lowest rack until the skin is golden, 30 minutes. While it roasts, fold a double layer of heavy foil into a triangle that will cover the breast.
7. **Finish at 350°F:** Lay the foil triangle over the breast and push a probe thermometer into the thickest part of the breast. Turn the oven to 350°F and roast until the breast reads 161°F, 1 1/2 to 2 hours more.
8. **Rest and carve:** Move the turkey to a carving board, tent it loosely with foil and rest 15 minutes, as the breast climbs to 165°F. Discard the aromatics and carve.
