## Chef's Note

Gina Homolka's lighter mac and cheese from Skinnytaste. Chicken broth stretches the skim-milk sauce and adds the savory depth that low-fat cheese can't give on its own. The broccoli cooks right in the pasta pot, and a breadcrumb and Parmesan top gives it crunch.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 375°F. Coat a large baking dish with cooking spray.
2. **Boil the pasta and broccoli:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta until al dente, adding the broccoli for the last 2 minutes. Drain well.
3. **Make the roux:** While the pasta cooks, melt the butter in a large heavy skillet over low heat. Add the onion and cook until soft, about 2 minutes. Whisk in the flour and cook until lightly golden, about 1 minute.
4. **Thicken the sauce:** Slowly whisk in the milk and broth. Bring to a boil, stirring, then simmer until smooth and thick enough to coat a spoon, about 5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
5. **Add the cheese:** Take the skillet off the heat and stir in the cheddar until melted. Fold in the pasta and broccoli and spread it in the baking dish.
6. **Top and bake:** Scatter the breadcrumbs and Parmesan over the top and mist with cooking spray. Bake until bubbling at the edges, 15 to 20 minutes. Broil until the top is golden, 1 to 2 minutes, watching closely.
