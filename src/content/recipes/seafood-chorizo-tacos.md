## Chef's Note

Fresh Mexican-style chorizo seasons these shrimp-and-squid tacos with its full rendered fat. Cook the sausage before adding the seafood so its endpoint does not force a longer seafood cook. Lager and lime stay in the filling; avocado, cilantro, sour cream and lime wedges complete the tacos.

## Directions

1. **Cook the Chorizo:** Use extra-virgin olive oil, fresh uncooked chorizo, salt, and black pepper at the sausage stage. Heat the oil in a large skillet over moderately high heat, then add the chorizo and its initial salt and pepper. Break up the chorizo as it browns; about 4 minutes is an early check, not guaranteed doneness. Cook fresh pork/beef chorizo to 160°F, or 165°F if its label identifies poultry. Keep the full oil and rendered fat in the pan. Use enough pan area for the actual load, or cook separate proportionate batches with their own full supplies.
2. **Soften the Onion:** Add small red onion and ground cumin and cook about 2 minutes, until onion begins to soften.
3. **Add the Garlic:** Add garlic clove and sauté for 30–45 seconds, until fragrant.
4. **Cook the Seafood:** Add raw medium shrimp, cleaned small squid, and Mexican lager and toss over moderately high heat. Begin checking around 3 minutes; continue as needed for the seafood to reach 145°F. Shrimp should be firm, pearly and opaque, and squid cooked through. Raw size, thin rings and pan loading determine the time; do not stop solely because 3 minutes have passed.
5. **Finish the Filling:** Once sausage and seafood are cooked, stir in fresh lime juice, salt, and black pepper, season to taste and take the skillet off the heat.
6. **Serve:** Use corn tortillas, avocado, fresh cilantro, sour cream, and lime wedges for serving. Warm or char the tortillas in a dry skillet or carefully over a suitable flame, then fill with all the seafood-chorizo mixture and top with the avocado, cilantro and sour cream. Keep the toppings cold until assembly and serve the lime wedges alongside.

## Oil Variation

To make the preparation with half the oil (one tablespoon in the original batch), use half the listed olive oil at the chorizo stage and omit the other half. Keep the full chorizo, seafood and rendered fat, all aromatics, lager and lime, and the same cooking checks and toppings. This is a separate choice; the main preparation uses all the listed oil.
