## Chef's Note

Shrimp Craig is Craig Plummer's own dish for two: butterflied jumbo shrimp in clarified butter with garlic, parsley and hot paprika, over rice. The card says it needs a good French white wine alongside. Keep the lemon soak short, since the acid starts to firm the shrimp after about 15 minutes.

## Directions

1. **Clarify the butter:** Melt the butter in a small saucepan over low heat until the white foam rises, about 5 minutes. Skim off the foam and pour the clear yellow butter into a cup, leaving the milky layer behind.
2. **Season the shrimp:** Toss the jumbo shrimp and seasoned salt in a bowl until coated.
3. **Add the lemon:** Squeeze the lemon over the shrimp and toss. Let sit for 5 minutes and no longer than 15.
4. **Cook the garlic:** Heat the clarified butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic clove and fresh parsley and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
5. **Cook the shrimp:** Lift the shrimp from the lemon juice and add them to the skillet in a single layer. Sprinkle with hot paprika. Cook, turning once, until pink and opaque all the way through, 7 to 8 minutes.
6. **Serve over rice:** Serve the shrimp over hot rice with the butter from the pan spooned over the top.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Splash 1/4 cup of the white wine into the skillet for the last minute of cooking. It turns the butter into a brighter sauce.
- Cook the shrimp in two batches, about 2 minutes per side, so they sear in a single layer and stay tender. Keep the first batch warm on a plate.
- Squeeze a little more lemon over the shrimp right before serving.
