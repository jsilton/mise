## Chef's Note

Roasted hot and fast, green beans shrivel and brown at the tips like skinny fries, and that is how they win over kids. Dry them well and give them room on the pan so they roast instead of steam.

## Directions

1. **Season the beans:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the green beans with the oil, garlic powder and salt on a large rimmed baking sheet and spread them in one layer.
2. **Roast:** Roast, turning once, until the beans are tender and shriveled and the tips are brown and crisp, 10 to 15 minutes.
3. **Add the topping:** If using the Parmesan or panko, sprinkle it over the beans, toss and roast until golden, 2 to 3 minutes more. Serve hot.
