## Chef's Note

Dry the beans after washing and spread them out so their surfaces can brown. Look for tender beans and browned tips; they need not become brittle or resemble potato fries.

## Directions

1. **Preheat:** Preheat to 425°F. Prepare enough baking sheets for an uncrowded layer.

2. **Dry:** Wash and trim the beans, then dry them thoroughly with a clean towel.

3. **Season:** Toss with all the listed oil, garlic powder and salt. Spread in one layer, with room to move the beans when turning.

4. **Roast:** Begin checking around 10–12 minutes. Turn if browning unevenly and continue until the beans are tender to bite and their tips are browned, avoiding blackened ends. Smaller beans may finish earlier; crowded or thicker beans may take longer.

5. **Finish:** Toss with the listed optional Parmesan OR panko and serve hot. Panko added now is a crumb topping, not a promise of a toasted crust; use ready-to-eat breadcrumbs.
