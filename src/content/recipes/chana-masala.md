## Chef's Note

Chana masala is the North Indian chickpea curry in a thick onion and tomato gravy. Whole cumin bloomed in hot fat gives it little bursts of flavor, and amchur, dried green mango, adds the fruity tang. Mashing a few chickpeas into the sauce at the end makes it cling to the rest.

## Directions

1. **Bloom the cumin:** Heat the ghee in a wide 4-quart pot over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and stir until they sizzle and turn a shade darker, 20 to 30 seconds.
2. **Brown the onion:** Add the onion right away. Cook, stirring often, until soft and evenly golden, 12 to 15 minutes. Lower the heat if the edges brown before the onion softens. Stir in the garlic, ginger and serranos and cook for 1 minute.
3. **Cook the masala:** Lower the heat and stir in the coriander, turmeric, amchur, chili powder and half the garam masala for 15 seconds. Add the tomatoes, return to medium heat and cook uncovered, stirring often, until thick and a spoon leaves a trail across the pot, 8 to 10 minutes.
4. **Simmer the chickpeas:** Add the chickpeas, most of the salt and the water. Bring to a simmer, then cover and cook over medium-low heat for 20 minutes, stirring once or twice.
5. **Thicken the curry:** Uncover and mash about a quarter of the chickpeas against the side of the pot. Simmer uncovered until the sauce clings to the beans but still moves, 3 to 5 minutes. Add a splash of hot water if it gets too thick.
6. **Finish and serve:** Stir in the rest of the garam masala and simmer for 1 minute, then turn off the heat. Stir in the lemon juice and cilantro. Taste and add the rest of the salt and more lemon. Pull out the serranos before serving if you want less heat.
