## Chef's Note

A French-style side that comes together in minutes. Blanch the beans so they stay bright green and crisp, then toss them in butter that has gently soaked up sliced garlic. Toasted almonds add a little crunch.

## Directions

1. **Blanch the beans:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Add the beans and cook until bright green and barely tender-crisp, 3 to 4 minutes. Drain well.
2. **Toast the almonds:** Toast the almonds in a dry large skillet over medium-low heat, stirring often, until golden and fragrant, 2 to 3 minutes. Move them to a plate.
3. **Make the garlic butter:** Put the butter and garlic in the same skillet over medium heat. Stir until the butter melts and the garlic is soft and fragrant but not browned, 60 to 90 seconds.
4. **Toss the beans:** Add the beans, kosher salt and pepper. Toss until the beans are hot and glossy, 1 to 2 minutes.
5. **Finish:** Toss in the almonds and move everything to a serving dish. Sprinkle with flaky salt and serve right away.
