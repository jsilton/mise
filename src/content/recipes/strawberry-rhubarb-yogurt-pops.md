## Chef's Note

Bright pink fruit pops for spring, when strawberries and rhubarb come in together. A quick simmer in orange juice just softens the tart rhubarb, and Greek yogurt and honey make the pops creamy instead of icy. A few pieces of cooked fruit stirred back in give every bite some texture.

## Directions

1. **Simmer the fruit:** Combine the strawberries, rhubarb, orange juice and salt in a medium saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until the fruit just begins to break down, 3 to 4 minutes.
2. **Cool the fruit:** Spread the fruit in a shallow dish and let it cool, about 15 minutes. Set the dish over ice to speed it up.
3. **Blend:** Lift out a few spoonfuls of fruit pieces with a slotted spoon and set them aside. Blend the rest of the fruit and all its juice with the yogurt and honey until smooth.
4. **Fill the molds:** Stir the reserved fruit pieces into the purée. Pour into the molds, leaving a little room at the top for the pops to expand.
5. **Freeze:** Freeze for 1 hour, until firm enough to hold the sticks upright. Insert the sticks and freeze until solid, at least 3 hours more.
6. **Unmold:** Run the outside of the molds under warm water for about 10 seconds, until the pops slide out.
