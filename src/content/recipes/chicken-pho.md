## Chef's Note

The serving meat and broth have different cooking needs. Remove the chicken quarters when cooked, then let the bones continue simmering for body and flavor. Aromatics vary between phở gà recipes; this version retains a modest amount of star anise, cinnamon, and coriander. Hoisin, chile sauce, and fresh herbs are optional at the table so the broth can be tasted on its own first.

## Directions

1. **Toast aromatics:** Put onion halves and ginger cut-side up on a rimmed sheet pan under the broiler, watching closely and turning as needed, for about 10–15 minutes until softened with charred patches. Cool enough to handle, remove loose blackened onion skin, and scrape off any burned debris. Toast star anise, cinnamon, and coriander in a dry skillet over medium heat for about 1 minute until fragrant.
2. **Start broth:** Put chicken quarters, wings or backs, aromatics, and spices in a 12-quart stockpot with the measured water. Bring just to a boil, skim surface foam, and reduce to a gentle simmer with only occasional bubbles. Do not wash raw chicken in the sink.
3. **Remove serving meat when cooked:** Begin checking quarters after about 30 minutes of simmering. Probe the thickest breast and thigh meat away from bone; remove each quarter only after it reaches 165°F / 74°C. Pieces may need 40 minutes or longer. Transfer to a clean tray and let stand about 10 minutes until safe to handle. Remove meat from bones, put the meat in a shallow covered container, and refrigerate promptly. Return the bones to the stockpot.
4. **Continue the broth:** Simmer bones and aromatics gently for about 2 more hours, skimming occasionally. Add a little water if needed to keep bones submerged. Avoid vigorously stirring the pot.
5. **Strain and balance:** Strain carefully through a fine-mesh sieve into another large pot and discard solids. Skim excess fat, leaving some for flavor. Aim for roughly 3.5–4 liters broth; reduce if weak or add water if overly concentrated. Stir in sugar and start with half the fish sauce and half the salt. Taste and add the remainder gradually only as needed.
6. **Prepare noodles and toppings:** Cook bánh phở according to its package until tender but not falling apart; different widths require different soaking or boiling methods. Drain and divide among six large bowls. Prepare scallion, cilantro, lime, and optional basil, chile, and cooked sprouts.
7. **Warm meat and assemble:** Slice the refrigerated chicken. Add it to simmering broth and heat through to 165°F / 74°C. Distribute meat among the noodle bowls, then ladle in about 500–600 ml broth per bowl. Finish with scallion and cilantro and offer the remaining toppings and sauces at the table. Refrigerate any unused broth promptly.
