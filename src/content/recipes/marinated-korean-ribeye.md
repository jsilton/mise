## Chef's Note

Bulgogi uses very thin slices so the beef can cook quickly in uncrowded pan loads. This marinade keeps its soy, sesame oil, brown sugar and grated pear; the pear contributes fruit and sweetness without a promise of enzyme-driven tenderness. If slicing a whole ribeye, partial freezer firming can make thin cuts easier, but the meat should remain sliceable.

## Directions

1. **Prepare thin slices:** Keep the beef cold. If starting with a whole ribeye, use about 45 minutes in the freezer as a first check until firm enough to slice thinly, still yielding to the knife; this adds preparation time. Pat unseasoned sliced beef dry with paper towels before marinating.
2. **Mix and marinate:** For the homemade route, use ribeye beef, soy sauce, toasted sesame oil, brown sugar, Bosc or Asian pear, garlic cloves, fresh ginger, green onions, and black pepper. Whisk the soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, grated pear, garlic, ginger, green onions and pepper together. Toss with all the sliced beef, cover and refrigerate at 40°F or below for at least 30 minutes or overnight. The pre-marinated option below replaces the complete homemade marinade.
3. **Cook in uncrowded loads:** Heat a heavy skillet or wok over high heat until hot, adjusting if the coating scorches rather than browns. Use suitable high-heat cooking oil as needed. Lift the marinated beef from excess pooled marinade, leaving a coating on the slices, and spread one uncrowded layer in the pan. Begin checking after 1–2 minutes for browned edges, then flip and begin checking after about 30 seconds. Continue until the pieces reach at least 145°F in the center with a thermometer suitable for thin food, inserted from the side; follow any higher endpoint on a product label. These are first checks, not guaranteed finishing times.
4. **Rest and serve:** Move fully cooked beef to a clean serving dish and rest at least 3 minutes before eating. Continue with the remaining pan loads, keeping waiting raw beef cold and using separate clean utensils for cooked beef. Discard pooled raw-contact marinade; do not add it at the end. More loads extend elapsed time.
