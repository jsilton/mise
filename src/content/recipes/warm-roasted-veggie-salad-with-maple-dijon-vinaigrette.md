## Chef's Note

Warm sweet potato and Brussels sprouts soften the kale slightly, while cranberries and Parmesan provide sweet and salty accents. Keep the roasting oil separate from the vinaigrette, and add the dressing in stages so the vegetables stay lightly coated rather than swimming in it.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Heat the oven to 375°F / 190°C. Dry the washed vegetables, cube the sweet potato evenly and halve the sprouts; quarter unusually large sprouts. Line a large rimmed sheet pan if desired.
2. **Roast:** Toss sweet potato, sprouts and onion with the 2 tablespoons roasting oil, salt and pepper. Spread in a single layer with space between pieces. Roast, turning once, starting to check at 20–25 minutes and allowing about 30 minutes or longer as needed. Remove when sweet potato is fork-tender and sprouts are browned; do not chase blackened edges.
3. **Whisk dressing:** While the vegetables roast, whisk the 1/3 cup dressing oil with vinegar, maple syrup, Dijon and garlic. Taste and season with salt and pepper. Stir again if it separates.
4. **Soften kale:** Wash, dry, stem and chop kale before measuring. Put the leaves in a large bowl with a small pinch of salt and rub gently for 1–2 minutes until pliable.
5. **Combine:** Let the roasted vegetables stop steaming fiercely, then add to the kale with Parmesan and cranberries. Add about half the dressing and toss with tongs; add more only as needed to coat. Refrigerate any unused dressing separately.
6. **Serve:** Divide into six side portions and serve warm. Refrigerate leftovers promptly rather than leaving the bowl at room temperature.

## Cooking Notes

About 45 minutes allows 15 minutes for preheating and preparation plus roughly 25–30 minutes roasting. Make dressing and massage kale while vegetables roast; large pieces or crowded trays take longer.

Refrigerate in a shallow covered container at 40°F / 4°C or below within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C. Use within 3 days. For best make-ahead texture, store dressing separately and assemble near serving.
