## Chef's Note

This NYT Cooking salad dresses summer tomatoes in soy, toasted sesame and olive oil, a mix that tastes both savory and bright. The tomatoes rest in half the dressing for a few minutes so their juices run into it. Make it only with tomatoes at their peak.

## Directions

1. **Make the dressing:** Whisk the olive oil, sesame oil, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic and sugar in a bowl until the sugar dissolves.
2. **Dress the tomatoes:** Lay the tomatoes on a wide, shallow platter without piling them deep. Spoon half the dressing over them and let them stand 5 minutes so their juices mix with it.
3. **Finish and serve:** Scatter the basil over the top and grind on black pepper. Taste a tomato and add flaky salt only if it needs it. Serve right away with the rest of the dressing on the side.
