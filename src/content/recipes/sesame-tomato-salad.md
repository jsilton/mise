## Chef's Note

The dressing is deliberately generous, but only part goes on the tomatoes at first. Their juices join the soy and sesame during a short rest; offer the remainder separately and taste before adding flaky salt. Adapted from [The New York Times’ Sesame Tomato Salad](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024154-sesame-tomato-salad).

## Directions

1. **Mix dressing:** Whisk olive oil, toasted sesame oil, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic and sugar until the sugar dissolves and the liquids combine. Set aside; whisk again before using if separated.

2. **Prepare tomatoes:** Wash and dry tomatoes, core large ones and slice or chop them; halve small tomatoes. Arrange on a wide shallow platter without piling them deeply.

3. **Dress and rest:** Spoon about half the dressing over the tomatoes. Let stand about 5 minutes so the tomato juices mingle with it. Keep the rest of the dressing in a separate serving bowl.

4. **Finish:** Scatter with basil and black pepper. Taste a dressed tomato before adding any flaky salt; it may not need more. Serve promptly with the remaining dressing on the side.
