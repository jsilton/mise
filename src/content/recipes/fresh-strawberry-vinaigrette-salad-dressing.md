## Chef's Note

This dressing comes from Rachel at Grow a Good Life and is best in spring and early summer, when strawberries are ripe and sweet. A half hour in balsamic and honey softens the berries and draws out their juice. Blended with olive oil, they make a thick, glossy dressing that is fruit and acid in one.

## Directions

1. **Macerate the berries:** Whisk the vinegar and honey in a small bowl. Add the strawberries, toss to coat and let stand until soft and sitting in their juice, about 30 minutes.
2. **Blend the dressing:** Scrape the berries and all their juice into a small food processor, or a tall cup if using an immersion blender. Add the olive oil, salt and pepper and blend until smooth and bright red.
3. **Taste and dress:** Taste it on a leaf. Add a splash more vinegar if it tastes flat, or a little honey if it is too tart. Toss with greens just before serving.
