## Chef's Note

Blended fruit gives this dressing body, while balsamic vinegar and honey balance its flavor. Give the sliced berries their rest before blending, then taste on the greens you intend to serve. Add only enough to coat them. Adapted from [Rachel at Grow a Good Life](https://growagoodlife.com/strawberry-vinaigrette/).

## Directions

1. **Prepare fruit:** Wash the strawberries, remove their hulls, slice and measure the listed amount.
2. **Rest:** Stir vinegar and honey together in a bowl, coat the berries with the mixture and leave for **30 minutes**. Keep all the released juice.
3. **Blend:** Transfer berries and liquid to a small processor or suitable blender vessel. Add olive oil and a little salt and pepper; blend until the fruit is finely broken down, stopping to scrape safely if needed.
4. **Taste and dress:** Taste a small amount on a leaf. Adjust with a little honey if sharply tart or a little vinegar if too sweet, then check salt and pepper. Toss into dry greens gradually just before serving and reserve excess dressing for another salad.
