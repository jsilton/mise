## Chef's Note

Mina Stone’s lemon olive-oil cake uses the full oil, whole milk, lemon juice and zest in a whisked batter. Keep the listed surface sugar amount separate from the batter sugar, and the pan oil separate from the batter oil. A golden top and a clean center tester are both needed before the cake cools in its pan.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. For the original batch use a 9-inch springform pan. Brush the pan lightly with extra-virgin olive oil, line its bottom with a parchment round and lightly oil the parchment. The extra pan oil is separate from the full measured batter oil. When scaling, keep a similar batter depth with proportional pan area or additional pans; do not put extra full batches into this one pan.
2. **Whisk the base:** Whisk extra-virgin olive oil, granulated sugar, and large eggs in a bowl until smooth and combined. This is the batter sugar and full measured batter oil, separate from the pan oil and sugar topping.
3. **Add milk and lemon:** Whisk in whole milk, large lemons, and pure vanilla extract, using the full zest and juice of the listed lemons.
4. **Mix dry ingredients:** In a separate bowl, whisk all-purpose flour, baking powder, baking soda, and sea salt until evenly distributed.
5. **Combine:** Make a well in the dry mixture and gradually whisk in the egg mixture, using circular motions. Stop when mostly smooth with no dry flour pockets; some small lumps are acceptable, but lumps are not required.
6. **Fill and top:** Scrape into the prepared pan and smooth the surface. Sprinkle granulated sugar over the top; this is additional to all the batter sugar.
7. **Bake:** Wait for the oven to reach 350°F. Bake about 50 minutes, until golden on top AND a tester inserted into the center comes out clean. Do not deliberately remove a wet-batter cake to rely on residual heat. Changed pan depths and oven loads need their own checks.
8. **Cool:** Set the pan on a wire rack and let the cake cool completely before unmolding. Cooling time is additional and has no fixed duration here.
9. **Serve:** Just before serving, dust with powdered sugar and lemon zest (if using), adding the extra zest only if desired.

## Cooking Notes

### Salt and make-ahead notes

The listed sea salt is the main version. For the publisher’s original-batch option, use either 1 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt or ½ tsp Morton kosher salt instead of the entire listed sea-salt amount. Multiply the chosen amount with the batch, add it with the dry ingredients and keep every other ingredient and step unchanged. These are the two exact source choices; neither is an established sea-salt equivalence.

The plain cake may be baked three days ahead. Cool before wrapping and store tightly wrapped at room temperature. Add the powdered sugar and optional extra zest shortly before serving. Any separately added perishable topping needs its own storage plan.
