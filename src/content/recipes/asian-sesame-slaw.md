## Chef's Note

Napa cabbage is softer and sweeter than green cabbage, so this slaw is tender-crisp instead of crunchy-hard. Edamame makes it hearty and a sesame-ginger dressing ties it together. Crisp fried wonton strips go on at the very last minute.

## Directions

1. **Cook the edamame:** Boil the edamame in salted water until tender, about 4 minutes. Drain, rinse under cold water and drain well.
2. **Fry the wonton strips:** If using, heat 1/2 inch of oil in a deep skillet to 350°F. Fry the wonton strips in small batches, stirring to separate them, until pale golden and puffed, 15 to 20 seconds. Lift them onto paper towels with a slotted spoon, sprinkle with the salt and let cool.
3. **Make the dressing:** Shake the neutral oil, sesame oil, vinegar, soy sauce, honey, ginger, garlic and chile flakes (if using) in a small jar until combined.
4. **Toss and serve:** In a large bowl, toss the cabbage, carrots, edamame, green onions and sesame seeds with the dressing until evenly coated. Scatter the wonton strips on top just before serving.
