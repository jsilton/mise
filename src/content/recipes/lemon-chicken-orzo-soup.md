## Chef's Note

A bright, comforting chicken soup with orzo in a lightly thickened broth. A little flour cooked with the vegetables gives the broth body, and lemon zest and juice stirred in off the heat keep it fresh.

## Directions

1. **Soften the vegetables:** Heat the butter and oil in a 5-quart pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, celery, pepper and a third of the salt. Cook, stirring, until the vegetables are soft, 7 to 9 minutes.
2. **Cook the flour:** Add the garlic and thyme and stir for 30 seconds. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 1 minute to cook out the raw taste.
3. **Add the broth:** Slowly pour in the broth, holding back the extra for thinning. Stir as you pour so the flour doesn't lump, and scrape up anything stuck to the bottom. Bring to a simmer over medium-high heat.
4. **Cook the chicken and orzo:** Add the chicken and orzo and lower the heat to a gentle simmer. Cook, stirring along the bottom often so the orzo doesn't stick, until the orzo is tender and the chicken is cooked through, 10 to 12 minutes.
5. **Season and finish:** If the soup is thick, thin it with some of the extra broth. Stir in the rest of the salt. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the parsley, lemon zest and half the lemon juice. Taste, add the rest of the juice and more salt if needed, and serve right away.
