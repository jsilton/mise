## Chef's Note

Buckwheat gives these chocolate-chip muffins a nutty flavor; whole-wheat flour is a separate listed choice with a different crumb. Squeeze excess liquid from the shredded zucchini, then stir only enough to incorporate the flour and shreds.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a standard 12-cup muffin tin with the pan allowance, or use liners. Prepare the listed shredded zucchini by squeezing excess liquid out in a clean towel.

2. **Dry mix:** Whisk the chosen buckwheat or whole-wheat flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon and salt together.

3. **Wet mix:** Whisk milk, egg, the chosen maple syrup or honey, the chosen avocado or canola oil, and vanilla together.

4. **Combine:** Stir the wet mixture into the dry just until no dry flour remains. Do not beat the batter or keep stirring to make it perfectly smooth.

5. **Fold:** Fold in all the chocolate chips and squeezed zucchini until distributed.

6. **Bake:** Fill muffin cups no more than ¾ full; use additional cups or a later batch for excess batter. Bake 16–18 minutes, until a center tester comes out without wet batter.

7. **Cool:** Leave in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack. Cool completely before sealing for storage.
