## Chef's Note

Buckwheat flour gives these muffins a nutty, earthy flavor that goes well with chocolate. Grated zucchini keeps them moist with only a little oil. Squeeze it dry first and stir just until the flour disappears, and the centers bake up tender instead of gummy.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.
2. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon and salt together in a large bowl.
3. **Mix the wet ingredients:** In another bowl, whisk the egg, milk, maple syrup, oil and vanilla until smooth.
4. **Make the batter:** Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir with a spatula just until no dry flour remains. A few lumps are fine. Fold in the zucchini and chocolate chips.
5. **Fill the cups:** Divide the batter among the cups, filling them about three-quarters full.
6. **Bake the muffins:** Bake until a toothpick in the center comes out with no wet batter, 16 to 18 minutes.
7. **Cool the muffins:** Cool the muffins in the pan for 10 minutes so they firm up, then move them to a rack.
