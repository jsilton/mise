## Chef's Note

Richard Blais’s risotto-style oats soak overnight in the refrigerator, then cook in all their soaking liquid until creamy with a slight chew. Mashed overripe banana gives body; drained sherry-vinegar raisins supply the sweet-sour finish. Generous Parmesan shavings add savory depth if using, with optional extra brown sugar for serving.

## Directions

1. **Refrigerate the overnight soak:** Combine steel-cut oats and water or chicken stock or broth in a covered bowl. Refrigerate overnight at 40°F / 4°C or below. Keep the covered oats and all soaking liquid cold until ready to cook.
2. **Soak the raisins:** Put golden raisins and sherry vinegar in a small bowl and stir so the vinegar coats the raisins. Let them stand while the oats cook; drain the soaking vinegar before topping.
3. **Bring the oats to a simmer:** Pour the oats and all their soaking liquid into a saucepan with headroom for bubbling. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Add light brown sugar and kosher salt and stir.
4. **Cook by texture:** Stir occasionally, including the bottom, and start checking around 20 minutes. Continue until the oats are tender with a slight chew, without hard centers. Lower the heat if the bottom catches. Use additional hot water only if extended simmering makes the mixture dry before the oats soften; add a small splash at a time, keeping the porridge cohesive.
5. **Fold in the banana:** Add overripe banana and cook about 2 minutes more, stirring until the banana is evenly incorporated and hot throughout.
6. **Drain the raisins and finish:** Drain the soaked raisins and discard the free vinegar. Divide the oats among the listed portions and distribute all the drained raisins among them. Use Parmesan cheese (if using) and light brown sugar (if using): add generous Parmesan shavings if using, and offer the optional extra sugar. Serve promptly.
