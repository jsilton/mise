## Chef's Note

These are buttery Yukon Gold mashed potatoes with a little cream cheese for richness and a gentle tang. Salt the cooking water well so the potatoes are seasoned all the way through. Then mash them gently, because overworked potatoes turn gluey.

## Directions

1. **Start the potatoes:** Put the potatoes in a large pot with the cold water and the kosher salt. The water should taste pleasantly salty.
2. **Simmer until soft:** Bring to a boil over high heat, then lower to a steady simmer. Cook until a knife slides through the pieces with no resistance, 18 to 22 minutes.
3. **Warm the dairy:** While the potatoes cook, warm the butter, cream cheese and half-and-half in a small saucepan over low heat. Stir until the butter melts and the cream cheese softens. Don't let it boil.
4. **Dry the potatoes:** Drain the potatoes well and return them to the pot over low heat. Shake the pot for 1 to 2 minutes, until the surfaces look dry and matte.
5. **Mash:** Off the heat, pass the potatoes through a ricer or mash them with a hand masher until no large pieces remain. Fold in the warm dairy in two additions with a spatula and stop as soon as it is combined. If the mash is stiff, fold in a little more warm half-and-half.
6. **Season and serve:** Fold in the pepper and half the fine salt. Taste and add more salt until the potatoes taste fully seasoned. Serve hot.
