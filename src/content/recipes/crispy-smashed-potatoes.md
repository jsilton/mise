## Chef's Note

Boiling baby potatoes until tender and then smashing them flat gives you jagged edges and crannies that roast up glassy and crisp, while the insides stay fluffy. Let them steam dry for a few minutes before the oil goes on, so the edges fry instead of steam.

## Directions

1. **Boil the potatoes:** Put the whole potatoes in a pot of cold, well-salted water. Bring to a boil and cook until a fork slides easily into the centers, 15 to 20 minutes. Drain. Heat the oven to 425°F while they cook.
2. **Smash:** Line a baking sheet with parchment and spread out the potatoes with space between them. Press each one with the bottom of a heavy glass to about 1/2 inch thick, keeping it in one piece.
3. **Steam dry:** Let the smashed potatoes sit for 5 minutes so the surface moisture cooks off.
4. **Season:** Drizzle the olive oil over the potatoes and sprinkle with the garlic powder, herbs, kosher salt and pepper.
5. **Roast:** Roast, flipping once halfway through, until deep golden brown and the edges are crisp, 25 to 30 minutes.
6. **Finish:** Sprinkle with flaky salt and serve hot.
