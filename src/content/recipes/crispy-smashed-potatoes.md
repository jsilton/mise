## Chef's Note

Boil the skin-on baby potatoes until soft, then flatten them gently to expose uneven edges. Let surface steam escape before coating with all the measured oil. Space the potatoes so those edges can brown, and use tenderness and color rather than expecting every tray to finish on the same clock.

## Directions

1. **Boil:** Scrub whole baby potatoes and keep the skins on. Place in cold water and salt it with the listed allowance. Bring to a boil and cook about 15–20 minutes after boiling, until the centers yield easily to a fork. Drain. Start preheating to 425 °F while they boil.

2. **Smash:** Line a baking sheet with oven-suitable parchment. Arrange with space between potatoes; use additional sheets if needed. Gently press each with a heavy glass bottom or masher to about 1/2 inch thick, keeping the skin-on pieces together.

3. **Dry:** Let the smashed potatoes stand about 5 minutes so surface steam can escape.

4. **Season:** Distribute all the measured olive oil across the total batch, including any extra sheets. Add all the garlic powder and chosen dried rosemary or thyme; salt and pepper to taste. Do not repeat the full measured oil/seasoning amount on each tray.

5. **Roast:** Roast at 425 °F for about 25–30 minutes as a first window, flipping carefully halfway through. Continue until edges are deep golden and crisp and centers remain tender. Extra trays may need rotation and longer; judge the edges by color and crispness as well as the tenderness of the centers.

6. **Finish:** Add the flaky sea salt finish to taste and serve hot. Refrigerate leftovers promptly; reheat to 165 °F and use the oven to help restore edges rather than expecting microwave crispness.

## Timing

Allow about 75 minutes including preparation and bringing water to a boil. The 15–20-minute tenderizing window starts after boiling; 5-minute steam dry and 25–30-minute roast follow. Preheat during boiling. More trays and larger potatoes can extend time.
