## Chef's Note

Dashi is the stock at the heart of Japanese cooking, made in minutes from just kombu and dried bonito flakes. This is ichiban dashi, the clean first stock used for miso soup, noodle broths and simmered dishes. Pull the kombu out just before the water boils, because boiling makes it bitter and slimy.

## Directions

1. **Soak the kombu:** Put the cold water and kombu in a large saucepan and let it soak for 10 minutes.
2. **Heat the kombu:** Heat over medium-high heat. Just before the water boils, when small bubbles cover the bottom of the pan, lift out the kombu.
3. **Add the bonito:** Scatter the bonito flakes over the water. Let the water just return to a boil, then take the pan off the heat right away.
4. **Steep:** Let it stand without stirring until the flakes sink, about 3 minutes.
5. **Strain:** Pour the dashi through a fine-mesh sieve or a coffee filter into a clean bowl. Don't squeeze the flakes, or the dashi turns cloudy.
