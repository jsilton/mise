## Chef's Note

This is a whole dinner in one pan. Chicken thighs sear first, then simmer on a bed of quinoa that soaks up the broth and the chicken juices. Brown the mushrooms well before the quinoa goes in, because that color is where the flavor comes from. Spinach and lemon go in at the end to keep it fresh.

## Directions

1. **Season the chicken:** Pat the chicken dry. Season it all over with the salt for the chicken, the pepper, rosemary and smoked paprika.
2. **Sear the chicken:** Heat half the oil in a wide 4- to 5-quart sauté pan over medium-high heat. Sear the thighs in two batches until browned, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Move them to a plate. They finish cooking later.
3. **Brown the mushrooms:** Add the rest of the oil, the mushrooms, onion and the remaining salt. Cook, stirring now and then, until the mushroom liquid has cooked off and they are golden, 8 to 10 minutes. Scrape up the browned bits as you stir.
4. **Simmer the quinoa:** Stir in the quinoa and broth and bring to a simmer. Nestle the chicken and its juices into the quinoa. Cover, turn the heat to low and cook until the quinoa is tender, most of the liquid is absorbed and the chicken reaches 165°F, 18 to 22 minutes. Add a splash of hot water if the pan dries out before the quinoa is done.
5. **Wilt the spinach:** Scatter the spinach over the top, cover and cook until it wilts, about 2 minutes. Take the pan off the heat and let it rest, covered, for 5 minutes.
6. **Finish with lemon:** Lift out the chicken. Fold the spinach and lemon juice through the quinoa and season with salt to taste. Serve each thigh on a scoop of quinoa.
