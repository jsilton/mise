## Chef's Note

This tuna-and-cream-cheese variation keeps its Philly name and its nori-outside wrapping. Cold cheese strips provide a defined creamy center beside cucumber; they should not be spread across the rice. Sesame goes onto the rice before rolling so it stays with the roll instead of falling off dry nori.

## Before buying raw tuna

Tell the supplier it will be eaten raw and confirm species, handling and applicable parasite controls. “Sushi-grade” alone does not establish suitability. Yellowfin has a specific FDA parasite-freezing exemption, but other raw-seafood hazards remain. Do not rely on domestic freezing, vinegar or appearance to establish raw safety. Thaw frozen tuna in the refrigerator as directed, removing vacuum packaging before refrigerator thawing.

FDA advises children, pregnant people, older adults and people with weakened immune systems to avoid raw seafood. Choose a fully cooked preparation for those diners. This roll contains fish, milk and sesame.

## Directions

1. **Prepare rice and the station:** Follow [Sushi Rice (Shari)](/mise/recipes/master-sushi-rice), weigh 320 g for this recipe and refrigerate surplus rice promptly. Let the rolling portion become barely warm during final preparation. Wrap a clean mat in food-safe plastic film. Mix the hand-dipping water and vinegar; it reduces sticking and is not a sanitizer. Do not dip a raw-filling-coated hand or blade into this clean water. Wash hands and clean the blade before returning to it, use a separate damp cloth for wiping the cutting blade, and replace the water if contaminated.
2. **Prepare cheese and cucumber:** During the rice process, cut cold cream cheese into strips about 6 mm thick. Wash the cucumber, remove its watery core and weigh 100 g, then cut into slender matchsticks and pat dry. Divide cheese into four equal portions of about 29 g and cucumber into four 25 g portions. Refrigerate, keeping the cheese firm.
3. **Prepare fish near rolling:** With separate clean equipment, remove tough visible connective tissue, weigh the listed usable amount of tuna and cut across the muscle grain where visible into strips about 6–10 mm thick. Shorter strips can be laid end to end. Divide into four equal portions of about 58 g and refrigerate. Clean the work area and hands before handling the rolling equipment.
4. **Portion rice and nori:** Divide the rice into four 80 g portions. Trim the first nori sheet to about 19 x 15 cm, saving its dry offcut airtight for another use. Keep the other sheets untrimmed until the first closure check. Place one rectangle shiny side down with the 19 cm edge nearest you. Lightly dampen fingers, shake off excess water and spread one rice portion evenly, leaving a 1 cm bare strip at the far edge. Scatter one quarter of the sesame seeds onto the rice.
5. **Fill and roll:** Take out one portion each of tuna, cheese and cucumber. Using clean utensils, arrange them in an even horizontal line about 4 cm from the near edge; keep their ends aligned and avoid a mound in the middle. Lift the mat's near edge and tuck nori and rice over the filling. Pull the mat out of the way while rolling forward so it stays outside. Close on the bare edge, barely moistening it if needed, and press gently through the mat. Check the first roll's closure, then trim the remaining sheets to match and proceed with the other three; no rice ridge is needed.
6. **Cut and serve:** Place seam-side down briefly while preparing the knife. Wipe and lightly dampen a sharp blade. Cut each roll in half, then halve each section twice, producing eight pieces per roll; wipe and dampen the blade as needed between cuts. Serve promptly as 32 pieces, about two main portions or four appetizer portions. Keep unused raw fish and cheese portions refrigerated during the work.
