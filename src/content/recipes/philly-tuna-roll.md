## Chef's Note

The Philadelphia roll is an American sushi bar classic, named for the cream cheese inside. This one trades the usual salmon for raw tuna, with cucumber for crunch. Cut the cream cheese cold into firm strips so every bite has a clean pocket of cream instead of a smear.

## Directions

1. **Make the rice:** Make the sushi rice and let it cool until barely warm.
2. **Set up:** While the rice cools, divide the tuna, cream cheese and cucumber into four portions and keep them cold. Wrap a bamboo mat in plastic wrap and set the bowl of vinegar water nearby.
3. **Spread the rice:** Set a nori sheet on the mat, shiny side down, with a long edge toward you. With damp hands, spread a quarter of the rice evenly over it, leaving a 1/2-inch strip bare at the far edge. Sprinkle with a quarter of the sesame seeds.
4. **Fill and roll:** Lay one portion each of tuna, cream cheese and cucumber in an even line about 1 1/2 inches from the near edge. Lift the mat and roll the nori over the filling, tucking it in snugly, then keep rolling to the bare edge. Press gently through the mat to seal. Repeat with the rest of the nori and fillings.
5. **Slice and serve:** Wipe a sharp knife with a damp cloth. Cut each roll in half, then each half into 4 pieces, wiping the blade between cuts. Serve right away.
