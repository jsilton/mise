## Chef's Note

Blend the greens with coconut water and orange before adding the frozen fruit. The listed whole-lime main is tart; the separate half-lime version starts gentler and allows more juice to taste.

## Directions

1. Wash the fresh spinach and citrus before cutting, unless the spinach package is already washed and ready to eat. Peel the orange, remove seeds and halve it. Follow any preparation instructions on the frozen fruit packages.

2. Load all the listed spinach, coconut water and orange according to the blender's instructions and working fill limit. Begin checking around 30 seconds and blend until the greens are finely broken down.

3. Add all the listed frozen mango, frozen pineapple, lime juice and sea salt. Blend, beginning to check around 60 seconds, until smooth. Use proportional batches if the actual appliance cannot accommodate the full load.

4. If too thick, add the separate extra coconut-water allowance a little at a time and blend again. Taste and add separate additional lime juice only if wanted.

5. Divide between the listed smoothie portions. Add slices from the separate optional garnish lime allowance and serve immediately.
