## Chef's Note

A tropical green smoothie with mango, pineapple, orange and a whole lime for a margarita-like tartness. Blend the spinach with the liquid first so no leafy bits remain, and don't skip the pinch of salt, which makes the fruit taste brighter.

## Directions

1. **Blend the greens:** Blend the spinach, coconut water and orange on high until completely smooth, about 30 seconds.
2. **Add the fruit:** Add the frozen mango, pineapple, lime juice and salt. Blend on high until thick and velvety, about 1 minute, adding a splash more coconut water if the blender stalls.
3. **Serve:** Pour into glasses, garnish with lime slices and serve right away.
