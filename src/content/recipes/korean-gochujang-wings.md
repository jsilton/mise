## Chef's Note

These are oven wings with a sticky Korean glaze of gochujang, honey, soy and rice vinegar. A night uncovered in the fridge with baking powder dries the skin so it roasts up blistered and crisp, close to fried. Toss the wings in the glaze just before serving so the skin stays crunchy.

## Directions

1. **Season the wings:** Pat the wings very dry. In a large bowl, toss them with the baking powder and salt until evenly coated, with no clumps of powder.
2. **Dry in the fridge:** Lightly oil two wire racks and set them over rimmed sheet pans. Spread the wings on the racks in a single layer with space between them. Refrigerate uncovered for 8 to 24 hours.
3. **Roast:** Heat the oven to 425°F with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Roast the wings for 25 minutes, then flip them and swap the pans. Roast until deep golden and crisp, 20 to 30 minutes more.
4. **Make the glaze:** While the wings roast, put the gochujang, honey, soy sauce, vinegar, water, garlic and ginger in a small saucepan. Simmer over medium-low heat, stirring, until the garlic smells mellow and the glaze coats a spoon, 3 to 4 minutes. Take it off the heat and stir in the sesame oil.
5. **Toss and serve:** Put the hot wings in a large bowl and toss with about two-thirds of the glaze, adding more for a thin, even coat. Top with the scallions, sesame seeds and gochugaru. Serve the rest of the glaze on the side.
