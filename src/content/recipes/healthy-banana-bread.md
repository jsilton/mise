## Chef's Note

Whole-wheat flour, measured mashed banana and olive oil make a moist loaf sweetened with honey or maple syrup. Whisk the wet ingredients first, then fold the flour briefly and cool the loaf completely before slicing. Chocolate chips or walnuts are optional additions.

## Directions

1. **Preheat and grease the pan:** Preheat the oven to 325°F. Grease a 9-by-5-inch loaf pan for the original batch with butter or neutral oil. Measure the mashed banana before mixing.
2. **Whisk the wet ingredients:** In a large bowl whisk the extra-virgin olive oil, honey or maple syrup, large eggs, ripe banana, and milk of choice, combining the oil and honey or syrup first, beating in the eggs, then whisking in the banana and milk. Whisk until evenly combined; small banana lumps may remain.
3. **Distribute the soda and seasonings:** Whisk in the baking soda, vanilla extract, sea salt, and ground cinnamon until evenly distributed; break up any baking-soda lumps.
4. **Fold in the flour:** Switch to a large spoon or spatula and fold in the white whole-wheat flour until no dry pockets remain. Stop once combined; small banana lumps are fine.
5. **Add the optional mix-ins:** Gently fold in the dark chocolate chips or walnuts (if using).
6. **Fill and finish the loaf:** Scrape the batter into the prepared pan and level it gently. Sprinkle with ground cinnamon (if using); run a knife tip in a shallow zigzag to swirl the topping.
7. **Bake and cool:** Bake for about 55–60 minutes, checking the center at 55 minutes. A tester through the crumb should have no wet batter; try another spot if it hits melted chocolate. Continue baking if necessary and tent loosely with foil if the top darkens first. Cool in the pan on a rack for at least 10 minutes, loosen the edges and turn out carefully. Cool completely on the rack before slicing; allow about an hour, longer if still warm.
