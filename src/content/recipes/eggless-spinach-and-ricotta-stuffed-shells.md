## Chef's Note

Stuffed shells without eggs or mozzarella, so the filling stays light and tastes of fresh ricotta, lemon and garlic. Pecorino binds it and adds a salty bite. Squeeze the spinach dry so the shells sit in thick sauce, not a watery pool.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven and water:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.
2. **Wilt the spinach:** Dip the spinach into the boiling water just until it wilts, about 30 seconds. Lift it out with a slotted spoon and let it cool. Squeeze it dry and chop it.
3. **Boil the shells:** Cook the shells in the same water until al dente, about 10 minutes. Drain and toss with a drizzle of olive oil so they don't stick.
4. **Mix the filling:** In a bowl, stir together the spinach, ricotta, pecorino, garlic, oregano, lemon zest, red pepper flakes, salt and black pepper.
5. **Fill the shells:** Spread the marinara over the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Spoon the filling into the shells and nestle them, open side up, in the sauce.
6. **Bake:** Cover with foil and bake until the sauce bubbles and the filling is hot in the center, about 20 minutes.
7. **Serve:** Sprinkle with more pecorino and the parsley. Pass extra warm marinara at the table.
