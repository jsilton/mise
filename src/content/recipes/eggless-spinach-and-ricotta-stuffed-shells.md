## Chef's Note

Ricotta and pecorino bind this filling without eggs or mozzarella. Squeeze the steamed spinach dry and drain the ricotta if liquid pools around it so the shells do not sit in watery sauce.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 425°F. For the original batch, have a 9×13-inch oven-safe dish ready. At other scales use enough dishes for similar shell spacing and sauce depth; divide all the marinara and filling among them before assembly.
2. **Steam spinach:** Wash and drain the fresh spinach. Set a steamer over a pot with 1 inch of water and bring to a simmer. Cover and steam the spinach about 1 minute, until wilted. Transfer to a strainer, let cool enough to handle, squeeze dry and chop.
3. **Cook shells:** In a large pot of salted boiling water, cook the listed shell count about 10 minutes or according to its package, until al dente. Drain and drizzle with the listed olive-oil allowance to prevent sticking.
4. **Mix filling:** Combine all the spinach, ricotta, measured pecorino, garlic, oregano, lemon zest, red pepper flakes, measured salt and black pepper in a bowl. Wash the lemon before zesting.
5. **Fill and bake:** Spread all the measured marinara among the prepared dishes. Divide all the filling among the cooked shells and arrange over the sauce. Cover with foil and bake about 20 minutes as a first check. Continue until the filling centers reach 165°F /74°C, checking each dish and allowing extra time for chilled shells.
6. **Serve:** Sprinkle with the additional pecorino and chopped parsley as desired. Serve with the additional marinara on the side.

## Cooking Notes

Assembled shells can be covered and frozen. Thaw completely in the refrigerator before baking, allow extra oven time for a chilled dish and check that the filling centers reach 165°F /74°C. Follow the baking dish manufacturer's instructions for temperature changes. The source’s roughly 10-hour thaw and 30-minute chilled bake are planning references, not guaranteed thawing or doneness clocks.
