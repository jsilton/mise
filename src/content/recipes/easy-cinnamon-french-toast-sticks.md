## Chef's Note

French toast cut into sticks for dipping, a weekend favorite with kids. Thick, day-old Texas toast holds up to a quick dip in honey and cinnamon custard, and foaming butter crisps all four sides. Dip fast so the centers stay tender, not soggy.

## Directions

1. **Mix the custard:** In a wide, shallow bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, cinnamon, honey, vanilla and salt until no streaks of egg remain.
2. **Heat the pan:** Melt a little of the butter in a large skillet over medium heat until it foams.
3. **Dip the sticks:** Working with as many sticks as fit the pan in one layer, roll each in the custard to coat all sides and lift it out right away. Let the extra drip back into the bowl.
4. **Cook in batches:** Lay the sticks in the pan and cook, turning, until all four sides are deep golden and crisp and the centers feel set when pressed, about 2 minutes per side. Repeat with the rest, adding butter for each batch.
5. **Serve:** Serve hot with warm maple syrup for dipping and berries on the side.
