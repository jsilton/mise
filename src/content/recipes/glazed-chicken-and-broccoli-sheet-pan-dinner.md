## Chef's Note

The chicken and potatoes get a head start before the broccoli and ginger-teriyaki glaze go onto the pan. Leave enough room for the broccoli to spread out so it roasts at the edges instead of sitting in a crowded mound. A thin layer of glaze gives flavor without leaving a large sugary puddle to scorch.

## Directions

1. **Start chicken and potatoes:** Preheat the oven to 450°F. Line a large rimmed sheet pan with foil, or parchment rated by its manufacturer for this temperature. Use skin-on chicken drumsticks, fingerling potatoes, 1/3 of the extra-virgin olive oil, salt, and black pepper: pat the drumsticks dry with paper towels and space them on one side. Put potatoes on the other side, leaving room in the center for broccoli. Toss potatoes with their one-third share of the oil, salt and pepper. Roast about 20 minutes, until potatoes begin to soften and chicken begins to brown.
2. **Add broccoli:** Use 2/3 of the extra-virgin olive oil, broccoli head, salt, and black pepper: add broccoli to the center space, tossing it with the remaining two-thirds of the oil, salt and pepper. Spread into one layer. If the vegetables crowd the chicken or pile up, use a second rimmed pan rather than compressing everything.
3. **Brush on glaze:** Mix teriyaki glaze and fresh ginger in a small bowl and brush a thin, even layer over the chicken. Keep this brush and bowl separate from serving utensils. Discard any glaze left in the bowl after the brush touches partly cooked chicken.
4. **Finish roasting:** Return to the 450°F oven and roast about 20–25 minutes more. The potatoes should be tender and the broccoli browned in spots; check the thickest part of every drumstick, away from bone, for 165°F / 74°C. Continue cooking as needed. If the glaze darkens too quickly, loosely shield the chicken with foil while it reaches its endpoint. If vegetables sharing the chicken pan finish first, check that they have reached 165°F / 74°C after contact with chicken juices before transferring them with clean utensils to a clean dish. Vegetables cooked on a separate pan without chicken contact can be removed when tender. Continue cooking the chicken to its endpoint.
5. **Rest and serve:** Rest the safely cooked chicken for 5 minutes, then divide chicken and vegetables among the portions. Leave behind any scorched glaze on the pan rather than scraping it over the potatoes.
