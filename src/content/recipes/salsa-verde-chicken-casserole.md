## Chef's Note

Janet McCormick's Taste of Home casserole layers chicken, corn tortillas and Monterey Jack like a lasagna, bound in a tangy sauce of salsa verde and sour cream. Halving the tortillas makes neat, even layers. Cold chopped tomato and avocado on top keep every bite fresh.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 375°F and grease an 8-inch square baking dish.
2. **Mix the filling:** In a medium bowl, whisk the sour cream with two-thirds of the salsa verde. Fold in the chicken and cilantro.
3. **Layer:** Spread the rest of the salsa over the bottom of the dish and cover with half the tortilla halves. Top with half the chicken mixture and half the cheese. Repeat with the remaining tortillas, chicken mixture and cheese.
4. **Bake:** Bake uncovered until the cheese is bubbling and golden at the edges and the center is hot, 25 to 30 minutes. Let it rest for 5 minutes.
5. **Top and serve:** Scatter the tomatoes, avocado and green onions over the top and serve.
