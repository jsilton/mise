## Chef's Note

Janet McCormick’s Taste of Home recipe layers cooked chicken, tortillas and Monterey Jack in a creamy tomatillo sauce. This version keeps the tomatoes and full avocado fresh for contrast; a complete baked-tomato route follows. Even layers help the casserole heat evenly, and the center temperature determines when it is ready.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat to 375°F. For the original six-serving batch, lightly grease an oven-safe 8-inch-square dish with headroom above the full layers. For other amounts, choose suitable dishes and distribute the full supplies with room above the layers; if a load would reach a rim, move it to suitable dishes before baking. Halve all the listed tortillas; the original eight make sixteen halves. Pan dimensions and baking time do not multiply.
2. **Mix the filling:** Combine all the sour cream with two-thirds of the listed salsa verde, then fold in all chicken and minced cilantro. Keep the remaining one-third salsa separate for the dish bottoms.
3. **Make two layers:** Divide the reserved bottom salsa among the dishes. In each dish, arrange half its tortilla halves over the salsa, overlapping as needed, then add half its chicken mixture and half its cheese. Repeat with its remaining tortillas, chicken mixture and cheese. Distribute the full listed supplies across the dishes without leaving an extra reserve.
4. **Bake:** Bake uncovered, beginning checks around 25–30 minutes. Continue until a thermometer in the center and other thick areas reads at least 165°F / 74°C. If the cheese browns first, tent with foil and continue; bubbling cheese alone is insufficient.
5. **Finish and serve:** Top with all the listed chopped tomatoes and sliced avocado. Add optional sliced green onions or extra cilantro leaves. Serve hot with [Cilantro Lime Rice](/mise/recipes/cilantro-lime-rice).

### Baked-tomato route

Use this complete method in place of the directions above, with the same full chicken, sour cream, salsa, tortilla, tomato, minced-cilantro and cheese quantities. Leave the tortillas whole for this route. Preheat to 400°F. For the original six-serving batch, lightly grease an oven-safe 8-inch-square dish with headroom; for other amounts use suitable dishes and distribute the full load with room above the layers. Mix all chicken and sour cream with half the listed salsa; set aside one-sixth of the salsa for the dish bottoms and reserve the remaining one-third for serving. Divide the bottom salsa among the dishes. In each dish, make a first layer with half its whole tortillas, half its chicken mixture, half its tomatoes, all its share of minced cilantro and half its cheese. Repeat with the remaining tortillas, chicken mixture, tomatoes and cheese. Use all supplies across the dishes. Bake uncovered, beginning checks around 20–25 minutes for the original batch and continuing until each center and other thick areas reach 165°F / 74°C; changed geometry can alter the time. Serve all the reserved salsa with all the listed sliced avocado. For the avocado-free garnish option, omit avocado and use optional sliced green onions or extra cilantro leaves. Pan dimensions and baking time do not multiply.
