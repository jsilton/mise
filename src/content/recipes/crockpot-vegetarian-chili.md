## Chef's Note

Three cooked beans provide different textures while small sweet-potato pieces soften in the tomato base. If more body is needed, mash a small portion near the end rather than assuming all the potato should dissolve. The vegetables supply sweetness, so finish with cider vinegar only after tasting the cooked pot. This is a six-serving Tex-Mex-inspired vegetable chili.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the cooker:** Put diced onion, celery, carrot, peppers and sweet potato in a 6-quart slow cooker. Add all drained beans, green chiles and juices, tomatoes and juices, and tomato sauce.
2. **Bloom measured spices:** Warm olive oil in a small skillet over medium-low heat. Add chili blend, cumin, garlic powder and oregano and stir about 20–30 seconds until fragrant, without darkening. Add a few tablespoons from the measured broth to loosen the paste, then scrape it into the cooker.
3. **Add liquid:** Add the rest of the initial 240 ml broth and the pepper. Stir thoroughly, cover and start cooking immediately. Keep salt, vinegar and fresh toppings for later.
4. **Cook and test vegetables:** Cook on HIGH about 4–5 hours or LOW about 7–8 hours, until carrot and sweet potato are fork-tender. Keep the lid closed between checks. Extend if needed; add a little extra broth only if the mixture becomes too dry before the vegetables soften.
5. **Set body and season:** If thin, mash roughly half a cup of cooked beans and sweet potato against the side and stir in. Taste and add measured salt gradually only if needed. Turn off heat and start with half the vinegar, then add more to taste.
6. **Serve:** Divide into six bowls. Offer the listed yogurt, avocado and cilantro as optional supplies for the whole batch; use plain plant-based yogurt or omit it for a vegan version. Bread or slaw is prepared separately.
