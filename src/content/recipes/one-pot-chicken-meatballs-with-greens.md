## Chef's Note

Yogurt and panko bind these Italian-American-style garlic-and-oregano chicken meatballs. Brown them gently, then cover them with chard and lemon slices to finish cooking in the greens’ moisture. Spoon the cooking juices over the meatballs and greens, and keep the lemon half for the finish.

## Directions

1. **Mix gently:** Combine all ground chicken, plain Greek yogurt, panko breadcrumbs, garlic cloves, scallions, dried oregano, red pepper flakes, and salt in a bowl, mixing just until evenly combined. The oregano and red pepper belong to this mixture; reserve cumin for the greens. Keep the raw chicken mixture cold if preparation pauses and do not taste it raw.
2. **Shape meatballs:** Use olive oil to lightly coat your palms, refreshing the coating as needed, and form all the mixture into uniform balls about 2 inches across. The original batch plans about 14 meatballs; at other batch sizes keep the size similar and use the entire mixture rather than holding the count fixed.
3. **Brown meatballs:** Use a heavy pot with a tight lid and a broad base; the original route uses a 12-inch Dutch oven. Heat over medium, then use additional olive oil and 1/2 of the extra-virgin olive oil, starting with half of the measured oil supply and adding the separate unmeasured browning allowance only if needed. Add the meatballs without crowding and turn gently when they release, about 7 minutes for initial browning. This is not their final temperature check. Use additional cooking vessels when the complete batch and greens cannot fit; divide the whole measured formula proportionally.
4. **Dress greens:** Meanwhile, in a clean bowl toss all Swiss chard, 1/2 of the lemon, 1/2 of the extra-virgin olive oil, ground cumin, and salt, using the remaining half of the measured oil and half the measured lemons as thin slices. Scrunch the leaves to coat; reserve the other lemon half for serving.
5. **Cover with greens:** Pile all the dressed greens and lemon slices directly over all the browned meatballs. Cover tightly and cook over medium for about 12–15 minutes as an initial estimate. The covered greens release moisture to finish cooking the meatballs.
6. **Check centers:** Continue covered cooking until meatball centers reach 165°F / 74°C and the greens and tender stalks have softened. Check large meatballs and different pot positions with a thermometer; browning alone does not establish the endpoint. Extra loads or a deeper mound of greens can take longer.
7. **Scoop and finish:** Scoop the meatballs and greens onto plates, spooning all the cooking juices over them. Use all 1/2 of the lemon for the finish: squeeze the reserved lemon juice over the portions. Use clean serving utensils.
