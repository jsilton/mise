## Chef's Note

Thin carrot coins get a head start before the kale. Add the leaves in manageable handfuls, then use the measured water or broth for a short covered steam. The lemon and optional sesame-oil finish go in off heat; different hearty greens can need different cooking times.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Wash kale and carrots under running water. Remove the tough kale stems and chop leaves into roughly 2-inch pieces. Slice the carrots into thin coins. About 8 cups kale and about 2 tbsp juice from half a lemon describe the original batch, not exact scaled equivalents.

2. **Cook carrots:** Heat all the measured olive or vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add all carrots and sauté about 3–4 minutes, tossing until starting to soften with some color on the edges. Use enough vessels for the full batch, dividing all the cooking oil and later ingredients among them.

3. **Add garlic:** Add all minced garlic and cook about 30 seconds until fragrant, reducing heat if it browns quickly.

4. **Wilt:** Add kale in handfuls, tossing each until it settles enough for the next. Do not pile more than the vessel can toss and cover.

5. **Steam:** Add all the measured water or broth, cover and steam about 2–3 minutes, then check a leaf and carrot. Continue as needed until tender to your liking; a bright green color alone is not a tenderness test.

6. **Finish:** Uncover and toss about 30–60 seconds to evaporate excess liquid without drying the pan. Remove from heat. Add juice from all the listed lemon, all measured salt and black pepper, and optional red-pepper flakes. Add the optional measured sesame oil now if wanted. Toss through.

7. **Serve:** Taste and use the listed additional-salt allowance if needed. Additional lemon juice or pepper is optional seasoning beyond the measured base; serve warm.

## Variations

**Mediterranean style:** At the original batch, add 1/4 cup toasted pine nuts or slivered almonds and 2 tbsp golden raisins in step 6; scale these additions proportionally and use all in the finish.

**Sesame-soy variation:** Replace the full listed cooking-oil amount with sesame oil in step 2; keep the separate listed optional finishing sesame-oil amount if wanted. Use heat suitable for the actual oil and reduce it if the oil smokes. At the original batch, add 1 tbsp soy sauce in step 6 and sesame seeds as an unmeasured garnish; scale the soy sauce proportionally. Keep all other base ingredients. Soy sauce adds salt, so taste before any additional salt; use a gluten-free soy sauce if required.

**Spicy variation:** Use twice the listed red-pepper flakes, or add 1 tsp sriracha in step 6 at the original batch, scaled proportionally. These are separate options, not an instruction to add both.
