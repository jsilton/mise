## Chef's Note

This cheesecake folds finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano into a classic sweet cream cheese filling, with more in the graham cracker crust. The aged cheese adds a salty, nutty depth, a little like a cheese course and dessert at once. Bake it gently in a water bath and mix slowly once the eggs go in, so it stays smooth and creamy.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the pan:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Line the bottom of a 10-inch springform pan with parchment and butter the sides.
2. **Make the crust:** Pulse the graham crackers and sunflower seeds to fine crumbs in a food processor. Add the lemon zest, cinnamon, sugar and Parmigiano and pulse to combine. Pulse in the melted butter until a squeezed handful holds together.
3. **Bake the crust:** Press the crumbs over the bottom and up the sides of the pan, about 1/2 inch thick, stopping 1/2 inch below the rim. Bake until lightly golden, 15 to 18 minutes, then let it cool. Lower the oven to 325°F.
4. **Beat the filling:** Beat the cream cheese with an electric mixer until smooth. On low speed, mix in the sugar, then the sour cream, vanilla and Parmigiano.
5. **Add the eggs:** Add the eggs one at a time on low speed, mixing only until each disappears. Scrape the bowl as you go.
6. **Set up the water bath:** Wrap the outside of the pan in two layers of heavy-duty foil. Pour the filling into the crust. Set the pan in a roasting pan and pour hot water into the roasting pan until it comes halfway up the sides of the springform.
7. **Bake the cheesecake:** Bake at 325°F until the edges are set and the center still jiggles slightly, 85 to 90 minutes.
8. **Cool in the oven:** Turn off the oven, prop the door open and leave the cake inside for 1 hour. Lift it out of the water bath and remove the foil.
9. **Chill:** Refrigerate for at least 6 hours, until cold and firm all the way through.
