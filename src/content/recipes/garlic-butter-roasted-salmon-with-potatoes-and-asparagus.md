## Chef's Note

This EatingWell sheet-pan dinner puts salmon, potatoes and asparagus on one tray. The potatoes get a head start so they brown and turn tender, and the fish and asparagus go in for the last few minutes. A spoonful of lemon-garlic butter over the top is all the sauce it needs.

## Directions

1. **Roast the potatoes:** Heat the oven to 400°F. On a large rimmed sheet pan, toss the potatoes with half the olive oil and some of the salt and pepper. Turn them cut side down and roast until a knife goes in with slight resistance and the cut sides are browning, 20 to 25 minutes.
2. **Prep the rest:** While the potatoes roast, toss the asparagus with the rest of the oil and a pinch of salt and pepper. Stir the melted butter, lemon juice and garlic together in a small bowl. Pat the salmon dry and season it with the rest of the salt and pepper.
3. **Arrange the pan:** Push the potatoes to one side of the pan. Lay the salmon in the center and the asparagus on the other side in a single layer. Spoon the garlic butter over the salmon and asparagus.
4. **Roast together:** Roast until the salmon is just opaque in the center and flakes and the asparagus is tender-crisp, 10 to 12 minutes.
5. **Serve:** Sprinkle with parsley and spoon the pan butter over each plate.
