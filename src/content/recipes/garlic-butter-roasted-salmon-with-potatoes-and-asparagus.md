## Chef's Note

Potatoes need a head start, but their tenderness matters more than a fixed fifteen-minute timer. Add salmon once a knife nearly slides through a potato, then check fish and asparagus separately. The lemon-garlic butter adds richness without requiring a separate sauce.

## Directions

1. **Heat and cut:** Heat the oven to **400°F / 205°C**. Halve baby potatoes; quarter larger ones so the pieces are about **1 inch across**. Pat salmon dry and check for pin bones. Keep it refrigerated while the potatoes start.
2. **Start potatoes:** Toss potatoes with **half the olive oil**, salt and pepper. Spread cut-side down on the rimmed tray. Roast for about **20–25 minutes**, checking at 15, until nearly tender with some browning. If the knife still meets a hard center, continue before adding fish.
3. **Prepare asparagus and butter:** While potatoes roast, toss trimmed asparagus with **the remaining olive oil**, salt and pepper. Stir melted butter, lemon juice and garlic together. Season the salmon lightly with salt and pepper.
4. **Arrange:** Move potatoes to one side without piling them deeply. Put salmon in the center and asparagus alongside, using a second tray if needed to prevent overlap. Spoon garlic butter over salmon and asparagus. For very thin asparagus, add it about 5 minutes after the salmon instead.
5. **Finish independently:** Roast about **10–15 minutes**, checking fish at 10 minutes. Measure the thickest center of each salmon portion from the side for **145°F / 63°C**. Check that potatoes are fully tender and asparagus has the bite you like. Lift each finished component to a clean plate while the rest continues; do not leave fish baking solely for the potatoes.
6. **Serve:** Divide salmon, potatoes and asparagus among the plates. Spoon over pan butter and scatter with parsley. This includes its own starch and green vegetable; an additional salad is optional.
