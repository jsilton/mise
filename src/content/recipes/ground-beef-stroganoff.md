## Chef's Note

This is Craig Plummer's stroganoff, a quick everyday version made with ground beef and a can of golden mushroom soup in place of strips of steak. Dry mustard and a spoonful of lemon juice keep the creamy sauce from tasting flat. Stir the sour cream in off the heat so it stays smooth.

## Directions

1. **Brown the beef:** Put the ground beef, medium onion, and garlic clove in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook, breaking up the beef, until no pink remains and the onion is soft, about 10 minutes.
2. **Season the beef:** Season the beef with salt and black pepper.
3. **Stir in the flour:** Sprinkle the all-purpose flour over the beef and stir for 1 minute.
4. **Simmer the sauce:** Stir in the mushrooms, dry mustard, condensed golden mushroom soup, water, and fresh lemon juice and bring to a simmer. Turn the heat to low, cover and simmer, stirring now and then, until the mushrooms are tender and the sauce is thick, 30 minutes.
5. **Cook the noodles:** While the sauce simmers, cook the wide egg noodles in a large pot of boiling salted water until tender, about 7 minutes. Drain.
6. **Stir in the sour cream:** Take the skillet off the heat and stir in the sour cream until smooth. Warm over low heat without letting it boil, then serve over the noodles.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Craig's card asks "wine?" To try it, pour 1/2 cup dry white wine into the beef after the flour and let it bubble away for 2 minutes before the soup goes in.
- Use 8 oz sliced cremini mushrooms for a meatier sauce.
- Spoon off all but 2 tbsp of the fat after browning if the beef is fattier than 85% lean.
