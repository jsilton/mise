## Chef's Note

A simple roasted side for chicken, pork or the holiday table. The carrots roast plain first, which dries their surface and starts the browning. The honey butter goes on only at the end, so it turns into a lacquered glaze instead of burning.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 425°F and line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment. Split any very thick carrot batons lengthwise so all the pieces are about the same size.
2. **Roast the carrots:** Toss the carrots with the olive oil, kosher salt and pepper and spread them in a single layer. Roast until nearly tender and starting to brown at the edges, about 20 minutes.
3. **Mix the glaze:** While the carrots roast, stir the melted butter, honey and thyme together in a small bowl.
4. **Glaze and finish:** Drizzle the glaze over the carrots and toss to coat. Roast until the glaze bubbles, the edges are deep golden and a knife slides easily into a thick piece, 8 to 10 minutes.
5. **Serve:** Sprinkle with flaky salt and a little more fresh thyme and serve warm.
