## Chef's Note

Roast the carrots with oil first, then coat them with all the honey-butter-thyme glaze. The glaze can darken quickly at 425°F: check it before the end of the timer, and take the pan out once the carrots are tender and the coating bubbles without burning.

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Wash and peel the carrots and cut uniform 3-inch batons, splitting especially thick pieces lengthwise for similar thickness, or use the listed whole baby-carrot option. Line enough rimmed sheets with parchment rated for 425°F or use bare oven-safe sheets.
2. Toss all the carrots with all the olive oil, kosher salt and black pepper. Divide among pans in a single layer, leaving room to turn. Roast about 20 minutes, then check a thick center for near-tenderness and turn as needed; thicker pieces may need longer before glazing.
3. Meanwhile stir all the melted butter, honey and measured fresh or dried thyme together. If using dried thyme, use half the listed fresh-thyme amount.
4. Remove the sheets and divide all the glaze among the carrots, tossing gently. Return to the oven, checking before 8–10 minutes and sooner if the honey darkens quickly. Finish when centers are tender and the glaze bubbles with golden edges; reduce heat or remove a finished pan rather than waiting for blackened honey.
5. Serve warm with the separate flaky-salt allowance and optional extra fresh thyme. The measured thyme has already gone into the glaze.
