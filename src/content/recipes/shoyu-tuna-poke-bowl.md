## Chef's Note

Poke began as a fisherman's snack in Hawaii, cubes of raw fish seasoned with whatever was on hand. The shoyu style dresses ahi in soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger and sweet Maui onion, with a pinch of chile. Dress the tuna just 10 minutes before serving so it tastes seasoned but stays silky.

## Directions

1. **Cook the rice:** Cook the short-grain rice, which takes about an hour with soaking and resting. While it cooks, slice the cucumber and cook and cool the edamame.
2. **Season the rice:** Stir the rice vinegar, rice sugar and salt in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves. Fold it gently into the hot rice with a paddle, then spread the rice in a wide bowl to cool until barely warm, about 10 minutes.
3. **Make the dressing:** While the rice cools, whisk the soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, dressing sugar, Maui onion, scallion and red pepper flakes in a medium bowl.
4. **Dress the tuna:** Fold the cold tuna into the dressing until every cube is coated. Refrigerate for 10 minutes, no longer.
5. **Assemble and serve:** Divide the rice among three bowls. Spoon the tuna over the rice and arrange the cucumber, edamame and avocado around it. Scatter on the sesame seeds and serve right away, with sriracha mayonnaise on the side.
