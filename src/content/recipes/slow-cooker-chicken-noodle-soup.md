## Chef's Note

The slow cooker develops a chicken-and-vegetable broth; the pasta needs a short, separate boil. Combining them only in each bowl avoids the large change in texture and liquid level that happens when cooked pasta sits in hot soup. Fusilli holds a little broth in its twists and is easy to portion into six bowls. Lemon and parsley finish the broth, with enough restraint to leave its chicken flavor clear.

## Directions

1. **Load the cooker:** Place onion, small carrot and celery pieces, garlic, thyme, rosemary and pepper in a 6-quart slow cooker. Add thawed chicken and stock. Cover and start cooking immediately; do not use a delayed start with raw chicken.
2. **Cook and check:** Cook on LOW about 5–6 hours, or HIGH about 3–4 hours, until several thick chicken pieces reach 165°F / 74°C and the carrots are tender. Extend as needed. Keep the lid closed between checks.
3. **Cook pasta near service:** Once the soup is close to ready, bring the pasta water to a boil in a separate pot. Cook fusilli according to its package until just tender, then drain. Cook a proportional amount if serving only part of the soup.
4. **Shred the chicken:** Transfer fully cooked chicken to a clean board, let stand briefly until safe to handle, and shred into bite-sized pieces. Return it to the hot soup and warm through. Avoid leaving it out while doing unrelated preparation.
5. **Balance:** Taste the fully cooked broth. Add measured salt gradually only if needed. Stir in parsley and start with half the lemon juice, adding more to taste.
6. **Assemble bowls:** Divide pasta among six bowls, then add chicken, vegetables and broth. Serve promptly. Keep any remaining pasta separate from the soup for storage.
