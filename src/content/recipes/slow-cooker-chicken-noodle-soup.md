## Chef's Note

Damn Delicious's chicken noodle soup builds its broth slowly in the cooker with chicken thighs, vegetables, thyme and rosemary. The pasta boils on its own and goes into each bowl, so it stays springy instead of soaking up the broth. A squeeze of lemon and fresh parsley at the end brighten the whole pot.

## Directions

1. **Load the cooker:** Put the onion, carrots, celery, garlic, thyme, rosemary, salt and pepper in a 6-quart slow cooker. Lay the chicken on top and pour in the stock.
2. **Slow-cook:** Cover and cook on Low until the chicken is tender and reads 165°F and the carrots are soft, 5 to 6 hours.
3. **Boil the pasta:** When the soup is nearly done, bring a pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the fusilli until just tender, then drain.
4. **Shred the chicken:** Move the chicken to a board and shred it with two forks. Return it to the soup.
5. **Finish the broth:** Stir in the parsley and half the lemon juice. Taste and add more lemon juice and salt until the broth tastes bright and savory.
6. **Serve:** Put some pasta in each bowl and ladle the soup over it.
