## Chef's Note

Lap cheong contributes sweet, savory pork flavor in a small quantity, folded through jasmine rice. Cooking it separately gives you a clear endpoint and avoids guessing how much time remains in an unfamiliar rice-cooker cycle. The chicken and broccoli are a separate skillet preparation. Together they make a complete meal; no additional rice side is needed.

## Directions

1. **Start rice:** Rinse rice in several changes of cool water and drain well. Combine with the measured rice water in a saucepan with a tight lid. Bring to a simmer over medium-high heat, stir once, cover, and reduce to low. Cook about 12–15 minutes until the water is absorbed. Turn off heat and rest covered for 10 minutes, then check a grain is tender. Follow the troubleshooting note if it needs more water or time.
2. **Cook sausage separately:** Meanwhile, place whole lap cheong links in a small lidded skillet with the measured sausage water. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook gently, turning occasionally, about 15–20 minutes or longer if the package requires it. Keep a little water in the pan. Verify the sausage centers reach 160°F / 71°C by probing from an end toward the center. Once cooked, transfer to a clean board, slice into thin coins, and fold into the finished rice. Keep covered briefly until the chicken is ready.
3. **Prepare sauce and slurry:** Mix soy, oyster sauce, and stock in a bowl. Keep the wine separate. In another small bowl, mix cornstarch with its cold water. Cut chicken and vegetables, using separate clean utensils and surfaces for ready-to-eat components.
4. **Steam broccoli:** Add broccoli and its measured steaming water to a large deep skillet over medium-high heat. Cover for 2–3 minutes until almost tender through the stems, extending as needed for larger pieces. Remove broccoli, drain excess water, and dry the pan carefully.
5. **Brown chicken and onions:** Season chicken with pepper. Heat 2 tsp oil over medium-high heat and cook half the chicken for about 4–5 minutes. Transfer to a plate and repeat with another 2 tsp oil and the remaining chicken. Add the final 2 tsp oil and onions; cook 4–5 minutes until softened. Add garlic and stir 30 seconds. Take the pan briefly off heat to add wine, then return to heat and simmer 1–2 minutes, scraping up golden residue.
6. **Finish chicken:** Add stock-sauce mixture, chicken, and plate juices. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook about 5–8 minutes, stirring, until several of the largest chicken pieces reach 165°F / 74°C. Continue as needed before adding broccoli.
7. **Glaze:** Return broccoli. Re-stir slurry and add about half while stirring the simmering sauce. Simmer for 1 minute, then add more slurry only if needed for a light coating, simmering again after each addition. Taste before deciding whether additional seasoning is needed; soy, oyster sauce, and sausage already supply salt.
8. **Serve:** Divide the sausage rice among six bowls and top with chicken, broccoli, and sauce. Serve promptly. The wine is an ingredient in the sauce; a short simmer does not make it reliably alcohol-free.
