## Chef's Note

This is the Gwock family's Chinese chicken, thighs and broccoli in a glossy gravy of soy, oyster sauce and sherry, spooned over white rice. Lap cheong, the sweet Cantonese pork sausage, steams right on top of the rice and comes to the table on the side. Stir-fry the broccoli first in a very hot wok so it stays bright green.

## Directions

1. **Start the rice:** Cook the rice in a rice cooker with water to the cooker's line.
2. **Steam the sausage:** After the rice has cooked for 20 minutes, wrap the sausage in foil, set it on top of the rice and close the lid. Let it steam until the rice is done.
3. **Stir-fry the broccoli:** Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat until a sprinkle of water sizzles. Add the broccoli, season with salt and stir-fry until bright green throughout and mostly cooked, about 3 minutes. Move it to a plate.
4. **Cook the aromatics:** Heat the rest of the oil in the wok over high heat. Add the onions and garlic and stir-fry for 1 minute.
5. **Sear the chicken:** Add the chicken and season with salt and pepper. Stir-fry over high heat until the outside is no longer pink, 3 to 4 minutes.
6. **Build the sauce:** Pour in the sherry and simmer for 1 minute. Add half the water and bring to a boil. Add the rest of the water, the soy sauce and the oyster sauce and return to a boil.
7. **Thicken the sauce:** Taste and adjust the seasoning. Stir the cornstarch mixture and add it a little at a time until the sauce thickens.
8. **Simmer with the broccoli:** Stir the broccoli back in and simmer until the chicken is cooked through, 5 minutes.
9. **Slice the sausage:** Take the sausage off the rice and cut it into 1/4-inch slices. Lay them on a small plate and drizzle with soy sauce.
10. **Serve:** Serve the chicken over the rice, with the sausage on the side.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Make it a lighter meal for six. Rinse 2 cups jasmine rice and cook it in a covered saucepan with 2 1/2 cups water on low for 12 to 15 minutes, then let it rest, covered, for 10 minutes.
- Skip the foil. Lay 3 oz lap cheong on the rice as soon as the pot is covered, so its fat drips into the grains as it steams. When the links reach 160°F, slice them and fold them into the rice.
- Steam 1 1/4 lb broccoli florets with 1/3 cup water in a covered skillet for 2 to 3 minutes instead of stir-frying them. Add them back only for the last minute so they stay crisp.
- Use 2 tbsp neutral oil and brown the chicken in two batches before you cook the onions.
- Make a lighter sauce with 1/4 cup sherry, 3 tbsp light soy sauce, 2 tbsp oyster sauce and 1 cup unsalted chicken stock in place of the water. Simmer the chicken in it until it reaches 165°F, then thicken it with 2 tbsp cornstarch stirred into 2 tbsp cold water, adding it a little at a time until the sauce coats lightly.
