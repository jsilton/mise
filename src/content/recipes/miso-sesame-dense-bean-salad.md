## Chef's Note

This is a dense bean salad, packed with chickpeas and edamame and held together by crunchy cabbage and carrots. The dressing is a rich, savory mix of miso, tahini and sesame, sharpened with lime and rice vinegar. It holds up for days, so make it once for several lunches.

## Directions

1. **Cook the edamame:** Boil the edamame in salted water until tender, 4 to 5 minutes. Drain and rinse under cold water until cool, then drain well.
2. **Combine the salad:** Put the chickpeas, edamame, snap peas, cabbage, carrots and green onions in a large bowl.
3. **Whisk the dressing:** Whisk the miso, tamari, rice vinegar, sesame seeds, lime juice, sesame oil, tahini, honey and chili crisp in a small bowl until smooth, with no lumps of miso.
4. **Toss and serve:** Pour the dressing over the salad and fold until everything is coated. Top with the nuts and serve, or chill for 30 minutes first.
