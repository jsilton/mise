## Chef's Note

These bowls borrow from Greek souvlaki, with chicken thighs marinated in lemon, oregano and garlic and seared until charred at the edges. Hummus goes on the bottom so it stays cool and creamy under the warm grain. A big pile of pickled onions and a squeeze of lemon keep every bite bright.

## Directions

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Toss boneless, skinless chicken thighs, extra-virgin olive oil, fresh lemon juice, dried oregano, garlic cloves, kosher salt, and freshly ground black pepper in a large bowl. Cover and refrigerate for at least 15 minutes and up to overnight.
2. **Sear the chicken:** Heat a large skillet or grill to medium-high. Lift the chicken out of the marinade and cook until charred at the edges and 165°F in the thickest part, 5 to 6 minutes per side. Turn the heat down a little if the outside darkens too fast.
3. **Rest and slice:** Move the chicken to a cutting board and rest for 5 minutes, then slice it into thin strips.
4. **Build the base:** While the chicken rests, divide hummus and cooked yellow rice or quinoa among deep bowls, spreading the hummus on the bottom and spooning the warm grain on top.
5. **Add the toppings:** Arrange the sliced chicken, English cucumber and cherry tomatoes in separate piles over the grain.
6. **Finish and serve:** Top with Greek feta, [Pickled Red Onions](/mise/recipes/pickled-red-onions), and lemon wedges and serve right away.
