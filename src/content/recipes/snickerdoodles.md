## Chef's Note

Mimi's snickerdoodles are the classic cinnamon-sugar cookie, with the gentle tang and soft middle that cream of tartar gives them. Rolling the dough into walnut-size balls makes a big batch of small, soft cookies.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dough:** Beat margarine or butter, sugar, and large eggs in a large bowl until smooth and light.
2. **Add the flour:** Add all-purpose flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, and salt and stir just until no dry flour remains.
3. **Chill:** Cover the bowl and refrigerate until the dough is firm enough to roll, about 30 minutes.
4. **Make the topping:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Mix sugar and ground cinnamon in a shallow bowl.
5. **Shape the cookies:** Roll the dough into walnut-size balls and roll each ball in the cinnamon sugar. Set them 2 inches apart on baking sheets to leave room for spreading.
6. **Bake:** Bake one sheet at a time for 8 minutes, until the edges are set and the centers are still soft.
7. **Cool:** Let the cookies firm up on the sheet for a few minutes, then move them to a rack to cool.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Use unsalted butter rather than margarine for a richer flavor and crisper edges.
- Roll larger balls for about 36 thicker, chewier cookies, and bake them 8 to 10 minutes.
