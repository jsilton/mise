## Chef's Note

This is Sally McKenney's cream cheese frosting, our go-to for carrot cake, red velvet and anything spiced. It is tangy, creamy and not too sweet. Use full-fat block cream cheese, not the tub, and let it and the butter soften fully so they beat smooth before the sugar goes in.

## Directions

1. **Beat the base:** In a large bowl, beat the cream cheese and butter with a stand mixer fitted with the paddle, or a hand mixer, on medium-high speed until smooth with no lumps, about 2 minutes. Scrape the bowl.
2. **Add the sugar:** On low speed, add the confectioners' sugar, vanilla and salt and mix until the sugar disappears, about 30 seconds. Raise the speed to high and beat until creamy and fluffy, about 2 minutes.
3. **Firm it for piping:** For a stiffer frosting, beat in the extra sugar. To pipe, fill the piping bag and chill it for 20 to 30 minutes, then keep to simple shapes.
4. **Frost the cake:** Spread on completely cooled cake or cupcakes. A batch covers 12 to 18 cupcakes or a 9x13-inch cake.
