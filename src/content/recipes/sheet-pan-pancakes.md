## Chef's Note

A rimmed pan replaces individual griddle batches, with strawberry, blueberry, chocolate and cinnamon-sugar regions. Keep the batter depth even, and keep the batter butter and cinnamon-topping butter separate so both reach their intended places.

## Directions

1. **Prepare pan:** Preheat to 425°F. Coat the original 9-by-13-inch rimmed sheet pan with nonstick spray. For larger batches use enough original-size pans, dividing all batter and toppings proportionally. For a smaller batch choose a smaller oven-safe rimmed pan with similar batter depth and adequate headroom, and check the center earlier. Dimensions and baking times do not scale with ingredients.
2. **Mix batter:** For the batter, use all-purpose flour, brown sugar, granulated sugar, baking powder, kosher salt, large eggs, whole milk, unsalted butter, and vanilla. In a large bowl, whisk the flour, both sugars, baking powder and salt. Add the eggs, milk, lukewarm melted butter and vanilla and whisk until combined and smooth. Stop once no dry pockets remain rather than continuing to beat; do not taste raw batter.
3. **Spread:** Pour the batter into the prepared pan or divide it proportionally among the chosen pans. Tap gently on the counter to level it. Imagine four equal topping regions in each pan.
4. **Strawberry region:** Use seedless strawberry jam and fresh strawberries for one region. Dollop small spoonfuls of the jam and swirl gently with a toothpick, then arrange the sliced berries over it. Divide these supplies proportionally among pans if using more than one.
5. **Blueberry region:** Scatter blueberries over a second region, sharing them proportionally among pans.
6. **Chocolate region:** Scatter chocolate chips over a third region, sharing them proportionally among pans.
7. **Cinnamon region:** Whisk unsalted butter, brown sugar, and ground cinnamon together. Dollop small spoonfuls over the fourth region and swirl with a toothpick; share the full mixture proportionally among pans.
8. **Bake:** Bake about 15–18 minutes for the original batch and pan, until the center springs back and a toothpick comes out without wet batter. Check both a plain area and beneath fruit rather than mistaking a melted chocolate chip for raw batter. If baking several pans, check each; a changed load or depth can alter the time.
9. **Slice and serve:** Cool for about a minute, then slice into portions and serve with maple syrup, butter or powdered sugar.

## Cooking Notes

Cool cooked portions promptly before storing. The refrigerator quality plan is up to 3 days; wrapped portions can be frozen for up to 2 months. Refrigerator-thaw frozen portions before warming. These storage plans do not require leaving a warm pan out until completely cold.
