## Chef's Note

Good pita should be warm and soft enough to fold around a filling, with a few toasted spots. A hot, dry skillet does it in a minute or two per bread. Wrap each one in a towel as it comes out of the pan so its own steam keeps it soft.

## Directions

1. **Heat the skillet:** Heat a dry cast-iron or heavy skillet over medium-high heat until hot, about 3 minutes. Lay out a clean kitchen towel.
2. **Warm the pita:** If a pita feels dry, brush it lightly with water. Lay it in the pan and cook until warm and soft with a few toasted spots, 30 to 60 seconds per side.
3. **Wrap and repeat:** Stack each pita in the towel as it finishes and fold the towel over to keep them warm.
4. **Finish:** Brush the warm pita with olive oil and sprinkle with flaky salt, if using. Serve warm.
