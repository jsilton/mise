## Chef's Note

This recipe blends medium-grain white rice with whole-grain purple rice to create a custom version of Korean Heukmibap. Cooking 100% whole-grain purple rice can result in a dense, heavy texture that competes with the light salmon. By blending them in a 3:1 ratio, you get the best of both worlds: a striking, deep violet color and a soft, fluffy texture with just a hint of nutty chew.

## Directions

1.  **The Rinse:** Combine the medium-grain white rice and whole-grain purple rice in a bowl. Cover with cold water, swirl with your hand, and drain. Repeat 3–4 times until the water runs mostly clear. This removes excess surface starch from the white rice so the grains stay distinct and fluffy instead of gummy.
2.  **Combine:** Transfer the rinsed and drained rice to your rice cooker pot (or a medium saucepan). Add the 2.25 cups water and 0.75 cups chicken stock (this specific ratio perfectly balances the savory depth needed for the salmon without overpowering the rice's natural nuttiness). Stir in the salt and oil or butter if using.
3.  **Rice Cooker Method (Recommended):** Close the lid and set the rice cooker to the standard **White Rice** or **Sushi Rice** setting. Check a few purple grains for tenderness at the end of the cycle; whole-grain products vary and may need longer hydration than the white rice. The stated schedule is a starting estimate for this blend.
4.  **Stovetop Method (Alternative):** If cooking on the stovetop, bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat. Stir once, cover tightly with a lid, and reduce the heat to the lowest simmer setting. Cook for 18–20 minutes, then check a few purple grains. If their centers remain hard, add a little boiling water and continue covered over low heat until tender; this extends the stated schedule.
5.  **The Rest:** Once the cooking cycle finishes, leave the lid closed and let the rice steam undisturbed for 10 minutes. This allows the moisture to redistribute evenly into the core of each grain.
6.  **Fluff and Serve:** Gently fluff the purple rice with a wooden rice paddle or fork, and serve warm as a base for your salmon bowls.

## Variations

### 100% Whole-Grain Aromatic Purple Rice

If you prefer to cook the whole-grain purple rice on its own without blending:

- Use **2 cups whole-grain purple rice** with the liquid amount specified on its package; **4 cups liquid** is a starting reference, not a universal ratio.
- Follow the package and your cooker’s **Brown Rice** or **Whole Grain** instructions. Allow approximately 45–50 minutes or the product’s stated time, then check for tender centers.
