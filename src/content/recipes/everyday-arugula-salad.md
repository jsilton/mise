## Chef's Note

Lemon and peppery arugula provide a lively counterpoint to a breaded cutlet, buttery pasta, or pan sauce. The dressing is deliberately sharp, at two parts oil to one part lemon juice. Start with most of it and judge the coated leaves before adding the rest; leaf size and dryness affect how much is needed.

## Directions

1. **Dry the greens:** Wash arugula if needed and spin or pat dry. Place in a wide mixing bowl. Shave the cheese.
2. **Mix dressing:** Whisk oil, lemon juice, measured salt, and pepper together in a small bowl.
3. **Toss just before eating:** Add about three-quarters of the dressing to the leaves and lift gently with clean hands or salad servers. Add the remaining dressing only if the leaves need it; they should look lightly glossy rather than wet.
4. **Finish:** Add Parmesan and toss once or scatter it over the top. Taste a leaf with cheese before deciding whether it needs any additional salt. Divide among four plates and serve immediately.
