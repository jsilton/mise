## Chef's Note

This is the dipping sauce you find on the table at dim sum: salty, tangy and sharp with fresh ginger. The base is four parts soy, two parts rice vinegar and one part sesame oil, so it scales easily for a crowd. A 10-minute rest lets the ginger and garlic flavor the sauce.

## Directions

1. **Mix the base:** Whisk the soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil and sugar in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves.
2. **Add the aromatics:** Stir in the ginger, garlic, green onion and chili garlic sauce.
3. **Rest and serve:** Let the sauce sit for 10 minutes. Taste, adding a splash more vinegar for tang or a pinch of sugar for balance, then stir and spoon into small dishes.
