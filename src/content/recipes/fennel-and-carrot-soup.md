## Chef's Note

Cook the covered vegetables gently until soft without browning, then simmer until the potato falls apart. The potato gives the purée body. Two-thirds of the butter cook the vegetables; the remaining third browns with the roasted chestnuts. Keep the maple crème fraîche separate until serving.

## Directions

1. **Sweat:** Wash the fennel, carrots, potato and thyme before trimming or peeling. In a large heavy pot, melt two-thirds of the butter over medium heat. Add sliced fennel and onion, chopped carrots, halved potato, thyme and bay leaf. Season with the listed salt and pepper. Use a pot with room for the vegetables, broth and stirring.
2. **Soften:** Reduce heat to medium-low, cover and cook for 45–60 minutes, stirring occasionally and reducing heat if needed. The vegetables should become very soft without browning; the clock is a guide to that texture.
3. **Simmer:** Add all the broth. Bring to a boil, season with salt and pepper as needed, then reduce heat and simmer about 8–10 minutes, until the potato is falling apart. Remove the thyme stems and bay leaf.
4. **Purée:** Take the pot off the heat. Use an immersion blender approved for this load, keeping its head submerged and switching it off before lifting it. For a countertop blender, use only a model approved for hot liquids and follow its temperature, fill, lid and venting instructions; work in batches. If your model requires cold ingredients, cool promptly in shallow containers before blending, then reheat the soup. Never seal hot soup in a personal blending cup. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a clean pot if desired, then taste with the listed salt and pepper.
5. **Brown chestnuts:** In a small saucepan over medium heat, melt the remaining one-third of the butter. Add all the chopped roasted chestnuts and cook, stirring often, for about 4 minutes until the butter smells nutty and the chestnuts toast. Remove from the heat before the butter blackens.
6. **Mix maple cream:** Whisk all the crème fraîche and maple syrup together.
7. **Serve:** Ladle the soup into bowls. Distribute all the maple cream and brown-butter chestnuts over the soup.
