## Chef's Note

The meat version uses the full ground-meat and richer sauce quantities listed here, with lime at the finish. The separate vegetable version uses different sauce proportions and scallions. Have the sauce and basil ready before cooking; add basil at the end, just long enough to wilt.

## Directions

1. **Prepare noodles and vegetables:** Wash the basil, pepper, chilies and lime. Prepare the rice noodles according to their package instructions, checking often for a cooked but still firm and chewy center; a soak alone may not cook every product. Drain well. Mince the garlic and chilies, slice the pepper, and have the basil ready.
2. **Mix sauce:** Whisk together all the fish sauce, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce and brown sugar. Keep the lime separate.
3. **Cook meat:** Heat enough of the oil allowance to coat a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat, staying within the pan's instructions. Add all the ground pork or chicken, break into small pieces and stir until cooked through: 160°F / 71°C for ground pork, or 165°F / 74°C for ground chicken. Brown color or a two-minute crust does not establish doneness. Cook in batches if crowded, transferring cooked meat to a clean plate.
4. **Aromatics:** Add all the garlic, chilies and pepper to the pan and stir until the garlic is fragrant and the pepper begins to soften, starting checks around 30 seconds. Return any separately cooked meat.
5. **Toss:** Add all the drained noodles and prepared sauce. Toss and scrape the pan until hot and evenly coated, reducing heat if the garlic or sauce begins to scorch. There is no need to char the basil or evaporate every drop of sauce.
6. **Wilt and finish:** Fold in all the basil just until wilted. Turn off heat. Squeeze half of the listed lime over the noodles and serve the remaining half as wedges. Serve promptly, with the optional chili crisp separately.

## Vegetable and Scallion Variation

Choose this complete route instead of the meat-and-lime version. For the original batch use 8 oz wide dried rice noodles; 1 tsp brown sugar dissolved in 1 tbsp hot water; 2 tsp light soy sauce; 1 tsp dark soy sauce; 1 tbsp fish sauce; 2 tsp oyster sauce; 2 tbsp neutral oil; 3 garlic cloves, sliced; 3 scallions cut into 3-inch pieces with whites and greens separated; 1–3 optional chopped Thai red chilies; 1/2 bell pepper, thinly sliced; and 2 cups Thai basil leaves. Scale all these amounts with the batch, keeping the scallion cut size fixed. Omit the ground meat, lime and main sauce quantities entirely.

Boil the noodles until cooked but still firm, starting checks around the source's 5-minute plan and following the actual package; drain, rinse cold and drain well. Mix the dissolved sugar with both soy sauces, fish sauce and oyster sauce. Heat all the oil in a suitable wok or skillet without exceeding its rating. Stir-fry the garlic, scallion whites, optional chilies and pepper until fragrant, starting checks around 30 seconds. Add all the noodles and toss until warmed, beginning checks around 1 minute. Add all the sauce and toss until uniformly coated, beginning checks around another minute. Fold in all the basil and scallion greens just until wilted, then serve. For a vegan version of this vegetable route, use vegan fish sauce and vegetarian mushroom-based oyster sauce in the same listed amounts; check their labels. Extra pan batches take longer.
