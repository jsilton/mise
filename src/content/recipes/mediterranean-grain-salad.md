## Chef's Note

Chewy farro carries the full olive-oil dressing, olives and feta, while diced vegetables and fresh herbs keep the salad varied. Pearled farro and whole wheat berries do not share a guaranteed cooking clock. Prepare the grain first, cool it promptly, then fold in feta and herbs close to serving.

## Directions

1. **Cook the farro:** Rinse all the dry pearled farro. Bring all the listed broth and measured kosher salt to a boil in a pot with room for the grain to expand. Add the farro, reduce to a gentle simmer and cover. Begin checking around 25–30 minutes, following the actual product instructions if they differ; the center should be tender with a pleasant chew, without a hard core. Drain excess cooking liquid.

2. **Cool and prepare:** Spread the drained grain in a shallow layer and refrigerate promptly to cool; do not leave a large warm batch on the counter waiting for room temperature. While it cools, wash the cucumber, tomatoes, pepper, onion and herbs under running water, then prepare as listed. Put all diced vegetables and halved olives in a large clean bowl.

3. **Make the dressing:** Whisk all the measured olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, grated garlic and black pepper together. These full quantities belong to the dressing; keep any additional salt and lemon juice separate for the finished salad.

4. **Combine:** Add the cooled grain to the vegetables and olives. Fold in all the dressing until the full salad is coated. Use enough bowl space, or divide all components proportionally among more bowls, rather than crowding and crushing the grains.

5. **Finish:** Just before serving, fold in all the feta, parsley, mint and dill. Taste and use the separate additional salt or lemon juice allowances only if wanted. Serve chilled, or briefly out of refrigeration for immediate service.

6. **Make ahead:** Refrigerate the grain, dressed vegetables and herbs/feta separately if preparing ahead. Combine and add the full herb/feta supplies close to serving. Keep at 40°F or below; count storage from the oldest prepared component.

## Wheat Berry Variation

Replace the listed dry pearled farro with the same listed dry volume of wheat berries. Use the full listed broth and cooking salt; follow the wheat berry product’s stovetop directions and tender-center cue rather than the farro clock. Bob’s Red Mill’s hard-red wheat berry route uses the same original one-cup grain and two-and-a-half-cup liquid starting amounts, covered for about an hour or until tender, then drained. Other products can differ: establish their required liquid before starting rather than assuming this ratio fits every wheat berry. Cool promptly and use the complete same salad, dressing, feta and herb quantities. Allow the longer grain cook and cooling; no identical cooked yield is claimed.
