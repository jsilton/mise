## Chef's Note

Dress the cooked pasta first, then cool it before adding romaine and avocado. The full listed dressing stays in the salad; reserve two-thirds for the cold assembly after one-third coats the warm pasta. Prepared roasted chickpeas supply crunch, with a plain cooked-chickpea option giving a softer texture.

## Directions

1. **Cook the pasta:** Cook all the dry pasta in the separate salted-water allowance according to its package until al dente. Drain thoroughly. This recipe starts with prepared vegan Caesar dressing and prepared roasted chickpeas; making them separately adds its own work and clock.

2. **Dress and cool:** Transfer the pasta to a clean large bowl and fold in one-third of the full listed dressing. The original batch’s half-cup amount is one-third of its one-and-a-half-cup supply; use that same proportion at other scales. Let stand about 5 minutes, then spread in a shallow layer and refrigerate promptly until no longer warm before adding lettuce.

3. **Combine:** Wash and drain the romaine and tomatoes, then chop and halve them as listed. Add all romaine and tomatoes to the cooled pasta and fold in the remaining two-thirds of the dressing. Keep raw-protein equipment away from the ready-to-eat salad.

4. **Finish:** Just before serving, fold in all the prepared chickpeas and sliced avocado. Wash the avocado exterior before cutting with clean utensils. Use a roomy bowl or more bowls with all components divided proportionally to avoid crushing the leaves.

5. **Serve:** Taste and season with the listed sea salt and black pepper allowance. Serve cold or briefly at room temperature for immediate service. Keep any unserved salad refrigerated.

## Source Dressing and Pasta Options

For the source dressing quantity, replace the measured dressing supply with one complete batch of [Ambitious Kitchen’s vegan Caesar dressing](https://www.ambitiouskitchen.com/vegan-caesar-dressing/) per original salad batch, scaling that separate recipe proportionally. This is a different supply from the current one-and-a-half-cup dressing main, not a fixed batch-to-cup equivalence. Toss that whole prepared dressing batch with the drained pasta, then cool promptly and add the complete same lettuce, avocado, tomatoes and chickpeas. Rotini, bowties or another pasta, including a suitable gluten-free pasta, can use the same listed dry weight and their own package cooking directions. The same listed amount of cooked, drained unroasted chickpeas can replace roasted chickpeas for a softer finish. Check the prepared dressing and chickpea product for vegan suitability.
