## Chef's Note

This is a Caesar salad turned into a pasta salad, with a creamy dairy-free dressing and roasted chickpeas standing in for croutons. Toss the warm pasta with some of the dressing first so it soaks up the flavor. Add the romaine and avocado only once the pasta is cool, so they stay crisp and bright.

## Directions

1. **Boil the pasta:** Cook the pasta in a large pot of well-salted boiling water until al dente, by the package time. Drain well.
2. **Dress the warm pasta:** Toss the pasta in a large bowl with a third of the dressing. Let it stand for 5 minutes, then refrigerate until cool, about 15 minutes.
3. **Add the greens:** Add the romaine, tomatoes and the rest of the dressing. Toss gently.
4. **Finish:** Just before serving, fold in the chickpeas and avocado. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper.
