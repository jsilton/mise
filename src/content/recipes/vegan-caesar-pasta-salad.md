## Chef's Note

This is a Caesar salad turned into a pasta salad, with a creamy tahini dressing sharpened with lemon, capers and nutritional yeast. Roasted chickpeas stand in for croutons. Toss the warm pasta with some of the dressing first so it soaks up the flavor. Add the romaine and avocado only once the pasta is cool, so they stay crisp and bright.

## Directions

1. **Roast the chickpeas:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the chickpeas on a rimmed baking sheet with the oil and salt for the chickpeas. Roast, shaking the pan once, until deep golden and crisp, 20 to 25 minutes. Cool them on the pan.
2. **Whisk the dressing:** In a bowl, whisk the tahini, lemon juice, nutritional yeast, mustard, capers, caper brine and garlic with the oil, salt and pepper for the dressing. Whisk in the cold water a little at a time until the dressing is smooth and pourable.
3. **Boil the pasta:** Cook the pasta in a large pot of well-salted boiling water until al dente, by the package time. Drain well.
4. **Dress the warm pasta:** Toss the pasta in a large bowl with a third of the dressing. Let it stand for 5 minutes, then refrigerate until cool, about 15 minutes.
5. **Add the greens:** Add the romaine, tomatoes and the rest of the dressing. Toss gently.
6. **Finish:** Just before serving, fold in the chickpeas and avocado. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper.
