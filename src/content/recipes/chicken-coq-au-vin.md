## Chef's Note

Coq au vin is the Burgundy classic of chicken braised in red wine with bacon, mushrooms and onion. Thighs stay tender through the slow simmer, and browning the mushrooms on their own keeps them meaty instead of soggy. A knob of cold butter swirled in at the end turns the reduced wine into a glossy sauce.

## Directions

1. **Render the lardons:** Pat the chicken dry and season it with the salt and pepper. Cook the lardons in a wide Dutch oven over medium heat, stirring, until they release their fat and start to crisp, 6 to 8 minutes. Move them to a bowl. Leave a thin layer of fat in the pot, adding the oil if there is too little.
2. **Brown the mushrooms:** Turn the heat to medium-high and add the mushrooms. Cook, stirring now and then, until their liquid cooks off and they turn golden, 6 to 8 minutes. Move them to a separate bowl.
3. **Brown the chicken:** Sear the chicken in two batches, skin side down, until the skin is deep golden and releases easily, 6 to 8 minutes. Turn and cook 2 minutes more, then move to a plate. Spoon off all but a thin layer of fat.
4. **Cook the vegetables:** Lower the heat to medium. Add the onion and carrots and cook, stirring, until the onion softens, 6 to 8 minutes. Stir in the garlic, tomato paste and flour and cook, stirring, for 1 minute.
5. **Reduce the wine:** Pour in the wine and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer uncovered over medium-high heat until reduced by half, 10 to 15 minutes.
6. **Braise the chicken:** Return the chicken, its juices and the lardons. Add the stock and thyme. The liquid should come halfway up the thighs. Bring to a simmer, cover and cook at a gentle bubble until the meat is tender near the bone and at least 165°F, 45 to 60 minutes. Add the mushrooms for the last 10 minutes.
7. **Reduce the sauce:** Move the chicken to a platter. Pull out the thyme stems and skim the fat from the surface. Simmer the sauce uncovered until it lightly coats a spoon, 5 to 10 minutes.
8. **Finish and serve:** Turn off the heat, let the bubbling stop and swirl in the cold butter. Taste for salt. Return the chicken to the sauce and scatter with parsley.
