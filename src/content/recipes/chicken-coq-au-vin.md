## Chef's Note

This coq au vin uses modern chicken thighs, bacon, and a red-wine braise. Brown the mushrooms separately so they develop color instead of steaming among the carrots and onions. Gentle cooking then softens the chicken; a reduced sauce and an off-heat butter finish supply body and gloss. Wine remains an ingredient after cooking, so this is not an alcohol-free dish.

## Directions

1. **Prepare and render:** Pat chicken dry and season with the measured salt and pepper. Cook bacon in a wide Dutch oven over medium heat for 6–8 minutes, stirring, until it releases fat and begins to crisp. Transfer bacon to a bowl. Leave about 2 tbsp fat in the pot, adding some of the optional neutral oil if there is too little.
2. **Brown mushrooms:** Add mushrooms and cook over medium-high heat for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until moisture evaporates and golden surfaces develop. Transfer to a separate bowl for the end of the braise.
3. **Brown chicken:** Keep about 2 tbsp fat in the pot. Sear chicken in two batches, skin-side down for 6–8 minutes, then 2 minutes on the other side. Transfer to a plate. Adjust heat if the browned residue darkens too far. Spoon out excess rendered fat, leaving about 2 tbsp.
4. **Cook vegetables:** Lower heat to medium. Add onion and carrots and cook 6–8 minutes, stirring, until onion softens. Add garlic and tomato paste and stir for 1 minute. Scrape away any black burned residue rather than incorporating it into the sauce.
5. **Reduce wine:** Take the pot briefly off the burner to add wine. Return to medium-high heat, scrape up golden browned residue, and simmer uncovered until the wine reduces by roughly half, about 10–15 minutes.
6. **Braise gently:** Return chicken, accumulated plate juices, and bacon. Add stock and thyme. Liquid should come about halfway to two-thirds up the thighs, not cover them; add a little stock only if needed. Bring to a simmer, cover, and lower heat to maintain gentle bubbles. Cook about 45–60 minutes, adding the reserved mushrooms for the last 10 minutes. Check meat away from the bone reaches at least 165°F / 74°C and feels tender near the joint; continue in 10-minute increments if still firm.
7. **Concentrate and finish:** Transfer tender chicken to a clean platter. Remove thyme stems and skim excess surface fat. Simmer the uncovered sauce about 5–10 minutes until it lightly coats a spoon. Turn off heat, let vigorous bubbling stop, and swirl in cold butter. Taste before adding any extra salt.
8. **Serve:** Return chicken to the warm sauce, spoon sauce over it, and finish with parsley. Serve with one starch such as mashed potatoes and a green vegetable.
