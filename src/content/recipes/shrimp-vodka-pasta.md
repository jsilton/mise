## Chef's Note

Cook the broccoli and shrimp separately, then bring them back after the tomato-cream sauce is ready. This gives the broccoli time to soften without overcooking the seafood. Basil goes partly into the sauce and partly over the finished bowls. Adapted from [MyRecipes’ Shrimp Vodka Pasta](https://www.myrecipes.com/recipe/shrimp-vodka-pasta).

## Directions

1. **Prepare and start pasta:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil and salt it. Cut broccoli, mince garlic, chop basil and dry shrimp. Cook rigatoni about 1 minute short of the package al dente time, coordinating it with the sauce. Reserve the listed pasta water before draining.

2. **Cook broccoli:** Heat one-third of the oil over medium heat. Add broccoli and sauté about 5–7 minutes until stems yield with a little resistance. If needed, add a small splash of water and cover briefly to finish the stems; uncover to evaporate free water. Transfer to a plate.

3. **Cook shrimp:** Add another third of the oil and arrange shrimp in a single layer. Cook about 1–2 minutes per side or as needed until firm, pearly and opaque through the center; a fine-tip thermometer inserted into a thick shrimp should read 145°F / 63°C. Transfer to a clean plate.

4. **Start sauce:** Lower heat to medium-low. Add remaining oil and garlic and stir about 30 seconds until fragrant. Take the pan away from the burner, add measured vodka carefully, then return to medium-low heat. Simmer gently for about 2–3 minutes, without deliberately igniting it.

5. **Simmer and combine:** Add marinara, cream, half the basil, black pepper and only as much of the measured salt as needed after tasting the prepared sauce. Simmer gently about 5 minutes. Add pasta and broccoli and toss over low heat until pasta is tender with a little bite, using reserved water in small splashes if necessary.

6. **Finish shrimp:** Fold in shrimp and their juices only long enough to warm through. Turn off heat, scatter with remaining basil and serve promptly. Avoid holding the finished pasta over heat.
