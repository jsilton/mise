## Chef's Note

Tinga is associated with Puebla; this version uses cooked shredded chicken in a chipotle-tomato sauce. Char the tomatoes and garlic on the dry skillet (tatemar) for a smoky edge, soften the onion, then bloom the oregano briefly before adding liquid. The chicken warms in the finished sauce, and the small lime squeeze at serving complements its smoky richness.

## Directions

1. **Char the tomatoes and garlic:** Heat a heavy dry skillet over medium-high heat. Add large tomatoes and garlic cloves, tomatoes cut-side down. Start checking at 5–7 minutes, turning as needed, until the tomatoes soften with charred patches and the garlic softens inside its skin. Remove individual garlic cloves if ready before the tomatoes; avoid burning them while waiting for the tomatoes.
2. **Cool and peel:** Transfer the charred vegetables to a clean plate and let them cool to room temperature before using a countertop blender. Peel the garlic and discard the skins. Blend as soon as the vegetables have cooled; refrigerate them promptly if preparing ahead. Cooling takes additional elapsed time.
3. **Blend the sauce:** Blend chipotles in adobo and adobo sauce with all the cooled charred tomatoes and peeled garlic until smooth. Use the lower end of the listed chipotle range for a milder sauce; the adobo sauce remains its separately measured amount. Keep below the blender's marked working capacity and blend in batches if necessary.
4. **Soften the onion:** Wipe out the skillet. Add small onion and cooking oil and sauté until the onion is translucent and softened.
5. **Bloom the oregano:** Stir in dried oregano and toast for 30 seconds, just until fragrant, before it burns.
6. **Simmer the sauce:** Pour the blended sauce into the onion pan. Stir in chicken broth and salt and simmer for about 5 minutes until slightly thickened, stirring to keep the base from catching.
7. **Heat the cooked chicken:** Fold cooked shredded chicken through all the sauce. Start checking after about 5 minutes and continue, stirring, until the chicken and sauce reach 165°F / 74°C throughout. Check more than one part of the pan, especially thick clusters of chicken.
8. **Finish and serve:** Stir in fresh lime juice and serve the saucy chicken among the planned portions.
