## Chef's Note

A dark, tart syrup of elderberries simmered with ginger, cinnamon and cloves, then sweetened with raw honey. Stir in the honey only once the liquid is lukewarm so it keeps its floral flavor. A spoonful is lovely in hot tea or sparkling water, or drizzled over yogurt.

## Directions

1. **Simmer the berries:** Combine the elderberries, water, ginger, cinnamon and cloves in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer uncovered until the liquid has reduced by about half, 30 to 45 minutes.
2. **Mash:** Take the pan off the heat and crush the berries with a potato masher to release their juice.
3. **Strain:** Pour through a fine-mesh sieve into a heatproof bowl, pressing hard on the solids. Discard the solids.
4. **Add the honey:** Let the liquid cool until just lukewarm, about 20 minutes. Stir in the honey until it dissolves completely.
5. **Bottle:** Pour into a clean jar, seal and refrigerate. Because of the raw honey, don't give it to babies under 1 year old.
