## Chef's Note

Whisk the cream cheese with yogurt before adding milk so large lumps do not remain in the oats. Both the juice and zest from the listed lemon portion are used: juice in the cheese mixture, zest with the strawberries. Fruit can release color and juice as it sits; that is expected rather than a guarantee against bleeding.

## Directions

1. **Whisk:** Whisk plain Greek yogurt, cream cheese, lemon, pure vanilla extract, and maple syrup until the cheese is dispersed without large lumps, using all the listed lemon juice here and reserving all its zest for the fruit step.
2. **Add liquid:** Stir unsweetened milk of choice and chia seeds into the cheese mixture until the chia is distributed.
3. **Add oats:** Stir in old-fashioned rolled oats until no dry pockets remain.
4. **Add fruit:** Fold in fresh strawberries and all the reserved lemon zest.
5. **Refrigerate:** Transfer the entire mixture to a roomy clean container with space to stir. Cover and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
6. **Serve:** Stir again, then add additional fresh strawberry (if using) and freshly ground black pepper (if using) if desired. The original batch makes one large planned portion or two smaller portions; finished volume is unmeasured.

## Cooking Notes

For a separate sweeter or unsweetened version of the original one-large-portion batch, use a heaping ½ cup rolled oats, ⅓ cup Greek yogurt, ⅔ cup milk, 3 tbsp softened cream cheese, ¼ cup chopped strawberries, 1 tbsp chia or ground flax, all juice and zest from ½ lemon, ½ tsp vanilla, a pinch of salt and 0–2 tbsp honey or maple syrup to taste. Omit the main recipe’s fixed maple amount and use only this chosen sweetener amount. Scale this complete set together; keep the same mixing destinations and refrigerated soak.

The full main milk, yogurt and cream-cheese amounts can each be replaced with plant-based versions; use maple and suitable garnishes for a vegan route. Certified gluten-free oats and checked toppings are needed for a gluten-free route. Extra strawberries, strawberry jam or crumbled graham crackers are unmeasured serving options; keep the optional black pepper if desired.
