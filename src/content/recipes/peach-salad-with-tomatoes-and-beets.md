## Chef's Note

This is a summer platter of earthy golden beets, ripe tomatoes and sweet peaches under a lemon, honey and shallot dressing, finished with goat cheese, mint and thyme. Cook the beets in a covered dish so they steam until tender, and let them cool before they meet the fruit.

## Directions

1. **Roast the beets:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Put the beets in a small baking dish with about 1 inch of water. Cover tightly with foil and bake until a knife slides easily into the center, 60 to 75 minutes. Add hot water if the dish runs dry.
2. **Peel and slice the beets:** Lift the beets out and let them cool until you can handle them. Rub off the skins, cut them into 1/4-inch rounds and let them cool completely, about 20 minutes.
3. **Make the dressing:** While the beets cool, whisk the olive oil, half the lemon juice, the honey and the shallot in a small bowl. Season with salt and pepper.
4. **Slice the fruit:** Cut the tomatoes into 1/4-inch rounds. Toss the peaches with the rest of the lemon juice.
5. **Layer the platter:** Overlap the beets and tomatoes on a large platter and season them with salt and pepper. Pile the peaches in the center and spoon the dressing over everything.
6. **Finish and serve:** Scatter the goat cheese, mint and thyme over the top and serve right away.
