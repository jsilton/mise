## Chef's Note

Bake the beets until a knife reaches the center easily, then cool them before layering with the peaches and tomatoes. The covered cooking keeps the beets moist; browning is not the goal. Mint, thyme and goat cheese go on at the end. Adapted from [MyRecipes’ Peach Salad with Tomatoes and Beets](https://www.myrecipes.com/recipe/peach-salad-tomatoes-beets).

## Directions

1. **Bake beets:** Heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C. Put scrubbed beets in a small roasting dish with about 1 inch of water. Cover tightly and bake until a thin knife slides easily to the center, checking after about 50 minutes and continuing as needed, often 60–75 minutes. Keep some water in the dish; carefully add hot water if it is drying out.

2. **Cool and slice:** Remove the cover carefully, keeping away from the escaping steam, and lift beets from the water. Cool until comfortable to handle, rub off skins, trim roots and cut into 1/4-inch slices. Spread slices on a plate and let them cool fully before assembling; allow roughly 20–30 minutes total cooling, longer if needed.

3. **Prepare dressing and fruit:** While beets cool, whisk oil, half the measured lemon juice, honey and shallot together. Season to taste. Slice tomatoes into 1/4-inch rounds. Pit and slice peaches and toss gently with the remaining half of the lemon juice.

4. **Assemble:** Layer cool beet and tomato slices on a wide platter and season lightly with salt and pepper. Arrange peach slices over them and spoon over the shallot dressing without stirring the fragile layers.

5. **Finish:** Scatter with goat cheese, mint and thyme. Serve promptly so the herbs remain fresh and the fruit holds its shape.
