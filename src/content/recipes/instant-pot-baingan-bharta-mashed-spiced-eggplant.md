## Chef's Note

Baingan bharta is the smoky mashed eggplant of North Indian home kitchens. Its defining flavor comes from charring the eggplant over an open flame, so don't skip that step. Golden onion, cumin and warm spices build the base, and a long, patient simmer at the end turns the juices into a glossy mash thick enough to scoop with naan.

## Directions

1. **Char the eggplants:** Set the whole eggplants directly over a gas flame or close under a hot broiler. Turn with tongs until the skin is black and blistered all over and the flesh is still firm enough to cube, about 8 minutes. Let them cool on a plate.
2. **Peel and cube:** Peel off the charred skin, leaving a few dark flecks for smoke. Trim the stems and cut the flesh into 1-inch cubes.
3. **Cook the onion:** Heat the oil in the pressure cooker on the medium sauté setting. Add the cumin seeds and stir until fragrant and a shade darker, about 30 seconds. Add the onion and salt and cook, stirring often, until soft with golden edges, 8 to 12 minutes.
4. **Bloom the spices:** Lower the heat. Add the garlic, ginger, coriander, garam masala, black pepper, turmeric, paprika and cayenne and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
5. **Deglaze:** Turn off the sauté setting and pour in the water. Scrape the bottom of the pot with a wooden spatula until no browned bits stick, 1 to 2 minutes.
6. **Layer the vegetables:** Spread the eggplant over the onion base. Spoon the tomatoes and their juices on top and don't stir them in, so they can't scorch on the bottom.
7. **Pressure-cook:** Seal the lid and cook at high pressure for 7 minutes. Turn off Keep Warm and let the pressure release naturally until the float valve drops, 20 to 30 minutes.
8. **Mash:** Open the lid. The eggplant cubes should collapse under a fork. Mash everything together with a potato masher, leaving some texture.
9. **Reduce:** Set the cooker to sauté and simmer uncovered, stirring and scraping the bottom often, until the mash is glossy and thick and a spoonful has no watery rim, 20 to 35 minutes. Lower the heat as it thickens. Taste and adjust the salt.
10. **Finish:** Turn off the heat and fold in the cilantro and half the lemon juice. Taste and add the rest of the lemon juice if it needs brightening.
