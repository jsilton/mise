## Chef's Note

Korma is a mild, creamy braise from the Mughal kitchens of North India, thickened with ground nuts. Here almond meal and coconut milk make the sauce. Blooming the onion, ginger and curry powder in ghee before they go into the cooker gives it depth, and garam masala and yogurt at the end add fragrance and tang.

## Directions

1. **Bloom the aromatics:** Melt the ghee in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion, stirring, until soft, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger and curry powder and stir until fragrant but not dark, about 1 minute.
2. **Load the cooker:** Spread the carrots in the bottom of a 6-quart slow cooker. Scrape the onion mixture over them and pile the cauliflower on top.
3. **Add the sauce:** Whisk the coconut milk with the almond meal, salt and pepper flakes. Pour it over the vegetables and turn the cauliflower gently to coat, leaving the carrots underneath.
4. **Slow-cook:** Cover and cook on high until a knife slides into a carrot with only slight resistance, 3 to 4 hours.
5. **Add the green vegetables:** Fold in the green beans, cover and cook on high until nearly tender, 30 to 45 minutes. Stir in the peas and cook until hot, 10 to 15 minutes more.
6. **Finish with yogurt:** Turn off the cooker and uncover until the bubbling stops, about 5 minutes. Stir in the garam masala. Whisk a few spoonfuls of the hot sauce into the yogurt, then fold it back into the curry. Taste and add salt.
