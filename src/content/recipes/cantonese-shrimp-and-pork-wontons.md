## Chef's Note

Cantonese wontons have a bouncy pork filling studded with sweet pieces of shrimp and crunchy water chestnut. Stir the seasoned meat hard in one direction until it turns sticky, then fold in the chunky ingredients so they keep their bite. Serve them in clear broth or with chili oil.

## Directions

1. **Make the paste:** In a large bowl, combine the pork, the finely chopped shrimp, ginger, garlic, Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, white pepper, salt and egg. Stir hard in one direction until the mixture turns pale, sticky and holds together, 3 to 5 minutes.
2. **Fold in the chunks:** Fold in the water chestnuts, green onions and shrimp pieces until evenly mixed.
3. **Taste the filling:** Cook a small flattened spoonful of filling in a skillet over medium heat until cooked through, about 2 minutes per side. Taste it and add a little more salt to the filling if it needs it.
4. **Wrap the wontons:** Keep the wrappers under a damp towel and set out a small bowl of water. Put a heaping teaspoon of filling in the center of a wrapper, wet the edges and fold it into a triangle, pressing out the air. Bring the two side corners together and pinch them to seal. Set the wontons on a lightly floured tray and keep them covered.
5. **Boil the wontons:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add a batch of wontons with room to move and stir gently so they don't stick. Once the water returns to a boil, lower the heat to a gentle boil and cook until the wontons float and the filling is firm and cooked through, 4 to 5 minutes.
6. **Serve:** Lift the wontons out with a slotted spoon into bowls of hot broth or onto a plate with dipping sauce. Cook the rest in batches.
