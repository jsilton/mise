## Chef's Note

Chorizo seasons the vegetables before the rice bakes in broth with chickpeas and raisins. This covered method uses Arborio rice. Bomba and Calasparra absorb differently and need their own liquid and timing adjustments; do not substitute them directly in this version.

## Directions

1.  **Render:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. In a large oven-safe skillet or Dutch oven with a tight-fitting lid, cook the chorizo rounds over medium heat until they are crispy and have released their red oil. Remove chorizo to a plate.
2.  **Sauté:** Add the onion, bell pepper, and garlic to the chorizo fat. Cook for about 8–10 minutes until softened, stirring and lowering the heat if the garlic browns too quickly.
3.  **The Bloom:** Stir in the tomato paste, smoked paprika, and saffron. Fry for 1 minute until fragrant.
4.  **Toast:** Add the dry rice to the pan. Stir for 2 minutes to coat every grain in the spiced oil.
5.  **Deglaze:** Pour in the sherry, scraping the bottom of the pan. Let it bubble for 1 minute.
6.  **Simmer:** Add the broth, chickpeas, raisins, and the reserved chorizo. Bring to a boil.
7.  **Bake:** Cover tightly and transfer to a 400°F oven. Bake for about 20–25 minutes, then check for absorbed liquid and a tender rice center. If the rice is firm and the pan is dry, add a little hot water, cover, and continue baking; if still wet and firm, keep cooking before adding liquid.
8.  **Rest:** Remove from oven and let sit, covered, for 10 minutes. Fluff gently before serving. Allow about 80 minutes overall: 20 minutes preparation, roughly 50 minutes for rendering, sautéing, heating liquid and baking, plus the 10-minute rest. Extra browning batches or firm rice extend this estimate.
