## Chef's Note

This simple version uses oil rather than butter and only a small amount of broccoli. Steam the florets first, keep the clean finishing sauce separate from the shrimp marinade, and use moderate heat so the honey coating does not burn. Adapted from [Layla Atik’s Honey Garlic Shrimp and Broccoli](https://gimmedelicious.com/quick-honey-garlic-shrimp-and-broccoli/).

## Directions

1. **Steam broccoli:** Place florets and measured water in a microwave-safe bowl with a vented cover. Microwave on high for about 2 minutes, then check a stem carefully, keeping your face away from escaping steam. Continue in short bursts if needed until tender with a little bite; add a little water if the bowl dries. Drain.

2. **Mix and divide sauce:** Whisk honey, soy sauce, garlic and ginger together. Put half in a clean bowl for finishing. Toss prepared shrimp with the other half and refrigerate 5 minutes; prepare the broccoli during this rest if not already done.

3. **Drain shrimp:** Lift shrimp from the marinade, letting excess drip off, and blot lightly if very wet. Discard used marinade. Have a clean plate ready for cooked shrimp.

4. **Cook in batches:** Heat half the oil over medium-high and cook half the shrimp in a single layer. Cook about 1–2 minutes per side, adjusting for size, until firm, pearly and opaque through the center; a fine-tip thermometer inserted into a thick shrimp should read 145°F / 63°C. Transfer to the clean plate and repeat with remaining oil and shrimp. Reduce heat if the honey darkens rapidly; remove burnt residue before the next batch.

5. **Finish sauce:** Lower heat to medium. Add the clean reserved sauce and broccoli; stir gently about 1 minute until bubbling and the broccoli is hot. Return shrimp and plate juices only briefly to coat. A small splash of water can loosen over-reduced sauce. Serve promptly with separately prepared rice and another vegetable side.
