## Chef's Note

Make the pesto mixture before handling raw chicken, and keep its bowl and spoon clean. Only measured portions go onto the roasting food; the rest is a separate clean reserve for another meal. Use enough pan space for the full chicken and vegetable load, and follow the chicken temperature and vegetable tenderness rather than a fixed two-stage clock. Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below and use within 3–4 days; freeze portions intended for later in the week.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. Keep raw chicken cold until preparation and thaw frozen chicken in the refrigerator. Prepare all the vegetables as listed. Use rimmed pans and parchment rated for 400°F, with enough space to spread the complete load without piling it up; use additional pans for larger batches.

2. **Make and reserve the clean mixture:** Before handling raw chicken, mix all the listed prepared basil pesto, Parmesan, garlic and 2/3 of the olive oil, with salt and pepper to taste. At the original batch this is 2 tbsp of the 3 tbsp oil. With a clean spoon, measure out the two roasting additions into separate small dishes:2 tbsp of the finished mixture for the first stage and another 2 tbsp for the second stage at the original batch, scaled proportionally. Cover and refrigerate all the remaining clean mixture for another meal. Do not dip a raw-contact spoon back into this reserve.

3. **First roasting stage:** Toss all the diced chicken and mushrooms with the remaining 1/3 of the olive oil and the first measured pesto-mixture addition. At the original batch this is 1 tbsp oil and 2 tbsp finished mixture. Spread over the pans and roast for about 10 minutes; the chicken is not yet assumed cooked.

4. **Add the vegetables:** Add all the broccoli, zucchini and cherry tomatoes, and toss with the second measured finished-mixture addition. At the original batch this is 2 tbsp; it comes from the separate clean dish, not a raw-contact reserve. Spread the complete load out again, using another pan if needed.

5. **Finish roasting:** Start checking after another 10 minutes. Continue until each chicken piece reaches 165°F in its thickest part and the vegetables are tender to your liking. Check several pieces in each pan; wet vegetables and crowding can lengthen cooking. If chicken finishes first, move it with clean utensils to a clean plate and continue the vegetables; any vegetable or liquid that contacted raw chicken must also reach 165°F before serving.

6. **Serve or store:** Divide all the roasted chicken and vegetables among the listed bowls. Pasta or rice is a separate accompaniment. Use refrigerated portions within 3–4 days and reheat to 165°F. Freeze later portions; vegetables may soften after reheating. Refrigerate both the meal and the separate clean pesto reserve within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F, at 40°F or below. Use the reserve within the shorter of its pesto product instructions and 3–4 days, or freeze it; it is not included in the served bowl allocation.
