## Chef's Note

Adapted from Mary Later’s recipe. Coarsely crushed corn flakes hold more texture than powder on the rich noodle custard. Warm noodles and lukewarm melted butter mix into the eggs without setting them early; check the center before the fifteen-minute rest.

## Directions

1. **Prepare dishes:** Heat the oven to 325°F with a rack in the center. For the original batch, use unsalted butter to grease a room-temperature 9×13-inch glass dish, preferably the existing deep 4-quart plan, and have a smaller greased oven-safe overflow dish ready. Follow the actual dish’s temperature and thermal-shock instructions.
2. **Cook noodles:** Set out medium-width egg noodles, water, and salt. Bring the salted water to a boil, then add the full dry noodle amount and cook just to al dente, following its package as a guide. Drain well and let the noodles cool until warm, not steaming hot, before adding to the custard.
3. **Mix custard:** Set out sour cream, large-curd cottage cheese, block-style cream cheese, 3/4 of the unsalted butter, large eggs, granulated sugar, and ground cinnamon. Beat the softened cream cheese with a little sour cream until smooth, then mix in the remaining sour cream and all the cottage cheese, eggs, granulated sugar, cinnamon and allocated butter, keeping the cottage-cheese curds if desired. The butter allocation here is three-quarters of the full butter amount; reserve the other quarter for the topping. Use lukewarm butter and warm noodles so they do not cook the eggs on contact.
4. **Combine and divide:** Fold all the warm noodles into all the custard. Set out golden raisins (if using). For a half-raisin pudding, divide the noodle mixture in half and add all the optional raisins to only one half. Spoon the two mixtures into opposite sides of the greased dish; omit raisins for an entirely plain pudding. Leave at least 1/2 inch below the rim for topping and slight puffing. Put excess mixture in the overflow dish rather than filling to the brim.
5. **Coat topping:** Toss 1/4 of the unsalted butter, corn flakes, and light brown sugar until all the coarse corn flakes are coated with the remaining one-quarter butter and packed brown sugar. Scatter across the full noodle mixture without pressing down. With an overflow dish, divide topping in proportion to surface area.
6. **Bake and check the center:** Bake at 325°F, beginning checks around 60 minutes for the original deep dish; 65–80 minutes is the existing planning range. The edges should be set, the center should have only a slight wobble with no liquid custard, and its center must reach 165°F /74°C. Check every dish; smaller overflow portions may finish earlier. Loosely tent if the topping colors before the center is ready.
7. **Rest and serve:** Set hot glass on a dry trivet following the vessel’s instructions. Rest 15 minutes before dividing into the listed portions. Keep the raisin and plain halves distinct when serving.
