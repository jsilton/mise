## Chef's Note

Broccolini’s stems and florets need different checks: split thick stems so they cook through, and watch the tips and thin garlic slices as they brown. Roast half the lemon in rounds alongside the stalks, then add the reserved juice from the other half at serving for a fresh acidic finish.

## Directions

1. **Heat and trim:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment rated for that heat, or use suitable foil or a bare sheet. Wash and dry the broccolini and lemon. Trim about ½ inch from the stalk bases, or more if needed to remove a tough end. Split stems thicker than a pencil lengthwise; leave the tender tops attached.
2. **Coat and arrange:** Toss bunches of broccolini, extra-virgin olive oil, garlic cloves, red pepper flakes, kosher salt, black pepper, and 1/2 of the lemon together, using the thin lemon rounds for this stage and reserving the other half’s juice. Spread in a single layer without piling, with enough sheets for the actual bunch size. Tuck garlic slices against or beneath the stalks rather than leaving them exposed alone at the edges of the sheet.
3. **Watch the garlic and stems:** Roast for 12–15 minutes, checking toward the early end. The thickest stems should yield to a knife with a little resistance; florets can have browned tips, and garlic should be golden rather than black. If exposed garlic is browning ahead of the stems, move it beneath the stalks or lift ready slices into the serving dish. Continue firm stems until tender and rotate the sheet if browning is uneven.
4. **Use the reserved juice:** Add 1/2 of the lemon and flaky sea salt, distributing the reserved lemon juice and tasting before adding the finishing salt. Serve the broccolini with the roasted lemon rounds and garlic; the rounds can be squeezed over the vegetables or eaten as preferred. Serve promptly.
