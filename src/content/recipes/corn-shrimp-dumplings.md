## Chef's Note

These Food & Wine dumplings put sweet summer corn inside a bouncy shrimp filling. Stir the shrimp hard in one direction until it turns sticky, which gives the filling its snap. The corn goes in last so the kernels stay whole and pop when you bite in.

## Directions

1. **Make the filling:** In a large bowl, combine the shrimp, scallions, ginger, garlic, sesame oil, salt and white pepper. Stir hard in one direction until the mixture turns sticky and pale, about 2 minutes. Fold in the corn.
2. **Fill the dumplings:** Put a heaping spoonful of filling in the center of a wrapper and wet the edge with water. Fold it into a half-moon, press out the air and pleat the edge to seal. Keep the finished dumplings under a damp towel.
3. **Steam the dumplings:** Line a bamboo steamer with parchment and set the dumplings in it with space between them. Steam, covered, over boiling water until the filling is pink and firm and the wrappers are tender, 4 to 5 minutes. Work in batches.
4. **Serve:** Serve hot with soy sauce, chili oil and pickled red onions, if using.
