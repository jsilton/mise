## Chef's Note

This bowl pairs Hawaiian-inspired chile-soy tuna with oi muchim, a Korean cucumber salad. The cucumber already supplies chile, garlic, acid and salt, so drain its excess dressing and taste before offering more soy. Nori goes on at the table for the clearest crisp contrast.

## Before buying raw fish

Tell the supplier the tuna is intended for raw service and confirm species, handling and applicable parasite controls; a “sushi-grade” label alone is insufficient. Yellowfin is specifically exempt from parasite-destruction freezing under the FDA Food Code, but that does not remove other raw-fish risks. Do not rely on vinegar, smell or a domestic freezer to establish suitability. Tuna's cold chain also matters because histamine formed during mishandling cannot be removed by cooking or freezing.

Raw seafood is not suitable for everyone. Children, pregnant people, older adults and people with weakened immune systems should choose fully cooked seafood instead. Thaw frozen tuna in the refrigerator following its label; remove vacuum packaging before refrigerator thawing.

## Directions

1. **Start the rice:** Prepare [Sushi Rice (Shari)](/mise/recipes/master-sushi-rice). Measure four cooked cups for these bowls; a full batch makes extra, which should be refrigerated promptly. Use only this amount rather than adding another rice side.
2. **Prepare cucumber and edamame:** Make a half batch of [Oi Muchim](/mise/recipes/korean-pickled-cucumbers), starting with 300 g cucumber; use the whole finished half batch across the four bowls. Follow its salting and dressing steps during rice preparation. Cook the shelled edamame according to its package, drain and chill promptly. Do not assume frozen beans need only thawing.
3. **Prepare toppings and dressing:** Dice avocado, slice scallions and cut nori with clean equipment. Keep nori dry. Lift the measured cucumber salad from excess dressing. In a separate bowl, whisk soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, ginger and sriracha; taste before adding fish.
4. **Cut and season tuna while rice cools:** During the final rice cooling period, using separate clean equipment, cut the cold tuna into 1/2-inch cubes. Fold into the dressing and refrigerate for about 5–10 minutes while preparing to serve. This is seasoning time, not a process that cooks or sanitizes the fish.
5. **Assemble:** Portion the rice among four bowls when it is barely warm. Divide tuna, avocado, cucumber salad and chilled edamame among them; lift tuna from pooled dressing rather than pouring all liquid over the rice. Add scallions, sesame and nori. Offer the measured optional soy and mayonnaise, using clean spoons that have not touched raw fish. Eat promptly.
