## Chef's Note

A Spanish-style pot of mussels from Bon Appétit. Crisp chorizo, garlic and fennel seed flavor the oil, and wine and cherry tomatoes cook down into a concentrated base before the mussels go in. As they open they add their own briny juices, and charred bread soaks up every drop.

## Directions

1. **Render the chorizo:** Heat the olive oil and chorizo in a large, heavy pot over medium heat. Cook, stirring, until the chorizo browns and crisps at the edges, about 4 minutes. Add the garlic and fennel seeds and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
2. **Reduce the base:** Add the tomatoes and wine and season with pepper. Simmer over medium-high heat until the liquid has reduced by about three-quarters and the tomatoes have softened, 6 to 8 minutes.
3. **Steam the mussels:** Add the mussels, throwing out any with cracked shells or any open ones that stay open when tapped. Cover tightly and cook, shaking the pot now and then, until the shells open, 6 to 8 minutes. Discard any that stay closed.
4. **Char the bread:** While the mussels cook, toast or grill the bread until charred at the edges. Drizzle it well with olive oil.
5. **Serve:** Ladle the mussels and all the broth into deep bowls. Scatter with tarragon and serve with the bread.
