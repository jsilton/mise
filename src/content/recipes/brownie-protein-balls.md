## Chef's Note

Powder absorption and almond-butter consistency determine whether the dough needs any added liquid. Mix first, then add the optional water or almond milk gradually within the full listed range. Chill before judging firmness. The portion count is a plan for dividing all the dough, not a measured tablespoon yield.

## Directions

1. **Mix:** Combine creamy almond butter, chocolate protein powder, almond flour, unsweetened cocoa powder, maple syrup, and sea salt with a spatula until a thick dough begins to form.
2. **Adjust hydration:** If the dough will not hold together, add water or almond milk (if using) a little at a time, mixing and checking after each addition. Use only as much of the listed range as needed; different powders and nut butters absorb liquid differently.
3. **Add inclusions:** Fold in ground flaxseed or chia seeds and mini chocolate chips until evenly distributed. If this makes the dough crumbly, use any remaining part of the same optional liquid range; do not count a second extra allowance.
4. **Portion:** Divide the entire dough into roughly equal balls. About twelve balls is the original-batch planning count; their size depends on the actual mixture. For a scaled batch, increase the count proportionally rather than keeping twelve larger balls or assuming a fixed tablespoon scoop.
5. **Set:** Arrange on a clean plate or tray, cover and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below for at least 20 minutes, until firm enough to handle.
6. **Store:** Keep covered and refrigerated. Use within the shortest after-opening instruction for the ingredients used, especially if almond milk was added. Keep combined preparation and serving time out of refrigeration within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C.

## Cooking Notes

Honey may replace the full listed maple range; use only one sweetener and scale the range with the batch. Ground flaxseed and chia seeds are exclusive choices for the full listed inclusion amount. Water and almond milk are exclusive choices for the conditional liquid.

Use packaged almond flour, protein powder and other ingredients suitable for a no-cook preparation; check the actual labels or manufacturer guidance. Do not replace almond flour with raw wheat flour or try to make raw flour safe through a home heat-treatment method. For a shorter rest, check firmness after 15–20 minutes; the main plan allows at least 20 minutes. Keep the mixture refrigerated.
