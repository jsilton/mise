## Chef's Note

Salt and drain the grated vegetables, then squeeze out excess liquid before mixing the egg and flour binder. Make the garlic yogurt separately and keep it cold; its oil, honey, mint and lemon belong in the sauce, not the fritter batter.

## Directions

1. **Make yogurt:** Whisk plain yogurt, fresh mint, fresh lemon juice, olive oil, honey, garlic clove, kosher salt, and black pepper in a clean bowl. Cover and refrigerate while making the fritters.
2. **Drain vegetables:** Toss small zucchini, small russet potato, medium onion, and kosher salt in a sieve set over a bowl and stand 30–40 minutes. Gather in portions in a clean towel and squeeze out excess liquid; the vegetables need not become bone dry.
3. **Mix binder:** Mix large eggs, garlic clove, ground cumin, fresh parsley, fresh mint, additional kosher salt (if using), and black pepper, then add all the squeezed vegetables. Keep this raw-egg mixture and utensils away from the cold yogurt and finished fritters.
4. **Bind:** Sprinkle all-purpose flour and baking powder over the vegetable-egg mixture and mix gently.
5. **Fry batches:** Heat vegetable oil in a suitable large skillet over medium-high until hot but not smoking. The listed supply does not guarantee ½-inch depth. Fry flattened scoops in uncrowded batches, with a ¼-cup scoop as an optional size reference. Start checking around 3 minutes per side, adjusting heat as needed; finish when golden with set centers at 160°F / 71°C, sampling representative fritters with a thin probe. Keep remaining raw batter refrigerated during extended batches.
6. **Drain and serve:** Drain cooked fritters on a clean rack. Use additional kosher salt (if using), olive oil (if using), additional fresh parsley (if using), and additional fresh mint (if using) as desired; serve the warm fritters with the cold garlic yogurt.
