## Chef's Note

Saag means leafy greens, and in Punjabi homes this dish is rustic, with chopped spinach fried in a spiced onion masala rather than blended smooth. Squeeze the greens well so they turn glossy instead of watery. Soaking the browned paneer in hot salted water keeps it soft and pillowy.

## Directions

1. **Blanch the spinach:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil and set a big bowl of ice water nearby. Working in batches, push the spinach under and cook until fully wilted, about 1 minute. Lift each batch into the ice water.
2. **Squeeze and chop:** Drain the spinach and squeeze it by the handful until it stops dripping but is still moist. Chop it into 1/4-inch pieces.
3. **Brown the paneer:** Stir the soaking salt into the hot water in a heatproof bowl. Heat a third of the ghee in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the paneer in two batches until golden on two sides, 2 to 3 minutes per side, then slip the cubes into the hot water to soak.
4. **Cook the onion:** Lower the heat to medium and add the rest of the ghee and the cumin seeds. When the seeds sizzle and smell toasty, about 30 seconds, add the onion. Cook, stirring, until soft and golden at the edges, 8 to 10 minutes.
5. **Add the aromatics:** Add the ginger, garlic and green chili and cook until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes. Stir in the turmeric and coriander for 20 seconds.
6. **Fry the greens:** Add the spinach and most of the salt. Stir hard to coat every bit in the spiced ghee and cook until hot and dry in the pan, 3 to 5 minutes. Add a splash of water if it sticks.
7. **Add cream and paneer:** Lower the heat and stir in the cream. Crush the kasuri methi between your fingers over the pan. Let it bubble gently for 1 to 2 minutes. Drain the paneer, fold it in gently and warm until hot through, 3 to 5 minutes.
8. **Season:** Take the pan off the heat and stir in the garam masala and lemon juice. Taste and add the rest of the salt as needed.
9. **Make the chile ghee:** For the optional chile ghee, melt the ghee in a small pan over low heat. Off the heat, stir in the Kashmiri chile powder for a few seconds. Spoon it over the adults' portions.
