## Chef's Note

The Gwock family’s orange gelatin salad brings crushed pineapple, mandarin oranges and a generous sour-cream finish to the holiday or potluck table. Keep the pineapple juice in this version and drain the mandarin packing liquid. Serve it chilled by the spoonful, allowing the center to set fully before bringing it to the table.

## Directions

1. **Prepare for cold holding:** Clear refrigerator space for a shallow 2-quart serving dish or bowl with room for the complete salad and some headroom. Chill the sour cream until needed. Clean the can lids before opening; drain canned mandarin oranges, keeping the pineapple juice with its fruit. Keep the fruit covered and cold until mixing.
2. **Dissolve the gelatin and sugar:** Combine regular orange gelatin dessert mix, granulated sugar, and boiling water in a heatproof mixing bowl. Stir for about 2 minutes and continue until no dry grains remain. Do not add the plain-gelatin package’s separate cold-water amount; this family route uses fruit, juice and sour cream instead.
3. **Whisk in the sour cream:** Let the dissolved mixture lose its steaming heat while still fluid. Whisk a few spoonfuls of full-fat sour cream into the still-fluid gelatin base, then gradually whisk in the remaining sour cream until evenly blended.
4. **Fold in both fruits:** Fold in canned crushed pineapple in juice and canned mandarin oranges gently, including all the pineapple juice and keeping the orange segments as intact as practical. Pour the complete mixture into the prepared serving dish; do not discard fruit or cream to make it fit.
5. **Chill until set:** Cover and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below promptly. Allow about 4–6 hours or overnight, continuing until the center holds a soft jiggle with no freely sloshing liquid. Serve cold from the bowl rather than relying on a clean unmold. If the center is still loose, continue chilling.

## Cooking Notes

Use canned pineapple for this gelatin mixture; raw pineapple can interfere with setting. The full added sugar and sour cream are part of its sweet, creamy character. Keep it refrigerated until serving.
