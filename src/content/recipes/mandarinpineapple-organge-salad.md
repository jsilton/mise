## Chef's Note

This orange congealed salad is a Gwock family recipe. Orange Jell-O, sour cream, crushed pineapple and mandarin oranges set into a sweet, creamy salad that's served cold by the spoonful. Whisking the sour cream into the hot Jell-O turns the salad smooth and opaque.

## Directions

1. **Dissolve the Jell-O:** Stir orange Jell-O, sugar, and boiling water together in a heatproof bowl until the Jell-O and sugar are completely dissolved, about 2 minutes.
2. **Whisk in the sour cream:** Add sour cream and whisk until smooth.
3. **Add the fruit:** Stir in crushed pineapple in juice and mandarin oranges. Pour the mixture into a serving dish.
4. **Chill until set:** Cover and refrigerate until set, at least 4 hours or overnight. Serve cold, by the spoonful.

## Suggested Tweak

- Let the dissolved Jell-O cool for a few minutes, until it stops steaming, before you whisk in the sour cream. Whisk in a few spoonfuls first, then the rest, so it blends without lumps.
- Set it in a shallow dish so the center firms up at the same time as the edges.
