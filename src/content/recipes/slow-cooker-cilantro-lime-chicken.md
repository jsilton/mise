## Chef's Note

Damn Delicious's taco night in a slow cooker: chicken cooks in salsa verde and Ro-Tel with black beans and corn, then gets shredded back into the pot. A big handful of cilantro and fresh lime stirred in at the end keep it bright. Pull the chicken as soon as it is done so the lean breast stays juicy.

## Directions

1. **Load the cooker:** Combine the beans, tomatoes with their juices, corn, salsa verde, onion, jalapeño if using, cumin, chili powder, pepper and salt in a 4-quart slow cooker. Nestle in the chicken and spoon some sauce over it.
2. **Slow-cook:** Cover and cook on Low until the thickest chicken reads 165°F, 3 to 4 hours. Move the chicken to a board.
3. **Thicken the filling:** If the bean mixture is watery or the onion is still firm, pour it into a wide saucepan. Simmer over medium heat, stirring gently, until the onion is tender and the filling mounds on a spoon, 5 to 10 minutes. Meanwhile, shred the chicken.
4. **Season:** Fold the chicken and its juices into the hot filling and turn off the heat. Stir in the cilantro and half the lime juice. Taste and add the rest of the lime juice and more salt as needed.
5. **Warm the tortillas:** Heat a dry skillet over medium heat. Warm the tortillas until soft and lightly spotted, 20 to 30 seconds per side, and wrap them in a towel.
6. **Serve:** Spoon the filling into the warm tortillas and top with sliced avocado.
