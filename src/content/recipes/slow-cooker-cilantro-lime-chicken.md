## Chef's Note

This Tex-Mex filling combines chicken with beans and corn, so six portions do not require six large servings of meat. Salsa acidity gives flavor, but the breast still needs an internal-temperature check and timely removal. Finish the liquid consistency before stirring in cilantro and lime. Two warm tortillas and a little avocado complete each serving.

## Directions

1. **Load:** Combine drained beans, tomatoes and their juices, corn, salsa verde, finely diced onion, optional jalapeño, cumin, chili blend and pepper in a 4-quart slow cooker. Nestle in fully thawed whole breasts and spoon some sauce over them. Cover and start immediately. Keep cilantro and avocado for serving.
2. **Cook and check:** Cook on LOW about 3–4 hours, beginning checks around 2 1/2 hours, or HIGH about 2–3 hours. Check several thick breast centers for 165°F / 74°C. Remove fully cooked chicken to a clean board even if the onion needs longer; check a piece of onion separately.
3. **Finish the filling base:** If onion is still firm or the base is watery, transfer the bean mixture to a wide saucepan and simmer, stirring gently, about 5–10 minutes or until onion is tender and excess liquid has evaporated. Add a small splash of water only if the onion needs more cooking and the pan is dry. Meanwhile shred the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
4. **Combine and season:** Fold chicken and its juices back into the hot filling and warm briefly through. Turn off heat. Add cilantro and half the lime juice, taste, then add remaining juice and measured salt only as needed.
5. **Warm tortillas:** Heat a dry skillet over medium heat. Warm tortillas in batches, about 20–30 seconds per side or following their package, until pliable and lightly spotted. Wrap in a clean towel as each batch finishes; do not char them until brittle.
6. **Serve:** Slice avocado just before serving. Divide filling among six diners, with two warm tortillas and one-sixth of the avocado each. Spoon excess cooking liquid sparingly so the tortillas stay manageable.
