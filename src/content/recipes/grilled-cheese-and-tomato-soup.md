## Chef's Note

The best rainy-day lunch: a creamy San Marzano tomato soup with a grilled cheese for dunking. Mayonnaise spread on the outside of the bread browns more evenly than butter and gives a crisp, golden crust. Thin slices of avocado inside add a cool, creamy layer against the sharp Cheddar.

## Directions

1. **Soften the onion:** Melt the butter in a 3-quart saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the onion and cook, stirring, until soft and golden, 6 to 8 minutes. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
2. **Simmer the soup:** Add the tomatoes and their juices, the broth and the basil, breaking up the tomatoes with a spoon. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 15 minutes.
3. **Blend the soup:** Take the pot off the heat and purée with an immersion blender until smooth. Stir in the cream, season with salt and pepper and keep warm over low heat.
4. **Build the sandwiches:** Spread a thin layer of mayonnaise on one side of each slice of bread. Set two slices mayonnaise side down in a cold large skillet. Layer half the cheese and avocado on top and cover with two more slices, mayonnaise side up.
5. **Toast the sandwiches:** Set the skillet over medium heat. Cook, pressing lightly with a spatula, until deep golden and the cheese has melted, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Lower the heat if the bread browns before the cheese melts.
6. **Toast the second batch:** Build two more sandwiches with the rest of the bread, cheese and avocado. Toast them over medium-low heat until deep golden and melted, 2 to 3 minutes per side.
7. **Serve:** Cut the sandwiches on the diagonal and serve with bowls of the hot soup for dipping.
