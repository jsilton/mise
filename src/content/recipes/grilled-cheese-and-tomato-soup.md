## Chef's Note

Make the soup first and keep it gently hot while the sandwiches cook. A thin mayonnaise layer helps the bread brown evenly, but heat control still matters: lower the heat if the crust darkens before the cheese melts. Avocado belongs inside, where it contrasts with the crisp exterior.

## Directions

1. **Soften aromatics:** Melt the measured butter in a saucepan over medium-low. Cook onion about **6–8 minutes** until soft, then add garlic for **30–45 seconds** until fragrant.
2. **Simmer soup:** Add all listed tomatoes and juices, broth and dried basil. Break up tomatoes with a spoon, bring to a gentle simmer and cook about **15 minutes**, stirring, until soft. Prepare sandwich ingredients while it simmers.
3. **Blend and add cream:** Remove from heat and let active bubbling stop. Blend with an immersion blender, keeping its guard submerged and stopping before lifting it. Alternatively, cool to your countertop blender’s permitted temperature and blend in as many small batches as its hot-liquid fill limit requires, with the lid and steam vent arranged exactly as its instructions specify. Start low; do not use a sealed personal cup for hot soup. Stir in cream and season with salt and pepper. Warm gently and keep hot at **140°F / 60°C or above** for the brief sandwich cooking period, without a hard boil.
4. **Assemble sandwiches:** Spread a thin layer of mayonnaise on one face of each bread slice, using only what is needed from the measured allowance. Place **half the bread slices**, mayonnaise-side down, in a cool skillet. Divide all the cheese and avocado evenly over these bases. Top with the remaining bread, mayonnaise-side up.
5. **Toast gently:** Set over medium-low to medium heat. Cook about **4–6 minutes per side**, turning carefully, until golden and cheese has melted in the center. Lower heat if bread colors too quickly; cover briefly if the center needs help warming, then uncover. Use additional rounds if the skillet is crowded.
6. **Serve together:** Cut sandwiches as desired and divide the soup among the intended portions. Serve immediately for dipping; do not leave the sandwiches sitting in the soup.
