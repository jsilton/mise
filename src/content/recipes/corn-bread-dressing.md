## Chef's Note

This is the Silton family's Thanksgiving cornbread dressing, served next to the turkey. Toasting the cornbread cubes first gives them a crust, so they soak up the herb butter and stock without turning to mush. Plenty of butter, shallots and fresh thyme make it rich and savory.

## Directions

1. **Toast the cornbread:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Spread the cornbread cubes on a baking sheet and bake, stirring now and then, until the edges form a crust, about 25 minutes. Let them cool completely.
2. **Soften the shallots:** Melt the butter in a large, heavy skillet over medium heat. Add the shallots and garlic and cook, stirring now and then, until soft, about 6 minutes.
3. **Add the mushrooms:** Add the mushrooms and celery and cook, stirring, for 3 minutes. Stir in the parsley, thyme and sage and take the pan off the heat.
4. **Mix the dressing:** Coarsely crumble the cornbread into a large bowl and add the mushroom mixture. Pour in just enough stock to hold the crumbs together, tossing gently. Season with salt and pepper.
5. **Bake:** Spoon the dressing into a buttered 9-by-11-inch baking dish, 2 inches deep. Bake at 350°F until golden brown on top, about 30 minutes.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it: Season with 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, then taste the moistened bread and add up to 3/4 tsp more before baking. The butter and stock are unsalted, so it needs it. For a clearer sage flavor, use 1 tbsp chopped fresh sage or 1 1/2 tsp rubbed sage in place of the ground sage.
