## Chef's Note

This is Craig Plummer's family recipe for cinnamon sweet potatoes. Thin rounds are tossed in butter, honey, lime and cinnamon and roasted in a hot oven until tender and glazed. The lime keeps them from tasting like dessert.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Coat a rimmed baking sheet with cooking spray.
2. **Mix the glaze:** Whisk the butter, honey, lime juice and cinnamon together in a large bowl.
3. **Coat the rounds:** Add the sweet potatoes and toss until every slice is coated.
4. **Season the rounds:** Spread the slices on the baking sheet in a single layer. Sprinkle them with salt and pepper.
5. **Roast the rounds:** Roast until a knife slides easily through the centers, about 25 minutes.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. For more browning and less risk of burnt honey, spread the rounds over two parchment-lined sheets and roast them with the butter, 1/2 tsp fine salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper for 15 minutes. Then turn them, brush on the honey warmed with the cinnamon, and roast until tender and bubbling, 10 to 15 minutes more, swapping the sheets halfway. Drizzle the lime juice over them at the end so it stays bright.
