## Chef's Note

Honey, cinnamon and butter give these orange sweet-potato rounds a sweet American-style side-dish character, with lime for a tart finish. Add the honey after the first roast so the glaze has less time to darken before the centers soften. Expect tender rounds with browned patches, rather than a dry, brittle fry.

## Directions

1. **Heat and prepare pans:** Heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C, with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Line two roughly 18-by-13-inch rimmed sheets with oven-safe parchment. Melt the butter and let it remain warm and fluid.
2. **Slice and season:** Peel and trim sweet potatoes to the listed weight. Slice crosswise into even one-third-inch rounds; halve unusually broad rounds if needed to handle them comfortably. Pat away surface water. Toss with all the melted butter, salt and pepper, then divide between the sheets in one layer with small gaps and no overlap.
3. **Begin roasting:** Roast for 15 minutes. Near the end of this first roast, warm the honey briefly in a microwave-safe bowl until fluid and stir in the cinnamon. Check the potatoes: bottoms should have some golden patches and centers should be beginning to soften. If still very firm and pale, give them another five minutes before glazing.
4. **Turn, glaze and finish:** Turn the rounds carefully with a thin spatula. Brush the upward-facing surfaces with all the honey-cinnamon mixture in a thin layer. Swap the sheets between shelves and rotate them front to back. Roast another 10–15 minutes, checking early, until several tested centers yield easily to a knife and the glaze bubbles lightly with brown edges. Remove ready pieces if some finish before thicker ones; do not wait for blackened sugar.
5. **Add lime and serve:** Drizzle the measured tablespoon of lime juice evenly over both pans. Transfer gently to a warm serving dish and serve promptly. Divide into four generous side portions, or six smaller holiday portions if the meal includes another starch. Avoid covering tightly while serving, which traps steam and softens the edges.
