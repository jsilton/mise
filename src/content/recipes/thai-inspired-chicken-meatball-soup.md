## Chef's Note

This is a Thai-inspired home soup built around ginger, garlic, cilantro, coconut and fish sauce. It is separate from galangal-led tom kha. The meatballs rely on a cohesive ground-meat mixture and controlled cooking, not a claim that grated ginger shields the protein from drying. Small, evenly sized balls are easier to brown and finish consistently. The broth should remain a soup around them, with lime added at the end.

## Directions

1. **Mix:** Keep chicken cold. Combine it with half the grated ginger, half the garlic, optional jalapeño, cilantro stems and 2 tbsp fish sauce. Mix just until the ingredients are evenly distributed and the mixture is slightly tacky; do not beat into a paste.
2. **Shape:** With damp hands, divide into 24 balls of roughly 40 g each, about 1 1/2 inches across. Keep uncooked portions refrigerated while working in batches. Set out broth ingredients before heating the pot.
3. **Brown in three batches:** Heat 2 tsp oil in a wide Dutch oven over medium heat. Add eight meatballs with space between them; brown two or three sides over about 5 minutes, turning gently after the surface releases. Transfer to a tray. Repeat twice with 2 tsp oil per batch. The centers are not yet fully cooked; continue directly to the broth stage.
4. **Build broth:** Remove any blackened residue from the pot, retaining brown fond. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil and stir the remaining ginger and garlic over medium-low heat for about 30–60 seconds until fragrant. Pour in broth and coconut milk, stirring up the brown residue, and bring to a gentle simmer.
5. **Finish the meatballs:** Return all meatballs and any tray juices. Simmer gently about 10–12 minutes, turning occasionally, until several large meatballs reach 165°F / 74°C at their centers. Extend as needed. Stir in spinach and cook about 1 minute until wilted.
6. **Season and serve:** Turn off heat. Taste the fully cooked broth, adding the remaining fish sauce gradually only if needed, and optional sugar only if it helps the balance. Add half the lime juice, taste, then add more if wanted. Stir in cilantro leaves. Divide into six bowls with four meatballs each and their share of broth and spinach. Serve rice separately if desired.
