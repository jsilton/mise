## Chef's Note

This NYT Cooking soup packs chicken meatballs with ginger, garlic and cilantro, then simmers them in a coconut broth with spinach and lime. Grating the ginger and garlic into a paste keeps the meatballs juicy and seasons them all the way through. Mix gently and let them finish cooking in the broth so they stay tender.

## Directions

1. **Mix the meatballs:** In a large bowl, combine the chicken, half the ginger, half the garlic, the jalapeño, the cilantro stems and two-thirds of the fish sauce. Mix with your hands just until the mixture feels tacky and holds together.
2. **Shape:** With damp hands, roll the mixture into 24 balls about 1 1/2 inches across.
3. **Brown the meatballs:** Heat a third of the browning oil in a wide Dutch oven over medium heat. Add a third of the meatballs and cook, turning each once it releases, until browned on a few sides, about 5 minutes. Move them to a plate and repeat twice with the rest of the browning oil and meatballs. They finish cooking in the broth.
4. **Build the broth:** Lower the heat to medium-low and add the oil for the aromatics. Stir in the remaining ginger and garlic until fragrant, 30 to 60 seconds. Pour in the broth and coconut milk, scrape up the browned bits and bring to a gentle simmer.
5. **Simmer the meatballs:** Return the meatballs and their juices to the pot. Simmer gently, turning now and then, until the centers reach 165°F, 10 to 12 minutes. Stir in the spinach until it wilts, about 1 minute.
6. **Season and serve:** Turn off the heat and stir in the rest of the fish sauce, the lime juice and the cilantro leaves. Taste and add more fish sauce, and the sugar if the broth tastes sharp. Ladle into bowls with four meatballs each.
