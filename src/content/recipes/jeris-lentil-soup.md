## Chef's Note

Pat Miller and Sean Kilpatrick gave us this recipe as a wedding gift. It comes from their friend Jeri, a Broadway dancer who started in the original West Side Story and later became a nutritionist. They make a double batch and freeze it in two-person portions to get through long rehearsal weeks.

## Directions

1. **Sauté the vegetables:** Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots and Italian seasoning and cook, stirring often, until the onion is translucent, about 5 minutes.
2. **Fill the slow cooker:** Put the crushed tomatoes in the slow cooker. Add the vegetables, broth and lentils and stir.
3. **Slow cook:** Cover and cook on LOW until the lentils are tender all the way through, about 5 hours.
4. **Finish the soup:** Stir in the wine, parsley and pepper. Cover and cook on LOW for 30 minutes more. Taste for pepper and serve hot.

## Suggested Tweak

Pour the wine into the skillet once the vegetables are soft and simmer until it reduces by half, about 3 minutes, before everything goes into the slow cooker. That cooks off the raw wine taste. Then finish the soup with 1 tbsp sherry vinegar or fresh lemon juice and salt to taste. For a creamier soup, pulse it a few times with an immersion blender, leaving most of the lentils whole.
