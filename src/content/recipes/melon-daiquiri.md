## Chef's Note

Honeydew and melon liqueur provide sweetness alongside the rum and lime. Blend the prepared flesh smoothly, then decide whether a little ice is needed for the texture you want. This generous melon mixture can exceed small cocktail glasses; keep room for the actual mixture or offer refills.

## Directions

1. **Prepare and chill the melon:** Wash and scrub both melon rinds under running water before cutting, then peel, deseed and cube the honeydew; the listed amount is prepared flesh (six cups in the original batch). Prepare the cantaloupe garnish separately. For a colder drink, spread the honeydew in a freezer-safe container and chill for about 30 minutes; the pieces may still be soft in the center. For the fresh-melon option, skip this wait.
2. **Blend in suitable loads:** Use honeydew melon, light rum, fresh lime juice, green melon liqueur, and ice cubes (if using). Start with the melon and measured rum, lime and liqueur, keeping within the actual blender’s fill limit. Work in smaller loads if needed, divide the measured liquids among them and recombine. Follow the machine’s speed instructions; blend until smooth, stopping safely to check. Add the optional ice gradually only if needed for a thicker pour. Do not keep adding ice to force a precise frozen texture.
3. **Prepare the skewers:** Use cantaloupe cubes and sugar cane skewers. In the original four-portion batch, thread three cantaloupe cubes onto each skewer. For another number of portions, keep the same garnish per full serving; fractional skewers are a serving choice, not something to break into the drink.
4. **Divide and serve:** Divide the recombined mixture evenly among the listed portions in glasses with room for the actual volume. Keep any excess alongside as a refill rather than force a small coupe. Add the prepared skewers and serve promptly.
