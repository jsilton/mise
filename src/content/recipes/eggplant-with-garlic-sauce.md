## Chef's Note

Eggplant browns on the outside before its center becomes silky. Two manageable batches and a little covered steam let you finish the texture without chasing color or continually adding oil. The measured garlic-ginger sauce goes in only after the eggplant is tender.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the sauce:** Make half the linked Garlic-Ginger Stir-Fry Sauce recipe, using vegetarian mushroom sauce, for a finished 120 ml. Alternatively, measure 120 ml from a previously prepared batch and keep its original storage date in mind.
2. **Cut the vegetables:** Trim eggplant and cut into roughly 2-inch batons about 1/2 inch thick. Slice scallions. Divide eggplant into two equal batches.
3. **Brown and soften in two batches:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a lidded skillet over medium-high. Add half the eggplant in one layer and cook 3–4 minutes, turning to color several surfaces. Reduce to medium, add 2 tbsp water, cover and cook another 3–5 minutes until a fork enters the center easily. Add a little more water only if the pan dries while centers are firm. Uncover to evaporate remaining water, then transfer to a plate. Repeat with the remaining tablespoon oil, eggplant and 2 tbsp water.
4. **Coat gently:** Return all eggplant to the pan with the 120 ml sauce and optional first teaspoon chili paste. Fold gently over medium heat for about 1–2 minutes until bubbling and evenly coated. If using refrigerated sauce, check the finished dish reaches 165°F / 74°C. Thin a very tight coating with a tablespoon of hot water if needed.
5. **Finish:** Take off heat, fold in scallions and taste. Add optional vinegar or the additional teaspoon chili paste only if wanted. Divide into four vegetable sides with rice and a separate main or protein component.
