## Chef's Note

Garlic-sauce eggplant is a Chinese restaurant favorite: silky, tender eggplant in a glossy, savory glaze. Brown it in two batches, then cover the pan briefly so a little steam softens the centers without soaking up more oil. A splash of black vinegar at the end gives it the classic malty tang.

## Directions

1. **Make the sauce:** Make a half batch of the Garlic-Ginger Stir-Fry Sauce with vegetarian oyster sauce and set it aside.
2. **Brown the eggplant:** Heat half the oil in a 12-inch lidded skillet over medium-high heat. Add half the eggplant in one layer and cook, turning, until browned in patches, 3 to 4 minutes.
3. **Steam until tender:** Lower the heat to medium, add half the water and cover. Cook until a fork slides easily into the center of the eggplant, 3 to 5 minutes. Uncover, let any water cook off and move the eggplant to a plate. Repeat with the rest of the oil, eggplant and water.
4. **Glaze:** Return all the eggplant to the pan with the sauce and sambal. Fold gently over medium heat until the sauce bubbles and coats every piece, 1 to 2 minutes.
5. **Finish:** Take the pan off the heat. Fold in the scallions and black vinegar and serve.
