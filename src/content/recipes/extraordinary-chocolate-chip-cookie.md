## Chef's Note

This dough has a large chocolate-chip filling and can be baked as soon as it is mixed. A refrigerated rest is optional. Cream the softened butter and sugars until pale and fluffy, then finish mixing as soon as the flour and chips are evenly incorporated.

## Directions

1. **Prepare and dry mix:** For immediate baking, start preheating the oven to 350°F before mixing. Whisk the flour, kosher salt, baking powder and baking soda together. For the refrigerated route, preheat near baking time instead.

2. **Cream:** With a paddle attachment, beat the softened butter and both sugars on medium speed until pale and fluffy, about 4 minutes. Scrape the bowl, then beat in the eggs one at a time and mix in the vanilla.

3. **Combine:** On low speed, add the dry ingredients just until no flour pockets remain. Fold in all the chocolate chips until evenly distributed.

4. **Choose the rest:** Bake the dough now, or cover and refrigerate it overnight. A longer 24–36-hour refrigerated rest is another option, not a requirement. Keep any rested dough covered at 40°F / 4°C or below.

5. **Portion:** Preheat the oven to 350°F if it is not already heating, and line sheets with parchment. Use a 2 1/4-inch scoop and leave 3 inches between portions. Keep waiting dough covered and refrigerated and use additional sheets or loads rather than crowding.

6. **Bake:** Load only when the oven reaches 350°F. Bake for 15–17 minutes per tray, until the edges are golden brown and the centers are set but still soft. For chilled dough, begin checking in the same window and continue as the actual cookies need.

7. **Cool:** Let the cookies sit on the sheet for 5 minutes, then carefully transfer them to a rack to cool completely. Allow the sheet to cool before loading more dough.
