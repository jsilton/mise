## Chef's Note

This is a Japanese-inspired twist on buttery mashed potatoes, with white miso whisked into the warm milk. The miso seasons the mash and gives it a deep, savory flavor without tasting like soup. Fold the warm miso butter in gently so the potatoes stay fluffy.

## Directions

1. **Simmer the potatoes:** Put the potatoes in a 5-quart pot, cover with cold water by 1 inch and add the salt for the water. Bring to a boil, then simmer until a knife slides through the pieces with no resistance, 18 to 22 minutes.
2. **Warm the miso milk:** While the potatoes cook, whisk the milk, miso and garlic powder in a small saucepan over low heat until the miso dissolves. Add the butter and warm until it melts, without boiling.
3. **Dry the potatoes:** Drain the potatoes well. Return them to the pot over low heat and shake gently until the surfaces look dry, 1 to 2 minutes.
4. **Mash and fold:** Pass the hot potatoes through a ricer or mash them by hand. Fold in the warm miso milk in two additions with a spatula. If the mash is stiff, warm a little extra milk and fold it in a splash at a time until creamy.
5. **Season and serve:** Taste before adding salt, since the miso is salty. Scatter the scallions over the top and serve hot.
