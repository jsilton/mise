## Chef's Note

Roast the squash until its centers are tender, then fold the cubes into the full cheddar sauce. Thicken the milk and flour before adding cheese off heat; the buttered breadcrumb and Parmesan topping stays separate. Use enough baking dishes for the entire mixture with headroom rather than pressing it into an overfilled dish.

## Directions

1. **Roast squash:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. Wash the whole squash before peeling and cutting. Toss butternut squash, olive oil, salt, and freshly ground black pepper on enough rimmed baking sheets to keep cubes in a single layer. Roast, beginning checks at 25–30 minutes for original-size cubes, until tender in the center and golden at the edges. Lower the oven to 350°F when the squash is done.
2. **Cook pasta:** While the squash roasts, use cavatappi or cellentani pasta, water, and salt in a pot with room to boil and stir. Cook the pasta until just short of al dente, about 2 minutes less than the actual package directions; it continues cooking in the oven. Drain.
3. **Make roux:** Use unsalted butter and all-purpose flour in a heavy pot with enough room for all the milk, cheese, pasta and squash. Melt the butter over medium-low heat; whisk in the flour and cook about 2 minutes until evenly combined and lightly golden.
4. **Thicken milk:** Gradually whisk in all whole milk. Cook over medium heat, stirring and scraping, until smooth and thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.
5. **Melt cheese:** Take the pot off heat. Stir in sharp cheddar, Dijon mustard, nutmeg, cayenne, salt, and freshly ground black pepper, adding the cheese gradually and using only gentle heat if needed to finish melting. Do not keep boiling the sauce after the cheese goes in.
6. **Fill dishes:** Fold all the drained pasta and all the roasted squash into the sauce. Grease enough 350°F-rated baking dishes with additional butter. Divide the entire mixture among them with headroom for bubbling; the full batch must not be forced into a single 2-quart dish.
7. **Add topping:** Toss panko breadcrumbs, unsalted butter, and Parmesan until evenly coated. Divide all the topping over the filled dishes in proportion to their contents.
8. **Bake and stand:** Bake at 350°F, beginning checks around 30 minutes for the all-panko route, until bubbling, golden on top and 165°F / 74°C in the center of every dish. Timing depends on fill depth and starting temperature. Let stand about 5 minutes before serving.
