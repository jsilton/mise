## Chef's Note

Toast the bread until it is crisp enough to support the cream cheese and toppings. Spread the full measured cream cheese over the warm toast, then add cold smoked salmon in loose folds. Capers, red onion, dill and the listed lemon juice finish each portion; assemble just before eating.

## Directions

1. **Toast:** Use cold-smoked salmon labeled ready-to-eat for uncooked serving; follow its refrigeration, opening and use-by instructions. Keep salmon cold while preparing the bread. Soften only the listed cream cheese needed for this batch. Rinse the dill, red onion and lemon under running water before cutting or juicing, and drain well. Toast all the bread until golden and firm enough to support the toppings, without scorching.

2. **Spread:** Divide all the listed cream cheese across the toast, spreading it gently to the edges.

3. **Salmon:** Divide all the salmon across the toast in loose folds. This preparation does not cook the salmon.

4. **Top:** Divide all the drained capers, sliced onion, dill and measured lemon juice over the toast. Add the black-pepper allowance to taste.

5. **Finish:** Use the optional everything-bagel seasoning allowance if desired.

6. **Serve:** Eat promptly after assembly, while the toast is crisp. Larger batches need more toaster cycles; hold the fish and dairy refrigerated rather than assembling early and leaving them warm.
