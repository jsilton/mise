## Chef's Note

This is bagel and lox moved onto toasted sourdough, which is sturdier and tangier than a bagel. Cold-smoked salmon goes on in loose folds over softened cream cheese. Capers, red onion and lemon cut through the richness.

## Directions

1. **Toast the bread:** Toast the bread until deep golden and firm, 3 to 5 minutes. It should not bend when you hold it out flat.
2. **Spread the cream cheese:** Spread the cream cheese over the warm toast, all the way to the edges.
3. **Add the salmon:** Drape the salmon over the cream cheese in loose, ruffled folds.
4. **Top and serve:** Scatter the capers, red onion and dill over the salmon. Drizzle the lemon juice over the top, add pepper and a sprinkle of everything bagel seasoning, and serve right away.
