## Chef's Note

These Chinese-American meatballs carry ginger, garlic and scallion inside and a glossy hoisin and plum glaze outside. Panko soaked in milk keeps them tender. Simmer the glaze only until it coats a spoon, so it stays shiny instead of sticky.

## Directions

1. **Soak the panko:** Heat the oven to 400°F and line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment. In a large bowl, soak the panko in the milk for 5 minutes.
2. **Season the binder:** Stir in the egg, half the garlic, half the ginger, the soy sauce, salt, scallion whites and pepper.
3. **Shape the meatballs:** Add the beef and mix gently with your hands just until combined. Roll into 24 balls about 1 1/2 inches across and set them on the pan with space between them.
4. **Bake:** Bake until browned and 160°F in the center, 15 to 18 minutes.
5. **Make the glaze:** While the meatballs bake, combine the hoisin, plum sauce, vinegar, water and the rest of the garlic and ginger in a wide skillet. Simmer over medium heat, stirring, until the glaze coats a spoon, 4 to 6 minutes. Add a splash of water if it turns pasty.
6. **Glaze and serve:** Add the meatballs and sesame oil and toss over low heat until coated, 1 to 2 minutes. Scatter the scallion greens and sesame seeds over the top.
