## Chef's Note

Hot pan, dry florets and a few minutes without stirring give broccoli dark, nutty spots. A quick splash of water under the lid then softens the stems so every piece is tender inside. Garlic, a little soy and a squeeze of lemon finish it.

## Directions

1. **Sear the broccoli:** Dry the broccoli well. Heat half the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Add half the broccoli, cut sides down, and a pinch of salt. Leave it alone until browned in spots, about 2 minutes, then turn and cook 1 to 2 minutes more.
2. **Steam until tender:** Lower the heat to medium, add half the water and cover. Cook until a knife slides into the thickest stem with a little resistance, 1 to 2 minutes. Uncover, let the water cook off and move the broccoli to a bowl.
3. **Cook the second batch:** Sear and steam the rest of the broccoli the same way with the rest of the oil and water. Add it to the bowl.
4. **Toast the garlic:** Lower the heat to medium-low. Add the garlic and pepper flakes to the oil left in the pan and stir until fragrant and pale gold, about 30 seconds.
5. **Finish and serve:** Return all the broccoli, add the soy sauce and toss until coated and hot. Turn off the heat, add half the lemon juice, then taste and add more. Serve right away.
