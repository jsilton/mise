## Chef's Note

A browned floret can still have a hard stem. Sear first for flavor, then add a measured splash of water and cover briefly for tenderness. Evaporate the water before finishing. Garlic goes in at lower heat so it can become fragrant without burning. This soy-and-lemon home side is distinct from the site's oven-roasted Parmesan broccoli.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Wash and dry broccoli thoroughly. Cut florets about 1 1/2–2 inches wide and slice thick attached stems about 1/4 inch thick. Divide into two batches. Slice garlic and measure water and seasonings.
2. **Brown the first batch:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a 12-inch lidded skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add half the broccoli in a loose layer with cut faces against the pan where possible. Leave about 2 minutes, then turn and cook another 1–2 minutes until several surfaces brown.
3. **Tenderize and dry:** Lower heat to medium, carefully add 2 tbsp water and cover for about 1–2 minutes. Check a thick stem with a knife; it should yield with a little resistance. Add a small splash of water and cover a little longer only if still hard. Uncover and let residual water evaporate, then transfer broccoli to a bowl.
4. **Repeat:** Cook the remaining broccoli with the other tablespoon oil and the remaining 2 tbsp measured water, following steps 2–3. Transfer to the bowl.
5. **Finish gently:** Lower heat to medium-low. Stir garlic and optional pepper flakes in the oil film remaining in the skillet for about 30 seconds, just until fragrant and pale gold. Return all broccoli, add soy sauce, and fold briefly to warm and coat. Turn off heat and add half the lemon juice. Taste, adding the rest only if wanted. Divide into four side portions and serve promptly.
