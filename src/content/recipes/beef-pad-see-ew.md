## Chef's Note

This beef version uses Thai black soy sauce, oyster sauce and a little fish sauce. Loosen the noodles before they reach the wok, cook in manageable batches and use a clean plate for the seared beef.

## Directions

1. **Step 1:** Toss steak with its marinade soy sauce, one teaspoon oil and cornstarch. Mix oyster sauce, sugar, regular soy sauce, Thai black soy sauce, fish sauce and white pepper separately.

2. **Step 2:** Loosen fresh rice noodles according to package instructions just before cooking. If using dried noodles, cook slightly short of package time, drain well and toss with one tablespoon of the measured cooking oil.

3. **Step 3:** Heat one tablespoon cooking oil in a wok. Sear beef in a thin layer, then transfer to a clean plate. Work in batches as needed; keep the remaining noodle ingredients divided proportionately.

4. **Step 4:** Add one tablespoon cooking oil, garlic and broccoli. Stir-fry about two minutes; add a splash of water and cover briefly if stems are still tough. Add noodles, sauce and beef. Toss gently until coated.

5. **Step 5:** Push the mixture aside. Add the remaining cooking oil and eggs. Scramble until set, then fold through the noodles. Continue cooking until everything is hot and the beef reaches 145°F / 63°C; allow the beef three minutes before serving. Cook longer if necessary, using tenderness and doneness rather than the original brief timer.
