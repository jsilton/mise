## Chef's Note

The Gwock family's strawberry congealed salad, a Southern side that doubles as dessert. Strawberries, pineapple, banana and pecans set in Jell-O, with a cool layer of sour cream through the middle. Letting the first layer set firm before the sour cream goes on keeps the layers clean.

## Directions

1. **Dissolve the Jell-O:** In a large bowl, stir strawberry Jell-O and boiling water until no grains remain, about 2 minutes.
2. **Mix in the fruit:** Stir in frozen strawberries, crushed pineapple, medium bananas, and pecans (if using) until everything is evenly mixed.
3. **Set the first layer:** Pour half the mixture into a 9x13-inch dish and refrigerate until firm, 1 to 2 hours. Leave the rest in the bowl on the counter so it stays pourable.
4. **Spread the sour cream:** Spread sour cream in an even layer over the set gelatin.
5. **Add the top layer:** Stir the rest of the gelatin mixture and pour it gently over the sour cream. Refrigerate until set, 1 to 2 hours. Cut into squares and serve cold.

## Suggested Tweak

- Keep the thawed strawberries with all their juice and drain only the pineapple. The juice carries most of the fruit flavor and gives the gelatin a softer set.
- If the reserved mixture starts to set on the counter, set its bowl in warm water and stir until it pours again. Let it cool before it goes over the sour cream.
- Chill the finished salad at least 4 hours, or overnight, for clean squares.
