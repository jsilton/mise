## Chef's Note

This Southern congealed salad keeps two strawberry-fruit layers around a full sour-cream layer. Drained strawberries and pineapple bring bright fruit, mashed bananas give body, and the optional full pecan amount adds crunch. Let the first layer support the cream before finishing the top, and keep the reserved fruit mixture refrigerated between stages.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the dish and fruit:** Make refrigerator space for a 9×13-inch dish at least 2 inches deep with about 3-quart capacity and a covered heatproof bowl for the reserved mixture. Clean the pineapple can lid before opening. Drain frozen strawberries and canned crushed pineapple well, discarding their free liquid; peel and mash medium banana. Keep the fruit and sour cream cold until needed.
2. **Dissolve the gelatin completely:** Put regular strawberry gelatin dessert mix and boiling water in a heatproof bowl. Stir about 2 minutes and continue until all dry grains dissolve. This concentrated family mixture uses the listed boiling water only; do not add the plain-package cold-water amount. Let it lose its steaming heat while it remains fluid.
3. **Combine the complete fruit mixture:** Fold in frozen strawberries, canned crushed pineapple, medium bananas, and pecans (if using), including the full chopped pecan amount if using. Stir until the fruit is evenly distributed. All of this mixture is used across the two gelatin layers.
4. **Chill both halves:** Divide the complete fruit-gelatin mixture into two equal halves, stirring between transfers so the fruit and nuts are shared evenly. Spread one half in the prepared dish, cover, and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below. Cover the entire reserved second half in its separate heatproof bowl and refrigerate it too. Begin checking the first layer around 1–2 hours, continuing until it is firm enough to support the sour-cream layer without flowing.
5. **Add all the sour cream:** Once the bottom layer is firm, spread full-fat sour cream gently and evenly over it, keeping the sour cream in a distinct layer. Return the dish to refrigeration while preparing the reserved mixture.
6. **Keep the reserved half pourable and add it:** Take the reserved mixture from the refrigerator. If it has set, place its bowl in water for an external warm-water bath (if using) and stir gently just until the gelatin becomes pourable; keep bath water out of the bowl and do not boil the mixture. Remove from the bath and let it become no longer warm to the touch while still pourable. Stir to redistribute fruit, then spoon or pour all of it gently over the cold sour-cream layer. If it is already fluid, use it directly. Keep total preparation time out of refrigeration within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C.
7. **Finish setting and serve cold:** Cover and refrigerate promptly. Allow at least 4 more hours or overnight, continuing until the upper layer holds a gentle jiggle without freely flowing liquid and the center supports a cut or spooned portion. Keep refrigerated until serving.

## Cooking Notes

Use canned pineapple and drained fruit as listed. Keep all of the sour cream in the middle layer. For the banana-forward version, use large bananas instead of the displayed medium bananas and half the displayed pecan amount; keep everything else the same. Divide the finished fruit-gelatin mixture equally so both layers receive their share of fruit and nuts.
