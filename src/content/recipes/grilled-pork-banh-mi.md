## Chef's Note

Bánh mì sandwiches bring grilled pork, crisp bread, cool pickled vegetables, cilantro and creamy mayonnaise together. This lemongrass version uses the listed sugar and honey with fish sauce and garlic; a medium grill helps keep the sweet coating from blackening. Drain the pickles for assembly so they brighten the filling without soaking the bread.

## Directions

1. **Marinate the pork cold:** Have pork tenderloin or pork butt, fish sauce, sugar, honey, garlic cloves, and lemongrass stalks ready. Whisk the full fish sauce, sugar, honey, garlic and lemongrass supply together, then toss with all the thinly sliced pork. Cover and refrigerate at 40°F or below for at least 2 hours, or overnight. Keep the entire batch coated and use enough covered food-safe vessels for larger batches.
2. **Prepare the pickles:** If not prepared ahead, make the linked [Pickled Carrots and Daikon](/mise/recipes/quick-pickled-carrots-and-daikon) while the pork marinates: allow about 20 minutes cutting and packing, then at least 1 hour in the refrigerator. Keep them refrigerated until assembly; choose the serving amount separately rather than assuming the full pickle recipe is used.
3. **Grill the thin pork:** Thread the pork onto suitable skewers or use a grill basket, following the equipment’s preparation instructions. Use suitable grilling oil as needed for the cooking surface. Lift the pork from the marinade and wipe away excess; discard the raw-contact marinade. Grill uncrowded slices over medium heat, starting checks around 3–4 minutes per side, until browned without blackening and at least 145°F in the thickest part, measured from the side with a thermometer suitable for thin food. Pork butt and tenderloin can differ in chewing texture; neither is guaranteed finished by the clock. Keep waiting raw pork refrigerated and add grill loads as needed.
4. **Rest cooked pork:** Transfer fully cooked pork to a clean platter and rest at least 3 minutes before assembly. Use clean utensils and remove any skewers before eating.
5. **Assemble and serve:** Use crusty baguettes, [Pickled Carrots and Daikon](/mise/recipes/quick-pickled-carrots-and-daikon), fresh cilantro, jalapeños, cucumber spears, mayonnaise, and liquid seasoning or soy sauce. Split the measured bread, spread generously with mayonnaise and add a few drops of liquid seasoning or soy sauce. Divide all the cooked pork among the serving lengths; layer cucumber, drained chilled pickles and cilantro, then jalapeños if wanted. Serve promptly. Four portions is the current original-batch plan, not a measured baguette-size yield.
