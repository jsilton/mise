## Chef's Note

Dal tadka is everyday Indian comfort food: soft, creamy lentils finished with a tadka, whole spices crackled in hot ghee with garlic and onion. Pour the tadka over the dal right before serving. The sizzle and the smell of toasted cumin and mustard seeds are the best part.

## Directions

1. **Simmer the dal:** Combine the dal, water, turmeric and salt in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then lower to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered, skimming off the foam, until the lentils are completely soft and starting to break down, 20 to 25 minutes. Add a splash of hot water if it gets too thick.
2. **Add the tomato:** Stir in the tomato and green chile and simmer until the tomato softens, about 5 minutes. Mash some of the lentils with a whisk so the dal is creamy with some whole lentils left.
3. **Cook the onion:** While the dal simmers, heat two-thirds of the ghee in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion, stirring, until soft and light gold, 7 to 10 minutes. Push it to one side of the pan.
4. **Make the tadka:** Add the rest of the ghee to the empty side of the pan, then the mustard and cumin seeds. When they pop, add the dried chiles and curry leaves, which will splatter, then the garlic. Stir until the garlic is pale gold, 30 to 60 seconds. Stir in the onion, turn off the heat and stir in the asafoetida and chile powder.
5. **Finish:** Pour the hot tadka over the dal and stir it in. Add the lemon juice, taste for salt and scatter with cilantro.
