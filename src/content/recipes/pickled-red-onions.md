## Chef's Note

Pickled red onions turn bright pink in half an hour and wake up tacos, bowls and sandwiches. Slice the onion as thin as you can so the hot brine mellows its bite quickly. They are good the same day and even better the next.

## Directions

1. **Pack the jar:** Pack the onion into a heatproof pint jar.
2. **Heat the brine:** Combine the vinegar, water, sugar and salt in a small saucepan over medium heat. Stir until the sugar and salt dissolve and the brine simmers, about 2 minutes.
3. **Cover the onion:** Pour the hot brine over the onion and press it down with a spoon so every slice is under the liquid.
4. **Rest and chill:** Let stand until the onion turns pink and the jar is cool, about 30 minutes. Cover and refrigerate.
