## Chef's Note

Oven wings made the way J. Kenji López-Alt taught: baking powder and an overnight dry in the fridge give the skin a blistered crunch with no frying. A quick toss in lemon zest butter with coarse black pepper finishes them, light enough that the skin stays crisp.

## Directions

1. **Season the wings:** Pat the wings dry. Toss them in a large bowl with the baking powder and salt until evenly coated, with no clumps.
2. **Dry overnight:** Lightly oil two wire racks and set them in rimmed sheet pans. Spread the wings on the racks with space between pieces. Refrigerate uncovered for 8 to 24 hours.
3. **Roast the wings:** Heat the oven to 425°F with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Roast the wings for 25 minutes, then flip them and swap the pans. Roast until the skin is deep golden and crisp and the thickest pieces reach 175°F, 20 to 30 minutes more.
4. **Make the lemon pepper butter:** While the wings finish, melt the butter in a small saucepan over low heat. Off the heat, stir in the zest, lemon juice, pepper, garlic powder and the remaining salt. Taste for salt.
5. **Toss and serve:** Toss the hot wings with the butter in a large bowl until lightly coated. Scatter with parsley and serve right away with the lemon wedges.
