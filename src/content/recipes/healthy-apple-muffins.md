## Chef's Note

Whole wheat apple muffins sweetened with maple syrup and kept moist with applesauce and Greek yogurt. Grated apple melts into the crumb while small diced pieces stay tender and distinct. A sprinkle of turbinado sugar gives the tops a crunchy crust.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.
2. **Mix the dry ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon and salt. Add the grated and diced apple and toss to coat.
3. **Mix the wet ingredients:** In another bowl, whisk the oil and maple syrup. Whisk in the eggs, yogurt, applesauce and vanilla until smooth.
4. **Combine:** Pour the wet mixture into the dry and stir with a spatula just until no dry flour remains. The batter will be thick.
5. **Bake:** Divide the batter among the cups and sprinkle the tops with the turbinado sugar. Bake until golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean, 13 to 16 minutes.
6. **Cool:** Cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then move the muffins to a rack.
