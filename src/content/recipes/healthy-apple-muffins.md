## Chef's Note

Grated apple spreads through the batter while the small diced pieces remain distinct. Applesauce and Greek yogurt stay alongside the full oil and maple syrup amounts. Mix until the dry ingredients are incorporated without extended beating, and distribute the whole turbinado sugar supply over the tops.

## Directions

1. **Prepare and dry:** Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line enough standard muffin cups; the original batch plans 12. Wash apples before grating or dicing and measure the prepared amounts. Whisk all the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon and salt together.
2. **Apples:** Add all the listed grated and diced apples to the dry mixture and stir to distribute them evenly.
3. **Wet:** Whisk all the oil and maple syrup together, then whisk in all the eggs, yogurt, applesauce and vanilla until evenly mixed.
4. **Combine:** Stir the complete wet mixture into the complete dry mixture only until incorporated. Small lumps are fine, but scrape the bottom and remove dry flour pockets; flour streaks are not required. Do not taste raw batter.
5. **Bake:** Divide all thick batter evenly among enough lined cups, leaving expansion room and maintaining the original cup size and similar fill depth. Sprinkle all the turbinado sugar over the tops. Bake about 13–16 minutes as the original-batch first check, until golden with set centers and a toothpick coming out clean. Extra tins or batches add time.
6. **Cool:** Cool in the pan about 5 minutes before carefully transferring to a rack to finish cooling; do not package warm muffins.
