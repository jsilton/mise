## Chef's Note

This is the family pizza: two pies with pepperoni, sweet pineapple and spinach over plenty of mozzarella. Salty, sweet and fresh all land in one bite. The spinach goes on at the very end so it just wilts.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 500°F with a rack in the middle.
2. **Stretch the dough:** On a lightly floured counter, stretch balls of pizza dough into 12-inch rounds with slightly thicker rims. If the dough springs back, cover it and let it rest 10 minutes. Set each round on its own lightly oiled pizza pan.
3. **Sauce the pizzas:** Spread pizza sauce over each round, leaving a 3/4-inch border.
4. **Add the toppings:** Scatter shredded mozzarella, pepperoni slices, and pineapple over the sauce.
5. **Bake:** Bake one pizza at a time until the rim is golden and the cheese bubbles, 10 to 13 minutes. Scatter spinach over the top and bake until it wilts and the bottom is browned, 1 to 2 minutes more.
6. **Rest and serve:** Let the pizza rest on a rack for 2 minutes, then slice and serve. Bake the second pizza the same way.

## Suggested Tweak

The family list gives no amounts. These measured amounts make two well-topped 12-inch pizzas: 1 1/4 lb (600 g) pizza dough in two balls, 1 cup thick pizza sauce, 12 oz whole-milk low-moisture mozzarella, 2 oz pepperoni, 3/4 cup fresh pineapple and 1 1/2 cups baby spinach. Split the toppings evenly between the pies, and blot the pineapple dry so the center bakes up crisp.
