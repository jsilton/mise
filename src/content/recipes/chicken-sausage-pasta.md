## Chef's Note

Brown the sausage, soften the pepper and reduce the tomatoes before adding the pasta. Wilt the spinach in the sauce first so the noodles do not wait for it to collapse. Chicken sausages vary: slice fully cooked links, or remove raw sausage from its casings and cook it as crumbles.

## Directions

1. **Prepare and heat water:** Read the sausage label: slice fully cooked links into rounds about 1/2 inch thick, or remove casings from raw links. Slice pepper, mince garlic and grate Parmesan. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and season moderately with salt.
2. **Brown sausage:** Heat olive oil in a large deep skillet over medium to medium-high. For fully cooked links, add the slices and cook about **5–7 minutes**, turning, until browned in patches. For raw sausage, add the meat and break it into small crumbles; cook about **8–12 minutes**, stirring, until representative thick pieces reach **165°F / 74°C**. If crowded, brown in rounds with the oil divided between them. Transfer to a clean plate.
3. **Build sauce:** Add pepper to the same pan and cook about **4–6 minutes** until starting to soften. Stir in garlic and dried basil for **30–45 seconds**. Add wine, scrape up browned bits and simmer about **2 minutes**. Add all the listed tomatoes and their juices. Simmer uncovered about **10–15 minutes**, stirring, until lightly thickened and pepper is tender.
4. **Time the pasta:** During the tomato simmer, cook pasta according to its package, tasting near the end. Aim for tender with a little bite; a brief sauce toss follows. Reserve **the listed cooking water** before draining without rinsing.
5. **Wilt and combine:** Return sausage to the sauce and add spinach in handfuls, folding until wilted, about **2–3 minutes**. Heat the sausage throughout to **165°F / 74°C**, then add pasta. Toss over low heat for about **1 minute**, adding reserved water in small splashes only if needed for a coating sauce. Use the pasta pot for this stage if the skillet is too full.
6. **Finish:** Remove from heat and fold in Parmesan. Add black pepper and taste before adding salt; sausage and cheese already contribute plenty. Divide into the intended portions and serve promptly.
