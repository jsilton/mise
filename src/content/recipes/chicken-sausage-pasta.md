## Chef's Note

This is a weeknight pasta of browned chicken sausage, sweet pepper and spinach in a red wine tomato sauce. Brown the sausage well, because its crust and the browned bits in the pan build the flavor of the sauce. A splash of starchy pasta water at the end helps the sauce cling to every strand.

## Directions

1. **Boil the water:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.
2. **Brown the sausage:** Heat the oil in a large, deep skillet over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and cook, turning now and then, until browned in patches, 5 to 7 minutes. Move it to a plate.
3. **Soften the pepper:** Add the bell pepper to the same pan and cook, stirring, until it starts to soften, 4 to 6 minutes. Stir in the garlic and basil and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
4. **Reduce the wine:** Pour in the wine and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer until it has mostly cooked away, about 2 minutes.
5. **Simmer the sauce:** Add the tomatoes and their juices. Simmer uncovered, stirring now and then, until the sauce thickens and the pepper is tender, 10 to 15 minutes.
6. **Cook the pasta:** While the sauce simmers, cook the pasta in the boiling water until al dente. Scoop out a mug of the cooking water, then drain the pasta.
7. **Wilt the spinach:** Return the sausage to the sauce. Add the spinach a handful at a time, folding until it wilts, 2 to 3 minutes.
8. **Toss and finish:** Add the pasta and toss over low heat for 1 minute, adding splashes of pasta water until the sauce coats the strands. Take the pan off the heat, fold in the Parmesan and season with black pepper. Taste before adding salt.
