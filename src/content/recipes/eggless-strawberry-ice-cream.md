## Chef's Note

This eggless formula keeps the full milk and cream, the listed sugar amount and all the lemon juice. Dissolve the sugar and chill the mixture thoroughly before churning. Prepare the machine’s freezing bowl in advance when required; a cold mixture does not compensate for an unfrozen bowl.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Prepare and freeze the ice-cream maker bowl in advance if its actual instructions require it. Use pasteurized milk and cream. Wash and hull the fresh strawberries, puree them and then measure the full listed puree amount; no whole-fruit weight is implied. Wash lemons before juicing if using fresh juice.
2. **Mix:** Whisk all the milk, cream, sugar, salt and vanilla until sugar dissolves, then stir in all the measured strawberry puree and lemon juice.
3. **Chill:** Cover and refrigerate until thoroughly cold, about 1–2 hours as a planning reference if the ingredients were already chilled.
4. **Churn:** Follow the actual ice-cream maker’s instructions and maximum liquid fill. Split the fully mixed cold base among batches if it exceeds that limit, keeping unused base refrigerated and preparing the bowl again if required. Churn to the machine’s soft-ice-cream consistency.
5. **Serve or firm:** Serve soft, or transfer to covered freezer-safe containers with expansion room for about 2–3 hours or longer until firm. Keep frozen between servings.
