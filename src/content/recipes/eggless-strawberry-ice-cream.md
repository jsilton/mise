## Chef's Note

A Philadelphia-style ice cream, made without eggs or cooking, so the fresh strawberry flavor comes through bright and clean. Plenty of sugar keeps it scoopable, and a little lemon juice sharpens the berries. Freeze the machine's bowl a day ahead.

## Directions

1. **Mix the base:** In a large bowl, whisk the milk, cream, sugar and salt until the sugar dissolves and the mixture no longer feels gritty. Stir in the strawberry puree, lemon juice and vanilla.
2. **Chill:** Cover and refrigerate until very cold, 1 to 2 hours.
3. **Churn:** Churn in an ice cream maker until it is as thick as soft serve, 20 to 25 minutes. If the base fills the machine more than two-thirds full, churn it in two batches.
4. **Freeze until firm:** Scrape into a freezer container, cover and freeze until scoopable, 2 to 3 hours.
