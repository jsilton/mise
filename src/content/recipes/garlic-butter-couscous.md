## Chef's Note

Pearl couscous needs time in simmering liquid, while fine instant couscous generally hydrates off the heat. A short butter toast gives this version a little color before the garlic goes in. Finish with parsley and optional zest after cooking so their flavor remains clear beside a saucy main.

## Directions

1. **Toast gently:** Melt 1 tbsp butter in a 2-quart saucepan over medium. Add pearl couscous and stir 2–3 minutes until a few pearls turn golden. Reduce heat if the butter darkens quickly.
2. **Add garlic and broth:** Stir garlic for about 30 seconds, then carefully pour in the measured broth. Add black pepper. Bring to a simmer; hold the salt until you know how salty the broth tastes after cooking.
3. **Cover and cook:** Reduce heat to low, cover and simmer about 10–15 minutes, or according to the package’s absorption method, until tender with slight chew. Check near the end. If dry and firm, add hot water 2 tbsp at a time; if tender with excess liquid, uncover briefly to reduce it without overcooking.
4. **Rest and finish:** Take off heat and leave covered 5 minutes. Fluff gently, fold in the remaining tablespoon butter, parsley and optional zest. Taste and add the measured salt only if needed.
5. **Portion:** Divide into four normal sides or use the smaller portion specified by a composed meal. This recipe already contains its finishing butter; add no extra oil automatically at the table.
