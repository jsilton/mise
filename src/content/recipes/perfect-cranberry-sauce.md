## Chef's Note

Homemade whole-berry cranberry sauce takes 20 minutes and beats the can every time. Most of the berries cook down into a glossy sauce, and a last handful goes in near the end so you still get tender whole berries. A strip of orange peel adds fragrance without hiding the tart fruit.

## Directions

1. **Dissolve the sugar:** Set aside about a quarter of the cranberries. Combine the water, sugar, orange peel and salt in a 2-quart saucepan over medium heat. Stir until the sugar dissolves and the liquid simmers.
2. **Cook the berries:** Add the rest of the cranberries and bring back to a boil, then lower the heat to a steady simmer. Cook, stirring now and then, until most of the berries have burst, 8 to 10 minutes.
3. **Add the last berries:** Stir in the reserved cranberries and simmer until they are tender when pressed with a spoon but still mostly whole, 3 to 5 minutes. The sauce should still be loose. It thickens as it cools. Remove the orange peel.
4. **Chill:** Pour the sauce into a shallow container and refrigerate until cold and spoonable, about 2 1/2 hours or overnight. Stir before serving.
