## Chef's Note

This is Craig Plummer's weeknight chicken, baked under a can of cream soup in a foil-covered dish. The small hole in the foil lets steam escape, so the chicken braises gently in the soup without turning watery. A short, hotter finish with the foil off browns the top.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Coat a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray.
2. **Arrange the chicken:** Lay the bone-in, skin-on chicken pieces in the dish skin side up in a single layer.
3. **Season the chicken:** Sprinkle salt and black pepper lightly over the chicken.
4. **Spoon on the soup:** Spoon the condensed cream of chicken or cream of mushroom soup over the chicken and spread it to cover the tops of the pieces.
5. **Bake covered:** Cover the dish tightly with foil and poke a small hole in the center of the foil to let steam escape. Bake for 1 hour.
6. **Brown the top:** Raise the oven to 375°F and take off the foil. Bake until the top is browned in spots and the thickest piece reads 165°F, about 10 minutes.
7. **Serve with the sauce:** Stir the sauce in the dish until smooth and spoon it over the chicken.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Whisk 1/4 cup milk or dry white wine into the soup before spooning it on. The sauce comes out pourable instead of thick.
- Use thighs and drumsticks. They stay juicier through the long covered bake than breasts do.
- Brown the chicken skin side down in a skillet for 5 minutes before it goes into the dish for a deeper color and flavor.
