## Chef's Note

Brown butter gives this French-style chocolate soufflé a toasted crust around its soft center. Beaten egg whites supply the bubbles that expand in the oven while the egg proteins set around them. Loosen the chocolate with a little meringue before folding in the rest, and bring the dish to the table as soon as it is baked.

## Directions

1. **Separate the eggs and heat the oven:** Separate large eggs, reserving all the whites in a clean, grease-free mixer bowl and all the yolks in a second bowl. Keep any yolk out of the whites. Set an oven rack in the middle and heat the oven to 375°F / 190°C. Have the remaining ingredients measured and plates ready before you begin mixing.
2. **Brown and cool the butter:** Melt unsalted butter in a small skillet over medium heat, swirling until the milk solids turn brown and smell nutty, about 4 minutes. Transfer immediately to a small heatproof bowl. Refrigerate for about 20 minutes, stirring halfway through, until opaque but soft enough to brush. Prepare the chocolate base while it chills.
3. **Make the ganache:** Use bittersweet chocolate, 65–70% cacao and heavy cream for the ganache. Put the chopped chocolate in a large heatproof bowl with room for folding later. Heat the cream in a small saucepan until steaming, about 2 minutes, then pour it over the chocolate. Stir occasionally for about 5 minutes, until smooth and slightly cooled.
4. **Finish the chocolate base:** Whisk Dutch-process cocoa powder, dark rum (if using), Morton kosher salt, and hot water into the ganache. Whisk in all the reserved yolks just until smooth; the base should be warm, not steaming hot.
5. **Whip the whites:** When the butter has chilled for about 10 minutes, add the meringue’s granulated sugar and cream of tartar to the reserved whites. Beat at medium-high speed until glossy medium peaks form, about 8–10 minutes. Lift the whisk to check: the peaks should hold but their tips should bend. Stop before the whites turn dry or clumpy.
6. **Coat the dish:** Brush the bottom and sides of the 1 1/2-quart soufflé dish generously with all the cooled, browned unsalted butter, including the browned solids, right up to the rim. Add the coating’s granulated sugar, turn the dish to distribute it, and tap out the excess. Refrigerate the coated dish while you fold the batter. If using a stand mixer, you can coat the dish as the whites finish whipping, watching their texture.
7. **Lighten, then fold:** Stir about one quarter of the meringue into the chocolate base until fully blended. Add the remaining meringue in three batches. For each, sweep a flexible spatula down through the middle and along the bottom, then lift the batter over itself as you turn the bowl. Stop once the batter is mostly uniform with no large white pockets; a few fine streaks are preferable to prolonged mixing. Scrape into the prepared dish and bake promptly.
8. **Bake:** Bake at 375°F / 190°C for about 35–40 minutes. Look for a risen top with a slightly darker, just-set surface and a gentle wobble in the center. Check near the end of baking that the center reaches at least 160°F / 71°C, without touching the dish with the probe. A liquid center needs more time; the clock and surface color alone do not establish doneness.
9. **Serve immediately:** Finish with powdered sugar and heavy cream: dust the top with the sugar, then spoon into dessert portions and pass the extra cream for drizzling. Reach through the airy top to the soft center. Some settling as you serve is normal.
