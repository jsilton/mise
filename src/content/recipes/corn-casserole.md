## Chef's Note

Glenna Johnson’s corn casserole combines cream-style corn, drained whole kernels and the full butter and sour cream. Let the melted butter cool until lukewarm before whisking it into the eggs. Fold the muffin mix in just until combined, then check the center as well as the golden edges. Cheddar and green chiles are optional additions.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9×13-inch oven-safe dish for the original full-can batch with the separate butter allowance. Check its actual depth and leave headroom. A 2-quart dish is another current option only when its actual load leaves adequate headroom; divide excess batter among additional greased dishes rather than assuming it all fits. Scaling uses separate dishes or an appropriate smaller dish, with similar batter depth.
2. **Wet mixture:** Whisk all the lukewarm melted butter, beaten eggs and sour cream together until smooth.
3. **Add corn:** Stir in all the cream-style corn and drained whole-kernel corn. Fold in all the listed optional green chiles if using them.
4. **Fold:** Sprinkle all the muffin mix over the wet mixture and fold just until combined.
5. **Fill:** Divide all the batter among the prepared dishes. If using the optional cheddar, divide its full listed amount over their tops.
6. **Bake and rest:** Bake for about 35–45 minutes, beginning checks at 35 minutes, until the center is set, the edges are golden and the casserole center reaches 165°F in each dish. A deeper load can need longer; tent a browning top if its center is not ready. Rest for 5 minutes before serving the listed planning portions.
