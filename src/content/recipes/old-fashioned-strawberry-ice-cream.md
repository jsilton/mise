## Chef's Note

This is old-fashioned strawberry ice cream on a rich vanilla bean custard. Macerating the berries in sugar and lemon draws out a bright syrup. Pureeing most of them into the base carries that flavor through every bite, and a few small chopped pieces stay soft instead of freezing into icy chunks.

## Directions

1. **Macerate the berries:** Toss the strawberries with the lemon juice and the sugar for the berries. Cover and refrigerate until syrupy, at least 3 hours.
2. **Heat the dairy:** In a saucepan over medium heat, warm the milk, cream, vanilla seeds and pod until steaming and just starting to simmer.
3. **Temper the yolks:** In a bowl, whisk the yolks, the custard sugar, the brown sugar and the salt until pale. Slowly whisk in a ladle of the hot dairy, then pour the yolk mixture back into the pan.
4. **Cook the custard:** Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly and scraping the bottom, until the custard coats the back of a spoon and reaches 170°F, 5 to 8 minutes. Don't let it boil.
5. **Chill the custard:** Pour the custard into a bowl set over ice water and stir until cool. Cover and refrigerate until very cold, at least 4 hours. Remove the vanilla pod.
6. **Puree the berries:** Blend two-thirds of the strawberries with all their juice until smooth. Chop the rest into small pieces.
7. **Churn:** Stir the puree into the cold custard. Churn in an ice cream maker until it looks like soft-serve, 25 to 35 minutes. Add the chopped berries in the last minute of churning.
8. **Freeze:** Pack the ice cream into a covered container and freeze until firm, at least 2 hours.
