## Chef's Note

Oats soften by absorbing milk in the refrigerator; yogurt adds body and tang, and chia thickens the surrounding liquid. In the original one-portion batch, the base uses equal ½-cup volumes of oats and milk plus ¼ cup yogurt. Rolled oats stay chewier; quick oats soften more, and steel-cut oats need a different preparation.

## Directions

1. **Mix:** Stir old-fashioned rolled oats, milk of choice, plain Greek yogurt, chia seeds, maple syrup or honey, vanilla extract, and kosher salt in a clean container with room for the whole mixture and later stirring. Distribute the chia throughout rather than leaving it in a clump on top.
2. **Refrigerate:** Cover and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below for at least 4 hours, or overnight. This is soaking time; keep the mixture refrigerated rather than soaking on the counter.
3. **Check texture:** Stir again. Use additional milk (if using) and additional rolled oats (if using) only if needed: milk loosens a thick mixture; a spoonful of oats can thicken a loose one. After adding oats, return to the refrigerator for about 10 minutes, then check again; more hydration time may be needed.
4. **Top and serve:** Add fresh fruit, nut butter, granola, toasted coconut or nuts (if using). Wash fresh produce before cutting. Serve cold, or transfer to a microwave-safe container and warm according to the appliance instructions, stirring and checking for even heating; do not microwave a sealed jar. Reheating time depends on the portion and appliance.

## Cooking Notes

### Flavor ideas for this base

These amounts describe the original one-portion batch; scale additions with the base. PB&J uses 1 tbsp peanut butter and unmeasured smashed raspberries. Banana Nut uses ½ mashed banana, with walnuts and a honey drizzle to taste. Berry uses mixed berries and granola to taste. Carrot Cake uses ¼ cup shredded carrot, a handful of raisins and a pinch of cinnamon, with walnuts and a maple drizzle to taste. Tropical replaces all the listed milk with coconut milk, then adds mango, toasted coconut and macadamia nuts to taste.

### Separate flavor set

For the original one-portion batch, use a base of ½ cup rolled oats, ½ cup dairy or dairy-free milk, ¼ cup Greek or dairy-free yogurt, 1 tbsp chia and 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup, without the main recipe’s base vanilla or salt. Choose one addition set below and scale the entire base and its additions together. Stir everything together, refrigerate at least 2 hours, preferably 8 hours, then stir and check that the oats have softened. The shorter soak may leave more chew.

- Banana Bread: ½ mashed banana, 2 tbsp chopped walnuts, ½ tsp vanilla, ½ tsp cinnamon and a pinch of ground flaxseed.
- Spiced Pear: ½ diced pear, 1 tbsp chopped pecans, ½ tsp cinnamon and a pinch of nutmeg.
- PB&J: 2 tbsp raspberry jam or purée, 1 tbsp peanut or almond butter and 1 tsp chopped pistachios.
- Piña Colada: replace the full milk amount with coconut milk; add ¼ cup small-diced pineapple, 1 tbsp shredded coconut and ¼ tsp vanilla.
- Carrot Cake: ¼ cup shredded carrot, 1 tbsp shredded coconut, 1 tbsp raisins, ½ tsp vanilla and ½ tsp cinnamon.
- Strawberry Protein: ¼ cup small-diced strawberry, 1 scoop protein or collagen powder, 1 tbsp sliced almonds and ½ tsp vanilla. Use a product suitable for consumption without further cooking, follow its scoop definition and expect its absorption to affect texture; no universal scoop weight is implied.

For a larger bowl batch of this separate version, Lisa Bryan suggests scooping about 1 cup of the chilled, thickened oats for a serving; choose the portion that suits you.

For a dairy-free main base, replace the complete milk and yogurt amounts with dairy-free versions. A vegan version also uses maple instead of honey and suitable toppings. Gluten-free preparation requires certified gluten-free oats and checking all chosen additions.
