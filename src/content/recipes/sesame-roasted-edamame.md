## Chef's Note

Shelled edamame and pods have different yields and roasting behavior. This recipe uses edible beans, with a drier surface and some browned spots after roasting. It does not promise the hard crunch or storage life of packaged dry-roasted edamame. Dry the prepared beans well, spread them out, and add toasted sesame oil at the finish for aroma.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the beans:** Heat the oven to 375°F / 190°C. Read the edamame package. If it requires cooking, cook as directed before roasting; drain afterward. If it is explicitly ready to eat, thaw as directed or overnight in the refrigerator and drain. Do not assume every frozen product has the same preparation status.
2. **Dry and season:** Spread beans on a clean towel, blot thoroughly and let steam escape briefly if hot. Toss with neutral oil, soy sauce and garlic powder. Spread in a single layer on a parchment-lined rimmed half-sheet pan.
3. **Roast and check:** Roast about 30–40 minutes, stirring after 15 minutes and checking regularly thereafter. Stop when the surface is drier with light brown patches and a cooled sample bean has a pleasant firm center. Some edges may crisp; do not keep baking solely to make every bean hard and crunchy.
4. **Finish:** Toss warm beans with toasted sesame oil, toasted sesame seeds and optional pepper flakes. Taste and add the measured salt only if needed. Divide into four side portions and serve soon. Refrigerate leftovers promptly rather than leaving them out as a pantry snack.
