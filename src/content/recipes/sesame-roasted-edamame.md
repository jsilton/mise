## Chef's Note

Roasting turns plain edamame into a snack you can eat by the handful. The beans dry out and brown in spots, and a last toss with toasted sesame oil and seeds makes them taste nutty and rich. Dry the beans well before they go in the oven, since wet beans steam instead of browning.

## Directions

1. **Dry the beans:** Heat the oven to 375°F and line a rimmed half-sheet pan with parchment. Spread the thawed edamame on a kitchen towel and blot them dry.
2. **Season:** Toss the edamame with the neutral oil, soy sauce and garlic powder on the pan. Spread them out in one layer.
3. **Roast:** Roast, stirring after 15 minutes, until the beans look dry and have light brown patches, 30 to 40 minutes. Stop while the centers are still pleasantly firm, not hard.
4. **Finish:** Toss the hot beans with the sesame oil, sesame seeds and red pepper flakes. Taste, add the salt if they need it and serve warm.
