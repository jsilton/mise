## Chef's Note

These Greek-style lamb burgers from Food & Wine are packed with mint and parsley and tucked into warm pita with a cool cucumber-mint yogurt. Grate the onion or mince it very fine so it melts into the meat, and mix gently so the patties stay tender.

## Directions

1. **Make the sauce:** Make the mint tzatziki and refrigerate it while you make the burgers. Heat the oven to 350°F for the pitas.
2. **Shape the patties:** Put the lamb, onion, garlic, mint, parsley, salt and pepper in a large bowl. Mix gently with your hands just until combined, then shape into four ovals about 3/4 inch thick.
3. **Brown the first patties:** Heat half the oil in a 12-inch heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Add two patties and cook until well browned underneath, 4 to 5 minutes. Flip, lower the heat to medium and cook until the centers reach 160°F, 4 to 5 minutes more. Move them to a plate.
4. **Cook the rest:** Spoon off most of the fat, add the rest of the oil and cook the other two patties the same way. While they cook, wrap the pitas in foil and warm them in the oven for 10 minutes.
5. **Assemble:** Spread some tzatziki in each warm pita. Add a patty and top with the feta, tomato and greens. Fold and serve with the rest of the sauce at the table.
