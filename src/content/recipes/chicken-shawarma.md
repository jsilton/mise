## Chef's Note

Shawarma is meat roasted on a vertical spit and shaved off in thin, crisp-edged slices, sold on street corners across the Middle East. At home, a hot oven and a quick sear in a skillet give the same tender meat and browned edges. Cumin, coriander, turmeric and a little cinnamon bring the warm aroma, and a long marinade carries it deep into the chicken.

## Directions

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Whisk most of the oil with the lemon juice, garlic, spices, salt and pepper, keeping a little oil back for the skillet. Toss the chicken in the marinade, cover and refrigerate for 4 to 24 hours.
2. **Roast the chicken:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Lay the thighs flat with space between them on a foil-lined rimmed sheet pan. Roast until the thickest part of each thigh reaches 165°F, 20 to 25 minutes. While it roasts, make the tahini sauce.
3. **Rest and slice:** Let the chicken rest on a board for 5 minutes, then slice it into strips 1/2 inch wide.
4. **Brown the edges:** Heat half the remaining oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add half the chicken in one layer and leave it until the edges brown, 1 to 2 minutes, then toss and move it to a platter. Repeat with the rest of the oil and chicken.
5. **Serve:** Drizzle the chicken with the tahini sauce, or pass it at the table.
