## Chef's Note

Shawarma is cooked on a rotating spit. This home adaptation uses a hot oven and a short skillet finish to create its own tender-meat and browned-edge contrast. Cumin, coriander, turmeric, and a restrained amount of cinnamon provide warm seasoning. It is a practical shawarma-style roast, with a different cooking process from spit-roasted meat.

## Directions

1. **Marinate:** Reserve 2 tsp olive oil for finishing. Mix the remaining oil with lemon juice, garlic, ground spices, salt, and pepper. Coat chicken, cover, and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below for 4–24 hours.
2. **Roast and check:** Heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C. Arrange chicken opened flat with gaps on a foil-lined rimmed sheet pan. Roast about 20–25 minutes, checking every thigh at its thickest part for 165°F / 74°C. Continue as needed. Meanwhile, prepare the tahini sauce and measure out 1/2 cup for this meal.
3. **Rest and slice:** Transfer cooked chicken to a clean board, rest for 5 minutes, and slice across the meat into strips about 1/2 inch wide.
4. **Brown briefly:** Heat half the reserved oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add half the sliced chicken in one layer and leave briefly until some edges brown, about 1–2 minutes, then toss and remove. Repeat with remaining oil and chicken. Stop at browned edges; do not blacken the spices or dry all the strips.
5. **Serve:** Divide chicken among four plates and drizzle each with 2 tbsp tahini sauce, or offer the sauce separately. Add pita and a fresh salad if desired; these are additional components.
