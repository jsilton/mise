## Chef's Note

A home version of the Starbucks sous-vide egg bites. Blending the eggs with cottage cheese makes a smooth, rich custard, and a low oven with a pan of steam underneath bakes them velvety and pale instead of browned and spongy.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Set one rack in the center and one below it, with a roasting pan on the lower rack. Heat the oven to 300°F and bring a kettle of water to a boil.
2. **Blend the eggs:** Blend the eggs, cottage cheese, salt and pepper until completely smooth, about 30 seconds. Pour into a bowl and fold in the Gruyère.
3. **Fill the tin:** Grease a 12-cup muffin tin well. Fill each cup about two-thirds full, then press the bacon gently into the tops.
4. **Bake in steam:** Cover the tin tightly with foil and set it on the center rack. Pour the boiling water into the roasting pan below. Bake until the centers are set and jiggle only slightly, 30 to 40 minutes.
5. **Rest and unmold:** Let the bites rest in the tin for 10 minutes, then loosen them with a small spoon and lift them out. Serve warm.
