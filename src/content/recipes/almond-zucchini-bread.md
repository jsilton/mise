## Chef's Note

Gina Matsoukas's grain-free loaf from Running to the Kitchen is sweetened with ripe banana and a little maple, and almond flour keeps the crumb tender and rich. Squeezing the zucchini dry is what keeps it from turning gummy.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Line a loaf pan with parchment, leaving some overhang on the long sides.
2. **Whisk the dry ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the almond flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon.
3. **Whisk the wet ingredients:** In another bowl, whisk the eggs, maple syrup, banana, coconut oil and vinegar or lemon juice until smooth.
4. **Make the batter:** Fold the wet ingredients into the dry until no dry pockets remain, then stir in the zucchini.
5. **Bake:** Scrape the batter into the pan and smooth the top. Bake until the top is deep golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean, 35 to 40 minutes. If the top darkens before the center sets, tent it loosely with foil.
6. **Cool:** Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then lift the loaf out by the parchment. Cool completely on a rack before slicing, about 1 hour.
