## Chef's Note

Braised brisket is the centerpiece of the Jewish holiday table at Rosh Hashanah and Passover. This is Ina Garten's version, slow-cooked in tomato juice under a tight seal with carrots, celery and six onions that melt into the sauce. Slice it across the grain so every piece is tender.

## Directions

1. **Season the brisket:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Mix the salt, pepper, garlic and oregano and rub it over every side of the brisket.
2. **Fill the pan:** Lay the brisket in a heavy roasting pan. Pile the carrots, celery, onions and bay leaves on and around it.
3. **Seal the pan:** Pour the tomato juice over everything. It should come about three quarters of the way up the meat. Cover with two sheets of parchment, then seal the pan tightly with heavy-duty foil.
4. **Braise:** Bake until a fork slides into the thickest part of the brisket with little resistance, about 3 1/2 hours. If it still feels firm, reseal the pan and bake 15 to 30 minutes more.
5. **Reduce the sauce:** Move the brisket to a cutting board and tent it with foil. Pull out the bay leaves. Set the roasting pan across two burners, or pour everything into a wide pot, and boil over medium-high heat, stirring now and then, until the sauce is glossy and thick, 20 to 30 minutes.
6. **Slice and serve:** Slice the brisket across the grain. Serve it with the vegetables and plenty of sauce.
