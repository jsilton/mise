## Chef's Note

These are little baked bites of quinoa, broccoli and sharp cheddar, held together with egg, from Everyday Dishes. Packed with vegetables and easy to pick up, they are a good lunchbox side. Press each scoop firmly so the cheese and egg bind the grains, and let them rest before lifting so they hold their shape.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the pan:** Heat the oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment and coat it lightly with oil spray.
2. **Mix:** In a large bowl, toss the quinoa, broccoli, cheddar and green onions.
3. **Bind:** Stir in the eggs, Parmesan, garlic powder, salt and pepper until every grain is coated.
4. **Shape the bites:** Scoop the mixture with a small cookie scoop or tablespoon, pressing each scoop firmly against the side of the bowl to pack it. Set the bites on the baking sheet.
5. **Bake:** Bake until the edges are golden and the bites feel firm, 20 to 25 minutes.
6. **Rest:** Let the bites rest on the pan for 5 minutes so the cheese sets, then lift them off with a spatula.
