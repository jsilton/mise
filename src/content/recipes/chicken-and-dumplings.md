## Chef's Note

This is Craig Plummer's chicken and dumplings, made the way many Southern kitchens do with a package of thin, flat dried dumplings instead of dough rolled by hand. The flour on the dumplings and a can of cream of chicken soup thicken the broth as they cook. Stir often, since the dumplings stick to the bottom of the pot.

## Directions

1. **Poach the chicken:** Put the small chicken, yellow onion, and lightly salted water in a large pot. Bring to a boil over high heat, then simmer until the thickest piece of chicken reads 165°F, 15 to 20 minutes.
2. **Bone the chicken:** Lift the chicken onto a board and let it cool until you can handle it, about 15 minutes. Pull the meat into bite-size pieces and discard the skin and bones.
3. **Make the broth:** Strain the broth, discard the onion and skim off the fat. Whisk the condensed cream of chicken soup into the broth, then measure about 2 quarts, adding water if you are short.
4. **Bring to a boil:** Return the measured liquid and the chicken to the pot. Season with salt and black pepper and bring to a boil over high heat.
5. **Cook the dumplings:** Drop in the dried flat dumplings a few at a time, stirring so they do not stick. Keep at a gentle boil, stirring often, until the dumplings are tender and the broth has thickened, about 30 minutes.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Add 2 carrots and 2 celery ribs to the pot with the chicken for a fuller broth.
- Measure out 2 quarts of broth before whisking in the soup, so none of the soup is left behind with any extra broth.
- Finish each bowl with a good grind of black pepper and a little chopped parsley.
