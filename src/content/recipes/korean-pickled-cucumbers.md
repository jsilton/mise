## Chef's Note

Oi muchim is a seasoned cucumber side dish. This version uses gochugaru, garlic, a little sugar, and sesame, with rice vinegar for brightness. A short salting period reduces the water that would otherwise dilute the dressing. Keep the cucumbers refrigerated and serve soon after dressing.

## Directions

1. **Slice:** Wash cucumbers and cut into rounds about 1/4 inch thick. If an English cucumber is very wide, halve it lengthwise first and slice into half-moons.
2. **Salt and drain:** Toss with the measured salt in a colander over a bowl. Leave for 20 minutes, tossing once halfway through. Discard the drained liquid and gently blot the slices with a clean towel; do not wring them dry. Taste a slice: if unusually salty, rinse briefly, drain, and blot again.
3. **Mix dressing:** During the wait, combine gochugaru, vinegar, soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, and garlic in a serving bowl. Stir until the sugar dissolves.
4. **Toss and serve:** Add drained cucumbers, scallions, and sesame seeds. Toss gently and serve promptly. If using as a noodle-salad topping, lift portions out with a slotted spoon so excess dressing does not flood the noodles.
