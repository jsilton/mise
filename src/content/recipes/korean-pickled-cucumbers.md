## Chef's Note

Oi muchim is the quick, spicy cucumber banchan on Korean tables, dressed with gochugaru, garlic, sesame and a splash of rice vinegar. Salting the slices first pulls out water, so the cucumbers stay crunchy and the dressing stays bold instead of watery. Start with less gochugaru if you're cooking for kids.

## Directions

1. **Salt the cucumbers:** Toss the cucumbers with the salt in a colander set over a bowl. Let them drain for 20 minutes, tossing once halfway.
2. **Mix the dressing:** While the cucumbers drain, stir the gochugaru, vinegar, soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil and garlic in a serving bowl until the sugar dissolves.
3. **Dry the cucumbers:** Discard the liquid and gently blot the cucumbers dry with a clean towel. If a slice tastes too salty, rinse them briefly and blot again.
4. **Toss and serve:** Add the cucumbers, scallions and sesame seeds to the dressing. Toss well and serve soon.
