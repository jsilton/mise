## Chef's Note

Riki Shore's meringues have a crackly shell and a soft, marshmallowy center studded with dark chocolate. The trick is warming the whites and sugar together until the sugar dissolves before you whip. That makes a silky, stable foam that bakes up chewy instead of dry.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 250°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment.
2. **Dissolve the sugar:** Whisk the egg whites, sugar and salt in a heatproof bowl set over a pot of simmering water. Keep whisking gently until the sugar dissolves and the mixture feels smooth, not gritty, when rubbed between your fingers, about 5 minutes.
3. **Whip the meringue:** Move the mixture to a stand mixer and add the cream of tartar. Whip on high until stiff, glossy peaks form and the bowl feels cool, 8 to 10 minutes.
4. **Fold and scoop:** Gently fold in the chocolate chips by hand. Scoop large spoonfuls onto the sheets about 1 inch apart. They won't spread.
5. **Bake:** Bake, rotating the sheets halfway, until the outsides are firm and dry but the centers still feel a little soft, 45 to 50 minutes.
6. **Cool:** Cool completely on the sheets so the shells set before you lift them off.
