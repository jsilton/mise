## Chef's Note

Use fully cooked apple chicken sausage, not raw sausage, and keep the full oil distributed over the gnocchi, squash and kale. A single layer leaves space for moisture to escape. Check the squash itself for tenderness; the kale may have crisp edges without every leaf becoming brittle.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line enough rimmed sheet pans with parchment rated for that temperature. Keep the gnocchi frozen as this route specifies; check that its product permits the roasting method. Slice the fully cooked sausage into roughly ¼-inch rounds. Wash the kale and drain it before shredding.

2. **Toss:** Combine all the gnocchi, sausage, squash and kale in a roomy bowl. Add the full listed olive oil, rosemary, thyme, paprika and garlic powder, with salt and pepper to taste. Toss until all components, including the leaves, are coated.

3. **Spread:** Spread in one layer with space between pieces, using additional pans if needed. Divide the same full mixed batch and its oil among those pans; do not add a fresh measured oil dose per pan.

4. **Roast:** Roast at 400°F, stirring halfway through. Check from about 24–26 minutes; continue until the squash is tender when pierced and the gnocchi have browned. Watch kale edges for scorching rather than requiring every leaf to be shatteringly crisp. Follow the fully cooked sausage package’s heating instructions; stored cooked sausage portions must be reheated throughout to 165°F / 74°C.

5. **Rest and finish:** Rest about 5 minutes, then distribute all the Parmesan or nutritional yeast over the batch and serve.
