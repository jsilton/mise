## Chef's Note

A fall weeknight dinner on one pan. Frozen cauliflower gnocchi roast straight from the freezer with sausage, squash and herbs until golden, no pot of water needed. Spread everything in a single layer so it browns instead of steaming, and add the kale near the end so it crisps without burning.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Line a large rimmed sheet pan with parchment, or two pans if the mixture would crowd one.
2. **Season:** In a large bowl, toss the frozen gnocchi, sausage and squash with the olive oil, rosemary, thyme, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper until coated.
3. **Roast:** Spread the mixture on the pan in a single layer with space between the pieces. Roast until the gnocchi start to brown, 15 to 20 minutes.
4. **Oil the kale:** While it roasts, rub the kale in the empty bowl with the oil and salt for the kale.
5. **Add the kale:** Stir the tray and scatter the kale over it. Roast until the squash is tender, the gnocchi are golden and the kale edges are crisp, 8 to 10 minutes more.
6. **Finish:** Sprinkle with the Parmesan while hot and serve.
