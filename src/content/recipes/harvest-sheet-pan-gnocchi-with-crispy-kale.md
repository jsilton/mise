## Chef's Note

Use fully cooked apple chicken sausage, not raw sausage, and keep the full oil distributed over the gnocchi, squash and kale. The kale goes on the tray only for the last 8–10 minutes, so it crisps while the squash finishes instead of burning. A single layer leaves space for moisture to escape. Check the squash itself for tenderness; the kale may have crisp edges without every leaf becoming brittle.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line enough rimmed sheet pans with parchment rated for that temperature. Keep the gnocchi frozen as this route specifies; check that its product permits the roasting method. Slice the fully cooked sausage into roughly ¼-inch rounds. Wash the kale and drain it before shredding.

2. **Toss:** Combine all the gnocchi, sausage and squash in a roomy bowl. Add 2 tbsp of the olive oil and all the rosemary, thyme, paprika and garlic powder, with salt and pepper to taste. Toss until all components are coated.

3. **Spread:** Spread the gnocchi mixture in one layer with space between pieces, using additional pans if needed. Divide the same full mixed batch and its oil among those pans; do not add a fresh measured oil dose per pan. In the emptied bowl, toss the kale with the remaining 1 tbsp oil and a pinch of salt, rubbing it into the leaves. Set the kale aside.

4. **Roast:** Roast at 400°F for about 15–20 minutes, then stir and scatter the oiled kale over the tray. Roast 8–10 minutes more, until the squash is tender when pierced, the gnocchi have browned and the kale edges are crisp. If the kale starts to blacken before the squash is done, lift it onto a plate and finish the squash without it. Follow the fully cooked sausage package’s heating instructions; stored cooked sausage portions must be reheated throughout to 165°F / 74°C.

5. **Rest and finish:** Rest about 5 minutes, then distribute all the Parmesan or nutritional yeast over the batch and serve.
