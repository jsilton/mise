## Chef's Note

This velvety carrot soup from Food & Wine gets its warmth from Madras curry powder and fresh ginger. The surprise is gingersnap cookies, which melt into the soup to thicken it and add their own spice. Tart apple and a splash of cider vinegar keep all that sweetness bright.

## Directions

1. **Soften the aromatics:** Melt the butter in a large heavy pot over medium-high heat. Add the onion, leek and fennel with a pinch of salt and pepper. Cook, stirring, until soft and starting to brown, about 9 minutes.
2. **Build the base:** Stir in the curry powder and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the carrots, celery root, apple, gingersnaps, garlic, ginger and thyme. Cook, stirring often so nothing catches, until the roots start to soften and the cookies break down, about 10 minutes.
3. **Simmer:** Add the stock and bring to a boil. Lower the heat and simmer until the carrots and celery root crush easily with a fork, about 25 minutes. Remove the thyme sprigs.
4. **Blend:** Purée the soup in batches in a blender with the sour cream and vinegar until completely smooth. Fill the blender only halfway and vent the lid so steam can escape.
5. **Season and serve:** Return the soup to the pot and warm it gently without boiling. Season well with salt and pepper. Ladle into bowls and top with pumpkin seeds, mint and cilantro.
