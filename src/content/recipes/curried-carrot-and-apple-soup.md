## Chef's Note

Carrots, celery root and tart apple make the base, while gingersnaps add ginger-spice flavor and body alongside the Madras curry powder. Cook both roots until very soft before blending with all the sour cream and the measured vinegar. Pumpkin seeds, mint and cilantro finish the bowls with texture and fresh aroma.

## Directions

1. **Aromatics:** Melt all the measured butter in a pot large enough for the roots, aromatics and stock with stirring room. Add onion, washed sliced leek and cored fennel with salt and pepper from the listed allowance. Cook over medium-high heat, stirring, about 9 minutes as a first check, until softened and just starting to brown; lower heat if butter or edges scorch.

2. **Build the base:** Add carrots, peeled chopped celery root, prepared apple, all the gingersnaps, curry powder, garlic, ginger and thyme sprigs. Cook while stirring for about 10 minutes, until the roots begin to soften and the cookies break down. Keep the bottom from catching rather than insisting every vegetable is already tender.

3. **Simmer:** Add all the chicken stock, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Stir occasionally and cook about 25 minutes as a first check, continuing until both carrots and celery root crush easily with a fork. Remove the thyme stems before blending.

4. **Blend:** Take the pot off the heat. Check the actual blender and vessel manual before starting. Never put hot soup in a sealed personal cup. For countertop blending, use a vessel approved for the mixture’s temperature and follow its fill, lid, venting and speed limits; cool first as the manual requires. Cooling and subsequent reheating add time. Purée in permitted loads with all the measured sour cream and vinegar divided among those loads. The entire listed dairy/vinegar amount is for the total batch, not each blender fill. Recombine all the purée; check for firm root pieces and blend further within the machine’s limits if needed.

5. **Finish:** Return to the pot and warm gently while stirring. Taste and adjust with the listed salt and pepper. Avoid prolonged hard boiling after the sour cream is included.

6. **Serve:** Ladle into bowls and add the toasted pumpkin seeds, chopped fresh mint and chopped fresh cilantro to taste. These are garnish allowances, not measured additions to each blender batch.
