## Chef's Note

Puree the spinach, banana and wet ingredients before gently folding them into the whole-wheat dry mixture. Butter, honey and cinnamon give the batter richness and warmth; check a center muffin for doneness and let the muffins mostly cool before serving.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F before mixing. For the original 18-muffin batch, line 18 standard cups across two tins, or grease the cups with the pan allowance. When scaling, prepare enough standard cups for the listed batch. Wash spinach unless labeled prewashed and ready to eat, and drain it. Melt the butter and let it cool slightly before combining it with the egg.

2. **Dry mix:** Whisk whole-wheat flour, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda and salt together in a large bowl.

3. **Puree:** Blend spinach, banana, melted butter, honey, milk, egg and vanilla until the spinach is fully pureed. Follow the actual blender or food-processor capacity and loading instructions; use proportionate loads for a larger batch rather than overfilling.

4. **Combine:** Pour the puree into the dry ingredients and fold just until no dry flour remains. Do not continue beating to smooth the batter.

5. **Bake:** Fill the prepared cups no more than ¾ full. Bake for 18–22 minutes, until the tops are firm and a center tester has no wet batter. Bake excess batter separately. If the required tins do not fit with room for air circulation, bake in separate oven batches.

6. **Cool:** Once the muffins can be handled without breaking, transfer them from the pans to a rack. Cool most or all of the way before serving, and fully before sealing for storage.
