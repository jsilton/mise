## Chef's Note

Bright green, banana-sweet muffins that sneak a whole bag of spinach into breakfast. Blend the spinach with the banana and wet ingredients until completely smooth so no leafy bits are left and the crumb stays tender. Honey and cinnamon make them taste like a treat.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Line 18 standard muffin cups with paper liners.
2. **Mix the dry ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the flour, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
3. **Blend the wet ingredients:** In a blender, combine the spinach, banana, butter, honey, milk, egg and vanilla. Blend on high until completely smooth and bright green, about 1 minute.
4. **Make the batter:** Pour the green purée into the dry ingredients and stir with a spatula just until no dry flour remains.
5. **Bake:** Fill the cups three-quarters full. Bake until the tops spring back when pressed and a toothpick in the center comes out clean, 18 to 22 minutes.
6. **Cool:** Cool in the pans for 5 minutes, then move the muffins to a rack to cool.
