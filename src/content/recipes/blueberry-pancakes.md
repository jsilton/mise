## Chef's Note

These are King Arthur Baking's blueberry pancakes, sweetened with honey, which browns the edges deeply. Whip the eggs and milk until foamy and rest the lumpy batter for five minutes so the pancakes cook up light. Scatter the berries onto each pancake on the griddle instead of stirring them in, so every bite has fruit and the batter stays golden, not purple.

## Directions

1. **Heat the griddle:** Heat a griddle to 350°F, or a large nonstick skillet over medium-low heat. Brush it lightly with butter.
2. **Whisk the wet ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, milk and vanilla until light and foamy, 2 to 3 minutes. Whisk in the melted butter and honey.
3. **Mix the batter:** In another bowl, whisk the flour, baking powder and salt. Add them to the wet ingredients and stir with a spatula just until no dry flour remains. Leave the small lumps.
4. **Rest the batter:** Let the batter sit for 5 minutes, until it thickens and turns a little bubbly.
5. **Cook the pancakes:** Scoop the batter onto the griddle with a quarter-cup measure and scatter a spoonful of blueberries over each. Cook until bubbles pop on top and the bottom is golden, about 2 minutes. Flip and cook until the center is set, about 2 minutes more.
6. **Serve:** Serve hot with salted butter and warm maple syrup. Keep finished pancakes warm in a 200°F oven while you cook the rest.
