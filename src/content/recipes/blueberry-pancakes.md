## Chef's Note

Whisk the eggs and milk until foamy, then stir the flour in briefly and let the batter rest for five minutes. Sprinkle berries onto each wet pancake so they stay evenly distributed. The goal is tender golden pancakes with cooked centers and berries in every bite.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Heat a griddle to 350°F, or a large nonstick skillet over medium-low heat. Lightly grease with the separate butter allowance; the measured melted butter all goes into the batter.
2. **Whisk the wet ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, and vanilla until light and foamy, about 2–3 minutes. Whisk in the cooled melted butter and honey until evenly combined.
3. **Add the dry ingredients:** Whisk the flour, salt, and baking powder together in another bowl, then add them to the wet mixture. Stir with a spatula just until no dry flour pockets remain. Small lumps are fine; scrape the bottom and sides, then stop rather than beating the batter smooth.
4. **Rest:** Let the batter sit for 5 minutes while the griddle finishes heating. Do not vigorously stir it again.
5. **Portion and add berries:** Drop batter by 1/4 cupfuls, leaving room to turn each pancake. Immediately sprinkle 1 tbsp blueberries onto each wet surface. Distribute the listed berries across the batch; the original 3/4 cup supplies twelve tablespoon portions.
6. **Cook and turn:** Cook until the underside is golden and the edges begin to set, with bubbles forming and popping, about 2 minutes. Flip gently and cook about 2–2 1/2 minutes more, until the center has no wet batter. Lower the heat if the exterior darkens before the center cooks. Repeat with the remaining batter, lightly renewing the griddle butter as needed.
7. **Serve:** Serve immediately, with salted butter and warm maple syrup if desired. If finishing another griddle round, hold the cooked pancakes briefly on a covered oven-safe platter in a 200°F oven. Covered pancakes may soften; serve promptly once the batch is ready.
