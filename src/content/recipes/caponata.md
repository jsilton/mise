## Chef's Note

Sicilian caponata brings fried eggplant and celery together with tomatoes, olives, capers and a sweet-and-sour vinegar finish. This version includes the pine nuts in Sicily’s regional recipe. Cook the vegetables separately, then fold them together gently so their pieces remain distinct.

## Directions

1. **Salt and drain the eggplant:** Put eggplant and coarse sea salt in a colander over a bowl. Toss the cubes with a light coating of coarse salt and leave to drain for at least 1 hour. Use this time for the other vegetables.
2. **Blanch the celery:** Use celery stems, water, and salt: bring the water to a boil with a little salt, add the celery and cook for about 5 minutes, until beginning to soften. Drain and spread on a clean towel to dry.
3. **Soften the onions:** Put white onions and extra-virgin olive oil in a wide pan and cook gently until softened without burning.
4. **Add olives, capers and pine nuts:** Add pitted green olives, desalted capers, and raw pine nuts to the onions. Cook over medium heat for about 10 minutes, stirring so the mixture does not catch.
5. **Cook the tomato base:** Add ripe red tomatoes. Cook for at least 20 minutes, stirring, until the tomato and onion mixture is well combined and the tomatoes have softened.
6. **Sauté the dried celery:** Use extra-virgin olive oil in a separate pan to sauté the blanched, dried celery until lightly colored. Keep it ready for the final assembly.
7. **Fry the drained eggplant:** Use neutral seed oil in a dry heavy frying pan with working room above the oil. Drain the eggplant thoroughly and dry it carefully. Heat the oil until a small piece of eggplant sizzles steadily, without smoke. Fry in small uncrowded batches, turning until golden and tender, and use a slotted spoon to drain each batch. Let the oil recover between loads and keep it below its smoke point. Do not leave it unattended or let water enter the hot oil. After frying, take the pan safely off the heat and let the oil cool.
8. **Bring the vegetables together:** Add the drained fried eggplant and sautéed celery to the onion-tomato mixture. Fold gently over medium heat for 3–4 minutes without crushing the cubes.
9. **Finish the sweet-and-sour sauce:** Add white vinegar and white sugar. Stir gently over the heat until the sugar dissolves and the sharp raw vinegar aroma softens. Keep the full listed amounts of both; the vegetables should remain recognizable in the sweet-and-sour mixture.
10. **Serve with basil:** Turn off the heat and add fresh basil leaves. Serve warm, or cool promptly in shallow containers under refrigeration and take out only the portions needed for service. Keep total room-temperature time within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C.

## Cooking Notes

The four portions are generous vegetable portions. The listed seed oil is for frying; drain the cooked eggplant before adding it to the other vegetables.

Serve warm or refrigerate promptly in shallow containers for later cool service. Vinegar in this dish does not replace refrigeration.
