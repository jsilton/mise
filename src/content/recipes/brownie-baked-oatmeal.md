## Chef's Note

Blend the oats with the very ripe banana, egg and milk until smooth for a soft, cake-like chocolate center. Keep the full cocoa and chocolate-chip amounts. A melted chocolate chip may stain a tester, but the egg-and-oat mixture around it should be cooked and set before serving.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease an oven-safe ramekin or small baking dish with the separate butter or oil allowance. Choose a dish that can hold the entire batter with room above it; banana size changes the volume.

2. **Blend:** Add all the oats, ripe banana, egg, milk, cocoa, baking soda and pinch of sea salt to the blender. Blend until smooth, following the blender’s load instructions.

3. **Fill and top:** Pour the whole mixture into the prepared dish and add all the measured chocolate chips on top. If the vessel cannot hold the batter with headroom, use a suitable larger dish or additional prepared vessels; different depth or portion size changes cooking time.

4. **Bake:** Begin checking around 15–18 minutes. The center should be set and soft, with no liquid egg mixture or wet oat batter; an egg-dish center should reach 160°F. Check beside a melted chocolate chip. Continue as needed for the actual dish and filling depth.

5. **Finish:** Let stand briefly until the dish is safe to handle and the center settles. Finish with a pinch of the flaky sea salt if desired and serve warm.
