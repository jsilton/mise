## Chef's Note

This birthday-style yellow cake pairs a buttery crumb with cocoa frosting. Cream the softened butter and sugar before adding the oil, then mix gently once the flour enters; the finished layers should be tender and fully set, with a few moist crumbs on the cake tester.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the pans:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Use butter or neutral oil to grease two 9-inch round cake pans; line the bottoms with parchment and grease the parchment. Have both the cake and frosting ingredients measured separately.
2. **Cream the butter and sugar:** Beat the cake’s unsalted butter and granulated sugar for about 3 minutes, until lighter in color and fluffy. Scrape the sides and bottom of the bowl.
3. **Add the oil:** Beat in the avocado or canola oil until evenly incorporated.
4. **Add the eggs and vanilla:** Add the large eggs, large egg yolks, and vanilla extract, beating in the eggs and yolks one at a time, then mixing in the vanilla. Scrape the bowl between additions.
5. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Whisk the cake’s all-purpose flour, baking powder, and salt together thoroughly in a separate bowl.
6. **Finish the batter:** On low speed, add the dry mixture in three additions, alternating with the whole buttermilk in two additions. Begin and end with the dry mixture; scrape the bowl and stop as soon as no dry pockets remain. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans.
7. **Bake and cool:** Bake for about 25–35 minutes, checking at 25 minutes, until the centers spring back lightly and a tester has a few moist crumbs but no wet batter. Set the pans on a rack for about 10 minutes, loosen the edges and invert onto racks. Remove the parchment and let the layers cool completely before frosting.
8. **Make the frosting:** While the layers cool, combine the frosting’s unsalted butter, unsweetened cocoa powder, confectioners’ sugar, whole milk, and vanilla extract: whisk the cocoa into the melted butter first, then beat in the confectioners’ sugar, milk and vanilla for several minutes until smooth and spreadable.
9. **Frost the cake:** Spread frosting between the completely cooled layers and over the top and sides. Finish with flaky sea salt (if using).

## Cooking Notes

If using salted butter, reduce the separately added salt; the amount depends on the butter’s salt content. Replacing all the oil with butter changes the crumb; the butter-and-oil combination is retained for moisture.
