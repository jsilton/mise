## Chef's Note

All'agro means "sour" in Roman cooking, where boiled or steamed greens are dressed simply with olive oil and lemon. A little raw garlic and the zest make the dressing sharp and fragrant. Toss the broccoli while it is still hot so it drinks the dressing in.

## Directions

1. **Make the dressing:** In a large serving bowl, whisk the olive oil, lemon zest, half the lemon juice, the garlic and the salt.
2. **Steam the broccoli:** Bring an inch of water to a boil in a pot fitted with a steamer basket. Add the broccoli, cover and steam until a fork slides into the thickest stem with slight resistance, 4 to 5 minutes.
3. **Toss and serve:** Let the broccoli drain for a moment, then add it hot to the dressing and toss to coat. Taste and add more lemon juice and salt. Serve warm or at room temperature.
