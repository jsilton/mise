## Chef's Note

Lemon juice and zest dress the steamed broccoli, with raw grated garlic for a sharp finish. Drain off surface water before gently tossing the hot florets with all the dressing. Keep the broccoli tender-crisp rather than forcing every batch into the same steaming time.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Wash the broccoli and cut the listed florets to similar sizes. Wash the lemon, zest it before juicing, and grate the garlic into a paste. Set up a steamer following its instructions, with cooking water below the basket and enough headroom for the florets; bring the water to a simmer.

2. **Make the dressing:** Whisk all the measured olive oil, lemon juice and zest, garlic and salt in a large serving bowl. This dressing keeps the garlic raw; do not hold a garlic-and-oil mixture at room temperature for later storage.

3. **Steam:** Add the broccoli to the basket, cover and steam. Start checking at 4–5 minutes: a fork should enter the thickest floret stem with slight resistance and the florets should remain intact. Continue as needed for their actual size. Use separate loads if the steamer is crowded.

4. **Drain and toss:** Lift out carefully, keeping clear of steam, and let excess surface water drain briefly. Add the hot broccoli to the dressing and toss gently until coated, scraping the bowl so the measured dressing reaches the whole batch. Serve promptly.

## Cooking Notes

The original batch uses two large heads and makes about six side portions; head size affects yield. When scaling, use the head count in the ingredient list.

Use a larger steamer or separate loads when scaling. Keep florets similarly sized and divide the measured dressing across loads; neither steaming time nor pot dimensions scale proportionally.

About 15–25 min includes preparation, heating and cooking; larger batches and product directions can add time.

Refrigerate promptly in a shallow covered container at 40°F / 4°C or below. Limit time out to 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C. Use within 3–4 days. Refrigerate any extra fresh garlic dressing promptly; lemon is not a validated preservation process. Reheated broccoli softens; reheat leftovers to 165°F / 74°C.
