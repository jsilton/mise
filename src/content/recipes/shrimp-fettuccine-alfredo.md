## Chef's Note

Shrimp Alfredo is the Italian-American take on the butter and Parmesan fettuccine Alfredo di Lelio served in Rome, made richer with cream. Sear the shrimp hard and fast so they stay tender, then build the sauce in the same skillet so the browned bits melt into the butter.

## Directions

1. **Heat the water:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.
2. **Season the shrimp:** While the water heats, pat the shrimp dry and toss them with the garlic powder, salt and pepper.
3. **Sear the shrimp:** Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the shrimp in one layer, in two batches if they crowd the pan, until pink and opaque all the way through, about 2 minutes per side. Move them to a plate.
4. **Boil the pasta:** Cook the fettuccine until al dente, about 10 minutes or as the package says. Scoop out the pasta water, then drain.
5. **Make the sauce:** While the pasta cooks, lower the skillet to medium and make the Alfredo sauce in it, scraping up the browned shrimp bits as the butter melts. Loosen it with a little hot water scooped from the pasta pot.
6. **Toss and serve:** Add the hot pasta and the shrimp to the sauce and toss over low heat until coated, adding more pasta water if it gets thick. Top with parsley and serve right away.
