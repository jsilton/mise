## Chef's Note

Prepare the full linked Alfredo batch and cook the shrimp just until firm and pearly opaque through their thickest centers. Pasta, shrimp and sauce can overlap, but the scratch sauce needs its own preparation time. The same-skillet route keeps the browned shrimp bits; finish heating the cream base before taking the pan off the heat for the Parmesan.

## Directions

1. For the scratch version, read the complete [Alfredo Sauce](/mise/recipes/real-alfredo-sauce) first and grate its Parmesan. Make one full linked batch for this original four-serving pasta recipe, matching the same scaling factor for both recipes. Use its full butter, cream and Parmesan amounts. Bring pasta water to a boil, using part of the listed salt allowance, and cook fettuccine according to its package. Reserve the listed pasta-water supply before draining.
2. Use raw peeled, deveined shrimp; if frozen, thaw in the refrigerator first and drain. Toss with the garlic powder and some of the remaining salt and black pepper. Keep the raw-shrimp bowl and utensils separate from the cooked-shrimp plate.
3. Heat some of the total olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook shrimp in uncrowded loads, dividing the same total oil across loads. Turn as needed, beginning checks around 2 minutes per side; time depends on size and pan heat. Continue until the thick centers are firm and pearly opaque. Move fully cooked shrimp to a clean plate.
4. For scratch sauce, follow the linked complete butter–cream–garlic stage in the shrimp skillet. Before the offheat Parmesan stage, simmer the cream base as directed and verify it reaches at least 145°F /63°C so liquid in the raw-contact pan is heated as well; then melt the Parmesan gently as the linked recipe directs. Alternatively, prepare that same complete batch in a separate clean saucepan. The sauce recipe can use up to ¼ cup of the original ½-cup shared pasta-water reserve; keep the rest for the final toss. Do not add the sauce allowance on top of the pasta reserve.
5. Toss the hot pasta and fully cooked shrimp with the sauce. Add remaining shared pasta water a little at a time if needed to loosen; not all of it may be necessary. Adjust with the remaining salt and pepper allowance without adding another fixed dose.
6. Top with the chopped parsley and serve immediately.
