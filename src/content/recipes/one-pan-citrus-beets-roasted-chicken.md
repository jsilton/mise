## Chef's Note

Crisp chicken thighs, sweet roasted beets and carrots, and soft fennel come together under a bright orange, garlic and rosemary finish. The roots get a head start on their own tray. The orange butter goes on the vegetables near the end, so it stays fresh and never soaks the chicken skin.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 425°F with racks in the upper and lower thirds.
2. **Roast the roots:** On a rimmed baking sheet, toss the beets and carrots with two-thirds of the oil, a third of the salt and half the pepper. Spread them in one layer and roast for 15 minutes.
3. **Set up the chicken:** Pat the chicken dry. On a second rimmed sheet, toss the fennel with the rest of the oil and arrange it around the thighs, skin side up. Season the chicken with half the salt and the chili powder, the fennel with the rest of the salt, and both with the rest of the pepper.
4. **Roast together:** Put the chicken in the oven with the roots. Roast for 30 minutes, swapping the trays halfway and stirring the vegetables.
5. **Mix the orange butter:** While the trays roast, stir the orange juice, zest, melted butter, rosemary and garlic together in a small bowl.
6. **Glaze the vegetables:** Spoon the orange butter over the roots and fennel, keeping it off the chicken skin, and stir. Roast until the skin is deep golden, the chicken reaches 165°F and the beets are fork-tender, 10 to 20 minutes more.
7. **Rest and serve:** Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes. Serve each thigh on a bed of the vegetables with a spoonful of the pan juices.
