## Chef's Note

A pot of cider steeped with cinnamon, allspice and cloves for cold nights and the holidays. A pinch of salt sharpens the apple flavor. Cider is already sweet, so start with a little brown sugar and add more only after you taste it.

## Directions

1. **Combine:** Put the cider, brown sugar, cinnamon sticks, allspice, whole cloves, nutmeg and salt in a large saucepan. Keep the cloves for the orange aside.
2. **Simmer:** Bring just to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Lower the heat, cover and simmer gently for 20 minutes.
3. **Stud the orange:** While the cider simmers, push a whole clove into each orange wedge.
4. **Strain:** Pour the cider through a fine-mesh sieve into a clean pot or slow cooker. Taste and stir in more brown sugar if you want it sweeter.
5. **Serve:** Ladle into mugs and float a clove-studded orange wedge in each.
