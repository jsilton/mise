## Chef's Note

Miso soup is the everyday soup of Japan, served with breakfast, lunch and dinner. The base is dashi, a light broth of kelp and bonito. Never boil the miso. Whisk it with a little hot broth and stir it in off the heat so it keeps its sweet, savory aroma.

## Directions

1. **Heat the dashi:** Bring the dashi to a gentle simmer in a saucepan over medium heat, then turn the heat to low.
2. **Add the tofu and wakame:** Slide in the tofu and wakame and simmer gently until the seaweed is soft and the tofu is hot, about 2 minutes. Stir carefully so the tofu stays whole.
3. **Loosen the miso:** Put the miso in a small bowl, ladle in a little of the hot broth and whisk until smooth and runny.
4. **Finish off the heat:** Turn off the heat and stir the miso back into the pot. Add the green onions and serve right away in small bowls.
