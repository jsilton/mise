## Chef's Note

Whisk miso smooth with some of the prepared dashi before returning it to the pot off the heat. This disperses the paste through the broth without crushing the silken tofu. Dashi can contain bonito; choose a clearly vegetarian stock if you need a vegetarian version, and measure its finished liquid rather than assuming starting water equals strained yield.

## Directions

1. **Prepare wakame:** Follow the dried wakame package's preparation instructions, using all the listed wakame. If it requires a separate soak and drain, complete that before adding it to the soup; use enough soaking water as instructed and discard it. Wash and thinly slice the green onions.
2. **Heat dashi:** Measure all the prepared dashi into a saucepan with room for tofu and seaweed. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, then reduce heat to low. Homemade [Dashi](/mise/recipes/dashi-japanese-sea-stock) is a separately prepared component; measure its strained output for this soup. Instant granules must be prepared according to their package.
3. **Warm tofu and seaweed:** Gently add all the tofu cubes and prepared wakame. Simmer gently, starting with about 2 minutes, until tofu is heated through and the seaweed is tender; product preparation and starting temperature affect the clock. Avoid rough stirring that breaks the tofu.
4. **Disperse miso:** Put all the miso in a small bowl. Ladle enough broth from this same pot to whisk it smooth, about 1/2 cup at the original batch. This is part of the measured dashi, not additional water or stock.
5. **Finish:** Turn off the heat. Gently stir all the miso mixture back into the pot. Add all the green onions and serve promptly in the listed planning number of small bowls.

## Six-serving variation

For a separate six-serving batch, use 6 cups prepared dashi, 1/2 cup dried wakame, 1/4 cup shiro miso, 1/2 lb soft tofu drained and cut into 1/2-inch cubes, and 1/4 cup washed, thinly sliced scallion greens. Do not combine these amounts with the four-serving ingredient list. Soak this wakame in warm water covering it by 1 inch for 15 minutes or until reconstituted, following its package if preparation differs; drain in a sieve and discard the soaking water. Whisk all the miso with 1/2 cup taken from the measured dashi. Heat the remaining dashi until hot, gently stir in tofu and wakame, simmer about 1 minute until heated through, then remove from heat and immediately stir in all the miso mixture and scallion greens. If scaling this separate version, scale all its measured ingredients together; package preparation and saucepan heating time do not multiply. The listed ingredient controls apply to the four-serving main version.
