## Chef's Note

Fine carrot shreds distribute through the whole-wheat crumb, while the full oil and yogurt amounts keep the mixture rich. Whisk the wet ingredients thoroughly, then fold the flour mixture gently until moistened and mix in all the carrots and nuts. Give the baked cake its pan rest before lifting it; cool it completely before adding cream-cheese frosting or wrapping for storage.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 375°F / 190°C. Line an 8-inch square pan with parchment for the original batch.
2. **Grate:** Wash and peel the carrots. Finely grate the carrots using the small holes of a grater. Use the listed weight; the original two-large-carrot description is only a shopping guide.
3. **Wet ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk large egg, granulated sugar, brown sugar, canola or avocado oil, plain Greek yogurt, and vanilla extract for about 3–5 minutes until thoroughly blended and frothy.
4. **Dry ingredients:** Whisk whole wheat flour, ground cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda, and sea salt together to distribute the leaveners and spice, then fold gently into the wet mixture until moistened without dry flour pockets.
5. **Fold:** Add carrots and walnuts or pecans. Fold gently until evenly distributed, stopping once combined.
6. **Bake:** Spread the batter evenly in the prepared pan and tap lightly. Load only once the oven has reached 375°F / 190°C. Begin checking at about 25–30 minutes: the center should be set, spring back gently and give a tester without wet batter. Continue as needed; pan material, filling depth and oven temperature affect the time. Pan dimensions and baking time do not multiply with ingredient scale; use suitable smaller pans or additional pans to keep similar filling depth.
7. **Cool:** Let stand in the pan for 10 minutes, then lift gently onto a wire rack. Cool completely before frosting or wrapping; warm serving after the pan rest is also an option.
