## Chef's Note

Chipotle in adobo gives these Mexican-inspired chicken bowls a smoky chile flavor, with honey, lime and Dijon rounding out the coating. The marinade mainly flavors the surface; a long soak does not guarantee that smoky flavor reaches the center. Even cutlets and controlled pan heat matter more than seeking black grill marks on a sugary coating.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the marinade:** Whisk chipotle, honey, lime juice, olive oil, Dijon, garlic and the measured salt in a bowl large enough for the chicken. Taste now if checking the heat; then put tasting utensils aside. Prepare the six evenly thick cutlets on a separate raw-meat board.
2. **Marinate cold:** Coat the cutlets, cover and refrigerate for thirty minutes, or up to two hours. Clean the raw-contact board, knife and work surface before preparing any bowl vegetables. There is no finishing sauce reserved from this raw-contact marinade.
3. **Start rice and toppings:** During marination, make two batches of [Cilantro-Lime Rice](/mise/recipes/cilantro-lime-rice) together in a 3-quart saucepan: select 2× on that recipe for all amounts, starting with 360 g rice and 600 ml water. Follow its texture checks and ten-minute covered rest. Wash and dry the greens and dice the tomatoes. Leave avocado cutting until assembly. Preheat the skillet over medium heat near the end of marination.
4. **Cook in two rounds:** Add one teaspoon of the neutral oil. Lift three cutlets from their marinade, allowing excess to drip off; lay them in a single uncrowded layer. Cook about four minutes on the first side and three on the second, then turn and check every one to two minutes as needed. Allow roughly 10–12 minutes per round. Adjust heat down if honey or garlic begins to blacken. Insert a thin thermometer sideways into each cutlet's thickest center and cook each to at least 165°F / 74°C before removing. Transfer to a clean plate and tent loosely. Remove scorched loose coating carefully with a paper towel held in tongs, add the remaining teaspoon of oil, and repeat. Discard the used marinade; use clean utensils for cooked meat.
5. **Rest and finish rice:** Rest the final chicken batch five minutes; the first batch rests longer while the second cooks. Finish the rice with its oil, herbs and lime after its covered rest. Slice the chicken across the grain, retaining clean resting juices to spoon over its slices.
6. **Build six bowls:** Divide the cooked rice among six bowls, roughly 3/4–1 cup each depending on yield. Add the greens, tomatoes and freshly diced avocado equally, then the sliced chicken. Serve one lime wedge per bowl and squeeze only as much as needed after tasting.

## Outdoor grill option

On a clean, lightly oiled grill at medium heat, cook the evenly thick cutlets without crowding, turning as needed to avoid scorching. Start checking centers after about eight minutes; allow roughly 10–12 minutes but use the same measured endpoint for every piece. Move away from flare-ups. The whole batch can cook together only if the grill has enough space; keep the rice and rest in the plan.

## Composition

The rice is an included base, not an extra side recommendation. For more tang, a small spoonful of [pickled red onions](/mise/recipes/pickled-red-onions) can replace some tomato; they are optional and require their own preparation time. The soy/fish-sauce [Honey-Lime Chicken Thighs for Tacos](/mise/recipes/honey-lime-chicken-thighs) is a distinct recipe, not another component of this bowl.
