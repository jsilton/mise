## Chef's Note

Tamagoyaki is the sweet, savory rolled omelet of Japanese breakfasts and bento boxes. Eggs whisked with dashi, mirin and soy are cooked in thin layers and rolled up one around the next. Keep every layer thin and the heat gentle, and the omelet comes out tender and evenly yellow.

## Directions

1. **Mix the eggs:** Whisk the eggs, dashi, mirin, soy sauce, sugar and salt until the sugar dissolves and no streaks of white remain. Stir in the mitsuba or chives.
2. **Heat the pan:** Heat a tamagoyaki pan or 8-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add the oil and wipe it over the pan with a folded paper towel, keeping the towel for later layers. Heat until the oil shimmers.
3. **Cook the first layer:** You will make two rolls, so use half the egg mixture for each. Pour in just enough egg to thinly coat the pan and tilt to spread it. Cook until the bottom is set and the top is still a little wet, 1 to 2 minutes.
4. **Roll it up:** Using a spatula or chopsticks, roll the egg from the far end toward you. Push the roll back to the far end.
5. **Add more layers:** Wipe the empty pan with the oiled towel. Pour in another thin layer and lift the roll so egg flows underneath it. When the new layer is nearly set, roll the log back toward you over it. Repeat until that half of the egg is used.
6. **Set the center:** Lower the heat to medium-low. Turn the roll gently on all sides until it is firm and no liquid egg seeps out when pressed, 1 to 2 minutes. Move it to a board and make the second roll the same way.
7. **Slice and serve:** Let the rolls rest for 2 minutes, then cut them into 1/2-inch slices. Serve warm or at room temperature, with soy sauce for dipping.
