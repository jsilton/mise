## Chef's Note

Muddle the basil and lemon gently, then transfer the entire muddled mixture into the shaker. Double-strain to remove small leaf and citrus pieces while keeping the juice and agave in the drink.

## Directions

1. Wash the basil and citrus before cutting or juicing. Cut the listed lemon portion into small muddling pieces; four pieces describes the original half-lemon portion. Chill a highball glass and have fresh serving ice ready.

2. In a glass, layer all the listed basil leaves, lemon pieces and agave in that order. Muddle gently to release juice and aroma without grinding the basil into a paste.

3. Transfer the entire muddled mixture, including all released juice and agave, into the shaker. Add all the listed gin, grapefruit juice and bitters, then shaking ice. Use separate proportional shaker batches if the full load leaves insufficient shaking space.

4. Shake vigorously until cold. Double-strain into the highball over fresh ice, using a glass with room for the drink and ice. Clap the separate optional garnish basil leaf gently between your hands and add it; serve immediately.
