## Chef's Note

A bright gin sour with muddled basil, lemon and fresh grapefruit, sweetened with agave. Muddle gently so the basil tastes fresh and green, not bitter. Double-strain so no leaf bits end up in the glass.

## Directions

1. **Muddle:** Put the basil leaves, lemon pieces and agave in a cocktail shaker in that order. Press gently with a muddler until the lemon releases its juice and the basil smells fragrant.
2. **Shake:** Add the grapefruit juice, gin, bitters and a handful of ice. Shake hard until the shaker is very cold, about 15 seconds.
3. **Strain and serve:** Double-strain through a fine sieve into a highball glass filled with fresh ice. Clap the garnish basil leaf between your hands and set it on top.
