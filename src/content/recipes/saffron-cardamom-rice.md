## Chef's Note

Saffron rice is the fragrant rice of North Indian feasts and the start of many a biryani. Whole spices fried in ghee perfume every grain, and saffron steeped in warm milk turns it gold. Make it when you want dal or a curry to feel like a special meal.

## Directions

1. **Steep the saffron:** Warm the milk until steaming, then crumble in the saffron. Let it steep while you start the rice, at least 15 minutes.
2. **Rinse the rice:** Rinse the basmati in a fine-mesh strainer under cool water, swishing gently, until the water runs mostly clear. Let it drain.
3. **Toast the spices:** Heat the ghee in a heavy 2- to 3-quart saucepan over medium heat. Add the green and black cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and bay leaf and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
4. **Cook the onion:** Add the onion and cook, stirring often, until soft and golden with a few browned edges, 10 to 12 minutes. Lower the heat if the spices start to darken.
5. **Coat the rice:** Add the drained rice and stir gently until every grain is glossy with ghee, about 1 minute.
6. **Simmer:** Pour in the water, the saffron milk with its threads and the salt. Stir once and bring to a boil. Cover tightly, turn the heat to low and simmer until the liquid is absorbed and a grain tastes tender, 15 to 18 minutes. If the rice is still firm and the pot is dry, sprinkle in a splash of hot water, cover and cook 3 to 5 minutes more.
7. **Rest and fluff:** Take the pan off the heat and let it rest, covered, for 10 minutes. Pick out the whole spices, then fluff gently with a fork and fold in the cilantro. Scatter the nuts on top.
