## Chef's Note

A quick weeknight red curry with a light, brothy coconut sauce made for spooning over rice. Makrut lime leaves simmered in the broth and a big handful of Thai basil at the end give it the real Thai perfume. The beef is sliced thin and cooks in just a few minutes, so it stays tender.

## Directions

1. **Fry the paste:** Whisk the curry paste with about a quarter of the coconut milk in a deep 10-inch skillet over medium heat. Simmer, stirring, until the paste dissolves and smells fragrant, about 2 minutes.
2. **Build the broth:** Stir in the rest of the coconut milk, the lime leaves, brown sugar, half the fish sauce and the pepper flakes. Bring to a gentle simmer.
3. **Cook the vegetables:** Add the bell pepper and onion. Simmer uncovered over medium-low heat, stirring now and then, until the onion is soft and the pepper bends but still has a little bite, 6 to 8 minutes.
4. **Cook the beef:** Add the beef a few slices at a time, separating them in the broth. Simmer gently, stirring, until the beef is just cooked through and no longer pink, 2 to 3 minutes.
5. **Finish and serve:** Take the pan off the heat and stir in the basil, lime juice and the rest of the fish sauce. Taste and add more fish sauce or lime if it needs it. Serve with lime wedges, and eat around the lime leaves.
