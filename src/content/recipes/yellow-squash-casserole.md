## Chef's Note

This is Craig Plummer's squash casserole, a Southern supper and holiday side of tender yellow squash beaten with butter, eggs and saltines under a Parmesan top. Let the squash drain well before it goes back in the pan, since any water left in it makes the casserole loose.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Coat a 2-quart baking dish with cooking spray.
2. **Cook the squash:** Put the medium yellow squash in a large saucepan and add water to cover. Bring to a boil over high heat, then simmer until very tender, about 15 minutes.
3. **Drain the squash:** Drain the squash in a colander and let it sit until it stops dripping, about 5 minutes.
4. **Beat in the butter:** Return the squash to the empty pan. Add the butter and onion and beat with a potato masher until the butter melts and the squash is mostly smooth.
5. **Add the eggs:** Let the squash cool for 5 minutes. Beat in the eggs, saltines, and salt and black pepper until the eggs disappear into the mixture.
6. **Top and bake:** Spread the mixture in the baking dish and sprinkle with the Parmesan cheese. Bake until the center is set and the top is lightly golden, about 30 minutes.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Press the drained squash gently with a spatula to push out more water. The casserole sets firmer and tastes more of squash.
- Cook the onion in the butter for 3 minutes before adding the squash. It softens and sweetens instead of staying sharp.
- For a crisp top, toss another 6 crumbled saltines with 1 tbsp melted butter and scatter them over with the Parmesan.
