## Chef's Note

This is a home take on gà nướng sả, the lemongrass grilled chicken of Vietnamese street stalls and rice plates. Fish sauce seasons it, brown sugar helps it char, and lime and ginger keep it bright. Mince the lemongrass very fine so it melts into the marinade instead of leaving woody bits.

## Directions

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Whisk the fish sauce, lime juice, brown sugar, oil, lemongrass, garlic, ginger and chili flakes in a bowl. Add the chicken and turn to coat. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 2 hours.
2. **Heat the grill:** Heat a grill to medium-high, leaving one side cooler. Oil the grates.
3. **Grill the chicken:** Lift the chicken from the marinade and let the excess drip off. Grill, turning every few minutes, until charred at the edges and 165°F in the thickest part, 10 to 12 minutes. Move pieces to the cooler side if the marinade darkens too fast.
4. **Rest and serve:** Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes. Slice it across the grain and scatter with cilantro and scallions.
