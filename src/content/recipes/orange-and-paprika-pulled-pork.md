## Chef's Note

This is an easy slow cooker pulled pork with a bright edge. Paprika, cumin and oregano season the shoulder, and orange juice cooks down with onion and garlic into the juices you toss it with at the end. Taste those juices for salt before they go back on the meat.

## Directions

1. **Season the pork:** Mix the paprika, oregano, salt, pepper and cumin in a small bowl. Trim any thick fat cap from the pork, then rub the spice mix all over it.
2. **Load the cooker:** Scatter the onion and garlic in the slow cooker and pour in the orange juice. Set the pork on top.
3. **Slow-cook:** Cover and cook on LOW until the pork pulls apart easily with a fork, about 8 hours.
4. **Shred the pork:** Move the pork to a board and shred it with two forks. Discard the bone, if there is one, and any large pieces of fat.
5. **Season the juices:** Skim the fat from the juices in the cooker. Taste and add salt until they taste well seasoned.
6. **Moisten and serve:** Toss the pork with enough of the juices to keep it moist. Serve the rest of the juices on the side.
