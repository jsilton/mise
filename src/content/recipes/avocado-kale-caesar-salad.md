## Chef's Note

Pinch of Yum's kale Caesar swaps the eggs and anchovies for a creamy avocado dressing, with miso for the deep, savory Caesar flavor. Massaging the shredded kale with a little oil and salt turns it tender and sweet. Pepitas, Parmesan and buttery crumbs add the crunch.

## Directions

1. **Toast the panko:** If using, melt the butter in a small skillet over medium-low heat. Add the panko and stir until golden, 3 to 5 minutes. Tip onto a plate to cool.
2. **Massage the kale:** Put the kale in a large bowl with the olive oil and a pinch of the salt. Rub it with your hands until the leaves turn darker and softer, 1 to 2 minutes.
3. **Blend the dressing:** Blend half the avocado with the water, mayonnaise (if using), garlic, lemon juice, Dijon, miso, pepper and the rest of the salt until smooth. It should pour like heavy cream. Add a splash more water if it is too thick.
4. **Dress the kale:** Toss the kale with the dressing and half the Parmesan. Let stand for 5 minutes.
5. **Top and serve:** Dice the rest of the avocado. Scatter it over the salad with the pepitas, the rest of the Parmesan and the toasted panko.
