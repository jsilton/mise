## Chef's Note

Finely shredded kale softens with a brief rub, then a creamy avocado dressing fills the spaces between the leaves. Add the diced avocado, seeds and optional crumbs after a short rest so the contrasting textures remain distinct. Adapted from [Pinch of Yum’s Avocado Kale Caesar Salad](https://pinchofyum.com/avocado-kale-caesar-salad-sweet-potato-fries).

## Directions

1. **Prepare greens and optional crumbs:** Wash, dry and shred kale. If making the topping, melt its butter in a small skillet over medium-low. Add panko and stir about 3–5 minutes until golden. Transfer to a plate to cool; do not leave crumbs in the hot skillet.

2. **Soften kale:** Put prepared kale in a wide bowl. Add the measured olive oil and a small pinch taken from the measured salt allowance. Rub and turn gently with clean hands for about 1–2 minutes until leaves become darker and more flexible. Stop before they turn wet or mushy.

3. **Blend dressing:** Blend half the avocado with the initial water allowance, optional mayonnaise, garlic, lemon juice, Dijon, miso or soy and black pepper. Add further water only as needed for a smooth spoonable dressing. Taste with a piece of kale and some Parmesan before adding more of the remaining salt.

4. **Dress and rest:** Toss kale with about half the dressing, adding more only as needed to coat without leaving a pool. Fold in half the Parmesan and let stand about 5 minutes while dicing the remaining avocado. Reserve unused dressing.

5. **Finish:** Gently fold in diced avocado. Scatter with pepitas, remaining Parmesan and optional cooled breadcrumbs. Divide into side portions and serve promptly.
