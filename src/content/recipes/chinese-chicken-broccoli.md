## Chef's Note

Chicken and broccoli is a Chinese-American takeout classic built on Cantonese technique. Velveting the sliced chicken in Shaoxing wine and cornstarch keeps it tender, and a quick steam makes the broccoli crisp-tender before it meets the hot pan. A glossy oyster sauce glaze brings it all together.

## Directions

1. **Coat the chicken:** Toss the chicken with the wine, cornstarch and light soy sauce for the coating. Refrigerate for 10 minutes while you prepare everything else.
2. **Mix the sauce:** Whisk the sauce ingredients together in a bowl and set it by the stove.
3. **Steam the broccoli:** Put the broccoli and water in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Cover and steam until the stems are nearly tender, 2 to 3 minutes. Move the broccoli to a plate and wipe the pan dry.
4. **Sear the chicken:** Heat half the oil in the pan over medium-high heat. Spread half the chicken in one layer and leave it for 1 minute, then stir-fry until no longer pink outside, 1 to 2 minutes more. Move it to a plate and repeat with most of the remaining oil and the rest of the chicken.
5. **Fry the aromatics:** Add the last of the oil, the garlic and the ginger. Stir until fragrant, 20 to 30 seconds.
6. **Glaze and serve:** Return the chicken, its juices and the broccoli to the pan. Stir the sauce to pick up the settled cornstarch and pour it in. Toss until the sauce bubbles and turns glossy and the chicken reaches 165°F, 1 to 2 minutes. Add a spoonful of water if the glaze gets too thick.
