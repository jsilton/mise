## Chef's Note

This Chinese-American-style stir-fry depends on cooking order. Broccoli needs a brief steam before the final toss, while thin chicken cooks quickly in oil. The sauce should leave a glossy coating with a little fluidity. It is seasoned with oyster sauce and soy, so there is no extra salt in the chicken coating.

## Directions

1. **Coat chicken:** Combine the coating wine, cornstarch, and light soy. Toss with chicken and refrigerate for 10 minutes while preparing broccoli and aromatics.
2. **Mix sauce:** Whisk oyster sauce, the sauce wine, dark soy, stock, sugar, and sauce cornstarch in a bowl. Set beside the stove.
3. **Steam broccoli:** Put broccoli and the measured steaming water in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Cover and steam about 2–3 minutes, until stems are nearly tender with a little resistance. Add another splash of water and more time if needed. Transfer broccoli to a plate, drain excess water, and carefully wipe the pan dry with a towel held in tongs.
4. **Cook chicken:** Heat 1 tbsp oil over medium-high heat. Spread half the chicken in one layer; cook about 1 minute before turning and tossing another 1–2 minutes. Transfer to a plate and repeat with 2 tsp oil and remaining chicken. Add the last teaspoon of oil, garlic, and ginger to the pan and stir for 20–30 seconds, reducing heat if needed to prevent scorching.
5. **Finish together:** Return chicken, its plate juices, and broccoli. Re-stir sauce thoroughly and pour it in. Bring to a simmer and toss 1–2 minutes until glossy. Check several of the thickest chicken slices reach 165°F / 74°C, probing from the side and keeping off the pan. Continue cooking if necessary, adding a tablespoon of water if the sauce thickens too far; lift broccoli out temporarily if already tender.
6. **Serve:** Transfer to a platter immediately. Serve with plain rice, with cucumber salad optional alongside.
