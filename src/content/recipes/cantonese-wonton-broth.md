## Chef's Note

Hong Kong wonton shops simmer their broth with dried shrimp, which gives it a gentle sea flavor that matches the shrimp in the wontons. Ginger and green onion keep it fragrant. Boil the wontons in a separate pot of water so their starch doesn't cloud the broth.

## Directions

1. **Simmer the broth:** Combine the chicken broth, dried shrimp, ginger and green onions in a pot. Bring to a gentle boil over medium-high heat, then lower the heat and simmer for 20 minutes.
2. **Strain:** Pour the broth through a fine-mesh strainer into a clean pot and discard the solids.
3. **Season:** Stir in the light soy sauce and season with salt and white pepper. Keep the broth hot over low heat.
4. **Serve:** Boil the wontons in a separate pot of water, lift them into bowls and ladle the hot broth over them.
