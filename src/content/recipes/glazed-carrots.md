## Chef's Note

A holiday-table side from Allrecipes that is easy enough for a weeknight. Thin carrot sticks simmer in a little water until just tender, then go back into the pan with butter and brown sugar for a glossy glaze. A pinch of white pepper keeps them from tasting like candy.

## Directions

1. **Simmer the carrots:** Put the carrots in a large saucepan and add salted water to a depth of 1 inch. Bring to a boil, then cover, turn the heat to low and simmer until just tender with a little bite, 3 to 4 minutes. Drain well without rinsing.
2. **Make the glaze:** Melt the butter in the same saucepan over low heat. Stir in the brown sugar, salt and white pepper until the sugar dissolves and the glaze bubbles, about 2 minutes.
3. **Glaze the carrots:** Return the carrots to the pan and toss gently over medium-low heat until hot and glossy, 2 to 3 minutes. Stir in the thyme and serve.
