## Chef's Note

This is Bette Lordeman's banana pudding, the no-cook kind that turns up at Southern potlucks and holiday tables. Two instant puddings, condensed milk and Cool Whip make it thick and creamy, and the wafers soften into cake-like layers as it chills.

## Directions

1. **Mix the pudding:** Whisk the milk and both pudding mixes in a large bowl until thick, about 2 minutes.
2. **Finish the filling:** Add the condensed milk, half the Cool Whip and the vanilla. Mix well until smooth and evenly pale.
3. **Layer the dish:** Set aside a few wafers for the top. In a serving dish, layer the vanilla wafers, bananas and pudding mixture, repeating until everything is used.
4. **Top it:** Spread the rest of the Cool Whip over the top and sprinkle with the reserved wafers, crumbled.
5. **Chill:** Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Use pure vanilla extract in place of the vanilla flavoring.
- Fold the Cool Whip in with a spatula instead of mixing, so the filling stays light.
- Slice the bananas 1/4 inch thick just before layering, and make two layers each of wafers, bananas and pudding in a 4-quart dish.
- Chill at least 4 hours so the wafers turn spoon-tender.
- Crush the reserved wafers over the top just before serving so they stay crisp.
