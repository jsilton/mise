## Chef's Note

Jodi Silton’s farfel muffins start by hydrating the matzo pieces, then cooling them before adding butter and eggs one at a time. Keep the full butter amount and serve warm. Matzo farfel is broken matzo, not toasted egg barley.

## Directions

1. **Hydrate and cool:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. Pour all the boiling water over all the matzo farfel in a heatproof bowl and stir to moisten evenly. Let hydrate and cool until the mixture is only mildly warm, so it will not cook eggs on contact. Grease enough muffin cups with the separate pan allowance while it cools.
2. **Mix:** Stir in all the measured melted butter, cooled if still very hot. Add all the eggs one at a time, mixing thoroughly after each, then mix in the listed kosher salt. Do not taste raw egg batter.
3. **Fill:** Divide all batter among the greased cups about three-quarters full, using enough cups to preserve similar fill depth. The source plans 8 muffins for its original batch but does not name the cup size; use actual capacity rather than forcing overflow or a fixed count at every scale.
4. **Bake:** Bake about 20 minutes as a first check, until golden and the centers are set without wet egg mixture. Check representative center muffins reach 160°F /71°C with a thin probe, keeping it away from the pan. Additional tins or different cup sizes can change the clock.
5. **Serve:** Let stand briefly until they can be lifted without breaking, and serve warm with optional salted butter.

## Cooking Notes

The batch doubles well. Divide among enough muffin tins to keep the same fill depth, and serve warm.
