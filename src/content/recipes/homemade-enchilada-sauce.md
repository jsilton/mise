## Chef's Note

A quick red enchilada sauce built on pure ancho chile powder, which gives it a deep, fruity chile flavor that store-bought sauce lacks. Toasting the chile and spices in the oil and flour wakes them up before the broth goes in. A splash of vinegar at the end brightens everything.

## Directions

1. **Toast the spices:** Mix the flour, chile powder, cumin, garlic powder, oregano, salt and cinnamon in a small bowl. Heat the oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour mixture and cook, whisking constantly, until fragrant and a shade darker, about 1 minute.
2. **Add the tomato paste:** Whisk in the tomato paste until smooth.
3. **Add the broth:** Slowly pour in the broth, whisking hard so no lumps form.
4. **Simmer:** Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then lower the heat and simmer until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon, 5 to 7 minutes.
5. **Finish:** Take the pan off the heat. Whisk in the vinegar and black pepper, then taste and adjust the salt.
