## Chef's Note

Eggs and milk set around the pasta while the two cheddars and pimentos remain distinct ingredients. Toss in all the melted butter, then let the pasta stop steaming before adding the egg mixture so it does not scramble on contact. Bubbling edges and a browned top are useful cues, but check the center before resting and serving.

## Directions

1. **Heat and boil:** Heat the oven to 375°F. Bring a roomy pot of water to a boil and salt it with the listed cooking allowance. Cook all the pasta about 2 minutes short of its package’s al-dente time, until firm but hydrated, then drain.

2. **Butter and cool slightly:** Return pasta to the pot and coat it with all the melted butter. Let it stop steaming hot, stirring occasionally, while preparing the egg mixture. This brief cooling helps avoid scrambling the eggs; butter coating does not guarantee that pasta cannot soften further.

3. **Mix the custard:** In a separate bowl whisk all the beaten eggs, whole milk, measured kosher salt, black pepper, garlic powder, dry mustard and smoked paprika until evenly combined. Keep raw-egg utensils separate from ready-to-eat food.

4. **Combine:** Mix three-quarters of EACH cheddar with all the pimentos into the buttered pasta. Stir in all the egg-milk mixture so there are no isolated pockets of custard. Reserve the remaining quarter of each cheese for the top.

5. **Load the dish:** Grease the baking dish with the separate allowance. The original batch uses a 9-by-13-inch dish, but actualdepth/capacity matters. Use additional or appropriately smaller oven-safe dishes if needed to keep a similar shallow layer with headroom. Distribute the full mixture and all remaining cheese across the dishes; do not overfill or discard custard to make it fit.

6. **Bake and check:** Bake, starting checks around 25–30 minutes for the original-size load, until the top is golden, edges bubble and the center is set without flowing egg mixture. Measure several central locations in every dish: each must reach 165°F. Continue baking if needed, shielding an already-browned top with suitable foil. For an optional crisp finish, use a broiler-rated vessel and the appliance’s clearance, watching continuously for up to about 60 seconds and stopping before scorching; a broil does not replace the center check.

7. **Rest and serve:** Rest 5 minutes before serving. The original batch is planned for six portions; use the listed portion count when scaling and portion according to appetite.
