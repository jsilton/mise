## Chef's Note

Kachumber is the chopped salad that sits on every Indian dinner table, cold, sour and crunchy beside rich curries and biryani. Seed the tomatoes so their pulp doesn't water down the dressing. Toast the cumin until it smells like popcorn, then crush it. That one step makes the salad.

## Directions

1. **Toast the cumin:** Shake the cumin seeds in a small dry skillet over medium heat until fragrant and a shade darker, 60 to 90 seconds. Tip them onto a plate and crush them with the side of a knife.
2. **Make the dressing:** In a small bowl, stir the lemon juice, crushed cumin, salt, pepper, chaat masala and olive oil, if using.
3. **Toss and serve:** Put the cucumber, tomatoes, onion, serrano, cilantro and mint in a bowl. Pour on the dressing and toss gently. Taste for lemon and salt and serve within 30 minutes.
