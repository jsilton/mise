## Chef's Note

Thit kho is everyday Vietnamese home cooking: pork braised in a dark caramel with fish sauce, garlic and shallots until it is spoon-tender and glossy. Coconut water adds a gentle sweetness to the braise. Cook the sugar until it smells toasty but never acrid, since that caramel is the whole flavor of the dish.

## Directions

1. **Make the caramel:** Warm the oil in a heavy 3-quart saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the sugar and stir until it melts, darkens and smells toasty, 2 to 3 minutes. If it smokes or smells acrid, start over.
2. **Coat the pork:** Carefully add the pork and stir so every piece touches the caramel. Let it sit for 2 minutes to color at the edges.
3. **Add the aromatics:** Add the shallots, garlic, chilies, pepper and fish sauce and stir to combine.
4. **Braise:** Pour in the coconut water and scrape up the caramel from the bottom of the pan. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover with the lid slightly ajar and turn the heat to low. Simmer, stirring now and then, until a fork slides easily into the pork, 60 to 90 minutes. If the liquid drops below a shallow layer before the pork is tender, add a splash of hot water.
5. **Reduce the sauce:** Uncover and simmer until the sauce is glossy and coats a spoon, 5 to 10 minutes. Skim off excess fat. Taste and add more fish sauce a few drops at a time if it needs salt.
6. **Serve:** Let rest off the heat for 5 minutes. Serve over rice with cilantro, scallions and lime wedges.
