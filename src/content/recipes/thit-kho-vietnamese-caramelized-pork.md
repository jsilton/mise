## Chef's Note

Caramel, fish sauce and slow-cooking pork make a concentrated savory-sweet sauce. Keep a little liquid in the pot until the meat is tender, then decide how far to reduce it. Plain rice and a crisp vegetable accompaniment balance the richness; finish with fresh herbs and lime at the table.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Pat the pork cubes dry. Have shallots, garlic, chilies, pepper, the braising portion of fish sauce and coconut water ready beside the stove.
2. **Make the caramel:** Warm the oil in a heavy saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the brown sugar or shaved palm sugar and stir with a silicone spatula until fluid and caramel-scented, about 2–3 minutes. Lower the heat if it smokes; discard and restart if it smells acrid.
3. **Coat the pork:** Carefully add the dry pork with tongs, keeping your hands away from possible splashes. Turn for about 2 minutes to coat in the caramel; a browned crust is not required on every cube.
4. **Add aromatics and liquid:** Add shallots, garlic, optional chilies, pepper and the measured braising fish sauce. Stir, then carefully add the coconut water or water. Scrape up dissolved caramel without scraping any burned material.
5. **Braise to tenderness:** Bring to a gentle simmer, cover with the lid slightly ajar and lower the heat. Stir occasionally and keep a shallow liquid in the pan, adding hot water if it dries before the meat softens. Start testing a cube after about 45 minutes; allow roughly 60–90 minutes, continuing longer if needed. A fork should enter easily. Check a thick cube reaches at least 145°F / 63°C; tenderness usually needs substantially more cooking than this minimum.
6. **Control the sauce:** If the meat is tender but sauce remains thin, lift the pork out and reduce the sauce uncovered, then return the meat. Skim excess surface fat if desired. Taste before adding optional finishing fish sauce, a little at a time; you may need none of the additional allowance. Stop when savory and lightly sweet with enough sauce for the rice.
7. **Rest:** Take off heat and rest for 5 minutes, which also exceeds the 3-minute rest required after the minimum pork temperature.
8. **Serve:** Serve alongside separately cooked rice. Add cilantro, scallions and lime to taste.
