## Chef's Note

Basmati, chicken thighs and kale cook together in one pot of spiced coconut milk. Cumin seeds fried in the chicken fat, fresh ginger and garam masala give it real depth, and soaking the rice first keeps the grains long and separate. Lime at the table cuts the richness.

## Directions

1. **Soak the rice:** Rinse the rice in a strainer until the water runs clear. Cover with cool water and soak for 30 minutes while you prep everything else, then drain well.
2. **Brown the chicken:** Toss the chicken with half the salt. Heat the oil in a wide, heavy 4-quart pot over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken in two batches, turning once, until golden in patches, 4 to 5 minutes per batch. It will still be pink inside. Move it to a plate.
3. **Bloom the spices:** Lower the heat to medium. Add the cumin seeds to the fat and let them sizzle until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the shallot and cook until soft, 3 to 4 minutes. Stir in the garlic, ginger, turmeric, garam masala and cayenne and cook for 30 seconds.
4. **Add the rice:** Stir in the coconut milk, water and the rest of the salt, scraping up the browned bits. Add the drained rice and the chicken with its juices, spread everything in an even layer and bring to a gentle simmer.
5. **Cook covered:** Cover tightly and turn the heat to low so the pot bubbles quietly. Cook without lifting the lid for 15 to 18 minutes.
6. **Steam the kale:** Spread the kale over the top, cover and cook until the rice is tender, the kale has wilted and the chicken reaches 165°F, about 5 minutes. If the rice is still firm and the pot is dry, add a splash of hot water and cook a few minutes more.
7. **Rest and serve:** Take the pot off the heat and let it rest, covered, for 10 minutes. Fold the kale through and serve with lime wedges and pickled chiles.
