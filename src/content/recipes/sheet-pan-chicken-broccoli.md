## Chef's Note

This is the chicken and broccoli casserole made from scratch, with no canned soup. Roasting the chicken and broccoli first browns them and keeps the broccoli from turning mushy. A quick cheddar sauce thickened with a roux pulls it all together under a crunchy panko top.

## Directions

1. **Season the pans:** Heat the oven to 425°F with racks in the upper and lower thirds. On one rimmed baking sheet, toss the chicken with half the olive oil, the chicken salt and half the pepper. On a second sheet, toss the broccoli with the rest of the oil, the broccoli salt and the rest of the pepper. Spread both out in one layer.
2. **Roast:** Roast both pans, swapping racks halfway. Take out the broccoli when it is just tender with browned tips, 15 to 20 minutes. Take out the chicken when it is golden and reaches 165°F, 18 to 25 minutes.
3. **Make the roux:** While the pans roast, melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Whisk in the flour and cook, whisking, until it smells nutty but has not darkened, 1 to 2 minutes.
4. **Thicken the sauce:** Whisk in the broth a little at a time until smooth. Simmer, stirring, until the sauce coats a spoon, 3 to 4 minutes.
5. **Melt in the cheese:** Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the cheddar a handful at a time until smooth, then the garlic powder, Dijon and sauce salt.
6. **Assemble:** Put the chicken and broccoli in a 3-quart baking dish, pour the sauce over and fold to coat. Toss the panko with the topping oil and scatter it over the top.
7. **Bake:** Bake at 425°F until the panko is golden and the edges bubble, 10 to 15 minutes. Let stand 5 minutes before serving.
