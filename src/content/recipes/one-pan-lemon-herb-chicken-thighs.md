## Chef's Note

Lemon, oregano and thyme give this skillet chicken its Mediterranean-style flavor. Brown the skin first, keep it above the sauce while roasting where possible, and judge thigh temperature and optional vegetable tenderness separately.

## Directions

1. **Preheat and season:** Preheat the oven to 425°F. Use an oven-safe skillet with room for all the measured thighs in one layer and, if using them, the vegetables; use additional oven-safe vessels if necessary. Pat all the measured thighs dry and season both sides with all kosher salt and black pepper. Divide every measured ingredient proportionally among any extra vessels; do not add extra oil or stock.
2. **Sear the chicken:** Heat the assigned two-thirds of olive oil over medium-high heat and use bone-in skin-on chicken thighs and 2/3 of the olive oil for searing. Put chicken skin-side down without crowding; work in loads if needed, using this same measured oil supply across the loads. Cook about 5–6 minutes until deeply golden and releasing from the pan, then turn and cook about 2 minutes. Transfer to a plate; keep all rendered fat in the cooking pan.
3. **Cook aromatics:** Use 1/3 of the olive oil, garlic cloves, fresh oregano, and fresh thyme for this stage. Reduce heat to medium, add the remaining one-third of the measured oil and all the garlic, and sauté for 30–45 seconds until fragrant. Then stir in all the oregano and thyme for about 15 seconds; do not let the garlic blacken.
4. **Add lemon and stock:** Use lemons and chicken stock or water to deglaze: pour in all the juice from the measured lemons and the stock or water, scrape the browned bits, then stir in all the reserved zest. Keep the whole sauce and rendered fat.
5. **Return chicken and optional vegetables:** Return all the partly cooked chicken and its plate juices to the pan, skin-side up, keeping skin above the liquid where possible. Nestle potatoes or root vegetables (if using) around the thighs. Transfer the oven-safe vessel or vessels to the preheated oven; do not crowd or stack the thighs.
6. **Finish to temperature and tenderness:** Start checking after 12–15 minutes without vegetables, or 15–18 minutes with them. These are first checks, not guaranteed finishing times. Every thigh must reach 165°F / 74°C in its thickest part away from the bone. Vegetables must be fork-tender; if chicken finishes first, remove it with clean utensils to a clean platter and continue cooking the vegetables and full sauce. Check raw-contact sauce and vegetables reach 165°F / 74°C before tasting or serving them, stirring liquid for a representative reading. Larger loads or different roots can need longer.
7. **Rest and garnish:** Rest cooked chicken for 5 minutes. Once chicken and the raw-contact sauce/vegetables have reached their endpoints, taste the sauce and scatter fresh parsley over the finished dish. Serve all the sauce and rendered fat with the chicken.
