## Chef's Note

Chicago chef Jimmy Bannos Jr. coats skin-on chicken thighs in sweet paprika and grills them until the skin crisps and the edges char. A cool yogurt sauce, sharp with lemon and hot with cayenne and chile flakes, balances the rich, smoky meat. Keep the skin facing out on the skewers so it renders and crisps.

## Directions

1. **Heat the grill:** Heat the grill to medium, leaving one side cooler.
2. **Season the chicken:** Toss the chicken with the olive oil, paprika, salt and half the pepper. Cover and refrigerate for 15 minutes while you make the sauce.
3. **Make the sauce:** Whisk the yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice, cayenne, red pepper flakes, salt and the rest of the pepper in a bowl. Chill until serving.
4. **Skewer the chicken:** Thread three pieces onto each of eight skewers with the skin facing out, without packing them tightly. Brush the grill grates with the neutral oil.
5. **Grill the kebabs:** Lay the skewers over the hot side, skin toward the grate first. Grill with the lid closed, turning every 2 to 3 minutes, until the skin is crisp and the largest pieces reach 165°F, 10 to 15 minutes. Move them to the cooler side if dripping fat flares up.
6. **Rest and serve:** Rest the kebabs on a platter for 5 minutes. Serve with the chile-yogurt sauce and lemon wedges.
