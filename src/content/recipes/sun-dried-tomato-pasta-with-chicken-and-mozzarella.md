## Chef's Note

The sauce should coat the pasta, with the gentle stretch of mozzarella. Cook chicken separately to its temperature endpoint, then build the sauce at lower heat. Reserve pasta water so you can loosen the final mixture a little at a time; the whole reserved cup rarely needs to go in at once.

## Directions

1. **Start pasta water:** Bring the measured water and pasta salt to a boil while preparing the other ingredients. Add penne and cook according to the package to al dente, coordinating it with the sauce. Reserve one cup cooking water before draining.
2. **Cook chicken:** Season chicken with its measured salt and paprika. Heat one tablespoon tomato oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken in an even layer about 3 minutes on the first side, then turn and cook another 2–3 minutes, checking the thickest pieces for 165°F / 74°C. Work in batches if crowded. Transfer finished chicken to a clean plate.
3. **Cook aromatics gently:** Lower heat to medium-low. Add remaining oil, tomatoes, garlic, and red pepper flakes; stir for about 1 minute until fragrant without scorching.
4. **Build and combine:** Add half-and-half and bring only to a gentle simmer for about 2 minutes. Fold in drained penne, cooked chicken and its juices, and spinach. Cook gently, folding, until spinach wilts, about 1–2 minutes. Add a small splash of reserved pasta water if the mixture is dry.
5. **Melt cheese off heat:** Take the pan off heat and wait for bubbling to stop. Add mozzarella in small handfuls, folding gently as it softens. If necessary, return briefly to very low heat without boiling. Add pasta water a tablespoon or two at a time to reach a creamy coating; you may not use all of it. Fold in basil.
6. **Serve:** Taste before adding any further seasoning, then divide into four portions. Serve promptly while the sauce is loose and the cheese soft.
