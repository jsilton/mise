## Chef's Note

A quick weeknight pasta with chicken, spinach and sun-dried tomatoes in a light cream sauce. Searing the chicken in the oil from the tomato jar gives the whole dish its flavor. Melt the mozzarella off the heat so it turns soft and stretchy instead of clumping.

## Directions

1. **Boil the water:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.
2. **Season the chicken:** Toss the chicken with the salt and paprika.
3. **Sear the chicken:** Heat half the tomato oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken in an even layer and cook until browned on the first side, about 3 minutes. Turn and cook until it reaches 165°F, 2 to 3 minutes more. Move it to a plate.
4. **Cook the pasta:** While the chicken cooks, add the penne to the boiling water and cook until al dente. Scoop out a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
5. **Cook the aromatics:** Lower the heat under the skillet to medium-low. Add the rest of the tomato oil with the sun-dried tomatoes, garlic and pepper flakes and stir until fragrant, about 1 minute.
6. **Simmer the cream:** Pour in the half-and-half and simmer gently for 2 minutes.
7. **Add the pasta:** Fold in the penne, the chicken and its juices, and the spinach. Cook, folding, until the spinach wilts, 1 to 2 minutes.
8. **Melt the cheese:** Take the pan off the heat. Fold in the mozzarella a handful at a time until it softens into strands, adding splashes of pasta water until the sauce is creamy. Fold in the basil, taste for salt and serve right away.
