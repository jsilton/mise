## Chef's Note

A bone-in turkey breast gives you the holiday bird in about two hours, with no dark meat to wait on. A day-ahead dry brine seasons the meat all the way through and keeps it juicy. Herb butter under the skin bastes it as it roasts.

## Directions

1. **Dry-brine the breast:** A day ahead, pat the breast dry and gently loosen the skin without tearing it. Sprinkle most of the salt on the meat under the skin and the rest over the outside. Refrigerate uncovered on a rack set over a tray for 12 to 24 hours. If you are cooking the same day, salt it as early as you can.
2. **Butter the breast:** Heat the oven to 325°F. Mix the butter, herbs and pepper. Spread most of it under the skin and rub the rest over the outside. Set the breast skin side up on a rack in a shallow roasting pan and pour the stock into the pan.
3. **Roast:** Roast until the thickest part of each side reads 165°F, with the thermometer clear of the bone, 1 1/2 to 2 1/4 hours. Start checking at 75 minutes. Add a splash of hot water if the pan dries out, and tent the skin with foil if it browns too fast.
4. **Rest the turkey:** Move the breast to a carving board, tent it loosely with foil and rest 20 minutes.
5. **Reduce the pan juices:** While it rests, skim the fat off the pan liquid and pour it into a small saucepan. Boil until slightly reduced and full-flavored, about 5 minutes.
6. **Carve and serve:** Cut each half of the breast off the bone in one piece, then slice across the grain 1/2 inch thick. Serve with the pan juices.
