## Chef's Note

Spinach, pasta, and cheese all change the water balance of the pan. Start with a little reserved cooking water, let the spinach wilt, then decide whether more is needed. Lemon zest contributes aroma without much acidity; juice is added at the end so you can control the tartness.

## Directions

1. **Boil the pasta:** Bring 3 quarts water and the pasta-water salt to a boil. Add pasta and stir. Cook until about 1 minute short of the package's suggested time, tasting for a slightly firm center. Reserve 1 cup cooking water before draining.
2. **Cook chicken alongside:** Pat chicken dry and season with its measured salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a deep 12-inch skillet over medium-high. Cook in a single layer, turning occasionally, for about 6–9 minutes, until golden in patches and several larger pieces measure 165°F / 74°C. Work in batches if needed. Transfer to a clean plate.
3. **Build the sauce:** Reduce heat to medium and add the remaining oil. Stir in garlic for about 30 seconds, until fragrant but not brown. Add wine and scrape up golden residue. Simmer 2–4 minutes, until reduced by roughly half.
4. **Finish the pasta:** Add drained pasta and 1/3 cup reserved water to the skillet. Toss over medium-low heat for 1–2 minutes, until the pasta is just tender. Add a splash of water if it sticks.
5. **Wilt the greens:** Add spinach in two or three batches, tossing each until it begins to wilt. Return cooked chicken and its juices. Toss just until the chicken is hot and the spinach is fully wilted, about 2–3 minutes. Keep a little liquid in the pan; add reserved water only as needed.
6. **Add cheese and lemon:** Remove the skillet from heat and wait until active bubbling stops. Add Parmesan in two additions while tossing. Add the zest and the lower end of the listed lemon-juice range. Taste a complete bite, then add more juice, pepper, or a pinch of salt if needed. Loosen with water by the tablespoon and serve promptly.
