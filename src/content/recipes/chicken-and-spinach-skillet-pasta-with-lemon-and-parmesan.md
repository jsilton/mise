## Chef's Note

This is a light weeknight pasta from EatingWell, with seared chicken, a whole bag of spinach and a lemony Parmesan sauce. There's no cream. Starchy pasta water, wine and a little cheese make a glossy sauce that clings to every piece.

## Directions

1. **Boil the pasta:** Bring 3 quarts of water and the pasta-water salt to a boil. Add the pasta and cook until 1 minute short of al dente. Scoop out a mug of the cooking water, then drain.
2. **Sear the chicken:** While the pasta cooks, pat the chicken dry and season it with the fine salt and pepper. Heat half the oil in a deep 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken in a single layer, turning now and then, until golden in spots and 165°F in the center, 6 to 9 minutes. Move it to a plate.
3. **Reduce the wine:** Lower the heat to medium and add the rest of the oil. Stir in the garlic until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Pour in the wine, scrape up the browned bits and simmer until reduced by half, 2 to 4 minutes.
4. **Finish the pasta:** Add the drained pasta and a splash of the pasta water. Toss over medium-low heat until the pasta is just tender, 1 to 2 minutes.
5. **Wilt the spinach:** Add the spinach in two or three handfuls, tossing until each wilts. Return the chicken and its juices and toss until hot, 2 to 3 minutes. Add pasta water if the pan looks dry.
6. **Finish and serve:** Take the pan off the heat and let the bubbling stop. Toss in the Parmesan, lemon zest and lemon juice, adding pasta water a spoonful at a time until the sauce is glossy. Taste, add more lemon, salt or pepper, and serve right away.
