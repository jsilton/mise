## Chef's Note

Chocolate discs make larger pockets of chocolate; the same listed amount of chips is also an option. The candy-coated chocolates add sweetness and color, with separate amounts inside and on top. Use a deep pan so the dough has room to spread, and let the whole cookie cake cool before slicing.

## Directions

1. **Prep:** Preheat the oven to 375°F. For the original batch, line a 14-inch deep round pizza pan with parchment and use butter or baking spray to grease the entire pan including the paper. Have enough appropriate pans for the full dough before mixing; keep similar dough thickness and expansion room when scaling.
2. **Cream:** In a large bowl, cream unsalted butter, light brown sugar, and granulated sugar until light and fluffy, about 3–5 minutes.
3. **Add eggs:** Add large eggs and vanilla extract and beat until combined.
4. **Dry mix:** Whisk all-purpose flour, baking soda, and sea salt together, then stir into the butter mixture until just combined.
5. **Fold:** Fold in semisweet chocolate discs and candy-coated milk chocolate candies.
6. **Shape:** Spread all the dough evenly onto the prepared pan, leaving the original 1/2-inch border for expansion. Sprinkle all candy-coated milk chocolate candies over the top, pressing them in lightly. At other scales divide all dough and its topping proportionally among enough appropriate pans; do not force every batch into the same 14-inch pan.
7. **Bake:** Bake for 20–25 minutes until the edges are deep golden brown and the center is set but still soft. Keep the same dough thickness when scaling; use additional pans, rather than multiplying pan dimensions or baking time.
8. **Cool:** Let cool completely in the pan before slicing so the structure can firm up.
