## Chef's Note

This version blends a dense chia-and-almond-milk mixture rather than leaving the seeds whole. Blend to a mostly smooth texture using the actual appliance, then chill for cold serving. Chia may remain faintly visible; complete disappearance in a fixed sixty seconds is not a requirement.

## Directions

1. **Blend:** Add unsweetened vanilla almond milk, chia seeds, maple syrup, unsweetened cocoa powder, vanilla extract, and sea salt to the blender. Start low and increase speed as the appliance allows. Blend until the chia is mostly broken down and the mixture is smooth enough for the texture you prefer. Stop the motor before scraping. Follow the actual fill limit; for larger batches, divide every ingredient proportionally across loads and recombine.
2. **Chill:** Transfer all the pudding to suitable clean covered containers and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below for at least 3 hours, or overnight, for cold serving. The original batch makes three planned portions; finished volume is unmeasured.
3. **Serve:** Stir and add fresh berries, chocolate chips or chopped nuts (if using). Wash fresh berries before adding them. Serve cold and return remaining pudding promptly to the refrigerator.

## Cooking Notes

Replace the full listed maple volume with honey if desired; honey changes flavor and is not vegan. For another sweetener, follow its substitution instructions; sweetness and texture may differ.

For immediate serving, the same complete blended mixture can be eaten once thick enough, without the cold rest. For colder pudding, refrigerate 3–4 hours or overnight. These are alternative serving routes, not different milk-to-chia formulas. Keep fresh berries, chocolate chips and nuts as unmeasured optional toppings.
