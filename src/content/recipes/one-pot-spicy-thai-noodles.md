## Chef's Note

This is a fast weeknight noodle dish, Thai in spirit, made in one pot. Rice noodles take a sweet, salty soy and sriracha sauce with ginger and fish sauce, and lime, cilantro and peanuts go on at the end. That fresh finish is what keeps the sweet sauce bright.

## Directions

1. **Cook the noodles:** Cook the rice noodles in a large pot of boiling water according to the package until tender but still chewy. Drain and rinse under cool water so they don't stick.
2. **Mix the sauce:** Whisk the soy sauce, brown sugar, sriracha, fish sauce and ginger in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves.
3. **Scramble the eggs:** Dry the pot and heat half the oil over medium heat. Add the eggs and the red pepper flakes, if using, and scramble until just set, about 1 minute. Move them to a plate.
4. **Cook the vegetables:** Add the rest of the oil and turn the heat to medium-high. Add the zucchini, mushrooms, carrots and garlic and cook, stirring, until tender-crisp, about 5 minutes.
5. **Toss with the sauce:** Turn the heat to low. Add the noodles, eggs, chicken or shrimp if using, and the sauce. Toss until the noodles are coated and hot, 1 to 2 minutes.
6. **Finish and serve:** Take the pot off the heat. Stir in the lime juice, green onions and cilantro and top with the peanuts. Pass more sriracha at the table for anyone who wants it hotter.
