## Chef's Note

This is the Thai street salad of crisp, sour green mango tossed in lime, fish sauce, palm sugar and chile. Every bite should taste sour, salty, sweet and hot at once. Add the herbs and peanuts at the last minute so they stay fresh and crunchy.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dressing:** Whisk the lime juice, fish sauce, palm sugar, garlic and chiles until the sugar dissolves.
2. **Taste with the mango:** Dip a strand of mango in the dressing and taste. Add a little more sugar if the mango is very sour, or more lime if it is sweet.
3. **Toss and serve:** Toss the mango and scallions with enough dressing to coat them lightly. Fold in the basil, mint and cilantro, scatter the peanuts over the top and serve right away.
