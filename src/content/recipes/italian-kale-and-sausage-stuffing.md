## Chef's Note

Italian sausage, Parmesan and ciabatta give this American holiday stuffing its character. The milk-soaked bread makes a tender binder without an egg; it should retain some pieces rather than become a paste. Cook the kale until tender and manage the rendered fat before combining everything. Those two decisions matter more than adding extra liquid to compensate at the end.

## Directions

1. **Prepare bread and oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F / 177°C. Grease a 9-by-13-inch baking dish with one teaspoon of the oil. Put the ciabatta pieces in a large bowl, pour over the milk and turn gently. Let them hydrate for about twenty to thirty minutes while cooking the sausage and vegetables, turning once more so exposed dry pieces get milk too.
2. **Brown the sausage:** In a large Dutch oven over medium heat, break the sausage into small bite-sized pieces and cook about eight to twelve minutes, stirring periodically, until browned and cooked through. Check the largest pieces reach 160°F / 71°C for pork. Transfer to a clean bowl with a slotted spoon. Pour off excess fat, leaving about one tablespoon in the pot. If there is less, add enough of the optional olive oil to reach that amount.
3. **Soften aromatics:** Cook onion and celery in that fat over medium heat for seven to ten minutes, until softened, stirring and scraping the base without burning it. Add garlic and stir for thirty seconds.
4. **Tenderize kale:** Add kale in handfuls and the measured water. Cover and cook five to eight minutes, turning once, until a tasted piece is tender rather than fibrous. Uncover and cook off any puddle for another two to three minutes. Remove from heat.
5. **Combine and season:** Fold the cooked sausage, kale mixture, parsley, Parmesan, basil, sage, rosemary and pepper into the hydrated bread with a large spoon. Keep some bread texture; do not knead it smooth or mix hot ingredients with bare hands. Taste a cooled spoonful now that the sausage is fully cooked. Add the optional quarter-teaspoon fine salt only if needed. Spread loosely and evenly in the prepared dish; do not compress it into a dense block.
6. **Bake and check:** Bake immediately, uncovered, for about thirty to forty minutes. The center must reach at least 165°F / 74°C and the top should be lightly browned. Cover loosely if the top colors before the center is ready.
7. **Rest and serve:** Rest ten minutes, then spoon into eight side portions. Expect a moist, rustic casserole, not slices of firm bread pudding.

## Sharing an oven with turkey

At 325°F / 163°C, allow roughly forty to fifty-five minutes baking and check the same center temperature. Use this only if the shallow dish and turkey pan fit with space for air to circulate; keep the stuffing above the raw bird so drips cannot reach it. If they do not fit, plan a second oven or bake and safely reheat the stuffing separately. A lower oven setting is not a reason to accept a lower center temperature.
