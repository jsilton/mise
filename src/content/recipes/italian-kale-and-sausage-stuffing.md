## Chef's Note

An Italian-American take on Thanksgiving stuffing, with sweet sausage, Parmesan and fresh herbs. Soaking the ciabatta in milk gives it a soft, custardy middle under a crisp top, with no egg needed. Cook the kale until truly tender and dry so it adds a pleasant bitterness without making the bread soggy.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with a little olive oil.
2. **Soak the bread:** Put the ciabatta in a large bowl, pour the milk over it and turn gently. Let it soak while you cook the filling, turning once more so every piece gets milk.
3. **Brown the sausage:** Cook the sausage in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat, breaking it into small pieces, until browned with no pink left, 8 to 12 minutes. Lift it into a bowl with a slotted spoon. Pour off all but about a tablespoon of the fat.
4. **Soften the vegetables:** Lower the heat to medium. Add the onion and celery and cook, stirring and scraping the bottom, until soft, 7 to 10 minutes. Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
5. **Cook the kale:** Add the kale in handfuls with the water. Cover and cook, turning once, until tender, 5 to 8 minutes. Uncover and cook until no liquid is left in the pot, 2 to 3 minutes.
6. **Combine:** Fold the sausage, kale mixture, parsley, Parmesan, basil, sage, rosemary and pepper into the soaked bread, keeping some chunks of bread. Taste a spoonful and add the salt if it needs it. Spread loosely in the baking dish without packing it down.
7. **Bake:** Bake uncovered until the top is golden and the center is steaming hot, 30 to 40 minutes. Lay foil loosely over the top if it browns too fast.
8. **Rest and serve:** Let the stuffing rest for 10 minutes before serving.
