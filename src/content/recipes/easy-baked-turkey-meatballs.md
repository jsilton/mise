## Chef's Note

These are easy weeknight turkey meatballs for a crowd, baked on a sheet pan instead of fried. Breadcrumbs soaked in milk, Parmesan and carrot minced almost to a paste keep lean turkey juicy. A hot oven browns the outside before the centers can dry out.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment.
2. **Soak the crumbs:** In a large bowl, stir the breadcrumbs into the milk and let them soak until soft, about 5 minutes.
3. **Mix:** Add the turkey, Parmesan, carrots, onion, eggs, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt and red pepper flakes. Mix gently with your hands just until evenly combined.
4. **Shape:** Roll the mixture into balls about 1 1/2 inches across and set them on the baking sheets with space between.
5. **Bake:** Bake until browned and the centers reach 165°F, 15 to 18 minutes.
