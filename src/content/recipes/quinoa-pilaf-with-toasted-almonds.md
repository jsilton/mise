## Chef's Note

A quinoa pilaf built like a rice pilaf. Onion and garlic soften in fat, and the grains toast before the broth goes in, so the quinoa tastes nutty and savory. Toasted almonds, lemon zest and fresh herbs folded in at the end keep it bright and crunchy.

## Directions

1. **Toast the almonds:** In a wide 3-quart saucepan over medium heat, stir the almonds until fragrant and light golden, 3 to 4 minutes. Move them to a plate.
2. **Soften the onion:** Add half the oil or butter, the onion and a pinch of salt to the pan. Cook until soft and translucent, 3 to 4 minutes. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
3. **Toast the quinoa:** Add the quinoa and stir until the grains are dry and smell nutty, about 2 minutes.
4. **Simmer:** Pour in the broth and add the salt. Bring to a boil, cover tightly and turn the heat to low. Simmer until the liquid is absorbed and the grains are tender, about 15 minutes.
5. **Rest:** Take the pan off the heat and let it sit, covered, for 5 minutes.
6. **Finish:** Fluff with a fork. Fold in the almonds, the rest of the oil or butter, the parsley, pepper and lemon zest, plus the dried fruit and dill or tarragon, if using. Taste and add salt if it needs it.
