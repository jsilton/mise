## Chef's Note

Do chua is the sweet-tart carrot and daikon pickle tucked into every bánh mì and served beside Vietnamese rice plates. Following Andrea Nguyen's method, a quick salt and sugar massage softens the matchsticks so they bend without losing their crunch and soak up the brine.

## Directions

1. **Salt the vegetables:** In a bowl, toss the carrots and daikon with the salt and the salting sugar. Knead gently until liquid pools in the bowl and a daikon stick bends without snapping, about 3 minutes.
2. **Rinse and pack:** Rinse the vegetables in a colander under cold water and press gently to drain. Pack them into a 3-cup jar without crushing them.
3. **Make the brine:** Stir the brine sugar into the lukewarm water until it dissolves, then stir in the vinegar.
4. **Pickle:** Pour the brine over the vegetables and press them under the liquid. If they aren't covered, press them down or move them to a narrower jar. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, and overnight for the best flavor.
5. **Serve:** Lift out what you need with a fork and let the brine drain back into the jar.
