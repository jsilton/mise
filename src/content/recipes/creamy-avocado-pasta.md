## Chef's Note

Ripe avocado, basil, spinach and the full olive-oil amount make this sauce creamy without cream. Blend while the pasta cooks, then toss in a bowl off the heat. The reserved pasta water is one shared supply for blending and tossing: add only enough to loosen the sauce, rather than spending the full reserve twice.

## Directions

1. Wash the avocados under running water before cutting. Rinse fresh basil, spinach and optional tomatoes under running water; produce labeled prewashed or ready-to-eat may be used as directed. Drain the leaves. Cook pasta in a large pot of salted water until al dente according to its package. Use some of the listed salt allowance for the pot and keep the rest for the sauce. Reserve the listed pasta-water amount before draining.
2. While pasta cooks, scoop avocado flesh into a blender. Add basil, spinach, garlic, lemon juice and some of the remaining salt allowance.
3. Start on low, drizzle in the full olive-oil amount and blend until smooth, about 1 minute. If the blender needs help turning, add a little of the shared reserved water. Follow the actual blender instructions for warm liquids; cool any water destined for a sealed or heat-restricted blender first.
4. Put hot drained pasta in a large bowl off the heat. Add the green sauce and toss until the noodles are coated.
5. Add more of the same reserve a little at a time if needed, tossing after each addition. You may not need it all. Season with the remaining salt as desired and the listed black pepper.
6. Serve immediately, offering halved cherry tomatoes, Parmesan shavings and chopped fresh basil as optional toppings.
