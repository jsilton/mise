## Chef's Note

Ripe avocado blended with basil, spinach, lemon and olive oil makes a bright green sauce as creamy as any cream sauce. The heat of the just-drained pasta warms it without cooking it, so the color stays vivid. Make it right before you eat, because the sauce darkens as it sits.

## Directions

1. **Boil the pasta:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook until al dente. Scoop out the pasta cooking water, then drain.
2. **Blend the sauce:** While the pasta cooks, put the avocado flesh, basil, spinach, garlic, lemon juice and salt in a blender. Blend on low while you drizzle in the olive oil, then blend until smooth and fluffy, about 1 minute. Add a splash of the pasta water if the blades stall.
3. **Toss:** Put the hot pasta in a large bowl and pour the sauce over it. Toss hard until every strand is glossy and coated, adding pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce clings without pooling.
4. **Serve:** Season with pepper and more salt to taste. Serve right away, topped with cherry tomatoes, Parmesan and chopped basil.
