## Chef's Note

This Delish stir-fry tosses rice noodles, shrimp and snow peas in sweet chili sauce, soy and fresh ginger. A little of the chili sauce goes on the shrimp first so they sear with caramelized edges. Cook the shrimp first and set them aside so they stay tender while the noodles finish.

## Directions

1. **Soak the noodles:** Put the rice linguine in a large heatproof bowl and cover with hot, not boiling, water. Soak until flexible but still a little firm, about 25 minutes, then drain and rinse with cold water.
2. **Season the shrimp:** While the noodles soak, pat the shrimp dry, season lightly with salt and pepper and toss with the sweet chili sauce for the shrimp. Refrigerate for 15 minutes. Stir the rest of the sweet chili sauce with the soy sauce in a small bowl.
3. **Sear the shrimp:** Heat half the oil in a 12-inch skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Sear the shrimp in one layer until pink and charred at the edges, about 1 minute per side. Move them to a plate.
4. **Stir-fry the peas:** Add the rest of the oil, the snow peas and the ginger. Stir-fry until the peas are bright green, 1 to 2 minutes. Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
5. **Toss the noodles:** Add the noodles and the chili-soy sauce. Toss until the noodles are hot, glossy and tender, 2 to 4 minutes, adding a splash of water if they stick.
6. **Finish and serve:** Return the shrimp and any juices and toss for 30 seconds. Serve right away.
