## Chef's Note

Start the rice noodles before preparing the vegetables and shrimp. Hydrate them according to their package, then finish in the pan with the sweet chili coating. Lift cooked shrimp out while the noodles finish so the two ingredients do not have to share one cooking time. Adapted from [Delish’s Shrimp & Snow Pea Stir-Fry](https://www.delish.com/cooking/recipe-ideas/a35131589/shrimp-and-snow-pea-stir-fry-recipe/).

## Directions

1. **Prepare noodles:** Follow the package instructions for stir-fried rice linguine. For a product calling for a hot-water soak, use a heatproof bowl and expect roughly 25 minutes; separate clumps gently with a fork. Drain when nearly tender, rinsing only if directed, and drain thoroughly.

2. **Marinate and prepare:** While noodles hydrate, pat shrimp dry and season lightly with salt and pepper. Toss with one-fifth of the sweet chili sauce and refrigerate for 15 minutes. Trim and slice peas, grate ginger and mince garlic. Mix the remaining four-fifths of the sweet chili sauce with the measured soy sauce in a clean bowl.

3. **Cook shrimp:** Heat half the oil over medium-high. Lift shrimp from the marinade, letting excess drip away; discard the raw-contact marinade. Cook in a single layer about 1–2 minutes per side, as needed, until firm, pearly and opaque through the center; a fine-tip thermometer inserted into a thick shrimp should read 145°F / 63°C. Transfer to a clean plate. Lower heat if the sweet coating darkens rapidly.

4. **Cook vegetables:** Add remaining oil, snow peas and ginger. Stir-fry about 2 minutes until the peas brighten and retain a little bite. Add garlic and stir for about 30 seconds without letting it burn.

5. **Finish noodles:** Add drained noodles and the clean chili-soy mixture. Lift and turn gently for about 2–4 minutes until hot and tender; use a small splash of water if they stick or need further softening. Avoid vigorous stirring that tears the strands.

6. **Combine:** Return shrimp and plate juices only long enough to warm through, about 30 seconds. Taste before adding any further salt and pepper. Divide noodles and the actual shrimp count evenly among bowls and serve immediately.
