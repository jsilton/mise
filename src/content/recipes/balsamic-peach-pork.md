## Chef's Note

Keep the full balsamic–honey sauce, feta and basil with these peach-topped pork chops. Give the chops a gentle stove finish before the brief broiler stage instead of trying to finish a cold center while a thick glaze scorches. Watch the peaches and sauce closely. Cook the pork chops to at least 145°F before removal, checking every thickest center away from the bone, then rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Directions

1. **Heat broiler and mix sauce:** Preheat the broiler to high using the oven’s instructions. Whisk Balsamic vinegar, Honey, and Fresh thyme leaves in a small clean bowl and keep separate from raw-pork utensils.
2. **Sear and gently finish chops:** Have Bone-in pork chops, Extra-virgin olive oil, Salt, and Black pepper ready. Season the chops with the listed salt and pepper allowances. Heat the measured oil in a broiler-safe skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Sear the chops about 3–4 minutes per side until golden, using more pan space or batches if crowded. Reduce to medium and continue gently cooking, beginning checks during the next 8–10 minutes. Check each thickest center away from the bone; allow longer as needed rather than adding a thick sweet glaze while the centers still require a long cook.
3. **Add sauce and peaches:** When the chops are close to their 145°F endpoint and need only a short finish, pour the prepared sauce over them and simmer gently about 2 minutes, watching its consistency. Remove the pan from the burner and arrange Fresh peaches over and around the chops.
4. **Keep sauce from reducing too far:** Keep Water (if using) ready as an optional whole-batch amount. Add only if the sauce is becoming too thick to remain saucy. If using multiple pans, divide the selected whole amount rather than adding it anew to each pan. Watch again during broiling; reduce exposure or remove the pan promptly if the glaze is blackening.
5. **Broil and verify centers:** Transfer the broiler-safe pan under the broiler and begin checking after a couple of minutes, using approximately 4–5 minutes only as a check window. Peaches should be lightly charred at the edges and every chop must reach at least 145°F /63°C in its thickest center, away from bone, before removal. If a chop is ready before the fruit, move it with clean utensils to a clean resting plate and finish the fruit separately; if fruit is ready before a chop, move the whole pan to gentle stove heat and finish the chop with the fruit and sauce still in the pan, watching the sauce instead of continuing to burn the glaze.
6. **Add feta and rest:** Top with Feta cheese, Fresh basil, and Crushed red pepper flakes. Rest every ready chop for 5 minutes after reaching 145°F, at least the minimum 3 minutes, then serve with peaches and all the pan sauce.
