## Chef's Note

Render pancetta before building the sauce, and keep the crisp pieces aside for serving. Cook the peas fully, then let the pan stop sizzling before adding ricotta and Parmesan. Warm cooking water loosens the sauce; it need not become perfectly smooth to coat the orecchiette well.

## Directions

1. **Prepare and heat water:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Mince shallot and garlic, zest the lemon and measure its listed juice amount, and grate Parmesan. Season pasta water moderately, remembering the pancetta and cheese.
2. **Render pancetta:** Put pancetta in a cold skillet and set over medium heat. Cook, stirring, about **8–12 minutes**, or as its package directs, until cooked through and crisp. Remove with a slotted spoon to a clean plate. Pour off excess fat into a heatproof container, retaining about **1 1/2–2 tablespoons of fat for each 4 ounces of pancetta cooked**, or what it releases if less. This is a ratio: retain proportionally less for a smaller batch and more for a larger one.
3. **Build the base:** Over medium-low, soften shallot in the retained fat for about **3–5 minutes**. If there is very little fat and it sticks, add a small splash of water. Add garlic for **30–45 seconds**, then wine or stock. Simmer about **2 minutes**, scraping browned bits, until reduced by roughly half. Turn off heat while pasta finishes.
4. **Cook pasta and peas:** Meanwhile boil pasta according to package directions until tender with a little bite; do not drain early for an unheated finish. Cook frozen peas according to their package, or simmer fresh peas separately until tender, then drain. Reserve **the listed pasta-water amount** before draining pasta. Combine cooked hot pasta and peas with the aromatic base.
5. **Finish away from heat:** Move the skillet completely off the burner and wait until it no longer sizzles; transfer to a large bowl if the pan stays very hot. Stir in ricotta, Parmesan, lemon zest, **half the measured lemon juice** and **half the reserved pasta water**. Toss gently until creamy, adding more water in small amounts only if needed.
6. **Taste and plate:** Add black pepper, then taste before adding salt and the remaining lemon juice. Divide into the intended portions and top with the crisp pancetta. Serve immediately.
