## Chef's Note

This cranberry crunch comes from the Hamilton/Gwock family, who make it for the holidays. Tart cranberries and apples bake under a sweet, buttery oat topping. It's done when the fruit bubbles up around the edges.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Spray a 9×13-inch baking dish with cooking spray.
2. **Add the fruit:** Spread the apples and cranberries in the bottom of the dish and cover them with the sugar.
3. **Mix the topping:** Mix the oatmeal, brown sugar, flour, melted margarine and pecans together.
4. **Top the fruit:** Spread the topping thinly over the fruit.
5. **Bake:** Bake until bubbly, 45 minutes to 1 hour.

## Suggested Tweak

- Use unsalted butter in place of the margarine for a richer topping.
- Use tart Granny Smith apples.
- Toss the fruit gently with the sugar so it coats evenly.
- Stir the melted butter into the topping until it looks like wet sand, then press the topping down lightly over the fruit.
- Bake until the topping is deep golden and a knife slides easily into the center apples.
- Let it rest 10 minutes before serving so the juices thicken.
