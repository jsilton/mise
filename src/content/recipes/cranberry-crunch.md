## Chef's Note

Hamilton/Gwock’s cranberry and apple crisp keeps its full buttery oat topping. Bake until the apples are tender, the juices bubble through and the topping is browned; the hot juices settle as the crisp rests before serving.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. For the original batch, lightly grease a 9×13-inch dish with the separate pan allowance. Wash apples and cranberries, discard damaged fruit, and peel and thinly slice the apples before measuring the full listed amount. Measure cranberries by the listed volume; bag sizes differ.
2. **Fruit:** Put all the measured apples and cranberries in the dish. Sprinkle all the granulated sugar over them and toss gently to coat.
3. **Topping:** Combine all the oats, brown sugar, flour and chopped pecans. Stir in the full measured melted butter until the topping is evenly moistened.
4. **Layer:** Spread all the topping thinly and evenly over the fruit. For other scales, divide all fruit, sugar and topping proportionally among appropriate dishes with similar layer depth and headroom.
5. **Bake:** Bake about 45–60 minutes as the original-batch planning range, until apples are tender when pierced, juices bubble through and the topping is browned. Check the center fruit as well as the edges; continue if still firm.
6. **Rest:** Let stand about 10 minutes before serving; the hot juices settle as they cool.
