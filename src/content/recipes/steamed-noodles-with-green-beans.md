## Chef's Note

Bian dou men mian uses a covered pan to finish noodles with green beans and meat. The pork coating seasons the meat first; the separate braising mixture supplies the moisture and seasoning that finish the noodles. Green beans should be cooked tender rather than stopped at the first bright-green color; the finished dish relies on chewy noodles for its firmer texture.

## Directions

1. **Coat and prepare:** Mix pork with the coating's light soy, cornstarch, Shaoxing wine and white pepper. Refrigerate for about 20 minutes while preparing beans and aromatics. Mix the separate braising soy sauces, sugar and 240 ml water.
2. **Steam the noodles:** Bring water below a steamer rack to a steady boil. Use a parchment liner with holes and space around its edges so steam can circulate. Loosely arrange the fresh noodles, toss with 2 tsp of the oil and steam covered for about 10–12 minutes, until flexible. The water must remain below the rack. Check it does not boil dry and handle escaping steam carefully.
3. **Brown pork before delicate aromatics:** Heat the remaining 4 tsp oil in a deep 12-inch pan over medium-high. Add pork and cook 3–4 minutes, turning, until it has some browned patches. Spoon off excessive rendered fat if needed, leaving a thin film for the next step. Lower to medium, add ginger and star anise for 30 seconds, then garlic and scallion whites for another 30 seconds.
4. **Start the beans and braise:** Add green beans and stir for 3 minutes. Pour in the measured braising liquid and scrape the base of the pan. Bring to a simmer, spread the beans evenly, then lay the steamed noodles loosely on top without stirring down into the liquid. Cover tightly and maintain a gentle active simmer over medium-low.
5. **Check both textures:** After 8 minutes, open the lid away from you and taste a cooled noodle and bean. Continue covered cooking, usually another 2–4 minutes, until noodles have no floury core and beans are tender. If the pan is drying while either is firm, add hot water around the edge 2 tbsp at a time before covering again. Do not use disappearance of all liquid as the only stopping rule.
6. **Finish and verify:** Fold the noodles through the pork and beans. Check sliced pork reaches at least 145°F / 63°C; after reaching that temperature, give them at least 3 minutes before serving. The chicken variation must instead reach 165°F / 74°C. Remove and discard the star anise, stir in scallion greens and adjust excess moisture briefly over the heat if needed. Divide among four bowls.
