## Chef's Note

Removing the backbone and flattening the chicken exposes more skin and can shorten roasting time. Breast and leg meat still differ in thickness, so check each required temperature site rather than assuming they finish together. Use a thermometer and remove the bird once all required sites reach 165°F /74°C. The listed rub is intentionally substantial; kosher salt crystal sizes vary, so retain the chosen brand rather than replacing its volume with the same volume of fine salt.

## Directions

1. **Remove the backbone:** Keep the fully thawed chicken cold until preparation. Place it breast-side down, with the backbone facing up. Cut along both sides of the backbone with sturdy kitchen shears or a suitable knife, and remove it. If saving it for stock, promptly refrigerate in a covered raw-poultry container for use within 1–2 days, or freeze it; stock can also be made without a backbone.

2. **Flatten:** Turn the chicken skin-side up and press firmly on the breastbone to flatten it. Use a board large enough to contain the bird and keep raw juices away from ready-to-eat food. Pat the skin dry with paper towels and discard the towels.

3. **Apply the whole rub:** Mix all the kosher salt, black pepper, garlic, herbs and optional paprika with all the listed rub olive oil. Rub all of this paste over the bird, including under gently loosened breast skin, the legs and the skin surface. The original batch uses 2 tbsp fresh rosemary or thyme, or its distinct 1 tbsp dried option; scale the chosen option proportionally. Keep the full rub quantity.

4. **Optional refrigerated seasoning:** Roast directly after preparation, or refrigerate the seasoned bird on a rimmed tray for 30–60 minutes or up to 8 hours. Leave it uncovered for drying only where it is protected from contamination and cannot drip onto other food. Do not use a counter warm-up as a substitute for refrigerator thawing.

5. **Preheat and prepare the pan:** Preheat the oven to 475°F with the rack in the lower-middle position. Use a rimmed metal pan or roasting pan rated for 475°F. If using an optional baking stone or steel, heat and position it according to its manufacturer’s instructions. Grease the roasting pan with all the 1 tbsp olive oil listed for the pan, and add all the ½ cup water or stock. These are original-batch amounts, scaled with the ingredients; pan geometry is a separate choice.

6. **Roast skin-side up:** Place the flattened bird skin-side up with room around it. Roast at 475°F and start checking after 35–45 minutes; actual birds and ovens can take longer. For larger batches, roast additional original-size 4–5 lb birds in enough rated pans with airflow, checking each separately. Ingredient scaling does not turn a fractional bird or larger bird into the same geometry or multiply the roasting clock.

7. **Check all sites:** Measure the thickest part of each breast and the innermost thighs and wings without touching bone. Every checked site must reach at least 165°F / 74°C before removal. Recheck cooler areas after further roasting; the timer and skin color do not establish doneness. If using the optional convection finish for skin color, follow the oven instructions and watch closely while the bird is still cooking; stop once all required sites reach 165°F rather than adding a mandatory 10–15 minutes after it is done.

8. **Rest and make optional sauce:** Transfer the cooked bird to a clean board with a lip and rest 10 minutes before carving. For the optional pan sauce, put the roasting pan on a burner only if its manufacturer permits stovetop use; otherwise carefully transfer the hot drippings and scraped browned bits into a saucepan. Heat the poultry-contact liquid to 165°F, then simmer, starting with 2–3 minutes, until slightly reduced. Taste with a clean spoon and use the separate optional sauce salt and pepper allowances if needed. Strain if desired.

9. **Carve and serve:** Carve into two leg quarters and two breast portions, or into smaller pieces as preferred. Serve with all the lemon wedges and the optional finishing salt, and spoon over the optional cooked pan sauce. Four portions are a planning yield for the original 4–5 lb bird, with sides; bird size and appetite vary. Refrigerate promptly in shallow covered containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F). Use within 3–4 days, or freeze portions for later. Reheat hot leftovers to 165°F throughout. For reheated optional pan sauce, USDA also recommends bringing sauces and gravies to a rolling boil. Stir for even heating and avoid prolonged boiling that over-reduces the jus. These handling limits do not promise unchanged texture.
