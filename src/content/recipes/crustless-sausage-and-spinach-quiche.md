## Chef's Note

Wilt spinach with the cooked sausage, then drain the released water before adding the custard. Nutmeg seasons the milk and eggs without turning the filling sweet. Use the listed total sausage weight; links vary in size between packages.

## Directions

1. **Prep:** Preheat the oven to 350 °F and generously spray the 9-inch pie dish. Use the listed total sausage weight. Link sizes vary; the three-link note describes the original 8.5 oz batch. Have a sieve ready for the cooked spinach.

2. **Cook the sausage:** Cook the raw chicken sausage in a large skillet over medium heat, breaking it into small crumbles, until cooked through and reaching 165 °F / 74 °C. About 5 minutes is a first estimate, not a guarantee; browning alone does not establish the endpoint.

3. **Wilt and drain:** Add the spinach in manageable handfuls, reduce heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, until fully wilted, about 7 minutes. Drain the sausage-spinach mixture in a sieve and press gently to release excess water; do not treat this as a requirement to strip away every bit of fat.

4. **The Custard:** Whisk the eggs, milk, half-and-half, measured kosher salt, pepper and nutmeg until smoothly blended without separate egg-white streaks. Do not whip to a thick foam.

5. **Assemble:** Spread the drained sausage and spinach in the prepared dish and distribute all the cheddar or Havarti and Parmesan over it.

6. **Pour:** Pour the custard over the filling, leaving space below the rim. Nine-inch dishes vary in depth; do not force an enlarged batch into this dish. For multiples, use separate original-size dishes; reduced batches need an appropriately smaller dish and earlier center checks.

7. **Bake:** Bake about 35–40 minutes as a first range, until the center is set with no liquid egg and measures 165 °F / 74 °C, checking without touching the dish. Edges may be lightly golden and puffed. Continue if the center has not reached the endpoint rather than relying on color.

8. **Rest:** Rest 5 minutes before slicing. The full original batch makes six wedges; apportion a different batch size accordingly. Refrigerate leftovers promptly in shallow containers, use within 3–4 days and reheat centers to 165 °F / 74 °C.
