## Chef's Note

A crustless quiche of crumbled Italian chicken sausage, spinach and two cheeses in a milk and egg custard. The secret to a quiche that isn't watery is pressing every drop of liquid out of the wilted spinach. A pinch of nutmeg is the French touch that makes the custard taste rich without making it sweet.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Spray a 9-inch pie dish well with cooking spray.
2. **Brown the sausage:** Cook the sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it into small crumbles, until browned with no pink left, about 5 minutes.
3. **Wilt the spinach:** Lower the heat to medium and add the spinach a few handfuls at a time. Toss until just wilted, about 3 minutes. Tip the mixture into a sieve and press firmly to squeeze out the liquid.
4. **Make the custard:** In a medium bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, half-and-half, salt, pepper and nutmeg until smooth.
5. **Fill the dish:** Spread the sausage and spinach in the pie dish and scatter the cheddar and Parmesan over the top. Pour the custard over everything.
6. **Bake:** Bake until the center is set and the edges are puffed and light golden, 35 to 40 minutes.
7. **Rest and slice:** Let the quiche rest 5 minutes, then cut it into wedges.
