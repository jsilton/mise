## Chef's Note

A dry surface helps the flour and egg coating adhere. Keep the sticks apart so the panko can brown; the zucchini should be tender inside, with an egg coating that is fully cooked rather than wet.

## Directions

1. **Dry the zucchini:** Wash the zucchini before cutting. Cut into roughly ½-inch-thick sticks. Sprinkle with all the listed kosher salt, stand on a clean towel for 5 minutes, then blot the surface dry. This uses the measured salt once; do not add another dose to the flour.

2. **Bread:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. Prepare enough parchment-lined baking sheets for a single uncrowded layer. Put all the flour and black pepper in one shallow bowl, all the beaten eggs in a second, and all the panko, Parmigiano-Reggiano, garlic powder and Italian seasoning in a third.

3. **Coat:** Coat a few sticks at a time in flour, shake off the excess, dip in egg, then press into the panko mixture. Keep the breading bowls and utensils away from ready-to-eat dips.

4. **Arrange:** Arrange in a single layer without touching. Use additional sheets or batches when scaling; keep the stick size the same.

5. **Oil:** Brush all the listed olive oil over the coated sticks, turning carefully to distribute it over both sides.

6. **Bake:** Bake for about 18–22 minutes, turning around halfway, until the coating is golden and set and a thick stick is tender. Check representative thick centers for 160°F / 71°C as a supplemental egg-dish endpoint; continue if necessary. Time depends on the actual stick thickness and loaded pans.

7. **Serve:** Serve promptly with marinara, ranch or fresh lemon if desired. Discard unused raw-egg breading mixtures; do not use them as dips.
