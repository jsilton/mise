## Chef's Note

Cook the peppers and onions before adding the eggs so their released moisture can evaporate. Warm tortillas fold more easily; keep the filling in a compact strip and leave room to tuck in the sides. The full cheese belongs inside both fresh and make-ahead burritos.

## Directions

1. **Cook vegetables:** Use already-cooked bacon or breakfast sausage. If it is a refrigerated leftover, reheat it separately to 165°F / 74°C before assembling; follow the package instructions for a ready-to-eat product. Melt all the butter in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add all the diced pepper and onion and cook for 3–4 minutes, stirring, until softened. Let visible watery juices cook away before adding eggs.

2. **Scramble:** Whisk all the eggs with the milk and listed salt. Pour over the vegetables. Fold gently from the edges toward the center until the curds are set with no liquid egg remaining; check the egg mixture reaches 160°F / 71°C. Start checking after 2–3 minutes, then remove from heat once cooked rather than leaving the curds in the hot pan.

3. **Warm tortillas:** In a separate dry skillet over medium-high heat, warm each tortilla for about 15–20 seconds per side until pliable. Keep warmed tortillas covered while finishing the rest.

4. **Fill and roll:** Divide the complete egg-and-vegetable mixture, all the cheese and the chosen complete bacon OR sausage quantity evenly among the listed tortillas. For the original four-tortilla batch, each gets one quarter of each filling. Add optional salsa, hot sauce, sour cream or avocado sparingly, or serve them alongside. Fold the bottom edge over the filling, tuck in both sides, then roll tightly. For a scaled batch divide among the listed tortilla count rather than using four portions.

5. **Serve or chill:** Serve promptly. For make-ahead burritos, include the cheese, omit wet optional toppings until serving, and refrigerate the burritos promptly in a single layer in shallow containers; do not leave a foil-wrapped pile to cool on the counter. Once chilled, wrap individually and freeze if desired. Reheat refrigerated or frozen burritos until the center reaches 165°F / 74°C. A 375°F oven is the oven option; use an oven-safe tray and cover or uncover as needed for the tortilla, checking the center rather than relying on 20 minutes from frozen.
