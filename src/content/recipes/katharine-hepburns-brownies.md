## Chef's Note

Katharine Hepburn's famous brownie uses just a quarter cup of flour, so it bakes up dense and fudgy instead of cakey. Whisking the cocoa into hot melted butter blooms it for a deeper chocolate flavor. Pull the pan while the center still has a slight jiggle.

## Directions

1. **Melt the butter and cocoa:** Heat the oven to 325°F and butter an 8×8-inch pan. Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over low heat, then whisk in the cocoa until smooth and glossy. Take it off the heat and let cool for 5 minutes.
2. **Whisk in the eggs:** Whisk the eggs into the chocolate mixture one at a time, then stir in the vanilla and salt.
3. **Fold in the dry ingredients:** Whisk the sugar and flour together in a small bowl. Add them to the saucepan with the nuts and stir with a spatula just until no dry flour shows.
4. **Bake:** Spread the batter in the pan. Bake until the center has a slight jiggle and a toothpick comes out with moist crumbs, 30 to 35 minutes.
5. **Cool and cut:** Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before cutting into squares.
