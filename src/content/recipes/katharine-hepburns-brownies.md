## Chef's Note

The small flour measure keeps these brownies dense and fudgy. Keep all the butter, sugar and nuts. Stop mixing once the flour disappears; the center should set while a toothpick still carries moist crumbs, rather than liquid batter. Cool completely before cutting.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Heat the oven to 325°F. For the original batch, grease an 8×8-inch square pan with the separate additional butter. Melt all the measured butter over low heat in a saucepan and stir in all the cocoa until smooth. Remove from the heat and cool for about 5 minutes, until warm rather than hot; transfer to a bowl large enough for the complete mixture.

2. **Add eggs:** Whisk in all the eggs one at a time, then all the vanilla and the pinch of salt.

3. **Fold:** Combine all the sugar, flour and chopped walnuts or pecans in a separate bowl. Stir them into the cocoa mixture just until no dry flour remains.

4. **Bake:** Spread all the batter evenly in the prepared pan. Begin checking at 30 minutes; the original batch usually needs 30–35 minutes. The center should be set and fudgy, without a liquid wobble, and a toothpick should carry moist crumbs rather than wet batter. A chocolate smear can differ from raw batter; check another nearby spot if uncertain.

5. **Cool:** Cool completely in the pan before cutting into the listed portions. Chill after cooling if you prefer a firmer slice.
