## Chef's Note

This is the Allrecipes classic, made chunkier with a cup of walnuts. Dissolving the baking soda in a little hot water spreads it evenly through the dough. The cookies bake up with crisp brown edges and soft, chewy centers.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. **Cream the butter:** Beat the butter, white sugar and brown sugar in a large bowl until smooth and creamy. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
3. **Add the baking soda:** Dissolve the baking soda in the hot water in a small cup. Stir it into the butter mixture along with the salt.
4. **Add the flour:** Stir in the flour just until no dry pockets remain. Stir in the chocolate chips and walnuts.
5. **Portion the dough:** Drop large spoonfuls of dough onto ungreased baking sheets, about 2 inches apart.
6. **Bake the cookies:** Bake one sheet at a time until the edges are browned and the centers are set but still soft, about 10 minutes.
7. **Cool the cookies:** Let the cookies firm on the sheet until they lift cleanly, about 2 minutes, then move them to a rack to cool.
