## Chef's Note

Dissolve the baking soda in the listed hot water before adding it to this walnut-and-chocolate dough. Keep the portions similar in size and leave room for them to spread; browned edges and a center that has set are more useful than a timer alone.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F / 175°C. Set out ungreased baking sheets and a cooling rack.

2. **Cream:** Beat the softened butter with the white sugar and packed brown sugar until smooth and creamy, scraping the bowl. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then mix in the vanilla.

3. **Dissolve:** In a small cup, dissolve the baking soda in the listed hot water. Stir this mixture and the salt into the butter mixture.

4. **Mix and portion:** Stir in the flour just until no dry pockets remain, then distribute the chocolate chips and chopped walnuts throughout. Drop similarly sized large spoonfuls onto the ungreased sheets, leaving about 2 inches between portions. Keep dough waiting for later trays covered and refrigerated.

5. **Bake:** Load the oven only once it has reached temperature. Bake for about 10 minutes per tray, until the edges are browned and the centers have set but remain soft. Check the first tray closely; scoop size and your pan affect the time. Bake the remaining dough in further loads as needed.

6. **Cool:** Let the cookies firm on the sheet until they can be lifted intact, then transfer them to a rack. Let the sheets cool before loading more dough. Cool the cookies completely before packing them.
