## Chef's Note

Pumpkin puree gives the batter moisture and body. Whisk the pumpkin mixture smooth before flour enters, then stir the dry ingredients in only until fully moistened. Small hydrated lumps are fine; cook modest portions until their middles are set as well as their surfaces browned.

## Directions

1. **Dry mix:** Whisk together the flour, brown sugar, baking powder, baking soda, pumpkin pie spice, and salt in a medium bowl. Break up any brown-sugar lumps so the sugar and leaveners are evenly distributed.
2. **Wet mix:** In a large bowl, whisk the milk, pumpkin puree, egg, and the measured vegetable or avocado oil until evenly blended and smooth.
3. **Combine:** Add the dry mixture gradually to the pumpkin mixture, stirring with a spatula until no dry flour pockets remain on the bottom or sides. Stop once combined; small hydrated lumps may remain. Do not beat the flour batter smooth.
4. **Rest:** Let the batter sit for 5 minutes while the griddle heats.
5. **Prepare the pan:** Heat a skillet or griddle over medium heat. Lightly grease with the separate butter or oil-spray allowance; the measured oil all belongs in the batter.
6. **Portion:** Drop batter by 1/4 cupfuls, leaving space to turn the pancakes. If it mounds, spread each portion gently with the back of the scoop so its center is not much thicker than the edges.
7. **Cook and turn:** Cook until the edges look set and the underside is lightly browned, with small bubbles on top, about 2 minutes. Flip gently and cook about 1–2 minutes more, until the middle is cooked through with no wet batter. If the outside browns too quickly, lower the heat and keep cooking the center. Repeat with the remaining batter, renewing the light pan coating as needed.
8. **Serve:** Serve immediately with salted butter and warm maple syrup if desired.
