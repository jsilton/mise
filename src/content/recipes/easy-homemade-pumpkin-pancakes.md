## Chef's Note

Fall pancakes with real pumpkin and warm spice in every bite. The puree makes the batter heavy, so stir in the flour only until it disappears and let the lumps be. A short rest lets the batter thicken and the pancakes rise tall and tender.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dry ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk the flour, brown sugar, baking powder, baking soda, pumpkin pie spice and salt, breaking up any lumps of sugar.
2. **Mix the wet ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the milk, pumpkin, egg and oil until smooth.
3. **Make the batter:** Add the dry mixture to the pumpkin mixture and stir with a spatula just until no dry flour remains. Small lumps are fine.
4. **Rest and heat:** Let the batter rest 5 minutes while a griddle or large skillet heats over medium heat.
5. **Cook the pancakes:** Grease the griddle lightly. Scoop the batter with a quarter-cup measure and spread each mound a little so the center isn't thick. Cook until bubbles form and the edges look dry, about 2 minutes. Flip and cook until golden and set in the middle, 1 to 2 minutes more. Grease the griddle again between batches.
6. **Serve:** Serve hot with salted butter and maple syrup.
