## Chef's Note

Sara Welch's caprese pasta from Dinner at the Zoo turns the Capri salad of tomato, mozzarella and basil into a quick summer dinner. The tomatoes only warm through, and the mozzarella goes in off the heat, so it softens into creamy pieces instead of melting into strings. Make it when tomatoes are at their ripest.

## Directions

1. **Boil the water:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.
2. **Warm the tomatoes:** Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and the oregano, if using, and cook until fragrant but not browned, 30 to 60 seconds. Add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt and cook until warm but still holding their shape, 2 to 3 minutes. Take the skillet off the heat.
3. **Cook the pasta:** Add the angel hair to the boiling water and cook until just tender, 2 to 3 minutes. Scoop out a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
4. **Toss:** Add the pasta and lemon juice to the tomatoes, off the heat, and lift with tongs to coat. Add a splash of the pasta water if it looks dry. Let it stand until it stops steaming hard, 1 to 2 minutes.
5. **Fold in the cheese:** Gently fold in the mozzarella and basil. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper and serve right away.
