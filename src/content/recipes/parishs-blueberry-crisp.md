## Chef's Note

Breanna Kinkead created this brown butter crisp at Parish in Atlanta, and the AJC printed it for home cooks. The butter is browned, strained and chilled solid before it goes into the flour, which gives the crumble a deep, nutty crunch. Kinkead just scatters the crumble on top and lets the fruit bubble through, so use as much as you like.

## Directions

1. **Heat the ice cream base:** Heat half-and-half and small vanilla bean in a large, heavy saucepan until it reaches 175°F. Take out the vanilla bean.
2. **Sweeten the base:** Stir in granulated sugar and salt until dissolved.
3. **Cook the custard:** Whisk eggs in a small bowl. Whisk in a few spoonfuls of the hot mixture, then keep adding it until the eggs are warm. Pour the eggs into the saucepan and cook over low heat, whisking constantly, until the custard reaches 160°F and coats the back of a metal spoon, about 5 minutes.
4. **Chill the custard:** Take the pan off the heat and stir in heavy cream and vanilla extract. Pour it into a bowl, press plastic wrap onto the surface and cool it in an ice bath. Refrigerate until cold.
5. **Churn and freeze:** Churn the custard in an ice cream maker according to the machine's directions. Pack it into a container, cover and freeze for at least 4 hours.
6. **Brown the butter:** Melt unsalted butter in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Keep cooking until it turns brown and smells nutty, about 5 minutes. Strain it into a bowl to remove the browned milk solids. Set the bowl in an ice bath or the fridge until the butter is solid.
7. **Make the crumble:** Combine all-purpose flour, light brown sugar, granulated sugar, and salt in a large bowl. Cut in the solid browned butter until the mixture forms small clumps. Spread it on a sheet pan and refrigerate until needed. The crumble keeps up to a week, covered, in the fridge.
8. **Prepare the dish:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a 9-inch square baking dish.
9. **Heat the berries:** Put blueberries, water, and fresh lemon juice in a large saucepan over medium heat. Heat until the berries bubble around the edges.
10. **Thicken the filling:** Stir granulated sugar and cornstarch together in a small bowl. Add it to the berries and cook, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens, about 4 minutes.
11. **Bake:** Pour the fruit into the dish and scatter the crumble over the top, as thick or thin as you like. Bake until the crumble is golden brown and the fruit is bubbling throughout, about 30 minutes.
12. **Serve:** Let it cool slightly. Serve warm with the ice cream, if you made it.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. Two changes are worth trying. Brown 12 tbsp (1 1/2 sticks) butter instead of 6 tbsp, so the crumble clumps and crisps instead of baking up sandy. Use 2 tbsp cornstarch instead of 2 tsp, so the filling sets instead of running.
