## Chef's Note

Drained cucumber keeps tzatziki thick enough to cling to grilled meat or pita. Salting and squeezing remove some free water; they do not make cucumber permanently dry. Garlic strength varies, so taste again after a short refrigerated rest rather than adding more immediately.

## Directions

1. **Grate:** Wash cucumber and grate on the coarse holes of a box grater. Place in a fine-mesh strainer over a bowl and toss with half the measured salt.
2. **Drain and squeeze:** Leave for 5 minutes, then gather in a clean kitchen towel and squeeze until the flow of water slows to occasional drops. Discard the drained liquid.
3. **Mix:** Stir cucumber into yogurt with garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, and dill. Keep the remaining salt aside.
4. **Chill and balance:** Cover and refrigerate for 15 minutes. Stir, taste, and add the reserved salt only if needed. Serve as a sauce in small portions; the full batch yields approximately 2 1/2–3 cups depending on cucumber drainage and yogurt thickness.
