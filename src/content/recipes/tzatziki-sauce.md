## Chef's Note

Tzatziki is the Greek yogurt and cucumber sauce served with grilled meat and warm pita. Salting and squeezing the grated cucumber keeps the sauce thick, and a generous handful of dill makes it taste fresh. A short rest in the fridge lets the garlic mellow into the yogurt.

## Directions

1. **Salt the cucumber:** Grate the cucumber on the coarse holes of a box grater. Toss it with half the salt in a fine-mesh strainer set over a bowl and let it drain for 5 minutes.
2. **Squeeze it dry:** Gather the cucumber in a clean kitchen towel and squeeze hard until only a few drops come out. Discard the liquid.
3. **Mix the sauce:** Stir the cucumber into the yogurt with the garlic, olive oil, lemon juice and dill.
4. **Chill and season:** Cover and refrigerate for 15 minutes. Stir, taste and add the rest of the salt if it needs it.
