## Chef's Note

Honey makes this a deliberately sweet glaze, balanced by lime and a little cayenne. Cook over moderate heat and stop when the shrimp are ready; reduce a watery sauce separately if necessary. Adapted from [Bee Yinn Low’s Garlic Honey Lime Shrimp](https://rasamalaysia.com/garlic-honey-lime-shrimp/).

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Pat thawed shrimp dry. Mince garlic, chop the finishing herbs and measure honey and lime juice. Divide lime juice into two equal portions. Have separately prepared rice and vegetables ready for service.

2. **Warm the garlic:** Heat olive oil and butter in the skillet over medium heat. When the butter foams, add garlic and stir about 20–30 seconds until fragrant without browning it deeply.

3. **Start the shrimp:** Add shrimp in a single layer. Cook about 1–2 minutes until the underside changes color, then turn. Keep the garlic moving around the shrimp so it does not burn.

4. **Glaze and finish cooking:** Add honey, half the lime juice, salt and cayenne. Stir gently and cook about 1–3 minutes more, checking the thicker pieces early, until firm, pearly and opaque through the center; a fine-tip thermometer inserted into a thick shrimp should read 145°F / 63°C. If the sauce remains watery, transfer cooked shrimp to a clean plate and simmer the sauce briefly on its own. If too thick, loosen with a small splash of water.

5. **Brighten and serve:** Turn off the heat. Stir in the remaining lime juice and return any removed shrimp, tossing to coat. Scatter with parsley or cilantro and serve immediately. Divide the batch among rice-and-vegetable bowls for modest portions, or serve as two larger portions.
