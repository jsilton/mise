## Chef's Note

Bee Yinn Low's quick shrimp from Rasa Malaysia gets a sticky glaze of honey and lime, with plenty of garlic and a pinch of cayenne. The honey goes in only once the shrimp are nearly cooked, so it glazes without scorching. A last splash of lime keeps it bright.

## Directions

1. **Sear the shrimp:** Pat the shrimp dry. Heat the olive oil and butter in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until the butter foams. Add the shrimp in a single layer and cook until pink underneath, 1 to 2 minutes, then turn them.
2. **Add the garlic:** Scatter the garlic among the shrimp and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
3. **Glaze:** Turn the heat to medium. Add the honey, half the lime juice, the salt and the cayenne. Stir until the sauce bubbles into a sticky glaze and the shrimp are opaque in the center, 1 to 2 minutes. If the glaze is still thin when the shrimp are done, lift them out and simmer the sauce alone for a minute.
4. **Finish:** Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the rest of the lime juice, return any shrimp you lifted out and scatter with the herbs. Serve right away.
