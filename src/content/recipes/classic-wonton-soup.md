## Chef's Note

Cantonese wonton soup is all about tender, silky pork wontons in a clear, fragrant broth. Stir the filling hard in one direction until it turns sticky, which keeps it bouncy and in one piece as it cooks. It is best with our Cantonese wonton broth.

## Directions

1. **Make the filling:** In a large bowl, combine the pork, ginger, soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, scallions, broth, salt and white pepper. Stir hard in one direction with chopsticks until the mixture turns sticky and pasty, 2 to 3 minutes.
2. **Fold the wontons:** Put a small spoonful of filling in the center of a wrapper and wet the edges with water. Fold it in half into a rectangle, pressing out the air, then bring the two folded corners together and pinch them to seal. Keep the wrappers and finished wontons under a damp towel as you work.
3. **Heat the broth:** Bring the wonton broth to a simmer in a saucepan, taste it and season with salt. Add the bok choy and simmer until the stems are tender-crisp, 2 to 3 minutes.
4. **Season the bowls:** Put a few drops of sesame oil and a pinch of white pepper in each serving bowl.
5. **Boil the wontons:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the wontons in batches, stirring gently so they don't stick, until they float and the filling is firm and cooked through, 3 to 4 minutes.
6. **Serve:** Divide the wontons and bok choy among the bowls and ladle the hot broth over them. Top with scallions and a spoonful of chili oil, if using.
