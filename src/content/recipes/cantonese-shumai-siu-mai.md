## Chef's Note

Shumai are open-topped Cantonese pork-and-shrimp dumplings. Mix the pork with the starch and measured filling water until sticky, then work in the oils before folding in the shrimp and vegetables. Dried shiitakes are hydrated and chopped before measuring; their soaking water is separate from the measured filling water. Thin round wrappers form cups around the filling rather than enclosing it like a wonton.

## Directions

1. **Prepare mushrooms and chosen scallops:** Have Water for soaking mushrooms and Water for soaking optional scallops (if using) ready. Soak dried shiitakes until fully softened, then drain, remove tough stems and chop before measuring Shiitake mushrooms. If selecting optional scallops, choose either the small count OR the large count, never both. Soak that chosen amount in water in the refrigerator at 40°F /4°C or below for at least 4 hours or overnight until soft enough to shred, allowing longer as needed. Drain and shred, and keep prepared scallops refrigerated. Soaking water is discarded, not part of the filling’s measured water.
2. **Hydrate the pork:** Stir Ground pork (80/20), Tapioca starch or cornstarch, and Water for filling in a bowl for several minutes until the pork becomes sticky and absorbs the full measured filling water. Keep the mixture cold; this is the rich route, not the separately specified wine route.
3. **Add oils and shrimp:** From Peanut or canola oil, Toasted sesame oil, and Raw shrimp, stir in both listed oils first, then fold in the finely chopped shrimp. Keep the mixture cold.
4. **Complete the rich filling:** Fold in Water chestnuts, Shiitake mushrooms, Fresh ginger, Scallions, Oyster sauce, Kosher salt, Sugar, White pepper, Small dried scallops (choose one scallop size only) (if using), Large dried scallops (choose one scallop size only) (if using), and Finely diced pork back fat or fat from pork belly (if using), adding optional shredded scallops of the chosen size and optional diced pork fat only if selected. Never combine both scallop-size rows. All mushroom and chestnut quantities refer to their prepared state. Refrigerate waiting filling.
5. **Form open cups:** Use Thin round siu-mai wrappers to hold approximately 2 tbsp filling per original-size dumpling, reducing the scoop only enough for the chosen wrapper to support it. Press the wrapper into an open cup with a flat base. Use all the prepared filling; the listed wrapper range and piece yield are approximate planning counts. Keep wrapper size and scoop similar when scaling, using additional trays/racks.
6. **Distribute carrots:** Divide all Carrots across the dumpling tops and press gently into the filling. Arrange in lined steamer racks with space between dumplings; keep uncooked waiting batches refrigerated.
7. **Steam and check each rack:** Bring Water below the steamer basket to a boil below the basket without touching the dumplings. Cover and maintain steam, ensuring the pot does not boil dry. Begin checking fresh dumplings after about 8–10 minutes. Use a thin-tip thermometer in the center of several larger fillings on every rack; continue until each checked filling reaches 165°F /74°C and the wrappers are cooked. Check upper racks independently. Lift the lid away from you and protect hands from steam; repeat in batches as needed.
8. **Serve or freeze:** Serve hot with Chili oil (if using) separately if desired. For make-ahead dumplings, freeze uncooked shumai promptly in a single layer and pack once firm. Steam from frozen without thawing, beginning checks after about 9–10 minutes and allowing longer as needed with the same multiple-center, every-rack 165°F checks.

## Cooking Notes

A 10-inch basket held about 15 dumplings in the source’s setup; use the actual spacing and rack capacity rather than forcing that count. Two racks can cook unevenly. Optional dried scallops add a concentrated seafood flavor; the source also permits a few tablespoons of finely diced pork back fat or fat from pork belly for a richer filling, folded in with the vegetables. That addition is a separate optional amount, not a replacement for the measured oils.
