## Chef's Note

Cynthia Christensen's skillet biscuits from But First We Brunch are tall, flaky and buttery, baked close together so they rise up instead of out. Keep the butter cold and leave pea-size pieces in the flour, and two quick folds give you layers you can pull apart.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 450°F with a rack in the lower third. Grease a 10-inch cast-iron skillet with butter.
2. **Cut in the butter:** Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt in a large bowl. Work in the cold butter with a pastry cutter or your fingertips until the mix looks like coarse crumbs with pea-size pieces of butter.
3. **Add the buttermilk:** Stir in the buttermilk just until the dough comes together.
4. **Fold the dough:** Turn the dough onto a floured counter and pat it into a rectangle about 1 inch thick. Fold it over on itself twice, then pat it back to 1 inch thick.
5. **Cut the biscuits:** Press a floured 2-inch cutter straight down without twisting. Gently gather the scraps and cut the rest. Set the biscuits in the skillet so they touch. If the dough feels soft, chill the skillet for 10 minutes.
6. **Bake:** Brush the tops with buttermilk. Bake until tall and golden, 15 to 18 minutes, moving the skillet to the upper rack for the last 3 minutes if the tops need more color.
7. **Finish:** Brush the hot biscuits with melted butter and let them rest in the skillet for 10 minutes before serving.
