## Chef's Note

This is old-fashioned peach ice cream on a rich egg-yolk custard, the kind to make in July and August when peaches are at their best. Letting the peaches sit in sugar draws out a syrup that flavors the whole base. Mash the fruit fine and fold it in at the end so every bite has soft peach, not icy chunks.

## Directions

1. **Macerate the peaches:** Toss the peaches with their sugar and the lemon juice. Let stand until very juicy, 30 minutes to 1 hour. Mash until only small pieces remain, then strain, keeping the syrup and the fruit separately. Refrigerate both.
2. **Heat the dairy:** Meanwhile, heat the cream, milk, salt and two-thirds of the custard sugar in a saucepan over medium heat until steaming, about 5 minutes. Do not let it boil.
3. **Temper the yolks:** In a bowl, whisk the yolks with the rest of the custard sugar. Whisk in a ladleful of the hot cream, then another, until the yolks are warm. Pour everything back into the saucepan.
4. **Cook the custard:** Stir constantly over medium-low heat until the custard coats the back of a spoon and reaches 170 to 175°F, 5 to 8 minutes. Do not let it boil. Off the heat, stir in the vanilla.
5. **Chill the base:** Strain the custard into a bowl and whisk in the peach syrup. Cover and refrigerate until very cold, at least 4 hours. If your ice cream maker has a freezer bowl, put it in the freezer now.
6. **Churn:** Churn the base in an ice cream maker until it looks like soft serve. Add the mashed peaches during the last 30 seconds.
7. **Freeze:** Pack the ice cream into a container and freeze until firm, 4 to 6 hours.
