## Chef's Note

This Mexican-inspired salmon dinner pairs sweet lime-honey glaze with cumin-scented beans and corn, fresh spinach and toasted pepitas. Bake the fish most of the way before glazing, then give the broiler your full attention. The warm salad softens the leaves slightly at serving while the seeds keep their crunch.

## Directions

1. **Prepare everything and heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C with a middle rack and a second rack positioned so the fish surface will sit 7–9 inches below the broiler. Cut corn from the cobs, chop onion, pepper, garlic and cilantro, and rinse and drain beans. Prepare the spinach and measure the pepitas. Line the metal half-sheet pan with foil and spread its teaspoon of oil where the fish will sit. Pat salmon dry, check for pin bones and divide into seven similar portions. Season with chili powder, smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp salt and all the black pepper. Keep chilled while charring corn. Whisk honey with the 2 tbsp glaze lime juice in a clean bowl; set aside the separate salad juice and serving wedges. Wash raw-fish tools and hands before returning to vegetables.
2. **Char corn in two batches:** Heat the deep, well-seasoned skillet over medium-high heat for about 2–3 minutes. Add half the dry corn without oil and spread into a shallow layer. Let it sit briefly, then stir occasionally for about 5–7 minutes, until juicy and tender with scattered browned patches. Lower heat if it starts blackening. Transfer to a large bowl. Let the pan recover its heat and repeat with the remaining corn; remove any burnt loose residue afterward.
3. **Start baking the fish:** Arrange salmon on the prepared pan with about an inch between portions. Bake on the middle rack while making the salad in the next two steps. Start checking the fish after 8 minutes; allow roughly 10–15 minutes for the specified thickness, aiming for about 135–140°F / 57–60°C before the glaze finish. This is an intermediate temperature, not the serving endpoint. If ready before the salad, remove the pan briefly to a heatproof surface.
4. **Soften the aromatics:** Reduce skillet heat to medium and add the tablespoon of olive oil, onion, bell pepper and remaining 1/2 tsp salt. Cook, stirring, about 6–8 minutes until the onion softens and pepper is tender with a little bite. Add garlic, the remaining 1 tsp cumin and optional red pepper flakes; stir 30–45 seconds until fragrant.
5. **Warm and finish the salad:** Fold the beans and all the corn into the skillet. Cook over medium heat for about 4–6 minutes, turning gently, until hot throughout. Remove from heat and fold in cilantro and the first tablespoon of salad lime juice. Taste and add some or all of the reserved lime and extra salt if wanted. Cover loosely and turn off the skillet before broiling.
6. **Glaze and watch the broiler:** Remove salmon from the oven if it is still inside and switch to high broil, allowing the element to heat according to the oven's instructions. Spread all the honey-lime mixture over the seven fillets in an even coating, without deliberately pooling it on the foil. Broil with the fish surface 7–9 inches below the heat for about 1–3 minutes, watching continuously; rotate the pan if needed. The glaze should bubble and develop golden patches without turning black. Check each fillet sideways in its thickest center and finish to 145°F / 63°C, with flesh that flakes under gentle pressure. If the glaze colors first, use the gentler oven finish in troubleshooting. Use clean utensils for serving and discard glaze left in the brushing bowl; it is not a cold sauce.
7. **Serve seven complete portions:** Divide all the spinach among seven wide bowls, then divide the warm bean-and-corn salad over it. Add one fillet per bowl. Dice the optional avocado and divide it among the bowls, then scatter the pepitas. Offer lime wedges for squeezing to taste. The spinach remains a fresh bed, lightly softened by the warm toppings.

## With rice

For a more substantial dinner, one full batch of [Aromatic Purple Rice](/recipes/purple-rice) supplies seven small side portions, roughly 3/4 cup each. Begin its 80-minute preparation before this recipe and allow time for the rice's transition and grain checks; leave the final fish broiling until the rice is resting or ready. The beans and corn are already included in every salmon portion.

## Smaller oven

Use the same bake-then-glaze method only with the appliance's approved broiler pan, foil placement and rack clearance. If seven portions do not fit with space between them, cook in separate batches and allow roughly 20–25 extra minutes. Divide the foil oil and glaze between pans and keep the waiting raw fish chilled. The first batch will rest longer and lose heat; a full-size oven is the simpler way to serve all seven portions together.
