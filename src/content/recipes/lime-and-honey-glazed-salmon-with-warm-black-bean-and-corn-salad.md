## Chef's Note

A Rachael Ray dinner by way of Lori Lange at RecipeGirl: chili-rubbed salmon under a honey-lime glaze, served on spinach with warm black beans and charred corn. Bake the fish most of the way, then glaze and broil it so the honey bubbles without burning. Pepitas and avocado add crunch and richness.

## Directions

1. **Season the salmon:** Heat the oven to 400°F with one rack in the middle and one about 8 inches below the broiler. Line a rimmed sheet pan with foil and rub it with the oil. Pat the salmon dry and season it with the chili powder, paprika, a third of the cumin, half the salt and the pepper. Lay the portions on the pan with space between them.
2. **Char the corn:** Heat a large, heavy skillet over medium-high heat with no oil. Add half the corn in a shallow layer and cook, stirring now and then, until juicy with brown patches, 5 to 7 minutes. Move it to a bowl and repeat with the rest.
3. **Bake the salmon:** Bake the salmon on the middle rack until it is opaque outside but still a little translucent in the center, 10 to 12 minutes. Take it out and leave it on the pan.
4. **Soften the vegetables:** While the salmon bakes, lower the skillet to medium and add the olive oil, onion, bell pepper and the rest of the salt. Cook, stirring, until the onion is soft and the pepper still has a little bite, 6 to 8 minutes. Add the garlic, the rest of the cumin and the pepper flakes and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
5. **Warm the salad:** Fold in the beans and corn and cook, turning gently, until hot through, 4 to 6 minutes. Off the heat, stir in the cilantro and the salad lime juice. Taste for salt and lime, then cover to keep warm.
6. **Glaze and broil:** Switch the oven to high broil. Whisk the honey and the glaze lime juice and brush it over the salmon. Broil on the upper rack, watching closely, until the glaze bubbles with golden spots and the center flakes, 1 to 3 minutes.
7. **Assemble the bowls:** Divide the spinach among wide bowls and spoon the warm salad over it. Top each with a piece of salmon, the avocado and the pepitas, and serve with lime wedges.
