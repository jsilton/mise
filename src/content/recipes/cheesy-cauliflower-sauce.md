## Chef's Note

Greek yogurt, cheddar and Parmesan give this cauliflower sauce a tangy, cheesy flavor. Blend the cooked cauliflower completely smooth and toss with pasta just before serving.

## Directions

1. **Step 1:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add cauliflower and cook about 6–8 minutes, until soft when pierced with a fork.

2. **Step 2:** Lift the cauliflower into a blender with a slotted spoon. Add yogurt, warm milk, cheddar, butter, Parmesan, salt and optional garlic powder. Vent the lid safely for steam and blend until smooth.

3. **Step 3:** If serving with pasta, cook it in the cauliflower water according to the package. Drain and return to the pot.

4. **Step 4:** Toss sauce into hot pasta just before serving. Taste and adjust seasoning. Add a little warm milk if needed to loosen the sauce.
