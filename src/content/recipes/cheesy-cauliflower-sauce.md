## Chef's Note

Soft cauliflower blended with Greek yogurt, sharp cheddar, butter and Parmesan makes a creamy, tangy sauce that tastes like mac and cheese with a vegetable hiding inside. Cooking the pasta in the cauliflower water seasons both and saves a pot.

## Directions

1. **Boil the cauliflower:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil and add the kosher salt. Add the cauliflower and cook until a fork crushes it easily, 6 to 8 minutes. Lift it out with a slotted spoon and keep the water boiling.
2. **Cook the pasta:** Add the pasta to the same water and cook until al dente, then drain it and return it to the pot.
3. **Blend the sauce:** While the pasta cooks, put the cauliflower, yogurt, warm milk, cheddar, butter, Parmesan, fine salt and garlic powder in a blender. Remove the center cap of the lid, cover the hole with a towel and blend until completely smooth.
4. **Toss and serve:** Pour the sauce over the hot pasta and toss off the heat, adding a splash of milk if it is thick. Do not let it boil once the yogurt is in. Serve with more Parmesan and black pepper.
