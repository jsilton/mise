## Chef's Note

This EatingWell salad plays peppery arugula against sweet strawberries and salty feta, with sunflower seeds for crunch. Blending a few berries into the vinaigrette carries fruit flavor onto every leaf. Make it when strawberries are at their peak.

## Directions

1. **Blend the dressing:** Blend the dressing strawberries with the olive oil, vinegar, honey and garlic in a small blender or with an immersion blender until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
2. **Dress the greens:** In a large bowl, toss the arugula with half the dressing until the leaves are lightly glossy.
3. **Finish and serve:** Top with the sliced strawberries, feta and sunflower seeds. Drizzle with more dressing to taste and serve right away.
