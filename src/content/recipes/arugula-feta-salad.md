## Chef's Note

Sweet berries, peppery leaves and salty feta work best when the strawberry dressing stays light. Blend the dressing separately, toss in only enough to coat the leaves and add the seeds at the end for crunch. Adapted from [EatingWell’s Arugula & Feta Salad with Strawberry Vinaigrette](https://www.eatingwell.com/recipe/252174/arugula-feta-salad-with-strawberry-vinaigrette/).

## Directions

1. **Prepare produce:** Wash and dry arugula if needed. Wash berries, dry them and remove their hulls. Slice the listed salad berries and keep the additional dressing berries separate. Crumble feta and measure toasted seeds.
2. **Blend dressing:** Blend the dressing berries, olive oil, vinegar, honey and garlic until smooth. Taste with an arugula leaf and a small piece of feta, then season with salt and pepper as needed. If the blender cannot catch the small amount, use a narrower vessel or small food processor rather than adding water just to raise the level.
3. **Dress lightly:** Put arugula in a wide bowl and toss gently with about half the dressing. Add sliced berries and most of the feta, lifting carefully to avoid crushing fruit. Add more dressing only if the leaves need it; reserve any excess instead of automatically pouring it all over the salad.
4. **Finish and serve:** Scatter over the remaining feta and sunflower seeds. Divide into the intended side portions and serve immediately while leaves and seeds retain their texture.
