## Chef's Note

A simple side that goes with almost anything, from a weeknight chicken to the holiday turkey. Slowly softened shallots bring a gentle sweetness, and the lemon goes in off the heat at the very end so it tastes bright and the beans stay green.

## Directions

1. **Boil the water:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil over high heat.
2. **Soften the shallots:** While the water heats, melt the butter in a 12-inch skillet over medium-low heat. Add the shallots and cook, stirring, until soft and translucent, 5 to 7 minutes.
3. **Cook the beans:** Add the beans to the boiling water and cook until they bend and bite through with a little resistance, 4 to 6 minutes. Drain well.
4. **Toss with the shallots:** Add the beans and honey to the shallots. Turn the heat to medium and toss until the beans are hot and coated, 2 to 3 minutes.
5. **Finish with lemon:** Take the pan off the heat. Add the lemon zest, lemon juice and pepper and toss. Taste and add salt or a little more lemon juice.
