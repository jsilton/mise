## Chef's Note

Brown the mushrooms before adding the onion, and add the garlic near the end so it stays fragrant. Dry vegetables and a prebaked pastry base keep this Gruyère quiche from turning watery. Rest it briefly after the center sets so each slice holds together.

## Directions

1. **Prebake the pastry:** Fit portion of unbaked single-crust pastry dough to appropriately sized pie plates, then follow the dough recipe or package’s custard-pie prebaking instructions, using weights if directed. The pastry sides must be set and the base dry before the egg filling is added; remove any weights and lining, and cool it while cooking the vegetables. The original six-portion batch uses one 9-inch plate with enough depth to keep the complete mixture below the pastry rim. When scaling, divide unrolled dough among suitable vessels; do not halve a formed shell or multiply its diameter. Set it on a rimmed baking sheet. Once the pastry is ready, heat the oven to 375°F.
2. **Prepare a dish for excess filling:** Coat a small extra oven-safe dish with cooking spray and set it aside. Use it if the full filling will not fit below the pastry rim.
3. **Brown the mushrooms:** Heat extra-virgin olive oil and fresh mixed mushrooms in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the mushrooms are browned and tender and released moisture has cooked away, starting from about 8 minutes. Cook in batches if necessary so they brown rather than stew.
4. **Soften the onion:** Add sweet onion and cook, stirring, until soft and lightly golden, about 5 minutes. Reduce the heat if the mushrooms or onion threaten to scorch.
5. **Add the garlic late:** Stir in garlic and cook for 30–45 seconds, just until fragrant. Do not brown or burn it.
6. **Wilt and dry the spinach:** Add fresh baby spinach and toss until fully wilted, about 1–2 minutes. Cook off any free liquid, then spread the vegetables out and cool them until only warm.
7. **Mix the complete custard:** Whisk large eggs, whole milk, half-and-half, Dijon mustard, fresh thyme leaves, fine salt, and ground black pepper just until smooth and evenly blended, without beating in a thick foam.
8. **Add cheese and fill:** Fold Gruyère cheese and all the cooled vegetables into the custard. Pour the complete mixture into the prebaked pastry, leaving space below its rim; bake any excess in the extra dish rather than overfilling or discarding it.
9. **Bake to a set center:** Bake at 375°F, beginning the first check around 30 minutes. The top should be lightly golden and the center set, with at most a small unified tremble and no liquid wave. The central egg filling must reach at least 160°F; keep the thermometer’s sensing area away from the pan. Continue until both the temperature and set texture agree. Shield darkening pastry edges or the top with foil if needed. Check an extra dish independently; depth and starting temperature change the time.
10. **Rest and slice:** Rest for 10 minutes before slicing. Add fresh thyme leaves (if using) if desired. Serve warm. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or 1 hour above 90°F, while still warm if needed.

## Crustless Version

For the original batch, this makes six planned portions. These are complete amounts for this version; keep them together when changing the batch, and use enough appropriately sized dishes. Omit the pastry.

- cooking spray, enough to coat the pie plate and any extra dish
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 8 oz fresh mixed mushrooms, sliced
- 1 1/2 cups sweet onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp garlic, thinly sliced
- 5 oz fresh baby spinach, coarsely chopped
- 6 large eggs
- 1/4 cup whole milk
- 1/4 cup half-and-half
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
- 1/4 tsp fine salt
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 1/2 cups Gruyère cheese, freshly grated
- fresh thyme leaves, a little extra for garnish, optional

1. **Prepare the crustless dish:** Heat the oven to 375°F. Coat a 9-inch pie plate with cooking spray. Use a plate deep enough to hold the full mixture below the rim; prepare a small extra coated dish if needed.
2. **Brown the mushrooms:** Heat extra-virgin olive oil and fresh mixed mushrooms in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the mushrooms are browned and tender and released moisture has cooked away, starting from about 8 minutes. Cook in batches if necessary so they brown rather than stew.
3. **Soften the onion:** Add sweet onion and cook, stirring, until soft and lightly golden, about 5 minutes. Reduce the heat if the mushrooms or onion threaten to scorch.
4. **Add the garlic late:** Stir in garlic and cook for 30–45 seconds, just until fragrant. Do not brown or burn it.
5. **Wilt and dry the spinach:** Add fresh baby spinach and toss until fully wilted, about 1–2 minutes. Cook off any free liquid, then spread the vegetables out and cool them until only warm.
6. **Mix the complete custard:** Whisk large eggs, whole milk, half-and-half, Dijon mustard, fresh thyme leaves, fine salt, and ground black pepper just until smooth and evenly blended, without beating in a thick foam.
7. **Add cheese and fill:** Fold Gruyère cheese and all the cooled vegetables into the custard. Spread the whole filling in the prepared plate below the rim; put any excess in the extra coated dish.
8. **Bake to a set center:** Bake at 375°F, beginning the first check around 30 minutes. The top should be lightly golden and the center set, with at most a small unified tremble and no liquid wave. The central egg filling must reach at least 160°F; keep the thermometer’s sensing area away from the pan. Continue until both the temperature and set texture agree. Shield a darkening top with foil if needed. Check an extra dish independently; depth and starting temperature change the time.
9. **Rest and slice:** Rest for 10 minutes before slicing. Add fresh thyme leaves (if using) if desired. Serve warm. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or 1 hour above 90°F, while still warm if needed.

Allow about 25–30 minutes preparation, a 30–45-minute baking plan at 375°F, and 10 minutes rest, continuing until the center is set and at least 160°F. The storage instructions apply to this version too.
