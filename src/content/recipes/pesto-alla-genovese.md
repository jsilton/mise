## Chef's Note

Pesto alla genovese is Liguria’s raw basil sauce, worked with garlic, pine nuts, two aged cheeses and mild olive oil. Gentle crushing and oil added at the end produce a cohesive sauce. Loosen the serving portion with pasta water and dress the pasta off the heat.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the leaves and cheeses:** Wash fresh Genovese basil leaves under running water and dry them carefully on a clean towel. Finely grate the cheeses and peel the garlic. Use a clean marble mortar and wooden pestle with room to work; divide a larger batch into mortar loads rather than filling it to the rim.
2. **Crush the garlic and pine nuts:** Put raw pine nuts and garlic cloves in the mortar. Crush the garlic to a paste, then work in the pine nuts until they form a thick paste.
3. **Work in the basil:** Add fresh Genovese basil leaves and coarse sea salt, feeding in the leaves a little at a time with the salt. Press and rotate the pestle gently against the mortar walls until the leaves form an even bright green paste. Scrape the sides as needed and work steadily without letting the mixture become warm.
4. **Add both cheeses:** Work in Parmigiano-Reggiano and Fiore Sardo until evenly distributed. Keep the full listed amounts of both cheeses.
5. **Finish with olive oil:** Drizzle in mild extra-virgin olive oil, stirring until the pesto is cohesive and creamy. Scrape into a small bowl for immediate use or a clean covered container for refrigeration.
6. **Dress the pasta off the heat:** When serving with pasta, add reserved pasta cooking water (if using) a little at a time to loosen the pesto, then toss with drained pasta in a bowl off the heat. Thin only the amount being served. Keep the remaining pesto cold.

## Cooking Notes

Use a mortar with room to work and divide the listed quantities into separate loads as needed. Choose the basil, cheese and oil amounts within their listed ranges; keep the full listed salt and pine nuts.

Refrigerate promptly at 40°F / 4°C or below and use within 3 days, or freeze for longer storage. Keep only the serving portion at the table.
