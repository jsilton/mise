## Chef's Note

A real cream of mushroom soup is nothing like the can. Browning the mushrooms hard in butter builds the deep flavor, shiitakes add an earthy depth and a spoonful of dry sherry at the end brightens the cream. A few browned mushrooms saved for the top give each bowl some bite.

## Directions

1. **Brown the mushrooms:** Melt the butter in a large heavy pot over medium-high heat. Add the cremini and shiitake mushrooms and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring now and then, until their liquid has cooked off and they are deep golden, 12 to 15 minutes.
2. **Save the garnish:** Spoon a few of the best browned mushrooms into a small bowl and set them aside for the tops.
3. **Cook the aromatics:** Lower the heat to medium. Add the shallot and cook until soft, about 2 minutes. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in the flour and thyme and cook, stirring, until the flour smells toasty, about 1 minute.
4. **Simmer:** Pour in the stock a little at a time, stirring and scraping the bottom so the flour doesn't lump. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer until the mushrooms are very soft, about 10 minutes.
5. **Blend:** Take the pot off the heat. Purée the soup until smooth with an immersion blender, or in small batches in a countertop blender with the lid vented, then return it to the pot.
6. **Finish:** Stir in the cream and sherry and simmer gently for 1 minute. Season with salt and pepper until the soup tastes rich and full.
7. **Serve:** Ladle into bowls and top with the reserved mushrooms.
