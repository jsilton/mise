## Chef's Note

Cook and cool the spinach before squeezing it dry: residual water can soak the tortillas. The full cream cheese and tomato-jar oil make the spread; divide all the cheeses and vegetables across the tortillas so the last rolls have the same filling as the first. Refrigerating the rolled tortillas before slicing makes them easier to cut.

## Directions

1. **Prepare spinach:** Cook all the spinach according to the actual package instructions. Spread it shallowly and refrigerate promptly to cool before cold assembly, then squeeze small handfuls to remove water and chop. Wash the basil under running water and dry it.
2. **Mix spread:** In a food processor, blend all the softened cream cheese, all the listed minced garlic, a portion of the chopped tomatoes and all the measured tomato-jar oil until combined, scraping the bowl as needed. Use the separate optional salt and pepper allowance if wanted. Reserve the remaining tomatoes for the rolls.
3. **Divide and layer:** Set out the listed number of tortillas. Divide the full spread, spinach, remaining tomatoes, Parmesan, feta and basil among them. Spread the cream cheese mixture on each, leaving about a half-inch border; layer the spinach, tomatoes and cheeses, with basil in a line along the center.
4. **Roll:** Start at an edge parallel to the basil line and roll firmly without tearing the tortilla. Wrap each roll and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before slicing.
5. **Cut and serve:** Use a serrated knife to cut about one-inch pieces, holding each roll gently. The original six-tortilla batch plans about 32 pieces; actual tortilla size and trimmed ends determine the count. Keep refrigerated until serving.
