## Chef's Note

These are party pinwheels with a Mediterranean filling of sun-dried tomato, spinach, feta and basil. Blending the cream cheese with the oil from the tomato jar makes a tacky, full-flavored spread that glues every layer to the tortilla. Squeeze the spinach bone dry so the rolls stay tight.

## Directions

1. **Cook the spinach:** Cook the spinach in the microwave or a small pan until hot, about 5 minutes. Spread it on a plate to cool, then squeeze it bone dry in small handfuls.
2. **Blend the spread:** In a food processor, blend the cream cheese, garlic, a small handful of the sun-dried tomatoes and the tomato oil until smooth, about 1 minute. Season with salt and pepper.
3. **Spread the tortillas:** Lay out the tortillas and spread the cream cheese mixture evenly over each one, leaving a 1/2-inch border along the top edge.
4. **Layer the filling:** Scatter the spinach, the rest of the sun-dried tomatoes, the Parmesan and the feta over the spread. Lay a single line of basil leaves across the center of each tortilla.
5. **Roll and chill:** Starting at the edge parallel to the basil, roll each tortilla up very tightly. Wrap each roll in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, until firm.
6. **Slice and serve:** Trim the ends and cut each roll into 1-inch slices with a sharp serrated knife. Serve cold.
