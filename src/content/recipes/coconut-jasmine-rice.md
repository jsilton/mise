## Chef's Note

Coconut rice is cooked all over Southeast Asia, from Thailand to Indonesia and the Philippines. Coconut milk and water together give the jasmine rice a rich, fragrant flavor without making it heavy. A knot of pandan leaf adds a sweet, grassy aroma if you can find it.

## Directions

1. **Rinse the rice:** Rinse the rice in a fine-mesh strainer under cold water, swishing it with your hand, until the water runs almost clear. Drain well.
2. **Warm the liquid:** In a heavy 3-quart saucepan, stir together the coconut milk, water, salt, sugar and pandan, if using. Warm over medium-low heat, stirring, until smooth and the salt dissolves.
3. **Cook the rice:** Stir in the rice and bring to a gentle boil. Cover tightly, turn the heat to low and cook without lifting the lid until the liquid is absorbed and the grains are tender, 18 to 22 minutes.
4. **Rest and fluff:** Take the pan off the heat and leave it covered for 10 minutes. Remove the pandan and fluff the rice gently with a fork.
