## Chef's Note

Coconut milk brings fat, flavor, and some of the water the rice needs. The milk-to-water ratio controls richness, but the total liquid and the pot's evaporation also matter. This is a savory jasmine-rice side with optional mild sweetness, distinct from coconut sticky-rice desserts and Jamaican rice and peas. Plain rice is also a useful choice alongside a rich coconut curry.

## Directions

1. **Rinse and drain:** Rinse jasmine rice in several changes of cool water until less cloudy. Drain thoroughly in a fine-mesh strainer; do not leave a bowlful of rinse water clinging to the batch.
2. **Combine liquid and seasoning:** Stir coconut milk, measured water, salt, optional sugar, and optional pandan in a heavy 3-quart saucepan. Warm over medium-low heat, stirring until smooth and the salt dissolves.
3. **Add rice:** Stir in drained rice, bring to a gentle boil, cover tightly, and reduce to low. Use only enough heat to maintain a gentle simmer.
4. **Cook and check:** Cook about 18–22 minutes without repeatedly lifting the lid. At the end, gently part the rice at the edge to check for pooled liquid. Taste a grain: it should be tender through the center. If hard and dry, use the small hot-water addition in troubleshooting; if liquid remains and rice is firm, cover and cook a few minutes longer.
5. **Rest:** Once rice is tender and free liquid has absorbed, take the pan off the burner. Leave covered for 10 minutes. Remove pandan, fluff gently, and divide into six side portions.

## Using a rice cooker

Use its coconut-rice instructions if provided, including fill limits and the appropriate program. Do not assume a plain-white-rice cycle, fill line, or timing will behave identically with coconut milk. The stovetop method above is the default for this formula.
