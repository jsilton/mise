## Chef's Note

Kimchi jjigae is the stew Korean home cooks make when the kimchi in the fridge has turned sour and funky. Pork belly and that well-aged kimchi simmer in stock with gochugaru and gochujang, and soft slices of tofu go in at the end. The older the kimchi, the deeper and more savory the broth.

## Directions

1. **Brown the pork:** Heat the vegetable oil in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the pork and let it sit for 1 to 2 minutes to color, then stir and cook until mostly opaque, 1 to 2 minutes more.
2. **Soften the aromatics:** Add the garlic and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the onion and mushrooms and cook, stirring, until they start to soften, about 2 minutes.
3. **Fry the kimchi:** Stir in the gochugaru, gochujang and the doenjang loosened with a spoonful of the stock, if using. Cook for 30 seconds, stirring so nothing scorches. Add the kimchi and cook until hot and fragrant, about 2 minutes.
4. **Simmer:** Add the stock and kimchi juice and bring to a simmer. Lower the heat and simmer uncovered until the pork is tender and the broth tastes deep and savory, 12 to 15 minutes.
5. **Add the tofu:** Slide in the tofu and spoon broth over it. Simmer gently for 5 minutes without stirring, so the tofu stays in whole slices.
6. **Season and serve:** Stir in the fish sauce and sugar and taste for heat. Take the pot off the heat and drizzle in the sesame oil. Top with the scallions and sesame seeds and serve hot.
