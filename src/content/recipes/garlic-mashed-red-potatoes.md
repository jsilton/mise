## Chef's Note

A rustic Taste of Home mash with the red skins left on for texture and an earthy flavor. Whole garlic cloves simmer with the potatoes, so they turn soft and sweet and mash right in. Butter and Parmesan make it rich enough for a holiday table.

## Directions

1. **Simmer the potatoes:** Put the potatoes and garlic in a large pot, cover with cold water and salt it well. Bring to a boil over high heat, then simmer until the thickest potato and the garlic crush easily, 15 to 20 minutes. Warm the milk while they cook.
2. **Dry the potatoes:** Drain the potatoes and garlic well. Return them to the warm pot over low heat for 1 minute to steam off the surface water.
3. **Mash:** Add the butter and a pinch of salt and pepper. Mash by hand, with the garlic, until the butter melts.
4. **Finish:** Add the Parmesan and most of the warm milk and mash just to combine. Add the rest of the milk if you want it looser, keeping bits of red skin throughout. Taste for salt and serve.
