## Chef's Note

Keep the red skins on and cook the potato quarters with whole garlic cloves until both crush easily. Drain well, then mash by hand with butter, warmed milk and Parmesan. A brief return to the warm pot removes surface water without needing a blender or perfectly smooth texture.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Scrub the potatoes and leave the skins on. Quarter them into similar-size pieces. Put potatoes and peeled whole garlic in a large pot, cover with cold water and add salt from the listed allowance.

2. **Simmer:** Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer about 15–20 minutes after boiling, until potato quarters and garlic crush easily. Larger quarters may need longer; check the thickest pieces.

3. **Drain:** Drain potatoes and garlic thoroughly. Return to the warm pot briefly, about 1 minute, letting surface steam escape without scorching the bottom.

4. **Mash:** Add all the butter and mash potatoes and garlic together by hand. Season with the listed salt and pepper to taste. Keep skin pieces and some rustic texture rather than beating vigorously.

5. **Finish the mash:** Gradually add all the warmed milk and Parmesan, mashing just enough to combine. Avoid a blender or food processor for the potatoes; stop at the desired rustic texture.

6. **Serve:** Serve promptly. Refrigerate leftovers in shallow containers and reheat gently to the measured leftover endpoint, stirring for even heating.
