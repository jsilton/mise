## Chef's Note

Squeeze the thawed spinach firmly before mixing it with the full ricotta, Parmesan, egg and whole-wheat flour. Add only the dusting flour needed to handle the dough. Floating is a useful first cue; cut a representative piece open and check that its center is cooked through.

## Directions

1. **Prepare spinach:** Thaw the spinach in the refrigerator. If its package requires cooking before use, follow that instruction, cool enough to handle and squeeze it firmly in a clean towel. Do not assume every frozen product is ready to eat.
2. **Mix:** Pulse all the dry spinach, ricotta, Parmesan, measured flour, egg, nutmeg and salt to taste in a food processor until combined into sticky dough. Use processor batches if needed, then combine all the dough. Do not taste the raw mixture.
3. **Shape:** Transfer to a floured board and dust just enough to prevent sticking. For the original batch, divide into about four logs; at other scales make more or fewer logs of the same roughly 1-inch diameter.
4. **Cut:** Cut into roughly 1-inch pieces for the current route and toss lightly with dusting flour.
5. **Boil:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook in uncrowded batches. Start checking around 3–5 minutes: the pieces should float, and a cut-open representative center should be cooked through without a raw floury core. Check representative egg-containing centers for 160°F; allow longer when needed.
6. **Finish:** Drain gently and season with the listed black pepper. Serve with your chosen tomato sauce or separately prepared brown butter and sage.
