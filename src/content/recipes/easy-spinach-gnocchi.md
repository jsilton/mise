## Chef's Note

Ricotta and spinach gnocchi need no potatoes and come together in a food processor. Squeeze the spinach bone-dry so the dough needs little flour, and the dumplings come out light and pillowy instead of dense. A pinch of nutmeg ties the spinach to the cheese.

## Directions

1. **Make the dough:** Pulse the spinach, ricotta, Parmesan, flour, egg, salt and nutmeg in a food processor until a soft, sticky dough forms. Don't overprocess.
2. **Shape the gnocchi:** Turn the dough onto a floured board and dust it with just enough flour that it stops sticking to your hands. Roll it into 1-inch-thick logs, cut them into 1-inch pieces and toss the pieces lightly in flour.
3. **Heat the water and sauce:** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Warm the marinara in a small saucepan over low heat.
4. **Boil in batches:** Drop in as many gnocchi as fit without crowding. Cook until they float and a cut piece has no floury center, 3 to 5 minutes. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
5. **Serve:** Spoon the warm sauce over the gnocchi and finish with black pepper and more Parmesan.
