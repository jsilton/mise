## Chef's Note

These soft, pull-apart rolls have a buttery cinnamon spiral and tangy cream-cheese frosting. Cooking a small portion of the flour with water helps the dough retain moisture; cool the paste until just warm before mixing it into the dough. Keep the filling butter soft but cool enough to hold its shape, so it spreads rather than runs out of the spirals.

Plan about 4 hours through warm serving, with longer if the dough rises slowly. Make the filling during the 30-minute dough rest and the frosting during baking; judge the final rise by the rolls doubling in size.

## Directions

1. **The Tangzhong:** In a small saucepan, whisk the tangzhong flour and water. Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until a thick paste forms, about 4–5 minutes. Scrape into a bowl and cool until just warm.

2. **The Mix:** In a stand mixer, combine the dough flour, granulated sugar, yeast and salt. Add the cream, milk, egg, dough vanilla and all the cooled tangzhong. Knead with a dough hook on low speed for about 2 minutes.

3. **The Butter:** On low speed, add the dough butter one tablespoon at a time, letting each addition incorporate. Knead for another 10–12 minutes, until smooth and elastic and a stretched piece forms a thin windowpane.

4. **Rest the Dough:** Shape into a ball, place seam-side down in a butter-greased bowl, cover and rest for 30 minutes.

5. **Make the Filling:** During the rest, mix all the filling butter, brown sugar and cinnamon with the optional vanilla powder into a spreadable paste.

6. **Shape:** On a lightly floured surface, roll to a 15 x 21-inch rectangle with a 21-inch edge facing you. Spread all the filling over the dough, leaving a 1-inch clean border along the far 21-inch edge.

7. **The Cut:** Cut across the 21-inch width into twelve strips, each 1.75 inches wide and 15 inches long. Roll each strip away from you toward its clean edge and place in a parchment-lined metal 9 x 13-inch pan.

8. **Final Rise:** Cover and let rise until doubled, about 1–2 hours. Preheat the oven to 325°F during this rise and wait for it to reach temperature before baking.

9. **Bake:** Bake for about 30–35 minutes, until the tops are lightly golden and the center roll is set rather than wet dough. Check the center as well as the edges; continue baking if the middle is still doughy.

10. **The Frosting:** While the rolls bake, beat all the frosting cream cheese and butter until smooth. Mix in the powdered sugar, frosting vanilla and cream, beating until fluffy.

11. **Finish:** Cool the pan on a rack for 15 minutes. Spread all the frosting over the warm rolls, then let stand another 15 minutes before serving warm.

Refrigerate frosted leftovers in a covered container at 40°F / 4°C or below within 2 hours, or within 1 hour if the room is above 90°F / 32°C. Bring out only the portions you plan to serve.

## Cooking Notes

Fluff flour, spoon it into the measuring cup and level it rather than scooping compacted flour. A pizza wheel makes the long dough strips easy to cut. For an overnight schedule, cover shaped rolls and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below for up to 24 hours, then let them rise until doubled before baking. For a freezer schedule, wrap the shaped pan tightly and freeze for 2–3 weeks for best quality; thaw in the refrigerator overnight, then let the rolls rise until doubled before baking. These schedules add chilling, thawing and the final rise to the listed elapsed time.
