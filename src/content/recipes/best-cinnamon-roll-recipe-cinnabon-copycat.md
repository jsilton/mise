## Chef's Note

These are big, soft Cinnabon-style rolls from Cambrea Bakes, with a buttery brown sugar spiral and cream cheese frosting. A tangzhong, flour cooked with water into a paste, lets the dough hold more moisture so the rolls stay soft for days. Frost them warm so some of the frosting melts into the spirals.

## Directions

1. **Make the tangzhong:** In a small saucepan, whisk the tangzhong flour and water. Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until it forms a thick paste, 4 to 5 minutes. Scrape it into a bowl and cool until just warm.
2. **Mix the dough:** In a stand mixer, combine the bread flour, sugar, yeast and salt. Add the cream, milk, egg, vanilla bean paste and tangzhong. Knead with the dough hook on low speed for 2 minutes.
3. **Knead in the butter:** With the mixer on low, add the European-style butter a piece at a time, letting each piece disappear before adding the next. Knead until the dough is smooth and elastic and a small piece stretches thin enough to see light through, 10 to 12 minutes.
4. **Rest the dough:** Shape the dough into a ball and set it in a buttered bowl. Cover and let it rest for 30 minutes.
5. **Make the filling:** While the dough rests, mix the filling butter, brown sugar, cinnamon and vanilla powder, if using, into a soft, spreadable paste.
6. **Roll and fill:** On a lightly floured counter, roll the dough into a 15-by-21-inch rectangle with a long side facing you. Spread the filling evenly over it, leaving a 1-inch bare strip along the far long edge.
7. **Cut and roll:** With a pizza wheel, cut across the long side into 12 strips, each about 1 3/4 inches wide. Roll each strip up toward its bare end and set the spirals in a parchment-lined 9-by-13-inch metal pan.
8. **Let them rise:** Cover and let the rolls rise until doubled, 1 to 2 hours. Toward the end, heat the oven to 325°F.
9. **Bake:** Bake until the tops are just turning golden and the center roll reads 190°F, 30 to 35 minutes.
10. **Make the frosting:** While the rolls bake, beat the cream cheese and butter until smooth. Beat in the powdered sugar, vanilla bean paste and cream until fluffy.
11. **Frost and serve:** Cool the rolls in the pan for 15 minutes. Spread the frosting over the warm rolls and serve.
