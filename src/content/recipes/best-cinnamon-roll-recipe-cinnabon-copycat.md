## Chef's Note

These soft, pull-apart rolls have a buttery cinnamon spiral and tangy cream-cheese frosting. Cooking a small portion of the flour with water helps the dough retain moisture; cool the paste until just warm before mixing it into the dough. Keep the filling butter soft but cool enough to hold its shape, so it spreads rather than runs out of the spirals.

Allow about 3–4 hours from mixing to frosting, with longer if the dough rises slowly. Make the filling during the 30-minute dough rest and the frosting during baking; judge the final rise by the rolls doubling in size.

## Directions

1.  **The Tangzhong:** In a small saucepan, whisk 1/4 cup flour and 3/4 cup water. Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly until it forms a thick paste. Let cool until just warm, not hot.
2.  **The Mix:** In a stand mixer, combine the dough flour, granulated sugar, yeast, and salt. Add the cream, milk, egg, vanilla, and the cooled Tangzhong. Knead with a dough hook for 2 minutes.
3.  **The Butter:** On low speed, add the 8 tbsp butter one tablespoon at a time. Once incorporated, knead for 10-12 minutes until the dough is smooth, elastic, and passes the windowpane test.
4.  **Rest the Dough:** Shape into a ball, place in a greased bowl, cover, and let rest in a warm spot for 30 minutes.
5.  **Make the Filling:** During the dough rest, mix the 12 tbsp filling butter, 3/4 cup brown sugar, and 2 tbsp cinnamon into a spreadable paste.
6.  **Shape:** On a floured surface, roll dough into a 15x21-inch rectangle with a 21-inch edge facing you. Spread the cinnamon filling to the edges, leaving a 1-inch border along the far 21-inch edge.
7.  **The Cut:** Cut across the 21-inch width to make twelve strips, each 1.75 inches wide and 15 inches long. Roll each strip away from you toward its clean edge and place in a parchment-lined metal 9x13-inch pan.
8.  **Final Rise:** Cover and let rise until doubled (1-2 hours). Preheat oven to 325°F.
9.  **Bake:** Bake for 30-35 minutes until the tops are just starting to turn golden brown.
10. **The Frosting:** While the rolls bake, whip the cream cheese and butter until smooth. Mix in powdered sugar, vanilla, and cream until fluffy.
11. **Finish:** Let the rolls cool for 15 minutes, then spread the frosting generously while they are still warm to allow some of it to seep into the spirals.

Refrigerate frosted leftovers in a covered container at 40°F / 4°C or below within 2 hours, or within 1 hour if the room is above 90°F / 32°C. Bring out only the portions you plan to serve.
