## Chef's Note

Kristyn Merkley’s buttermilk waffles get their tang from buttermilk, while the baking soda and powder provide lift. Mix the flour in briefly, rest the batter for ten minutes, and let each waffle cook until its middle is set. Keep finished waffles on a rack so steam can escape and the edges stay crisp.

## Directions

1. **Dry mix:** In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
2. **Wet mix:** Let the melted batter butter cool until lukewarm. In a separate bowl, whisk it with the buttermilk, then whisk in the eggs until evenly combined.
3. **Combine:** Add the wet ingredients to the dry. Whisk gently just until no dry flour pockets remain, scraping the bowl bottom and sides. Small hydrated lumps can remain. Fold in the vanilla.
4. **Rest and preheat:** Let the batter rest for 10 minutes while the waffle iron preheats according to its instructions. Avoid beating the rested batter again.
5. **Fill the iron:** Lightly grease the hot plates with the separate butter or oil allowance if recommended for your iron. Use the manufacturer’s batter amount; 1/3 to 1/2 cup suits some small irons, but a large Belgian iron may need more. Leave room for expansion rather than filling to the edge.
6. **Cook the batch:** Follow the iron’s readiness guidance and cook until steam has mostly subsided, the waffle releases readily and the exterior is golden. Check that the middle is cooked through with no wet batter. Repeat with the remaining batter; the first waffle’s cycle is not the cooking time for the whole batch.
7. **Serve or hold briefly:** Serve with salted butter, maple syrup, and fresh berries if desired. While finishing the batch, keep cooked waffles in a single layer on a rack over a sheet pan in a 225°F oven, rather than stacking them and trapping steam.

## Cooking Notes

For the original batch, six means portions, not six waffles of a fixed size. Allow roughly 25–40 minutes to cook the batch on a single iron; its capacity and cycle time control the schedule. A smaller iron may take longer.
