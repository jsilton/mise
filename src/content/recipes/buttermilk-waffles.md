## Chef's Note

Kristyn Merkley's buttermilk waffles from Lil' Luna are crisp outside and fluffy inside, with a little tang from the buttermilk. Mix the batter only until the flour disappears, then let it rest while the iron heats. Keep the finished waffles on a rack in a low oven, never stacked, so they stay crisp.

## Directions

1. **Heat the iron:** Heat the waffle iron. Heat the oven to 225°F and set a wire rack on a sheet pan for holding the waffles.
2. **Whisk the dry ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
3. **Whisk the wet ingredients:** In another bowl, whisk the buttermilk and melted butter. Whisk in the eggs and vanilla.
4. **Mix and rest the batter:** Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and whisk until just combined. A few small lumps are fine. Let the batter rest for 10 minutes, until slightly bubbly.
5. **Cook the waffles:** Brush the hot iron with butter. Pour in enough batter to cover the plates without reaching the edges and close the lid. Cook until the steam slows, the waffle is deep golden and it lifts out easily, 4 to 6 minutes.
6. **Hold and serve:** Set each waffle on the rack in the oven in a single layer while you cook the rest. Serve with salted butter, maple syrup and berries.
