## Chef's Note

Jeera rice, basmati perfumed with cumin seeds bloomed in ghee, is the everyday rice of North Indian kitchens. A cinnamon stick and bay leaf add a little warmth. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear so the grains stay separate, and let the pot rest covered at the end so they finish evenly.

## Directions

1. **Rinse the rice:** Swirl the rice in a bowl of cool water and pour off the cloudy water. Repeat 3 or 4 times until the water runs nearly clear, then drain well.
2. **Bloom the spices:** Melt the ghee in a heavy 2- to 3-quart saucepan over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds, cinnamon stick and bay leaf. Sizzle until the cumin smells toasty and just starts to color, 30 to 45 seconds.
3. **Coat the rice:** Add the rice and stir until every grain is glossy with the spiced ghee, about 1 minute.
4. **Simmer:** Add the water and salt, bring to a boil and stir once. Cover tightly, turn the heat to the lowest setting and cook until the water is absorbed, 15 to 18 minutes. If the grains are still firm and the pot is dry, sprinkle in a splash of hot water, cover and cook a few minutes more.
5. **Rest and fluff:** Take the pan off the heat and let it sit, covered, for 10 minutes. Remove the cinnamon and bay leaf and fluff the rice gently with a fork.
