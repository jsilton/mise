## Chef's Note

Roasting fresh Roma tomatoes with whole garlic cloves concentrates their sugar and gives this soup a deep, slightly smoky sweetness that canned tomatoes alone can't reach. The basil goes in right before blending, so it stays bright and green.

## Directions

1. **Roast the tomatoes:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Toss the Roma tomatoes and garlic with two-thirds of the oil and a good pinch of salt and pepper. Arrange the tomatoes cut side up on a rimmed baking sheet with the garlic tucked among them. Roast until the tomatoes collapse and brown at the edges, 40 to 45 minutes.
2. **Soften the onion:** When the tomatoes have about 15 minutes left, warm the rest of the oil in a large pot over medium-low heat. Add the onion and red pepper flakes and cook, stirring, until soft and golden, 8 to 10 minutes.
3. **Simmer the soup:** Add the canned tomatoes and their juices, the broth, and the roasted tomatoes, garlic and pan juices. Break up the whole tomatoes with a spoon. Bring to a simmer and cook until everything is very soft, about 15 minutes.
4. **Blend:** Take the pot off the heat and stir in the basil. Blend with an immersion blender until smooth.
5. **Finish and serve:** Stir in the cream, if using, and warm gently without boiling. Season with salt and pepper and serve hot.
