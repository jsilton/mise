## Chef's Note

Roast the fresh tomatoes until soft with browned edges, then combine them and their juices with the canned tomatoes. Protect the garlic from blackening and add basil near the end for aroma. Cream is optional; the roasted tomatoes still provide body without it.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Heat the oven to **400°F / 205°C**. Halve tomatoes, peel garlic and chop onion. Divide the oil: **two-thirds for roasting, one-third for the onion**.
2. **Roast:** Toss fresh tomatoes and garlic with the roasting oil, salt and pepper. Arrange tomatoes cut-side up in one layer on a rimmed tray, garlic nestled among them. Roast about **40–45 minutes**, checking garlic early and removing softened cloves if they darken too quickly. Tomatoes should collapse and show browned edges, not need to become black.
3. **Soften onion:** During the last part of roasting, heat the remaining oil in the soup pot over medium-low. Add onion and pepper flakes and cook about **8–10 minutes**, stirring, until soft without scorching.
4. **Combine and simmer:** Add canned tomatoes and all their juices, broth, roasted tomatoes, garlic and tray juices. Break up whole tomatoes with a spoon. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook about **15 minutes**, stirring, until fully softened.
5. **Blend:** Stir in basil. Remove from heat and let active bubbling stop. Blend with an immersion blender, keeping its guard submerged and stopping before lifting it. Alternatively, cool to your countertop blender’s permitted temperature and blend in as many small batches as its hot-liquid fill limit requires, with the lid and steam vent arranged exactly as its instructions specify. Start low; do not use a sealed personal cup for hot soup.
6. **Finish:** Stir in cream if using and warm gently without a hard boil. Taste for salt and pepper, then serve.
