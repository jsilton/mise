## Chef's Note

This is the Silton family's Thanksgiving squash, boiled, mashed by hand and left a little coarse. Butter, maple syrup and a little sugar make it sweet and rich, and nutmeg and black pepper keep it from tasting like pie.

## Directions

1. **Boil the squash:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add butternut squash and cook until a knife slides easily into the thickest piece, about 25 minutes.
2. **Mash:** Drain the squash well and return it to the pot or a heavy saucepan. Mash it coarsely with a potato masher.
3. **Stir in the butter:** Set the pot over low heat and add unsalted butter, sugar, pure maple syrup, freshly grated nutmeg, and freshly ground black pepper, adding the sugar a little at a time until it tastes right to you. Stir until the butter melts and the purée is hot, about 5 minutes.
4. **Season and serve:** Taste, season with salt and serve hot.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. For deeper flavor, roast the squash instead of boiling it. Cut it into 1-inch cubes, toss with 2 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt, and roast in a single layer at 400°F until tender with golden edges, 30 to 40 minutes. Then mash and season as above.
