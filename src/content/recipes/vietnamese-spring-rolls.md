## Chef's Note

Mint, cilantro and Thai basil bring freshness to shrimp, noodles and crisp vegetables. Drain the fillings well, then let each damp wrapper soften on the work surface. Peanut-hoisin sauce supplies the rich, savory finish; keep it separate until serving so the filling stays tidy.

## Directions

1. **Cook the shrimp:** Bring a saucepan of lightly salted water to a boil. Add thawed shrimp and reduce to a gentle simmer. Start checking after about 2 minutes; cook until firm, pearly and opaque throughout, checking 145°F / 63°C in the thickest part with a suitable thin probe. Transfer to clean ice water just until cold, drain and pat dry. Halve lengthwise with clean utensils.
2. **Prepare the noodles:** Use fresh water to cook or soak the dry rice vermicelli according to package directions until tender without a hard center. Drain, rinse under cool running water and drain well again. Snip long strands into manageable lengths.
3. **Prepare and portion:** Remove tough lettuce ribs, prepare the leaves and herbs, and cut carrot and cucumber into thin matchsticks. Divide all shrimp and the other fillings among the intended wrappers rather than assuming a fixed shrimp count. Keep any excess for additional rolls or a side salad.
4. **Mix the sauce:** Whisk hoisin, peanut butter, sriracha and the measured warm water until smooth. Add a little more water only if too thick to dip. Scatter the crushed peanuts over the sauce.
5. **Wet a wrapper:** Fill a wide shallow dish with water at the temperature recommended on the wrapper package. Briefly wet both sides of one wrapper and place it on a clean smooth work surface. Wait until pliable and slightly tacky; do not soak until limp.
6. **Fill and roll:** Place a portion of lettuce, noodles, carrot, cucumber and all three herbs across the lower third, leaving clear side margins. Fold the bottom over the filling and fold in the sides. Arrange that roll's share of shrimp curved side down near the fold, then roll snugly to close without stretching. Repeat, using spare wrappers for excess filling or tears.
7. **Serve:** Share the rolls among four as a side, with the peanut-hoisin sauce. Cut with a sharp damp knife if desired. Keep rolls separate and covered in the refrigerator if serving is delayed.
