## Chef's Note

Gỏi cuốn are Vietnam's fresh rice paper rolls, packed with shrimp, rice noodles and handfuls of mint, cilantro and Thai basil. Dip each wrapper only briefly, since it keeps softening as you fill it. A thick peanut-hoisin sauce on the side makes them rich enough for a meal.

## Directions

1. **Cook the shrimp:** Bring a saucepan of salted water to a boil. Add the shrimp and simmer until pink and opaque, about 2 minutes. Move them to ice water, then drain, pat dry and halve lengthwise.
2. **Cook the noodles:** Bring the water back to a boil. Cook the vermicelli until tender, 3 to 4 minutes. Drain, rinse under cold water and drain well. Snip into shorter lengths with scissors.
3. **Make the sauce:** Whisk the hoisin, peanut butter, sriracha and warm water until smooth, adding more water if it is too thick to dip. Scatter the peanuts on top.
4. **Set up to roll:** Arrange the lettuce, herbs, carrot, cucumber, noodles and shrimp on a board. Fill a wide, shallow dish with warm water.
5. **Soften a wrapper:** Dip one wrapper in the water for 2 to 3 seconds and lay it flat on a damp work surface. It should still feel a little firm.
6. **Fill and roll:** Lay some lettuce, noodles, carrot, cucumber and herbs across the lower third. Fold the bottom edge over the filling and tuck in the sides. Lay a few shrimp halves pink side down above the fold, then roll up snugly. Repeat with the rest.
7. **Serve:** Cut each roll in half with a sharp, wet knife. Serve right away with the sauce.
