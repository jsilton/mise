## Chef's Note

This is a big pot of old-fashioned cream of tomato soup, rich with butter and smoothed with half-and-half. Cook the onions slowly so they turn soft and sweet without browning. A little honey at the end rounds off the acid of the tomatoes.

## Directions

1. **Soften the onions:** Melt the butter in an 8-quart heavy pot over medium-low heat. Add the onions and cook, stirring often, until soft and translucent but not browned, about 20 minutes.
2. **Cook the flour:** Stir in the flour until no dry spots remain. Cook, stirring, until the mixture is pale gold, about 3 minutes.
3. **Add the tomatoes:** Stir in the broth a little at a time until smooth. Add the tomatoes and their juices, the sugar, celery salt and pepper. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring so nothing sticks.
4. **Simmer:** Lower the heat and simmer gently, scraping the bottom of the pot often, until the onions and tomatoes are very soft, about 30 minutes.
5. **Blend:** Take the pot off the heat and blend with an immersion blender until smooth.
6. **Finish and season:** Stir in the half-and-half and honey. Warm over low heat without boiling, about 5 minutes. Taste and add salt, since the celery salt already seasons it.
