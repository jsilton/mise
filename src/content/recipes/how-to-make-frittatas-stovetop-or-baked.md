## Chef's Note

The Italian open-faced omelet, and the best way to use up a drawer of vegetables. Cook the vegetables until tender and dry before the eggs go in, so the frittata never weeps. A splash of cream keeps the eggs custardy, and a few minutes on the stove set the edges before the oven puffs the top.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 425°F with a rack in the center.
2. **Whisk the eggs:** Whisk the eggs, cream and salt in a large bowl just until the yolks and whites are blended. Stir in half the cheese.
3. **Cook the vegetables:** Heat the olive oil in a 12-inch oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Add the firmest vegetables first, such as peppers, then zucchini, then greens. Season with a pinch of salt and cook, stirring, until tender and no liquid is left in the pan, 6 to 10 minutes.
4. **Set the edges:** Pour the eggs over the vegetables and spread them evenly. Cook without stirring until the edges look set and opaque, about 1 minute.
5. **Bake:** Sprinkle the rest of the cheese over the top. Bake until puffed and the center barely wobbles when you nudge the pan, 8 to 12 minutes.
6. **Serve:** Let the frittata settle for 5 minutes. Scatter the herbs over the top and cut into wedges. Serve warm or at room temperature.
