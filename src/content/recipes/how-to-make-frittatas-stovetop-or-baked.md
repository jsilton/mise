## Chef's Note

Cook the vegetables until tender and let excess moisture evaporate before adding eggs; spinach and zucchini contribute different amounts of water. Whisk the eggs just until blended, then finish gently enough to keep the center tender. The skillet method starts the edges on the stove; the casserole and muffin versions begin with cooked vegetables and bake at 350 °F.

## Directions

1. **Choose the method and blend:** Preheat to 425 °F for the stovetop-to-oven method, or 350 °F for a baked variant. Whisk eggs, dairy and measured salt just until yolks and whites blend. Fold in half the cheese, reserving half for topping. The original skillet batch uses a 12-inch oven-safe skillet; the pan and handle must tolerate the oven temperature.

2. **Cook the vegetables:** Heat the measured olive oil in the skillet over medium heat. Add dense vegetables such as peppers first, then zucchini and finally spinach. Cook until tender rather than merely tender-crisp, letting released water evaporate so there is no puddle before adding eggs. Use the listed raw vegetable amount; its cooked volume depends on the mix.

3. **Set the skillet edges:** For the stovetop method, pour the eggs over the cooked vegetables and stir briefly to distribute. Let the outside edge become pale and opaque, about 30 seconds to 1 minute. For a baked variant, skip to its method below after cooking the vegetables.

4. **Finish the skillet:** Sprinkle the reserved half of the cheese over the eggs and transfer carefully to the 425 °F oven. Bake about 7–14 minutes as a first range, until puffed and opaque, with a center that is gently set rather than liquid and reaches 160 °F / 71 °C. Check without touching the skillet. Remove as soon as the endpoint is reached; do not chase a deeply browned top.

5. **Baked casserole variant:** For the original batch, grease a 9-by 13-inch baking dish with the separate butter or oil allowance. Let the cooked vegetables cool briefly, stir them into the eggs, pour into the dish and add the reserved cheese. Bake at 350 °F for about 20–25 minutes as a first range, until the center is set and reaches 160 °F / 71 °C. Check earlier or continue as needed for the actual depth.

6. **Baked mini variant:** Instead of a casserole, grease standard muffin cups with the separate butter or oil allowance. Briefly cool the cooked vegetables and stir them into the eggs. The original batch fills about 18 cups with a scant 1/3 cup mixture each; scaled batches need more or fewer standard cups at the same depth. Add reserved cheese and bake at 350 °F for about 13–17 minutes as a first range, until centers are set and reach 160 °F / 71 °C. Check partially filled tins early and allow oven space or extra batches.

7. **Finish:** Let settle briefly, garnish with the fresh herbs and serve. The full original batch makes six portions; apportion a different batch size accordingly. The methods are alternatives using one batch of eggs, vegetables and cheese. Serve promptly; refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90 °F, and reheat centers to 165 °F / 74 °C.
