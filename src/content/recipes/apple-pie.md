## Chef's Note

Cook the apples until they start to soften, catch every drop of their juice and reduce it to a syrup before returning it to the fruit. Cool the filling before enclosing it in cold pastry. The vodka dough and the egg dough use different liquids and seasoning; choose a complete route rather than mixing the two.

## Directions

1. **Make dough:** Pulse all-purpose flour, unsalted butter, granulated sugar, kosher salt, ice-cold vodka, and ice-cold water in this order: flour, dough sugar, salt and butter until coarse meal and pea-size butter pieces remain; then add all vodka and measured water and pulse briefly until the dough holds when pressed. Gather by hand without kneading smooth. Wrap and refrigerate for at least 1 hour. If the mixture cannot hold together with the listed liquid, stop and check the measurements rather than replacing vodka with an assumed equal egg mixture.
2. **Cook apples:** Use baking apples, granulated sugar, butter, and lemon juice as follows: toss all peeled, cored slices with lemon juice and filling sugar. Melt all filling butter in a large skillet over medium heat, add the apples and cook, stirring, starting checks around 7 minutes until they release juice and begin to soften. Continue as needed for the actual apple variety and slice thickness. Use suitable extra skillet rounds when scaling, retaining all fruit, butter and juice.
3. **Reduce juices and cool:** Strain apples in a colander over a clean bowl, catching all the juice. Return that juice to the skillet and simmer, beginning checks around 5–8 minutes, until thick and syrupy; stop before it scorches. Toss all the reduction and ground cinnamon and ground nutmeg back with the apples. Let the filling cool completely before assembly.
4. **Roll and fill:** Use all-purpose flour lightly on the work surface. Divide dough into equal halves for bottom and top. For the original batch, roll each half to roughly 11–12 inches and fit the bottom into a 9-inch pie plate, leaving enough overhang to seal. Add all cooled apples and syrup, mounding slightly if needed without forcing an overfull pan. Cover with the second round and seal/crimp the edges. Cut four steam vents; keep the dough cold, returning it to the refrigerator if it softens.
5. **Wash and sprinkle:** Use egg and granulated sugar to brush a thin coat over the sealed crust and sprinkle the separate topping sugar. Do not take sugar from the filling or dough. Discard unused raw egg wash and clean its tools before handling the baked pie.
6. **Bake:** Heat the oven to 375°F with a rack in the lower third. Place the pie on a rimmed baking sheet to catch spills. Begin checking around 50 minutes for the original-size pie, continuing until the crust is well baked and golden and filling juices bubble through the vents. Cover a browning edge with foil if needed; a fixed 50-minute clock does not establish a finished crust in every oven.
7. **Cool:** Cool on a rack for at least 2 hours before slicing so the hot filling settles. Keep the cooked-apple juices in the pie; a short rest produces looser slices.
