## Chef's Note

Weeknight tacos with the sticky, charred edges of carnitas in under an hour. A spoonful of fish sauce disappears into the honey, soy and lime glaze and gives it deep savory flavor. Roast the thighs through first, then glaze and broil them just long enough to bubble, and finish the sliced meat with fresh lime.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Make the pickled onions first if you don't have them, so they chill while you cook. Heat the oven to 425°F with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Line two rimmed sheet pans with foil.
2. **Mix the glaze:** Whisk the honey, soy sauce, fish sauce, half the lime juice, the zest, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic, oil and pepper. Pour half the glaze into a small bowl and set it aside for brushing.
3. **Season the chicken:** Pat the thighs dry, sprinkle them with the salt and toss them with the other half of the glaze. Lay them flat on the two pans with space between them.
4. **Roast:** Roast until the thickest thigh reaches 165°F, 20 to 30 minutes, swapping the pans halfway through.
5. **Glaze and broil:** Turn on the broiler. Brush the chicken with the reserved glaze. Broil one pan at a time about 6 inches from the heat until the glaze bubbles and browns in spots, 1 to 3 minutes.
6. **Rest and slice:** Rest the chicken for 5 minutes. Slice it thin across the grain and toss it with the rest of the lime juice.
7. **Warm the tortillas:** While the chicken rests, warm the tortillas in a dry skillet over medium heat until soft and pliable, 20 to 30 seconds per side. Stack them in a clean towel to keep warm.
8. **Fill the tacos:** Fill each tortilla with chicken, avocado, pickled onions and cilantro. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, if using.
