## Chef's Note

Honey, soy and fish sauce make a concentrated surface glaze; they do not turn chicken into slow-cooked carnitas. Roasting cooks the center, a brief broil adds color, and fresh lime finishes the sliced meat. Check each job separately so you do not burn the sugar while waiting for the chicken to cook.

## Directions

1. **Start onions and heat:** Make the [pickled onions](/mise/recipes/pickled-red-onions) if needed, then let them chill while you cook. Heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Prepare two broiler-safe rimmed metal trays, bare or lined with suitable foil; do not use parchment.
2. **Divide the glaze:** Whisk honey, soy sauce, fish sauce, 2 tbsp of the lime juice, zest, cumin, paprika, garlic, oil and pepper. Reserve half in a clean bowl. Keep the remaining 2 tbsp lime juice separate for the final finish. Toss chicken with the other half of the glaze. No extra salt is needed by default.
3. **Roast with space:** Open thighs flat and distribute them between the trays with gaps. Roast about 25–35 minutes, rotating the pans and swapping rack positions after fifteen minutes. Start checking the thickest part of each thigh at twenty-five minutes. Continue until each reaches at least 165°F / 74°C; remove finished pieces if others need longer.
4. **Glaze and briefly broil:** Brush cooked chicken with the clean reserved glaze using a clean brush; divide it between the trays. Place one tray at a time about 6–8 inches below the broiler, following the appliance guidance. Broil for roughly 1–3 minutes, watching constantly, until bubbling and browned in spots. Stop before blackening. A broiler is optional: the final glaze can set for a few minutes in the hot oven instead.
5. **Rest, slice and warm:** Rest cooked thighs five minutes, then slice across the meat and toss with the reserved fresh lime juice. Meanwhile warm tortillas in batches in a dry skillet over medium heat, about 20–30 seconds per side or until pliable, and keep wrapped in a clean towel. Drain one cup onions and slice the avocado.
6. **Assemble:** Divide chicken among sixteen tortillas, then distribute avocado, drained onions, cilantro and optional sesame seeds. Serve two tacos per person. Bread is already counted; rice would be a replacement starch for a different bowl, not another required taco ingredient.
