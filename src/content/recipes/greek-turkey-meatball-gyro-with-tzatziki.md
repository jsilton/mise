## Chef's Note

These are gyro flavors in a weeknight form: oregano and garlic turkey meatballs in warm pita with a cool cucumber-yogurt sauce. Breadcrumbs soaked in milk keep the lean turkey tender, and roasting all the meatballs on one hot pan browns them without any turning.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Brush a rimmed half-sheet pan with a little of the oil.
2. **Make the sauce:** Squeeze the grated cucumber in a clean towel until it stops dripping. Stir it into the yogurt with the lemon juice, dill, garlic powder and salt, then refrigerate.
3. **Soak the breadcrumbs:** In a large bowl, stir the breadcrumbs into the milk and let them soften for 5 minutes. Beat in the egg.
4. **Shape the meatballs:** Add the turkey, spinach, minced onion, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper. Mix gently with your hands just until combined. Shape into 12 balls about 1 1/2 inches across, set them apart on the pan and brush them with the rest of the oil.
5. **Roast:** Roast until lightly browned and the centers reach 165°F, 15 to 20 minutes.
6. **Warm the pitas:** While the meatballs roast, warm the pitas one at a time in a dry skillet over medium heat, about 30 seconds per side. Stack them in a clean towel to stay soft.
7. **Fill the pitas:** Taste the sauce and add more lemon juice if it needs it. Spread sauce in each pita, add three meatballs and top with the tomato, diced cucumber and sliced onion.
