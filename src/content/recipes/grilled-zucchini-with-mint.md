## Chef's Note

A quick summer side for anything off the grill. Slicing the zucchini into long planks gives plenty of surface to brown and keeps the pieces from falling through the grates. The lemon, mint and last drizzle of olive oil go on after cooking, so they stay bright and fresh.

## Directions

1. **Heat the grill:** Heat a grill or grill pan to medium-high.
2. **Season the zucchini:** Pat the planks dry. Brush both sides with most of the oil and season with the salt and pepper.
3. **Grill the zucchini:** Lay the planks across the grill bars. Grill until browned in patches and tender with a little resistance in the center, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Work in batches on a grill pan.
4. **Dress and serve:** Spread the zucchini on a platter. Drizzle with the rest of the oil and the lemon juice, scatter the zest and mint over the top and finish with flaky salt. Serve warm or at room temperature.
