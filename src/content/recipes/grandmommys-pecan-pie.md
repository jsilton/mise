## Chef's Note

Pecans, light corn syrup and vanilla make a rich filling that firms as it chills. Give the center time to set while protecting the pastry edges, then refrigerate promptly and let the pies cool completely before slicing.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the shells:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Set unbaked pastry pie shells on a rimmed baking sheet. Prepare or handle the pastry according to its dough recipe or package instructions; this route uses unbaked pastry shells. Leave enough space below the rims for the filling to move slightly during baking. For the original batch, each shell must hold half the complete filling; do not force a deep mound into a small shell.
2. **Mix the syrup filling:** Combine granulated sugar, light corn syrup, and large eggs in the bowl in this order: stir the sugar and syrup together, then mix in the slightly beaten eggs until evenly blended.
3. **Add the melted fat and vanilla:** Stir in unsalted butter, vanilla extract, and sea salt until uniform. The melted fat should be warm enough to remain liquid but not hot enough to cook the eggs.
4. **Stir in all the pecans:** Stir in pecan halves last, keeping all the nuts and syrup mixture together.
5. **Divide the complete filling:** Share the whole nut-and-syrup mixture equally among the prepared shells, stirring between pours so each receives nuts as well as syrup. Check that the filling stays below the rims; if the chosen shells are too small, use larger shells rather than discarding filling.
6. **Bake until the filling is set:** Bake at 350°F, beginning the first check around 40 minutes. Continue until the edges are set and the center moves together with only a gentle tremble, rather than a liquid wave. A food thermometer placed in the central filling, away from a nut or the pan, must read at least 160°F; keep baking until the filling also has the set texture. Shield the pastry edges with foil if they brown too quickly. Shell depth, frozen pastry and oven heat can change the time, so check each pie separately and remove it when ready.
7. **Cool, then chill completely:** Set the baked pies on a rack until the initial steam subsides, then refrigerate them while still warm if necessary. Get them into the refrigerator within 2 hours of baking, or within 1 hour above 90°F. Leave them in the refrigerator until completely cool and firm before slicing; do not extend counter cooling to wait for a clean slice.
