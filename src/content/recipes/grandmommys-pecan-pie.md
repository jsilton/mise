## Chef's Note

This is Grandmommy's pecan pie. The filling is mixed in a set order, sugar first and pecans last, and it makes two shallow pies thick with nuts. Let them cool completely so the filling sets.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. **Mix the sugar and syrup:** Stir sugar and light corn syrup together in a large bowl.
3. **Add the eggs:** Stir in eggs until smooth.
4. **Add the margarine:** Stir in margarine and vanilla extract.
5. **Add the pecans:** Stir in pecans last.
6. **Fill the shells:** Set unbaked 9-inch pie shells on a rimmed baking sheet. Divide the filling between them, stirring between pours so each pie gets its share of pecans.
7. **Bake:** Bake until the filling is puffed and set at the edges and the center jiggles only slightly, about 40 minutes.
8. **Cool:** Let the pies cool on a rack until the filling is firm, about 2 hours, before slicing.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- Use unsalted butter in place of the margarine for a richer, toastier flavor.
- Add a pinch of sea salt with the vanilla to balance the sweetness.
- If the crust edges brown before the center sets, cover them loosely with foil and keep baking.
