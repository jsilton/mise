## Chef's Note

Sausage, thawed hash browns, full heavy cream and sharp Cheddar make a rich breakfast bake for a crowd. Foil protects the custard during the first stage; the uncovered hotter finish browns the cheese. Dijon and hot sauce provide tang, while a short rest helps the slices hold together.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Prepare scallions and butter or nonstick spray: reserve the chopped dark-green scallion parts for the finish and use the white/light-green parts in the skillet. Grease a 9-by-13-inch or other 3-quart baking dish for the original batch. Check space for the full mixture and baking headroom; scaled batches need proportionate pan area/depth, sometimes separate dishes. Do not simply pile a larger batch deeper into one dish.
2. **Brown sausage:** Have raw bulk breakfast sausage and red bell pepper ready. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown the sausage, breaking it into small pieces. The original sausage browning takes about 6–8 minutes; check several thicker pieces for 160°F / 71°C for pork/beef sausage or 165°F / 74°C for poultry sausage. Add the red bell pepper and chopped white/light-green scallion parts and cook about 3 minutes until the pepper starts to soften. Keep the rendered fat with the mixture. Transfer to a heatproof plate to cool slightly while you whisk the custard; do not leave it out for an extended cooling period.
3. **Whisk custard:** Whisk large eggs, heavy cream, Dijon mustard, hot sauce, salt, and black pepper in a large bowl until well combined. Do not taste the raw egg mixture.
4. **Combine:** Use the fully thawed hash browns for this step. Fold frozen shredded hash browns and 3/4 of the sharp Cheddar and all of the cooked sausage, vegetables and rendered fat into the custard. The cheese allocation is three quarters here and one quarter reserved for the top at every batch size.
5. **Assemble:** Transfer the full mixture to the prepared dish or divide proportionally among enough dishes, keeping a similar depth and baking headroom. Smooth into an even layer. For overnight assembly, promptly cover and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or below, then bake within 24 hours. Follow the dish manufacturer’s cold-to-hot oven instructions; a fixed counter wait is not a substitute.
6. **Bake covered:** Cover with foil and bake at 350°F for about 50 minutes for the original batch and dish.
7. **Finish uncovered:** Remove the foil and scatter 1/4 of the sharp Cheddar over the top. Increase the oven to 400°F and bake about 10–15 minutes more for the original batch, until the top is golden, the center is set and the casserole reaches 165°F / 74°C in the center and other thick areas of each dish. A cold assembled casserole or changed pan depth can need longer; color alone is not the endpoint.
8. **Rest and serve:** Let stand for 10 minutes, then garnish with all of the reserved dark-green scallion parts and serve.

## Cooking Notes

For a scallion-in-custard version, cook the white and light-green parts as above, whisk about half of the remaining chopped green parts into the eggs and reserve the other half for garnishing. In this version the hot sauce can be omitted. The measured seasoning option is 1½ teaspoons salt and 1 teaspoon black pepper for the original batch; scale proportionally instead of using the main to-taste allowances. The salt crystal size is unspecified, so these are not equal-volume fine-salt/kosher-salt alternatives. All sausage, fat, cream, eggs, potatoes and cheese remain unchanged.

The baked casserole can be frozen in wrapped portions for up to 3 months as a quality plan. Cool and refrigerate promptly before packaging for the freezer; refrigerator-thaw before reheating.
