## Chef's Note

This is the ultimate holiday tart. The key is roasting the sliced heirloom tomatoes at 425°F for 40 minutes before adding them to the tart. This concentrates their flavor into a tender consistency and prevents the bottom crust from turning into a soggy mess. For the crust, adding cider vinegar inhibits gluten development, resulting in a tart shell that is shatteringly tender.

## Directions

1.  **The Crust:** In a food processor, pulse flour, baking powder, 2 tsp thyme, and 1 tsp salt. Add butter and 4 oz goat cheese; pulse until crumbly. Pulse in vinegar, then add ice water 1 tbsp at a time just until the dough comes together; you may not need all 4 tbsp. Wrap and **refrigerate for 30 minutes**.
2.  **The Roast:** Preheat oven to 425°F. While the dough chills, arrange tomato slices across two large rimmed sheet pans. Drizzle with 3 tbsp oil and season with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper. Roast for 30-45 minutes until shrunk and dry to the touch. Remove.
3.  **Thermal Prep:** Roll dough into a 12-inch circle. Press into an 11-inch tart pan. **Freeze for 20 minutes** (mandatory for structure).
4.  **Blind Bake:** Line the frozen crust with foil and pie weights. Bake at 450°F for 20-25 minutes until set and beginning to turn golden at the edges. Carefully remove the weights and foil; cool for about 10 minutes. Reduce the oven to 425°F.
5.  **The Emulsion:** Whisk ricotta, garlic, the remaining 2 tsp thyme, and the remaining 8 oz goat cheese until smooth. Spread evenly over the bottom of the crust.
6.  **Assemble:** Layer the roasted tomatoes in a beautiful overlapping pattern over the cheese. Drizzle with the remaining 1 tbsp oil and sprinkle with the remaining 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.
7.  **Final Bake:** Bake at 425°F for 20-25 minutes until the tomatoes and crust are browned.
8.  **Serve:** Cool for 20-30 minutes until just warm before removing from the tart pan and slicing. Garnish with fresh purple and green basil for the aromatic **High Note**.
