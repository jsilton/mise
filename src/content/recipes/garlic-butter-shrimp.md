## Chef's Note

Lift the cooked shrimp out before reducing the wine. That gives the sauce time to concentrate while the shrimp stay tender. Add lemon and parsley at the end, then bring everything together only long enough to coat.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Zest the lemon before juicing it and measure the listed juice. Mince garlic and chop parsley. Pat shrimp dry, then season with half the salt and all the pepper. Keep the remaining salt for tasting the sauce.

2. **Cook the shrimp:** Melt half the butter over medium to medium-high heat, dividing this allocation between batches if needed. Add shrimp in a single layer without crowding. Cook about 1–2 minutes on the first side, turn, then cook another 1–2 minutes or as needed until firm, pearly and opaque through the center; a fine-tip thermometer inserted into a thick shrimp should read 145°F / 63°C. Move cooked shrimp to a clean plate; repeat with the remaining shrimp.

3. **Cook the garlic:** Reduce heat to medium-low and let the pan cool briefly if very hot. Add the remaining butter and garlic. Stir about 30 seconds until fragrant, then add pepper flakes for another 15 seconds. Do not let the garlic darken.

4. **Reduce:** Pour in wine or stock and scrape up the pale brown pan juices. Bring to a gentle simmer and reduce the liquid by roughly half, about 2–4 minutes, leaving the shrimp on their plate.

5. **Finish:** Turn off the heat and stir in lemon juice, parsley and zest. Add the shrimp and their juices and toss to coat; warm briefly over low heat only if needed. Taste and add remaining salt as needed. Swirl in a small piece of optional extra butter if you want a rounder finish.

6. **Serve promptly:** Divide shrimp and sauce evenly and serve with separately cooked noodles, rice or bread. Do not hold the finished shrimp in a simmering sauce.
