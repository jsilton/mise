## Chef's Note

Esquites is Mexican street corn off the cob, sold in cups from carts and dressed with lime, chile, cheese and mayonnaise. Brown the kernels in a hot skillet in two batches so they sear instead of steam. The toasty patches balance the creamy dressing and sharp lime.

## Directions

1. **Mix the dressing:** Whisk the mayonnaise, crema, half the lime juice, the ancho chile and the garlic in a large bowl.
2. **Brown the first batch:** Heat half the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high until it shimmers. Spread half the corn in the pan and leave it alone for 2 minutes. Stir and cook until tender with golden-brown patches, 3 to 5 minutes more. Tip it onto a plate.
3. **Brown the second batch:** Add the rest of the oil and the rest of the corn and cook it the same way.
4. **Dress the corn:** Let the corn stop steaming for 2 to 3 minutes. Add it to the dressing and toss, then fold in the cotija, scallions, cilantro and jalapeño.
5. **Season and serve:** Taste and add the rest of the lime juice and a little salt if it needs it. Serve warm or cool.
