## Chef's Note

Esquites takes several forms, including brothy cups and fried preparations. This is a skillet salad using sweet corn, not a claim that all Mexican esquites must be charred. Brown some surfaces for flavor, then finish with chile, lime, cheese, and a modest creamy dressing. Blackening every kernel would hide the corn's sweetness.

## Directions

1. **Prepare and mix the dressing:** Drain corn thoroughly and blot away surface water. Whisk mayonnaise, crema, 1 tbsp lime juice, ground ancho chile, and garlic in a large bowl. Set aside the remaining lime, cheese, scallions, cilantro, and optional jalapeño.
2. **Brown the first batch:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Add half the corn and spread it out. Leave for about 2 minutes, then stir and continue cooking for 3–5 minutes, until tender with golden-brown patches. Reduce heat if it spits violently or scorches. Transfer to a wide plate.
3. **Repeat:** Add the remaining oil and corn. Cook the second batch in the same way, adjusting the heat as needed. If dark residue is burning, remove it carefully before adding the second batch.
4. **Dress while warm:** Let the corn stop steaming vigorously for 2–3 minutes. Add both batches to the dressing and toss. Fold in cotija, scallions, cilantro, and optional jalapeño.
5. **Balance and serve:** Taste, add the remaining lime by the teaspoon as needed, and add salt only if the cheese has not supplied enough. Divide into six side portions and serve warm or cool. Chill promptly if making ahead.
