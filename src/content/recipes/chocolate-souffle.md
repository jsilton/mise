## Chef's Note

A French chocolate soufflé is dinner-party theater. It rises tall in the oven and starts to sink at the table. Extra egg whites keep it light, and bittersweet chocolate keeps it intense, so have everyone seated when it comes out.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the dish:** Heat the oven to 375°F. Butter a 6-cup soufflé dish generously. Coat it with the sugar for the dish and knock out the excess.
2. **Melt the chocolate:** Melt the chocolate in a heatproof bowl over barely simmering water, stirring until smooth. Take it off the heat, let it cool for 2 minutes, then whisk in the yolks. The mixture will stiffen.
3. **Beat the whites:** In a clean bowl, beat the egg whites and salt until they hold soft peaks. Gradually add the remaining sugar and beat until the whites are stiff and glossy.
4. **Lighten the base:** Stir about a fifth of the meringue into the chocolate to loosen it.
5. **Fold in the meringue:** Gently fold in the rest of the meringue, lifting from the bottom, until no white streaks remain.
6. **Fill the dish:** Spoon the batter into the dish. Run your thumb around the inside rim to make a shallow groove so the soufflé rises straight.
7. **Bake the soufflé:** Bake on the middle rack until puffed and crusted on top, with a center that still jiggles slightly, 24 to 26 minutes.
8. **Dust and serve:** Dust with powdered sugar and serve right away with the whipped cream.
