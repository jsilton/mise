## Chef's Note

Blended oats and cottage cheese make a batter that cooks up like a flour pancake, with a good dose of protein and no added sugar. The ripe banana sweetens it but also browns fast, so cook these low and slow until the centers set.

## Directions

1. **Blend the batter:** Put the oats, banana, cottage cheese, egg, baking powder, cinnamon and vanilla in a blender. Blend until completely smooth, 30 to 45 seconds, scraping down the sides once.
2. **Heat the skillet:** Set a large nonstick skillet over medium-low heat and coat it lightly with oil spray.
3. **Pour the pancakes:** Drop the batter by 1/4 cupfuls into the skillet, leaving room between them. Sprinkle blueberries or chocolate chips over the wet tops, if using.
4. **Cook and flip:** Cook until the edges look set and the bottoms are golden, 2 to 3 minutes. Flip gently and cook until the centers are set and springy, about 2 minutes more.
5. **Cook the rest:** Wipe the skillet clean between batches so the banana sugars don't scorch, then spray it again and cook the remaining batter.
6. **Serve:** Serve hot, with Greek yogurt or peanut butter.
