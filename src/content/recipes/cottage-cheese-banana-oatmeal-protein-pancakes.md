## Chef's Note

Blend the oats and cottage cheese smooth before cooking this small batch. Medium-low heat gives the egg-rich centers time to set as the banana sweetens and browns the outside. Keep the pancakes small and flip when their edges hold together.

## Directions

1. **Blend:** Place the oats, banana, cottage cheese, egg, baking powder, cinnamon, and vanilla in a blender. Blend for about 30–45 seconds until smooth, stopping and scraping with the blender off if needed. Use a small blender cup if a wide jug cannot catch this small amount of batter.
2. **Heat:** Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium-low heat. Lightly coat it with oil spray suitable for the pan, following the spray and pan instructions.
3. **Pour:** Drop batter by 1/4 cupfuls, leaving room to slide a spatula underneath. If using blueberries or dark chocolate chips, sprinkle them onto the wet surface now. The original batch makes roughly 3–4 pancakes; the count depends on scoop size.
4. **Cook and flip:** Cook until the underside is golden and the edges look set, about 2–3 minutes; there may be only a few bubbles. Slide a spatula fully underneath and flip gently. Cook about 2 minutes more, until the middle is set with no wet batter. Lower the heat if the outside colors before the center sets. If checking with a thin thermometer from the side, the center should reach 160°F.
5. **Repeat:** Between batches, move the skillet off the heat and carefully remove browned residue with a heatproof utensil and a folded towel held with tongs. Reapply a light coating of spray as directed, then return the pan to medium-low heat and cook the remaining batter.
6. **Serve:** Serve immediately, with Greek yogurt or peanut butter if desired.
