## Chef's Note

Crinkle cookies are a holiday cookie-tray classic, fudgy inside with a snowy, cracked top. Rolling the dough in granulated sugar before the powdered sugar keeps the white coat from melting into the dough. Chill the dough fully so the balls hold their shape.

## Directions

1. **Melt the chocolate:** Melt the chocolate and butter in a heatproof bowl set over a pan of gently simmering water, stirring until smooth. Take the bowl off the heat and let it cool slightly.
2. **Mix the dry ingredients:** Whisk the flour, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda and salt together in a bowl.
3. **Beat the eggs and sugar:** In a large bowl, beat the brown sugar, the granulated sugar for the dough, the eggs and the vanilla on medium-high speed until pale and frothy, about 3 minutes.
4. **Make the dough:** Beat in the chocolate mixture. On low speed, add the dry ingredients just until combined.
5. **Chill the dough:** Cover and refrigerate until the dough is firm enough to roll, 2 to 4 hours.
6. **Set up the sugars:** Heat the oven to 350°F and line two baking sheets with parchment. Put the rolling sugar in one shallow bowl and the powdered sugar in another.
7. **Roll the cookies:** Scoop tablespoon-size pieces of dough and roll them into balls. Roll each ball in granulated sugar, then generously in powdered sugar. Set them 2 inches apart on the sheets.
8. **Bake the cookies:** Bake until the tops have cracked and the edges feel firm, 10 to 12 minutes. Keep the rest of the dough in the fridge between trays.
9. **Cool the cookies:** Let the cookies cool on the sheet for 5 minutes, then move them to a rack.
