## Chef's Note

This is the Pioneer Woman's chocolate cream pie: a cornstarch pudding made rich with egg yolks and a full bar of bittersweet chocolate, chilled in a flaky crust. Bring the filling to a real bubble before the chocolate goes in, and it sets up silky and firm enough to slice.

## Directions

1. **Mix the sugar and starch:** Whisk the sugar, cornstarch and salt together in a medium saucepan.
2. **Whisk in the milk:** Slowly whisk in the milk and egg yolks until smooth.
3. **Cook the filling:** Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly and scraping the bottom, until the filling bubbles and turns thick and glossy like pudding, 6 to 8 minutes. Let it bubble for 1 minute more so the cornstarch thickens fully.
4. **Melt in the chocolate:** Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the chocolate, butter and vanilla until the chocolate melts and the filling is smooth.
5. **Fill the crust:** Pour the hot filling into the crust. Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface so no skin forms.
6. **Chill the pie:** Refrigerate until cold and set, at least 4 hours.
7. **Top and serve:** Top with whipped cream and shaved chocolate.
