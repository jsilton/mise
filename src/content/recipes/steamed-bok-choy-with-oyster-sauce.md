## Chef's Note

A Cantonese restaurant classic. The bok choy sears for a minute, then steams under a lid so the thick stems turn tender while the leaves just wilt. A quick garlic and oyster sauce glaze and a few drops of sesame oil finish it.

## Directions

1. **Mix the sauce:** Stir the oyster sauce, soy sauce and the water for the sauce together in a small bowl.
2. **Steam the first batch:** Heat a third of the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Lay in half the bok choy cut side down and cook until spotted golden, about 1 minute. Add half the steaming water, cover and cook until a knife slides into the thickest stem with a little resistance, 2 to 3 minutes. Move to a plate.
3. **Steam the second batch:** Repeat with another third of the oil, the rest of the bok choy and the rest of the steaming water.
4. **Make the garlic sauce:** Pour off any liquid left in the pan and lower the heat to medium. Add the last of the oil and the garlic and stir until fragrant but still pale, 20 to 30 seconds. Pour in the sauce and let it bubble.
5. **Toss and serve:** Return the bok choy and turn it in the sauce for 30 seconds. Take the pan off the heat, drizzle with the sesame oil and top with fried shallots.
