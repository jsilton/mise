## Chef's Note

Grandmommy's dressing combines unsweetened cornbread, white bread and poultry seasoning for a soft, spoonable center and browned edges. Cook the vegetables until tender before mixing, then add stock gradually until the bread is moist without a pool of liquid.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the cornbread:** Heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C. Grease an 8-inch square metal pan with one teaspoon of the pan butter. Whisk the self-rising cornmeal mix and flour in one bowl. In another, whisk the milk, eggs and just-warm melted butter. Fold wet into dry only until no dry flour remains. Do not taste the raw batter. Spread in the pan and bake promptly.
2. **Bake and cool:** Bake about 30–40 minutes, checking at 30 minutes, until golden and a tester inserted in the center comes out with crumbs but no wet batter. Cool in the pan on a rack for 15 minutes, then turn out carefully and cool another 30–45 minutes, until no warmth remains in the middle. A compact crumb is acceptable for this dressing; a gummy, underbaked center is not.
3. **Dry the white bread during cooling:** Reduce the oven to 300°F / 150°C. Tear the white bread into small pieces and spread in one layer on a rimmed sheet. Dry about 15–25 minutes, turning once, until a cooled piece feels dry through the center, with little browning. Already dry bread may need less time. Cool on the sheet while the vegetables cook; this cooling overlaps the cornbread cooling period.
4. **Tenderize the vegetables:** Put onion, celery and half a cup of the measured stock in a covered skillet. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover and cook about 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the celery is tender and the onion has no raw bite. If the pan dries before the vegetables soften, add a spoonful of stock from the measured supply. Keep any remaining pan liquid; it counts toward the dressing's total stock.
5. **Crumble, season and hydrate:** Heat the oven to 350°F / 177°C and grease a roughly 3-quart, 9-by-13-inch dish with the remaining teaspoon of butter. Crumble the entire cooled cornbread into coarse crumbs in a very large bowl; add the dried white bread, cooked vegetables and their liquid. Fold in two teaspoons of poultry seasoning, the pepper and half a teaspoon of salt. Fold in further stock in half-cup additions, allowing each addition to absorb before deciding on the next. Once evenly damp, wait five minutes, then add more stock a quarter-cup at a time only if dry centers remain. Stop when a spoonful forms a loose, moist mound without liquid collecting beneath it; the full reserve is not compulsory. Stock used for the vegetables comes from this same measured supply. Taste a cooled spoonful now that the eggs and flour are cooked; add the remaining seasoning or salt only if needed.
6. **Bake immediately:** Spread loosely in the prepared dish without pressing it dense. Bake uncovered about 50–60 minutes, until the center reaches at least 165°F / 74°C and the surface and edges are golden. Check around 40 minutes; if the top browns too quickly, cover loosely with foil and finish to the measured center temperature.
7. **Rest and serve:** Rest 10 minutes and spoon into twelve side portions. Serve with turkey and tart cranberry sauce; the cranberry supplies brightness without changing the family dressing's flavor.
