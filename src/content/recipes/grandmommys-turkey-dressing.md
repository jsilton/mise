## Chef's Note

This is Grandmommy's dressing for the turkey. The onion, celery and seasoning go straight into the cornbread batter, so the flavor is baked into every crumb. Then the cornbread is crumbled with white bread and soaked in broth, which bakes up soft and spoonable with browned edges.

## Directions

1. **Mix the cornbread:** Heat the oven to 400°F and grease an 8-inch square pan. Stir together the cornmeal, flour, milk, melted margarine, eggs, celery, onion, salt, pepper and poultry seasoning until combined.
2. **Bake the cornbread:** Pour the batter into the pan. Bake until golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean, about 40 minutes. Let it cool enough to handle.
3. **Combine the breads:** Turn the oven down to 350°F and grease a 9-by-13-inch baking dish. Crumble the cornbread and the white bread together in the dish.
4. **Add the broth:** Pour the broth over the bread until it just reaches the top of the crumbs. Stop before it pools, so the dressing is moist but not soupy.
5. **Bake the dressing:** Bake until golden on top and set, 50 to 60 minutes. It may take a little longer.

## Suggested Tweak

This is a family recipe, so it stays as written. If you want to try it:

- If you use unsalted stock in place of the canned broth, season the cornbread batter with 1 1/2 tsp fine salt. Taste the soaked dressing before baking and add up to 3/4 tsp more. Canned broth is already salted, so season more lightly with it.
- Use unsalted butter in place of the margarine.
- Tear the white bread and dry it on a baking sheet at 300°F for 15 to 20 minutes before mixing. Dry bread soaks up the broth without turning gummy.
