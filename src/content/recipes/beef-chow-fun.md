## Chef's Note

Beef chow fun is a Cantonese classic of wide rice noodles fried with tender beef, scallions and bean sprouts. A pinch of baking soda in the marinade keeps the flank steak silky. Let the noodles sit against the hot pan so they toast in spots, and fold them gently so the ribbons stay whole.

## Directions

1. **Coat the beef:** In a bowl, stir together the soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, water, cornstarch and baking soda for the beef. Add the steak and mix until evenly coated, then mix in the oil for the beef. Refrigerate for 20 minutes.
2. **Loosen the noodles:** If the noodles are cold and stiff, warm them on a covered plate in the microwave in 20- to 30-second bursts until they peel apart without tearing. Separate them into ribbons.
3. **Mix the sauce:** Whisk the sauce ingredients in a small bowl.
4. **Sear the beef:** Heat a wok over high heat until it smokes lightly. Add a little of the oil and spread the beef in a single layer. Sear until browned, about 1 minute per side, then move it to a plate.
5. **Fry the aromatics:** Add a little more oil to the wok with the ginger and scallion whites. Stir until fragrant, about 20 seconds.
6. **Fry the noodles:** Add the rest of the oil and the noodles and spread them out. Let them sit until toasted underneath, about 1 minute, then lift and turn them gently. Repeat until hot and spotted brown, 2 to 3 minutes. Add a splash of water if they stick or stay firm. In a 12-inch skillet, fry the noodles in two batches, then return them all to the pan.
7. **Season and toss:** Pour in the sauce and return the beef and its juices. Fold gently from the bottom until the noodles are evenly colored, about 1 minute.
8. **Finish and serve:** Add the bean sprouts and scallion greens. Toss until the sprouts just wilt, about 30 seconds, and serve right away.
