## Chef's Note

Crisp-skinned salmon sits on warm orzo with tomatoes, feta and fresh herbs, with smoky roasted chickpeas scattered over the top. The sauce is a lemon, olive oil and oregano dressing shaken in a jar until it turns cloudy. Shake it again right before you spoon it on.

## Directions

1. **Roast the chickpeas:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Toss the chickpeas with their olive oil, smoked paprika and a pinch of salt and pepper and spread them on a rimmed baking sheet. Roast, shaking the pan halfway through, until browned and crisp outside, 25 to 30 minutes.
2. **Shake the dressing:** While the chickpeas roast, put the olive oil, garlic, honey, oregano and half the lemon juice in a jar and shake until cloudy. Taste and add more lemon juice, salt and pepper until it's bright and tangy.
3. **Cook the orzo:** Boil the orzo in well-salted water until al dente, 8 to 10 minutes, then drain. Toss it with its olive oil, the tomatoes, feta, parsley and mint and cover to keep warm.
4. **Sear the skin:** When the chickpeas have about 10 minutes left, pat the salmon skin dry. Season the flesh with the lemon zest, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high until it shimmers, using two skillets if the fillets would crowd one. Lay the salmon in skin side down, press gently for a few seconds and cook without moving until the skin is crisp and releases easily, 4 to 5 minutes.
5. **Finish the salmon:** Flip the fillets and cook until the center is just opaque, 2 to 4 minutes, depending on thickness.
6. **Plate and serve:** Divide the orzo among plates, scatter the chickpeas over it and set the salmon on top, skin up. Shake the dressing again and spoon it over the fish and orzo.
