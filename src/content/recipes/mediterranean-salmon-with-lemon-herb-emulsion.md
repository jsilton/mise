## Chef's Note

This lemon-herb emulsion is a shaken olive-oil dressing: it turns cloudy as oil disperses into lemon juice, then separates again as it stands. Shake just before serving. Add roasted chickpeas at the last moment so the moist orzo does not immediately soften their crisp edges.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Heat the oven to **400°F / 205°C**. Drain, rinse and thoroughly dry the chickpeas. Zest the lemon, then juice it. Halve tomatoes, chop herbs and measure the separate oil amounts. Pat salmon skin dry, remove pin bones and refrigerate while starting the sides.
2. **Roast chickpeas:** Toss chickpeas with **their allotted oil, half the smoked paprika**, salt and pepper. Spread in one layer on a rimmed tray. Roast about **25–35 minutes**, shaking at about 15 minutes, until lightly crisp and beginning to brown. Begin checking at 20 minutes; do not insist that the centers turn hard.
3. **Mix dressing and cook orzo:** Shake **the dressing oil**, garlic, honey, oregano and **half the lemon juice** in a closed jar. Taste, then add more of the remaining lemon juice, salt and pepper as needed. Meanwhile cook orzo in boiling salted water according to its package until tender with a little bite. Drain, then toss with **the orzo oil**, tomatoes, feta, parsley and mint. Cover briefly to keep warm while the fish cooks.
4. **Heat the skillet:** When chickpeas are about 10 minutes from ready, season salmon with lemon zest, **the remaining smoked paprika**, salt and pepper. Heat **the salmon oil** in a broad skillet over medium-high until shimmering, not smoking heavily. If portions cannot fit with space between them, use two skillets with the oil divided between them, or cook in batches, reserving a proportional share of the searing oil for each batch.
5. **Sear and finish:** Lay salmon skin-side down, away from you. Press gently with a spatula for the first few seconds to keep skin in contact. Cook about **4–6 minutes** until the skin browns and releases; reduce heat if spices darken rapidly. Turn carefully and cook about **2–6 minutes** more, depending on thickness. Check the thickest center from the side for **145°F / 63°C**. If the outside is done before the center, lower the heat and continue gently. Remove each portion when ready.
6. **Assemble:** Divide the herbed orzo among plates, scatter over roasted chickpeas and add salmon with its skin facing up. Shake the dressing again and spoon around the fish and over the orzo, keeping the skin exposed. Serve immediately. The pasta, legumes and tomatoes are already part of this dish; a separate grain is unnecessary.
