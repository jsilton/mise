## Chef's Note

A quick Cantonese-style stir-fry of sweet shrimp and crisp snow peas in a light, glossy sauce. A five-minute soak in salted water seasons the shrimp and keeps them plump and snappy. Plenty of garlic and ginger go in after the peas, so they stay fragrant instead of scorching.

## Directions

1. **Brine the shrimp:** Stir the brining salt into the cold water until dissolved. Add the shrimp and refrigerate for 5 minutes. Drain, rinse briefly and pat very dry.
2. **Mix the sauce:** Whisk the broth, Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, cornstarch, sugar and white pepper together in a small bowl.
3. **Sear the shrimp:** Heat half the oil in a 12-inch skillet or wok over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Add the shrimp in one layer and leave them for 1 minute. Turn and cook until pink and opaque in the center, about 1 minute more. Move to a plate.
4. **Cook the snow peas:** Add the rest of the oil, the snow peas and their salt. Stir-fry until the peas are bright green and tender with a little bite, about 2 minutes. Add the garlic and ginger and stir until fragrant, 20 to 30 seconds.
5. **Thicken and serve:** Stir the sauce again and pour it in. Simmer, stirring, until it turns glossy and lightly thick, 30 to 60 seconds. Return the shrimp and their juices and toss to coat. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the scallion and sesame oil and serve.
