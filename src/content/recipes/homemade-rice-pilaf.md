## Chef's Note

Toasted orzo gives this pilaf a nutty aroma and a texture slightly different from the rice. The rice and pasta share the broth, so the liquid has to hydrate both. A tight lid controls evaporation; tasting and a covered rest are more useful than treating one cooking time as universal.

## Directions

1. **Rinse rice:** Rinse the rice in several changes of cool water, then drain thoroughly in a fine-mesh strainer. Measure the other ingredients.
2. **Toast orzo:** Melt butter in a 2–3 quart saucepan over medium heat. Add dry orzo and stir frequently for 3–4 minutes until golden brown. Add drained rice and stir for 1 minute.
3. **Add seasonings and broth:** Stir in garlic powder, onion powder, optional turmeric, salt, and pepper for about 15 seconds. Pour in broth carefully and bring to a gentle boil.
4. **Simmer:** Stir once, cover tightly, and reduce heat to maintain a gentle simmer. Cook about 15–18 minutes. Check that the liquid is absorbed and taste a grain. If it remains hard and dry, add the measured hot water and cook covered a little longer.
5. **Rest and finish:** Once grains are nearly tender, turn off heat and rest covered for 10 minutes. Fluff gently and fold in dried parsley and optional lemon juice. Taste before adding any further seasoning.
