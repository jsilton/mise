## Chef's Note

A buttery side in the style of Middle Eastern rice with toasted noodles, made here with orzo. Toasting the orzo in butter until golden gives it a nutty flavor, and a tight lid and a 10-minute rest leave every grain tender and separate.

## Directions

1. **Toast the orzo:** Melt the butter in a 2- to 3-quart saucepan over medium heat. Add the orzo and stir often until golden brown, 3 to 4 minutes.
2. **Toast the rice:** Add the rice and stir until coated in butter and translucent at the edges, about 1 minute.
3. **Bloom the spices:** Stir in the garlic powder, onion powder, turmeric, salt and pepper until fragrant, about 15 seconds.
4. **Simmer:** Pour in the broth and bring to a boil over high heat. Stir once, cover tightly and lower the heat to a gentle simmer. Cook until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is nearly tender, 15 to 18 minutes. If the pan is dry and the rice is still hard, add the hot water, cover and cook 3 to 5 minutes more.
5. **Rest:** Take the pan off the heat and let it sit, covered, for 10 minutes.
6. **Fluff and serve:** Fluff with a fork and fold in the parsley and lemon juice.
