## Chef's Note

Italian-American cooks folded tomato into shrimp scampi and made it a red-sauce staple. The move that makes this one is a quick stock from the shrimp shells, which carries most of the shrimp flavor into the sauce. Grated fresh tomatoes keep it bright, and cold butter at the end makes it glossy.

## Directions

1. **Brine the shrimp:** Toss the peeled shrimp with the salt and the baking soda, if using. Refrigerate while you make the stock, 15 to 30 minutes.
2. **Make the shell stock:** Heat the stock oil in a small saucepan over medium-high heat. Cook the shells, stirring, until pink and fragrant, 2 to 3 minutes. Add a splash of the wine and the water and simmer for 15 minutes. Strain, pressing hard on the shells, and boil the stock down to about half the water you started with.
3. **Toast the crumbs:** If making the topping, warm its oil in a 12-inch deep skillet over medium-low heat. Toast the panko, stirring, until golden, 2 to 3 minutes. Add the grated garlic and a pinch of salt and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Tip into a bowl.
4. **Sear the shrimp:** Heat the shrimp oil in the skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Pat the shrimp dry and sear them in one layer until just opaque and curled into a loose C, about 1 minute per side. Move them to a plate. They finish in the sauce.
5. **Soften the shallots:** Lower the heat to medium. Add the shallot oil, the shallot butter and the shallots. Cook until soft but not browned, 4 to 5 minutes. Add the garlic and red pepper and stir until fragrant, about 45 seconds.
6. **Simmer the sauce:** Pour in the rest of the wine, scrape up the browned bits and simmer until reduced by half, 2 to 3 minutes. Add the tomatoes with their juices and the shell stock. Simmer gently until glossy and lightly thickened, 10 to 15 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
7. **Boil the pasta:** While the sauce simmers, bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. When the sauce is ready, cook the linguine until just short of tender, about 2 minutes or as the package says. Scoop out a mug of the pasta water, then drain.
8. **Toss the pasta:** Stir the spinach into the sauce until it wilts, about 1 minute. Add the pasta and a splash of pasta water and toss over medium heat until the pasta is tender and coated, about 1 minute.
9. **Finish with butter:** Take the pan off the heat. Swirl in the cold butter a piece at a time until the sauce turns creamy and clings to the noodles. Return the shrimp and their juices and toss until heated through, 30 to 60 seconds.
10. **Season and serve:** Stir in the lemon zest, the lemon juice and most of the basil. Taste for salt and heat. Serve in warm bowls with the rest of the basil, a drizzle of olive oil and the crumbs.
