## Chef's Note

This site's New York Crunch Roll combines tuna, avocado, cucumber and tempura flakes with nori outside. The name does not establish one universal restaurant formula or a New York origin. Prepared tenkasu adds a delicate crunch, but it will soften against the filling, so roll and eat promptly.

## Before buying raw tuna

Tell the supplier the tuna is for raw service and confirm species, handling and applicable parasite controls; “sushi-grade” alone is insufficient. Yellowfin has a specific FDA parasite-freezing exemption, not a guarantee against other hazards. Neither domestic freezing nor the vinegar used for dampening fingers makes raw tuna safe. Thaw frozen fish in the refrigerator as directed, removing vacuum packaging before refrigerator thawing.

FDA advises children, pregnant people, older adults and people with weakened immune systems to avoid raw seafood. Choose a fully cooked preparation for those diners. Check tenkasu ingredients for wheat, egg, fish or shellfish; the roll itself contains tuna, and packaged ingredients vary by brand.

## Directions

1. **Prepare rice and equipment:** Make [Sushi Rice (Shari)](/mise/recipes/master-sushi-rice), weigh out 320 g and refrigerate surplus rice promptly. Let the rolling portion become barely warm during final preparation. Wrap a clean mat in food-safe film. Mix water and vinegar for lightly dampening fingers and the knife; it is not a sanitizer. Do not dip a raw-filling-coated hand or blade into this clean water. Wash hands and clean the blade before returning to it, use a separate damp cloth for wiping the cutting blade, and replace the water if contaminated.
2. **Prepare vegetables:** Wash the cucumber, remove the watery core and weigh 100 g; cut into slender matchsticks and pat dry. Near rolling time, halve the avocado, lift its pit out with a spoon, scoop out the flesh and weigh 150 g. Slice about 5–6 mm thick. Divide cucumber into four 25 g portions and avocado into four equal portions, about 38 g each. Refrigerate briefly until needed. Keep tempura flakes sealed and dry.
3. **Cut tuna and portion rice:** With separate clean equipment, remove tough visible connective tissue, weigh the listed usable amount of cold tuna and cut across its muscle grain where visible into strips about 6–10 mm thick. Short strips can be laid end to end. Divide into four equal portions of about 58 g and refrigerate. Clean the work area and hands. Divide rice into four 80 g portions using a clean utensil.
4. **Lay out the first roll:** Trim the first full nori sheet to about 19 x 15 cm and save its dry offcut airtight for another use. Leave the other sheets untrimmed until the first closure check. Place one rectangle shiny side down with its 19 cm edge nearest you. Lightly dampen fingers and shake off extra water, then spread one rice portion evenly, leaving a 1 cm bare strip along the far edge. Take out one portion each of tuna, avocado and cucumber and arrange in a compact horizontal line about 4 cm from the near edge.
5. **Add crunch and roll:** Sprinkle one quarter of the tempura flakes, about 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp, along the filling immediately before rolling. Lift the mat's near edge and tuck nori and rice over the filling. Lift the mat clear as you continue forward so it stays outside the roll. Seal on the bare nori edge, barely moistening it if necessary, then press gently through the mat without crushing the avocado. Check the first roll's closure, then cut and serve it following the next step. Trim the remaining sheets to match and continue one roll at a time, cutting and serving each as it is completed for the clearest crunch. Do not build a rice ridge.
6. **Cut and serve:** Set seam-side down briefly while preparing a sharp knife. Wipe and lightly dampen the blade, cut each roll in half, then halve each section twice for eight pieces per roll. Wipe and dampen between cuts as needed. Serve promptly as 32 pieces, about two main portions or four appetizer portions. Keep unused fish portions cold while working.
