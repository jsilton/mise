## Chef's Note

The crunch roll is a Japanese-American sushi-bar favorite. Tuna, avocado and cucumber get a sprinkle of tempura flakes right before rolling, so every bite has a little crackle. The flakes soften fast, so roll, cut and eat one roll at a time.

## Directions

1. **Set up:** Wrap the bamboo mat in plastic wrap. Stir the rice vinegar into the water in a small bowl for dampening your hands and knife.
2. **Portion the fillings:** Divide the tuna, avocado, cucumber and rice into four portions. Keep the tuna in the fridge until you roll.
3. **Spread the rice:** Lay a nori sheet on the mat, shiny side down, with a long edge toward you. With damp fingers, spread one portion of rice evenly over it, leaving a 1/2-inch strip bare along the far edge.
4. **Add the filling:** Lay one portion each of tuna, avocado and cucumber in a line about 1 1/2 inches from the near edge. Sprinkle a quarter of the tempura flakes along the filling.
5. **Roll:** Lift the near edge of the mat and tuck the nori over the filling. Roll forward, keeping the mat on the outside, and seal the roll on the bare edge. Press gently through the mat without crushing the avocado.
6. **Cut and serve:** Wipe the knife with a damp cloth. Cut the roll in half, then cut each half into four pieces, wiping the blade between cuts. Serve right away and make the next roll.
