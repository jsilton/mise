## Chef's Note

This pulled pork uses a bone-in Boston butt, hickory-smoked salt, smoky-sweet rub
and a simple vinegar–Worcestershire pot base. The
clock gets the shoulder close, but the finished meat should meet both a safe
temperature and the fork-pullable texture that makes it ready to shred. Decant
the cooking liquid before returning any of it so the meat stays moist rather
than soupy.

## Directions

1. **Mix and apply the rub:** Start with a fully thawed pork shoulder. Mix the brown sugar, hickory smoked salt, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin and pepper. Rub it over every surface of the pork. For an optional overnight seasoning, put the pork in a covered container and refrigerate at 40°F / 4°C or colder; this time is not included in the recipe total.
2. **Build the pot base:** Scatter the onion and garlic across the bottom of a 6-quart slow cooker. Pour in the apple cider vinegar and Worcestershire sauce.
3. **Load the shoulder:** Set the rubbed shoulder, fat cap up, on the aromatics. The lid must close fully. Do not add extra liquid; the pork releases juices as it cooks.
4. **Cook until safe and pullable:** Cover and cook on LOW for about 10–11 hours. Check the thickest pork away from the bone with an instant-read thermometer; it must reach at least 145°F / 63°C. It is ready to pull only when a fork turns and separates the meat easily and the bone slides out cleanly. If it is safe but still firm, cover and continue in 30-minute intervals until tender.
5. **Pull the pork:** Transfer the shoulder to a cutting board and rest for at least 3 minutes before pulling. Remove the bone and discard large pieces of fat or gristle. Pull or chop the pork into rough pieces.
6. **Decant, skim and moisten:** Pour the cooking liquid into a heatproof jug and skim off excess fat. Return the pork to the slow cooker. Add the defatted liquid a little at a time, tossing after each addition, until the meat is moist and flavorful rather than submerged.
7. **Serve or store promptly:** Serve with cornbread and coleslaw, or use the pork in tacos, bowls or sandwiches. For later meals, portion and chill it promptly using the storage guidance.
