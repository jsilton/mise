  - 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  - 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce

  - 1/2 tsp salt

## Chef's Note

Spinach Madeleine first appeared in River Road Recipes, the Junior League of Baton Rouge cookbook, and became a Southern holiday standard. The sauce is made with the spinach's own cooking liquid and evaporated milk, which keeps it rich without cream. It holds well assembled a day ahead, which frees the stove on a busy holiday.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. **Cook the spinach:** Cook the spinach in a covered saucepan over medium heat, breaking it up, until thawed and steaming, about 8 minutes.
3. **Drain the spinach:** Drain the spinach in a strainer set over a bowl and press hard to save the liquid. Measure the liquid, adding water if you are short.
4. **Melt the butter:** Melt the butter in the empty saucepan over low heat.
5. **Soften the onion:** Add the onion and cook until soft but not browned, about 3 minutes.
6. **Stir in the flour:** Stir in the flour until smooth and cook for 1 minute.
7. **Thicken the sauce:** Slowly whisk in the reserved spinach cooking liquid, evaporated milk, and Worcestershire sauce. Cook, whisking, until smooth and thick enough to coat a spoon, about 3 minutes.
8. **Melt the cheese:** Add the crushed red pepper, garlic salt, celery salt, salt, black pepper, and pepper Jack cheese and stir over low heat until the cheese melts and the sauce is smooth, about 3 minutes.
9. **Fill the dish:** Stir in the drained spinach and pour it into a 1 1/2-quart baking dish.
10. **Top the dish:** Toss the panko and melted butter in a small bowl and scatter over the top.
11. **Bake until bubbly:** Bake until hot through and bubbling at the edges, 15 to 20 minutes.
