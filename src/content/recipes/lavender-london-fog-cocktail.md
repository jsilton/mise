## Chef's Note

This is a London Fog latte turned into a cold gin cocktail. Earl Grey and vanilla carry it, oat milk makes it light and dairy-free, and a touch of lavender sits behind the bergamot. Chill the tea and syrup well so the drink pours cold and lightly frothy.

## Directions

1. **Brew the tea:** Steep the tea in the hot water for 4 minutes. Strain it through a fine-mesh strainer, then top it up with water to the volume you started with.
2. **Make the syrup:** Stir the sugar into the hot water until it dissolves. Add the lavender, steep for 3 minutes, then strain finely and stir in the vanilla.
3. **Chill:** Refrigerate the tea and syrup in separate covered containers until cold, about 1 hour. Chill a large coupe glass in the freezer.
4. **Shake:** Pour the gin, tea concentrate, oat milk and syrup into a cocktail shaker. Fill it with ice and shake hard until the shaker is frosty, 10 to 12 seconds.
5. **Strain and serve:** Double strain through the shaker's strainer and a fine-mesh strainer into the chilled coupe. Serve right away, with no ice or garnish.

## Suggested Tweak

For a lavender note you can really taste, use 1/2 tsp dried culinary lavender in the syrup and steep it 5 minutes. Taste, and steep up to 10 minutes if you want more. Longer can turn soapy.
