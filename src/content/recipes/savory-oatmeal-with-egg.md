## Chef's Note

Oatmeal doesn't have to be sweet. Cooked in broth and topped with a runny fried egg, scallions and soy sauce, it eats more like a bowl of congee than breakfast cereal. A few drops of sesame oil at the end tie it all together.

## Directions

1. **Cook the oats:** Bring the broth and salt to a boil in a saucepan. Stir in the oats, lower the heat to medium-low and cook, stirring now and then, until creamy and tender, about 5 minutes.
2. **Fry the eggs:** While the oats cook, heat the olive oil in a small nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Crack in the eggs and cook until the whites are set with lacy, crisp edges and the yolks are still runny, 2 to 3 minutes. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.
3. **Finish the oats:** Take the oats off the heat and stir in the butter, soy sauce and sesame oil until glossy.
4. **Serve:** Divide the oats between two bowls. Top each with a fried egg, the scallions and a pinch of red pepper flakes, plus any toppings you like. Break the yolk into the oats as you eat.
