## Chef's Note

These are French-style scrambled eggs, cooked low and slow until the curds are so small and soft they eat like a savory custard. The secret is gentle heat and constant stirring. Take them off the heat while they still look a little wet, because the hot eggs keep cooking on the way to the plate.

## Directions

1. **Whisk the eggs:** Whisk the eggs and salt in a bowl until no streaks of white remain.
2. **Cook the eggs:** Melt the butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat until it foams without browning. Add the eggs and stir constantly with a flexible spatula, sweeping the bottom and sides of the pan, until small, soft curds form and the eggs are mostly set but still glossy, 5 to 7 minutes. If the curds form fast or stick, lift the pan off the heat for a few seconds as you stir.
3. **Finish off the heat:** Take the pan off the heat and stir in the crème fraîche and a grind of pepper. The eggs finish setting in a few seconds.
4. **Serve right away:** Spoon onto warm plates right away. Taste for salt and scatter the chives over the top.
