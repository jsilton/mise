## Chef's Note

The everyday rice of Japanese and Korean tables: soft, a little sticky and just right for chopsticks. Rinsing clears the loose surface starch, a half-hour soak hydrates the grains so the centers cook through, and a 10-minute rest off the heat finishes them.

## Directions

1. **Rinse the rice:** Put the rice in a bowl, cover with cold water, swish and drain. Repeat until the water is only a little cloudy, then drain well in a strainer.
2. **Soak:** Put the rice and the cooking water in a heavy 2-quart saucepan and let it soak for 30 minutes.
3. **Bring to a boil:** Set the pan over medium heat with the lid slightly ajar. As soon as the water bubbles steadily, close the lid and turn the heat to low.
4. **Simmer:** Cook on the lowest heat that keeps a gentle simmer, without lifting the lid, until the water is absorbed, 12 to 15 minutes.
5. **Rest and fluff:** Take the pan off the heat and leave it covered for 10 minutes. Fold the rice gently with a paddle, reaching the bottom without mashing it.
