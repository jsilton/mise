## Chef's Note

This is a winter salad from southern Italy, where fennel and oranges are in season together. Crisp, anise-sweet fennel meets juicy citrus, with buttery olives and Parmesan for salt. Shave the fennel as thin as you can and dress it right before serving, since it wilts once the juice hits it.

## Directions

1. **Shave the fennel:** Halve the bulbs lengthwise and cut out the hard core. Shave them paper-thin on a mandoline or with a sharp knife. Drop the slices into a bowl of ice water to crisp for 5 to 10 minutes.
2. **Segment the citrus:** While the fennel crisps, cut the top and bottom off each orange and the grapefruit. Stand each on a cut end and slice away the peel and white pith. Working over a bowl, cut between the membranes to free the segments, then squeeze the membranes to catch the juice.
3. **Make the dressing:** Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, saved citrus juice, honey, salt and pepper in a small bowl.
4. **Assemble and serve:** Drain the fennel and spin or pat it dry. Spread it on a platter, arrange the citrus over it and scatter on the olives. Drizzle with the dressing, then top with the Parmigiano-Reggiano and torn fennel fronds. Serve right away.
