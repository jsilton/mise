## Chef's Note

The dates supply the sweetness, with the full butter, egg and Greek yogurt forming the wet mixture. Blend that mixture evenly, then stir it into the two flours and quick oats. Freezing is a make-ahead route; the actual cold texture depends on the dates and how the cookies were baked.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Have butter or baking spray and hot water (if using) ready: lightly grease cookie sheets with the fat or spray. The optional hot water is only for the other-date option; pit and measure the full listed date volume first, soak those dates until soft, then drain before making the paste. Follow the food processor’s actual loading and temperature instructions, and let the melted butter become only mildly warm before it will meet the egg.
2. **Make date paste:** Process Medjool dates and unsalted butter until a smooth, thick paste forms, stopping and scraping as needed. Work within the processor’s capacity; split a larger batch proportionally into complete dough batches rather than overfilling it.
3. **Mix wet ingredients:** Add large egg, plain Greek yogurt, and vanilla extract and process until evenly mixed, stopping to scrape as needed. Stop when the mixture is uniform, without unmixed egg or yogurt.
4. **Mix dry ingredients:** Whisk buckwheat flour, white whole wheat flour, quick oats, baking soda, ground cinnamon, and sea salt together in a mixing bowl, including the pinch of salt.
5. **Combine:** Stir the date mixture into the dry ingredients until a thick, sticky dough forms and no dry flour remains.
6. **Shape:** Use all the dough in small balls, about 1 tbsp each, and press them into ½-inch-thick disks on the prepared sheets, leaving room between them. The listed cookie count is a planning value; tablespoon portions may give a different number. Keep portion size and thickness consistent and use more sheets and oven loads when scaling.
7. **Bake:** Wait for the oven to reach 350°F, then bake for 12–15 minutes per load until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are set, not wet dough. Keep remaining raw dough refrigerated at 40°F / 4°C or below between loads.
8. **Cool and freeze:** Cool the baked cookies completely on a rack, then pack airtight in a freezer bag or container and label the date. Freeze at 0°F / −18°C or below; avoid trapping steam in the package. If staging cooled cookies before freezing, keep them refrigerated at 40°F / 4°C or below rather than leaving them out. They can be eaten cold from the freezer if comfortably chewable, or thawed covered in the refrigerator; cold texture varies with the dates and bake.

## Cooking Options

### Other dates

Other types of dates may replace the same listed volume of Medjool dates. Pit and measure the full listed date amount, then soak in hot water until soft and drain before processing; the soaking water is separate from the dough. Date size and moisture can change paste consistency and cookie texture, so use the same disk thickness and check that the baked centers are set. No soaking time or additional dough liquid is specified.
