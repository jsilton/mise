## Chef's Note

Greek yogurt and eggs make a thick, sticky dough with chopped spinach, chives and cooked ham. Fold the fillings and two thirds of the cheese through it so each biscuit gets a share, then use the remaining cheese on top. Browned cheese is a surface cue; check that the thick centers are cooked before cooling or packing the biscuits.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line a large baking sheet, or additional sheets as needed, with parchment. Use cooked ham, diced small. Wash and dry the spinach and chives, then chop them finely.

2. **Wet and dry:** Whisk all the yogurt and eggs in a large bowl. Separately whisk all the measured flour, flaxseed, garlic powder, red pepper flakes, baking powder and sea salt until distributed.

3. **Combine:** Add the dry mixture to the wet mixture gradually, stirring until the flour is moistened and a thick, sticky dough forms without dry pockets.

4. **Fold:** Fold in all the spinach, chives, cooked ham and two thirds of the measured cheddar. Distribute the fillings through the dough, stopping once combined; use your hands gently if needed. Keep the remaining one third of the cheese for the tops.

5. **Shape:** Lightly dust your hands with the separate flour allowance. Divide the whole dough evenly into the listed number of biscuits; the original batch plans twelve. Form disks about 1 inch thick with space between them on the prepared sheets. Use additional sheets or oven loads rather than crowding them or discarding extra dough.

6. **Bake:** Scatter all the remaining measured cheese over the biscuits. Load only once the oven has reached 400°F. Bake at 400°F for 5 minutes, then reduce the oven setting to 350°F without opening the door and bake about 20 minutes more. Keep the same temperature sequence for additional oven loads; do not multiply the heat settings or make thicker disks when scaling.

7. **Check and cool:** The bottoms should be golden and the cheese browned, with set centers and no wet flour or egg mixture. Check the center of a thick biscuit; for this cooked-ham-and-egg bake, use 165°F as the casserole-style endpoint. Continue baking as needed. Let stand briefly until firm enough to move, then serve warm or cool promptly before packing.
