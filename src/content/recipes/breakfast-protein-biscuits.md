## Chef's Note

These drop biscuits from Nicole Addison of Nourished by Nic use Greek yogurt and eggs in place of butter and cream, so they bake up tender and full of protein. Ham, spinach, chives and sharp cheddar make each one a breakfast on its own. A hot start at 400°F sets the rise before the oven drops to cook the thick centers through.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the pan:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Line a large baking sheet with parchment.
2. **Whisk the wet ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the yogurt and eggs until smooth.
3. **Mix the dry ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk the flour, flaxseed, baking powder, salt, garlic powder and red pepper flakes.
4. **Make the dough:** Add the dry ingredients to the yogurt mixture a little at a time, stirring until a thick, sticky dough forms with no dry flour.
5. **Fold in the fillings:** Fold in the spinach, chives, ham and two thirds of the cheddar until spread evenly through the dough. Use your hands if you need to, but don't overwork it.
6. **Shape:** Flour your hands lightly. Divide the dough into 12 mounds and pat each into a 1-inch-thick disk on the baking sheet, leaving space between them. Scatter the rest of the cheddar over the tops.
7. **Bake:** Bake at 400°F for 5 minutes, then lower the oven to 350°F without opening the door. Bake until the bottoms are golden, the cheese is bubbly and crisp and the centers are set, about 20 minutes more.
