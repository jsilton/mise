## Chef's Note

Good guacamole is about texture. Mash only half the avocado into a creamy base and fold the rest in as rough chunks. Grinding the chile and a little onion with the salt in a molcajete spreads their flavor through every bite.

## Directions

1. **Make the chile paste:** In a molcajete, grind the salt, jalapeño and a spoonful of the onion into a coarse paste. Without a molcajete, mash them together in a bowl with a fork.
2. **Mash the avocado:** Halve and pit the avocados and scoop the flesh into the bowl. Add the lime juice and the cumin, if using. Mash about half the avocado into a creamy base, leaving the rest in rough chunks.
3. **Finish and serve:** Fold in the rest of the onion, the cilantro and the tomato, if using. Taste and add more salt or lime if it needs it. Serve right away with corn chips.
