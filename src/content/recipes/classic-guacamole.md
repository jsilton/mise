## Chef's Note

Mash part of the ripe avocado into a creamy base and fold in the rest as chunks. Use the measured salt once, then taste the finished mixture before deciding whether it needs more. Lime adds flavor and surface contact wrap limits air exposure, but neither keeps guacamole permanently green. For a mild version, reduce or omit the jalapeño.

## Directions

1. **Prepare aromatics:** If using a molcajete, grind the measured salt, jalapeño and one-eighth of the diced onion into a paste (1 tbsp onion at the original batch). Otherwise, finely mince the jalapeño and mix it with that same onion share and all the measured salt. Reserve the rest of the onion.

2. **Add avocado:** Halve the washed avocados, remove pits and scoop the flesh into a bowl. Add the aromatic mixture, measured lime juice and cumin. Do not add the measured salt a second time.

3. **Mash:** With a fork, mash about half the avocado into a creamy base, leaving the rest as rough chunks. Fold gently to combine rather than pureeing all the flesh.

4. **Finish:** Fold in the reserved onion, cilantro and optional seeded tomato. Taste after mixing; adjust only if needed, remembering that all the measured salt is already included.

5. **Serve or chill:** Serve with tortilla chips if using, ideally within 1 hour for color and texture. If waiting, cover directly against the surface and refrigerate promptly. Set out small dipping portions rather than keeping the whole batch on the table.

## Variations

- **Spicy:** Add 1 serrano pepper for every 4 avocados in the base amount, or leave jalapeño seeds in.
- **Smoky:** Add 1 minced canned chipotle pepper for every 4 avocados in the base amount.
- **Creamy:** Fold in 2 tbsp sour cream for every 4 avocados in the base amount. This variation contains dairy.
- **Chunky:** Use one-quarter more avocado than the base amount (5 instead of 4 at the full batch) and mash less.
