## Chef's Note

Oven sweet potato fries with crisp, caramelized edges and soft, creamy middles. Sweet potatoes hold more water than russets, so spread them across two pans with space around every fry so they roast instead of steaming. Smoked paprika adds a savory, wood-fired note against the sweetness.

## Directions

1. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Line two rimmed baking sheets with parchment.
2. **Season the fries:** In a large bowl, whisk the oil, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Add the sweet potatoes and toss until every stick is coated.
3. **Spread them out:** Divide the fries between the two pans in a single layer, with no fries touching.
4. **Roast and flip:** Roast until the bottoms start to brown, about 15 minutes. Flip with a metal spatula and swap the pans between racks.
5. **Finish roasting:** Roast until the centers are tender and the edges are dark brown and crisp, 10 to 12 minutes more. Serve right away, uncovered so they stay crisp.
