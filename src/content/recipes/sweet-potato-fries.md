## Chef's Note

Spread the sticks apart and turn when the bottoms brown. Oven-roasted sweet potatoes can have browned edges and tender centers without becoming uniformly crisp; the smoked paprika remains part of this version.

## Directions

1. **Preheat:** Preheat to 400°F. Prepare two rimmed lined sheets for the original batch; use more or fewer sheets as needed for uncrowded scaled batches.

2. **Cut:** Wash and peel the potatoes. Cut into roughly ½-inch-wide, 3-inch-long sticks; keep those dimensions when scaling.

3. **Season:** Toss with all the listed oil, garlic powder, smoked paprika, sea salt and black pepper until distributed.

4. **Arrange:** Spread in a single layer without touching. Divide all the potatoes and seasoning oil among pans.

5. **First roast:** Roast about 15 minutes, until the bottoms begin to brown, then turn with a spatula.

6. **Finish roast:** Begin checking after another 10–12 minutes; continue until centers are tender and edges browned. Rotate sheets if needed; do not wait for blackened edges or promise every stick will become crisp.

7. **Serve:** Serve promptly without trapping steam under a cover.
