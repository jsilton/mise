## Chef's Note

These are pancakes baked in a muffin tin, blended from banana, oats, cottage cheese and eggs. The cottage cheese makes them rich and soft with plenty of protein, so they hold a kid until lunch. Blend until completely smooth, then press the berries into the tops so every muffin gets its share.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the tin:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
2. **Blend the batter:** Blend the bananas, oats, cottage cheese, eggs, maple syrup and vanilla on high until completely smooth, with no oat bits or curds left, about 1 minute.
3. **Fill the tin:** Divide the batter among the cups. Press a few frozen blueberries into the top of each.
4. **Bake:** Bake until the muffins are puffed, golden and firm in the center, 25 to 30 minutes. A toothpick should come out with no wet batter.
5. **Cool:** Let the muffins rest in the tin for 5 to 10 minutes so they firm up, then lift them out.
