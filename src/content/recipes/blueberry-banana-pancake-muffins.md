## Chef's Note

Blend the bananas, oats, full-fat cottage cheese and eggs until the oats and curds are smooth, then add the blueberries to the individual wells. These muffins have a soft, dense interior, with richness from the full-fat cottage cheese. Give the cooked muffins time to settle in the tin before lifting them.

## Directions

1. **Prepare:** Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease enough standard-size muffin wells for the listed planning count with the separate oil or spray allowance; the original batch plans twelve. Use ripe bananas and keep the frozen blueberries separate.

2. **Blend:** Blend all the bananas, oats, cottage cheese, eggs, maple syrup and vanilla until the oat pieces and curds are smooth, following the blender’s load instructions. Stop safely to scrape if needed. For larger batches, blend proportional smaller loads and recombine them before portioning.

3. **Portion and add berries:** Divide the mixture evenly across the listed planning number of prepared wells, with space for it to rise. Distribute all the measured blueberries across their tops and press them gently into the surface. Use the full listed berry amount; the original twelve-muffin batch uses half a cup. Berry size varies, so a fixed count is only a guide. Use extra prepared wells if the actual tin cannot hold the full mixture.

4. **Bake:** Bake about 25–30 minutes, until the muffins feel firm and the center is set. A skewer should show no wet batter; check beside a berry. An egg-dish center should reach 160°F. Puffing or browning alone does not establish a cooked center.

5. **Cool:** Leave in the tin for 5–10 minutes before lifting gently to a rack. They may settle as they cool. Serve warm, or refrigerate promptly for cold service.
