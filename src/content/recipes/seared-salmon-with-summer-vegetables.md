## Chef's Note

Soften the aromatics before reducing the balsamic vinegar, then whisk in cold butter gradually over gentle heat. The sauce should coat the fish without turning oily. Drain the spinach well and cook the salmon last so the vegetables and sauce are ready when the small strips finish.

## Directions

1. **Prepare the components:** Slice the salmon crosswise into similar strips for the intended number of portions. Pat dry and keep cold. Separate the shiitake stems from the quartered caps. Measure each butter and oil allocation, keeping the sauce-finishing butter chilled. Prepare a colander, warm serving plates and a bowl for straining sauce.
2. **Soften and reduce:** Melt **the aromatic butter** in a nonreactive saucepan over medium-low. Add onion, sliced garlic, mushroom stems, tomato, peppercorns and bay leaf. Cook about **10–12 minutes**, stirring, until softened without browning. Add balsamic vinegar and the measured water. Simmer over medium to medium-high about **4–8 minutes**, stirring more often near the end, until syrupy but still moist; do not let it scorch dry.
3. **Finish the sauce:** Turn heat to very low or move off heat if the pan is very hot. Whisk in **the chilled sauce butter** a few cubes at a time, letting each addition blend before adding more. If butter stops softening, briefly return to low heat; do not boil. Strain into the bowl, pressing lightly, and discard solids. Season with salt and pepper. Hold briefly above warm water, with the bowl not touching the water, and stir occasionally while finishing the meal.
4. **Wilt spinach:** Heat **the spinach oil** in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the whole garlic clove and spinach in handfuls, turning with tongs until wilted, about **3–5 minutes**. Remove the garlic, season and transfer spinach to the colander. Let excess liquid drain; keep covered briefly while cooking the next components.
5. **Cook mushrooms and corn:** Carefully wipe the skillet clean. Melt **the vegetable butter** over medium heat. Sauté mushroom caps about **3–4 minutes** until softened, then add corn and cook about **3 minutes** until hot and tender. Season with salt and pepper and transfer to a warm bowl. Do not keep heating solely to force charring.
6. **Sear salmon:** Wipe the skillet if needed and heat **the searing oil and butter** over medium-high, reserving a proportional share of both for each additional batch. Season fish with salt and pepper. When butter foams without scorching, lay in the strips with space between them, in batches if necessary. Cook about **2–3 minutes on the first side**, turn gently, then cook about **2–4 minutes more**, adjusting for thickness. Check representative thick centers from the side for **145°F / 63°C**; lower the heat if the surface browns before the center is ready. Remove each strip as it finishes.
7. **Plate promptly:** Divide drained spinach, corn and mushrooms among the intended portions, add salmon, then spoon over balsamic butter sauce. Sprinkle with chives. Serve immediately; this sauce is intended for a short finish at the stove, not extended warm holding.
