## Chef's Note

Michael Romano's dish sets seared salmon on garlicky spinach, sweet corn and shiitakes, with a balsamic butter sauce in the style of a French beurre rouge. The trick is whisking cold butter a few cubes at a time into the reduced vinegar so the sauce turns glossy instead of greasy.

## Directions

1. **Soften the aromatics:** Keep the cubed butter cold. Melt the other sauce butter in a nonreactive saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the onion, sliced garlic, shiitake stems, tomato, peppercorns and bay leaf. Cook, stirring, until soft but not browned, 10 to 12 minutes.
2. **Reduce the balsamic:** Add the vinegar and water. Simmer over medium heat, stirring more often near the end, until syrupy but still moist, 4 to 8 minutes.
3. **Whisk in the butter:** Turn the heat to very low. Whisk in the cold butter a few cubes at a time, letting each addition melt before adding more, until the sauce is creamy and glossy. Don't let it boil. Strain it into a bowl, pressing on the solids, season with salt and pepper and keep it warm over a pan of warm water.
4. **Wilt the spinach:** Heat the olive oil for the vegetables in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the garlic clove and the spinach by handfuls, turning with tongs until wilted, 3 to 5 minutes. Discard the garlic, season the spinach and let it drain in a colander.
5. **Cook the mushrooms and corn:** Wipe out the skillet and melt the butter for the vegetables over medium heat. Cook the shiitake caps until soft, 3 to 4 minutes. Add the corn and cook until hot and lightly browned, about 3 minutes. Season and keep warm.
6. **Sear the salmon:** Wipe out the skillet and heat the oil and butter for the salmon over medium-high heat until the butter foams. Season the salmon with salt and pepper and sear the strips until browned underneath, 2 to 3 minutes. Turn and cook until just opaque in the center, 2 to 3 minutes more.
7. **Plate:** Divide the spinach among warm plates and top with the corn, mushrooms and salmon. Spoon the sauce over the fish and scatter with chives.
