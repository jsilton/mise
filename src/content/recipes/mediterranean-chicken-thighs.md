## Chef's Note

A measured marinade lets you control lemon, oil and salt instead of assuming every bottled dressing has the same balance. Give the chicken a coating and a refrigerated rest, then roast it opened flat. A second tray lets the peppers and onions lose moisture and brown without forcing the chicken to wait for them.

## Directions

1. **Marinate cold:** Whisk marinade oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, chicken salt and chicken pepper. Toss with chicken, cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes, or up to 4 hours for a longer preparation window. Keep the chicken’s original storage/use-by limit in mind; marinating does not reset it.
2. **Prepare trays and vegetables:** During the marinade, heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Toss peppers and onion with their separate oil, salt and pepper and spread on one rimmed half-sheet pan. Lift the thighs from the marinade, allow excess to drip off, open them flat and space them on a second pan. Discard the raw-contact marinade; do not use it as a serving sauce. Wash raw-meat utensils and hands.
3. **Roast and check separately:** Put chicken below the vegetable tray. Roast for about 25–35 minutes, turning the vegetable pieces and rotating each tray front to back after fifteen minutes. Start checking chicken at twenty-five minutes: insert a thermometer into the thickest part of every thigh and continue until each reaches at least 165°F / 74°C. Remove finished pieces to a clean board. Vegetables should be soft through the thick onion wedges with browned edges; let them continue separately if needed.
4. **Rest and portion:** Rest the cooked chicken for five minutes, then slice across the meat. Divide the full chicken and vegetable batches among four plates. Scatter the optional measured parsley over the vegetables. This recipe supplies the protein and roasted vegetables; bread, grains and sauces are additional components.

## About variations

This method is written for boneless thighs with a measured center temperature. Bone-in pieces and breasts need different preparation and timing; do not assume adding ten minutes makes them equivalent. For a spice-led home shawarma adaptation, use the separate [Chicken Shawarma](/mise/recipes/chicken-shawarma) recipe rather than renaming this lemon-oregano roast after adding two spices.
