## Chef's Note

This is Greek sheet-pan chicken, with boneless thighs soaked in lemon, garlic and oregano and roasted hot. The peppers and onions get their own tray so they roast instead of steaming in the chicken juices. They come out sweet and browned at the edges.

## Directions

1. **Marinate the chicken:** Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper for the chicken in a large bowl. Add the chicken and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes and up to 4 hours.
2. **Season the vegetables:** While the chicken marinates, heat the oven to 425°F with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Toss the peppers and onion with their olive oil, salt and pepper and spread them on a rimmed sheet pan.
3. **Lay out the chicken:** Lift the thighs from the marinade and let the excess drip off. Open them flat on a second rimmed sheet pan with space between each piece.
4. **Roast both trays:** Put the chicken on the lower rack and the vegetables on the upper rack. Roast for 15 minutes, then stir the vegetables and rotate both pans. Keep roasting until the chicken is browned and 165°F in the thickest part and the peppers are soft with browned edges, 10 to 20 minutes more. Take out each tray when it's done.
5. **Rest and serve:** Rest the chicken for 5 minutes, then slice it across the grain. Pile it on a platter with the peppers and onions and scatter the parsley over the top.
