## Chef's Note

Maduros are the sweet cousin of tostones, ripe plantains fried until their sugar turns to a sticky, jammy crust. Ripeness is everything. The skins should be mostly black with a few yellow patches and the fruit soft to the touch, so buy yellow plantains four or five days before you plan to cook them.

## Directions

1. **Fry the plantains:** Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat until it shimmers. Add the slices in a single layer without crowding. Work in batches, using the oil left in the pan for each one. Cook until deep golden and caramelized outside and soft in the center, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Lower the heat if they darken before the centers soften.
2. **Drain and season:** As each batch comes out, drain it on a paper towel-lined plate and sprinkle with the salt and cinnamon. Serve hot.
