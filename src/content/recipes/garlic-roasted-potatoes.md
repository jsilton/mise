## Chef's Note

Ina Garten's roasted potatoes are a holiday and weeknight standby. Give every piece room on the pan, cut side down, so the faces brown instead of steaming. The garlic goes in for the last few minutes only, so it turns sweet and fragrant instead of bitter.

## Directions

1. **Season the potatoes:** Heat the oven to 400°F. On a large rimmed sheet pan, toss the potatoes with all but a spoonful of the oil, the salt and the pepper. Spread them in one layer, cut side down, with a little space between pieces. Use two pans if they crowd.
2. **Roast:** Roast, turning once with a wide spatula after 25 minutes, until the cut faces are golden and a knife slides easily into the center, 40 to 55 minutes.
3. **Add the garlic:** Stir the garlic into the reserved oil and toss it through the potatoes. Roast until the garlic is fragrant and pale gold, about 5 minutes.
4. **Finish:** Toss the potatoes with the parsley in a serving bowl. Taste for salt and serve.
