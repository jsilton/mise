## Chef's Note

Pai huang gua is a northern Chinese classic. Smashing the cucumbers instead of slicing them leaves jagged surfaces that soak up the garlic, black vinegar and sesame dressing. A short salt and drain keeps the cucumber crisp and the dressing bold.

## Directions

1. **Smash the cucumbers:** Lay the cucumbers on a board, cover them with a towel and whack them with a rolling pin until they crack open but hold together. Cut into rough 1-inch pieces, scraping out any watery seed core.
2. **Salt and drain:** Toss the pieces with the salt in a colander set over a bowl. Let them drain in the fridge for 15 minutes, then shake off the liquid.
3. **Mix the dressing:** While the cucumbers drain, stir the vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, garlic and chili crisp together until the sugar dissolves.
4. **Toss and serve:** Toss the cucumbers with the dressing until glossy. Scatter the sesame seeds and scallion over the top and serve right away.
