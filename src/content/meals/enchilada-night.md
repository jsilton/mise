## Meal Overview

Chicken and green chile enchiladas under red sauce and melted cheese, with arroz rojo, seasoned black beans and guacamole. The guacamole is made while the enchiladas bake. For five, the twelve enchiladas fill one 9x13-inch dish and give everyone two or three.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Cook the rice:** Rinse and toast the rice, add the tomato and broth and simmer covered for 15 to 18 minutes. Leave it covered off the heat.
2. **Roll the enchiladas:** Heat the oven to 375°F. Soften the tortillas in hot oil, dip them in the sauce, fill them and lay them seam side down in the baking dish.
3. **Bake:** Pour the rest of the sauce over the rolls, scatter the cheese on top and bake until bubbling at the edges, 15 to 20 minutes.
4. **Simmer the beans:** While the enchiladas bake, simmer the beans until their liquid coats them, 8 to 12 minutes.
5. **Make the guacamole:** While the beans simmer, make the guacamole.
6. **Serve:** Let the enchiladas stand for 5 minutes and serve everything together.
