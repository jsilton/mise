## Meal Overview

A smaller holiday dinner with dry-brined cider turkey, sausage-kale stuffing, cream cheese mashed potatoes, cranberry sauce and lemon-dressed arugula. The stuffing and potatoes are both intentional holiday sides: serve modest portions of each rather than adding bread or another starch. The crisp salad and tart fruit balance the rich cooked dishes.

For eight diners, prepare these amounts once:

- **Turkey:** one whole 10–12 lb bird using the linked fixed-size recipe. It is written for about ten people with sides, so this dinner allows some extra meat; the actual carved yield determines leftovers.
- **Stuffing:** one full eight-portion batch, with 340 g ciabatta, 450 g sausage and 300 g trimmed kale. Bake in its shallow 9-by-13-inch dish.
- **Potatoes:** one full eight-portion batch, starting with 1360 g peeled potatoes. Serve about three-quarters of a cup each.
- **Cranberry sauce:** one full batch, yielding roughly two cups. Offer two to three tablespoons per diner and refrigerate the remainder. Use either the peel-strip version or its measured orange variation, not two sauces.
- **Gravy:** one full eight-portion batch of [Turkey Pan Gravy](/mise/recipes/turkey-pan-gravy), about two cups. Its unsalted stock and limited pan liquid account for the brined bird’s salinity.
- **Arugula salad:** two batches, with 284 g arugula and all the doubled dressing and cheese quantities. Dress only at service.

Allow about **thirty hours elapsed from a fully refrigerator-thawed turkey**, including its twenty-four-hour dry brine, and about **two and a half hours hands-on work across both days**. Thawing is additional. This is a preparation schedule, not a guarantee that every bird finishes at a chosen clock time.

## Equipment and oven plan

Before starting, check that the turkey roasting pan and the stuffing dish fit on separate oven racks with air space. The **stuffing goes above the turkey** so raw drips cannot reach it. Both use **325°F / 163°C** here; the stuffing recipe supplies its longer baking allowance at that temperature. Reserve two hob positions near service for potatoes and gravy, with a heatproof bowl for a brief potato hold if needed.

If both dishes do not fit, use a second oven for stuffing or bake the stuffing fully the day before, cool it promptly in shallow portions and refrigerate. Reheat it covered in a suitable shallow oven-safe dish to **165°F / 74°C** after the turkey comes out; uncover briefly for the top. Allow roughly **45–60 minutes after the turkey leaves the oven** for this fallback, using 350°F / 177°C and checking the stuffing rather than promising an exact reheat time. Rest the turkey for thirty minutes, then carve near the end of the stuffing reheat and serve promptly. This extends the turkey’s rest-to-service interval; keep the entire counter interval below two hours, and use measured hot holding or prompt cooling if dinner is further delayed. Do not squeeze pans into an overcrowded oven. The shared-oven plan below is the default.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **The day before:** Begin the fully thawed turkey's twenty-four-hour uncovered refrigerated dry brine. Make cranberry sauce and refrigerate in a shallow container. Wash and dry salad leaves, keeping them cold and undressed. If chopping stuffing vegetables early, keep them refrigerated; leave bread separate and do not mix the wet stuffing ahead.
2. **About four and a half hours before the planned meal:** Preheat to 325°F / 163°C while finishing the turkey's pan preparation, then roast according to its recipe. Keep the cavity empty. Prepare the stuffing ingredients, gravy ingredients and potato equipment while the bird cooks. The turkey's first temperature checks begin at about two and a half hours roasting.
3. **About ninety minutes before the estimated turkey removal:** Start hydrating the stuffing bread while browning sausage and softening its vegetables. Combine only when ready to bake, and get the shallow dish into the oven roughly forty to fifty-five minutes before the bird is expected to finish. Check the stuffing center reaches **165°F / 74°C**; the bird's readings do not establish the stuffing's doneness. If the oven is slow, allow more time and shift dinner.
4. **About one hour before service:** Start the potatoes, including peeling/cutting and heating their water. Warm their dairy during simmering. Finish gently when fully tender. If the turkey or stuffing needs longer, use the potato recipe's short covered water-bath hold above **140°F / 60°C**, checking the temperature and limiting the hold to about thirty minutes for texture.
5. **When the turkey is actually done:** Confirm all required breast, inner-thigh and wing sites reach **165°F / 74°C**. Transfer to a clean board to rest thirty minutes. When the stuffing has independently reached its endpoint, cover loosely. If it needs a short hold, turn the now turkey-free oven down to 200°F / 93°C and check that the stuffing stays above **140°F / 60°C**. Avoid a long uncovered hold that dries the top and edges.
6. **Make gravy during the turkey rest:** Follow one full batch of [Turkey Pan Gravy](/mise/recipes/turkey-pan-gravy), including its straining, fat separation, measured roux, gradual stock additions and simmer. Taste the turkey pan liquid first and use the water substitution if it is bitter or excessively salty. Keep the sauce hot and pourable while carving; it is not all the roasting-pan fat poured into a saucepan.
7. **Finish and serve:** Carve the rested turkey. Dress the arugula with the linked recipe's doubled quantities, beginning with three-quarters of the dressing as instructed. Bring out the cold cranberry sauce, hot potatoes, stuffing and gravy. Divide modest portions of each side across eight plates, then offer extra turkey and sauce at the table.

## If timing changes

Follow temperature and tenderness, not the clock. An early turkey can rest while the remaining components finish, but the entire room-temperature interval still counts toward the two-hour limit. If dinner is substantially delayed, use measured hot holding or cool and refrigerate the cooked food promptly, then reheat to **165°F / 74°C**. A lukewarm bird, an unmeasured slow cooker or a long counter rest is not a holding plan.

## Leftovers

Carve remaining turkey meat off the carcass and cool cooked components in separate shallow containers. Refrigerate perishables within two hours, or one hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below. Use turkey, stuffing, potatoes, gravy and cranberry sauce within three to four days; reheat hot components to 165°F / 74°C throughout. Dressed salad is best eaten at this meal; refrigerate any remainder promptly and use by the next day for quality. These limits follow the component guidance and [USDA's leftover handling advice](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).
