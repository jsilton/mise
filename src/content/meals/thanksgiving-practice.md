## Meal Overview

A smaller holiday dinner with dry-brined cider turkey, sausage-kale stuffing, cream cheese mashed potatoes, cranberry sauce and lemon-dressed arugula. The stuffing and potatoes are both holiday sides, so serve modest portions of each. The crisp salad and tart fruit balance the rich cooked dishes.

For eight diners, prepare these amounts once:

- **Turkey:** one whole 10 to 12 lb bird. It feeds about ten people with sides, so this dinner leaves some extra meat.
- **Stuffing:** one full eight-portion batch, with 340 g ciabatta, 450 g sausage and 300 g trimmed kale. Bake in its shallow 9-by-13-inch dish.
- **Potatoes:** one full eight-portion batch, starting with 1360 g peeled potatoes. Serve about three-quarters of a cup each.
- **Cranberry sauce:** one full batch, yielding about two cups. Serve two to three tablespoons per diner and refrigerate the remainder. Make the peel-strip version.
- **Gravy:** one full eight-portion batch of [Turkey Pan Gravy](/mise/recipes/turkey-pan-gravy), about two cups. Its unsalted stock and small amount of pan liquid balance the brined bird’s salt.
- **Arugula salad:** two batches, with 284 g arugula and all the doubled dressing and cheese quantities. Dress it just before serving.

Allow about **thirty hours elapsed from a fully refrigerator-thawed turkey**, including its twenty-four-hour dry brine, and about **two and a half hours hands-on work across both days**. Thawing is additional.

## Equipment and oven plan

The turkey roasting pan and the stuffing dish go on separate oven racks with air space. The **stuffing goes above the turkey** so raw drips cannot reach it. Both use **325°F / 163°C** here. Keep two burners free near the end for the potatoes and gravy.

If both dishes do not fit, use a second oven for the stuffing or bake the stuffing fully the day before and refrigerate it in shallow portions. Reheat it covered in a shallow oven-safe dish at 350°F / 177°C after the turkey comes out, until it reaches **165°F / 74°C**. Uncover it briefly to crisp the top. Allow about **45 to 60 minutes after the turkey leaves the oven**. Rest the turkey for thirty minutes, then carve near the end of the stuffing reheat.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **The day before:** Begin the thawed turkey's twenty-four-hour uncovered dry brine in the refrigerator. Make the cranberry sauce and refrigerate it. Wash and dry the salad leaves and keep them cold and undressed. Chop the stuffing vegetables and refrigerate them, separate from the bread.
2. **About four and a half hours before the meal:** Preheat to 325°F / 163°C, finish the turkey's pan preparation and roast it with the cavity empty. Prepare the stuffing ingredients, gravy ingredients and potato equipment while the bird cooks. Begin checking the turkey's temperature at about two and a half hours.
3. **About ninety minutes before the turkey comes out:** Start hydrating the stuffing bread while browning the sausage and softening its vegetables. Combine them just before baking, and get the shallow dish into the oven about forty to fifty-five minutes before the bird finishes. Bake until the stuffing center reaches **165°F / 74°C**.
4. **About one hour before serving:** Peel and cut the potatoes and start them in their water. Warm their dairy while they simmer. Mash gently when fully tender, then cover the pot to keep the mash warm.
5. **When the turkey is done:** The breast, inner thigh and wing joint all read **165°F / 74°C**. Move the bird to a board to rest for thirty minutes. Cover the finished stuffing loosely and hold it in the oven at 200°F / 93°C.
6. **Make gravy during the turkey rest:** Make one full batch of [Turkey Pan Gravy](/mise/recipes/turkey-pan-gravy): strain the pan liquid, separate the fat, cook the roux, add the stock gradually and simmer. Taste the pan liquid first and use water in its place if it is bitter or very salty. Keep the gravy hot and pourable while carving.
7. **Finish and serve:** Carve the rested turkey. Dress the arugula, starting with three-quarters of the doubled dressing. Bring out the cold cranberry sauce, hot potatoes, stuffing and gravy. Divide modest portions of each side across eight plates, and put the extra turkey and sauce on the table.

## If timing changes

An early turkey rests while the remaining components finish. If dinner runs late, keep the hot dishes covered in the 200°F / 93°C oven.

## Leftovers

Carve the remaining turkey off the carcass and refrigerate the cooked components separately. Use turkey, stuffing, potatoes, gravy and cranberry sauce within three to four days. Dressed salad is best eaten at this meal. The food safety service has [leftover handling advice](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety) for holiday meals.
