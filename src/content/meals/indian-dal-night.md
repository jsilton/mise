## Meal Overview

Creamy mung dal with an onion-and-spice tadka, saffron-cardamom rice, tender potatoes and cauliflower in a clinging tomato masala, and cool cucumber raita. Rice and potatoes share the starch portion. The dal anchors the plate, while yogurt and fresh herbs give a tangy contrast.

Allow about **1 hour 50 minutes elapsed** and **80 to 90 minutes hands-on** for one cook starting with dry rice and dal and uncut vegetables. Use three burners, a lidded rice saucepan, a dal saucepan, a deep lidded vegetable pan and a small skillet. The short spice stages need your attention. Prepare the other dishes while the dal simmers and the vegetables cook covered.

## Portions for Four

- **Dal:** Make one full [Dal Tadka](/mise/recipes/dal-tadka), starting with **200 g split hulled yellow mung dal and 720 ml water**, with all its onion, three tablespoons of ghee and the complete spice finish. Divide the finished pot among four small bowls, about a cup each.
- **Rice:** Make **half the [Saffron Cardamom Rice](/mise/recipes/saffron-cardamom-rice)**: **150 g dry aged white basmati, 300 ml water, 30 ml whole milk, 1/4 tsp loosely measured saffron, 1 1/2 tbsp ghee, three green cardamom pods, one clove, a 3/4-inch cinnamon piece, half an Indian bay leaf, 75 g sliced onion and 1/4 tsp fine salt**. Keep **30 ml hot correction water** separate. Finish with **1 1/2 tsp cilantro**. Leave out the black cardamom so the saffron and green cardamom stay prominent beside the two spiced dishes. Use a narrow **1 1/2 to 2-quart saucepan**. This gives about **2 1/4 to 2 1/2 cups cooked rice**, or a little over half a cup each.
- **Aloo gobi:** Make **half the [Aloo Gobi](/mise/recipes/aloo-gobi-potato-cauliflower-curry)** with **340 g trimmed potatoes and 340 g cauliflower florets**, halving its remaining ingredients. Use **1 1/2 tbsp oil, 1/2 tsp initial fine salt and 1/4 tsp finishing salt**. Its cooking water is **30 ml with potatoes plus 30 ml with cauliflower**, with a separate **30 ml hot correction reserve**. Use a deep **10-inch, 3 to 4-quart pan with a tight lid**. Divide all the finished vegetables among four. They are a substantial side to the dal.
- **Raita:** Make **half the [Cucumber Raita](/mise/recipes/cucumber-raita)**: **240 g plain unstrained whole-milk yogurt, 150 g cucumber, 1/2 tsp cumin seeds, 2 tbsp chopped mint and 1/8 tsp fine salt**. The finished **1 1/4 to 1 1/2 cups** gives about **five to six tablespoons per person**. The mint is a fresh contrast to the cilantro in the other dishes.

Serve the rice in a small mound with the dal beside it, the aloo gobi alongside, and the raita in a cool dish.

## Cooking Strategy

Have a **2 to 3-quart dal saucepan**, the small rice saucepan, the deep lidded vegetable pan and a **small skillet for cumin and the dal's onion/tadka** ready. The skillet takes the dal's burner once the dal is cooked. Keep cooked pots covered while they wait.

1. **0 to 10 minutes, start dal and saffron:** Rinse the dal and start it on burner one with its water, turmeric and first salt. Bring to a boil, then simmer gently. Warm the rice's 30 ml milk in a small heatproof bowl, crumble in the saffron and set it aside to steep. Measure the spices for the raita, rice, aloo gobi and tadka into separate groups.
2. **10 to 30 minutes, prepare the vegetables and rice:** Cut the aloo gobi potatoes into 3/4-inch cubes and the cauliflower into 1-inch florets. Chop its onion, garlic, ginger and tomato. Prepare the dal's tomato, onion and garlic and slice the rice onion. Rinse and drain the rice without soaking. Once the lentils crush easily, add the dal's tomato and green chile and simmer another 5 to 8 minutes. Thin the dal until spoonable, cover it and set it aside.
3. **About 30 to 48 minutes, begin aloo gobi:** On burner two, fry the whole spices, then cook the onion, the garlic and ginger, and the tomato masala. Add the potatoes and the first 30 ml cooking water. Bring to a gentle simmer and cover. Give the potatoes a **12 to 18 minute head start**.
4. **About 48 to 65 minutes, start rice while potatoes cook:** On burner three, cook the rice's whole spices in ghee and soften its onion to light gold, then fold in the rice. Add its 300 ml water, saffron milk and salt, bring to a gentle boil and cover for the **15 to 20 minute simmer**. Add the cauliflower and its 30 ml cooking water when the potatoes have softened outside with some resistance in the center. Cover again and cook until both vegetables are tender, checking every five minutes.
5. **About 65 to 85 minutes, make raita and check both covered pans:** Toast the raita cumin in the small skillet on the dal's burner, then tip the seeds out to cool. Grate and drain the cucumber, crush the cumin, mix the raita and refrigerate it. When the rice timer sounds, move the rice off the heat for its **ten-minute covered rest**. Once both vegetables are tender, cook the aloo gobi uncovered until dry and finish it with garam masala, lemon and cilantro. Turn it off and cover it loosely.
6. **About 85 to 105 minutes, finish the dal:** Return the dal to a gentle simmer, adding hot water because it thickens while standing. In the small skillet, cook its onion in **two tablespoons ghee for 7 to 10 minutes**. Add the remaining tablespoon of ghee, then the mustard, cumin, dried chile, curry leaf and garlic. Switch off the skillet and add the hing and ground chile. Pour the tadka into the hot dal, stir in the lemon and finish with cilantro.
7. **About 105 to 110 minutes, serve:** Pick the whole spices you can see out of the rice and fluff it gently. Warm the vegetable pan briefly if it cooled during the tadka. Bring the raita straight from the refrigerator and divide all four components evenly.

## For Five Diners

Follow the same cooking sequence with the amounts below in place of the four-person quantities. Small seasonings are rounded for practical measuring.

- **Dal:** **250 g split hulled yellow mung, 900 ml water plus hot water for consistency, 5/8 tsp turmeric, 5/8 tsp fine salt, 190 g diced tomato, one slit green serrano, one large diced yellow onion, 4 tbsp ghee, 1 1/4 tsp each cumin and black mustard seeds, ten curry leaves, two to three dried red chiles, five sliced garlic cloves, 1/4 tsp hing, 1 1/4 tsp Kashmiri chile powder, 2 1/2 tbsp cilantro and 1 1/4 tbsp lemon juice**. Divide the ghee into **2 1/2 tbsp for onion and 1 1/2 tbsp for the final spices**.
- **Rice:** **190 g dry aged basmati, 375 ml cooking water, 2 1/2 tbsp (37.5 ml) whole milk, 3/8 tsp loosely measured saffron, 2 tbsp ghee, four green cardamom pods, one clove, a 1-inch cinnamon piece, half an Indian bay leaf, 95 g sliced onion, 3/8 tsp fine salt and 2 tsp cilantro**. Keep **2 1/2 tbsp hot correction water** separate. Leave out the black cardamom.
- **Aloo gobi:** **425 g each trimmed potato and cauliflower, 2 tbsp oil, 5/8 tsp each cumin seeds, lightly crushed coriander seeds, turmeric and ground coriander, then 65 g diced onion, three garlic cloves, 2 tsp grated ginger, one slit serrano, 95 g diced tomato, 5/8 tsp initial fine salt and 3/8 tsp finishing salt**. Use **2 1/2 tbsp water with potatoes and another 2 1/2 tbsp with cauliflower**, plus a separate **2 1/2 tbsp hot correction reserve**. Finish with **3/8 tsp garam masala, 1/8 tsp cayenne, 2 tsp lemon juice and 1 1/4 tbsp cilantro**.
- **Raita:** **300 g plain unstrained whole-milk yogurt, 190 g cucumber, 5/8 tsp cumin seeds, 2 1/2 tbsp chopped mint and 1/8 tsp fine salt**. Toast, drain, mix and chill as for four.

A **5/8 tsp** measure is **1/2 tsp plus 1/8 tsp**. **3/8 tsp** is **1/4 tsp plus 1/8 tsp**. Use a **2-quart rice saucepan**, a **3-quart dal saucepan**, and a **4-quart deep lidded vegetable pan**. Allow a further **10 to 15 minutes** for the larger preparation. Divide each finished component five ways.

## Make Ahead and Leftovers

For a shorter cooking day, make the dal and aloo gobi ahead and refrigerate them separately. With those two made, allow about **65 to 75 minutes elapsed and 35 to 45 minutes hands-on**. Make the saffron rice, mix fresh raita during its simmer, and reheat the dal and aloo gobi separately until steaming while the rice cooks and rests. Loosen the dal with hot water. Turn the vegetables gently so the florets stay intact.

Refrigerate cooked rice, dal and vegetables separately and use them within **3 to 4 days**. Raita keeps for one day and loosens with time.
