## Meal Overview

Eggs poached in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce, with warm pita for scooping and a crisp fattoush salad with sumac and mint. The salad is prepped while the sauce simmers. For five, the six eggs give one each with one to spare, so warm a fifth pita.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Toast the pita chips:** Heat the oven to 375°F. Toss the torn pita for the fattoush with its oil and sumac and bake until golden and dry, 8 to 10 minutes.
2. **Simmer the sauce:** Soften the onion and pepper, bloom the spices and simmer the crushed tomatoes until thick, about 10 minutes.
3. **Prep the salad:** While the sauce simmers, whisk the fattoush dressing and combine the vegetables, lettuce and herbs.
4. **Poach the eggs:** Slide the eggs into wells in the sauce. Cover and cook on medium-low until the whites set, 5 to 8 minutes.
5. **Warm the pita:** Warm the pita breads in a hot dry skillet, 30 to 60 seconds per side, and wrap them in a towel.
6. **Serve:** Dress the salad and fold in the pita chips. Scatter the herbs and feta over the shakshuka and serve it from the skillet.
