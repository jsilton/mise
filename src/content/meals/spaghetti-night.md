## Meal Overview

Classic slow-cooked ragù becomes a weeknight dinner when the sauce is made ahead. Reheat it while the pasta water heats, then finish the spaghetti in the sauce. Garlic bread and arugula with feta and strawberry vinaigrette round out the meal. Allow about 45–60 minutes from refrigerated, already-cooked ragù, softened butter and toasted salad seeds; cooking the ragù from scratch takes a separate 3–4 hours.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the water and oven:** Bring a large pot of water to a boil for enough spaghetti for six diners, following the pasta package's serving guidance. Heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C for the garlic bread.
2. **Reheat the ragù:** Put six cooked sauce portions in a saucepan and stir frequently over moderate heat. If stiff, loosen with a little water. Bring the sauce to a boil and check that it reaches 165°F / 74°C throughout; then keep it hot over low heat.
3. **Prepare the bread and salad:** Make one batch of garlic bread with softened butter and bake for about 12–15 minutes, checking the center and edges. Prepare salad for six, keeping the dressing separate until service.
4. **Cook and finish the spaghetti:** Cook to the package's first al dente check and reserve some cooking water. Toss the drained spaghetti with the hot ragù in a wide pan, adding small splashes of pasta water until the sauce coats the strands. Use batches if the pan is crowded.
5. **Serve together:** Dress the salad, cut the bread and serve the pasta promptly, with freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano as wanted.

## Notes

- The ragù's milk is already cooked into the sauce; follow its formula when preparing it ahead.
- Thaw frozen ragù in the refrigerator before using this dinner plan. A frozen block takes longer to reheat.
- Refrigerate leftovers promptly within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C) and use cooked ragù within 3–4 days, following the linked recipe's storage guidance.
