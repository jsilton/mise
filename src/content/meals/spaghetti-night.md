## Meal Overview

Slow-cooked ragù becomes a weeknight dinner when the sauce is made ahead. It reheats while the pasta water boils, and the spaghetti is finished in the sauce. Garlic bread and an arugula salad with feta and strawberry vinaigrette round out the meal.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Heat the oven and water:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil for 1 1/2 lb of spaghetti.
2. **Reheat the ragù:** Warm the ragù in a wide pan over medium heat, stirring often, until it simmers. Loosen it with a splash of water and keep it hot on low.
3. **Bake the bread:** Spread the garlic butter on the bread and bake until the edges are golden, 12 to 15 minutes.
4. **Blend the dressing:** Blend the strawberry vinaigrette while the bread bakes.
5. **Cook the spaghetti:** Boil the spaghetti until al dente and save a mug of the water. Toss the pasta with the ragù and splashes of pasta water until the sauce coats the strands.
6. **Serve:** Dress the salad, slice the bread and serve the pasta with grated Parmigiano-Reggiano.
