## Meal Overview

For four people, make one full batch of Lemon-Oregano Chicken with Roasted Peppers, one full batch of Warm, Soft Pita Bread, and **half a batch of [Mint Tzatziki with Honey](/mise/recipes/mint-tzatziki), approximately 3/4 cup**. The mint sauce adds a cool, creamy contrast to the lemon-oregano chicken. For a dill sauce, use the alternative below.

The main supplies 900 g raw boneless chicken thighs, 400 g trimmed peppers and 200 g onion. Four pitas are the starch. Serve the roast, warm bread, cool sauce and crisp vegetables family-style, dividing them into four generous portions. Fill each pita with some chicken and vegetables and eat the rest alongside; this amount of food will not all fit inside one small bread.

## Fresh platter ingredients

These are additional to the main's roasted vegetables and the sauce's cucumber. Prepare this spread once:

- 200 g cherry tomatoes, washed and halved
- 200 g English cucumber, washed and sliced
- 120 g romaine leaves, washed, thoroughly dried and shredded
- 60 g crumbled feta, optional

Leave the fresh vegetables undressed and offer the optional feta at the table. Plan about three tablespoons of mint sauce per diner, shared between the pita and the vegetables. The main's lemon-oregano seasoning, sweet roast peppers, fresh mint and cool yogurt give each part a different role.

## Cooking Strategy

Allow **about 90 minutes elapsed and 65 minutes active** for one cook, including the minimum refrigerated marinade, all chopping, two roasting trays, four separate bread-warming rounds and assembly. Use two rimmed half-sheet pans, about 18 × 13 inches, on separate oven racks, plus a skillet wide enough to hold one pita flat on one burner. Confirm that the trays fit with space for air to circulate. Thaw frozen chicken and fully baked pita before starting; thawing and a longer marinade are additional.

1. **Start the marinade:** Make the main recipe's measured marinade, coat the thighs and refrigerate them for at least thirty minutes. Wash hands and clean the raw-chicken work area before preparing the ready-to-eat components. Begin heating the oven to 425°F / 220°C, with racks in the upper and lower thirds.
2. **Prepare the cold and roasted vegetables:** During marination, cut the peppers and onion to the main recipe's dimensions and prepare all the fresh platter vegetables. Keep the fresh vegetables covered and refrigerated. Make the mint sauce using its half-batch setting: this uses 120 g Greek yogurt and 50 g finely diced cucumber, separately from the 200 g sliced platter cucumber. Stir, taste the divided salt as directed, and refrigerate. A half small garlic clove is enough for this sauce batch; save the other half for another use. Allow roughly the first 45 minutes of the meal for the marinade preparation, vegetable and sauce work, tray loading and cleanup.
3. **Load the trays and roast:** Toss peppers and onion with their measured vegetable oil and seasoning and spread them on one tray. Lift the thighs from the marinade, let excess drip off and open them flat on the other tray with space between pieces. Discard the raw-contact marinade and clean the utensils. Put chicken below vegetables. Roast for 25–35 minutes, turning the vegetables and rotating each tray front to back after fifteen minutes; keep the chicken on the lower rack.
4. **Warm bread and check the roast:** After that fifteen-minute tray turn, heat the dry skillet and warm the four pitas in separate rounds using their recipe. Use its measured optional water if the bread feels dry; omit the optional finishing oil and salt for this platter. Wrap each warm bread in a clean towel. Start checking every thigh at twenty-five minutes of roasting and cook each to at least 165°F / 74°C in its thickest part. Take a pita out of the skillet before pausing to handle the oven or thermometer. Peppers should be soft and onion wedges tender through the center, with browned edges; let their tray finish separately if needed. Do not keep finished chicken roasting merely to brown the vegetables.
5. **Rest and serve:** Rest cooked chicken on a clean board for five minutes, then slice. Finish any remaining bread rounds and keep the stack wrapped. Divide the complete chicken and roasted vegetable batches, four breads and fresh platter among four diners. Stir the mint sauce and divide it into four portions of approximately three tablespoons; use some inside each pita and the rest alongside. Divide the optional feta and the main's optional measured parsley if using them. Bring the hot and cold components to the table together and assemble close to eating.

## Dill sauce alternative

Use **1/2 cup prepared [Dill Tzatziki](/mise/recipes/tzatziki-sauce)** in place of the entire mint sauce allocation, about two tablespoons per diner. A quarter batch of that recipe supplies the half cup with a little extra; its full batch is much larger than the mint recipe's. Follow its five-minute cucumber drain, squeezing and fifteen-minute refrigerated chill. Start the small dill batch early in the chicken's marination interval, then prepare the platter while it chills. Keep the two sauces' herbs, cucumber preparation and batch sizes distinct, and make only the chosen sauce.

## Make ahead and leftovers

The main's refrigerated marinade can run up to four hours within its original raw-chicken storage limit. Make the chosen sauce and cut fresh vegetables ahead, keeping them covered and refrigerated. Store leftovers separately as chicken/roast vegetables, cold sauce, fresh produce and unfilled bread. Refrigerate perishables within two hours, or one hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below. Use cooked chicken, roast vegetables and sauce within three to four days, counting sauce storage from its original mixing date and following any earlier ingredient limit. Reheat chicken and roast vegetables to 165°F / 74°C; keep the sauce cold. The cut fresh vegetables are best by the next day for texture. Follow the bread's package guidance for untouched, unfilled leftovers; filled bread follows the filling's refrigeration limit.
