## Meal Overview

For four people, make one full batch of Lemon-Oregano Chicken with Roasted Peppers, one full batch of Warm, Soft Pita Bread, and **half a batch of [Mint Tzatziki with Honey](/mise/recipes/mint-tzatziki), about 3/4 cup**. The mint sauce adds a cool, creamy contrast to the lemon-oregano chicken. For a dill sauce, use the alternative below.

The main supplies 900 g raw boneless chicken thighs, 400 g trimmed peppers and 200 g onion. Four pitas are the starch. Serve the roast, warm bread, cool sauce and crisp vegetables family-style, dividing them into four generous portions. Fill each pita with some chicken and vegetables and eat the rest alongside.

## Fresh platter ingredients

These are in addition to the main's roasted vegetables and the sauce's cucumber:

- 200 g cherry tomatoes, washed and halved
- 200 g English cucumber, washed and sliced
- 120 g romaine leaves, washed, thoroughly dried and shredded
- 60 g crumbled feta

Leave the fresh vegetables undressed and put the feta on the table. Serve about three tablespoons of mint sauce per diner, shared between the pita and the vegetables.

## Cooking Strategy

Allow **about 90 minutes elapsed and 65 minutes active** for one cook, including the thirty-minute marinade, all chopping, two roasting trays, four bread-warming rounds and assembly. Use two rimmed half-sheet pans, about 18 × 13 inches, on separate oven racks, plus a skillet wide enough to hold one pita flat. Thaw frozen chicken and pita before starting.

1. **Start the marinade:** Make the marinade, coat the thighs and refrigerate them for at least thirty minutes. Heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C, with racks in the upper and lower thirds.
2. **Prepare the cold and roasted vegetables:** While the chicken marinates, cut the peppers and onion and prepare all the fresh platter vegetables. Cover and refrigerate the fresh vegetables. Make the half batch of mint sauce with 120 g Greek yogurt and 50 g finely diced cucumber, separate from the 200 g sliced platter cucumber. Use half a small garlic clove. Season and refrigerate the sauce. This work takes about the first 45 minutes.
3. **Load the trays and roast:** Toss the peppers and onion with their oil and seasoning and spread them on one tray. Lift the thighs from the marinade, let the excess drip off and open them flat on the other tray with space between pieces. Discard the marinade. Put the chicken on the lower rack and the vegetables above. Roast for 25 to 35 minutes, turning the vegetables and rotating each tray front to back after fifteen minutes.
4. **Warm bread and check the roast:** After the fifteen-minute tray turn, heat the dry skillet and warm the four pitas one at a time, without oil or salt. Wrap each warm bread in a towel. Start checking the thighs at twenty-five minutes and take each one out when it reaches 165°F / 74°C in its thickest part. The peppers are done when soft, and the onion wedges when tender through the center with browned edges. Leave the vegetable tray in a few minutes longer if it needs it.
5. **Rest and serve:** Rest the chicken on a board for five minutes, then slice it. Divide the chicken, roasted vegetables, four breads and fresh platter among four diners. Stir the mint sauce and divide it into four portions of about three tablespoons. Share out the feta and the main's parsley. Bring the hot and cold components to the table together.

## Dill sauce alternative

Use **1/2 cup prepared [Dill Tzatziki](/mise/recipes/tzatziki-sauce)** in place of all the mint sauce, about two tablespoons per diner. A quarter batch of that recipe makes the half cup with a little extra. Drain the cucumber for five minutes, squeeze it and chill the sauce for fifteen minutes. Start it early in the chicken's marinating time, then prepare the platter while it chills.

## Make ahead and leftovers

The chicken marinates for up to four hours. Make the sauce and cut the fresh vegetables ahead and refrigerate them covered. Store leftover chicken and roast vegetables, sauce, fresh produce and unfilled bread separately. Use the chicken, roast vegetables and sauce within three to four days. The cut fresh vegetables keep until the next day.
