## Meal Overview

For four people, make one full batch of Lemon-Oregano Chicken with Roasted Peppers, one full batch of Warm, Soft Pita Bread, and set aside **1/2 cup prepared Tzatziki Sauce**. A quarter batch of the tzatziki recipe supplies that amount with a little extra; do not make or serve a whole sauce batch by default.

The main supplies 900 g raw boneless chicken thighs, 400 g trimmed peppers and 200 g onion. Four pitas are the starch. The cool sauce contrasts with the hot roast, and the fresh vegetables below add crispness. This is a generous dinner for four, with no extra rice, hummus or second cheese dip needed.

## Fresh platter ingredients

These quantities preserve the original chicken recipe’s serving spread. They are counted here once, separately from the main’s roasted vegetables:

- 200 g cherry tomatoes, washed and halved
- 200 g English cucumber, washed and sliced
- 120 g romaine leaves, washed, thoroughly dried and shredded
- 60 g crumbled feta, optional

Leave the fresh vegetables undressed; the measured tzatziki provides a creamy accent. Offer the optional feta at the table, especially if the sauce or bread is already salty.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the chicken:** Mix its measured marinade and refrigerate the thighs for thirty minutes. Never leave them on the counter to marinate. Allow about ninety minutes elapsed and fifty-five active for the complete dinner; a longer marinade extends elapsed time.
2. **Make a small sauce batch:** Use the tzatziki recipe’s quarter-batch ingredient setting, following its draining, squeezing and refrigerated chilling steps. Set aside half a cup for serving and refrigerate any excess. This is the standalone dill/grated-cucumber sauce, not the lamb burger’s mint sauce.
3. **Prepare vegetables and oven:** During marination, prepare the main’s peppers and onion and all the fresh platter vegetables. Refrigerate the fresh vegetables until service. Preheat the oven to 425°F / 220°C. Arrange chicken on its own tray and roasted vegetables on a second tray; keep raw-meat utensils away from the cold components.
4. **Roast and warm bread:** Follow the main recipe’s separate chicken and vegetable checks, keeping the chicken tray below the vegetables. Measure every thigh to at least 165°F / 74°C. During the last fifteen minutes of roasting, warm all four pitas in a separate skillet using their recipe, omitting optional finishing oil and salt. Keep them wrapped in a clean towel. Do not crowd a third tray into the already occupied oven or assume a five-minute bread warm at another temperature is equivalent.
5. **Rest and assemble:** Let cooked chicken rest five minutes, then slice. Divide the full chicken and roasted vegetable batches among four plates. Add one warm pita, one-quarter of the fresh vegetables and two tablespoons tzatziki to each, with optional feta divided across the table. Assemble close to eating so the bread and lettuce keep their texture.

## Make ahead and leftovers

The main’s refrigerated marinade can run up to four hours within its original raw-chicken storage limit. Make sauce and cut fresh vegetables ahead, keeping both covered and refrigerated. For this dinner, store leftovers as separate chicken/roast vegetables, cold sauce, fresh produce and unfilled bread. Refrigerate perishables within two hours, or one hour above 90°F / 32°C. Use cooked chicken and sauce within three to four days at 40°F / 4°C or below; reheat chicken to 165°F / 74°C. The fresh platter is best by the next day for texture.
