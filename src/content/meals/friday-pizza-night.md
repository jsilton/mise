## Meal Overview

Homemade pizza on a no-knead dough that rises for 18 hours, so it starts on Thursday night. On Friday everyone tops their own pizza, and a lemony arugula salad goes beside it. The dough makes five 9-inch pizzas, one for each of us.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Mix the dough on Thursday:** Around 10 PM on Thursday, stir the flour, water, salt and yeast until no dry flour remains. Cover the bowl and leave it at room temperature for about 18 hours.
2. **Shape the balls:** On Friday, 2 hours 15 minutes before dinner, cut the dough into five pieces and fold each into a ball. Cover and rest them for 1 hour.
3. **Make the sauce:** While the dough rests, simmer the tomato sauce until thick enough to spread, 10 to 15 minutes. Tear and blot the mozzarella. Divide the mozzarella, Parmesan and toppings into five portions.
4. **Heat the oven:** Heat the oven to 500°F with a rack in the middle. Oil two pizza pans.
5. **Top and bake:** Stretch a ball into a 9-inch round, top it and bake until the rim is golden and the cheese bubbles, 12 to 15 minutes. Shape and top the next pizza while each one bakes.
6. **Toss the salad:** Toss the arugula with its lemon dressing and Parmigiano while the last pizza bakes.
7. **Serve:** Slice each pizza as it comes out and serve it hot with the salad.
