## Meal Overview

For four diners, prepare one full batch of Greek-Style Lamb Burgers with Mint Yogurt and one full Greek Village Salad. The main already includes **680 g lamb, four pitas and one batch of [Mint Tzatziki with Honey](/mise/recipes/mint-tzatziki), about 1 1/2 cups**.

Leave the feta, tomato and greens out of the burgers. The salad already provides 600 g tomatoes, 300 g cucumber, 120 g pepper, 50 g onion, 60 g olives and 150 g feta. The mint yogurt goes in the pitas. This gives rich hot lamb, soft bread, cool tangy sauce and crisp vegetables.

Allow about **75 minutes elapsed and 55 minutes active** for one cook using a skillet that holds two patties at a time. Warm all four pitas in the oven while the second pair cooks.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare the cold components:** Make the full batch of [Mint Tzatziki with Honey](/mise/recipes/mint-tzatziki), about 1 1/2 cups, and refrigerate it. Wash and cut all the village-salad vegetables. Refrigerate them separately from the dressing, feta and olives.
2. **Shape and heat:** Mix the lamb with its onion, garlic, herbs and seasonings. Form four equal patties about 3/4 inch thick. Heat the oven to 350°F / 175°C and preheat the skillet. Refrigerate the second pair of patties while the first pair cooks.
3. **Cook the first pair:** Heat the first teaspoon of skillet oil. Brown both sides, lowering the heat if the crust darkens too fast, until the centers reach 160°F / 71°C. Move the burgers to a plate and cover them loosely.
4. **Finish meat and bread:** Cook the second pair in the remaining teaspoon of oil to 160°F / 71°C. During this batch, warm the four pitas as a foil-wrapped stack using [Warm, Soft Pita Bread’s oven method](/mise/recipes/warm-pita-bread#oven-alternative), without its oil and salt. Keep the stack wrapped until serving.
5. **Dress and assemble:** Toss the salad with its oil, vinegar and oregano, then add the olives and feta. Stir the mint yogurt. Spread 2 to 3 tablespoons inside each pita, add one patty, and put the rest of the sauce on the table. Divide the salad into four generous sides.

## Couscous plate variation

For a plate without bread, **replace all four pitas** with one full batch of [Lemon and Herb Instant Couscous](/mise/recipes/lemon-herb-couscous): 250 g dry instant couscous and 420 ml liquid. Keep the four patties, mint yogurt and full salad. Leave the feta out of the couscous because the salad already has cheese.

The couscous takes about twenty minutes. Prepare its lemon and herbs with the salad vegetables, then heat and hydrate the grain on a separate burner during the second burger batch. Fluff and finish it before dressing the salad. Allow **80 to 85 minutes** for one cook.

## Make ahead and leftovers

Make the sauce and wash the vegetables ahead and refrigerate them. Dress the salad and fill the pitas just before serving. Store cooked meat and sauce separately and use them within 3 to 4 days. The dressed salad keeps until the next day.
