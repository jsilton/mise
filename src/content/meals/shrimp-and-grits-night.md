## Meal Overview

Make **one full batch Shrimp and Grits** and **one full batch Charred Broccoli with Garlic** for four diners. The main starts with one cup dry grits, 450 g peeled shrimp and 120 g bacon. The side uses 450 g prepared broccoli. Divide the cheesy grits and shrimp evenly among four bowls and serve the broccoli alongside.

Tender shrimp and creamy cheddar grits contrast with crisp bacon and the broccoli's browned surfaces and tender stems. Scallions, parsley and lemon keep the rich main lively. The broccoli has a small soy-and-lemon finish of its own. Leave the pepper flakes off the broccoli.

Allow **about ninety minutes elapsed and fifty minutes hands-on** for one cook using the main's stone-ground grits with a forty-five-to-sixty-minute simmer and thawed, peeled shrimp. The whole dinner cooks on the stovetop.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare:** Measure the main's ingredients, grate the cheddar and prepare the scallions, garlic, parsley and lemon. Heat the grits water while doing this. Wash and dry the broccoli, cut its florets and slice the attached stems thin. Set out a 3-quart grits pot, a 12-inch skillet for the bacon and shrimp and a separate 12-inch lidded skillet for the broccoli.
2. **Start grits and bacon:** Start the grits at a low simmer and stir across the bottom every few minutes. Cook the bacon in its skillet while the grits simmer. Transfer the crisp bacon to a plate and leave a tablespoon of fat in the skillet for the two shrimp batches. Set this skillet off the heat until the grits and broccoli are nearly ready.
3. **Cook broccoli in its two batches:** Brown each batch, add its water and cover briefly until the thick stems are tender. Finish with the gently warmed garlic and the soy sauce, then take the pan off the heat and add half the lemon juice. Taste and add the rest. Finish about ten minutes before serving and cover the pan loosely.
4. **Finish the grits before shrimp:** When the grits are tender, fold in the butter, cheddar and pepper and season with the remaining salt. Stir in hot water to keep them soft and spoonable. Cover and keep them warm over the lowest heat.
5. **Cook shrimp last:** Cook the shrimp in two batches of about 225 g until the thickest centers are firm and opaque, 145°F / 63°C. Lift them out. Briefly warm the scallion whites and garlic and loosen the pan with the quarter cup of water. Off the heat, add the lemon, greens and parsley, then return the shrimp just long enough to coat.
6. **Plate:** Divide the grits among four bowls, add the shrimp and pan juices, and scatter the crisp bacon and scallion greens over the top. Serve the broccoli on the side. Serve right away so the shrimp do not keep cooking.

## Equipment and timing adjustments

Two burners handle this sequence because the bacon and shrimp skillet waits off the heat while the broccoli cooks beside the grits. Heat the grits’ adjustment water in a kettle. With only one skillet, cook the bacon first, save its fat, wash the pan, cook the broccoli, wash and dry the pan again, then cook the shrimp. Allow another ten to fifteen minutes for the extra handling.

For coarser grits that take about ninety minutes unsoaked, allow about two hours fifteen minutes to two hours thirty minutes for the meal. Cook the broccoli and shrimp near the end of the grits' simmer.

For five diners, use 1.25× the component ingredients: about 565 g peeled shrimp, 1 1/4 cups dry grits and 565 g broccoli, with the main's cheese, bacon and seasonings also increased. Keep the pan layers loose and add a third round to each skillet.

## Leftovers

Refrigerate shrimp, grits, bacon and broccoli separately and use them within three to four days. Loosen the grits with a little water and add the shrimp near the end of reheating so they stay tender. The dinner contains shellfish, dairy and pork, and the broccoli's soy sauce contains wheat. Use gluten-free tamari for a gluten-free meal.
