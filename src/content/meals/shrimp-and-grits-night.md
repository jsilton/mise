## Meal Overview

Make **one full batch Shrimp and Grits** and **one full batch Charred Broccoli with Garlic** for four diners. The main starts with one cup dry grits, 450 g peeled shrimp and 120 g bacon; the side uses 450 g prepared broccoli. Divide the cheesy grits and shrimp evenly among four bowls and serve the broccoli alongside. The main includes its starch and light pan juices, so no extra sauce or bread is required.

Tender shrimp and creamy cheddar grits contrast with crisp bacon and the broccoli's browned surfaces and tender stems. Scallions, parsley and measured lemon keep the rich main lively. The broccoli has a small soy-and-lemon finish of its own; begin with half its listed lemon juice and taste before adding more. Its optional pepper flakes can be omitted.

Allow **about seventy-five minutes elapsed and fifty minutes hands-on** for one cook using the main's named Palmetto Farms stone-ground grits and thawed, peeled shrimp. A coarser product with a longer cooking method changes this schedule. This dinner uses the stovetop: the linked broccoli is browned and briefly steamed in a skillet, so an oven does not need preheating.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Check products and prepare:** Check the grits package first, then measure the main's ingredients, grate cheddar and prepare scallions, garlic, parsley and lemon. Heat the grits water while doing this. Wash and dry broccoli, cut its florets and thin the attached stems as its recipe directs. Keep raw shrimp cold and use clean tools for the vegetables. Set out a 3-quart grits pot, a 12-inch bacon/shrimp skillet and a separate 12-inch lidded broccoli skillet.
2. **Start grits and bacon:** Start the grits' low simmer and stir across the bottom every few minutes. Cook the bacon in its skillet while the grits simmer. Transfer crisp bacon to a plate and measure the tablespoon of fat needed for the two shrimp batches; excess fat is not part of the pan coating. Park this skillet off heat until the grits and broccoli are nearly ready.
3. **Cook broccoli in its two batches:** Use the linked side's pan-browning and short covered-water stages, retaining its separate oil, water, garlic and seasoning quantities. Test thick stems rather than stopping at dark edges. Finish with gently warmed garlic and the soy sauce, then take off heat and add half the lemon juice. Taste before the rest. Plan to finish about ten minutes before serving; a brief loose cover keeps it warm but softens some surface contrast.
4. **Finish the grits before shrimp:** Taste a cooled spoonful of grits to confirm the centers are tender, then fold in the main's butter, cheddar and pepper. Taste before the remaining salt. Use its listed hot water to keep the grits soft and spoonable. Keep covered and gently warm above 140°F / 60°C during the short shrimp finish; no long holding period is planned. If the grits are not ready, delay the seafood.
5. **Cook shrimp last:** Follow the main's two roughly 225 g batches and check the thickest centers for 145°F / 63°C and firm, pearly, opaque flesh. Remove cooked shrimp while briefly warming scallion whites and garlic and loosening the pan with the main's measured quarter-cup water. Finish off heat with its lemon, greens and parsley, then return shrimp only long enough to coat. Do not add another helping of butter, unlisted stock or a separate sauce.
6. **Plate promptly:** Divide grits among four bowls, add shrimp and pan juices, and scatter over the reserved crisp bacon and scallion greens. Serve broccoli on the side. Taste a complete bite before any further seasoning: bacon, cheddar and the broccoli's teaspoon of soy already supply salt. Serve immediately so the shrimp do not continue cooking while dinner waits.

## Equipment and timing adjustments

Two active burners can handle this sequence because the bacon/shrimp skillet waits off heat while broccoli cooks beside the grits. Use a kettle or microwave for the grits’ adjustment water, or heat it before the broccoli occupies the second burner and keep it covered in an insulated jug. Three burners provide more flexibility, but one cook still needs to watch the short shrimp finish. With only one suitable skillet, cook bacon first, save its measured fat, wash the pan, cook broccoli, wash and dry again, then cook shrimp; allow another ten to fifteen minutes for the extra handling. Keep raw shrimp refrigerated until needed.

For coarse grits such as unsoaked Anson Mills, allow roughly two hours fifteen minutes to two hours thirty minutes for the meal and follow that mill's liquid and stirring method. Cook broccoli and shrimp near the end, not while the grits still have a long simmer ahead. The main's quick-grits alternative shortens the starch stage but does not remove chopping, bacon cooking or two broccoli and shrimp batches; do not turn it into a promised twenty-five-minute dinner.

For five diners, use 1.25× the component ingredients: about 565 g peeled shrimp, 1 1/4 cups dry grits and 565 g broccoli, with the main's cheese, bacon and seasonings also increased. Keep the specified loose pan layers and allow extra rounds where needed; the seventy-five-minute estimate is for the four-person batch.

## Leftovers

Refrigerate shrimp, grits, bacon and broccoli separately in shallow containers within two hours, or one hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below. Use within three to four days and reheat cooked components to 165°F / 74°C, following the component pages. Loosen grits with a little water and add shrimp near the end of reheating to limit further toughening. Expect softer broccoli and firmer shrimp; this dinner is best freshly served. It contains shellfish, dairy and pork, and the default broccoli's soy sauce contains wheat; use its specified gluten-free tamari alternative if needed.
