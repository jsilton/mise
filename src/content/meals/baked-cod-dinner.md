## Meal Overview

Lemon-butter cod with a light panko crust, golden garlic potatoes and tender broccoli dressed with garlic oil. The fish supplies the sauce; spoon it beneath the cod so its crumb topping stays crisp.

For four diners, make **one full batch of Cod with Lemon Panko, half a batch of Garlic Roasted Potatoes and one full batch of Steamed Broccoli with Garlic Oil**. Choose four fish portions suited to the diners. The potato side uses **1 1/2 lb potatoes, 2 tbsp olive oil, 3/4 tsp kosher salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1 tbsp minced garlic and 1 tbsp parsley**. The broccoli supplies **450 g trimmed vegetables** across four small sides. Use each component's remaining quantities as written; no extra sauce is necessary.

Allow about **85 minutes elapsed and 45 minutes hands-on**, with longer allowed if the potatoes need it. Use a rimmed half-sheet pan for potatoes, a shallow baking dish for fish, a steamer saucepan and a small pan for garlic oil. The oven must hold the potato pan and fish dish on separate racks without blocking air circulation. Both roast at **400°F / 205°C**.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the potatoes:** Heat the oven and prepare the half batch, keeping the recipe's piece size unchanged. Reserve one-quarter of its oil for the late garlic finish. Spread the potatoes in one layer and begin their **45–60-minute roast**, following the turning and tenderness checks.
2. **Prepare the remaining components while potatoes roast:** Trim broccoli, mince the separate amounts of garlic, and chop parsley. Prepare the lemon and crumb mixture for the cod. Handle the raw fish last, refrigerate the assembled dish until needed, and clean raw-contact tools before returning to vegetables.
3. **Check potatoes before starting fish:** After about **40 minutes roasting**, test a thick potato center. Start the cod when potatoes are nearly tender and likely to finish in the next **15–20 minutes**. If potatoes remain firm, give them more time before adding fish. Keep the cod on the center rack and potatoes on the other rack; rotate the potato pan if browning is uneven.
4. **Finish potatoes and steam broccoli:** Add the potatoes' garlic with their reserved oil only near the end, as their recipe directs. Start heating steamer water as the fish enters the oven. Steam broccoli for its **4–6-minute** tenderness window and warm its garlic oil on the other burner under close attention. Drain and dress when tender. Do these short steps sequentially if checking fish or turning potatoes needs your attention.
5. **Check each fish portion:** Begin checking cod at **10–12 minutes** and remove individual pieces once their thickest centers reach **145°F / 63°C**. Many pieces take **12–20 minutes**; thickness and the shared oven can change this. Crumb color is not the doneness test. Do not leave ready fish baking while waiting for a side.
6. **Serve:** Toss parsley through the potatoes. Plate one fish portion with a quarter of each side, spooning lemon butter around the fish. Serve promptly. A brief uncovered wait preserves potato and crumb texture better than a tight cover; avoid planning a long hold.

## Make Ahead and Leftovers

Prepare vegetables and keep them refrigerated ahead if useful; dry potato surfaces before roasting. Make the fish close to serving. If the oven cannot hold both dishes, finish the potatoes first, then bake fish while steaming broccoli; allow about **20 minutes extra** and expect the potatoes to lose some crispness. For a longer wait, keep cooked food at **140°F / 60°C or above** rather than leaving it on the counter.

Refrigerate components separately in shallow containers within **2 hours**, or **1 hour above 90°F / 32°C**, at **40°F / 4°C or below**. Use within **3–4 days** and reheat leftovers to **165°F / 74°C**. Fish is best freshly cooked; reheating firms the flesh and softens its topping.
