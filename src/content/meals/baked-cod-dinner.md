## Meal Overview

Lemon-butter cod with a light panko crust, golden garlic potatoes and tender broccoli dressed with garlic oil. The fish supplies the sauce. Spoon it beneath the cod so its crumb topping stays crisp.

For four diners, make **one full batch of Cod with Lemon Panko, half a batch of Garlic Roasted Potatoes and one full batch of Steamed Broccoli with Garlic Oil**. The potato side uses **1 1/2 lb potatoes, 2 tbsp olive oil, 3/4 tsp kosher salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1 tbsp minced garlic and 1 tbsp parsley**. The broccoli supplies **450 g trimmed vegetables** across four small sides.

Allow about **85 minutes elapsed and 45 minutes hands-on**. Use a rimmed half-sheet pan for potatoes, a shallow baking dish for fish, a steamer saucepan and a small pan for garlic oil. The potato pan and the fish dish go on separate racks. Both roast at **400°F / 205°C**.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the potatoes:** Heat the oven and prepare the half batch. Reserve one-quarter of its oil for the late garlic finish. Spread the potatoes in one layer and begin their **45 to 60 minute roast**.
2. **Prepare the remaining components while potatoes roast:** Trim broccoli, mince the separate amounts of garlic, and chop parsley. Prepare the lemon and crumb mixture for the cod. Assemble the fish dish last and refrigerate it until the potatoes are nearly tender.
3. **Check potatoes before starting fish:** After about **40 minutes roasting**, test a thick potato center. Start the cod when the potatoes are nearly tender, with **15 to 20 minutes** left to roast. Keep the cod on the center rack and potatoes on the other rack. Rotate the potato pan if browning is uneven.
4. **Finish potatoes and steam broccoli:** Add the potatoes' garlic with their reserved oil near the end of the roast. Start heating steamer water as the fish enters the oven. Steam broccoli for its **4 to 6 minute** tenderness window and warm its garlic oil on the other burner. Drain and dress when tender.
5. **Check each fish portion:** Begin checking cod at **10 to 12 minutes** and remove individual pieces once their thickest centers reach **145°F / 63°C**. Most pieces take **12 to 20 minutes**.
6. **Serve:** Toss parsley through the potatoes. Plate one fish portion with a quarter of each side, spooning lemon butter around the fish. Serve right away.

## Make Ahead and Leftovers

Trim the broccoli and cut the potatoes ahead and refrigerate them. Dry the potato surfaces before roasting. Make the fish close to serving. In an oven that cannot hold both dishes, finish the potatoes first, then bake the fish while the broccoli steams. That adds about **20 minutes**, and the potatoes lose some crispness.

Refrigerate the components separately and use them within **3 to 4 days**. Reheating firms the fish and softens its topping.
