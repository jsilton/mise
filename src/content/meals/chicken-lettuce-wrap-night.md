## Meal Overview

Warm hoisin chicken in crisp butter lettuce with a cold, sharp cucumber salad. It is a light dinner for four that comes together in 45 minutes. For five, make one and a quarter batches of the filling with 1 1/4 lb chicken and buy a second head of lettuce.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the cucumbers:** Smash and salt the cucumbers and let them drain in the fridge for 15 minutes. Mix their dressing.
2. **Prep the wraps:** Wash and dry the lettuce and keep it cold. Mix the hoisin sauce and dice the water chestnuts and shiitakes.
3. **Cook the filling:** Brown the chicken over medium-high heat, 5 to 6 minutes. Add the mushrooms and aromatics, then glaze with the sauce until the chicken reaches 165°F.
4. **Dress the cucumbers:** Toss the drained cucumbers with their dressing and top them with the sesame seeds and scallion.
5. **Serve:** Bring the hot filling, cold lettuce and cucumbers to the table and fill the wraps as you eat.
