## Meal Overview

Warm chicken filling in crisp lettuce with a cool, sharp cucumber salad. Make one full batch each of [Chicken Lettuce Wraps](/mise/recipes/chicken-lettuce-wraps/) and [Smashed Cucumber Salad](/mise/recipes/smashed-cucumber-salad/) for four light dinners: about three wraps and a cucumber side per person.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the cucumber first:** Allow about 45 minutes for one cook. Crack and cut the full 600 g cucumber batch, salt it with the component's measured draining salt, and let it drain for 15 minutes. Mix its dressing during the drain.
2. **Prepare the wraps during the drain:** Wash and thoroughly dry about 12 usable lettuce leaves, then refrigerate them. Measure the filling's sauce and prepare its celery, scallions, ginger and garlic. The standard filling uses celery; water chestnuts are a substitution, not an additional required ingredient.
3. **Cook the filling:** Follow the lettuce-wrap recipe in a wide skillet. Brown the chicken, add celery and scallion whites, then the aromatics and sauce. Check several thick clusters reach 165°F / 74°C before finishing with sesame oil and scallion greens.
4. **Finish the salad:** Drain and blot the cucumbers; taste and dress as directed in the salad recipe. Keep the dressed salad chilled until the filling is ready.
5. **Serve promptly:** Put the warm filling and cold lettuce on the table separately, with the cucumber salad beside them. Add the component's optional ready-to-eat crispy noodles only if wanted. Fill the leaves as you eat to keep them crisp.

## Notes

The 45-minute estimate includes the cucumber drain and both recipes' preparation. Do not compress it into a ten-minute chicken cook plus ten minutes of preparation; the leaves and salad need their own handling time.
