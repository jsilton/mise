## Meal Overview

Seared, soy-marinated flank steak sliced over romaine and butter lettuce with avocado, radishes, pepitas, crispy shallots and a lemon vinaigrette. Start the marinade two hours ahead and the rest is quick.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Marinate the steaks:** Score both flank steaks, 2 1/2 lb in all, and turn them in a double batch of the marinade. Refrigerate them for 2 hours.
2. **Fry the shallots:** Make the crispy shallots while the steaks marinate.
3. **Make the vinaigrette:** Shake the lemon vinaigrette in a jar and refrigerate it. Wash and tear the lettuces.
4. **Sear the steaks:** Pat the steaks dry. Sear them one at a time in a smoking-hot cast-iron skillet until deeply browned and 130°F in the thickest part, 4 to 5 minutes per side.
5. **Rest and slice:** Rest the steaks for 10 minutes. Slice the cucumbers, avocados and radishes while they rest, then slice the steaks thinly across the grain.
6. **Build the salad:** Toss the greens with half the vinaigrette. Add the vegetables and steak, drizzle with the rest and scatter the pepitas and crispy shallots over the top.
