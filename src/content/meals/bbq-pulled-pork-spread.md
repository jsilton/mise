## Meal Overview

Pulled pork from the weekend's slow cooker becomes a weeknight plate. Warm buttermilk cornbread, charred corn with cotija and lime, and garlicky broccoli go beside it. The pork is already cooked, so dinner takes an hour.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Bake the cornbread:** Heat the oven to 400°F and the grill to medium-high. Mix the cornbread batter and bake it until golden at the edges, 20 to 25 minutes.
2. **Reheat the pork:** Put the pork and its juices in a covered pan over medium-low heat. Stir now and then until it is steaming hot all the way through, about 15 minutes.
3. **Grill the corn:** Mix the crema coating while the grill heats. Grill the corn, turning every 2 to 3 minutes, until charred in spots, 8 to 10 minutes.
4. **Steam the broccoli:** Steam the broccoli while the cornbread cools in its pan for 10 minutes. Toss it with the warm garlic oil.
5. **Serve:** Brush the corn with the coating and top it with cotija, cilantro and chili powder. Cut the cornbread into squares and set everything out family-style.
