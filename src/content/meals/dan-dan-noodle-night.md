## Meal Overview

Sichuan sesame-chili noodles with seasoned pork and preserved mustard greens, alongside crisp garlic-and-vinegar cucumber salad. Prepare the sauce and toppings before cooking the noodles so each bowl can be mixed and eaten promptly.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the cucumber salad:** Make one full four-side-portion recipe, using its 600 g cucumber and separate measured draining salt. Crack and cut the cucumber, then begin its 15-minute salt-and-drain step, preferably chilled. Mix its complete dressing during the drain; keep raw-pork tools separate.
2. **Prepare the noodle sauce:** Make the full four-generous-portion Dan Dan formula with Chinese roasted sesame paste, light soy, sugar, five-spice powder, Sichuan pepper, prepared chili oil and garlic. Keep its measured whole-batch noodle water out until cooking water is available. Vinegar is optional in the noodle recipe; it is part of the cucumber dressing.
3. **Cook the toppings:** Follow the main recipe's separately allocated pork and yacai oil. Brown and season the pork to 160°F, then cook the preserved greens as directed. Keep the ready toppings and sauce at hand before starting the noodles.
4. **Finish the salad:** Drain and blot the cucumber without squeezing it into pulp. Follow its taste-and-dress instruction, then add scallion and sesame seeds. Keep chilled briefly if noodle cooking is not finished.
5. **Cook and assemble the noodles:** Blanch optional bok choy and cook the noodles by their package. Reserve hot cooking water before draining. Whisk the main recipe's measured whole-batch water into the whole sauce, loosening only as needed, then divide sauce, noodles, greens and toppings among four bowls. The water amount is not repeated in every bowl.
6. **Serve promptly:** Add the optional peanut, scallion and chili-oil garnishes. Toss each noodle bowl thoroughly just before eating and serve the cucumber salad alongside.

## Notes

- Prepared chili oil is included in this timing. Making a separate chili oil recipe adds time; follow its own complete method and storage guidance.
- The main plans four generous bowls and the salad four side portions. For the main's complete milder saved variation, use all of that variation's quantities and method rather than selectively reducing the main formula.
- Store prepared noodle components separately as directed by their recipe. The cucumber salad is best freshly dressed; its overnight texture differs. Follow each recipe's cooling and leftover guidance.
