## Meal Overview

Sichuan sesame and chili noodles with seasoned pork and preserved mustard greens, beside a cold garlic and black vinegar cucumber salad. The sauce and toppings are ready before the noodles boil, so each bowl is mixed and eaten hot. For five, divide the pound of noodles and the pork among five bowls.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the cucumbers:** Smash and salt the cucumbers and let them drain in the fridge for 15 minutes.
2. **Mix the sauces:** Stir the sesame and chili sauce for the noodles. Mix the cucumber dressing.
3. **Cook the pork topping:** Brown the pork, season it until dark and glossy and stir in the fried preserved greens.
4. **Boil the greens and noodles:** Blanch the bok choy for 1 minute in a large pot of boiling water. Cook the noodles in the same water and save a mug of it.
5. **Dress the cucumbers:** Toss the cucumbers with their dressing and top them with the sesame seeds and scallion.
6. **Build the bowls:** Whisk hot noodle water into the sauce and divide it among four bowls. Add the noodles, bok choy and pork, top with the peanuts and scallions and toss at the table.
