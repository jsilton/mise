## Meal Overview

A full-day project. Pork bones boil hard for 8 to 12 hours until the broth turns creamy and opaque, then four bowls come together in the last half hour with tare, fresh noodles, chashu and jammy eggs. Start by 7 AM for dinner at 7 PM. The recipe makes four bowls and does not scale in one pot. For five, cook five portions of noodles and five eggs and divide the broth among five bowls, with smaller bowls for the kids.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Blanch the bones:** Boil the pork bones and shoulder for 2 minutes. Drain them, rinse off the scum and scrub the pot.
2. **Boil the broth:** Cover the bones with the water, bring to a rolling boil and skim the foam for 20 minutes. Add the ginger, onion, garlic and shiitake and boil, partly covered, for 8 to 12 hours. Top up with boiling water to keep the bones covered.
3. **Soak the pork:** About 2 1/2 hours into the boil, lift out the shoulder and refrigerate it in the soy sauce and mirin.
4. **Make the tare:** While the broth boils, whisk the soy sauce, miso, mirin and sesame oil.
5. **Cook the eggs:** In the last hour, boil the eggs for 7 minutes, chill them in ice water and peel them.
6. **Strain and steep:** Strain the broth into a clean pot when it is creamy and opaque. Steep the kombu in it off the heat for 10 minutes.
7. **Ready the toppings:** Blanch the bok choy, slice and warm the pork and slice the scallions and nori.
8. **Build the bowls:** Bring the broth back to a boil and stir it into the tare in four warm bowls. Boil the noodles, divide them among the bowls and add the toppings.
