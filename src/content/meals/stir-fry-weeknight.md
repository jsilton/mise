## Meal Overview

Chicken and crisp snow peas in a light garlic sauce, with plain short-grain rice and a small bowl of salted edamame. For **four diners, make one full batch of each component**: 340 g chicken and 450 g snow peas, 300 g dry rice, and 450 g edamame in their pods. The chicken is a light portion. Rice and edible edamame beans round out the meal. The pod weight includes the shells, which are discarded.

Allow **about 70 minutes elapsed and 40 minutes hands-on** with thawed chicken. The rice's 30-minute soak and 10-minute rest are part of that time. Use a covered rice saucepan, a separate edamame saucepan and a wide skillet. Cook and drain the edamame before starting the stir-fry. They are served warm.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Rinse and soak rice:** Rinse and drain the rice, then soak it in its cooking water for 30 minutes.
2. **Prepare the main during the soak:** Trim and dry the snow peas, measure the sauce into its own bowl and keep the finishing sesame oil separate. Slice the chicken evenly, coat it and refrigerate it for its 10-minute rest.
3. **Cook rice and edamame:** At the end of the soak, bring the rice to a boil, cover and reduce to a gentle simmer. Meanwhile, boil the edamame in the separate pot for their package time until a bean is tender. Drain thoroughly and season with half the salt, then taste and add the rest. Put the pods in a serving bowl and set out a bowl for the empty pods.
4. **Rest rice and stir-fry:** Once the rice has absorbed its water, take it off the heat for its covered 10-minute rest. Cook the chicken and the snow peas separately. Return the chicken and its juices to the peas, re-stir the sauce, add it and simmer until glossy and the thickest chicken slices reach **165°F / 74°C**.
5. **Finish and serve:** Turn off the heat and stir in the sesame oil. Fluff the rice and divide it among four bowls, then add the chicken, peas and sauce. Serve the edamame alongside.

## Timing and Leftovers

The rice stays covered after its rest while the main finishes. Do all the slicing and sauce measuring before heating the pan, because pausing to find an ingredient overcooks the peas.

Refrigerate leftovers separately and use them within three to four days. Snow peas soften on reheating. Edamame keep their texture for two days.
