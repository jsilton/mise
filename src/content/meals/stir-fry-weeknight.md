## Meal Overview

Chicken and crisp snow peas in a light garlic sauce, with plain short-grain rice and a small bowl of salted edamame. For **four diners, make one full batch of each component**: 340 g chicken and 450 g snow peas, 300 g dry rice, and 450 g edamame in their pods. The chicken is a light portion; rice and edible edamame beans round out the meal. The pod weight includes the shells, which are discarded.

Allow **about 70 minutes elapsed and 40 minutes hands-on** with thawed chicken. The rice's 30-minute soak and 10-minute rest belong in the dinner schedule. Use a covered rice saucepan, a separate edamame saucepan and a wide skillet or wok. Cook and drain the edamame before starting the attended stir-fry; they can be served warm rather than piping hot.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Rinse and soak rice:** Follow the rice recipe, soaking the drained rice in its measured cooking water for 30 minutes. Keep that water for cooking. A rice cooker may use a different cycle; follow its matched cup and fill lines and allow its actual duration.
2. **Prepare the main during the soak:** Trim and dry the snow peas, measure the sauce into its own bowl and keep the finishing sesame oil separate. Slice the chicken evenly, coat it as directed and refrigerate for its 10-minute rest. Clean raw-chicken contact surfaces before handling cooked food.
3. **Cook rice and edamame:** At the end of the soak, bring rice to a boil, cover and reduce to its gentle simmer. Meanwhile, boil the edamame in the separate pot for the package's required time, including any longer minimum. Check a cooled bean for tenderness, drain thoroughly and season gradually. Put the pods in a serving bowl and provide a discard bowl. Finish this task before heating the stir-fry pan.
4. **Rest rice and stir-fry:** Once the rice has absorbed its water, take it off heat for its covered 10-minute rest. Cook chicken and snow peas separately according to the main recipe, using batches if necessary. Return chicken and its juices, re-stir the sauce and simmer until glossy and several thick chicken slices reach **165°F / 74°C**. Remove peas temporarily if they become tender before the chicken is ready.
5. **Finish and serve:** Turn off the stir-fry heat before adding its measured sesame oil. Fluff the rice and divide among four bowls, then add chicken, peas and sauce. Serve edamame alongside; eat the beans and discard the pods. If putting edamame directly in the rice bowls, shell them first and allow extra preparation time.

## Timing and Leftovers

The rice can remain covered briefly after its rest while the main finishes. Keep all slicing and sauce measuring ahead of the hot-pan stage: pausing to find an ingredient can overcook the peas. Extra chicken batches or a longer edamame package time can extend the schedule; finish those beans first rather than dividing attention between two fast-cooking pans.

Refrigerate leftovers in separate shallow containers within two hours, or one hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below. Use within three to four days and reheat to **165°F / 74°C** throughout. Snow peas soften on reheating; edamame are best within two days for texture. Cool rice promptly rather than leaving it in a covered pot at room temperature.
