## Meal Overview

Make one full six-serving Turkey and Sweet Potato Chili and one 8-inch pan of Buttermilk Cornbread. Cut the bread into twelve small squares, serve one per diner and save the other six. The chili already includes 900 g raw turkey, 500 g sweet potato and about 510 g drained beans, so the bread is a small accompaniment.

Top the six bowls with 60 g Cheddar, 60 g sour cream, 150 g avocado flesh and 1/4 cup cilantro. Allow about 1 hour 40 minutes elapsed and about 70 minutes active work for one cook, including preparation, two browning batches and bread assembly.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare chili ingredients:** Cut sweet potato evenly, dice onion and measure the tomato, broth and spices. Drain beans but keep them for the later stage. Grate the cheese and chop the cilantro. Refrigerate the toppings.
2. **Brown and build:** Brown the turkey in two batches, then soften the onion and sweet potato and briefly cook the spices. Add the turkey, tomatoes and broth. Bring to a gentle, partly covered simmer.
3. **Start cornbread during the simmer:** Heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C and grease an 8-inch metal pan. Measure the dry and wet mixtures separately. Combine them when the oven is ready, then bake until the center is set, 20 to 25 minutes.
4. **Finish the chili:** Stir occasionally until a sweet-potato cube is tender and the turkey reaches 165°F / 74°C. Add the drained beans near the end. Take the pot off the heat and cover it once the texture is right.
5. **Cool bread and finish bowls:** Cool the cornbread for ten minutes. During that time, season the chili with salt and lime. Dice the avocado and set the toppings out in small bowls.
6. **Serve and save:** Cut twelve small cornbread squares. Serve one with each of six chili bowls. Cool and wrap the six remaining squares and freeze them.

## Notes

Refrigerate leftover chili and store the toppings separately.
