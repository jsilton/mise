## Meal Overview

Make one full six-serving Turkey and Sweet Potato Chili and one 8-inch pan of Buttermilk Cornbread. Cut the bread into twelve small squares, serve one per diner and save the other six. The chili already includes 900 g raw turkey, 500 g sweet potato and about 510 g drained beans, so the bread is a small accompaniment rather than another full starch course.

Use only the chili's listed optional toppings: up to 60 g Cheddar, 60 g sour cream, 150 g avocado flesh and 1/4 cup cilantro across the six bowls. Choose those wanted; the meal does not require every topping. Allow about 1 hour 40 minutes elapsed and roughly 70 minutes active work for one cook, including preparation, two browning batches and bread assembly.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare chili ingredients:** Cut sweet potato evenly, dice onion and measure the tomato, broth and spices. Drain beans but keep them for the later stage. Prepare any cheese and cilantro; refrigerate toppings and slice avocado close to serving.
2. **Brown and build:** Follow the main's two turkey batches, then soften onion and sweet potato and briefly cook the spices. Add the turkey, tomatoes and measured broth. Bring to the gentle, partly covered simmer.
3. **Start cornbread during the simmer:** Heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C and grease the specified 8-inch metal pan. Measure the dry and wet mixtures separately. Combine only when the oven is ready, then bake for the bread's 20–25-minute window and actual center-set endpoint.
4. **Check chili independently:** Stir occasionally and test a sweet-potato cube for tenderness and substantial turkey pieces for 165°F / 74°C. Add the drained beans near the end as directed. Stop cooking once the texture is right; the bread's schedule is not a reason to dissolve all the potato. A brief covered wait off heat is preferable to continued hard simmering.
5. **Cool bread and finish bowls:** Give cornbread its full ten-minute cooling period. During that time, set chili consistency, then adjust salt and lime. Dice avocado if using and arrange the measured toppings in small bowls.
6. **Serve and save:** Cut twelve small cornbread squares. Serve one with each of six chili bowls. Cool and wrap the six remaining squares for freezing or short storage according to the bread recipe.

## Notes

There is no tomato paste, scallion or jalapeño in the planned component lists. Use the measured mild chili blend rather than assuming an unspecified powder has a child-friendly heat level. Chill leftover chili promptly in shallow containers and store toppings separately; next-day flavor and texture should be judged after reheating, not promised to improve automatically.
