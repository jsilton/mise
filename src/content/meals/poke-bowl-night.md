## Meal Overview

Make **one complete batch of Tuna Poke Bowls with Chile and Cucumber Salad** for four bowls. The main already includes four cups sushi rice, 450 g prepared tuna, a half batch of Oi Muchim starting with 300 g cucumber, 75 g shelled edamame, one avocado and its garnishes. Divide the entire prepared cucumber half batch among the bowls. Each diner receives about one cup rice, 113 g tuna, cucumber from 75 g raw starting weight, a quarter avocado and a small edamame topping.

The Korean cucumber salad supplies chile, garlic, acidity and crunch. Nori supplies a dry contrast when added just before eating. Its dressing and the tuna's shoyu dressing both season the bowl, so leave pooled liquid behind.

Follow the main's raw-tuna sourcing note before shopping. FDA advises children, pregnant people, older adults and people with weakened immune systems to avoid raw seafood. For dinner with those diners, [Shrimp and Grits Night](/mise/meals/shrimp-and-grits-night) is a fully cooked seafood menu for four with a five-person adjustment.

## Cooking Strategy

Allow **about ninety minutes elapsed and forty minutes hands-on**, including fresh sushi rice, cucumber salting and cooked edamame chilling. Start with thawed fish. Use a 3-quart rice pot, a small saucepan for the beans, a colander for the cucumber, a wide rice bowl and separate boards for vegetables and fish.

1. **Start sushi rice first:** Make [Sushi Rice (Shari)](/mise/recipes/master-sushi-rice) with its 400 g dry-rice batch: soak, cook covered, rest and season. This takes about eighty minutes and makes more than the four cooked cups needed. Refrigerate the extra rice.
2. **Cook the beans early:** During the rice soak, boil the 75 g frozen shelled edamame for their package time. Drain them, spread them in a shallow container and refrigerate until chilled.
3. **Prepare the cucumber component:** Make half the [Oi Muchim](/mise/recipes/korean-pickled-cucumbers) recipe, starting with 300 g cucumber and halving every seasoning. Salt the cucumber for twenty minutes while preparing the dressing and other toppings. Drain and gently blot it, then refrigerate the cucumber and its dressing separately. Toss them just before serving.
4. **Prepare the finish before fish:** Slice the scallions and cut the nori, keeping the nori dry. Mix the tuna's dressing in its own bowl. Once the rice is tender, season it with its vinegar, then measure four cooked cups and let them cool until barely warm. Dice the avocado and toss the cucumber salad.
5. **Cut and season tuna while rice cools:** Keep the tuna refrigerated until this step. Cut the fish into 1/2-inch cubes, coat it with its dressing and refrigerate it for five to ten minutes while arranging the bowls.
6. **Assemble and eat:** Divide the rice, tuna, cucumber salad, beans and avocado among four bowls. Lift the tuna and cucumber from their pooled dressing. Add scallions, sesame and nori at the table, with the soy and spicy mayonnaise alongside.

## Timing choices and portions

The ninety-minute plan fits the cucumber and beans into the rice soaking and cooking. It does not include thawing frozen tuna in the refrigerator, which takes overnight. Leave the tuna refrigerated until the other components are ready.

Plain rice in place of sushi rice gives a less sweet and acidic bowl. The plain-rice recipe takes about 65 minutes, and the whole meal about eighty. With rice, cucumber salad and beans made ahead and refrigerated, allow about 35 to 45 minutes, including reheating the rice and cooling it briefly before the fish goes on. Chilled cucumber salad keeps until the next day.

For more diners, scale the whole main with its cucumber salad and cook a larger batch of rice.

## Leftovers

Eat the raw tuna the day it is cut. Assembled bowls do not keep.

Refrigerate leftover cooked rice and beans separately and use them within three to four days. The cucumber salad keeps until the next day. Keep the nori dry and separate. This meal contains fish, soy and sesame. Ordinary soy sauce adds wheat, and the mayonnaise adds egg.
