## Meal Overview

Make **one complete batch of Tuna Poke Bowls with Chile and Cucumber Salad** for four bowls. The main already includes four cups sushi rice, 450 g prepared tuna, a half batch of Oi Muchim starting with 300 g cucumber, 75 g shelled edamame, one avocado and its garnishes. Divide the entire prepared cucumber half batch among the bowls. Each diner receives approximately one cup rice, 113 g tuna, cucumber from 75 g raw starting weight, a quarter avocado and a small edamame topping.

The Korean cucumber salad supplies chile, garlic, acidity and crunch. Nori supplies a dry contrast when added just before eating. Its dressing and the tuna's shoyu dressing both season the bowl, so leave pooled liquid behind and taste before offering the main's measured extra soy or spicy mayonnaise.

Follow the main's raw-tuna sourcing note before shopping. Raw seafood is a deliberate choice; FDA advises children, pregnant people, older adults and people with weakened immune systems to avoid it. For dinner with those diners, [Shrimp and Grits Night](/mise/meals/shrimp-and-grits-night) is a fully cooked seafood menu for four with a five-person adjustment. This raw poke menu does not include a cooked child plate.

## Cooking Strategy

Allow **about ninety minutes elapsed and forty minutes hands-on**, including fresh sushi rice, cucumber salting and cooked edamame chilling. Start with thawed fish. Use a 3-quart rice pot, a second small saucepan or package-directed microwave equipment for beans, a colander for cucumber, a wide rice bowl and separate vegetable/fish cutting equipment.

1. **Start sushi rice first:** Make [Sushi Rice (Shari)](/mise/recipes/master-sushi-rice), following its 400 g dry-rice batch, soak, covered cooking, rest and seasoning. This takes about eighty minutes and makes more than the four cooked cups needed. The meal uses one rice preparation; keep the extra rice separate for prompt refrigeration.
2. **Cook the beans early:** During the rice soak, cook the main's 75 g frozen shelled edamame according to its package. Drain, spread in a shallow container and refrigerate promptly until chilled. The weight is edible beans, not pods; a separate full pod-edamame side is not part of this menu.
3. **Prepare the cucumber component:** Make half the [Oi Muchim](/mise/recipes/korean-pickled-cucumbers) recipe, starting with 300 g cucumber and halving every seasoning. Allow its twenty-minute salting period while preparing the dressing and other toppings. Drain and gently blot as directed, then refrigerate the cucumber and its dressing separately if the rice still needs time. Toss them near service and use the whole half batch, lifted from excess liquid. It is a fresh salad, not preserved pickles.
4. **Prepare the finish before fish:** Slice scallions and cut the nori, keeping nori dry. Mix the tuna's clean dressing in its own bowl. Once rice is tender, finish its vinegar seasoning, then measure four cooked cups and let the serving portion become barely warm. Refrigerate surplus rice promptly. Dice avocado and finish the cucumber salad before switching to raw-fish equipment. Carry out the next step during the rice's final cooling period.
5. **Cut and season tuna while rice cools:** Keep tuna at 40°F / 4°C or below until this step. Follow the main's supplier and refrigerator-thawing guidance, removing vacuum packaging before refrigerator thawing. Cut the fish into 1/2-inch cubes, coat with its dressing and refrigerate for about five to ten minutes while arranging bowls. Clean and sanitize the raw-fish work area. The dressing and its short wait do not cook or sanitize the tuna.
6. **Assemble and eat:** Divide rice, tuna, cucumber salad, beans and avocado among four bowls. Lift tuna and cucumber from pooled dressing. Add scallions, sesame and nori at the table; offer the main's measured optional soy and mayonnaise with clean spoons. Add fish to rice only when ready to eat.

## Timing choices and portions

The ninety-minute plan overlaps cucumber preparation and bean cooking/chilling with rice soaking and cooking. It includes the rice rest and brief cooling, not refrigerator thawing of frozen tuna, which often needs overnight. A slower cooker, rice needing correction or beans still warm at service extends the schedule; leave tuna refrigerated until the components are ready.

Using the main's four-cup plain-rice substitution gives a less sweet and acidic bowl; keep the cucumber salad and omit the shari preparation. Follow the actual plain-rice recipe's approximately 65-minute process and allow roughly eighty minutes for the whole meal. With rice, cucumber salad and beans already safely refrigerated, allow roughly 35–45 minutes including reheating rice to 165°F / 74°C and brief cooling before fish is added. Chilled cucumber salad is best used by the next day.

The four portions include modest bean toppings. For more diners, scale the complete main and its cucumber dependency, and measure the cooked rice allocation. Larger rice batches need a suitable pot or separate batches and may take longer; keep fish refrigerated in small portions during preparation.

## Leftovers

Prepare raw tuna for same-day service within any earlier supplier use limit. Keep it cold until assembly; do not save assembled raw bowls as meal-prep lunches. Raw-contact dressing is not a clean condiment. Count preparation and eating time together, and discard perishables left out of refrigeration beyond two hours, or one hour above 90°F / 32°C.

Refrigerate untouched cooked rice and beans promptly in separate shallow containers at 40°F / 4°C or below; use within three to four days and reheat cooked leftovers to 165°F / 74°C. Refrigerate cucumber salad and use by the next day for texture. Keep nori dry and separate. This meal contains fish, soy and sesame; ordinary soy adds wheat and optional mayonnaise may add egg. Check the main's optional condiment labels.
