## Meal Overview

For four diners, make **one full batch Chicken Marsala**, **half a batch Cream Cheese Mashed Potatoes** and **one full batch Green Beans with Shallots and Lemon**. The mash starts with 680 g peeled potatoes and supplies about three cups.

The Marsala recipe uses 680 g opened-flat chicken thighs, prosciutto and fresh mushrooms. The potatoes provide a creamy base for its sauce, and 450 g beans give a vegetable portion alongside. Leave the honey out of the green beans. The softened shallots and Marsala are sweet enough, and the lemon gives contrast.

Allow **eighty minutes elapsed and about sixty hands-on** for one cook. This dinner uses a chicken skillet, a bean skillet and two pots.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Set out and prepare:** Halve the potato recipe, starting with 680 g peeled potatoes. Trim the beans, dice the shallots, juice the lemon and slice the mushrooms. Put the potato water on to heat and start the bean water in a separate pot.
2. **Brown the chicken:** Pound the thighs into cutlets and brown them in two batches. Move them to a plate.
3. **Cook potatoes and beans alongside:** Simmer the potatoes until fully tender and warm their dairy in a small pan. Boil the beans until crisp-tender, then drain them.
4. **Build the Marsala sauce:** Brown the mushrooms and prosciutto, reduce the wine, then add the stock. Return the chicken and its juices and simmer until the thickest pieces reach **165°F / 74°C**. Loosen the sauce with stock if it reduces too far.
5. **Finish the sides:** Gently mash the potatoes with the warm dairy and season them. Cover the pot to keep the mash warm. Soften the shallots in the second skillet, warm the beans in them, then add the lemon off the heat.
6. **Mount the sauce and serve:** Move the chicken to a platter, take the pan off the heat and swirl in the cold butter. Divide the mash and beans among four plates, add the chicken, then spoon the mushrooms and sauce over the chicken and some of the potato.

## Timing adjustments

The bean and chicken skillets need attention at the same time near the end. Prepare the bean shallots before the final chicken simmer, then warm the beans while the chicken finishes. On a stove that cannot fit all four vessels, boil the beans first and use the freed burner for the potatoes.

For diners avoiding alcohol, use the chicken recipe's stock and lemon swap. It makes a different mushroom pan sauce. The main contains pork, wheat and dairy, and the mash contains dairy.

## Leftovers

Keep the chicken and sauce, the potatoes and the beans in separate containers and use them within three to four days. The sauce separates and the beans soften on reheating. Loosen the sauce with a little unsalted stock and stir gently.
