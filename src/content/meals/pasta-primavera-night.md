## Meal Overview

Lemony spaghetti with broccoli and peas, a small portion of garlic bread and freshly dressed arugula. For four diners, set the pasta recipe to four servings, **two-thirds of its full six-portion batch**. Make **half the garlic-bread recipe** and **one full batch of Everyday Arugula Salad**. This gives each diner a moderate pasta portion and a small bread accompaniment rather than stacking two full starch portions.

The pasta uses 10 2/3 ounces dry spaghetti, 3 1/3 cups broccoli and 2/3 cup peas at this setting; scale its entire sauce and water reserve too. Half the bread recipe uses 200 g bread and half of every topping ingredient; cut it into eight small pieces for two per diner. The salad remains 5 ounces arugula with 20 g Parmesan and its complete dressing.

Allow about 50 minutes, including preparation and heating. Use a large pasta pot, serving bowl, a tray at 400°F /205°C, and a small saucepan or microwave-safe bowl for peas. Start with softened garlic-bread butter; cold butter adds softening time. About 35 minutes is hands-on.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Heat and measure:** Start pasta water and heat the oven to 400°F /205°C. Set aside the scaled pasta ingredients, half-batch bread ingredients and full salad ingredients so their cheeses and lemon are not counted twice by eye.
2. **Prepare vegetables and bread:** Cut broccoli small, grate pasta cheese and prepare lemon. Make garlic butter and spread it on the weighed half-batch bread. Wash and dry salad greens if needed, shave salad cheese and mix dressing separately.
3. **Bake bread as pasta cooks:** Put bread in the oven for its 12–15 minute bake, then coordinate pasta by its package time. If bread finishes first, remove it; a short wait is preferable to delaying sauced pasta. Skip the optional broil so it does not compete with the final pasta toss.
4. **Finish vegetables:** Add broccoli during the last 3–5 minutes of pasta cooking, checking both independently. Cook peas fully according to their package in parallel. If broccoli needs longer, lift pasta out and let the florets finish. Reserve the scaled cooking-water amount before draining.
5. **Toss and serve:** Finish pasta in the bowl away from direct heat, adding cheese and cooking water gradually. Dress arugula near the last pasta minutes, then portion pasta, bread and salad immediately. The water reserve is available for adjusting texture; it is not all automatically poured in.

## Notes

- Choose suitable vegetarian-rennet cheeses and bread for a vegetarian meal.
- Keep each component's measured lemon, cheese and salt distinct, and taste before adding extra seasoning.
- Refrigerate leftovers promptly in shallow containers within two hours, or one hour above 90°F /32°C, at 40°F /4°C or below. Store components separately. Use pasta and dressed salad the next day for texture; follow the bread's two-to-three-day quality guidance and reheat pasta to 165°F /74°C.
