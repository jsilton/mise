## Meal Overview

Lemony spaghetti with broccoli and peas, a small portion of garlic bread and freshly dressed arugula. For four diners, set the pasta recipe to four servings, **two-thirds of its full six-portion batch**. Make **half the garlic-bread recipe** and **one full batch of Everyday Arugula Salad**. Each diner gets a moderate pasta portion and two small pieces of bread.

The pasta uses 10 2/3 ounces dry spaghetti, 3 1/3 cups broccoli and 2/3 cup peas at this setting. Scale its sauce and pasta-water amounts the same way. Half the bread recipe uses 200 g bread and half of every topping ingredient. Cut it into eight small pieces for two per diner. The salad is 5 ounces arugula with 20 g Parmesan and all its dressing.

Allow about 50 minutes, including preparation and heating. Use a large pasta pot, a serving bowl, a tray for the 400°F / 205°C oven, and a small saucepan for the peas. Start with softened butter for the garlic bread. About 35 minutes is hands-on.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Heat and measure:** Start the pasta water and heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C. Measure the scaled pasta ingredients, the half-batch bread ingredients and the salad ingredients into separate groups.
2. **Prepare vegetables and bread:** Cut broccoli small, grate pasta cheese and prepare lemon. Make the garlic butter and spread it on the 200 g of bread. Wash and dry the salad greens, shave the salad cheese and mix the dressing separately.
3. **Bake bread as pasta cooks:** Put the bread in the oven for its 12 to 15 minute bake, then start the pasta. Take the bread out when it is golden, even if the pasta is not ready.
4. **Finish vegetables:** Add the broccoli for the last 3 to 5 minutes of pasta cooking. Boil the peas in the small saucepan until tender. Save the scaled amount of cooking water before draining.
5. **Toss and serve:** Finish the pasta in the bowl, adding the cheese and cooking water gradually until the sauce coats the spaghetti. Dress the arugula in the last minutes of pasta cooking, then portion the pasta, bread and salad right away.

## Notes

- For a vegetarian meal, use cheeses made with vegetarian rennet.
- Refrigerate leftover components separately. Eat the pasta and dressed salad the next day. The bread keeps two to three days.
