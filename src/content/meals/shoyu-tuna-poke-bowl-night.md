## Meal Overview

Make **one complete batch of Shoyu Tuna Poke Bowl** for three generous bowls. It includes 450 g prepared tuna, four cups lightly seasoned cooked rice, 300 g fresh cucumber, 150 g shelled edamame and one avocado. Each diner receives about 150 g tuna, 1 1/3 cups rice, 100 g cucumber, 50 g beans and a third of the avocado. The main supplies the rice, vegetables and dressing for this dinner.

Fresh cucumber and a mildly sweet shoyu dressing give this bowl a gentler flavor than the chile-and-cucumber-salad poke meal. Serve the sriracha mayonnaise on the side. The rice has its own salt and vinegar seasoning.

Read the main's sourcing note before buying fish. FDA advises children, pregnant people, older adults and people with weakened immune systems to avoid raw seafood. For a fully cooked seafood dinner, make [Shrimp and Grits Night](/mise/meals/shrimp-and-grits-night), which serves four and includes a five-person adjustment.

## Cooking Strategy

Allow **about 85 minutes elapsed and 40 minutes hands-on** with thawed tuna, starting the rice from dry. The rice soak, heating, covered cooking, rest and brief cooling are included.

1. **Plan cold storage first:** Buy the tuna as the main's sourcing note describes. Thaw frozen tuna overnight in the refrigerator, out of its vacuum packaging. Set out the rice pot, a small saucepan for the edamame, a wide rice bowl and separate boards for vegetables and fish.
2. **Begin the plain rice:** Make [Plain Short-Grain White Rice](/mise/recipes/steamed-white-rice), starting with 300 g dry rice. Start its thirty-minute soak before preparing toppings. The rice takes about 65 minutes and makes about four cooked cups.
3. **Use the soak and cooking time:** Boil the 150 g shelled edamame for their package time early in the rice process. Drain them, spread them in a shallow container and refrigerate until cold. Wash and cut the cucumber and scallion and refrigerate them. Mix the fish dressing in one bowl. Measure the rice's vinegar, sugar and salt into another bowl.
4. **Season and cool the rice:** Once the rested rice is tender, fold the rice seasoning into four cooked cups. Spread the rice gently in the wide bowl and let the steam escape until barely warm, about ten minutes.
5. **Dress the cold tuna while rice cools:** Slice the avocado. Cut the tuna into 1/2-inch cubes, coat it with its dressing and refrigerate it for five to ten minutes while setting out the bowls.
6. **Portion and serve:** Divide the rice, tuna, cold beans, cucumber and avocado among three bowls. Lift the fish from its excess dressing, add the sesame seeds and serve the mayonnaise on the side.

## Rice option and advance preparation

For sushi rice, use four cups [Sushi Rice (Shari)](/mise/recipes/master-sushi-rice) and leave out the main's three Rice-section seasoning ingredients. Keep the vinegar and sugar in the tuna dressing. The full shari batch makes extra and takes about eighty minutes from dry. Allow about ninety minutes for the meal.

Rice and beans cooked ahead and refrigerated shorten the day-of work. Allow about 35 to 45 minutes, including reheating the rice and spreading it to cool until barely warm before adding the tuna.

## Leftovers and serving limits

Eat the raw tuna the day it is cut. Assembled bowls do not keep.

Use refrigerated cooked rice and beans within three to four days. Cucumber and avocado are best freshly cut. The main contains fish, soy and sesame. Ordinary soy sauce adds wheat, and the mayonnaise adds egg.
