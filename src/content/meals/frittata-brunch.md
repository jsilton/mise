## Meal Overview

A puffed vegetable and Gruyère frittata with cumin-chili roasted sweet potatoes and a lemony arugula salad. The sweet potatoes and the frittata share a 425°F oven, so brunch is on the table in 50 minutes. For five, serve all six wedges of the frittata and roast 2 1/2 lb of sweet potatoes.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Roast the sweet potatoes:** Heat the oven to 425°F with a lined sheet on the lower rack. Season the wedges and roast them flat side down for 20 minutes.
2. **Cook the vegetables:** Whisk the eggs with the cream, salt and half the cheese. Cook the frittata vegetables in the skillet until tender and dry, 6 to 10 minutes.
3. **Flip the potatoes:** Flip each wedge and return the sheet to the oven for 10 to 12 minutes more.
4. **Bake the frittata:** Pour the eggs over the vegetables and cook until the edges set, about 1 minute. Add the rest of the cheese and bake on the center rack until puffed, 8 to 12 minutes.
5. **Toss the salad:** Toss the arugula with its dressing while the frittata settles for 5 minutes.
6. **Serve:** Cut the frittata into wedges and serve it with the sweet potatoes, lime and salad.
