## Meal Overview

Four panko-coated chicken cutlets, vegetable curry sauce, plain short-grain rice, and small bowls of cucumber sunomono make a complete dinner. Use **one batch each** of Chicken Katsu Curry, Plain Short-Grain White Rice, and Japanese Cucumber Sunomono. The main already includes the curry sauce and its vegetables.

Allow about **85 to 90 minutes**, including the rice soak and rest, vegetable preparation, and two frying batches. The curry sauce waits while the chicken fries, and the cutlets go to the table straight from frying. Rice provides a plain base, and the vinegar-dressed cucumber brings acidity and a different texture beside the rich sauce.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start rice hydration:** Rinse the rice and soak it for 30 minutes in its cooking water. Set out a saucepan for curry, the rice pot, and a deep skillet for chicken.
2. **Prepare sauce and cucumber:** Cut all the vegetables. Start the curry sauce. Salt and drain the sliced cucumber while the sauce simmers. Refrigerate the cucumber and dressing separately.
3. **Cook rice and prepare chicken:** After soaking, bring rice to a boil, cover, and reduce heat. Pound and bread the chicken while the rice cooks.
4. **Make room for frying:** When the curry vegetables are tender and the sauce is spoonable, cover the sauce off the heat. Move the rice off the burner for its ten-minute covered rest. Heat the frying oil and set a rack in a low oven for the first batch.
5. **Fry last:** Fry the cutlets in two batches until each reaches 165°F / 74°C in its center. Rest the last batch briefly on the rack. Rewarm the sauce and thin it with a little water if it has thickened.
6. **Finish and plate:** Toss cucumber with dressing and finish with sesame. Fluff rice and divide it among four plates. Add one sliced cutlet per person, spoon vegetable curry beside it, and serve the cucumber in small bowls.

## Notes

Store leftover cutlets, sauce, rice and cucumber separately.
