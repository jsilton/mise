## Meal Overview

Four panko-coated chicken cutlets, vegetable curry sauce, plain short-grain rice, and small bowls of cucumber sunomono make a complete dinner. Use **one batch each** of Chicken Katsu Curry, Plain Short-Grain White Rice, and Japanese Cucumber Sunomono. The main already includes the curry sauce and its vegetables.

Allow about **85–90 minutes**, including the rice soak and rest, vegetable preparation, and two frying batches. The curry sauce can wait briefly while the chicken fries; the cutlets should reach the table soon after frying. Rice provides a plain base, and the vinegar-dressed cucumber brings acidity and a different texture beside the rich sauce.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start rice hydration:** Rinse the rice and begin its full 30-minute soak in the measured water. Set out a saucepan for curry, the rice pot, and a deep skillet for chicken. The rice can rest off the burner before frying begins, so two burners can handle the finish.
2. **Prepare sauce and cucumber:** Cut all vegetables before handling raw chicken. Start the curry sauce as directed. Salt and drain the sliced cucumber while the sauce simmers; keep cucumber and dressing refrigerated separately until near serving.
3. **Cook rice and prepare chicken:** After soaking, bring rice to a boil, cover, and reduce heat. Pound and bread the chicken during the covered cooking period, using the cutlet recipe's separate flour and tools. Clean the work surface after raw chicken preparation.
4. **Make room for frying:** Check that the curry vegetables are tender and sauce is spoonable, then cover the sauce off heat. Move cooked rice off the burner for its ten-minute covered rest. Heat the frying oil and prepare the rack and brief low-oven hold for the first batch.
5. **Fry last:** Fry cutlets in two batches, checking each for 165°F / 74°C in its center. Let the last batch rest on the rack briefly. Rewarm the sauce and thin it with a little water if it has become too thick.
6. **Finish and plate:** Toss cucumber with dressing and finish with sesame. Fluff rice and divide it among four plates. Add one sliced cutlet per person, spoon vegetable curry beside it, and serve the cucumber in small bowls.

## Notes

This meal uses the sauce already included in Chicken Katsu Curry. The separate stewed Japanese Chicken Curry is another dinner, and chicken Parmesan is not a katsu component. Keep cutlets, sauce, rice, and cucumber separate when storing leftovers; follow each recipe's chilling and reheating guidance.
