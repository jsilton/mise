## Meal Overview

Linguine and shrimp in a generous lemon-butter and white-wine sauce, with peppery arugula and a small portion of garlic bread. Make **one full batch of scampi, one full batch of salad and half a batch of garlic bread for four diners**. The main supplies one pound each of dry pasta and shrimp. The salad uses 5 oz / 142 g arugula. For the bread, use 200 g bread and half of every topping ingredient, then cut into four portions.

The small bread portion sits beside the pasta without becoming another large starch serving. Dress the salad lightly. Put the main's Parmesan on the table.

Allow about **one hour elapsed and 50 minutes hands-on** for one cook, with thawed, peeled shrimp and softened bread butter. Use a large pasta pot, a deep 12-inch sauté pan, a rimmed baking sheet and a salad bowl. Finish the bread before the final pasta toss so the last few minutes can focus on the butter sauce and salad.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start heating and prepare the bread:** Heat the oven to **400°F / 205°C** and put the pasta water on to boil. Spread the half-batch garlic butter evenly over the cut surfaces of the bread. Bake until the center is warm and the edges are golden, about **12 to 15 minutes**.
2. **Prepare the salad and main:** While the bread bakes, wash and dry the arugula, shave its cheese and mix its dressing separately. Prepare the scampi shallots, garlic, parsley, lemon juice and butter pieces. Dry the shrimp last. Take the bread out and cover it loosely.
3. **Cook shrimp separately:** Cook the shrimp in the oil over medium-high heat in a single layer until the thick centers are firm and opaque, **145°F / 63°C**. Transfer them to a plate.
4. **Coordinate sauce and linguine:** Soften the shallots, add the garlic and pepper flakes briefly, then reduce the wine. Boil the linguine until one minute short of al dente, timed to finish with the sauce. Save the pasta water before draining. Lower the heat under the sauce if it gets ahead.
5. **Finish off heat:** Toss the pasta with the wine base and a little pasta water until tender with a slight bite. Take the pan off the heat and add the cold butter and finishing oil, then fold in the shrimp, their juices, the parsley and the first portion of lemon juice. Taste, then season with the remaining lemon, salt and pepper. Loosen a tight sauce with more pasta water.
6. **Dress and serve:** As soon as the pasta is ready, toss the arugula with its dressing and add its Parmesan. Divide the pasta and shrimp evenly among four plates, serve the salad alongside and cut the bread into four portions.

## Leftovers

Refrigerate pasta, salad and bread separately. Use the pasta within three to four days and reheat it gently with a little water. Shrimp firm on reheating. Eat dressed salad by the next day and garlic bread within two to three days.
