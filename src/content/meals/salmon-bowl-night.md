## Meal Overview

Honey-sriracha salmon, jasmine rice, cucumber, avocado and edamame make a generous bowl with spicy mayonnaise and a small topping of sweet-tart Vietnamese carrot-daikon pickle. The fresh cucumber cools the rich fish. The drained pickle adds a sharper crunch.

Make **one full batch of [Honey Sriracha Salmon Bowls](/mise/recipes/honey-sriracha-salmon-bowls)** and share **a quarter of the finished [Do Chua batch](/mise/recipes/quick-pickled-carrots-and-daikon)** among four bowls. The main includes all the rice, beans, vegetables and mayonnaise needed for this dinner.

| Component           | Amount for four bowls                            | Per bowl                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| Salmon              | 680 g skinless, boneless raw fish                | 170 g raw fish                |
| Jasmine rice        | 120 g dry, cooked with the main’s measured water | About 1/2 cup cooked          |
| Shelled edamame     | 150 g frozen beans, cooked and drained           | A quarter of the cooked beans |
| Cucumber            | 300 g                                            | 75 g                          |
| Avocado             | 1 large                                          | A quarter avocado             |
| Sriracha mayonnaise | 1/2 cup prepared                                 | 2 tbsp                        |
| Do Chua             | A quarter finished batch, about 1/2 cup drained  | About 2 tbsp                  |

Allow **about ninety minutes elapsed and sixty-five minutes hands-on** when making a full pickle jar and the complete main. With prepared pickles, allow **sixty minutes elapsed and about forty-five minutes hands-on**. Start with thawed salmon and prepared sriracha mayonnaise. Keep the remaining pickles under brine in the refrigerator for up to one week.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare the pickles first:** Take about twenty minutes to peel, cut, massage, rinse and pack the full Do Chua batch. Keep the small massage sugar separate from the larger sugar portion dissolved in the brine. Cover the vegetables with brine and refrigerate for at least one hour.
2. **Start rice and marinate fish:** Cook 120 g dry jasmine rice in 240 ml water. While the rice heats, mix the salmon marinade, cut the fish and refrigerate it in the marinade for twenty minutes. Keep its three tablespoons of sauce water separate from the rice and edamame water.
3. **Prepare the toppings during marination:** Slice the cucumber, portion the mayonnaise and boil the 150 g frozen shelled edamame in its saucepan. Drain the beans. Slice the avocado shortly before assembly.
4. **Sear in two batches:** Drain the fish and keep the marinade in a bowl. Sear two 340 g batches in a 12-inch skillet, with two teaspoons oil per batch, about 6 to 8 minutes each. Move each piece to a plate when its thickest center reaches 145°F / 63°C. Lower the heat if the honey darkens too quickly. Wipe out burnt bits before the next batch.
5. **Boil and reduce the sauce:** With all the fish out of the pan, bring the marinade to a full bubbling boil. Boil for one minute, then reduce gently for 1 to 3 minutes until it lightly coats a spoon. Turn off the heat and fold the fish and its juices into the sauce just long enough to coat. Loosen a thick sauce with the reserved water.
6. **Assemble:** Drain the serving portion of pickles well. Divide all the main’s components among four bowls and add about two tablespoons pickle to each. Return the jar to the refrigerator and serve.

For a smaller pickle batch, use one quarter of every Do Chua ingredient in a small, narrow container. It needs less cutting and the same refrigerated hour, so dinner still takes ninety minutes.

## Cucumber-salad variation

For a garlic-and-sesame version, **omit the Do Chua and replace the main’s 300 g plain cucumber with half a batch of [Smashed Cucumber Salad](/mise/recipes/smashed-cucumber-salad)**. Use 300 g cucumber, 1/4 tsp fine salt for draining, 2 1/4 tsp black vinegar, 1 tsp light soy sauce, 1/2 tsp each toasted sesame oil and sugar, 1 small garlic clove, 1/2 tsp chili crisp, 1/2 tsp sesame seeds and half a scallion.

Start the cucumber’s fifteen-minute salt drain before preparing the fish marinade. Mix the dressing during that drain, then shake and blot the cucumber and keep it chilled. Toss and finish the salad just before assembling the bowls. Keep the main’s rice, beans, avocado and mayonnaise amounts unchanged. Leave off the main’s sesame garnish, because the salad has its own seeds.

Allow **sixty-five to seventy minutes elapsed and about fifty-five minutes hands-on**. The dressed cucumber supplies the bowl’s tangy topping as well as its fresh vegetable crunch.

## For five diners

Make **1 1/4 batches of the main**: 850 g trimmed salmon, 150 g dry jasmine rice with 300 ml cooking water, about 188 g shelled edamame, 375 g cucumber, 1 1/4 large avocados and 10 tbsp prepared sriracha mayonnaise. Scale the marinade, cooking oil and garnish by the same factor. For the pickle topping, use **5/16 of a finished full Do Chua batch**, about 5/8 cup drained or ten tablespoons total.

Cook the fish in **three batches of about 285 g**, sharing the scaled five teaspoons oil evenly among them. Allow **about 100 to 105 minutes from scratch**, or **70 to 75 minutes with prepared pickles**. The first fish batch waits longest, so finish the sauce and serve as soon as the last batch is cooked.

For five bowls with cucumber salad, use **5/8 of its full batch**, replacing all 375 g plain cucumber and omitting the pickle. Scale every dressing ingredient with the cucumber and allow **75 to 85 minutes**.

## Equipment and leftovers

Use a lidded rice saucepan, a small edamame saucepan, a 12-inch fish skillet, a fine-tip thermometer and a pickle jar. The whole dinner cooks on the stovetop.

Keep the pickle covered by brine in its own refrigerated jar and use it within one week. Refrigerate cooked rice, fish and beans separately from the cold toppings and use them within three to four days. Eat assembled leftovers within a day. The cucumber salad keeps for one day.
