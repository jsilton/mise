## Meal Overview

Honey-sriracha salmon, jasmine rice, cucumber, avocado and edamame make a generous bowl with spicy mayonnaise and a small topping of sweet-tart Vietnamese carrot-daikon pickle. The fresh cucumber cools the rich fish; the drained pickle adds a sharper crunch.

Make **one full batch of [Honey Sriracha Salmon Bowls](/mise/recipes/honey-sriracha-salmon-bowls)** and share **a quarter of the finished [Do Chua batch](/mise/recipes/quick-pickled-carrots-and-daikon)** among four bowls. The main includes all the rice, beans, vegetables and mayonnaise needed for this dinner.

| Component           | Amount for four bowls                            | Per bowl                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| Salmon              | 680 g skinless, boneless raw fish                | 170 g raw allocation          |
| Jasmine rice        | 120 g dry, cooked with the main’s measured water | About 1/2 cup cooked          |
| Shelled edamame     | 150 g frozen beans, cooked and drained           | A quarter of the cooked beans |
| Cucumber            | 300 g                                            | 75 g                          |
| Avocado             | 1 large                                          | A quarter avocado             |
| Sriracha mayonnaise | 1/2 cup prepared                                 | 2 tbsp                        |
| Do Chua             | A quarter finished batch, about 1/2 cup drained  | About 2 tbsp                  |

Allow **about ninety minutes elapsed and sixty-five minutes hands-on** when making a full pickle jar and the complete main. With prepared pickles, allow **sixty minutes elapsed and about forty-five minutes hands-on**. Start with thawed salmon and prepared sriracha mayonnaise. Save the remaining pickles under brine in the refrigerator for another meal, within their one-week limit.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare the pickles first:** Allow about twenty minutes to peel, cut, massage, rinse and pack the full Do Chua batch. Keep its small massage sugar separate from the larger sugar portion dissolved in the brine. Cover the vegetables with brine and refrigerate for at least one hour. Set a timer from when the jar enters the refrigerator.
2. **Start rice and marinate fish:** After putting the pickles away, clear the work area and follow the main’s rice method using 120 g dry jasmine rice and 240 ml cooking water. While the rice heats, mix the salmon marinade, cut the fish and refrigerate it in the marinade for twenty minutes. Keep its three tablespoons of sauce water separate from the rice and edamame water. Clean the raw-fish board, utensils and your hands before handling toppings.
3. **Prepare the toppings during marination:** Slice cucumber, portion the mayonnaise and cook the 150 g frozen shelled edamame in its separate saucepan as directed. Drain the beans. Have all toppings ready before searing; slice avocado shortly before assembly. The edamame saucepan finishes before the fish skillet needs its burner, so two burners suffice.
4. **Sear in two batches:** Drain the fish, keeping the raw-contact marinade in a separate bowl. Follow the main’s two 340 g batches in a 12-inch skillet, using two teaspoons oil per batch. Allow about 6–8 minutes per batch plus heat recovery. Remove individual pieces to a clean plate when their thickest centers reach 145°F / 63°C; lower the heat if the honey darkens too quickly. Clear burnt deposits before the next batch or sauce.
5. **Boil and reduce the sauce:** With all cooked fish out of the pan, bring the reserved marinade to a full bubbling boil throughout. Maintain the boil for one minute, then reduce gently for 1–3 minutes until it lightly coats a spoon. Turn off the heat and fold the fish and its juices into the sauce just long enough to coat. Use the main’s reserved correction water only if the sauce needs loosening.
6. **Assemble promptly:** Confirm that the pickles have spent at least a full hour refrigerated in brine. Drain the serving portion well. Divide all the main’s components among four bowls and add roughly two tablespoons pickle to each. Return the remaining jar to the refrigerator and serve.

For a smaller fresh pickle batch, use one quarter of every Do Chua ingredient in a suitably small, narrow container. It needs less cutting but the same full refrigerated hour. Start it first and keep the ninety-minute dinner allowance.

## Cucumber-salad variation

For a garlic-and-sesame version, **omit the Do Chua and replace the main’s 300 g plain cucumber with half a batch of [Smashed Cucumber Salad](/mise/recipes/smashed-cucumber-salad)**. Use 300 g cucumber, 1/4 tsp fine salt for draining, 2 1/4 tsp black vinegar, 1 tsp light soy sauce, 1/2 tsp each toasted sesame oil and sugar, 1 small garlic clove, optional 1/2 tsp chili crisp, 1/2 tsp sesame seeds and half a scallion.

Start the cucumber’s fifteen-minute salt drain before preparing the fish marinade. Mix the dressing during that drain, then shake and blot the cucumber and keep it chilled. Toss and finish the salad just before assembling the bowls. Keep the main’s rice, beans, avocado and mayonnaise amounts unchanged. Omit the main’s optional sesame garnish when using the salad’s seeds.

Allow **sixty-five to seventy minutes elapsed and about fifty-five minutes hands-on**. The dressed cucumber supplies the bowl’s tangy topping as well as its fresh vegetable crunch.

## For five diners

Make **1 1/4 batches of the main**: 850 g trimmed salmon, 150 g dry jasmine rice with 300 ml cooking water, about 188 g shelled edamame, 375 g cucumber, 1 1/4 large avocados and 10 tbsp prepared sriracha mayonnaise. Scale the marinade, cooking oil and optional garnish to the same factor. For the pickle topping, use **5/16 of a finished full Do Chua batch**, about 5/8 cup drained or ten tablespoons total.

Cook the fish in **three batches of roughly 285 g**, sharing the scaled five teaspoons oil evenly among them. Keep the same cube size, heat control and temperature endpoint. Allow **about 100–105 minutes from scratch**, or **70–75 minutes with prepared pickles**; the extra pan round and assembly extend the four-person schedule. The first fish batch has a longer wait, so finish the sauce and serve promptly once the final batch is cooked.

For five bowls with cucumber salad, use **5/8 of its full batch**, replacing all 375 g plain cucumber and omitting the pickle. Scale every dressing ingredient with the cucumber and allow **75–85 minutes** for this version.

## Equipment and leftovers

Use a lidded rice saucepan, a small edamame saucepan, a separate 12-inch fish skillet, a fine-tip thermometer and a clean pickle jar. The dinner uses the stovetop; no oven is needed. A smaller skillet, thicker fish pieces or slow burner recovery will take longer.

Keep the pickle covered by brine in its own refrigerated jar and use within one week of making it. Refrigerate cooked rice, fish and beans separately from the cold toppings, in shallow containers within two hours, or one hour above 90°F / 32°C. Keep at 40°F / 4°C or below. Use cooked components within three to four days, counting from their original cooking dates, and reheat to 165°F / 74°C before adding cold toppings. Eat assembled leftovers within a day for better texture. Keep cucumber salad to its one-day quality window and follow the mayonnaise label if its limit is shorter.
