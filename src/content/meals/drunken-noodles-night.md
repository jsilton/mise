## Meal Overview

Use one full batch of Pad Kee Mao and one batch of Edamame with Sea Salt for four diners. The main includes 800 g fresh noodles, 450 g chicken, shallots and baby corn. The side uses 450 g pods before shelling, so each diner gets a small bowl of pods.

This Thai noodle dinner has a simple Japanese-style pod side. Leave the sesame oil and chile flakes off the edamame for a plain contrast with the aromatic main. Allow about 60 to 65 minutes using two burners. Serve each noodle plate as its batch finishes.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare the main:** Separate the fresh noodles. Measure the sauce and prepare the vegetables and basil. Keep the basil separate for the finish.
2. **Set up the side:** Set the edamame pot of water on the second burner near the end of preparation. Keep the pods frozen until the water boils.
3. **Cook the protein and vegetables:** Cook the chicken in two batches to 165°F / 74°C, then cook the shallots, corn and aromatics. Divide the noodles, sauce and cooked ingredients into four portions.
4. **Cook and drain edamame:** Boil the pods for their package time until a bean is tender. Drain well and season with half the salt, then taste and add the rest. Divide into four small bowls and set out a bowl for the empty pods.
5. **Finish one noodle plate at a time:** Cook each of the four noodle portions with its sauce and cooked ingredients, then fold the basil in off the heat. Send out each plate with its small edamame bowl.

## Notes

The main uses holy basil. Thai basil changes the aroma. Store leftover components separately.
