## Meal Overview

Use one full batch of Pad Kee Mao and one batch of Edamame with Sea Salt for four diners. The main includes 800 g fresh noodles, 450 g chicken, shallots and baby corn. The side uses 450 g pods before shelling, so each diner receives a small pod portion rather than 112 g edible beans. No additional rice is needed.

This Thai noodle dinner uses a simple Japanese-style pod side. Keep its optional sesame oil and chile flakes out for a plainer contrast with the aromatic main, and add finishing salt gradually. Allow about 60–65 minutes using two burners; dried-noodle preparation can add time. Serve noodle plates as their individual batches finish.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare the main:** Follow its fresh-noodle instructions or dried alternative. Measure sauce and prepare vegetables and basil before handling raw chicken. Keep basil separate for the finish.
2. **Set up the side:** Read the edamame package and set its pot of water on the second burner near the end of preparation. Keep the pods frozen until ready to cook them.
3. **Cook the protein and vegetables:** Follow the main's two chicken batches, verifying 165°F / 74°C, then cook its shallots, corn and aromatics. Divide noodles, sauce and cooked ingredients into four portions.
4. **Cook and drain edamame:** Cook pods for their package time and check a bean's tenderness. Drain well, season with about half the salt supply first, then taste before adding more. Divide into four small bowls and provide a discard bowl for pods.
5. **Finish one noodle plate at a time:** Cook each of the four noodle portions with its sauce and cooked ingredients, then fold basil in off heat. Taste the first portion before repeating. Send out each plate promptly with its small edamame bowl.

## Notes

Holy basil is the main's default. Thai basil is an aroma-changing substitute, not the same herb. Edamame are served in their pods as a separate side; shell them first if putting beans directly onto noodles. Cool leftovers promptly and store components separately according to their recipe instructions.
