## Meal Overview

Make **one full batch Miso Salmon with Bok Choy** and **half a batch Smashed Cucumber Salad** for four diners. The main includes 900 g trimmed salmon, 600 g prepared bok choy and 270 g dry jasmine rice. The half salad uses 300 g cucumber and half of every dressing ingredient. Divide the rice, fish and greens evenly, with a small cucumber side on each plate.

Jordan's citrus-miso marinade and oyster-seasoned greens give this dinner its character. The cucumber adds cool texture and a sharper vinegar note. Leave the chili crisp off the cucumber, because the fish and greens are already well seasoned.

Allow **about sixty-five minutes elapsed and forty-five minutes hands-on** for one cook. The jasmine rice is part of the main recipe.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start rice and heat the oven:** Start the jasmine rice and heat the oven to 400°F. Use a lidded saucepan, a 12-inch lidded skillet and a broiler-safe metal half-sheet pan.
2. **Start the cucumber while the rice cooks:** Crack and cut the cucumber, add half the salad's salt and drain it for fifteen minutes in the refrigerator. Mix half the dressing while it drains. Shake off the liquid, blot the cucumber gently, and refrigerate it undressed until serving.
3. **Marinate fish and prepare greens:** Mix the marinade and set aside two tablespoons for the glaze. Marinate the fish for fifteen minutes in the refrigerator. Wash and dry the bok choy and divide its oil, water, oyster sauce, sesame oil and aromatics into two batches.
4. **Roast and cook the greens:** Drain the fish, discard the marinade and roast the fish. Cook the two bok choy batches while it roasts. Turn off the skillet before switching the oven to broil.
5. **Watch the final broil:** Brush on the reserved glaze and broil, watching the whole time, until each fillet's center reaches 145°F / 63°C.
6. **Dress and serve:** Fluff and divide the rice. Dress the cucumber. Plate the fish and bok choy with the small cucumber side.

## Adjustments and leftovers

For five diners, use 1.25 times the main and 0.625 times the full salad: 1,125 g trimmed fish, 750 g bok choy, about 338 g dry jasmine and 375 g cucumber, with their seasonings scaled too. Keep the pan layers loose.

Refrigerate cooked fish, rice and greens separately and use them within three to four days. The cucumber salad keeps for one day. Reheated greens are softer and the salmon firmer. The dinner contains fish, soy, wheat and oyster.
