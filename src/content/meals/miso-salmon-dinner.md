## Meal Overview

Make **one full batch Miso Salmon with Bok Choy** and **half a batch Smashed Cucumber Salad** for four diners. The main includes 900 g trimmed salmon, 600 g prepared bok choy and 270 g dry jasmine rice. The half salad uses 300 g cucumber and half of every dressing ingredient. Divide the rice, fish and greens evenly, with a small cucumber side on each plate.

Jordan's citrus-miso marinade and oyster-seasoned greens give this dinner its character. The cucumber adds cool texture and a sharper vinegar note. Taste the drained cucumber before dressing and keep its optional chili crisp optional; there is already seasoning in the fish and greens. Extra rice, sesame oil, lemon or a second sauce is not part of this dinner.

Allow **about sixty-five minutes elapsed and forty-five minutes hands-on** for one cook. Use the main's jasmine method for this schedule. Its rice is already included; the separate short-grain rice page is a different preparation with a soak and should not be added as a second base.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start rice and heat the oven:** Follow the main's measured jasmine rice and 400°F oven preparation. Use a lidded saucepan, a separate 12-inch lidded skillet and a broiler-safe metal half-sheet pan. Two burners suffice. Check that the oven racks leave the stated clearance for the watched broil.
2. **Start the cucumber while the rice cooks:** Crack and cut the half-batch cucumber, add half the salad's specified salt and drain for fifteen minutes, preferably chilled. Mix half its dressing while it drains, and continue with the fish preparation. When the fifteen minutes are up, shake off the liquid, blot the cucumber gently, and refrigerate it undressed until serving. Keep the dressing separate. This short extra preparation is included in the meal allowance.
3. **Marinate fish and prepare greens:** Mix the main's marinade, reserving its two clean tablespoons before raw fish touches the rest. Give the fish fifteen minutes in the refrigerator while washing and drying bok choy and dividing its oil, water, oyster sauce, sesame oil and aromatics into two batches. Use separate clean equipment for the salad. The acidic fish marinade is a brief step; do not leave it overnight.
4. **Roast and cook the greens:** Drain and roast the fish as directed, discarding the raw-contact marinade. Cook the two bok choy batches while it roasts. Finish the greens and turn off the skillet before switching to broil. If fish needs to wait briefly for that final step, set its pan on a heatproof surface as its recipe directs.
5. **Watch the final broil:** Brush on the clean reserved glaze and attend to the broiler continuously. Check each fillet's center for 145°F / 63°C; brown glaze alone is not a doneness test. Use the gentler oven finish if needed. Do not leave a hot broiler unattended to dress the salad.
6. **Dress and serve:** Once the fish is finished, fluff and divide the rice. Taste a drained cucumber piece, then dress according to the salad's instructions. Plate the fish and bok choy with the small cucumber side and serve promptly. A short loose cover keeps the first greens batch warm but softens its browned surfaces.

## Adjustments and leftovers

Thicker salmon, a small skillet or slow oven can extend the schedule. For five diners, use 1.25 times the main and 0.625 times the full salad: 1,125 g trimmed fish, 750 g bok choy, about 338 g dry jasmine and 375 g cucumber, with their seasonings scaled too. Keep the pan layers loose and add batches as needed; the sixty-five-minute allowance is for four.

Refrigerate cooked fish, rice and greens separately in shallow containers within two hours, or one hour above 90°F / 32°C, at 40°F / 4°C or below. Use them within three to four days and reheat to 165°F / 74°C; count rice from its original cooking date when made ahead. Follow the salad's shorter one-day quality window and keep it cold rather than reheating it with the fish. Expect softer greens and firmer reheated salmon. The standard dinner contains fish, soy, wheat and oyster; the component substitutions explain which sauces to change if needed.
