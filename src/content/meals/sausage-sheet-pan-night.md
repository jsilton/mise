## Meal Overview

Italian sausages roasted on one pan with baby potatoes that crisp in the sausage fat, with garlicky steamed broccoli, warm rolls and mustard. It needs ten minutes of prep. For five, make one and a quarter batches of the sausage and potatoes, using about 2 lb of each.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Roast the potatoes:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Season the potatoes on a sheet pan and roast them cut side down for 15 minutes.
2. **Add the sausage:** Nestle the sausages among the potatoes and prick each link. Roast until the sausages reach 160°F and the potatoes are golden, about 20 minutes, turning the sausages halfway.
3. **Steam the broccoli:** In the last 10 minutes, steam the broccoli for 4 to 6 minutes and toss it with the warm garlic oil.
4. **Serve:** Slice the sausages on the bias. Serve them with the potatoes, broccoli, warm rolls and mustard.
