## Meal Overview

Creamy spinach-and-cheese shells, crisp-edged garlic bread and peppery arugula with strawberries and feta make six generous dinner plates. Use one full batch of each linked recipe: all the shells, twelve pieces of garlic bread and six small salad portions. The berry dressing and fresh leaves bring a lighter texture beside the two baked components.

Allow about **95 to 110 minutes elapsed and 55 to 70 minutes hands-on**. The shells bake at 375°F. The bread follows at 400°F while the shells rest.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare the main, about 35 minutes:** Heat the oven to 375°F while you cook the pasta and spinach, mix the filling and fill the shells. Arrange them in one layer in shallow dishes that fit in the oven together. Start with four cups of prepared sauce.
2. **Bake the shells, about 40 to 45 minutes:** Bake covered for 25 minutes, then uncovered for 15 to 20 minutes, until the filling at the center of each dish reaches 165°F / 74°C.
3. **Prepare the sides during that bake:** Make the salad dressing, wash and dry the leaves and berries, and portion the feta and toasted seeds. Keep everything refrigerated and the leaves undressed. Mix the garlic butter, spread it on the bread and refrigerate the bread until its oven turn.
4. **Change the oven, then bake the bread:** Take out the shells and tent them loosely. Raise the oven to 400°F, about 5 to 10 minutes. Bake the garlic bread until its center is hot and its edges are golden, 12 to 15 minutes. The shells rest for at least ten minutes while the bread bakes.
5. **Finish together:** As the bread finishes, dress the salad lightly and add the seeds. Cut the bread into twelve pieces, two per person. Divide the shell batch and salad into six portions and serve.

## Notes

- Shells assembled a day ahead and baked cold from the refrigerator need longer in the oven.
- If the shell dishes do not fit in the oven together, bake them in two rounds.
- For a vegetarian meal, use vegetarian cheeses in all three components, including the hard grating cheeses and feta.
- Refrigerate leftovers and keep undressed salad separate.
