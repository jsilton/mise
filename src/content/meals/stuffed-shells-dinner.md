## Meal Overview

Creamy spinach-and-cheese shells, crisp-edged garlic bread and peppery arugula with strawberries and feta make six generous dinner plates. Use one full batch of each linked recipe: all the shells, twelve pieces of garlic bread and six small salad portions. The berry dressing and fresh leaves bring a lighter texture beside the two baked components.

Allow about **95–110 minutes elapsed and 55–70 minutes hands-on** from the starting conditions above. The shells bake at 375°F; the bread follows at 400°F while the shells rest. A refrigerator-cold casserole or separate oven loads needs longer.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare the main, about 35 minutes:** Heat the oven to 375°F while you cook the pasta and spinach, mix the filling and arrange the full shell batch. Use enough shallow dishes for one layer, and verify they fit in the oven together with room for heat to circulate. Start with four measured cups of prepared sauce; making sauce now adds time.
2. **Bake the shells, about 40–45 minutes:** Follow the main recipe’s 25-minute covered bake followed by 15–20 minutes uncovered. Check representative central filled shells in every dish for 165°F / 74°C; extend baking as needed.
3. **Prepare the sides during that bake:** Make the salad dressing, wash and dry the leaves and berries, and portion the feta and toasted seeds. Keep everything refrigerated and the leaves undressed. Mix the garlic butter, spread it on the bread and refrigerate the prepared bread until its oven turn. These tasks overlap the bake but still count as hands-on work.
4. **Change the oven, then bake the bread:** Remove the finished shells and tent loosely. Raise the oven to 400°F and wait until it reaches that temperature; allow roughly 5–10 minutes for planning, but follow the oven rather than that estimate. Bake the garlic bread for its stated 12–15 minutes until its center is hot and edges golden. The shells’ minimum ten-minute rest overlaps this transition and bake. An optional brief broil needs continuous watching.
5. **Finish together:** As the bread finishes, dress the salad lightly and add the seeds. Cut the bread into twelve pieces, two per person. Divide the shell batch and salad into six portions and serve promptly.

## Notes

- Day-ahead shells are useful, but a cold pan needs additional baking time and the same checked center temperature. Follow the bakeware maker’s cold-to-hot instructions.
- If all shell dishes cannot fit with circulating air, allow a second oven round. Do not stack shells or crowd pans to preserve the headline time.
- A vegetarian version needs suitable cheeses in all three components, including the hard grating cheeses and feta. Keep the default ingredients unless making that deliberate substitution.
- Refrigerate perishable leftovers promptly, within 2 hours or 1 hour above 90°F / 32°C. Follow each component’s storage guidance; keep undressed salad separate when possible.
