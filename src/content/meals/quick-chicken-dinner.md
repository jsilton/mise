## Meal Overview

For four diners, make **one full batch Buttermilk Cornflake Baked Chicken**, **half a batch Cream Cheese Mashed Potatoes** and **one full batch Green Beans with Shallots and Lemon**. That means 1360 g bone-in chicken pieces, 680 g peeled potatoes and 450 g trimmed beans. The mash supplies about three cups, or three-quarters of a cup per plate. Share the breast meat and divide the dark-meat pieces among the plates.

For the half-batch mash, use **680 g peeled potatoes, about 1.5 L cold water** to cover by an inch, **6 g cooking-water salt, 37.5 g butter, 57.5 g cream cheese and 90 ml half-and-half**, with **30 ml more** to loosen a stiff mash. Finish with **1/4 teaspoon fine salt** and **1/4 teaspoon black pepper**.

The cornflake-and-Parmesan crust gives crunch beside soft mash, and lemon-finished beans balance the rich chicken and dairy. Leave the honey out of the beans. Set the chicken beside the mash so the coating stays crisp.

Allow **at least four hours forty minutes elapsed**, including ten minutes to assemble the marinade, the chicken's three-hour refrigerated soak and about ninety minutes for the cooking session. Hands-on work across both sessions is about seventy-five minutes, including about ten minutes before marinating.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the marinade early:** Mix the marinade, add the chicken and refrigerate it for three to twelve hours.
2. **Prepare vegetables before handling the chicken:** During the final twenty minutes of the soak, peel and cut the potatoes and put them in their pot with the cold water and cooking salt. Leave the heat off. Trim the beans, dice the shallots and measure the lemon and potato dairy. Set out the sheet pan and oven-safe rack, a serving rack and tray, thermometer, potato pot, bean pot, dairy pan, 12-inch skillet and masher. Preheat the oven to 400°F / 205°C.
3. **Coat the chicken and start potatoes:** Prepare the crumbs, drain the excess marinade, coat the pieces, arrange them on the rack and put them in the oven. Bring the potato pot to a boil, then simmer until the one-inch pieces are completely tender. Warm the dairy during the simmer.
4. **Prepare the bean finish and check chicken:** While the potatoes simmer, soften the shallots in the skillet for five to seven minutes, then take the skillet off the heat. Heat the bean water in its pot. Begin checking the chicken pieces at thirty minutes and take each one out when it reaches 165°F / 74°C. Drain, briefly dry and mash the potatoes with the warm dairy. Cover the pot to keep the mash warm.
5. **Cook beans near the end:** Once the potatoes are mashed and the last chicken pieces are nearly done, boil the beans until they bite cleanly, about ten minutes with draining. Warm them with the softened shallots. Add the lemon off the heat just before plating.

6. **Rest, divide and serve:** Rest the last chicken pieces for five minutes on their rack, uncovered. Divide the mash and beans among four plates, then set the chicken alongside, clear of the mash and bean juices.

## Timing and equipment adjustments

The potato pot, bean pot and shallot skillet take three burners at once. Warm the potato dairy before the bean skillet goes on. On a smaller stove, cook the beans a day ahead, chill them and reheat them in the shallot skillet before the lemon finish.

For five diners, scale each component so every diner gets at least one substantial chicken piece. A larger chicken batch needs a second sheet pan and rack. Space the pieces, swap the shelf positions during baking and allow extra time.

## Leftovers

Store the chicken, mash and beans separately and use them within three to four days. The chicken coating and green beans soften in storage, and the mash needs a little more dairy when reheated. The meal contains dairy, and ordinary cornflakes often contain barley malt. Use gluten-free cereal for a gluten-free meal.
