## Meal Overview

Beef chuck with carrots and potatoes in a wine-rich sauce, served with hot garlic bread. Make one full batch of [Classic Beef Stew](/mise/recipes/classic-beef-stew/) for six and one full batch of [Garlic Bread](/mise/recipes/garlic-bread/), two bread pieces per person.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Allow the full afternoon:** Plan about 4 hours 30 minutes from raw ingredients, including a buffer beyond the stew's four-hour estimate. Prepare a Dutch oven, bread sheet pan and thermometer; set out the bread butter to soften. An unfamiliar cut may take longer to become fork-tender.
2. **Build the stew:** Follow the component's batch searing, pancetta, onion and celery steps, then its tomato paste, garlic and wine reduction. Add the beef, thyme and stock for a gentle covered braise. Keep the carrots and potatoes out for now.
3. **Add vegetables at the tenderness check:** After about two hours of braising, check several beef pieces. Add carrots and potatoes only when the beef is starting to soften, then allow the component's additional 35–50 minutes. If the beef still resists a fork, give it longer before adding the vegetables.
4. **Prepare the bread during the braise:** Make the garlic spread and cut the bread, using the full component quantities. When the vegetables are nearly tender and the stew is approaching its finish, heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C. Bake the bread for 12–15 minutes, using its hot-center and golden-edge cues.
5. **Finish together:** Complete the stew's peas, sauce adjustment and vinegar finish while the bread bakes. Divide the stew among six bowls and serve with the bread. If the beef needs more time, delay baking the bread rather than serving tough meat to meet the estimate.

## Notes

The hands-on estimate includes preparation, batch searing and bread assembly; much of the elapsed time is gentle braising. This plan starts with raw beef, not reheated stew.
