## Meal Overview

Beef chuck with carrots and potatoes in a red wine sauce, served with hot garlic bread. The stew braises gently for most of the afternoon, and the bread bakes while the stew finishes.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Brown the beef:** Season and flour the beef and brown it in three batches in a Dutch oven. Set the butter for the bread out to soften.
2. **Build the braise:** Cook the pancetta, onion and celery, toast the tomato paste and reduce the wine. Return the beef with the thyme and stock, cover and braise on low for 2 hours.
3. **Add the vegetables:** Stir in the carrots and potatoes once the beef is starting to soften. Simmer covered until the beef is fork-tender, 35 to 50 minutes.
4. **Bake the bread:** Heat the oven to 400°F when the vegetables go in. Spread the garlic butter on the bread and bake until the edges are golden, 12 to 15 minutes.
5. **Finish the stew:** Stir in the peas and balsamic vinegar and season the stew. Ladle it into six bowls and serve with the hot bread.
