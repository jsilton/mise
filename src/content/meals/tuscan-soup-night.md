## Meal Overview

Zuppa Toscana with Italian sausage, potatoes, kale and cream, with garlic bread for dunking and a lemony arugula salad. The soup is one pot and under an hour.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the soup:** Brown the sausage in a Dutch oven, about 8 minutes, and move it to a plate. Soften the onion in the fat and stir in the garlic and pepper flakes.
2. **Simmer the potatoes:** Add the potatoes, broth, salt and pepper and simmer until a fork slides through the potatoes, 10 to 12 minutes.
3. **Bake the bread:** Heat the oven to 400°F while the soup simmers. Bake the garlic bread until the edges are golden, 12 to 15 minutes.
4. **Finish the soup:** Stir in the cream and sausage and heat gently for 5 minutes without boiling. Wilt the kale for 1 to 3 minutes.
5. **Serve:** Toss the arugula with its dressing. Ladle the soup into bowls and serve it with the bread and salad.
