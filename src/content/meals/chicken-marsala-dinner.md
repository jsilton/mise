## Meal Overview

Chicken thighs in a Marsala and mushroom sauce over garlic butter pearl couscous, with broiled asparagus under Parmesan and lemon. The couscous soaks up the sauce, and the asparagus takes the last 6 minutes. For five, make one and a quarter batches of the chicken, using about 2 lb of thighs.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Brown the chicken:** Pound, season and dredge the thighs. Brown them in two batches and move them to a plate.
2. **Start the couscous:** Toast the couscous in butter, add the garlic and broth and simmer covered until tender, 10 to 12 minutes. Leave it covered off the heat.
3. **Make the sauce:** Brown the prosciutto and mushrooms, 6 to 8 minutes. Reduce the Marsala by half, add the stock and simmer the chicken in the sauce until it reaches 165°F.
4. **Broil the asparagus:** Heat the broiler while the Marsala reduces. Broil the asparagus until the tips char, 4 to 6 minutes, then top it with the Parmesan and lemon.
5. **Finish and serve:** Swirl the cold butter and parsley into the sauce. Fluff the couscous with the rest of its butter and serve it under the chicken.
