## Meal Overview

Chef Kriger's sweet and savory brisket with garlic mashed red potatoes and butter-glazed carrots. The brisket is braised a day ahead so it slices cleanly when cold, then it reheats in all of its sauce while the sides cook.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Braise the day before:** Season the brisket and set it in the pan with the carrots, onion and sauce. Cover tightly and braise at 325°F until a fork turns easily in the thickest part, 3 1/2 to 5 hours.
2. **Chill overnight:** Move the brisket, vegetables and sauce to shallow containers. Refrigerate for at least 8 hours.
3. **Slice and reheat:** About 1 hour 15 minutes before dinner, heat the oven to 325°F. Lift off the set fat, slice the cold brisket thinly across the grain and lay it in a baking dish. Boil the sauce, pour it over the slices, cover and bake until hot through, 45 minutes to 1 hour.
4. **Mash the potatoes:** Simmer the potatoes and garlic until they crush easily, 15 to 20 minutes. Drain and dry them, then mash them with the butter, Parmesan and warm milk.
5. **Glaze the carrots:** Simmer the carrot matchsticks until just tender, 3 to 4 minutes. Toss them in the butter and brown sugar glaze until glossy.
6. **Serve:** Bring the brisket to the table in its sauce with the potatoes and carrots.
