## Meal Overview

Crisp-skinned salmon with lemon-oregano dressing, herbed orzo, chickpeas, tomatoes and feta, alongside whole roasted cauliflower with tahini and shaved fennel citrus salad. The salmon recipe already supplies pasta and legumes; no separate quinoa batch is needed.

Use one batch of the salmon dish and cauliflower for four diners, with **half a batch of fennel salad** shared as a small fresh side. The main provides four 6–8 oz fish portions, one cup dry orzo, one cup drained chickpeas, tomatoes and feta. Choose a cauliflower near its stated 900 g size and use only its listed half cup of prepared tahini sauce; refrigerate any remaining sauce.

For the half salad, use **one large fennel bulb, one large orange, optional half a small grapefruit, 2 tbsp oil, 1/2 tbsp each lemon juice and captured citrus juice, 1/2 tsp honey or agave, 1/8 tsp each kosher salt and pepper, 1 tbsp shaved Parmesan and optional 2 tbsp olives**. Fronds come from that bulb. Add dressing and salt gradually after tasting with the main’s feta and lemon dressing. This is a generous dinner with several components. Allow about **2 hours 20 minutes**, including the whole cauliflower's covered and uncovered stages, then the salmon recipe's chickpea roast at a lower oven temperature. A large cauliflower or a second salmon pan batch can take longer.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the whole cauliflower:** Follow its complete **425°F / 220°C** method: approximately **30–40 minutes covered**, then **30–40 minutes uncovered**, with preparation and a short rest. Tenderness at the core governs; this is not a twenty-minute tray of florets.
2. **Prepare the cold components:** During cauliflower cooking, prepare its tahini sauce and the half-batch fennel-citrus salad ingredients, allowing about twenty minutes for the salad knife work. Refrigerate salad components and dress just before serving. Measure the salmon recipe's separate oil allocations, prepare its vegetables and dressing, and keep raw fish refrigerated.
3. **Change the oven for chickpeas:** Once cauliflower is tender, take it out and lower the oven to **400°F / 205°C**. Roast the salmon recipe's chickpeas according to its **25–35-minute** method, checking the hot oven early after the temperature change. The cauliflower may be served warm rather than piping hot; if holding longer, keep it at **140°F / 60°C or above** using suitable separate equipment.
4. **Cook orzo and salmon:** Cook the main recipe's orzo by its package timing. Sear fish near the end of the chickpea roast, using enough pan space and checking each portion for **145°F / 63°C** at its thickest center. Allow extra time if fish requires successive batches. Shake the olive-oil dressing again at service; it contains no butter.
5. **Finish together:** Dress the fennel salad, add the cauliflower's measured tahini finish, and plate salmon with its orzo and chickpeas. Keep salmon skin facing up and spoon dressing around it to retain some crispness.

## Leftovers

Refrigerate cooked components in shallow containers within **2 hours**, or **1 hour above 90°F / 32°C**, at **40°F / 4°C or below**. Follow each component's storage guidance; reheat cooked leftovers to **165°F / 74°C**. Keep salad separate and use promptly for texture.
