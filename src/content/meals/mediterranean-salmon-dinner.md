## Meal Overview

Crisp-skinned salmon with lemon-oregano dressing, herbed orzo, chickpeas, tomatoes and feta, alongside whole roasted cauliflower with tahini and shaved fennel citrus salad.

Use one batch of the salmon dish and cauliflower for four diners, with **half a batch of fennel salad** shared as a small fresh side. The main provides four 6 to 8 oz fish portions, one cup dry orzo, one cup drained chickpeas, tomatoes and feta. Buy a cauliflower of about 900 g and dress it with half a cup of the tahini sauce. Refrigerate the remaining sauce.

For the half salad, use **one large fennel bulb, one large orange, half a small grapefruit, 2 tbsp oil, 1/2 tbsp each lemon juice and captured citrus juice, 1/2 tsp honey, 1/8 tsp each kosher salt and pepper, 1 tbsp shaved Parmesan and 2 tbsp olives**. The fronds come from that bulb. Allow about **2 hours 20 minutes**, including the whole cauliflower's covered and uncovered stages, then the salmon recipe's chickpea roast at a lower oven temperature.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the whole cauliflower:** Roast it at **425°F / 220°C** for **30 to 40 minutes covered**, then **30 to 40 minutes uncovered**, until a knife slides into the core.
2. **Prepare the cold components:** While the cauliflower roasts, make its tahini sauce and prepare the half-batch fennel salad, about twenty minutes of knife work. Refrigerate the salad components. Prepare the salmon recipe's vegetables and dressing.
3. **Change the oven for chickpeas:** Once the cauliflower is tender, take it out and lower the oven to **400°F / 205°C**. Roast the chickpeas for **25 to 35 minutes**. The cauliflower is served warm.
4. **Cook orzo and salmon:** Boil the orzo for its package time. Sear the fish near the end of the chickpea roast until each portion reaches **145°F / 63°C** at its thickest point. Shake the olive-oil dressing again before serving.
5. **Finish together:** Dress the fennel salad, spoon the tahini over the cauliflower, and plate the salmon with its orzo and chickpeas. Keep the salmon skin facing up and spoon the dressing around it so the skin stays crisp.

## Leftovers

Refrigerate cooked components separately. Keep the salad separate and eat it soon, while it is crisp.
