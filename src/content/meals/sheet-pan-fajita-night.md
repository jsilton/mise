## Meal Overview

Chicken and three colors of peppers in warm tortillas, with cilantro-lime rice, lightly thickened black beans and chunky guacamole. Make **one full batch of each recipe** for six diners. The main supplies two pounds of chicken and twelve tortillas. Share the rice and beans as six small sides. The guacamole replaces the main recipe's sliced avocado.

The rice starts with 180 g dry rice, the beans use two undrained cans, and the guacamole starts with four avocados. Put some filling inside each tortilla and serve the sides alongside.

Allow about **75 minutes elapsed and 65 minutes hands-on** for one cook. Use two rimmed half-sheet pans, separate rice and bean saucepans, a mixing bowl and a microwave for tortillas. In an oven that fits only one sheet pan, cook in two rounds and allow another 20 to 30 minutes.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prep the produce first:** Wash and prepare the produce and cilantro.
2. **Start the fajitas:** Heat the oven to **375°F / 190°C**. Slice the chicken and vegetables to an even size, toss them with the seasoning and spread the filling across both pans.
3. **Start rice as the chicken goes into the oven:** The rice takes forty minutes, including heating and its covered rest.
4. **Simmer the beans while the pans roast:** Warm the garlic and cumin, add the beans with their canning liquid and the oregano, then simmer and mash a few beans until the sauce lightly coats them, about twenty-five minutes in all. Add the lime and season at the end.
5. **Make guacamole while the pans roast:** Start it once the beans are simmering, so it goes to the table fresh.
6. **Check both pans:** Roast until the thickest chicken strips on each pan reach **165°F / 74°C**. If the vegetables need longer, move the chicken to a covered bowl and return the pans to the oven.
7. **Finish and serve:** Add the main's remaining lime, fluff and finish the rice, and season the guacamole. Warm the tortillas in small stacks in the microwave. Share the filling, rice, beans and guacamole among six diners, with two tortillas each. Put the salsa and hot sauce on the table.

## Timing adjustments

Keep rice, beans and cooked chicken covered while they wait, and loosen the beans with a splash of water. Press plastic wrap onto the surface of the guacamole until serving. Long holds soften vegetables and dry chicken.

The guacamole is made without its jalapeño, and hot sauce goes on the table. The chicken seasoning has its own chile heat.

## Leftovers

Refrigerate chicken and vegetables, rice and beans separately and use them within three to four days. Press plastic wrap onto the surface of the guacamole and eat it the next day.
