## Meal Overview

Grilled chicken thighs under a basil and mint corn salsa, with crisp smashed potatoes and grilled zucchini with lemon. The potatoes take the longest, so they start first and roast while the grill does the rest. For five, grill 2 3/4 lb of thighs and boil 2 lb of potatoes.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Boil the potatoes:** Boil the potatoes in salted water until a fork slides in, 15 to 20 minutes. Heat the oven to 425°F and the grill to medium-high.
2. **Smash and roast:** Smash the potatoes on a lined sheet, let them steam dry for 5 minutes and season them. Roast until deep golden and crisp, 25 to 30 minutes, flipping once.
3. **Grill the chicken:** Season the thighs and grill them skin side down first until the skin is crisp and the meat reaches 165°F, 20 to 25 minutes.
4. **Grill the corn:** Grill the corn beside the chicken until charred in spots, about 10 minutes.
5. **Grill the zucchini:** Move the chicken to a platter to rest. Grill the zucchini planks for 3 to 4 minutes per side and dress them with the lemon and mint.
6. **Make the salsa:** Pulse the herb sauce and toss it with the corn kernels.
7. **Serve:** Spoon the corn salsa over the chicken and serve with the potatoes and zucchini.
