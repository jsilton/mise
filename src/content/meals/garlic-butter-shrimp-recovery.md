## Meal Overview

Garlic-butter shrimp with lemon, plain short-grain rice, sautéed green beans and peppery arugula salad. Make one full batch of each component for four diners. Allow about 75 minutes from starting the rice, including its thirty-minute soak and ten-minute covered rest; shrimp preparation and the vegetables fit around that longer rice process. The full batches supply 1 1/2 lb prepared shrimp, 300 g dry rice, one pound green beans and 5 oz arugula with 20 g Parmesan. Divide each into four portions. This combines Italian-American shrimp and vegetables with plain Japanese-style rice; do not season the rice as sushi rice.

Use a lidded rice saucepan, a 12-inch lidded bean skillet and a separate shrimp skillet. Allow about 45 minutes of hands-on work; washing loose greens or extra bean/shrimp rounds adds time.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the rice first:** Follow the complete short-grain rice recipe, including rinsing, a thirty-minute soak in measured water, heating, covered cooking and a ten-minute rest. It takes about 65 minutes, not a quick unattended warming.
2. **Prepare the other components during the soak:** Trim and dry beans, mince the shrimp garlic, slice the beans' garlic separately, prepare lemon and parsley, and wash and dry salad greens. Make dressing but keep it separate. Dry prepared thawed shrimp last and clean raw-contact tools.
3. **Cook rice and then beans:** Put the soaked rice over heat on schedule. Once the rice is simmering, cook the prepared beans in their own skillet, allowing their full ten-to-fifteen-minute cooking stage. Brown first, check the center and use the conditional covered steam before adding garlic over lower heat. Additional rounds add five to eight minutes each. Finish beans before beginning the attended shrimp sauce; keep them covered for that short wait and expect some softening.
4. **Cook shrimp last:** Follow the main's moderate to medium-high heat and single-layer batches. Remove cooked shrimp before making the garlic and wine reduction; judge their centers by the main's endpoint, not a fixed two-minutes-per-side rule. Return only briefly to coat with the finished sauce. Allow about fifteen minutes for this stage.
5. **Finish and serve:** Fluff rested rice, dress arugula at the last moment and divide shrimp, rice, beans and salad among four plates. Keep rice hot at 140°F / 60°C or above if it must wait briefly; longer waits also soften the beans.

## Notes

- Don't overcrowd the pan with shrimp - cook in single-layer batches
- Use the measured lemon in each component and taste a complete bite before adding more acid or salt.
- If using previously cooked rice, follow the rice recipe’s storage and reheating instructions and heat it to 165°F / 74°C throughout.

Refrigerate cooked leftovers separately in shallow containers within **2 hours**, or **1 hour above 90°F / 32°C**, at **40°F / 4°C or below**. Use green beans within three days and rice/shrimp within three to four days; reheat cooked components to **165°F / 74°C**. Refrigerate dressed salad and use by the next day for texture.
