## Meal Overview

Garlic-butter shrimp with lemon, plain short-grain rice, sautéed green beans and peppery arugula salad. Make one full batch of each component for four diners. Allow about 75 minutes from starting the rice, including its thirty-minute soak and ten-minute covered rest. Shrimp preparation and the vegetables fit around that longer rice process. The full batches supply 1 1/2 lb prepared shrimp, 300 g dry rice, one pound green beans and 5 oz arugula with 20 g Parmesan. Divide each into four portions. The rice is plain, with no sushi seasoning.

Use a lidded rice saucepan, a 12-inch lidded bean skillet and a separate shrimp skillet. Allow about 45 minutes of hands-on work.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the rice first:** Rinse the rice and soak it in its cooking water for thirty minutes. Cook it covered, then rest it for ten minutes. The whole process takes about 65 minutes.
2. **Prepare the other components during the soak:** Trim and dry beans, mince the shrimp garlic, slice the beans' garlic separately, prepare lemon and parsley, and wash and dry salad greens. Make the dressing and keep it separate. Dry the thawed shrimp last.
3. **Cook rice and then beans:** Put the soaked rice over heat. Once the rice is simmering, brown the beans in their own skillet, cover them to steam until tender, then add the garlic over lower heat. This takes ten to fifteen minutes. Keep the beans covered while the shrimp cook.
4. **Cook shrimp last:** Cook the shrimp over medium-high heat in single-layer batches until just opaque, and move them to a plate. Make the garlic and wine reduction, then return the shrimp briefly to coat them with the sauce. This stage takes about fifteen minutes.
5. **Finish and serve:** Fluff the rested rice, dress the arugula at the last moment and divide the shrimp, rice, beans and salad among four plates.

## Notes

Refrigerate cooked leftovers separately. Use the green beans within three days and the rice and shrimp within three to four days. Dressed salad keeps until the next day.
