## Meal Overview

A 30 minute soup night. Frozen mini wontons simmer in ginger broth with baby bok choy and shiitakes, and a cold smashed cucumber salad sits beside the bowls. For five, cook 50 wontons in 7 1/2 cups of broth.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the cucumbers:** Smash and salt the cucumbers and let them drain in the fridge for 15 minutes. Mix their dressing.
2. **Infuse the broth:** Bring the broth, ginger and garlic to a boil and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. **Cook the vegetables and wontons:** Simmer the bok choy and shiitakes for about 3 minutes. Add the frozen wontons and simmer until they float, 3 to 5 minutes.
4. **Dress the cucumbers:** Toss the cucumbers with their dressing and top them with the sesame seeds and scallion.
5. **Serve:** Season the soup with the soy sauce, sesame oil and white pepper. Ladle it into four bowls and top with the scallion greens.
