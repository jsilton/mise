## Meal Overview

Korean barbecue at home: flanken short ribs marinated overnight in soy and pear, grilled fast over charcoal and served with rice, fresh kimchi, spicy cucumbers and sesame spinach. The kimchi and the marinade are made the day before. On the day, dinner takes 1 hour 15 minutes.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Make the kimchi the day before:** Salt the vegetables for 1 hour, drain and squeeze them and toss them with the dressing. Refrigerate overnight.
2. **Marinate the ribs:** While the kimchi vegetables stand, rub the ribs with the sake and sugar and pour the soy marinade over them. Refrigerate for at least 8 hours, turning once.
3. **Start the rice:** On the day, rinse and soak the rice 1 hour 15 minutes before dinner. Cook it and leave it covered.
4. **Dress the cucumbers:** Salt the cucumbers for 20 minutes, blot them dry and toss them with their dressing.
5. **Season the spinach:** Blanch and squeeze the spinach and toss it with the sesame oil, garlic and scallion.
6. **Light the grill:** Light the charcoal 30 minutes before dinner and bank the coals to one side. Oil the grates.
7. **Grill the ribs:** Grill the ribs over direct heat until seared and lightly charred, 2 to 3 minutes per side.
8. **Rest and serve:** Rest the ribs for 5 minutes and cut between the bones with kitchen shears. Serve with the rice, kimchi, cucumbers and spinach.
