## Meal Overview

Lightly browned paneer and chopped spinach in a creamy fenugreek sauce, with plain basmati rice and cool cucumber raita. The spinach is the vegetable as well as the sauce. The yogurt, cucumber and fresh herbs give a tangy contrast to the ghee and cream.

Allow about **90 to 95 minutes elapsed and 70 to 80 minutes hands-on** for one cook starting with fresh spinach, dry rice and uncut vegetables. Use two burners, a 6-quart blanching pot, a deep 12-inch nonstick skillet, a narrow 1 1/2 to 2-quart rice saucepan and a small pan for the cumin and the chile ghee. Heat the paneer soaking water in a kettle.

## Portions for Four

- **Saag paneer:** Make the full [Saag Paneer](/mise/recipes/saag-paneer): **397 g paneer and 680 g trimmed fresh spinach**, with all the recipe's onion, three tablespoons ghee, 80 ml cream, fenugreek and spices. Divide the cheese and greens evenly among four. Spoon the separate tablespoon of chile ghee over the saag at the table.
- **Rice:** Make **half the [Basmati Rice](/mise/recipes/basmati-rice)** using **200 g dry aged white basmati, 360 ml water, 1 1/2 tsp ghee and 3/8 tsp fine salt**, with **1 tbsp hot correction water** reserved. Leave out the cumin, cinnamon and bay for plain rice. This gives about **3 cups cooked rice**, about **3/4 cup each**.
- **Raita:** Make **half the [Cucumber Raita](/mise/recipes/cucumber-raita)**: **240 g plain unstrained whole-milk yogurt, 150 g cucumber, 1/2 tsp cumin seeds, 2 tbsp chopped cilantro and 1/8 tsp fine salt**. The **1 1/4 to 1 1/2 cups** supplies about **five to six tablespoons per person**. Keep the raita cold until serving.

## Cooking Strategy

The rice simmers while you prepare raita and brown paneer, then rests while the spinach sauce finishes.

1. **0 to 20 minutes, prepare the main:** Put the blanching water on to boil and set up the ice bath. Wash and trim the spinach, dice the onion and prepare the ginger, garlic and chile. Measure the saag spices, cream, lemon, salt and ghee. Cube the paneer and pat it dry. Keep the soaking salt apart from the seasoning salt.
2. **20 to 35 minutes, blanch, cool and chop:** Blanch the spinach in four batches, letting the pot return to a boil between them. Cool it in the ice bath, drain it, squeeze out the dripping liquid and chop it. Leave the greens moist. Clear the large pot off the stove.
3. **35 to 43 minutes, start rice:** Rinse and drain the rice. Heat its ghee in the rice saucepan, coat the grains, add the 360 ml water and 3/8 tsp salt, and bring to a gentle boil. Cover and lower to a gentle simmer for 15 to 18 minutes. If the grains are hard and the pot is dry at the end, add the tablespoon of hot water.
4. **43 to 53 minutes, make raita:** Toast the cumin in the small dry pan for 1 to 2 minutes and let it cool. Grate the cucumber and let it drain for about five minutes while measuring the yogurt and chopping the cilantro. Squeeze it gently, mix it with the yogurt, crushed cumin, cilantro and salt, and refrigerate. Wash and dry the small pan for the chile ghee.
5. **53 to 67 minutes, brown paneer:** Prepare the hot salted soaking water. Brown the paneer in two batches in its browning ghee and transfer it to the soaking bowl. When the rice finishes its simmer, give it a ten-minute covered rest, then leave it covered off the heat until serving.
6. **67 to 82 minutes, build the spinach sauce:** Cook the cumin and onion in the masala ghee, 8 to 10 minutes for the onion. Add the ginger, garlic and chile, then the ground spices and the spinach with its first salt. Cook the greens until hot and evenly coated. Boil off any puddle, and loosen a dry pan with a little of the sauce water. The paneer soaks during this work.
7. **82 to 95 minutes, finish and serve:** Add the cream and fenugreek, drain the paneer and heat it gently in the sauce until hot in the center. Off the heat, stir in the garam masala, lemon and finishing salt. Make the chile ghee in the small pan. Fluff the rice, stir the chilled raita, and divide all three components among four diners.

## For Five Diners

Follow the same sequence with these quantities in place of the four-person amounts. Small seasoning measures are rounded for practical spoons. Allow **10 to 15 extra minutes** for the additional spinach batch, cutting and pan handling. Use a 2-quart rice saucepan and the same deep 12-inch skillet, cooking the paneer in two uncrowded batches of about 250 g.

- **Saag:** **500 g paneer, 850 g trimmed fresh spinach, 190 g diced onion, 3 3/4 tbsp ghee** split **1 1/4 tbsp for browning and 2 1/2 tbsp for masala**, **1 1/4 tsp cumin seeds, 1 1/4 tbsp grated ginger, five minced garlic cloves, 1 1/4 tbsp diced jalapeño, 5/8 tsp turmeric, 1 1/4 tsp ground coriander, 5/8 tsp initial fine salt, 3/8 tsp finishing salt, 100 ml heavy cream, 1 1/4 tbsp loosely measured dried fenugreek leaves, 1 1/4 tsp garam masala and 1 1/4 tbsp lemon juice**. Reserve **75 ml sauce water**. Use the same **3 liters blanching water and 2 liters cold water with 500 g ice**, working in five small spinach batches. Add fresh cold water or ice if the cooling bath warms. For paneer, use **900 ml hot drinking water and 5/8 tsp fine salt**, then discard after soaking. The chile ghee is **1 1/4 tbsp ghee with 5/8 tsp Kashmiri chile powder**.
- **Rice:** **250 g dry aged white basmati, 450 ml water, 2 tsp ghee and 1/2 tsp fine salt**, with **1 tbsp plus 3/4 tsp hot correction water** reserved. Leave out the whole spices. Divide the finished pot into five portions.
- **Raita:** **300 g plain unstrained whole-milk yogurt, 190 g cucumber, 5/8 tsp cumin seeds, 2 1/2 tbsp chopped cilantro and 1/8 tsp fine salt**. Toast, grate, drain and mix as for four, then chill.

For the spoon measures, **5/8 tsp is 1/2 plus 1/8 tsp**, and **3/8 tsp is 1/4 plus 1/8 tsp**.

## Prepare Ahead

With a **fully cooked, refrigerated batch of saag paneer**, allow about **45 to 50 minutes elapsed and 25 to 35 minutes hands-on** for fresh rice and raita plus reheating. Start the rice, prepare raita during its simmer, and reheat the saag gently on the other burner until steaming hot, loosening it with a little water.

The main recipe also has a frozen-spinach version, which skips the blanching batches.

## Leftovers

Keep rice, saag and raita separate. Use cooked rice and saag within three to four days. Raita keeps for one day.
