## Meal Overview

Orecchiette with pancetta, sweet peas and lemon ricotta, finished with Parmesan. Make one full batch for three generous main portions: twelve ounces dry pasta, four ounces pancetta and one cup peas with the complete sauce. This is a pasta-centered dinner; it does not assume a particular adult-and-child split or include a separate salad.

Allow about 40 minutes using a large pasta pot, a deep 12-inch skillet and a small saucepan or microwave-safe bowl for the peas. Most stages need some attention; allow about 30 minutes hands-on. A large heatproof bowl is useful if the skillet remains too hot for the cheese finish.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start water and prepare:** Put pasta water on to heat before rendering pancetta. Mince shallot and garlic separately, grate cheese and prepare the measured lemon juice and zest. Read the pea and pasta cooking instructions.
2. **Render and retain fat:** Crisp pancetta as the main directs and lift onto a clean plate. Retain fat in proportion to the amount of pancetta cooked: about 1 1/2–2 tablespoons for each 4 ounces, using only what the pork actually releases. It is not an additional ingredient to buy.
3. **Build the base while pasta cooks:** Soften shallot in retained fat, then briefly cook garlic. Add wine or stock and reduce. Coordinate the pasta by its package timing and taste before draining; do not deliberately undercook it for an off-heat finish.
4. **Cook peas completely:** Follow frozen-pea package directions, or cook fresh peas until tender. Drain and have them hot before the cheese enters. Reserve the main's pasta water and combine pasta, peas and aromatic base.
5. **Finish off heat:** Move the skillet off the burner and wait until sizzling stops, transferring to a bowl if needed. Fold in ricotta, Parmesan, zest, measured lemon juice and cooking water as directed. Taste before adding salt, then divide among three bowls and top with crisp pancetta. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Wine reduction does not establish an alcohol-free sauce; use the listed stock option when avoiding wine.
- An egg-free version requires pasta and prepared ingredients without egg. The sauce itself does not use eggs.
- Refrigerate leftovers promptly in shallow containers within two hours, or one hour above 90°F /32°C, at 40°F /4°C or below. Keep pancetta separate if possible, use the next day for texture and reheat pasta to 165°F /74°C; the sauce will be less smooth.
