## Meal Overview

Orecchiette with pancetta, sweet peas and lemon ricotta, finished with Parmesan. Make one full batch for three generous main portions: twelve ounces dry pasta, four ounces pancetta and one cup peas with the complete sauce.

Allow about 40 minutes, with about 30 minutes hands-on. Use a large pasta pot, a deep 12-inch skillet and a small saucepan for the peas.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start water and prepare:** Put pasta water on to heat before rendering pancetta. Mince shallot and garlic separately, grate the cheese and zest and juice the lemon.
2. **Render and retain fat:** Crisp the pancetta and lift it onto a plate. Keep about 1 1/2 to 2 tablespoons of its fat in the skillet.
3. **Build the base while pasta cooks:** Soften the shallot in the fat, then briefly cook the garlic. Add the wine and reduce it. Boil the pasta until tender.
4. **Cook peas:** Boil the peas until tender and drain them. Save some pasta water, then combine the pasta, hot peas and aromatic base.
5. **Finish off heat:** Move the skillet off the burner and wait until the sizzling stops. Fold in the ricotta, Parmesan, zest, lemon juice and pasta water. Season, divide among three bowls and top with the crisp pancetta. Serve right away.

## Notes

- Use the stock swap in place of the wine for an alcohol-free sauce.
- The sauce has no egg. Check the pasta package for an egg-free meal.
- Refrigerate leftovers with the pancetta separate and eat them the next day. The reheated sauce is less smooth.
