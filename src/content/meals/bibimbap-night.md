## Meal Overview

Rice bowls topped with bulgogi, three vegetable banchan, a fried egg and gochujang sauce. Each topping is a small recipe of its own, and they all fit inside the hour the rice takes. Everyone mixes their own bowl at the table. For five, fry a fifth egg and share the rice and toppings five ways.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the rice:** Rinse the rice and soak it for 30 minutes. Cook it and leave it covered until the bowls are ready.
2. **Marinate the beef:** Toss the sliced ribeye with its marinade and refrigerate it for 30 minutes.
3. **Salt the cucumbers:** Salt the cucumbers and let them drain for 20 minutes. Toss them with the gochugaru dressing.
4. **Cook the vegetables:** Steam the bean sprouts in a covered pot for about 10 minutes and season them. Blanch, squeeze and season the spinach.
5. **Sear the beef:** Sear the beef in batches in a smoking-hot skillet until the edges char.
6. **Fry the eggs:** Stir the gochujang sauce. Fry four eggs sunny side up until the whites set, 2 to 3 minutes.
7. **Build the bowls:** Divide the rice among four bowls. Arrange the beef and vegetables in piles, set an egg in the center and spoon the sauce over the top.
