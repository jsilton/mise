## Meal Overview

Use one full Cooked-Chicken Taco Bar recipe and one full Skillet Esquites recipe for six people. That gives eighteen small tortillas, 600 g cooked chicken, fresh toppings and six corn sides.

This 55-minute plan starts with cooked chicken. Cabbage and radish balance the soft filling and creamy toppings. The corn supplies a small warm vegetable side.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Gather and prepare the cold ingredients:** Measure both recipes and keep the lime, dairy and oil for each one separate. Drain and dry corn, make its dressing, and prepare cabbage, radish, onion and cilantro. Keep dairy and salsa chilled. Slice avocado later.
2. **Brown the corn in two batches:** Use a 12-inch skillet, 1 tbsp oil and 300 g corn per batch. Cook each batch until it has browned patches, about 5 to 7 minutes. Transfer the corn to a wide plate and let the steam die down. Fold it into its dressing and finishing ingredients.
3. **Reheat the chicken:** Wipe out the skillet. Warm the chicken in it with the oil, cumin and broth, covered, stirring now and then until it is steaming hot. Season it with the lime and salt.
4. **Warm tortillas in batches:** Cover the filling off the heat. Microwave four to six tortillas at a time between damp paper towels until hot and flexible, 30 to 45 seconds per batch. Wrap the warm tortillas in a towel.
5. **Finish and serve:** Slice the avocado and squeeze its lime over it. Bring out the cold toppings and divide the corn into six sides. Serve three small tacos per person, with about one-eighteenth of the filling in each tortilla. Set out the salsa, crema, cheese and hot sauce.

## Notes

For a long party, keep the filling warm in a covered pan over low heat and bring the cold toppings out in small bowls from the refrigerator.
