## Meal Overview

Use one full Cooked-Chicken Taco Bar recipe and one full Skillet Esquites recipe for six people. That gives eighteen small tortillas, 600 g cooked chicken, measured fresh toppings and six corn sides. If choosing the cooked-pork variation, use 600 g cooked meat total across both choices.

This 55-minute plan starts with cooked meat. It does not include preparing carnitas or roasting chicken from raw. The main already provides avocado, salsa and tortillas, so a second guacamole recipe and rice bowl would repeat components. Cabbage and radish balance the soft filling and creamy toppings; the corn supplies a small warm vegetable side.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Gather and prepare the cold ingredients:** Confirm the cooked meat has been stored appropriately and is within its original refrigerated storage window. Measure both recipes, keeping their lime, dairy and oil allocations separate. Drain and dry corn, make its dressing, and prepare cabbage, radish, onion and cilantro. Keep dairy and salsa chilled; slice avocado later.
2. **Brown the corn in two batches:** Use the esquites recipe's 12-inch skillet, 1 tbsp oil and 300 g corn per batch. Allow roughly 5–7 minutes per batch and look for browned patches. Transfer to a wide plate, pause until vigorous steaming subsides, then combine with its dressing and finishing ingredients. It can wait briefly while the chicken reheats.
3. **Reheat the cooked filling:** In the same cleaned skillet, follow the main's oil/cumin/broth method. Cover and stir periodically, adding broth only as needed. Check several portions reach 165°F / 74°C, then taste before adding lime or salt. For cooked pork use the main's reduced-oil variation; this is reheating, not a new raw-pork cooking method.
4. **Warm tortillas in batches:** With the filling covered briefly off heat, microwave four to six tortillas at a time between damp paper towels, 30–45 seconds per batch or until hot and flexible. A second dry skillet is another option but requires more hands-on attention. Wrap warmed tortillas briefly in a clean towel.
5. **Finish and serve:** Slice avocado and add its measured lime. Bring out the main's cold toppings and divide corn into six sides. Serve three small tacos per person, using about one-eighteenth of the filling per tortilla before toppings. Offer the measured salsa, crema, optional cheese and heat for individual choice.

## Notes

There are no extra beans, rice or unspecified cheese bowls to prepare. A prolonged party service needs temperature control rather than the brief covered wait used here: keep hot filling at least 140°F / 60°C and cold toppings at most 40°F / 4°C. Otherwise chill perishables within two hours, or one hour above 90°F / 32°C, following the component guidance.
