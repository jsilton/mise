## Meal Overview

Use one full batch each of Panang Curry with Chicken, Plain Short-Grain White Rice, and Edamame with Sea Salt for four diners. The curry supplies 450 g raw chicken, 150 g pepper, 300 ml coconut milk and ground peanuts. Rice supplies 300 g dry grain across the meal. The 450 g edamame weight includes discarded pods, so it makes four small sides.

This is a Thai curry dinner with a plain short-grain rice base and a Japanese-style pod side. Rice provides a mild contrast to the concentrated coconut-peanut sauce. Leave the sesame oil and pepper flakes off the edamame. Allow about 70 to 75 minutes for one cook using two burners.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start rice soaking:** Rinse the rice and soak it for 30 minutes in its cooking water. Set out the rice saucepan, a wide curry skillet and a pot for edamame.
2. **Prepare curry ingredients:** During the soak, grind the peanuts, finely slice the lime leaves and pepper, measure the coconut milk and seasonings, then slice the chicken and refrigerate it.
3. **Cook rice, then start curry:** After the soak, bring rice to a boil, cover and turn to low. Once it is gently cooking, begin the curry's coconut-and-paste stage in the other pan. Reduce and season the sauce, then add the chicken and pepper.
4. **Move rice off heat:** When the rice has absorbed its water, move it off the burner for its ten-minute covered rest. Heat the edamame water on the freed burner. Simmer the curry until the thickest chicken slices reach 165°F / 74°C, then season it with fish sauce and sugar.
5. **Finish the side:** Boil the edamame for its package time until a bean is tender, and drain well. Season with half the salt, taste and add the rest. Take the curry off the heat while the pods drain.
6. **Serve:** Fluff the rice and divide it among four bowls. Add the curry and serve the edamame in separate small bowls, with a bowl for the empty pods.

## Notes

The curry contains peanuts, including finely ground nuts in the sauce. Store leftovers separately.
