## Meal Overview

Use one full batch each of Panang Curry with Chicken, Plain Short-Grain White Rice, and Edamame with Sea Salt for four diners. The curry supplies 450 g raw chicken, 150 g pepper, 300 ml coconut milk and ground peanuts; rice supplies 300 g dry grain across the meal. The 450 g edamame weight includes discarded pods, so it makes four small sides rather than four full servings of shelled beans.

This is a Thai curry dinner with a plain short-grain rice base and a Japanese-style pod side. Rice provides a mild contrast to the concentrated coconut-peanut sauce. Keep the edamame's optional sesame oil and pepper flakes out for this meal, and add its salt gradually. Allow about 70–75 minutes for one cook using two burners.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start rice soaking:** Rinse the rice and begin its full 30-minute soak in the measured water. Set out the rice saucepan, a wide curry skillet and a pot for edamame; only two need to be on burners at once.
2. **Prepare curry ingredients:** During the soak, grind peanuts, finely slice lime leaves and pepper, measure coconut milk and seasonings, then slice chicken. Clean the raw-chicken work surface and keep chicken refrigerated until needed. Read the edamame package so its cooking requirement is known.
3. **Cook rice, then start curry:** After the soak, bring rice to a boil, cover and turn to low. Once it is gently cooking, begin the curry's coconut-and-paste stage in the other pan. Reduce and season the sauce as directed before adding chicken and pepper.
4. **Move rice off heat:** At the rice's endpoint, move it off the burner for its full ten-minute covered rest. Use the freed burner to heat the edamame water. Meanwhile finish the curry until thick chicken slices reach 165°F / 74°C, then taste and adjust fish sauce and sugar.
5. **Finish the side:** Cook edamame for its package time, check a bean's tenderness and drain well. Begin with only half its finishing salt. The curry can wait briefly off heat while the pods are drained; avoid keeping chicken simmering just to fill that short gap.
6. **Serve:** Fluff rice and divide among four plates or bowls. Add the modest panang portions and serve edamame in separate small bowls, with a discard bowl for pods. Do not add an extra lime-juice or basil finish; the main already specifies its makrut-leaf finish.

## Notes

The curry contains peanuts, including finely ground nuts in the sauce. Its seed variation still requires checking the selected curry paste and other packaged ingredients. If using a rice cooker, follow that appliance's cycle and holding guidance rather than adding the stovetop soak twice. Store leftovers separately and follow each recipe's chilling and reheating instructions.
