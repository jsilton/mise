## Meal Overview

Caramel-braised pork with coconut jasmine rice and fresh shrimp-and-avocado summer rolls. Keep the pork gently simmering until tender before reducing its sauce. The rolls add fresh herbs and a cool contrast to the rich main. For four diners, make one full batch of pork, half a batch of coconut jasmine rice and all six summer rolls. Cut the rolls in half to serve, three pieces per person.

The half rice batch starts with 180 g dry rice, 200 ml coconut milk and 120 ml water. Halve its seasonings too. It is shared as four small portions because the rolls also contain rice noodles and rice paper.

Allow about 2 hours 30 minutes elapsed and 1 hour 45 minutes hands-on for one cook. Use a heavy saucepan for the pork, a smaller saucepan with a snug lid for the rice, a shrimp pot and a bowl for soaking the noodles.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the pork first:** Make the caramel, then braise the pork gently until tender, about 2 hours 15 minutes.
2. **Prepare the rolls during the braise:** Cook the shrimp, make the sauce and cool the fillings before wrapping. Assemble the rolls close to serving and refrigerate them covered.
3. **Start rice about 40 minutes before serving:** Make the half batch of coconut jasmine rice in a small pan with a snug lid. Give it the full cooking time and covered rest.
4. **Finish the pork:** When the pork is tender, reduce the sauce and season it with fish sauce.
5. **Serve:** Divide the pork and half-batch rice among four plates. Cut each of the six rolls in half and give each diner three halves, with its dipping sauce.

## Notes

- The pork uses coconut water. The rice uses coconut milk.
- Prepare the cold components while the pork braises. Dinner is ready when the pork is tender.

## Timing and leftovers

Check that the pork is nearly tender before starting the rice. Keep the rice covered if it finishes first, and keep the rolls cold. Reduce the sauce while the rice rests.

Refrigerate pork and rice separately and use them within three to four days. The rolls are best the day they are made.
