## Meal Overview

Spaghetti carbonara with crisp guanciale, a peppery arugula salad with strawberries and feta, and a little garlic bread. The bread and salad are ready before the pasta goes in the water, because carbonara waits for no one. For five, divide the carbonara five ways. The salad and the garlic bread already stretch to five.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Bake the bread:** Heat the oven to 400°F and put the pasta water on to boil. Spread the garlic butter on the bread and bake until the edges are golden, 12 to 15 minutes.
2. **Blend the dressing:** Blend the strawberry vinaigrette while the bread bakes. Keep the arugula and toppings separate.
3. **Render the guanciale:** Cook the guanciale over medium heat until crisp, 8 to 10 minutes. Take the skillet off the heat.
4. **Boil the pasta:** Cook the spaghetti until al dente. Whisk the eggs, yolks, Pecorino and pepper over the pasta pot until warm.
5. **Toss the carbonara:** Add the drained pasta to the skillet off the heat. Pour in the egg mixture and a splash of pasta water and toss fast until creamy.
6. **Serve:** Dress the salad, slice the bread and serve the carbonara right away.
