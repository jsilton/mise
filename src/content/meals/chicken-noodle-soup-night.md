## Meal Overview

Make one full six-serving batch of Slow-Cooker Chicken Noodle Soup and half a batch of Garlic Bread, shared among six diners. The soup supplies 680 g raw chicken, 550 g onion, carrot and celery, and 225 g dry pasta. The bread is a small crisp accompaniment to the broth: use 200 g bread, 3 tbsp (42.5 g) butter, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp parsley, 12.5 g Parmesan and up to 1/8 tsp fine salt. Cut the finished half batch into six pieces, one per bowl.

Allow about 6 hours 40 minutes elapsed on LOW, or about 4 hours 40 minutes using a four-hour HIGH cook, with roughly 45 minutes of hands-on work. Cooker performance varies: chicken temperature and tender carrots determine readiness. The last 25–30 minutes use the oven and one pasta burner alongside the slow cooker. Have softened butter ready for that finishing stage.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare and start the soup:** Follow the main recipe's vegetable cuts and fully thawed chicken requirement. Load the cooker, cover and start immediately. Begin checking around five hours on LOW or three on HIGH; do not start the bread bake while the soup still needs substantial cooking.
2. **Prepare the finish:** When the soup is nearly ready, heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C and put the pasta water on to boil. Prepare the half batch of garlic butter and spread it over the split 200 g piece of bread. Keep the remaining loaf and unused ingredients for another meal.
3. **Confirm soup readiness and bake bread:** Check several thick chicken pieces for 165°F / 74°C and a carrot for tenderness. Once both are ready, put the bread in the oven for its 12–15-minute bake. Remove chicken to a clean board to shred while the bread cooks, then return it promptly to the hot broth.
4. **Cook pasta separately:** Time the fusilli's package cooking duration to finish near the bread. Drain when just tender. Keep it separate from the soup so each bowl gets its share and any leftovers retain more texture. If carrots need extra stove cooking, delay both bread and pasta until they are close to tender.
5. **Finish and serve:** Season the fully cooked soup with its measured salt, parsley and staged lemon juice. Check that the bread's center is hot and its edges are golden; omit the optional broil unless more color is wanted. Divide pasta, chicken, vegetables and broth among six bowls, and give each diner one small piece of bread.

## Notes

The bread's parsley, garlic, butter and cheese are additional to the soup ingredients; the quantities above are the complete half-batch supply. Soup already contains pasta, so extra crackers are not part of this meal. For leftovers, promptly refrigerate soup and pasta separately in shallow containers and follow each component's storage guidance. Reheat soup on the stove or in the microwave to 165°F / 74°C, then combine with reheated pasta by portion.
