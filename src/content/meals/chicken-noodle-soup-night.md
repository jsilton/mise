## Meal Overview

Make one full six-serving batch of Slow-Cooker Chicken Noodle Soup and half a batch of Garlic Bread, shared among six diners. The soup supplies 680 g raw chicken, 550 g onion, carrot and celery, and 225 g dry pasta. The bread is a small crisp accompaniment to the broth: use 200 g bread, 3 tbsp (42.5 g) butter, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp parsley, 12.5 g Parmesan and 1/8 tsp fine salt. Cut the finished half batch into six pieces, one per bowl.

Allow about 6 hours 40 minutes elapsed on LOW, with about 45 minutes of hands-on work. A four-hour HIGH cook takes about 4 hours 40 minutes. The last 25 to 30 minutes use the oven and one pasta burner alongside the slow cooker. Have softened butter ready for that finishing stage.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Prepare and start the soup:** Cut the vegetables and load the cooker with the thawed chicken. Cover and start it right away. Begin checking at five hours on LOW or three on HIGH.
2. **Prepare the finish:** When the soup is nearly ready, heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C and put the pasta water on to boil. Prepare the half batch of garlic butter and spread it over the split 200 g piece of bread.
3. **Check the soup and bake bread:** Cook until the thickest chicken pieces reach 165°F / 74°C and a carrot is tender. Put the bread in the oven for its 12 to 15 minute bake. Shred the chicken on a board while the bread bakes, then return it to the hot broth.
4. **Cook pasta separately:** Boil the fusilli so it finishes with the bread. Drain it when just tender. Keep it separate from the soup so each bowl gets its share.
5. **Finish and serve:** Season the soup with its salt, parsley and lemon juice. Take the bread out when its center is hot and its edges are golden. Divide the pasta, chicken, vegetables and broth among six bowls, and give each diner one piece of bread.

## Notes

Refrigerate leftover soup and pasta separately. Reheat the soup on the stove until steaming, then add the pasta by portion.
