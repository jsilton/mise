## Meal Overview

Mustard and rosemary pork tenderloin wrapped in maple bacon, with balsamic Brussels sprouts and garlic roasted potatoes. The pork chills in its coating for an hour, which is when the potatoes roast. Then the sprouts and pork share a hotter oven.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Coat the pork:** Rub the mustard and rosemary coating over both tenderloins. Wrap them and refrigerate for 1 hour.
2. **Roast the potatoes:** Heat the oven to 400°F. Roast the potatoes cut side down until golden and tender, 40 to 55 minutes, turning them once after 25 minutes.
3. **Finish the potatoes:** Toss the potatoes with the garlic oil and roast 5 minutes more. Take them out, set an empty baking sheet in the oven and raise the heat to 425°F.
4. **Wrap the pork:** Lay the bacon over the tenderloins and tie them with twine. Set them on the rosemary and garlic in a roasting pan.
5. **Roast the sprouts and pork:** Spread the sprouts cut side down on the hot sheet and roast for 20 minutes. Roast the pork beside them until it reads 140°F, 18 to 25 minutes.
6. **Glaze and broil:** Toss the sprouts with the balsamic glaze, roast 5 minutes more and take them out. Broil the pork until the bacon crisps and the meat reaches 145°F, 1 to 2 minutes.
7. **Rest and serve:** Rest the pork for 10 minutes and return the potatoes to the turned-off oven to rewarm. Slice the pork into rounds and toss the potatoes with the parsley.
