## Meal Overview

Pesto chicken roasted with broccoli, zucchini, mushrooms and tomatoes, served in bowls over lemon herb quinoa with cumin-chili sweet potatoes. The sweet potatoes need a hotter oven, so they roast first. For five, use four chicken breasts and spread everything over two sheet pans.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Roast the sweet potatoes:** Heat the oven to 425°F with a lined sheet on the lower rack. Roast the seasoned wedges for 20 minutes, flip them and roast 10 to 12 minutes more.
2. **Cook the quinoa:** Rinse and toast the quinoa, then simmer it covered for 15 minutes. Rest it covered for 5 minutes.
3. **Prep the chicken:** Stir the pesto mixture and cube the chicken while the sweet potatoes roast.
4. **Roast the chicken:** Take out the sweet potatoes and lower the oven to 400°F. Roast the chicken and mushrooms for 10 minutes.
5. **Add the vegetables:** Add the broccoli, zucchini and tomatoes and roast until the chicken reaches 165°F, about 10 minutes more.
6. **Serve:** Fold the lemon and herbs into the quinoa. Divide it among four bowls with the chicken, vegetables and sweet potatoes and spoon the reserved pesto over the top.
