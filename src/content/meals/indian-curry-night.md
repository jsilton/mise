## Meal Overview

Coconut-and-almond vegetable korma, a small mound of plain basmati and a soft naan for each person. The curry supplies the vegetables and sauce. Rice and bread share the starch portion. Yogurt gives the korma a little tang, so it needs no extra cream or separate sauce.

Allow about **5 1/2 hours elapsed** and **90 to 110 minutes of hands-on work** when making everything from scratch. The slow cooker and the dough rise set the pace. This is a weekend meal with a mostly unattended curry and an active bread-making finish.

## Portions for Six

- **Korma:** Make one full [Slow Cooker Vegetable Korma](/mise/recipes/slow-cooker-vegetable-korma) batch, including its beans, peas and yogurt finish. Serve about **1 1/4 cups each**, dividing the vegetables and sauce evenly.
- **Rice:** Make **half the [Basmati Rice](/mise/recipes/basmati-rice) recipe**, plain: **1 cup (200 g) dry aged white basmati, 1 1/2 cups (360 ml) water, 1 1/2 tsp ghee, and 3/8 tsp fine salt**. Leave out the whole spices. Keep **1 tbsp hot water** ready for rice that is dry but still firm. Use a narrow **2-quart saucepan** with a tight lid. Rinse and drain without soaking. This gives about **3 cups cooked**, or **1/2 cup per person**.
- **Naan:** Make **half the [Naan](/mise/recipes/naan) dough** and divide into **six balls of about 70 g**. Use **250 g flour, 1 tbsp oil in the dough plus 1/2 tsp for coating, 2 tbsp unstrained whole-milk yogurt, 3.5 g active dry yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 3.75 g fine salt, 1/2 tsp baking powder and 120 ml warm whole milk**. Keep **1 tbsp flour** for dusting and **1 tbsp milk** for correction separate. Roll each ball to 5 by 8 inches. Leave off the garlic butter for these plain breads. Cook six rounds, allowing about **20 to 25 minutes** for rolling and cooking.

These are modest rice portions because everyone also gets a naan. For five diners, make the same batches and serve the extra rice, curry and sixth bread as seconds.

## Cooking Strategy

Use a conventional **6-quart slow cooker**, two burners, a **2-quart lidded rice saucepan**, and a **12-inch heavy skillet**. Keep the hot bread wrapped in a towel.

1. **About 5 1/2 hours before dinner, start the curry:** Take 25 minutes to wash, trim, weigh and cut the vegetables and mix the coconut sauce. Load the cooker and start it on **High** right away. Refrigerate the prepared beans and yogurt. Leave the peas frozen.
2. **About 3 hours before dinner, make the naan dough:** Hydrate the yeast, mix the half batch of dough, knead it and cover it for the **60 to 90 minute rise**, until it has about doubled. Wash the mixing tools and set the table during this wait.
3. **After 3 hours of curry cooking, check the vegetables:** Test a carrot and a thick cauliflower stem. Add the beans when both are nearly tender, and cook about **45 to 60 minutes** more on High. Begin checking the beans after **30 to 45 minutes**. Add the peas when the beans are nearly tender, for the last **10 to 15 minutes**. If the carrots are still hard at the first test, check again in 20 to 30 minutes and start the rice and bread later. If the curry finishes early, hold it covered on Warm with the yogurt still separate.
4. **About 45 to 50 minutes before serving, set up the final round:** When the dough has doubled, divide it into six balls and cover them for a ten-minute rest. Rinse the rice and start it on the first burner with the half-batch water and salt. Once it reaches a gentle boil, cover, reduce to a gentle simmer and set a timer for **15 to 18 minutes**.
5. **Cook the breads while the rice simmers and rests:** Heat the dry skillet on the second burner during the last five minutes of the dough-ball rest. Roll two balls, then cook them one at a time. Take the pan off the burner while rolling the next pair, and reheat it before the next bread. Tear the first naan at its thick center to check that no raw dough remains, and wrap each finished bread in the towel. When the rice timer sounds, stir the reserved hot water into rice that is dry but still firm and cover it for a few more minutes. Then turn off the heat for the **ten-minute covered rest**.
6. **Finish the curry and serve:** Switch off the slow cooker when the peas are hot and all the vegetables are tender. Let the curry stop bubbling for about five minutes, temper and fold in the yogurt, then season with salt. Fluff the rice and bring all six breads to the table with the curry.

## Make Ahead and Leftovers

For less bread work on the day, make and freeze the six plain naan ahead, then thaw and warm them in a covered skillet. With ready naan and curry made from raw vegetables that day, allow the same curry window and about **45 to 60 minutes hands-on**, including rice and reheating the breads.

The curry also cooks ahead **without its yogurt finish**. With refrigerated curry and thawed plain naan, allow about **45 to 60 minutes elapsed and 30 to 40 minutes hands-on**. Start the rice, reheat the curry in a **4-quart saucepan** on the other burner until it simmers, then cover it and use that burner to warm the naan. Temper in the yogurt just before serving.

Refrigerate curry and rice separately and use them within **3 to 4 days**. Wrap cooled plain naan and keep it for up to two days at room temperature, or freeze it.
