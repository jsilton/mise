## Meal Overview

Chicken thighs braised in red wine with lardons, mushrooms and carrots, served with garlic mashed red potatoes and lemony green beans. The coq au vin takes two and a half hours from start to finish, and the sides are made in its last half hour. For five, braise 2 1/2 lb of thighs in the same pot. The potatoes already make six portions.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Brown everything:** Render the lardons, brown the mushrooms and sear the chicken in two batches in a Dutch oven.
2. **Reduce the wine:** Soften the onion and carrots, stir in the garlic and tomato paste and reduce the wine by half, 10 to 15 minutes.
3. **Braise the chicken:** Return the chicken and lardons with the stock and thyme. Cover and simmer gently until the meat is tender at the bone, 45 to 60 minutes. Add the mushrooms for the last 10 minutes.
4. **Cook the potatoes:** Start the potatoes 30 minutes before the braise ends. Simmer them with the garlic until they crush easily, then mash them with the butter, Parmesan and warm milk.
5. **Cook the green beans:** Soften the shallots in butter while the bean water boils. Boil the beans for 4 to 6 minutes, then toss them with the shallots and lemon.
6. **Finish the sauce:** Move the chicken to a platter and simmer the sauce until it coats a spoon, 5 to 10 minutes. Swirl in the cold butter and return the chicken.
7. **Serve:** Spoon the chicken and sauce beside the potatoes and beans.
