## Meal Overview

Chicken thighs braised with red wine, bacon, mushrooms and carrots, with mashed potatoes and lemony green beans. Make one full four-portion batch of [Coq au Vin](/mise/recipes/chicken-coq-au-vin/), one full batch of [Garlic Mashed Red Potatoes](/mise/recipes/garlic-mashed-red-potatoes/) and one full four-side-portion batch of [Green Beans with Shallots and Lemon](/mise/recipes/green-beans-with-shallots-and-lemon/). The potato recipe makes six portions; reserve two for another meal.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Plan three hours from raw ingredients:** Allow a wide Dutch oven for the chicken, pots for potatoes and bean water, and a skillet for shallots. The chicken component alone allows 2 hours 30 minutes; the dinner estimate gives one cook extra preparation and finishing time.
2. **Follow the chicken's order:** Render bacon, brown and reserve the mushrooms, then sear the thighs in two batches. Cook the onion and carrots, add garlic and tomato paste, and reduce the wine before adding the chicken, bacon, stock and thyme. The recipe uses chopped yellow onion, not an additional supply of pearl onions.
3. **Braise gently:** Allow 45–60 minutes after the pot reaches a gentle simmer. Add the reserved mushrooms for the last ten minutes. Check chicken reaches at least 165°F / 74°C away from the bone and is tender near the joint; extend the braise if needed.
4. **Start the sides during the braise:** Prepare the potatoes, garlic, beans, shallots and lemon. Begin the potato component about 35 minutes before expected service, allowing time for water to reach a boil as well as its 15–20-minute simmer. Start the green-bean component about 35 minutes before service, softening shallots while the bean water heats. Use separate pots, or allow additional time if burners are limited.
5. **Finish to the chicken's readiness:** When the chicken is tender, follow its sauce reduction and off-heat butter finish. Drain, steam-dry and mash the potatoes; finish the beans in the shallot skillet and add their lemon off heat. If the chicken needs more time, delay the final bean cook and potato mash.
6. **Plate:** Divide chicken and sauce among four plates, add four of the six potato portions, and divide all the beans among the plates. Reserve the extra potatoes promptly following normal leftover handling.

## Notes

This schedule starts from raw chicken. A prepared-ahead version needs a separate reheating plan; the possibility of cooking coq au vin ahead does not shorten the displayed from-scratch time.
