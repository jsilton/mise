## Meal Overview

Crisp baked chicken with mozzarella and marinara over fresh egg pasta, with garlic bread and a lemony arugula salad. Making the pasta from scratch sets the pace. The chicken and bread go in the oven once the noodles are cut. For five, use 2 lb of chicken for five cutlets and share the pasta five ways. The bread already makes six portions.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Make the pasta dough:** Mix and knead the dough. Wrap it and rest it for 1 hour at room temperature.
2. **Prep during the rest:** Toast the panko and mix it with the Parmesan. Mash the garlic butter and whisk the salad dressing.
3. **Roll and cut the pasta:** Roll the dough into thin sheets and cut them into ribbons. Toss the ribbons with semolina and set them in loose nests.
4. **Bake the chicken:** Heat the oven to 425°F and bring the pasta water to a boil. Bread the cutlets and bake them on a rack until golden and 165°F, 15 to 20 minutes.
5. **Bake the bread:** Bake the garlic bread on a second rack beside the chicken until the edges are golden, about 12 minutes.
6. **Melt the cheese:** Spoon a third of the warm marinara over the cutlets, top with the mozzarella and bake until it melts, 3 to 5 minutes.
7. **Boil the pasta:** Cook the fresh pasta until tender with a slight bite, 2 to 3 minutes. Toss it with the rest of the marinara and a splash of pasta water.
8. **Serve:** Toss the arugula with its dressing. Serve the chicken over the pasta with the bread and salad.
