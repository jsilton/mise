## Meal Overview

Breaded chicken with mozzarella and tomato sauce over fresh egg pasta, with garlic bread and peppery arugula. Allow about 2 hours 30 minutes when making the pasta from scratch.

## Component Portions

- **Chicken and sauce:** Make one full four-portion batch of [Baked Chicken Parmesan](/mise/recipes/baked-chicken-parmesan/), using all its chicken, coating, cheese and marinara. Omit its 8 oz dried pasta and its separate pasta-water salt; the fresh-pasta component replaces both.
- **Fresh pasta:** Make one full four-portion batch of [Fresh Egg Pasta](/mise/recipes/fresh-egg-pasta/), including its own dough, dusting supplies, cooking water and water salt. Keep the chicken recipe's sauce split: one third on the chicken and two thirds for the pasta.
- **Sides:** Make a full four-portion [Arugula Salad](/mise/recipes/everyday-arugula-salad/) and a full six-side-portion [Garlic Bread](/mise/recipes/garlic-bread/). Serve four bread portions and reserve two.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Mix and rest the dough first:** Mix and knead the fresh pasta as directed, then rest it covered for one hour. Set out the garlic-bread butter to soften. Allow more time if learning to roll pasta by hand.
2. **Prepare during the rest:** Wash and dry the arugula, make its dressing separately, and prepare the bread spread. Prepare and refrigerate the chicken cutlets; toast the crumbs, cool them and prepare the remaining coating and sauce. Keep raw-chicken utensils and surfaces separate from the dough and salad.
3. **Roll before cooking the chicken:** After the full dough rest, roll and cut the pasta following its thickness cues. Keep the noodles in loose dusted nests; refrigerate them if delayed. Heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C and bake the garlic bread for its full 12–15 minutes during the end of the rolling work. Remove the bread, then raise the oven to 425°F / 220°C for the chicken.
4. **Bake and check the chicken:** Coat the cutlets and bake according to their recipe, checking after 15 minutes and continuing until each center reaches 165°F / 74°C. Meanwhile, bring the fresh pasta's cooking water to a boil and warm the marinara.
5. **Coordinate the last five minutes:** Add the chicken's sauce and mozzarella and return it to the oven for 3–5 minutes. Cook the fresh noodles for about 2–5 minutes, checking for no floury core rather than following dried-pasta package timing. Reserve cooking water and toss the noodles with their share of sauce. Rewarm the bread loosely covered with foil alongside the chicken if needed; do not broil it near parchment.
6. **Serve:** Toss the arugula with dressing just before plating. Divide chicken, pasta and salad among four and serve the warm bread alongside.

## Notes

Prep and cooking estimates overlap the dough rest and other tasks; they are not an additive elapsed-time formula. The 2-hour-30-minute plan includes mixing, the full rest, rolling, chicken cooking and side preparation. Already shaped refrigerated pasta can shorten a later service, but that is not the starting assumption here. Keep raw dough and noodles within the handling limits in the pasta recipe.
