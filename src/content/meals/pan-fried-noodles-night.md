## Meal Overview

Cantonese pan-fried noodles: a crisp noodle cake under shrimp in a glossy gravy, with a cold smashed cucumber salad on the side. The salad is made first, because the noodles must be eaten while the edges are still crisp. For five, cut the noodle cake into five wedges and make one and a half batches of the cucumber salad.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the cucumbers:** Smash and salt the cucumbers and let them drain in the fridge for 15 minutes. Mix their dressing.
2. **Prep the shrimp and gravy:** Treat the shrimp with the baking soda for 5 minutes, then rinse and dry them. Mix the gravy and the slurry.
3. **Dress the cucumbers:** Toss the cucumbers with their dressing and top them with the sesame seeds and scallion.
4. **Fry the noodle cake:** Boil the noodles for about 1 minute and drain them very well. Fry them in a thin cake until golden and crisp, about 5 minutes per side.
5. **Blanch the greens:** Blanch the yu choy in the noodle water for 30 seconds to 1 minute and arrange it around the noodle cake.
6. **Make the shrimp gravy:** Stir-fry the carrots, mushrooms, ginger and garlic. Sear the shrimp for 1 minute per side, then pour in the gravy and thicken it with the slurry.
7. **Serve:** Pour the shrimp and gravy over the noodle cake and serve at once with the cucumbers.
