## Meal Overview

Ragù alla bolognese simmered for three hours and tossed with fresh egg pasta, with garlic bread and an arugula salad. The pasta is made and rested while the ragù simmers, so the whole afternoon is one long, easy overlap.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the ragù:** Render the pork belly, soften the vegetables and brown the beef. Reduce the wine, add the tomato and broth and cover at the barest simmer for 1 to 1 1/2 hours.
2. **Make the pasta dough:** While the ragù simmers, mix and knead the dough. Wrap it and rest it for 1 hour.
3. **Add the milk:** Stir the milk into the ragù. Cover and simmer until the meat is very tender and the sauce is glossy, 1 to 1 1/2 hours more.
4. **Roll and cut the pasta:** Roll the dough into thin sheets and cut them into ribbons. Toss them with semolina and set them in loose nests.
5. **Bake the bread:** Heat the oven to 400°F and bring the pasta water to a boil. Bake the garlic bread until the edges are golden, 12 to 15 minutes.
6. **Boil and toss the pasta:** Cook the pasta for 2 to 3 minutes. Toss it with the hot ragù and a splash of pasta water until the sauce coats the ribbons.
7. **Serve:** Toss the arugula with its dressing and serve everything with grated Parmigiano-Reggiano.
