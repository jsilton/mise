## Meal Overview

Roasted eggplant, peppers and squash folded into a fresh tomato sauce and spooned over creamy polenta, with a lemony arugula salad. The polenta and the ratatouille both take about an hour and cook side by side. For five, make one and a quarter batches of polenta, using 1 1/4 cups. The ratatouille stretches to five.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the polenta:** Heat the oven to 425°F. Bring the water, milk and salt to a gentle boil and whisk in the polenta. Turn the heat to its lowest setting and cook for 35 to 40 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes.
2. **Roast the vegetables:** Roast the eggplant on one sheet and the pepper and squash on another for 15 minutes. Stir, swap the racks and roast until tender, 10 to 20 minutes more.
3. **Simmer the sauce:** Cook the onion in a Dutch oven until golden at the edges, 8 to 10 minutes. Add the garlic and grated tomatoes and simmer until thick.
4. **Combine:** Fold the roasted vegetables into the sauce and simmer for 5 minutes. Stir in the basil, pepper flakes and oregano off the heat.
5. **Finish the polenta:** Stir the butter and Parmigiano-Reggiano into the polenta off the heat.
6. **Serve:** Toss the arugula with its dressing. Spoon the ratatouille over the polenta and serve the salad beside it.
