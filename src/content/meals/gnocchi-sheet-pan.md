## Meal Overview

Gnocchi roasted on a sheet pan with peppers, cherry tomatoes and red onion until plump and golden, with an arugula salad. One pan and one bowl make dinner in 40 minutes. For five, add the half pound of shrimp from the gnocchi recipe.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Roast the gnocchi:** Heat the oven to 450°F. Toss the gnocchi, peppers, tomatoes, onion and garlic with the oil and rosemary and roast on a large sheet pan for 18 to 20 minutes, stirring once halfway.
2. **Make the salad:** Whisk the lemon dressing in a wide bowl while the pan roasts. Toss in the arugula just before serving and top it with the Parmigiano.
3. **Finish and serve:** Toss the gnocchi and vegetables with the basil and Parmigiano-Reggiano. Serve in shallow bowls with the salad.
