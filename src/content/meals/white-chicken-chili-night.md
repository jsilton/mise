## Meal Overview

Prepare one full six-serving White Chicken Chili with Hominy and one 8-inch pan of Buttermilk Cornbread. The chili contains 680 g raw thigh, about 510 g drained white beans and 255 g drained hominy. Cut the cornbread into twelve small squares, serve six and save six; the beans and hominy already supply much of the meal's starch.

The chili's 120 g sour cream is stirred into the pot, not an additional topping for each bowl. Finish with its two scallions and, if wanted, the listed 60 g Monterey Jack across all six servings. Plan about 5 hours 40 minutes elapsed using a five-hour LOW cook, or about 3 hours 40 minutes if HIGH takes three hours, with roughly 50 minutes active work.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the chili:** Follow the main recipe's drained weights, partial bean mashing, quarter-inch onion dice and fully thawed thighs. Load the slow cooker with the complete base and start immediately. Keep sour cream, lime, scallions and cheese for the finish.
2. **Plan bread near actual readiness:** Begin chicken checks at four hours on LOW or two on HIGH. When service is about 50 minutes away, heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C and prepare the specified 8-inch metal pan. If the cooker is running slowly or onion is still firm, adjust the bread start rather than following the clock blindly.
3. **Mix and bake bread:** Measure and mix the cornbread as directed, baking 20–25 minutes to its center-set endpoint. Allow ten minutes cooling before slicing. Keep surfaces used for raw chicken separate from bread ingredients and ready-to-eat toppings.
4. **Shred and finish the base:** Once thick thighs reach 165°F / 74°C and shred readily, remove and shred them, returning promptly to the hot base. Check onion tenderness and adjust bean body and salt as the main describes. Finish any firm onion before adding sour cream.
5. **Add dairy near serving:** During the cornbread cooling period, turn off the chili heat and let bubbling stop. Temper and stir in the measured sour cream, then add lime gradually. Do not add cream cheese or heavy cream, or hold the dairy-finished chili at a boil while waiting for the bread.
6. **Serve:** Divide chili among six bowls, finish with scallions and optional measured Jack, and serve one small cornbread square each. Cool and wrap the other six squares for a later meal.

## Notes

This schedule uses the main's raw-thigh slow-cooker method; an unquantified rotisserie-chicken shortcut is not interchangeable with it. No extra avocado, cilantro, tortilla chips or sour cream is required beyond the component lists. Refrigerate leftover chili promptly in shallow containers and reheat gently to 165°F / 74°C on the stove or in the microwave.
