## Meal Overview

Prepare one full six-serving White Chicken Chili with Hominy and one 8-inch pan of Buttermilk Cornbread. The chili contains 680 g raw thigh, about 510 g drained white beans and 255 g drained hominy. Cut the cornbread into twelve small squares, serve six and save six. The beans and hominy already supply much of the meal's starch.

The chili's 120 g sour cream is stirred into the pot. Finish the six bowls with its two scallions and 60 g Monterey Jack. Allow about 5 hours 40 minutes elapsed with a five-hour LOW cook and about 50 minutes active work. A three-hour HIGH cook takes about 3 hours 40 minutes.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the chili:** Drain the beans and hominy, mash part of the beans, cut the onion into quarter-inch dice and use fully thawed thighs. Load the slow cooker and start it right away. Keep the sour cream, lime, scallions and cheese for the finish.
2. **Time the bread:** Begin checking the chicken at four hours on LOW or two on HIGH. About 50 minutes before serving, heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C and grease an 8-inch metal pan.
3. **Mix and bake bread:** Mix the cornbread and bake until the center is set, 20 to 25 minutes. Cool it for ten minutes before slicing.
4. **Shred and finish the base:** Once the thickest thighs reach 165°F / 74°C and shred easily, lift them out, shred them and return them to the hot base. Cook until the onion is tender, then season with salt.
5. **Add dairy near serving:** While the cornbread cools, turn off the chili and let the bubbling stop. Temper and stir in the sour cream, then add the lime gradually.
6. **Serve:** Divide the chili among six bowls, top with the scallions and Jack, and serve one small cornbread square each. Cool and wrap the other six squares for a later meal.

## Notes

Refrigerate leftover chili and reheat it gently on the stove.
