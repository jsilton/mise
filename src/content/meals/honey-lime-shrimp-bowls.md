## Meal Overview

Honey-lime shrimp over rich coconut jasmine rice, with juicy bok choy in garlic and oyster sauce. Make **one full batch of each component for four bowls**: one pound of shrimp, rice starting with 360 g dry jasmine rice and 400 ml coconut milk, and 600 g trimmed bok choy. The rice recipe's six side portions become four generous bowl bases. The shrimp are a modest topping.

Leave the sugar out of the rice, because the shrimp already bring a sweet honey glaze. Bok choy adds a savory vegetable contrast. Use just enough of its sauce to coat it, so the bowl is not too salty.

Allow about **one hour elapsed and 55 minutes hands-on** for one cook, with peeled, deveined and thawed shrimp. Use a heavy lidded rice saucepan, a lidded 12-inch vegetable skillet and a separate 12-inch shrimp skillet.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the rice:** Rinse and drain the rice, combine it with the coconut milk, water and seasonings, and bring to a gentle boil. Cover and simmer for 18 to 22 minutes, then rest it covered for ten minutes.
2. **Prepare everything while rice simmers:** Wash and drain the bok choy, cut the thick bases to an even size, mix its oyster-soy sauce and measure its oil in three parts. Prepare the shrimp garlic, herbs, honey and lime juice. Dry the shrimp last and keep them cold.
3. **Check the rice:** At the end of its 18 to 22 minute simmer, check that the grains are tender and the liquid is absorbed. Take it off the heat for its covered rest.
4. **Cook bok choy first:** Pan-steam the bok choy in two batches, then finish it with the garlic sauce, about fifteen minutes in all. Drain excess liquid between stages, finish with sesame oil, then transfer to a warm serving dish.
5. **Cook shrimp last:** Cook the garlic, then the shrimp, then the honey glaze over medium heat, about ten minutes. The shrimp are done when the thickest ones are firm and opaque at the center, **145°F / 63°C**. Lift them out if the glaze needs more reducing. Add the remaining lime off the heat.
6. **Build bowls:** Fluff the rice and divide it among four bowls. Share the bok choy and shrimp evenly, spoon the glaze over the shrimp and add the herbs. Serve as soon as the shrimp finish.

## Timing and leftovers

The rice finishes before the final pans. Keep it covered in its pot. Keep the bok choy warm while the shrimp cook, and serve right away because holding softens its leaves.

Refrigerate leftover components separately and use them within three to four days. Shrimp firm up and bok choy softens on reheating.
