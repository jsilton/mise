## Meal Overview

For four people, make one full batch each of Mongolian Beef with Ginger and Scallions, Plain Short-Grain White Rice and Bok Choy with Garlic and Oyster Sauce. That is 680 g raw beef, 300 g dry rice and 600 g trimmed bok choy. The beef supplies a sweet-savory glaze. Plain rice takes up a little sauce, and the bok choy provides juicy stems and tender greens.

Allow about 75 minutes elapsed and 50 minutes active for one cook. The rice's thirty-minute soak and ten-minute rest are part of that time. Use the rice saucepan, a skillet for the beef and a separate lidded skillet for the bok choy.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start the rice:** Rinse the 300 g rice, drain it and soak it in its 400 ml water for thirty minutes.
2. **Prepare the beef during the soak:** Slice all 680 g across the grain, toss it with the coating and divide it into three pan batches. Mix the sauce and the slurry. Slice the scallion whites and greens separately.
3. **Wash the bok choy and heat rice:** Thoroughly clean and drain 600 g bok choy, cut larger heads into quarters, and mix its oyster sauce. After the soak, bring the rice to a boil and cook it covered at a gentle simmer. Set a timer for the cooking and the off-heat rest.
4. **Cook the bok choy first:** Pan-steam the bok choy in two batches and finish it briefly with the garlic. Transfer it to a warmed serving dish and cover it loosely while the beef cooks.
5. **Finish the beef:** Sear the three batches, each in its share of the oil. Cook the aromatics and thicken the sauce. Toss the beef and scallion greens in the sauce briefly. Rest it for three minutes before serving.
6. **Serve together:** Fluff the rested rice and divide all three components into four portions. Spoon just enough bok choy sauce over the greens to coat them. Top the bok choy with its fried garnish.

## Cool spinach variation

For a different green side, replace the bok choy with one full batch of [Sigeumchi Namul (Sesame Spinach)](/mise/recipes/garlic-sesame-spinach). Its 450 g raw spinach makes four small portions after blanching and gently squeezing. It is a smaller vegetable serving than the bok choy. Keep the beef and rice quantities unchanged.

Make the spinach during the rice soak. Blanch it in two brief batches, cool it, drain it, press it gently, then dress it with its garlic and sesame seasoning. Refrigerate until serving. Serve the spinach cool beside the hot beef and rice. The dinner still takes 75 minutes.

## Make-ahead and leftovers

Measure the sauces and wash and drain the bok choy ahead to shorten the dinner session. Keep the sliced beef refrigerated until cooking.

Refrigerate leftovers and use them within 3 to 4 days. Reheat portions with a small splash of water. The beef coating and bok choy soften in storage.
