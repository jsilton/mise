## Meal Overview

Serve one full batch each of Thai Basil Chicken & Eggplant, Coconut Jasmine Rice, and Japanese Cucumber Sunomono among four diners. The rice recipe normally makes six side portions; here its 360 g dry rice is shared four ways as the main starch. The chicken supplies about 112 g raw meat and 112 g eggplant per person, with a small 100 g cucumber side before draining.

The coconut rice adds richness to the savory glaze, while the Japanese cucumber side adds a cool texture and sweet-sour contrast. Allow about 65–70 minutes for one cook with two burners.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start rice first:** Rinse the jasmine rice and start the coconut-rice method in its lidded saucepan. Keep its full cooking and ten-minute rest; do not compress the rice timing to match the quick final stir-fry.
2. **Prepare vegetables and cucumber:** Cut the eggplant and stir-fry aromatics. Slice, salt and drain the cucumber as directed. Mix its dressing but refrigerate dressing and drained cucumber separately until the finish.
3. **Prepare chicken and sauce:** Slice and marinate chicken, keeping it refrigerated during the remaining preparation. Mix the main's sauce, then clean the raw-chicken work area. Set the skillet beside the rice pot.
4. **Soften eggplant first:** Follow the main's two eggplant batches, including the covered steaming stage. Meanwhile check the rice at its stated endpoint and move it off heat to rest. Use a timer so neither pan is forgotten.
5. **Cook chicken and finish:** Cook chicken to 165°F / 74°C before combining with aromatics, sauce and tender eggplant. Add basil and scallion greens off heat. The chicken does not remain in the skillet during the eggplant's full cooking time.
6. **Dress and serve:** Toss cucumber with dressing for its brief chill near the end of cooking. Finish with sesame, fluff rice, and share the three components among four plates. Serve the stir-fry promptly.

## Notes

Choose the main's chile quantity for the diners before cooking. No extra eggplant side, second protein, or additional sauce is required. Store rice, stir-fry and cucumber separately and follow each component's chilling and reheating guidance.
