## Meal Overview

Serve one full batch each of Thai Basil Chicken & Eggplant, Coconut Jasmine Rice, and Japanese Cucumber Sunomono among four diners. The 360 g dry rice is shared four ways. The chicken supplies about 112 g raw meat and 112 g eggplant per person, with a small 100 g cucumber side before draining.

The coconut rice adds richness to the savory glaze, while the Japanese cucumber side adds a cool texture and sweet-sour contrast. Allow about 65 to 70 minutes for one cook with two burners.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Start rice first:** Rinse the jasmine rice and start the coconut rice in its lidded saucepan. Give it its full cooking time and ten-minute rest.
2. **Prepare vegetables and cucumber:** Cut the eggplant and stir-fry aromatics. Slice, salt and drain the cucumber. Mix its dressing and refrigerate the dressing and drained cucumber separately.
3. **Prepare chicken and sauce:** Slice and marinate the chicken and refrigerate it. Mix the main's sauce. Set the skillet beside the rice pot.
4. **Soften eggplant first:** Cook the eggplant in two batches, browning each and then steaming it covered. When the rice has absorbed its liquid, move it off the heat to rest.
5. **Cook chicken and finish:** Cook the chicken to 165°F / 74°C, then add the aromatics, sauce and tender eggplant. Add the basil and scallion greens off the heat.
6. **Dress and serve:** Toss the cucumber with its dressing near the end of cooking and chill it briefly. Finish it with sesame, fluff the rice, and share the three components among four plates.

## Notes

Store rice, stir-fry and cucumber separately.
