## Meal Overview

A weekend dim sum spread made at home: translucent shrimp har gow, pork and shrimp shumai, a soy ginger dipping sauce and a cold smashed cucumber salad. The 43 dumplings give five people eight or nine each. Shaping takes most of the three hours, and the shumai are the easier ones for kids to fold.

## Cooking Strategy

1. **Soak the mushrooms:** Soak the dried shiitakes for the shumai in hot water for 30 minutes.
2. **Make the har gow filling:** Stir the shrimp filling until sticky, fold in the bamboo shoots and refrigerate it for 30 minutes.
3. **Shape the shumai:** Mix the pork and shrimp filling. Cup each wrapper around a heaping tablespoon of filling, top it with carrot and set it in a lined steamer basket.
4. **Shape the har gow:** Make the wheat starch dough and cut it into 18 pieces. Press each piece into a thin round, fill it and pleat it closed.
5. **Make the sauce:** Stir the dipping sauce and let it sit for 10 minutes.
6. **Make the salad:** Smash and salt the cucumbers, drain them for 15 minutes and dress them.
7. **Steam in batches:** Steam the shumai over boiling water for 8 to 10 minutes a batch. Steam the har gow for about 6 minutes a batch, until the wrappers turn translucent.
8. **Serve:** Bring each basket to the table as it finishes, with the dipping sauce and cucumbers.
